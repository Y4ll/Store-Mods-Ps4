Custom Capes is a cape pack for Minecraft Bedrock. Here you can find capes that came with our texture packs and custom capes with themes.

This first version includes five custom capes, such as penguins, PVZ, etc., and five Halloween-themed capes. We'll be adding more capes later, around December.

This addon will be updated every few months, depending on the date, such as Halloween, Christmas, or Valentine's Day.
