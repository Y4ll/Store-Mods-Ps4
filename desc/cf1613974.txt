More Body Actions + Pick up & Carry with OP HUD

WARNING: Needs Beta APIs to be enabled.

By TNTBROS

Lets you sit and more, with a new HUD! This Minecraft Bedrock addon adds a whole new layer of body actions to your character, plus the ability to pick up and carry other entities, all controlled through a slick custom on-screen HUD.

- To sit, look down and sneak.

- To crawl, walk forwards while sitting.

- To climb, walk up to a wall, look slightly up, then jump.

- To lie down, look up then sneak

- To carry, look at the object/entity, sneak then jump.

Features

- Sit down on blocks, stairs, and slabs

- Crawl, Climb, and other new body poses

- Pick up mobs, players, and blocks and carry them around

- Throw or place down whatever you're carrying

- Custom OP HUD with quick-access buttons for every action

- Fully client-side friendly, works great in survival or roleplay worlds

Installation

- Download the .mcaddon file

- Open it with Minecraft Bedrock installed, it will import automatically

- Create or edit a world and enable the behavior pack and resource pack

- IMPORTANT Make sure "Beta APIs" is turned on in your world settings (Experiments)

Compatibility

Works with the latest Minecraft Bedrock release. May conflict with other addons that modify the HUD or player animations.
May have a little lag.
