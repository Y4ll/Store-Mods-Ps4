Project Description:

Say goodbye to outdated texture packs that make every stone block disappear! This feature delivers genuine, full-fledged Ore X-Ray vision straight to your survival world without messing up your textures or ruining your game's visual style. Hunt for diamonds, netherite, and rare minerals faster than ever while keeping your world looking pristine.

Important Information:

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Vibrant Visuals will need to be activated for some features.

- Includes Glowing Ores.

- Genuine x-ray vision without loss of textures.

- Behaviour & Resource packs are both required for the project to work.

- Have something to say? Join the Discord.

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

Just by holding an Ender Pearl and sneaking, you will be able to see any ore within a massive radius! Uncover hidden veins of Diamond, Ancient Debris, Gold, and Iron instantly through deepslate and stone without modifying any textures or cluttering your visual field.

Never miss a vein again! All ores feature full brightness illumination, helping you spot hidden Diamonds and Netherite instantly from across the darkest caves.
