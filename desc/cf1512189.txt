Night Vision [ON/OFF]

Tired of carrying torches everywhere or wasting potion slots just to see in the dark? This addon gives you a simple, clean night vision toggle, no items, no crafting, no inventory clutter.

How to Use

Simply Crouch + Jump at the same time to toggle Night Vision on or off. That's it. Nothing to craft, nothing to carry.

Features

- Instant toggle Crouch + Jump to switch ON/OFF anytime

- All dimensions Works in the Overworld, Nether and The End

- Death-proof Night Vision is automatically restored after respawn

- Never runs out Quietly renewed in the background, even on long sessions or after drinking milk

- Remembers your choice Turn it off and it stays off, even after you leave and rejoin

- Auto-enabled New players start with Night Vision ON automatically

- Achievement-safe Does NOT disable achievements or trophies

- Multiplayer ready Each player controls their own Night Vision independently

- Clear feedback Sound + on-screen message confirms every toggle

- Lightweight One small script, no items, no entities, no resource pack

Server & Map Maker Control (optional)

Night Vision can also be switched from commands, command blocks and functions:

- /scriptevent nv:on

- /scriptevent nv:off

- /scriptevent nv:toggle

These act on the player who runs them. From a command block, use /execute as @a run scriptevent nv:on to target players.

Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.20+ , 26.10+

- Works on Android, iOS, Windows, Console

- Behavior Pack only no Resource Pack needed

- Compatible with other addons

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Tap/double-click to auto-import into Minecraft

- Apply the pack to your world under Behavior Packs

- Play - Night Vision starts ON automatically!
