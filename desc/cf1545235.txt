Super TNT Addon for Minecraft Education & Bedrock

Take explosions to the next level with the Super TNT Addon! This addon massively upgrades TNT in Minecraft, creating huge explosions, extreme destruction, and chaotic gameplay moments.

Whether you want to destroy entire landscapes, prank your friends, or create explosive YouTube content, Super TNT makes Minecraft far more intense and entertaining.

Perfect for:

- Survival chaos

- TNT experiments

- Challenge worlds

- Multiplayer fun

- Content creators and videos

- Minecraft Education lessons and demonstrations

Features

- Extremely powerful TNT explosions

- Massive destruction effects

- Works in Minecraft Bedrock Edition

- Compatible with Minecraft Education Edition

- Easy .mcaddon installation

- Fun and chaotic gameplay

Installation

- Download the .mcaddon file

- Open the addon with Minecraft

- Activate both the behaviour pack and resource pack

- Enable the addon in your world

- Light the TNT and enjoy the chaos!

Warning

This addon creates very large explosions and may cause lag or crashes on lower-end devices if too many TNT blocks are activated at once.
