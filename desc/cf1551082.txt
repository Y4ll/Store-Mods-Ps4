Chroma Visuals is a lightweight yet stunning shader for Minecraft Pocket Edition / Bedrock Edition designed to give your world a vibrant cinematic feel. From glowing oceans and realistic skies to colorful forests and atmospheric lighting, this shader enhances every biome with smooth and immersive visuals.

Whether you're exploring oceans, cherry groves, savannas, or survival worlds, Chroma Visuals brings your game to life with bright colors, realistic water reflections, soft bloom, and dynamic ambience.

Features

Vibrant and colorful lighting

Crystal-clear realistic water

Soft bloom and cinematic atmosphere

Enhanced sky and cloud visuals

Improved biome colors

Smooth fog effects

Better sunlight and shadows

Performance-friendly optimization

Beautiful underwater visuals

Natural aesthetic for survival gameplay
