Full description:

You can find different breeds of dogs in the woods and tame them with bones.

<figure class="image"></figure>

When a dog is housebroken it will be given a leash that indicates it is housebroken. You can change the color of the leash using a dye.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Your tame dog will protect you from attacking enemies or attacks.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Poop

Your dog will release his poop in a short period of time.

<figure class="image"></figure>

With the poop you will be able to obtain natural charcoal only from the melting furnace.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Dog cookies

You can use the cookies to feed your dogs and restore their health.

<figure class="image"></figure>

You will be able to make special dog cookies that will give you unique abilities:

<figure class="image"></figure>

List of cookies:

- Red cookie: Will give you regeneration I

- Purple Cookie: Will give you strength I

- Orange Cookie: Will grant fire resistance I

- Green Cookie: Will give you slow fall

- Blue cookie: Will grant resistance I and resistance to thrust.

Ball and frisbee

You can play with your dogs by throwing the ball or the frisbee for your dog to go for it.

<figure class="image"></figure>

When your dog picks up the ball or frisbee, interact with it to pick it up and the dog will release experience orbs.
