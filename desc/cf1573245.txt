Pushing villagers into boats, building railroads, fencing them off just to move them a few hundred blocks we've all been there. Leashable Villagers fixes all of that. Slap a lead on any villager and walk them straight to your trading hall. Simple as that.

How to Use

- PC: Hold a Lead and right-click on a villager

- Mobile: Hold a Lead and tap the villager

The villager is now leashed and follows you. Walk them to your base, trading hall, or anywhere you want.

Achievement Friendly?

Yes fully achievement friendly. Behavior-only pack, no experimental features, no achievement lock.

Your villagers. Your rules. Take them anywhere.
