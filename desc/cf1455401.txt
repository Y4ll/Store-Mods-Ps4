magic list:
Energy = the core of all magic, allowing it to be converted into various forms
Fireball = crafted from Blaze Powder, allowing you to shoot fireballs
Lightning = a conversion of Fire Magic, allowing you to shoot fast projectiles
Meteor = a fireball enhanced with Energy Magic, creating powerful and dangerous explosions
Love Magic = applies regeneration to the target
Bedrock Magic = creates an explosion on the target
Darkness Magic = applies the Darkness effect to the target

Magic+ does not require mana or stamina. However, your magic items are limited. As long as you still have magic items, you can cast spells as much as you want.
In other words, your magic supplies act as your mana source.
