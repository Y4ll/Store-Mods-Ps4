Riley came up with the idea for this pack; therefore, I have to give him credit for it as I didn't intend to create a left handed texture pack.

Check him out - 65KRiley - YouTube

Other left hand texture packs change the position to make it look like you're using left hand; however, that's not the case with this one. You actually use your left hand as your main hand and your right hand stays in the same position.

Picture example - Visible Offhand

Picture - Complex Left Hand

Items that go in the off hand which is now right hand show in your right hand and not the left hand.

Blocks are supported for both hands, left hand and right hand

Pack Config will be in the pack settings

If you want to change the position and rotation of the hands, you can to adjust to your liking.
