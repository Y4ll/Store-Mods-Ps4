Welcome to DBE, an add-on for Minecraft Bedrock that brings the Dragon Ball experience to your world!

Face challenges to become stronger, but be prepared: new threats will always emerge. Explore the world in search of legendary masters like Goku, Kami-Sama, and many others. Learn new techniques and decide how to use them for good or evil.

Gather the Dragon Balls and make your wish come true!

Content

- Choose your race and unlock powerful transformations.Train with legendary masters and learn new skills.

- Improve your attributes to become even stronger.

- Explore new places and face epic challenges.

- Decide your path: protect the world or bring about its destruction.
