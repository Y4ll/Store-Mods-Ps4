Main Features:

Works on Everything: Catch vanilla mobs AND custom mobs from other addons!

Achievement & Tame Friendly: Capturing a tamed wolf, cat, or horse won't break their tame status. Your pets are safe!

Custom UI System: No random dropping. We have a clean UI menu so you can choose exactly which mob you want to release.

Multiplayer Compatible: Works flawlessly on Realms and servers with your friends.

How to Use:

Crafting:

To Catch a Mob: Hold the Mob Catcher in your hand, look directly at the mob, and Right-Click (or Long-Press on mobile). Make sure you are NOT crouching! You can even give them a name to mark them.

To Release a Mob: Crouch (Sneak) and use the Mob Catcher. A UI will pop up showing the mobs you've caught. Just click the one you want to release, and they will spawn right in front of you!

Compatibility: This addon does not use player.json, so it is completely compatible with almost any other addon you have installed.
