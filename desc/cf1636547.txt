Better Offhand

Better Offhand adds practical offhand functionality using normal vanilla items, quick hand swapping, and mobile-friendly controls.

Features

- Vanilla Items Only: No custom items or crafting recipes.

- Double Crouch: Quickly move items between your main hand and offhand.

- Offhand Utility: Supports torches, lanterns, blocks, buckets, compasses, clocks, and more.

- Offhand Placement: Place supported blocks and light sources from your offhand.

- Smooth Visibility: Offhand torches and lanterns improve cave visibility without effect flashing.

- Mobile-Friendly: Designed for Android, iOS, and Windows controls.

How to Use

- Hold a normal vanilla item in your main hand.

- Crouch twice quickly to move it into your offhand.

- Double crouch again to move the item back.

- Crouch and press the use button to place or activate supported offhand items.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file.

- Open the file with Minecraft and wait for the import to finish.

- Create a new world or edit an existing world.

- Open Behavior Packs My Packs.

- Select Better Offhand and press Activate.

- Remove older versions of Better Offhand to prevent conflicts.

- Enter the world and double crouch while holding an item.

Enable experimental features only if required by your Minecraft version.

Compatibility

Designed for Minecraft Bedrock 26.x on Windows, Android, and iOS. Some items may have limited functionality because Bedrock does not provide complete Java-style dual wielding.
