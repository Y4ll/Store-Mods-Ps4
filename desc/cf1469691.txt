Available Machines (Version 1.0.0)

At the moment, the following machines support tiers:

- Crusher

- Electro Press

- Incinerator

How It Works

All tiered machines behave exactly like their base versions.
They keep full compatibility with:

- Upgrades

- Item Conduits

- Smart Filters

The difference comes from scaling:

- Increased Processing Slots

- Higher Energy Capacity

Processing Slots by Tier

- Basic 3 slots

- Advanced 5 slots

- Expert 7 slots

- Ultimate 9 slots

Upgrade System

Machines are upgraded in the UtilityCraft Workbench.

Progression starts from the base machine, and each tier requires the corresponding tier materials:

- Basic Chips

- Advanced Chips

- Expert Chips

- Ultimate Chips
