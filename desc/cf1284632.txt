Player grows in size every 30 seconds! This iron is designed for players to make their game even more challenging.

As you grow, you will tower over trees, stomp through forests, and gain incredible giant powers. But being a giant is not all easy, your massive scale will make navigating the world and dealing with enemies a whole new experience!

How It Works

- Constant Growth: Every 30 seconds, all online players grow by 1 level.

- Size Levels: Start at Size Level 1 and grow all the way up to Size Level 50 (MAX)!

- Dynamic HUD: A live action bar HUD displays your current size level and counts down the seconds until the next growth (e.g., GROWTH LEVEL: 1 | Next: 30s).

- Persistent Scaling: Your growth level is saved automatically, so leaving the world or dying won't reset your progress. You will spawn right back at your current size level!

Giant Abilities & Status Effects

As your size increases, you unlock powerful positive status effects to help you survive (and crush everything in your path):

- Speed & Jump Boost: Starts at Level 2, scaling up to Speed IV and Jump Boost IV at max level, allowing you to run faster and leap over obstacles.

- Extra Hearts (Max Health): Starts at Level 2, granting additional max health hearts as you grow, reaching up to +50 extra hearts (+100 HP) at max level!

- Resistance: Starts at Level 10, scaling up to Resistance IV to protect you from heavy damage.

- Strength: Starts at Level 15, scaling up to Strength IV to let you deal massive damage to foes.

- Haste: Starts at Level 20, scaling up to Haste IV to speed up your mining.
