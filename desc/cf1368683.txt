A fully functional add-on that allows you to create and manage waypoints in your Minecraft world, with the ability to teleport to them at any time!

SHOWCASE

FUNCTIONALITY

To use the addon...

Just open the menu you want! You can do the following with each one!

Waypoints Manager

- Use it to create, edit, delete, and teleport to your waypoints.

- Create waypoints with custom names.

- Save the exact coordinates of your current location.

- Instantly teleport to any of your saved waypoints (only in the same dimension!).

- Edit your waypoints' names or update their coordinates.

- Delete waypoints you no longer need.

Vanilla Waypoints Configs Menu

- Access all addon configurations and info.

- Adjust the maximum limit of waypoints you can create.

- Toggle visual particles on/off.

- Toggle sound effects on/off.

- Server mode: restrict configuration access to operators only.

- Operator permission system for multiplayer servers.

FEATURES

- Create custom waypoints - Save important locations with unique names.

- Teleportation - Travel to your saved waypoints easy.

- Waypoint editing - Change names or update coordinates whenever you want.

- Complete management - Delete waypoints you no longer need.

- Intuitive interface - Easy-to-use form menus.

- Slot system - Control how many waypoints you can create (configurable).

- Multi-dimension - Waypoints work in Overworld, Nether, and The End.

- Server mode - Permission system for administrators in multiplayer.

- Customization - Enable/disable sounds and particles according to your preference.

- Lag-free - Optimized to not affect game performance.

INSTALLATION PROCESS & SUPPORT

Correct installation process:

- Download both files, the behavior pack [BP] and the resource pack [RP] (If you only download one of the two .mcpack files, the add-on may not work properly).

- Import both files into Minecraft Bedrock. One at a time.

- Activate the add-on in an old or new world. When you activate one of the two, the other will automatically activate. For example: if you activate [RP] -> [BP] will automatically activate.

- Enjoy :)
