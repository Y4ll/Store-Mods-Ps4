Nicos Magic Spells adds scroll-based spellcasting, new magical villagers, structures, and over 70 custom scrolls to Minecraft. Craft powerful spells, trade rare magical items, and explore a world filled with mystical lore and enchanted discoveries!

(v1.3.4)

NEW UPDATES:

Version: 1.3.4

Version: 1.3.4

NOTE:

- This update only works with Minecraft version 26.30 and higher.

ITEMS:

- Added new Magic Scrolls:

- Scroll of Earthquake (IIII): Slows and shakes the vision on all nearby mobs within a 4-block radius upon impact.

- Scroll of Lightning Bolt (IIII): Summons lightning strikes on all nearby mobs within a 4-block radius upon impact.

- Summoning Scroll of Bogged: Summons a Bogged upon impact.

- Summoning Scroll of Magma Cube: Summons a Magma Cube upon impact.

- Summoning Scroll of Parched: Summons a Parched upon impact.

- Summoning Scroll of Stray: Summons a Stray upon impact.

- Summoning Scroll of Sulfur Cube: Summons a Sulfur Cube upon impact.

- Fixed the Scroll of Hoarding bug, where it also teleports nearby fish mobs.

- Improved the Scroll of Flourishing, the effects now also work underwater, allowing it to grow underwater plants and corals.

TECHNICAL CHANGES:

- Updated several files to 26.30 version.

Version: 1.3.2

Version: 1.3.2

NOTE:

- This version may no longer be able to update over older versions.

- This update only works with Minecraft version 26.10 and higher.

TECHNICAL CHANGES:

- Changed the UUID to fix importing issues on Realms. (Note: This will prevent the add-on from updating over older versions.)

- Added the PBR or Vibrant Visuals capabilities.

STRUCTURES:

- Improved structure generation. Structures are now generated using jigsaw structures.

- Structures now generate more naturally, blending better with the terrain and no longer appearing buried.

- Decorated Pots in structures may now contain different types of items.

- Structures can now be located using the /locate command in Creative Mode with their identifier names:

- ntk:badlands_scrollwright_house

- ntk:desert_villager_library

- ntk:plains_scrollwright_house

- ntk:plains_villager_library

- ntk:savanna_villager_library

- ntk:swamp_scrollwright_house

- ntk:taiga_scrollwright_house

- ntk:taiga_villager_library

RECIPES:

- Reworked magic scroll recipes. They now require fewer items and more common materials, making them easier to craft.

- Scroll of Acceleration no longer requires a Cookie or Lapis Lazuli.

- Scroll of Berserk no longer requires a Diamond and now uses an Iron Ingot instead of an Iron Nugget.

- Scrolls of Cleansing II and III now use an Empty Scroll instead of a Scroll of Cleansing.

- Scroll of Converging now uses a Honeycomb instead of a Honey Block.

- Scroll of Displacement now uses a Slime Ball instead of a Slime Block.

- Scroll of Field Levitation no longer requires a Wind Charge.

- Scroll of Fire Blast now uses an Empty Scroll instead of a Scroll of Flame Burst, and now requires a Fire Charge.

- Scroll of Fire Protection now uses an Iron Nugget instead of Blaze Powder.

- Scroll of Flame Burst now uses a Wind Charge instead of Gunpowder.

- Scrolls of Flourishing II and III now use an Empty Scroll instead of a Scroll of Flourishing.

- Scroll of Hoarding no longer requires a Gold Nugget.

- Scroll of Invisibility now uses a Fermented Spider Eye and no longer requires Nether Quartz or Lapis Lazuli.

- Scroll of Lethargy now uses a Spider
