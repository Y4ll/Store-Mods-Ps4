What if you fell through reality... and landed somewhere you were never meant to be?

This addon recreates the eerie and endless world of The Backrooms inside Minecraft Bedrock Edition but without jumpscares. Instead, the horror is ambient, psychological, and immersive.

How to Enter:
Noclip is triggered when the player gets buried inside falling blocks like gravel or sand. Once clipped through, reality collapses... and the backrooms begin.

How to Escape:
You must explore and survive until you either:

-

Reach the final level

-

Escape through Level Run

Features:

-

Multiple handcrafted levels based on backrooms lore

-

Unique, mysterious entities that behave differently per level

-

Functional blocks and level-based structure generation

-

An atmosphere of liminality and psychological isolation

-

Light survival mechanics but focus is exploration and immersion

There are no cheap jumpscares. The experience leans into eerie quietness, surreal architecture, and unsettling logic.

Supports multiple languages including English, Japanese, Spanish, Korean, Russian, and more.
