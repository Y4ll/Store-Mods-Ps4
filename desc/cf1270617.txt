This is the latest update of World Animals released in 2024 works for the most updated versions of Minecraft Bedrock, a new and improved version of this add-on is being created.

How does it work?

All animals can be found in our Minecraft worlds according to their respective biome, some are tamable, others are aggressive and not capable of taming.

Elephants (Asian and African):

We can find them in savannah and jungle biomes, they feed on sugar cubes, we can tame them using the golden bone, at the moment of taming we can equip them with an elephant saddle so that we can ride them, handle them and store our things as if they were a chest.

Features:

- Health: 70

- Damage: 7

Big Cats:

There are only seven different types of big cats, we can find them in our Minecraft worlds according to their respective biome and can feed them any kind of meat.

We can tame them using the golden bone, when we have a big cat tamed we can equip it with a mount for big cats so we can ride them and we can store our things as if it was a chest.
