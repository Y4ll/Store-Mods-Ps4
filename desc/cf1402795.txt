What is The Companions?

The Companions adds unique companions that can accompany you throughout your adventures. Each companion has its own personality, special abilities, exclusive items, and enhanced forms through Ambrosia.

Some specialize in combat, others focus on support, while some offer completely unique abilities.

As the addon continues to grow, new companions, items, mechanics, and visitors from other realities will be introduced.

Official artwork by Genki

This addon does NOT disable achievements.

Conelli Your First Companion

Conelli is much more than just a companion. She is a loyal friend who will accompany you from the very beginning of your adventure and do everything she can to keep you safe.

Unlike most companions, Conelli prefers helping over fighting. She will stay close to her owner, provide healing whenever needed, and avoid dangerous situations whenever possible.

More Information:

Where to find Conelli

Conelli can only be found inside her house, a small structure that generates exclusively in Birch Forests.

Inside her home, you can find:

- Carrots and Golden Carrots

- The Carrot Hammer

- And Conelli waiting for you

How to tame Conelli

Conelli can only be tamed using a Golden Carrot.

How to Heal Conelli

- Golden Carrots

- Can also be fully healed instantly using a Companion Potion.

Special Ability

When her owner is injured, Conelli will automatically provide healing support.

Her ability to keep players alive makes her one of the most reliable companions for exploration and survival.

Health

- Normal: 35 Hearts

- Enhanced: 70 Hearts

Can follow you or sit down

Can sleep in beds like a player

Enhanced Conelli Evolution with Ambrosia

After consuming Ambrosia, Conelli becomes much more resilient and her healing abilities improve significantly, allowing her to support her owner even during the most dangerous adventures.

Carrot Hammer Exclusive Item

A unique weapon that can be found inside Conelli's house.

- Damage: 5 Hearts

- Has a chance to briefly immobilize enemies

- Compatible with sword enchantments

- Can be repaired using carrots

Gregory Your Second Companion

Gregory is a fierce wolf that inhabits the depths of the Mega Taiga.

His instincts are entirely focused on combat and protection. Once tamed, he will fearlessly attack any threat that stands between him and his owner.

During the day, he usually remains calm and watchful, but when night falls, his wild side becomes impossible to ignore.

More Information:

Where to find Gregory

Gregory can only be found inside a cave that generates exclusively in Mega Taiga biomes.

Inside his den, you can find:

- The Wolfsbane Sword

- Ingredients for Rabbit Stew

- Bones and Rotten Flesh

- And Gregory waiting in the darknes

Wild Gregory

Neutral during the day

At night he becomes aggressive and will attack any nearby creature, including players

How to tame Gregory

Gregory can only be tamed using a rabbit stew.

How to Heal Gregory

- Any food that can normally be used to heal wolves.

- Can also be fully healed instantly using a Companion Potion.

Special Ability

Gregory specializes in hunting enemies and protecting his owner.
