Hello it's me again :)

I made this weapons pack as a result of my continued hyper fixation with turning Minecraft bedrock into a somewhat realistic zombie survival scenario,a major aspect of this is the visuals. I hereby present to you, yes you, a weapons pack that doesn't adds over the top items and still true to the Minecraft 2d style melee weapons (3D versions of the weapons are also included in a separate pack).

The weapons added are just over 8 in number ranging from iron pipes to fire axes, rocks and so on, the variety of the weapons was my main focus and not just the quantity. I wanted to make the weapons feel very different and close to their realistic inspirations as much as possible some of them even having various special effects (buffs and debuffs). A different category of items added other than the melee weapons are the healing items which are only two in number alongside some few new recipes for items like leather, gunpowder e.t.c, this is done in moderation and only to facilitate a better experience while using this add-on.

So hope you enjoy using this add-on in anyone of your zombie/apocalypse themed worlds/servers it could really help prevent zombies from chomping on your ankles.

it is compatible with all other add-ons btw, you can use this in any server or video. Please consider sharing the original link and not using a third party app to share it,bye .
