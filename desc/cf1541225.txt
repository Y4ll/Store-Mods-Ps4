Ultimate TNT Expansion

Ultimate TNT Expansion is a powerful and feature-rich mod that completely enhances the explosive experience in Minecraft. This mod introduces a wide variety of custom TNT types, each with unique abilities, effects, and mechanics designed to transform gameplay, whether for survival, creative building, or large-scale destruction.

Features

Multiple New TNT Variants

Discover a collection of highly customized TNT blocks, each with distinct explosion behaviors and visual effects.

Advanced Explosion Mechanics

Experience explosions beyond vanilla limits, including massive blasts, controlled detonations, chain reactions, and special effects.

Unique Abilities

Some TNT types may include:

Area mining explosions

Entity-targeting blasts

Environmental transformations

Elemental effects (fire, lightning, etc.)

Optimized Performance

Designed to handle powerful explosions while maintaining stable gameplay performance.

Survival & Creative Friendly

All TNT types can be balanced for survival progression or freely used in creative mode for testing and fun.

Compatibility

Compatible with the latest versions of Minecraft (check file details for supported versions)

Works alongside most mods (unless they directly modify explosion mechanics)

Installation

Download the mod file

Install a compatible mod loader (Forge / Fabric)

Place the file in your mods folder

Launch Minecraft and enjoy!

Why Use This Mod?

If youve ever felt that vanilla TNT is too limited, this mod takes explosives to the next level. Whether you want massive destruction, efficient mining, or just chaotic fun, Ultimate TNT Expansion delivers a completely new explosive experience.

Note

Due to the high power of some TNT types, use with cautionespecially in survival worlds or multiplayer servers
