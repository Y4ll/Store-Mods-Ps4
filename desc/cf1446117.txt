Better Vein Miner and Tree Cutter is a Minecraft Bedrock addon that makes mining and wood-cutting faster. Just sneak and break a block to automatically mine the connected ore vein or cut down the entire tree. Its lightweight, easy to use, and works smoothly in survival worlds.

Important Note

This pack will not appear in the main Settings Packs menu. To activate it, edit or create a world, go to the Behavior Packs section, and enable the addon there.

How To Use

Crouch (Sneak)
Break an ore, a log, or a block of clay, mud, snow or soul sand
The addon will automatically mine the connected vein or cut the full tree.
No commands, nothing to set up, just sneak and break!

What It Mines

- Ores - coal, copper, iron, gold, lapis, redstone, diamond, emerald, nether gold, nether quartz and ancient debris, including deepslate versions

- Trees - all log types, including cherry, pale oak, mangrove with its roots, and crimson and warped stems

- Shovel blocks - clay, mud, snow blocks, soul sand and soul soil

Dirt, sand and gravel are left out on purpose, so one break can't strip away a whole beach or hillside.

Features

- Mines the whole connected vein in one break, and stone and deepslate versions of the same ore are mined together

- Cuts the full tree, including diagonal branches on acacia, dark oak, jungle and mangrove trees

- Needs the right tool: a pickaxe for ores, an axe for logs, a shovel for clay, mud, snow and soul sand

- Respects pickaxe tiers, so a stone or copper pickaxe won't collect diamonds. Ores your pickaxe can't mine are left in place, not destroyed

- Silk Touch and Fortune work like vanilla

- Gives the same XP vanilla gives for each ore

- Uses tool durability for every extra block, with Unbreaking taken into account

- Shows how many blocks you harvested, with a sound when it finishes

- Capped at 256 ores or 512 logs per break, so it stays fast even on huge veins and trees

- Achievement friendly, no experimental toggles needed
