Features

- Random Loot Every time you open a chest, its contents will be randomly generated.

- 5 Luck Levels Your loot can fall into one of five categories:

- Terrible Your luck is truly suffering.

- Bad Not great, but at least you got something.

- Normal The loot stays roughly as expected.

- OP Your luck is looking pretty good!

- Super OP Congratulations, you've hit the jackpot!

- Structure Chests Only The feature only works with chests naturally generated inside structures.

- Achievement Friendly The addon does not disable Minecraft achievements.
