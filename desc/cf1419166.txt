Eternal Winter - Snowfall in All Biomes

This Addon works perfectly on 26.13 and above (this line is updated regularly)

Transform your Minecraft world into a winter wonderland! Eternal Winter brings beautiful snowfall and winter to every biome in the Overworld.

Features

True Eternal Winter

- Snow falls in ALL biomes - deserts, jungles, plains, everywhere!

- Its not constant just snowfall, it follows a pattern, sometimes it stops, sometimes it snows for 20 minutes, and more. its a fully automated system (customizable if you want)

- Custom snow particles but using vanilla textures, staying true to game's core.

- Natural weather cycle between snowfall and clear skies

- Sky color matches the dull winter style

- All the leaves, vines, grass and more are snowy, along with real accumulation of snow from snowfall

- Special dense fog for snowfall that makes you FEEL it.

- Completely custom enviromental fog bluish haze.

Snow Accumulation

- Snow actually builds up on the ground over time

- Layers stack on top of each other naturally

- Every area is snowy even without accumulation, though keeping this on is recommended because real uneven snow on ground makes it 1000 times better

Customizable:

Everything can be customized through an easy in-game menu like accumulation, its speed and more.

Commands

For Everyone

/snow:status - check current addon status

For Admins:

/snow:settings - Open the configuration menu
/snow:startsnow - Force start snowfall
/snow:stopsnow - Force stop snowfall

How to Use

Basic Usage:

- Install the addon (Behavior Pack + Resource Pack)

- Apply both packs to your world

- Join the world - your world will be a winter land.

Configuring Settings (Admins):

- Type
/snow:settings
to open the menu

Or just keep all settings as it is, there is no need to open the menu at all!

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.130 or higher

This pack doesn't use experiments, so it will probably continue to work as intended even after updates to game.
