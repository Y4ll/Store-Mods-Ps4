Core Mechanic: Structural Gravity
Blocks cannot hover in the air without support. If a block loses its foundation (below) or horizontal connection (side wall), it falls. Falling blocks crush fragile decorations (grass, flowers, moss, carpets) but safely stop on solid grass blocks.
Block Stability (Max Horizontal Reach)
Obsidian 12 blocks
Netherrack 10 blocks
Wood / Planks 5 blocks (Plus, they float in water!)
Stone / Cobblestone 3 blocks
Dirt / Grass Blocks 1 block
Sand / Gravel 0 blocks (Collapses instantly)
