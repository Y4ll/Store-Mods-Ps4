The Skinwalker

Something is hiding among the animals.

At first, everything seems normal. You explore the world, encounter familiar creatures, and continue your journey as usual.

But sometimes...

One of them isn't what it seems.

The Skinwalker is an intelligent and dangerous creature capable of disguising itself as animals, stalking you from the shadows and waiting for the perfect moment to reveal its true form.

---

Features

- Disguises itself as different animals.

- Advanced and unpredictable AI inspired by the behavior of the Goatman.

- Can stalk you through forests and wilderness.

- Reacts to your presence and actions.

- More aggressive and dangerous than ordinary creatures.

- Can follow you without immediately attacking.

- May suddenly turn a peaceful encounter into a terrifying chase.

- Unpredictable encounters designed to keep you questioning what you see.

---

How It Works

The Skinwalker doesn't simply spawn and attack.

It watches.

It follows.

It waits.

You might see an animal standing strangely far away from the others.

Maybe it disappears when you look away.

Maybe it starts following you.

Or maybe...

You were never looking at an animal in the first place.

The Skinwalker's behavior is unpredictable, making every encounter different. Sometimes it may observe you from a distance, while other encounters can quickly become much more dangerous.

And once it decides to reveal itself...

It may already be too late.

---

Trust Nothing

The most dangerous part isn't knowing that the Skinwalker exists.

It's not knowing which animal it is.

A harmless-looking creature could simply wander away.

Or it could be watching you.

Waiting.

Following.

Learning.
