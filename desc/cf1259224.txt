Alylica's Advancements is a faithful port of the Java Advancements System, including 116 of the 120 advancements found on Java for players to earn!

As a bonus, combine this addon with some of my other addons to get even more advancements themed around those addons!

How do Advancements work?

Advancements are tasks that you can complete, once an advancement is earned there will be a notification in the chat and on the top of your screen

Some Advancements are known as Challenges which not only have a unique sound for completetion, but also award some exp!

How do I View Them?

By using the /showadvancements command, you will see a helpful menu that lists every advancement and their requirements

operators can use /showplayeradvancements to view the advancements of another player

Helpful Commands

/advancement - Allows operators to give and revoke advancements from themselves or others

/toasts - Allows players to toggle wether or not they see notifications in the top right for earning an advancement
