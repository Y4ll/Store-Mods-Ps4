What is Ghoulcraft Garden BE?

Ghoulcraft Garden is originally made as a optifine texture pack that uses CIT in java. It was made by Missghoul and ported by Kao for bedrock (fix by Gooblicraft to work in latest version in bedrock). This addon contains over 140+ blocks to decorate our world aesthetically beautiful. It has awnings, apiaries, chicken coops, plants, crops, tools to plant, lattices, pots, seed bags, shutter and many more. All of it are blocks and some "can be rotate with a minecraft stick". Some also can be put in an item frame just like a CIT in java to rotate large blocks. It also supports survival and low end devices, this version improves the experience and performance of the pack to seamlessly load and work in your world.

1.) Supports Low end devices and Survival
2.) 140+ blocks to decorate your world
3.) Rotate-able and change able
4.) Supports 1.26+ minecraft bedrock versions
5.) Can be played in realms

I use also Mizuno's 16 CIT 2026 addon.

Ghoulcraft BE only works on Windows 10, Android, & Apple Devices Running Minecraft Bedrock/Pocket Edition 1.26.0+ and above (Advisable to use 1.26.0+). I dont know if it works on consoles or playstation!

Importing

- Download the pack from any of this website PMC, MCPEDL, or Curseforge.

- Click on the file and open it to Minecraft.

- Create a New World.

- In the Game Tab, turn on the following: Creative.

- Go to Resource Packs Tab and enable the pack there.

- Go to Behavior Packs Tab and make sure the pack`s behavior is also enabled.

- Make sure the pack is on the very top of the load order.

- Play on the world and do a test by going to creative menu and look for items and ghoulcraft base on spawn eggs section.

- If you got the item or it shows, congrats! You just installed the pack into your game and it's working fine.
