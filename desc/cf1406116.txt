Newb x Balance is a Java-inspired shader for Minecraft Bedrock, focused on warm lighting, cinematic skies, and dimension-specific visuals, while keeping smooth performance on low to mid-range devices.

Features

Java-style lighting with warm highlights and cool shadows.

Fully animated 3D auroras with stronger color shaping.

Glowing ores.

Added subpacks (low-high) with different cloud styles and performance levels.

New sky colors gradients for day, night and sunset.

Dimension-specific lighting (Overworld, Nether, End).

Improved water with clean reflections.

Custom torch colors and ambient lighting.

Performance-friendly, suitable for low to mid-range devices.
