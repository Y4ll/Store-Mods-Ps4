PureTone Visuals is a Vibrant Visuals shader pack built for Minecraft Bedrock's new rendering pipeline. It delivers cinematic atmospheric lighting, violet-to-magenta dreamy skies, ACES-graded color tuning, warm golden sunlight, an animated violet aurora sky phenomenon, and a full PBR material overhaul, all while staying true to vanilla's clean aesthetic.

Reimagined Visual Style

- Reworked color grading with punched-up contrast and saturation across highlights, midtones, and shadows

- Balanced between cinematic vibrancy and vanilla-friendly aesthetics

- PBR-reflective water with metalness/roughness/emissive maps for realistic surface behavior

- Two full atmosphere identities built in golden-violet default and a softer pastel-pink alternate

Advanced Atmospherics System

- Custom sky color transitions with 20+ keyframes spanning the full day-night cycle

- Tuned Rayleigh and Mie scattering for realistic sky depth, sun glow, and horizon blending

- Rich violet-to-magenta sunrise and sunset gradients

- Full atmosphere coverage across every vanilla biome

Violet Aurora at Night

- A rare, animated aurora glimmers across the night sky, adding a signature atmospheric touch

- Additive-blended light beams create a soft, glowing violet ribbon effect rather than a flat texture

- Beams drift and pulse in width and height over their lifetime for a natural, shifting curtain-light look

- Spans wide across the sky above the player, sweeping and arcing rather than sitting in one fixed spot

Lighting

- Warm golden directional sunlight with soft 1024-texel shadows

- Alternate rose-toned "pink lighting" mode with violet moonlight for a completely different mood

- Over 50 individually recolored local light sources torches, lanterns, candles, ores, portals, froglights, and more

- Smooth sun illuminance ramp from full daylight to night

Color Grading & Fog

- ACES tone mapping with a 6200K white balance for a warm, natural look

- Split highlight/midtone/shadow color grading for cinematic contrast

- Two volumetric fog profiles cool blue-grey default and rose/magenta alternate

- Custom Henyey-Greenstein scattering for natural light diffusion in air, water, and weather

Extras

- Global PBR fallback gives every block, item, actor, and particle a baseline reflective sheen

- Custom biome colormaps for grass, foliage, birch, evergreen, swamp, and mangrove tinting

Requirements
PureTone Visuals requires Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.80+ with Vibrant Visuals / PBR rendering enabled.

Installation

- Download the .zip file

- Open to import into Minecraft

- Enable PureTone Visuals in Global Resources

- Turn on Vibrant Visuals in Video Settings
