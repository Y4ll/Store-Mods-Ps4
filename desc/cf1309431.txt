Ores Plus is a Minecraft Bedrock addon that expands your world with new ores, tools, weapons, armors and food, while keeping a vanilla-friendly style that blends perfectly with the base game.

This addon has been updated to the new Minecraft format; it also doesn't use JSON, so it's compatible with the rest of the addons.

Steel armory

Alexandrite armory

Alexandrite can be obtained when killing the ender dragon, this mineral is used to create alexandrite equipment.

Elemental gems and essences

Elemental Essences:

Elemental essences are used to craft elemental gems.

How to get them?:

Amber, cryo, jade, spinel and wither essences can be crafted using the following recipes, requires crafting table:

Infernal, Oceanic, Desertic, End essences & Angelic essences can be obtained when killing; Blazes(5% prob), Elderguardians(100% prob), Guardians(5% prob), Husks(5% prob), Endermans(5% prob), Shulkers(10% prob)& Phantoms(5% prob). Broken gems that also required for the elemetal gems craft can be when killing the same mobs:

Wither: 100% chance

Ender dragon : 100% chance

Elder guaridan : 100% chance

Elemental Armors

They are new armor sets, each with special effects.

To craft any set of elemental armor, you need 1 elemental gem with required type and 1 white diamond armor set.
-The white diamond is found rarely only between layers -30 to -54

All elemental armors are better than diamond so they will have the following statistics;

Helmet : 4 protection, 814 durability

Chestplate : 9 protection, 1184 durability

Leggings : 7 protection, 1110 durability

Boots : 4 protection, 962 durability

Elemental Swords

There are 4 types of elemental swords.

Cryo Sword

Cryo sword have the following characteristics:

- Damage: + 9 Attack Damage

- Power: While hitting the enemy, the "Slowness IV" effect is applied to him for 8 second

- Durability: 2031

- Enchantable: 10 levels

Infernal Sword

Infernal sword have the following characteristics:

- Damage: +9 Attack Damage

- Power: Hitting igniting an enemie

- Durability: 2031

- Enchantable: 10 levels

Wither Sword

Wither sword have the following characteristics:

-The fragments to craft the essence are obtained by killing the wither, it drops 2

- Damage: +9 Attack Damage

- Power: Inflicts the "Wither IV" effect on the enemy by hit
