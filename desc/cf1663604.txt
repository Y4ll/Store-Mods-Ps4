Ever wondered what would happen if Dirt and Grass Blocks actually had some value? OP DIRT DROPS turns ordinary Dirt and Grass into a source of crazy loot.

Break blocks and you can get anything from useful ores and resources to Diamond Gear, Netherite Gear, Enchanted Books, Obsidian, TNT, Anvils, XP Bottles, End Crystals, Totems, Golden Apples and much more.

The loot is randomized, so you never know what you're going to get from the next block. Rare items have lower chances, making those lucky drops feel much more rewarding.

No complicated setup. Just activate the addon, enter your world, break some Dirt or Grass, and see what you get.
