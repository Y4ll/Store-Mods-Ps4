OP Toolsmith Trade by BAKU

Upgrade your Minecraft experience with OP Toolsmith Trade! This pack gives the Toolsmith villager overpowered trades, making it easier to obtain powerful tools and valuable resources using simple emerald trades. Perfect for Survival, Hardcore, PvP, and Creative worlds.

Features

- Overpowered Toolsmith trades

- Easy access to valuable items

- Faster survival progression

- Works with Minecraft Bedrock Edition

- Lightweight and easy to use

Installation

- Download the pack.

- Open the .mcpack file.

- Import it into Minecraft.
