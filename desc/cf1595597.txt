What is BlockBuste Studio?

A directors toolkit for Bedrock. You record actors (mobs or custom skins), build tracks, layer keyframes, attach cameras, and play everything back in sync including blocks you place or break. Scenes can start from a walk-in zone or a held item. Multiplayer-friendly recording keeps each players work from stomping on everyone elses blocks.

- Multi-track recording & playback several actors, one timeline feel

- Keyframe scrubber animation, particles, sounds, commands, scale, transform

- Scene Builder group tracks, delays, concurrent scenes, shared-track rules

- Vegapunk AI errors, tutorials, and tips in chat (/bb ai)

- 20 player-layout skins + no-brain cinema puppets (bbnb:*)

- Cinematic cameras attach presets, free cams, smoother FOV moves

How to start

- Enable both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack on your world.

- In-game run /bb give for the studio kit, or open the Studio Controller item.

- Follow Vegapunks tutorial: /bb tutorial

- Record a track play it back open the Keyframe Scrubber while paused to dress the take.

- Need help? /bb ai help /bb help

Who is it for?

- Map makers and adventure creators who need controlled performances

- Content creators staging fights, cutscenes, and cinematic camera moves

- Anyone who wants multi-actor playback without fighting vanilla AI every take
