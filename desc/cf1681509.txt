Step into the darkness. The Warden Dimension is a massive underground world hidden beneath the Deep Dark filled with glowing Warden Trees, ancient ruins, new mobs, unique gear and a terrifying boss waiting to be awakened.

Important

This addon requires Beta APIs to be enabled in your world settings. Go to World Settings > Experiments and turn on Beta APIs. Nothing in this addon will work without it.

How to Enter

Find the Deep Dark biome and look for a sculk stone gateway. Right click the frame with an Echo Shard or Flient & Steel and walk through. You can also build your own frame using Sculk Stone if you cannot find one naturally.

How to Get Out

Use the portal inside the dimension to return home. Or dig through the world floor it is deepslate down there, not bedrock and fall back out into your own sky.

What Is Down There

- A vast roofed cavern filled with glowing Warden Trees

- Ruins with loot chests waiting to be found

- Echo Ore a new ore unique to this dimension

- Sculk animals and Sculk Villagers living in the dark

- Glowing lamps scattered across the cavern floor

Echo Gear

Diamond level protection and a unique perk mobs in this dimension will ignore you while you wear the full Echo set.

Warden Gear

- Sonic Beam Sword fires a sonic beam at enemies

- Warden Armour detailed 3D armour set

- Wearing the full set unlocks powerful bonus effects

Warden Heart

The Warden Heart is obtained only through crafting or found inside loot chests in the dimension.

How to Summon the Boss

- Craft the Heart of the Warden

- Find a boss arena inside the dimension

- Right click the altar to charge it

- Right click again and the boss climbs out
