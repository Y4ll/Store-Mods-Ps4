YGP! Minecraft But Trees Give OP Items

Break a tree. Roll the reward. Survive the chaos.

YGP! Minecraft But Trees Give OP Items is a Minecraft Bedrock addon where breaking trees can trigger random rewards, powerful YGP equipment, End loot, OP items, enemies, allies, traps, teleport events, Tree Guardians, special weather events, and the YGP Immortal Dog.

You never know what will happen when you break the next tree.

Created by YGP Studio / YGP Minecraft.

TREE GUARDIAN

A rare event can summon the Tree Guardian, turning an ordinary tree into a dangerous encounter.

Stay ready.

Your next tree might fight back.

YGP IMMORTAL DOG

The previous Loyal Wolf system has been replaced by the YGP Immortal Dog.

When the tree event occurs, the Immortal Dog is automatically associated with the player as their companion.

No separate taming is required for the integrated companion.

The system uses player-specific ownership for multiplayer worlds.

SURVIVAL REWARDS
