Have you ever wanted Dynamic Lighting like in Javas Optifine? Then this add-on is perfect for you! It makes every light source (torches, lanterns...) shine when you hold it. Now exploring caves is much more realistic and you will no longer have to constantly place torches.

NEW: With Jump and Crouch you can quickly move the torch to your Second Hand.

EXAMPLE VIDEOS:

HOW TO MOVE THE TORCH TO THE OFFHAND (JUMP and SNEAK):

You can move all types of Torches and Lanterns from your Main Hand to your Second Hand by clicking Jump and Sneak at the same time.
