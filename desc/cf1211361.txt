Are you looking for a new player animation? well I got you, this pack add simple custom animation, smoothing out vanilla animation and cape are still working. Don't expect too much in this pack, this is mainly for smoothing vanilla animation only.

Basic Information

Fay's Player Animation Specifications

- Player:

- Walk.

- Idle.

- Footprints.

- Jump.

- Flying.

- Sprint.

- Sprint leaning left, right.

- Sprint particles.

- Sprint while holding any sword, bow and trident.

- Sneaking.

- Sneaking while holding cooked food type.

- Sneaking while holding diamond.

- Bow while aiming.

- Bow while aiming + Sneak.

- Sneaking underwater.

- Swimming.

- Swimming idle.

- Bubble particle underwater.

- Sleep.

- Elytra gliding.

- Gliding Particle while using Elytra.

- Holding zombie arm.

- Dressing room.

- Fishing.

- Holding [Torches, Lanterns. Carrots on a stick, Warped fungus on a stick].

- Critical damage particles.

- Supporting attachable.

- On Screen Animation.

- Eat and Drink.

- Mount.

- Death.

- Spawn.

- Cape.

- Armory Equip.
