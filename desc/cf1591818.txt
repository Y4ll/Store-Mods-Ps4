OP Loot M

OP Loot Mobs Addon Tired of getting nothing but rotten flesh from zombies or string from spiders? Its time to break the game! The OP Loot Mobs Addon completely overhauls the drop system in your Minecraft Bedrock world. Every creature you defeat has a random chance of dropping OP (overpowered) items, giving your survival experience a chaotic, fun, and highly rewarding twist!

Features:

Random OP Drops: Mobs can drop anything from full sets of enchanted Netherite armor, golden apples, and potions to diamond blocks and valuable resources.

Ideal for Survival: Perfect for chaotic challenges, speedruns, or just having fun with friends. Compatible with Multiplayer and Realms.
