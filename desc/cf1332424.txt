Arcane Magic Wand

Channel elements and combine them to cast powerful combos or use direct spells to crush your enemies and help you in your adventure.

How it works ?

- Use your wand to channel magic and access spells and elements.

- Cast instantly your direct spells and use them as many time as you want in your slots 2,4,6,8

- Cast your elements once to launch their basic version

- Use several elements in a while to combine them and cast a combo

- Use the reset item to exit channeling and come back to your normal hotbar

Direct Spells

- Explosion : launch an explosion with a radius of 10 blocks

- Teleportation : teleport yourself to the targeted block with a 128 blocks maximum range

- Chain of lightning : strike the targeted entity with a lightning, then strikes two other mobs

- Ice Wall : create an ice wall in front of you that stays in place for 10 seconds

Elemental Basic Spells

- Fire : cast an explosion with a radius of 1.5 blocks that sets mobs on fire

- Water : cast a water jet in front of you that slows down and damages mobs

- Earth : hit the targeted entity with a with a large, sharp stone stone that inflicts 8 points of damage

- Air : cast a shockwave from the targeted block that pushes everything (including you) back and deals damage to mobs

Combos

- Fire + Fire : cast an explosion with a radius of 6 blocks that sets mobs on fire

- Water + Water : cast a circle of water around you that heals you and other entities within a radius of 8 blocks

- Earth + Earth : give you a level 3 resistance effect for the first 2 seconds, then a level 2 effect for 8 seconds

- Air + Air : casts a shockwave 5 blocks wide and 30 blocks long that pushes back mobs and deals damage to them

- Water + Earth : generate a shield with a 50 damage points capacity

- Air + Fire : cast a fire tornado that blows mobs away and sets them on fire

- Air + Water : cast a thunder storm that strikes mobs within a radius of 10 blocks

- Air + Fire : cast a steam cloud to blind and slow your enemies while speeding you up

- Earth + Fire : cast a meteor that inflicts 22 damage on impact and 5 damage around it

- Air + Earth : cast 5 shards of earth that strike the target in turn, inflicting 8 damage
