Possible questions users might ask:

Q1: Why when using custom texture packs with a custom hotbar, It stays the same as default?

: The image file in the pack called "gui.png" has the pause/menu, chat, emote, and interact button which are seen at the top middle of your screen. So having this pack above your custom packs with gui files overwrites it. You can whatsover delete/rename that file to have your pack work, but doing that also removes those textures.

Q2: Why is the potion effects menu glitched/bugged?

: This has always been a problem way back from old versions too, again the "gui.png" file somewhat overwrites these textures and a solution for that problem is putting the Minimalist Button Pack below your pack or having another texture pack to fix that issue.

Recommendations:

Setting the buttons and joystick opacity to 15% makes them unobstructive and helps u better see in game.
