DUAL WIELDING

All weapons from this Add-On can now be used in the Off-Hand, which in turn makes it possible to fire two of them at the same time.

HOW TO USE

To use a gun in your offhand, you simply need to grab it in your inventory and put in the second hand slot, where you would put a shield.

To actually fire the gun all you need to do is interact like you would normally (right click/left trigger/holding your screen) in order to make it shoot. As simple as that

You use any two guns you want, you are not limited whatsover in this area. Feel free to try combinations at your will.

ACTUAL DAMAGE SYSTEM

You might think, "but wait, Minecraft has a system that makes hurt mobs invincible for a couple of ticks, isn't this kind of pointless?" Well thanks to the brand new ACTUAL DAMAGE SYSTEM that is no longer a concern!

The ADS (Actual Damage System) allows for you to bombard an enemy with bullets as fast as you want and have them take all of the damage. This means if you use two guns at the same time, you will effectivly deal double damage!
