Cuarentine Parasites

A lightweight parasite survival add-on inspired by Scape and Run: Parasites.

Currently in Beta

Cuarentine Parasites is designed for players who want a parasite survival experience on low-end devices. While it contains fewer features than The Dark Infection, it focuses on smooth performance without sacrificing the core gameplay of an evolving parasite outbreak.

The infection spreads across the world as contaminated creatures appear, stronger parasite variants emerge, and the outbreak gradually becomes more dangerous.

Optimized for Performance

Unlike my larger parasite projects, Cuarentine Parasites prioritizes:

- Better performance on low-end phones and tablets

- Lower memory usage

- Simplified systems

- Stable Survival Mode gameplay

This makes it ideal for players who want an infection experience without heavy lag.

Survival Progression

The parasite evolves naturally while you survive.

As the outbreak grows:

- New parasite variants appear.

- The infection becomes more aggressive.

- Stronger contaminated creatures begin roaming the world.

- Players must adapt or be overwhelmed.

Phase System

The add-on includes a progression system that controls parasite evolution.

As parasites spawn, the outbreak becomes stronger.

Defeating parasites slows their evolution, allowing players to temporarily delay the next phase.

Infection System

The parasite is capable of infecting living hosts.

When players die to parasites, they can become infected and transform into contaminated forms.

The infection system also includes:

- Parasite Organs

- Parasite Reborn

- Random parasite transformations

- Integration with the phase progression system
