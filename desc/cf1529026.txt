Aqueous Depths Introduces several new features to the Oceans, including new biomes, structures and even a whole new boss!

BIOMES:

Drenched Void: the Drenched Void is a biome that has very dark and foggy waters, the biome itself can extend all the way to the bottom of the world

Thermal Seas: the Thermal Seas are a yellow colored ocean with magma, boiling bubbles and floating islands, it has a bunch of fire themed mobs in the water

Glowing Ocean:
Glowing Oceans feature Sea Glowstone, Glowing Cod and Glow Squids!

Dread Reef: the Dread Reef is a pale version of the coral reef with pale fish and an exclusive hostile mob:

Island Ocean: Island Ocean consists of several islands and spikes

Iceberg Ocean: the Iceberg ocean is a variant of the Frozen Ocean featuring tall icebergs and spikes

MOBS:

Mobs in the pack have a food chain system, if a mob is classed as a predator, pray or neutral, theyll behave differently.

Prey: Mostly passive mobs (all fish and squids are classified as pray)
Neutral: will attack when provoked or attack certain mobs to just kill them (guardians and drowned are classed as neutral)
Predator: Hostile to nearly everything in the water (you can throw chum clusters in the water to make them avoid you)

Clams: Clams are mobs that can be found in all the ocean biomes, when killed they drop food, when they have their mouth open, use shears on them to obtain a pearl, the pearl color is dependent on the color of the clam, each color is found in a different biome
(Classification: Prey)

Jellyfish: Jellyfish just swm around and glow, nothing too special
(Classification: Prey)

Reefopods: Reefopods spawn in coral reefs and swim around the area
(Classification: Prey)

Shrimp: Shrimp are another fish mob that drops food:

(Classification: Prey)

Merfolk: Merfolk are a humanoid race that attacks players that arent wearing Scrap Metal Armor, they can be traded with when not provoked (you can cycle their trades by provoking them). they attack undead mobs, the babies can be adopted via giving them Malachite, when adopted they can be fed, given potions and milk and many other things, when the adopted merfolk becomes an adult, you can give them armor and weapons and they will fight for you (they also have a few secret interactions with certain items)

(they also have a completely hostile brute version which is the 3rd image)
