Break a single block of dirt and get a diamond block. Break stone and get a saddle. Kill a chicken and it drops a netherite ingot. This addon turns every block break and mob kill into a chaotic loot lottery nothing drops what it's supposed to anymore.

How It Works

- Break any block in the game it's instantly removed and drops 1 to 3 completely random items instead of its normal drop

- Kill any mob same thing, 1 to 3 random items drop instead of the mob's usual loot

- The random pool pulls from every single item in the game tools, blocks, food, mob drops, everything is possible

- Random stack sizes too sometimes you get 1 item, sometimes a full stack of 64

Why You'll Love It

Survival becomes pure chaos. Mining a single block could give you diamonds, or it could give you a single rotten flesh. Every break and every kill is a gamble perfect for challenge runs, content creation, or just messing around with friends.

Achievement Friendly?

Yes fully achievement friendly. No cheats required, no experimental toggles, no achievement lock.

Every block is a mystery box. Break responsibly.
