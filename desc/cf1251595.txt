Introduction

"AG2" is a 3D-only weapon addon based on its iconic predecessor, Actual Guns CSO (a project originally released 7 years ago), with the goal of becoming the largest Minecraft Bedrock gun addon ever made in terms of weapon quantity without compromising quality.

Check just how big our scope is for this addon: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gKeDW8Ky9K_OibBefXF4z-PaFsj4ZSgGJRBnmzMbAGQ

Budget of the addon

All weapons are made with commissions, meaning I spend money out of my own pocket to update this addon to get the models and animations, and I'm currently at ~$786 loss. Please manage your expectations.

I do not take any percentage of donations. All donations go directly to the developer who creates the weapon models & animations (DaffaTheOne). I only do the coding part of the addon. In some cases, I may help cover small funding gaps or support custom requests, such as rare animation preferences. So far, 9 people have donated.

The Money Spent section is updated every time I spend money on the addon, even if what I paid for hasn't been added yet. So if the amount spent seems really high compared to the number of guns or features currently in the addon, don't worry about it. A lot of the bigger things I've paid for are still being worked on and haven't made it into the addon yet, this is because of the massive remake that's being currently worked on.

You can donate and choose a weapon of your choice from the Google Docs above to get it added much sooner!
