"HD Controls" in Minecraft PE refers to custom user interface (UI) texture packs or built-in settings that optimize mobile touch buttons.

Core Features

Cleaner Visuals: Replaces large, bulky default buttons with crisp, transparent, or entirely invisible overlays.

Custom Layouts: Allows resizing and moving individual buttons anywhere on the screen for better comfort.

PvP Advantage: Clears up your screen view, making it easier to track enemies and aim during combat.
