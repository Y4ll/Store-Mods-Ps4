Bare Bones X Spectral Vanilla

This pack takes the smooth, custom tool animations from SpectralPumpkinV4 and gives them the minimalist Bare Bones aesthetic. Unlike the Pumpkin version, this edition restores the original vanilla colors for a clean, nostalgic, and vibrant experience.

Designed to transform your Minecraft Tool Set into a visually engaging and authentic experience.
