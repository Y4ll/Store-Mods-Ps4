Durability Display shows durability information everywhere you need it, on your HUD, in your inventory, in item tooltips, and for all your armor pieces.

Features

- HUD Display See your selected hotbar item's durability with an item icon in the corner of your screen

- Armor & Offhand All 4 armor slots + shield durability shown in the corner of your inventory

- Hover Text Durability added to item tooltips when you hover over a slot

- Low Durability Warning Blinking [!] alert on the HUD when your item drops below 25%

- Color Coded Armor and offhand numbers shift from red to green based on remaining durability (20-level gradient)

- Animated Counting Smooth number transitions when durability changes

- Container Support Works inside chests, ender chests, and large chests

- Mobile Friendly Full pocket/touch UI support

No behavior pack required. Works as a pure resource pack on all platforms.

Subpack Options

Choose your preferred display style in the subpack settings. Each option sets both the number format and which side of the screen the HUD and armor readout sit on:

- Numbers (Right) - Colored numbers like 150/250, anchored bottom-right (default)

- Numbers (Left) - Colored numbers like 150/250, anchored bottom-left

- Percentage (Right) - Colored percentage like 60%, anchored bottom-right

- Percentage (Left) - Colored percentage like 60%, anchored bottom-left

Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.0+

- Windows, Mobile, Console

- No experimental features required
