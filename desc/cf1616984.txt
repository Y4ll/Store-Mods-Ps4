Every Block Drops Are Random

Experience Minecraft Bedrock survival in a completely different way with Every Block Drops Are Random by FleetMc Studio. Every block you break has a chance to drop a completely different in-game block instead of its normal drop, making every mining trip, building project, and survival adventure full of surprises.

From incredibly valuable blocks to completely useless junk, you never know what you'll receive next. One moment you could get an extremely rare block that helps you progress faster, and the next you might end up with something totally unexpected. Every block becomes a new opportunityand a new challenge.

How Does It Work?

Simply start breaking blocks as you normally would. Instead of receiving the block's original drop, the addon randomly selects another Minecraft block from the game and drops it for you.

Every block you mine can reward you with something completely different, making exploration, mining, and resource gathering feel fresh every time you play.

Completely Random Block Drops

Every broken block has a random outcome. You might receive common building blocks, decorative blocks, useful survival blocks, rare materials, or incredibly valuable blocks that completely change your progress.

Because every drop is random, no two survival worlds will ever feel exactly the same. Adapt to your luck and make the best use of whatever the game gives you.

From Super OP to Complete Junk

Not every reward is valuableand that's part of the fun! Some random drops can be extremely powerful and help you progress much faster, while others may be common or nearly useless blocks.

Learning how to survive with random resources creates a unique Minecraft challenge where luck plays an important role alongside strategy and creativity.

Normal Minecraft survival eventually becomes predictable because every block always drops the same item. This addon changes that by making every block break exciting and unpredictable.

Installation Guide

- Download the addon.

- Open it using ZArchiver or any file manager app.

- Select Open With and choose Minecraft.

- Wait for Minecraft to import the addon.

- Open your world settings.

- Enable the addon under Behavior Packs.

- Enter your world and enjoy completely random block drops.
