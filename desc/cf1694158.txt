Horror Stalkers

Turn your Minecraft world into a place where you can never feel completely safe.

With Horror Stalkers, creepy creatures can appear when you least expect them. They may hide in dark areas, watch you from a distance, or suddenly show up behind you while you are exploring.

One of the most important mechanics is hiding. If things get too dangerous, you can hide under the bed and wait until the danger passes.

But dont get too comfortable. The stalkers can appear at unexpected moments, so always watch your surroundings and be careful about what is happening behind your back.

How to Use It

Setting up the addon is simple.

First, enter this command in the chat:

/stalker_config

This will open the Stalker Configuration Menu.

Inside the configuration menu, you can customize the addon and change different stalker settings to create the horror experience you want. Adjust the available options and then start playing.

Features

- Creepy stalker creatures

- Unexpected appearances

- Dark and tense atmosphere

- Hide under beds to stay safe

- Stalkers can follow and watch you

- Customizable through /stalker_config

- Great for horror survival worlds

- Perfect for playing alone or with friends

You never know exactly when they will appear.

Stay alert. Check behind you. And when something is coming... hide.
