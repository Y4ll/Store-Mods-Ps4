MAKE SURE TO CHANGE THE WORLD SIMULATION DISTANCE TO 8 OR HIGHER FOR IT TO WORK AS INTENDED!! but if you don't want you may not do it hehe (if you encounter bugs report to me via discord).

Important: before you remove the pack, because the grass physically replaces the blocks in your world, please open the Grass Tuner, set the mode to Restore, and walk around your build first. This turns everything back into normal vanilla grass. If you remove the pack without restoring, the grass will show up as unknown blocks.
