Round Logs A More Natural Look for Logs in Minecraft Bedrock

Have you ever felt that Minecraft logs are just too... square? Well, heres the solution! Round Logs transforms the top texture of logs to look more rounded, while keeping their classic cubic shape.

Key Features:

Visually rounded log tops (still square, but with a smoother, more natural texture). Works with any texture pack (a simple yet effective visual tweak). Stays true to Minecrafts style (doesnt change gameplay, only improves aesthetics). Optimized for performance (lightweight and lag-free for all devices).

Why Use Round Logs?

If you love building and want your trees and wooden structures to look more organic, this pack is for you! It enhances your worlds natural beauty without altering Minecrafts core look.

Download & Installation

1 Download the .mcpack file . 2 Open it with Minecraft Bedrock and enable the texture in your world. 3 Enjoy a more immersive forest!

Compatible with: Minecraft Bedrock (MCPE, Windows, Consoles). No shaders or mods required (just a lightweight texture tweak).
