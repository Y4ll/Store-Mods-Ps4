This Addon adds +101 new foods, one more ore, 3 new useful blocks and more that you can get in your survival world, this addon bring us food such as more crops, cakes, candies, donuts, ice creams, jams, juices, sandwiches, tacos and more, this addon also add more trees which we can get fruits of them.

Next the blocks, items and food will be divided by their kind.

Blocks

Below you'll find a video showing how to use the oven, juice dispenser, toaster, and jar.

Oven

With the oven you can cook any food that can be cooked (meatball, beef, chicken, bacon, fish, salmon, egg, mutton, porkchop and rabbit), you can make yellow cheese and caramel with a milk bucket and sugar respectively.

Juice dispenser

With the juice dispenser, as its name indicates, you can make a lot of diferent juices with it, you can make (each of them gives 3 of nutrition):

- Apple juice

- Banana juice

- Berry juice

- Cherry juice

- Chocomilk

- Glass of milk

- Grape juice

- Kiwi juice

- Mango juice

- Melon juice

- Orange juice

- Pineapple juice

- Strawberry juice

Toaster

The toaster only works for toasted bread (1 of nutrition), but with it you can make more food (Later it's shown that).

Jar

With the jar you can use it to store candies or simply as a decoration.

Ice Cream Machine

Crafting this block helps you making ice creams (you can see more in ice cream section).

you'll need snowballs to fill the machin so you can make any ice cream.

How to use these blocks:

Salt ore

With the salt ore you can get salt mining it as well as burning it in a furnace, salt works to make some food that you can look later.

Juices

Banana Juice - Jump Boost - 15 seconds

Berry Juice - Speed - 15 seconds

Chocomilk - Haste - 20 seconds

Glass of Milk - Resistance - 15 seconds

Grape Juice - Water Breathing - 15 seconds

Kiwi Juice - Slow Falling - 10 seconds

Melon Juice - Instant Health - Instant
