Force Spectator Pack

Force Spectator Pack adds a special Spectator Mode experience to Minecraft Bedrock while keeping achievements enabled.

With easy-to-use Creator Settings toggles, you can switch between Survival Mode and Spectator Mode whenever you want, making it perfect for exploration, building inspections, map reviews, and cinematic recording without changing your world's core gameplay.

Features

Spectator Mode access in Survival worlds

Achievements remain enabled

Easy toggle controls in Creator Settings

Switch between Survival and Spectator instantly

Useful for exploring caves, structures, and builds

No commands required

How to Use

Install and activate the pack.

Open Creator Settings.

Enable the Spectator Mode toggle.

Use the provided toggles to switch between:

Survival Mode

Spectator Mode

Enjoy free camera movement while keeping your world progression intact.

Notes

Designed for Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

Make sure the pack is enabled in your world settings before playing.
