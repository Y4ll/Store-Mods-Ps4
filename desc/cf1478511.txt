Fused Vanilla is a Vanilla Plus texture pack that enhances Minecrafts look while staying true to the original style. From detailed door designs to a full nature overhaul, it adds extra depth so your builds feel just as rich and lively as the world around them. With custom models and unique variants throughout the pack, the game simply feels more alive and fun to explore.

Familiar... but better in every way

Features

This Pack comprises a Texture Pack and an optional Models Pack which modifies the shapes of some blocks. Features included in the Models Pack have been marked with a '*'

- Butterflies!

Butterflies have been added as new ambient particles that naturally spawn around leaves. They come in four random variants, each with animated flapping textures, bringing more life and movement to forests and green spaces.

watch the video below to see them in action!

- Birds!

Birds have been added as new ambient particles that naturally spawn only around trees. They come in four random variants, each with animated flapping textures, bringing more life and movement to empty skies.
Some spawn near the ground and some spawn high in the sky.

- Better Rain, Splashes & Puddles!

This texture pack also enhances rain by improving textures and adding ripples, splashes, and puddles for a more realistic, lively world. It also upgrades water effects and particles.

Features:

- New, improved rain texture

- Realistic ripples and splashes when rain hits the ground or water

- Randomly spawning puddles during rainfall

- Water ripples and splashes when items are thrown in

- Water ripples and splashes when players swim

watch the video below to see them in action!

Doors and trapdoors now have new detailed textures!

- New Iron Theme

Some iron blocks have a completely new theme

- More Block Variants

Following blocks have 2-8 different variants

- New Torch, Ladder, Crafting Table & Lantern

Following blocks have a new texture

- Better Grass/Dirt Sides

Following blocks have a new side texture

- Better Leaves*

- Better Leaves Model*

The leaves have a new 3d model and look bushy. This feature is optional and available by using the Models Pack(Better Leaves) version.

- Improved Tree Colors

Trees will have different colors depending upon the biome they are in:

-Birch trees will be pink in forest biomes, orange in cold biomes and yellow in all other biomes.

-Oak trees will be orange in cold biomes and green in all other biomes.

-Dark Oak trees will be red in all biomes.

-Swamp trees and Acacia trees are more greener.

- New Flowers

Every flower has a new texture and 4-6 variants

- New Tall Flowers

Every tall flower 4 different variants for the top and bottom parts. This means each tall flower has a total of 16 different variants!

- Better Falling Leaves

This texture pack also adds more variety to falling leaf particles and enhances their physicsthey now land on the ground, drift in water, and make your worlds feel more alive.
