About SoundTweaks

SoundTweaks is an immersive, one of high-quality sound enhancement ASMRS made specifically for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. It replaces and improves vanilla sounds to feel more natural, realistic, and atmospheric without hurting performance or changing your core gameplay.

Wet Grass Sounds Special step sounds that play automatically when it rains or grass is wet

Enhanced Underwater Ambience New enter/exit sounds, deep loop audio, plus extra details like bubbles, whale calls, and underwater crackles

Improved Block Sounds Wood, stone, cobweb, deepslate, chests, beacons, campfires, brewing stands, barrels, and more with balanced volume & natural pitch variation

Portal & Crossbow Full new audio for Nether portal travel, trigger, and crossbow shooting

Balanced Volume Adjusted levels so sounds are crisp but never too loud or overwhelming

100% Vanilla-Friendly No lag, no extra requirements, works on all Bedrock devices
