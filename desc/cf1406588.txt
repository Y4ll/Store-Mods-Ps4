MOBS

Mobs

Sculk Creeper: the Sculk Creeper behaves like a normal creeper except when it gets near a target, it charges itself, if the target escapes, it uncharges itself

Sculk Skeleton: shoots sonic booms instead of arrows

Sculk Zombie: attacks, but once they hit you, they dash away and temporarly get a strength and speed boost

Mimic: Mimics attack similar to slimes, spawns by randomly replacing chests in structures.

Hallow: strong attacking mob that inflicts blindness

Pixie: tamable passive mobs that give free speed and slow falling when tamed (tamed with Aether Fruit)

Molten: behaves like a zombie but has a trail of fire where it walks

Gem Crawlers: drops whatever ore is on their back on death, will run away from all mobs

Creeder: half creeper half spider

Light Allay: tamable via golden berries, emits light as they move

Sodalite Energizer: flying mob that fires projectiles

Iceologer: summons ice blocks above the player/villager

Aeroslime: the Aeroslime behaves identically to the slime, except it can fly
