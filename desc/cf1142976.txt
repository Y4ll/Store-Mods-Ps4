Raccoon Addon Natural & Fascinating Feline Behavior

The Raccoon Addon introduces one of the most expressive, curious, and dynamic creatures ever added to Minecraft Bedrock.
These raccoons behave with personality, instincts, and social intelligence interacting with players, the environment, and even other raccoons.
Get ready to meet your new mischievous furry friend!

Important Update

Wild raccoons can steal items from nearby chests!
Tame them to stop this behavior and turn them into loyal companions.

Personalization: Name your raccoon to make it unique.

New BiomeBased Textures: Raccoons now spawn in different colors depending on the biome gray, white, black, and brown.

General Behaviors

Item Collection

Raccoons can pick up and equip items they find.
Be careful if its edible, they might eat it!

Mounting Behavior

Baby raccoons ride on the back of nearby adults.
When they grow up, they hop off and explore independently.

Orchard Raider

Raccoons love orchards and may steal crops like sweet berries.
Protect your farms!

Nighttime Activity

Raccoons become more active at night, while during the day they may nap or seek shelter.

Player Interaction

Tamed raccoons follow you, sit on command, and even sleep beside you.
The cutest part? When sleeping together, they may give you items as a sign of affection!

Environmental Interactions

Relaxed Postures

Raccoons can randomly sit or lie down, adding realism and charm.

Item Equipment

They can carry certain items.
Want them to drop it? Throw food on the ground theyll trade it!

Skilled Hunter

Raccoons hunt small animals like rabbits and chickens.
After the hunt, they eat whatever the prey drops.

Defense Behavior

Tamed raccoons defend their trusted player from nearby threats.

Life Cycle

Breeding

Feed raccoons sweet berries to breed them.
Babies follow their parents and grow over time.

Personality & Social Intelligence

Taming

Use sweet berries to tame a raccoon.
Once tamed, they become affectionate and may offer gifts.

Avoiding Enemies

Raccoons avoid hostile mobs like wolves and polar bears, and stay cautious around untamed players.

Defensive Attacks

They may occasionally defend the player and engage in melee attacks.

A More Lively World

With all these behaviors, raccoons become one of the most complex and dynamic creatures in Minecraft Bedrock.
They enrich exploration, survival, and roleplay with personality, intelligence, and adorable interactions.
