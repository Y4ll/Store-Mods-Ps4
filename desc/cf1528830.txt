Tired of manually chasing every ore vein block by block? This addon lets you mine an entire connected ore vein instantly, no commands, no crafting, no extra items.

- How to Use

Simply Sneak + Break any ore block to instantly harvest every connected ore in the vein. That's it. Works with your pickaxe exactly as normal.

- Features

- Instant vein mining Sneak + Break an ore to mine the full connected cluster

- Fortune support Fortune enchantment applies to every block in the vein

- Silk Touch support Silk Touch drops the ore blocks themselves, not raw resources

- All ore types Coal, Iron, Gold, Copper, Diamond, Emerald, Lapis, Redstone, Quartz, Nether Gold, Ancient Debris + all Deepslate variants

- All dimensions Works in Overworld, Nether, and The End

- Achievement-safe Does NOT disable achievements or trophies

- Multiplayer ready Works independently for each player

- Zero lag Lightweight BFS script, no tick-heavy loops, capped at 512 blocks

- Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21+, 26.0+

- Works on Android, iOS, Windows, Console

- Behavior Pack only, no Resource Pack needed

- Compatible with other addons

- Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Tap/double-click to auto-import into Minecraft

- Apply the pack to your world under Behavior Packs

- Play, Sneak + Break any ore to vein mine!
