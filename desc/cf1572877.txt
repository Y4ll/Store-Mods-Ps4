Project Description:

The world you knew is gone, but the fight for survival has just begun. Test your survival skills in a vanilla Zombie Apocalypse featuring more zombies than ever before. Prepare to face relentless hordes, scavenge for scarce resources, and build fortified defenses as you navigate a desolate landscape where every shadow hides a new threat. Whether you choose to brave the wasteland alone or band together with other survivors, teamwork and quick thinking are your only guarantees of seeing another sunrise.

Important Information:

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Increased zombie spawn rate.

- Spawn rules not effected.

- Have something to say? Join the Discord.

Zombies will spawn all over your world, day and night completely immune to the sunlight.
