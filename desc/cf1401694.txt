Lumina Visuals is built for players who want their world to look stunning without compromising gameplay. Every biome gets its own volumetric fog, so a cherry grove at midday and a dark forest in the rain each get atmosphere built specifically for them.

Skies run from a clean daylight blue through violet-over-gold at dusk into deep moonlit blue at night. Water reflects it all back. Nothing here asks you to stop playing to look at it.

FEATURES

- Realistic deferred lighting on the current Vibrant Visuals pipeline

- Chromatic volumetric fog tuned individually for every biome

- Violet-and-gold sunrise and sunset skies

- Moon-driven night lighting with true blue tones

- 109 coloured light sources, with glowing bioluminescent plants

- Soft cinematic shadows

- Bloom & glow highlights

- Reflective water with animated caustics

- Custom foliage and grass colours, clouds and End sky

- Full Nether & End support - Nether Wastes, Crimson Forest, Warped Forest, Soul Sand Valley, Basalt Deltas and The End, each with its own atmosphere and lighting

REQUIREMENTS

Vibrant Visuals must be enabled in your world or global video settings. Place Lumina Visuals at the top of your resource pack stack for best results.
