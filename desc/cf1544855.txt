Cinematic Fog adds thick atmospheric fog, dramatic sunrays, and biome-tinted volumetric depth to Minecraft Bedrock. Simply place the Cinematic Fog pack above any Vibrant Visuals shader to enhance atmosphere and depth without replacing your visuals.

- Recommended settings

For the best experience, configure your Vibrant Visuals as follows: Volumetric Fog Enabled

- Subpacks

MEDIUM (Recommended) Balanced fog with controlled sunrays

HIGH Stronger sunrays for more dramatic lighting
