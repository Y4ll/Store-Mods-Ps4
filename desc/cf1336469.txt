Endless Fabulous is an add-on inspired by the Java mod Avaritia, bringing extremely powerful armor and tools. This is a playable BETA version.

-----------------------------------------------------------

**Enable the Beta APIs experiment and the Minecraft Education option.**

-----------------------------------------------------------

**The Recipe Book will include recipes for both the Extreme Crafting Table and the Neutronium Compressor.**

Armor of Infinity

Infinity Helmet:

- Grants Night Vision when equipped.

- Grants Water Breathing while in water.

- Immunity to the effects of Blindness and Darkness.

- By keeping your gaze fixed on a mob or player for 3 seconds, you deal 3 points of damage to the target.

Infinity Chestplate:

*It is mandatory to enable the Education Edition option.*

- Grants the ability to fly when equipped.

- Grants immunity to explosions.

- Grants Regeneration while health is below maximum.

Infinity Leggings:

- Increases running speed.

- Grants Slow Fall when falling from great heights.

- Grants immunity to the Slowness effect.

Infinity Boots:

- Grants Jump Boost when equipped.

- Grants immunity to fall damage.

Full Armor:

- Grants immunity to any type of damage.

- Grants immunity to the /kill command.

Sword of the Death:

- Infinite Damage.

- It kills players even in Creative mode.

- It does not affect whoever is wearing the Armor of Infinity.

Skullfire Sword:

- 100% chance that, when killing any type of skeleton in Minecraft, it will drop a Wither Skeleton Skull.

Reality Shatterer (Pickaxe):

- Breaks Bedrock.

- Fortune X

- Breaks blocks extremely fast.

- Hammer Mode: breaks a 777 area, and the items are stored in a Matter Cluster.

Press and hold Shift on the screen or right-click to switch between Pickaxe and Hammer modes.

Neutron Collectors:

- Normal: Generates 1 Pile of Neutron every 10 minutes.

- Enhanced: Generates 1 Pile of Neutron every 5 minutes.

- Supreme: Generates 1 Neutronium Nugget every 1 minutes.

Endest Pearl:

When thrown, it creates a black hole.
