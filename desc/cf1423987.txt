MORE VANILLA TOOLS

Unique Tools with Special Abilities!

ABOUT

More Vanilla Tools expands your Minecraft Bedrock experience with new materials and unique tools, each with its own special effects and abilities! From magical amethyst crystals to ancient sniffer artifacts, every tool feels fresh and exciting.

Why You'll Love This Addon:

- No overpowered items - carefully balanced for vanilla gameplay

- Unique themed effects - each material has its own identity

- Beautiful particles - custom cherry blossoms & bamboo leaves

- Quality of life features - area tilling, timber chopping, item magnet

- Works in Survival - all tools have proper durability
