Better Waving Leaves brings life to Minecraft's foliage with smooth, realistic wind animations no mods, no scripts, no behavior pack required.

Every leaf type waves in the breeze: oak, birch, spruce, jungle, acacia, mangrove, cherry, azalea, bamboo, and pale oak. Flowers nod dandelions, roses, tulips, alliums, orchids, sunflowers, and more. Tall grass and ferns sway. Vines drift weeping, twisting, cave, and regular. Crops ripple as they grow: wheat, carrots, potatoes, and beetroots all animate through their growth stages. Even the lily pad shifts on the water's surface.

74 natural blocks animated in total, all using optimized flipbook textures that blend seamlessly into vanilla Minecraft's art style. Nothing looks out of place it just looks alive.

Drop it in your resource pack folder and you're done.

Details

- 74 animated block textures

- Covers all leaf types, flowers, grasses, vines, crops, and more

- Flipbook-based lightweight, no lag

- Works alongside other resource packs

- No behavior pack or experimental toggles needed

- Bedrock Edition only requires 1.21.80 or higher
