Planter Barter

Planter Barter lets you barter seeds/saplings in exchange for an emerald. With this addon, you don't need to cut down trees just to get the sapling or harvest your crop farm just to get more seeds. Designed for survival worlds, hardcore worlds, resource-focused worlds, and farms. Compatible to the players/devices that play Minecraft:Bedrock/MCPE with NO scripts, commands, and experimental features needed. Whether you are farming and ran out of seeds, this addon is for you!

In this addon, the entity will let you barter seeds/saplings in exchange for an emerald. The seeds and saplings are available to barter. This is affordable if you have enough emeralds. This entity can barter for 0.5 second/half a second. This entity is also fast, what I mean fast I mean FAST. The entity spawns to the overworld with the surface grass block.
