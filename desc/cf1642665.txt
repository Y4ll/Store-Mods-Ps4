Lynx Simple Tree Capitator

Chop down an entire tree with a single swing! Hold Sneak (Shift) while breaking a log with any axe, and every connected log in that tree including diagonal branches on trees like acacia and jungle comes crashing down instantly.

Features:

Sneak-activated: hold Shift while chopping to fell the whole tree; chop normally (without Shift) to cut just one log, exactly like vanilla

Works with any axe and all wood types, including nether stems (crimson/warped)

Detects diagonally-connected logs, so leaning and irregular tree shapes get fully chopped

Realistic durability cost one point of damage per log, and fully respects the Unbreaking enchantment

If your axe breaks mid-chop, the process stops immediately, just like vanilla

Capped at a safe number of logs per chop to protect server performance no lag spikes on giant trees

Leaves are left untouched and decay naturally, keeping the addon lightweight

Pure behavior pack, no resource pack or texture changes fully compatible with other add-ons

Works on singleplayer, Realms, and dedicated (BDS) servers

Requirements:

Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.40+

"Beta APIs" experimental toggle enabled in world settings

Download Addon

How to use:

Equip any axe, hold Sneak, and break a single log the rest of the tree falls with it.
