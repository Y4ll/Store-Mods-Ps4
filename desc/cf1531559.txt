Whats the difference between the Mutant Wither and the regular Wither?

The regular Wither already has a unique combat pattern, but it can feel repetitive over time. In contrast, the Mutant Wither takes everything to a much more extreme level. The fight becomes significantly harder, with more aggressive behavior and unpredictable attacks. Youll need far better timing, stronger preparation, and much more strategyeasily ten times the effort compared to fighting a normal Wither.

Mutant Wither can spawn additional Wither Skeletons when it is under pressure.
