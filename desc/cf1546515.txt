WEAPON++

Adds 3 weapons with independent skill systems to Minecraft Bedrock. Each weapon works standalone and does not conflict with the others. Requires Beta APIs enabled in world settings.

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.60 or higher

- **Beta APIs** enabled (Experiments Beta APIs)

- Compatible with Java PVP CORE

Lumina Blade

https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft-bedrock/addons/sword-lumina-blade

Damage:10 | Durability: 1200 | Enchant slot: sword (value 10) | Use cooldown:0.5s

A radiant light blade with 4 skills triggered by how you hold/use it:

- Radiant Nova(hold continuously for 20s while standing still): Explodes with light around you, dealing 8 dmg + blindness/weakness/slowness to enemies within 6 blocks, with camera shake.

- Lumina Ward(crouch continuously for 3s): An 8s protective aura absorption, resistance, regeneration, glowing, with a rotating particle ring around the player.

- Solar Requiem (right-click, 8s cooldown) ultimate skill in 4 phases: charge-up piercing light beam across 22 blocks (18 dmg + ignite) 5 falling light meteor pillars (9 dmg each) central detonation (12 dmg + knockback), self-buffs speed/strength afterward.

- Ascension Strike (hold for 20s + jumping): Launches you upward in a light spiral, then slams down, dealing 10 dmg + knockback within 5 blocks.

Yamato Katana

https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft-bedrock/addons/weapons-yamato-katana

Damage:9 | Durability: 3600 | | Has JavaPvP tags (`jpvp:dm:9`, `jpvp:cd:12`) | Repaired with Netherite Ingot (+1000) or itself.

A Vergil-style (DMC) katana with a live cooldown HUD on the actionbar:

- Yamato Barrier(passive, 15s cooldown): Automatically absorbs 60% of incoming damage as healing.

- Judgment Cut(crouch next attack, 5s cooldown): Cuts through 8 blocks in your facing direction, 18 dmg to all targets in the line.

- Doppelganger (Sneak + Use, 10s cooldown): Summons a shadow clone for 5s Speed III, glowing, +50% bonus damage on hits.

- Devil Trigger (Sneak + Jump, 30s cooldown): Demon transformation for 10s Strength IV, Resistance II, Speed II, Regen II, +8 flat damage per hit.

- Majin Trigger (Sneak + Jump while DT is active, 45s cooldown replaces DT): Ultimate demon form for 6s Strength VI, Resistance IV, Absorption IV, +18 flat damage, pulses a wither aura around you every 15 ticks.

- Summoned Swords(Sprint + Use, 8s cooldown): Launches 6 phantom swords that lock onto the nearest targets, 10 dmg each.

- Sin Stinger (Sprint next attack, 4s cooldown): A forward lunge that pierces the nearest target ahead, 32 dmg.
