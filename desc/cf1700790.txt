Project Description:

Never lose your path in the wild. Claim your territory, spark a Waystone to life, and conquer the map in the blink of an eye. When you stumble upon a hidden valley, a sprawling fortress, or a secret mining outpost you know you'll need to visit again, don't leave your return to chance
