Minecraft is all about creativity and survival, but building a proper shelter on your first day can be time-consumingespecially for new players. Thats where the Craftable Houses Addon comes in! With this addon, you can instantly spawn three unique houses using special craftable items. Whether you need a quick base, a medium-sized home, or a cozy decorative house, this addon has you covered.

House 2: The Medium Rustic Home

Description: House 2 is a warm, medieval-style home with two tall towers and detailed wooden roofing.
Design: This house is larger than a basic starter hut, featuring a medieval design and multiple entry points.
Features:

- Crafted with wood planks (see recipe).

- Spacious two-floor layout.

- Room for storage, crafting, and expansion.

- Decorative details like beams and angled roofs.

House 3: The Compact Stone Cottage

Description: House 3 is a charming stone-and-wood starter home, ideal for players who want a compact but stylish base.
Design: A balanced mix of stone bricks and wood makes this home feel cozy yet durable.
Features:

- Crafted using wood and stone (see recipe).

- Two levels: lower floor for crafting/storage, upper floor as a bedroom.

- Chimney and rustic design details.

- Perfect for small survival worlds.

House 4: The Large Fantasy Home

Description: House 4 is the most detailed home in the addon, built with a fantasy style that feels grand and magical.
Design: With towering walls, curved roofs, and large interiors, this house makes you feel like youre living in a castle-like base.
Features:

- Crafted using a mix of wood planks (see recipe).

- Large multi-floor layout with plenty of space.

- Pre-lit to keep mobs away.

- Beautiful detailing for players who love aesthetics.
