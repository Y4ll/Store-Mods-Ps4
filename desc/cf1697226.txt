ARCE Visuals is an effects-only Vibrant Visuals pack for Minecraft Bedrock, built to make ordinary worlds look worth capturing. Sunrises burn amber through volumetric haze, storms roll in with prismatic rain and fork lightning, water runs with live caustics and glassy wave motion, and the night sky opens up with aurora curtains, meteors and a cratered moon. Colored local lights warm every cave and lantern, portals and enchanted gear pick up cinematic glints, and particles, clouds, celestial bodies and block-break effects get real PBR response.
