BAKU CINEMATIC CAMERA

AFK CINEMATICS CUSTOM CAMERA PATHS BUILD SHOWCASE

Baku Cinematic Camera turns your Minecraft world into a smooth cinematic showcase. When a player stays AFK, the addon can automatically activate a cinematic camera and smoothly showcase the surrounding area, builds, bases and environments.

You can also create your own custom cinematic paths, adjust camera settings and instantly preview your shots without waiting for the AFK timer.

MAIN FEATURES

Automatic AFK Cinematic
Cinematic mode automatically starts when the player remains inactive for the selected amount of time.

Adjustable AFK Timer
Choose how long the player needs to stay inactive before cinematic mode starts.

Smooth Cinematic Camera
Enjoy smooth camera movement designed for showcasing nearby builds and environments.

Automatic Build Showcase
Perfect for castles, survival bases, cities, maps and other Minecraft creations.

Custom Cinematic Path Editor
Create your own cinematic route by saving multiple camera positions.

Custom Camera Angles
Each custom camera point can store its own position and viewing direction.

Instant Preview
Preview your cinematic route immediately without waiting for AFK activation.

Manual Cinematic Control
Use the Cinematic Controller to manually start and manage cinematic mode.

Clean Cinematic View
HUD elements are hidden while cinematic mode is active for cleaner recordings.

Automatic HUD Restore
The normal Minecraft HUD automatically returns when cinematic mode ends.

Multiplayer Support
Every player has their own AFK timer and cinematic state.

CINEMATIC MODES

AUTO MODE

The addon automatically generates cinematic camera movement around the player's current location. This mode is useful when you want quick cinematic shots without manually creating a camera route.

CUSTOM MODE

Create your own cinematic path using multiple custom camera points. This gives you much more control over how your build is shown.

Custom Mode is especially useful for trailers, build showcases, map previews and cinematic recordings.
