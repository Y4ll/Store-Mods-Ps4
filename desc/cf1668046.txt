Water Wave

Water Wave is a Minecraft Bedrock pack that adds beautiful and realistic animated water waves to your world!

Features:

Realistic animated water waves

Smooth water movement

Dynamic wave effects

Custom water textures

Better and more immersive ocean, river, and lake visuals

Performance-friendly options

added particle

Choose Smooth Mode for better performance or Dynamic Mode for stronger and more visible waves.

Bring your Minecraft world to life with realistic moving water!
