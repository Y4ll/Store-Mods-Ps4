**Utility Combat & Parkour**

An advanced addon that enhances Minecraft Bedrock Edition with dynamic combat and parkour movement skills for fluid, pro-level gameplay.

**Key Parkour Features:**

- **Slide** Quick ground slides for evasion and speed boosts

- **Wall Climb** Scale vertical walls effortlessly

- **Wall Fall / Wall Slide** Controlled descent down walls with dust particles for style

- **Ceiling Climb** Stick to and traverse ceilings upside-down

- **Crawl** Squeeze through tight spaces and under low obstacles

- **Roll** Dodge rolls for agile evasion in combat

- **Double Jump** Extra mid-air leap for reaching new heights

- **And many more** Bunny hop, air strafe, ledge grab, and customizable utilities!

All skills integrate with a customizable Stamina system for balanced, tactical gameplay. Enjoy smooth animations, immersive sound effects, realistic particles, and seamless controls that feel like modern action games (e.g., Apex Legends, Titanfall).
