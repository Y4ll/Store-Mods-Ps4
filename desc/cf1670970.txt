Project Description:

Feel the heavy stare of the watcher pressing against your neck, catching the fleeting motion of something stalking just at the edge of your peripheral vision. Did you actually catch a glimpse of the shape lurking in the dark, or is your mind simply playing terrifying tricks on you?

The Watcher is a mysterious creature of unknown origin whose primary compulsion is observation, though it fiercely avoids being seen. It typically begins its hunt by stalking targets from a safe distance, slowly building curiosity until it draws closer to inspect them. Spotting it triggers an immediate retreat, but the brief glimpse offers little comfortit always returns.

Seeking refuge inside a building can keep you from being killed, but it offers no rest from the torment the watcher will inflict, knocking on windows and doors just for its own pleasure. Inside, the safety is entirely an illusion. The walls block its physical reach, but they do nothing to muffle the soundor the dread. It knows you are trapped, and it prefers the game of psychological siege over a quick kill, dragging out the night until the silence between its knocks feels louder than the noise itself.
