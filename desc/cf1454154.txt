Project Description:

In this hybrid sandbox experience, players can cultivate crops within Minecrafts blocky soil while utilizing advanced growth cinematics and deep soil-quality mechanics to transform a simple homestead into a thriving, high-fantasy estate.

Important Information:

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Real-time growth of custom crops.

- Dynamic 3D models.

- Custom crafting benches.

- Behaviour & Resource packs are both required for the project to work.

- Have something to say? Join the Discord.

The Workbench is a custom crafting table used to craft custom items that have been added into this Add-On. The Workbench also crafts anything a Vanilla Crafting Table can.

The Farmers Workbench is a custom crafting table used to craft custom items relating to farming.

Use a Hoe on Grass to create Farmland.

Using a Watering Can, water your Farmland. Wet Farmland will grow crops 2.5x faster then Dry Farmland.

To get your Farm started you will need to find a Wandering Farmer, he will spawn about 20 blocks away from a Wandering Tradesman. The Wandering Farmer will trade you some Seed Bags for Wheat.

The Wandering Farmers trades will change every ten minutes, the price will be random so sometimes waiting for a re-suffle might be the right plan.

Once you have a Farm up and running you can use the Farmers Workbench to create your own Seed Bags.

All Crops have been giving a 3D model or an updated 3D model.
