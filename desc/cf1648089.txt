AuraFX Visuals is a Vibrant Visuals shader pack built for Minecraft Bedrock's new rendering pipeline. It delivers cinematic atmospheric lighting, dreamy pink-and-purple skies, volumetric sun rays, warm forest sunlight, blazing magenta sunsets, and deep blue starlit nights, all while staying lightweight and smooth across low-end and high-end devices.

Reimagined Visual Style

- Vibrant, saturated color identity built around pink, purple, and gold atmospheric tones

- Balanced between cinematic drama and vanilla-friendly readability

- Designed to feel alive and immersive without overwhelming vanilla builds

- Clean reflective water and soft volumetric fog transitions

Advanced Atmospherics System

- Custom sky zenith and horizon color tuning for a signature dreamy gradient

- Smooth day-to-night sky transitions with tuned Rayleigh scattering for color depth

- Enhanced sun Mie scattering for a glowing, glare-rich sun disc

- Fiery magenta-to-gold sunsets and sunrises with strong horizon blending

- Deep blue moonlit nights with a crisp starfield and soft moon Mie glow

Lighting & Sun Rays

- Volumetric sun rays with boosted intensity through forest canopies and foliage

- Smooth illuminance ramp from vibrant daylight to cool nighttime ambience

- Blue-tinted moonlight for soft, natural night visibility

- Tuned horizon blend stops for a seamless sky-to-treeline transition

Color Grading & Fog

- ACES tone mapping with lifted contrast and boosted saturation across highlights and midtones

- White balance tuned at 6400K for a natural, slightly warm base look

- Biome-aware volumetric fog for atmospheric depth in forests and groves

- High-saturation highlight and midtone grading for punchy, vibrant scenes

Enhanced Local Lighting

- Hand-tuned point and static light colors for every glowing block. warm amber torches and campfires, icy cyan soul fire, glowing purple portals and amethyst. giving light sources a richer, more natural warm-to-cool color range instead of flat vanilla white light

Requirements
AuraFX Visuals requires Minecraft Bedrock/PE with Vibrant Visuals enabled, on version 1.21.80 or higher. Supported devices include Adreno 640+ (Snapdragon 855), MediaTek Dimensity 1000+, and PCs with DirectX 12 FL11 support.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file 2. Open to import into Minecraft 3. Enable AuraFX Visuals in Global Resources 4. Turn on Vibrant Visuals in Video Settings
