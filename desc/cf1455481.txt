Stop wasting time checking if your crops are ready! This resource pack adds a subtle, professional animated glint to all crops only when they have reached their final growth stage. Perfect for massive survival farms and technical players.

Compatibility & Technical Info

Compatible with any Addons & Realms Optimized for Action and Stuff

[] ACHIEVEMENTS FRIENDLY [] NO EXPERIMENTS [] NO PLAYER.JSON

Key Features:

- Visual Clarity: No more guessing between stage 7 or 8. If it shines, its ready!.

- 100% Achievement Compatible: As a pure Resource Pack, you can keep earning trophies in your survival world.

- Performance Optimized: Uses efficient flipbook animations. Designed to run smoothly even in fields with thousands of crops without dropping FPS.

- Vanilla Friendly: It uses original game textures, making it compatible with almost any other Add-on or texture pack.

Supported Crops:

This pack currently highlights the following crops in their final stage:

- Wheat (Stage 7)

- Carrots (Stage 3)

- Potatoes (Stage 3)

- Beetroots (Stage 3)

- Nether Wart (Stage 2)
