About This Add-on

Welcome to an ultra-powerful custom tools add-on for Minecraft PE. This mod adds craftable, super-OP tools that transform normal survival into a power-fantasy playground. Each tool has unique effects and flashy combat interactions perfect for players who want instant chaos and fun.
