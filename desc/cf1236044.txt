Beautiful Beds Texture Pack

Give your Minecraft beds a more detailed, colorful and cozy look!

Beautiful Beds redesigns the vanilla beds with a custom model and 16 unique themed designs, inspired by Minecraft mobs, biomes and environments.

Each bed features its own visual identity, with themed blankets, improved wool textures and wood styles designed to better match its biome or theme.

Features

- 16 unique bed designs

- Custom 3D bed geometry

- Unique mob and biome-inspired blankets

- Different wood styles depending on each bed's theme

- Improved wool textures

- Different design for every vanilla bed color

- Lightweight resource pack

- No scripts or behavior pack required

- Designed for Minecraft Bedrock Edition

16 Beds, 16 Unique Themes

Every vanilla bed color has its own custom appearance. The designs combine different mobs, biomes, colors and wood types to make each bed feel unique while still fitting naturally into Minecraft.

Simply craft or dye your beds normally and the corresponding custom design will automatically be used.

Themes

Beautiful Beds includes designs inspired by different Minecraft environments, mobs and themes, including:

- Ocean

- Jungle

- Desert

- Nether-inspired designs

- Axolotl

- Panda

- Armadillo

- And many more!

The latest visual update also improves the materials used throughout the pack, giving the beds cleaner wool textures and more appropriate wood styles for their respective themes.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file from CurseForge.

- Open the downloaded file.

- Minecraft should automatically import the resource pack.

- Open your world settings.

- Go to Resource Packs.

- Activate Beautiful Beds.

- Enter your world and enjoy!

Compatibility

Beautiful Beds is made for Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

Check the Files section for the latest supported Minecraft versions and available releases.
