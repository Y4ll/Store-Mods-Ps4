Bosya's Selection Box Plus+ V3

Selection Box is a lightweight Bedrock resource pack that adjusts block selection outlines to match their actual visual shapes.

REQUIRED: This pack REQUIRES the Behavior Pack (BP) to work!

DISCLAIMER: The outlines may not be perfectly accurate in all situations. Please make sure to enable "Outline Selection" in your Minecraft Video Settings!

Requirements:

- Minecraft Bedrock v1.21.130+

- Achievement Friendly: Play with your achievements ON! No experiments or Beta APIs required.
