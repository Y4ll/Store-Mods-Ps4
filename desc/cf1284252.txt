Ultimate Vain Miner - Complete Mining Automation

Tired of mining ore block by block? Ultimate Vain Miner revolutionizes your mining experience for Minecraft Bedrock (v1.21+). Clear entire ore veins in one swing with stunning visual effects!

V2.0 UPDATE - NOW WITH FULL CUSTOMIZATION!

New Features:

4 Mining Modes:

- Instant - Classic fast mining

- Cascade - Top-to-bottom waterfall effect

- Explosion - Dramatic explosive visuals

- Progressive - Animated one-by-one with progress

In-Game Menu (Jump + Right Click):

- Full visual configuration

- Speed control (Fast/Normal/Slow)

- Toggle particles & sounds

- Optional durability damage system

Special Effects Per Ore:

- Diamond: Blue sparkles + XP sound

- Ancient Debris: Epic explosion effect

- Emerald: Green particles + level up

- Gold: Golden totem particles

New Tool Support: Copper & Golden pickaxes

Core Features

Smart Vein Detection - Breaks all connected ores within 6-block radius
Works with Any Pickaxe - Wood, Stone, Copper, Iron, Gold, Diamond, Netherite + modded
Harvest Level Enforcement - Respects vanilla mining tiers with warnings
28 Supported Ores - All vanilla ores including deepslate & nether variants
Custom Particles & Sounds - Satisfying visual and audio feedback
Persistent Settings - All config saved per-player

Controls

- Sneak + Right Click - Toggle On/Off

- Jump + Right Click - Open settings menu

- Mine any ore - Watch the magic happen!
