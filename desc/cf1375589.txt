ERROR 183, also known as "The Forgotten Prototype", is a fictional lost version of Minecraft inspired by Minecraft Creepypasta entities, Error 422, and other corrupted versions.

Paranormal activities will occur over time and become more and more common.. Until your world starts to collapse. But this mod will not interact with your device.

Can you survive in this harsh, broken, unsupported version of Minecraft?

Fictional lore:

The Prototype Edition was Mojangs earliest attempt to create something... but it was unstable, corrupted, and seems to be alive.

It attempt to mimic the newer versions of Minecraft to appear normal, but it is not. And it eventually got abandoned and forgotten by the developers...

There is one main entity in this mod : failed_exp_183.

It will have a chance of appearing to attack you after 50 minutes of gameplay, and it will happen again after a while. So be aware!

Before it strike, it will stand from a distance watching you.

If youre on PC and cant download the mod. Try to Extract everything, select the files (both behaviour pack and resource pack), right-click, and compress them into a new ZIP. Rename the new file back to .mcaddon and try importing again.

If some PC players still have trouble, i suggest you to use the manual method:

Rename your file to .zip and extract it.

Manually copy the folders into the Minecraft directory on PC:

Path:
%AppData%\Roaming\Minecraft Bedrock\Users\Shared\games\com.mojang

Place contents into the resource_packsand behavior_packs folders respectively.

If still doesnt work. Try to download it on a mobile device since it works for mobile somehow.
