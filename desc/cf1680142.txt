This is and Addon that will add creatures to your world.

Some of them try to kill you, some don't...

Entities

-Sun Stalker: spawns anytime anywhere any day

-Night Stalker: an entity that spawns at night

-The Hunter: an entity that spawns every 5 days and wants to kill you

-Window stalker: and entity that spawns every 4 days outside your windows

-The Visitor: an entity that spawns every 6 days outside your door

-Cave Stalker: an entity that spawns in caves

-Bed stalker: an entity that watches you sleep

-Ender Stalker: an entity that spawns in the end dimension

-The wanderers: entities that are harmless but spawn anytime any day anywhere

If there are some bugs please tell me so I can fix them.
