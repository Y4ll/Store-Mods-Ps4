# Shadow Powers Addon

## Unleash The Power Of Shadows

Shadow Powers Addon adds dark magical powers and legendary shadow weapons to Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

Become a powerful shadow warrior with epic combat abilities and mystical dark energy.

---

# Features

- Nightmare Blade weapon

- Shadow powers

- Dark fantasy combat

- Epic visual style

- Powerful combat system

- Survival compatible

# Gameplay

Fight mobs with legendary shadow weapons and become stronger with dark powers.

Perfect for:

- Survival

- PvP

- Adventure maps

- Roleplay

# Installation

1. Download the addon

2. Open the .mcaddon file

3. Import into Minecraft

4. Activate:

- Resource Pack

- Behavior Pack
