Deltarune Dark Fountains Resource Pack

Hey everyone! Welcome to this simple, lightweight, but incredibly cool visual resource pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. If you've ever wanted to recreate that epic Deltarune Dark Fountain aesthetic right in the middle of your own Minecraft world, this pack is exactly what you need. It focuses entirely on bringing that specific visual flair to your game with zero hassle.

How It Works

Wear any cape, hold an iron sword, and sneak for over 1.5 seconds to make a fountain.

That's literally it! Just go to your character creator, equip whichever cape you like best, make sure you have a standard vanilla iron sword actively held in your hand, and press your crouch button. Once you hold that sneaking position for just a little bit over a second and a half, the spectacular Dark Fountain particle effect will burst into existence right around you.

Key Features & Details:

To make sure you know exactly what you are getting with this pack, here is a detailed breakdown of how it runs:

- Load it in Global Resources: For the absolute best and most convenient experience, you only need to load this pack directly into your Global Resources in the main menu settings. Once it is activated there, you are completely good to go.

- Anywhere, Anytime: Because it works through your Global Resources, you have the ultimate freedom to open a Dark Fountain wherever you want. Whether you are in your own single-player survival world, visiting a friend's realm, or just exploring, you can trigger the fountain anytime and anywhere.

- 100% Achievement Safe: Are you worried about losing your survival progress or getting locked out of your Xbox Live achievements? Don't be! Applying this pack does not affect achievements in any way, shape, or form. You can keep grinding for your completionist goals while looking incredibly cool.

- No Behavior Pack Required: This is entirely client-side and visual. There is absolutely no behavior pack required to make the mechanics work.

- No Cheats Needed: You do not need to go into your world settings and toggle "Enable Cheats" on. It works perfectly in strict, vanilla, cheat-free survival worlds.

- Pure Particle Effect (No Function): Please keep in mind that this is purely an aesthetic upgrade. The fountain is a 100% pure particle effect. It has absolutely no functional features, it doesn't change your gameplay, it doesn't give you buffs, and it doesn't teleport you to any Dark Worlds. It is simply there to give you that awesome, dramatic visual effect whenever you want to strike a pose.
