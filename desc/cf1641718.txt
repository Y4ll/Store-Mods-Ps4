The Flight Model

Speed

Cruise up to 28 B/S. Go above Y200 and she opens up to 34 B/S. Climbing costs speed. Diving gives it back. That's the whole game.

Takeoff

You need a real runway. 70 blocks of roll before she rotates. Concrete runs smooth, dirt fights you, A 100 Runway blocks long is the bare minimum.

Limits

Slow + nose high = stall. Hold the stall = spin. Dive past 37 B/S = hull eating damage. Fly smart.

Controls

Ailerons, elevators, rudder, flaps - all live, all on your screen moving with your inputs.

Landing Grades

Every touchdown gets scored: BUTTER, NICE, DECENT, BAD, TERRIBLE. Descent rate, speed, pitch, roll, surface. Land like trash and the game says it. Land clean.

The Systems Grind

Fuel

Full tank = five Fuel Canisters at +20% each. One craft gives two cans now (I buffed it). In a pinch, coal works - +5% per piece, caps at half a tank.

Hull

Hard landings, rough strips, wall hits - it all costs hull. Repair Wrench fixes +25% a use, 50 uses per wrench. Hit 0% and the engine dies mid-flight. Don't hit 0%.

Retrieval

Crouch-hit the plane 3 times - packs into an item with fuel, hull, paint saved. Costs 15% hull each time, so land it first like a normal person.

How You Fly It

Mode 1 - Click Controls

Full hotbar setup. Ignition with a live icon, throttle in 10% steps, taxi/takeoff/pushback modes, parking brake with a run-up boost trick.

Mode 2 - W / S Controls

Console and PC players, this is your mode. Throttle lives on W and S while you fly. Cleanest way to play, bar none.

Camera and HUD

Chase cam that tracks where you look. 4 HUD styles you flip by hand. Settings menu got a full glow-up for 2.0.0.
