Minecraft But Every Block You Break Explodes:

When you break a block in the game, it will instantly explode, there's no timer, it will just instantly explode.

Easy, Medium, And Hard Mode:

There's 3 modes on this Addon, easy, medium, and hard mode.

Easy:

In easy mode, the explosion is weak but still dangerous.

Medium:

In medium mode, the explosion is stronger now so getting very near at a block after you break it will kill you.

Hard:

In hard mode, the explosion is very strong so even if you are far away from the block, it will still kill you.

Works with modded items:

This Addon also works with modded items!! So if you want to use this with mods or addons, you can now!!
