Notice:

- To drive, first execute the command /function setting.

- Build with powered rails.

- All experiments must be enabled (except "New Trade and Recipes").

- MCA Addon is required for control.

Train Controls (Notch System)

This addon uses a notch control system, allowing you to precisely manage speed and braking in steps, just like in a real train.

- Not P (Power) Each use increases the power notch, making the train accelerate. Use repeatedly to reach your desired speed.

- Not B (Brake) Each use increases the brake notch, slowing the train down. Use it when approaching stations or curves.

- Not N (Neutral) Sets the notch to neutral, maintaining your current speed without further acceleration or braking. Ideal for long straight sections or speed limits.

- Reverse Reverses the train's travel direction. Important: Do not use while moving! Bring the train to a complete stop first.

Functional

- Sit anywhere place a seat anywhere you like. (may not work on 1.18+)

- Movement drive the train along powered rails.

- Door control choose a side and open/close doors.

Door control

- Choose the side you want to open (OC Left / OC Right).

- Use the Doors Open / Close button. (deprecated, use new mtm version)
