This addon makes any door you place underwater automatically create an air pocket inside it. If youre mining or exploring the ocean and need a quick spot to breathe, this addon will make things a lot easier.

Its simple to use: just place a door underwater, and the water inside the door will disappear, turning into an air pocket. This works with all vanilla doors.

Note: if you're using custom doors from other addons, make sure their item ID ends with "_door" so the air-pocket feature works properly.
