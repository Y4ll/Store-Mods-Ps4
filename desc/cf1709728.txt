Installation

- Open the .mcaddon file.

- In your world, activate True Yamato [BP]. The resource pack is added automatically.

- Get the sword by crafting it or with /give @s ytaun:yamato_katana.

Crafting: Netherite Ingot, Amethyst Shard, Netherite Ingot / Gold Block, Iron Sword, Gold Block / Iron Block (bottom middle)

Requires Minecraft Bedrock 26.10 or newer.
