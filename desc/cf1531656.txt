Mini Me Pixel Pack
Build sub-block pixel art with rainbow placer wands, stamps and a paint brush then shrink yourself to walk among your tiny creations.

A whimsical Minecraft Bedrock add-on that lets you zap yourself to flea size and decorate the world with tiny 1/16-block "pixels" in eleven colours.

What's in the pack

- Shrink Ray turns mobs into babies, or shrinks YOU to tiny size

- Grow Ray restores baby mobs back to adults (works on every shrinkable mob, even zombies)

- Pixel Placer Wand places tiny coloured pixels exactly where you point

- 11 Pixel Colours green, blue, red, yellow, pink, orange, purple, cyan, white, black, and a glowing torch yellow

- Colour Picker cycles colours; the wand's gem auto-changes to match

- Chisel the only thing that can break placed pixels (or your bare fists when you're shrunk!)

- Stamps heart, smiley, tree and star: one-click pre-made pixel patterns

- Paint Brush toggle continuous painting; pixels stream out wherever you aim

- Tiny Steve avatar when you shrink, your body becomes invisible and a miniature Steve takes your place; the camera drops to his eye height so the world looks giant

- Tiny powers speed, jump-boost, slow-falling and a sparkle trail while shrunk

- Crafting recipes playable in survival

How to use each tool

Shrink Ray point at any mob and right-click to shrink it to its baby form. To shrink yourself, look straight up at the sky and right-click your body vanishes, a tiny Steve takes your place, and the camera drops down to his eye level. Look up + right-click again to grow back to normal.

Grow Ray point at a baby mob and right-click to turn it back into an adult.

Pixel Placer Wand right-click on a block face to place a tiny pixel. You can also right-click on existing pixels to stack more next to them. A small ghost cube shows you exactly where the next pixel will land.

Colour Picker right-click to cycle to the next colour. The wand in your hotbar auto-swaps to a variant with a matching gem so you always know what colour you're placing.

Chisel hold it and left-click any pixel to pop it. When you're shrunk, your bare fists do the same job.

Stamps (Heart / Smiley / Tree / Star) pick the colour you want with the Colour Picker, then right-click a stamp at a wall or floor. A whole pre-made pattern of pixels appears in that colour.

Paint Brush right-click once to turn paint mode ON, then just hold the brush and aim pixels stream out continuously where you look (about 10 per second). Right-click again to turn it OFF.

Torch / Glow Pixels cycle the Colour Picker until you reach the torch colour. Pixels placed in this colour glow bright yellow.

Crafting recipes

- Shrink Ray redstone (top), iron diamond iron (middle), stick (bottom)

- Grow Ray glowstone dust (top), iron emerald iron (middle), stick (bottom)

- Pixel Placer emerald + 2 sticks placed diagonally

- Chisel iron ingot above a stick

- Colour Picker redstone above a stick

The wand, chisel, picker, stamps and rays also appear in the Equipment tab in Creative.

Installation

- Download the MiniMe_by_ChocAndRoll.mcaddon file

- Double-click it Minecraft Bedrock will import the behaviour and resource packs automatically

- When creating a world (or in an existing world's settings), apply both Mini Me BP and Mini Me RP

- Enable the "Beta APIs" experiment if your version requires it (the mod uses Bedrock's scripting API)
