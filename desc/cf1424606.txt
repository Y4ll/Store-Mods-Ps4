This gun add-on provides military-based gun variants.
To obtain guns in Survival mode, you must craft them first.
The main material for each gun is iron ingots; you can find the rest of the ingredients in the crafting table.
You will also need dye or leather for some guns.

Gun List:

- Revolver

- Compact Pistol

- Beretta M9

- Colt M1911

- SIG Sauer M17

- Rifle

- Hunting Rifle

- Assault Rifle

- Sniper Rifle
