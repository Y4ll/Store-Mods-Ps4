The Glass Connections Add-on connects all Minecraft glass block textures, prioritizing optimization and visual comfort when using this complement in your world. Simply place any glass block in your world, and it will automatically connect to adjacent glass blocks!

- ACHIEVIMENT FRIENDLY!

- Support for ACTIONS and STUFF!

- Compatible with Vibrant Visuals!

- Extremely optimized.

- Fast loading!
