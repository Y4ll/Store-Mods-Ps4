The Disturbed

Something is watching you.

You may not see it at first.

You may only hear strange sounds in the distance. A noise that doesn't belong there. Something moving nearby when nobody should be around.

Then...

You see it.

And before you have time to understand what happened, it's gone.

Features

- Advanced AI focused on stalking and psychological horror.

- Sudden and unpredictable appearances.

- Disturbing and bizarre sounds during gameplay.

- Two mysterious entities with different behaviors.

- A gray-faced entity that watches, stalks, and frightens you without directly attacking.

- A red entity that is far more aggressive and dangerous.

- Unpredictable encounters designed to keep you constantly on edge.

- A horror experience focused on atmosphere, tension, and the fear of being watched.

---

Two Faces

The Gray Entity

It doesn't seem interested in killing you.

Instead...

It watches.

You may find it standing in the distance.

You may hear something nearby and turn around just in time to see it disappear.

Sometimes it may simply appear where it shouldn't be.

Its purpose isn't always to attack.

Sometimes, making you afraid is enough.

---

The Red Entity

The other one is different.

More aggressive.

More dangerous.

Where the gray entity prefers to observe...

The red entity may decide to act.

When it appears, you may not have much time to wonder why.

---

How It Works

As you play normally, strange events can begin occurring around you.

A disturbing sound.

An unexpected appearance.

A figure standing somewhere in the distance.

You never know exactly when it will happen.

The entities don't constantly reveal themselves, allowing silence and uncertainty to become part of the experience.

Sometimes you'll see nothing.

Sometimes you'll hear something.

And sometimes...

You'll turn around at exactly the wrong moment.

Recommended Experience

For the intended experience:

- Use headphones.

- Keep the volume turned up.

- Play without spoilers.

- Explore during the night.

- Never ignore strange sounds.

Disclaimer

Not every appearance is an attack.

Not every sound means immediate danger.

Eventually, you'll start wondering whether you're imagining it.

And that is exactly when you should be worried.
