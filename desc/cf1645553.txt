Verity Skins

Step into a strange and unsettling world with Verity Skins, a horror-themed Minecraft skin pack inspired by creepy smiles, yellow faces, and mysterious characters.

The skins combine simple smiley-style faces with dark, unusual outfits to create a disturbing but unique look. Some characters appear almost friendly at first, while others feel much more corrupted and eerie.

Features

Creepy yellow smile-inspired skins
Horror and mystery-themed designs
Different outfits and character styles
Great for horror maps and roleplay
Perfect for SMPs and multiplayer
Disturbing but recognizable visual style

Whether you want to look strange, unsettling, or completely different from normal players, Verity Skins gives your character a mysterious horror-inspired appearance.
