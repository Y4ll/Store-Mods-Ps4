Keep your base a complete secret with Phase Blocks, Pass through blocks that know one else knows about! Keep all your items safe behind a secret wall!

Phase Blocks allows you to keep your base hidden away from everyone else.

As an extra security measure you could even add a hidden room inside your secret base just incase someone follows you home. The perfect place to hide all the loot you want to keep safe.

Craft a Phase Table and you will be able to turn any block into a Phase Block, they will look exactly the same as the vanilla block but you will be able to phase through them to get to your base.

Place any block on the Phase Table to reduce it's mass allowing you to phase through it.

-Works with Texture Packs-

This add-on DOES NOT require experimental features.

The player.json IS NOT used in this add-on
