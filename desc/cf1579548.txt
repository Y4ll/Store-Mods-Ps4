Bring a Java-style Freecam experience to Minecraft Bedrock Edition!

This add-on lets you leave a player dummy behind while freely exploring your world in spectator mode. Perfect for building, recording videos, inspecting terrain, checking redstone contraptions, and taking screenshots without moving your character.

Features

Java-style Freecam system

Use /freecam or /fc to toggle Freecam

Leaves a player-sized dummy behind

Dummy displays your player name

Automatically returns you to your original location

Restores your previous gamemode when exiting

Return instantly by toggling Freecam again

If the dummy is damaged, you are automatically returned

Designed for survival, creative, and adventure mode worlds

Multiplayer compatible

How It Works

- Use /freecam or /fc

- A dummy is left behind where you activated Freecam

- You are switched into spectator mode

- Fly around and explore freely

- Use the command again to return

If your dummy is damaged while in Freecam, you will automatically return to your body.

Uses

- Building large structures

- Cinematic screenshots

- Video recording

- Base inspections

- Terrain scouting

- Redstone debugging

- Server administration

Notes

This add-on is designed to recreate the feel of Java Edition Freecam as closely as possible within Bedrock Editions add-on system.

You can add custom skins to the dummy based on name using this pack https://mcpedl.com/java-like-freecam-custom-dummy-skins/
