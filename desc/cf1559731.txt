Wield the legendary blade of Vergil the Yamato.
A katana forged from pure demon steel, capable of cutting through the boundary between the human world and the demon world itself.

This addon adds the Yamato Katana (ytaun:yamato_katana) to Minecraft Bedrock Edition, bringing DMC3-inspired combat mechanics powered by the Script API.

THE BLADE

The Yamato is not just a weapon it is an extension of will.
Every swing, every technique, every movement echoes the cold precision of Sparda's son.

Item ID: ytaun:yamato_katana
Requires: Behavior Pack + Resource Pack

SKILLS

YAMATO BARRIER Passive
"A warrior of my caliber does not fall so easily."
When struck, the Yamato's barrier absorbs 60% of incoming damage and restores health equal to the absorbed amount. Has a 15-second cooldown. A ring of spectral particles erupts on activation.

JUDGMENT CUT Sneak + Attack
"Jackpot."
Queue a Judgment Cut by pressing Sneak, then swing your blade. A phantom slash erupts forward in a 66 cone slicing through all enemies within 8 blocks for 18 damage. Enhanced during Devil Trigger.

DOPPELGANGER Sneak + Use (Right-Click)
"Your motivation is admirable... but misguided."
Summon a phantom shadow of yourself. Every attack deals an additional 50% bonus damage from your spectral clone. Grants Speed III + Glowing for 5 seconds. 10-second cooldown.

DEVIL TRIGGER Sneak + Jump
"Might controls everything. And without strength, you cannot protect anything."
Unleash Vergil's true demonic power. Grants Strength IV, Resistance II, Speed II, and Regeneration II for 10 seconds. Every hit deals +8 bonus damage. A spiral of violet energy surrounds you on activation. 30-second cooldown.

HUD

A real-time action bar HUD appears while holding the Yamato, displaying cooldown progress bars for all 4 abilities with live countdowns (in seconds).

REQUIREMENTS

Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21+
Experimental: Beta APIs enabled
Script API module: @minecraft/server
Both Behavior Pack AND Resource Pack required

INSTALLATION

1. Download the .mcaddon file
2. Open with Minecraft both packs will import automatically
3. Create or load a world
4. Enable "Beta APIs" under Experiments
5. Apply both packs
6. Craft or obtain the Yamato Katana (ytaun:yamato_katana)
7. Hold it and master the art of the blade
