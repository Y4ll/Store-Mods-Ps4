Rise and Survive is a progression-based apocalypse addon where zombies grow stronger every day.

Evolving Zombies & Growing Difficulty

- A dynamic difficulty bar increases every day, boosting zombie spawn rates.

- Every 8 days, zombies move faster, grow stronger, and may unlock new abilities.

- Higher difficulty = higher chance of zombies spawning with armor and weapons.

Unique Zombie Classes - Each zombie type brings new threats, new behaviors, and new strategies:

- Miner Zombies break blocks to reach you (faster on higher difficulty).

- Block Placer Zombies tower up, bridge, and chase you no matter how high you build.

- Witch Zombies throw and drink potions.

- Bomber Zombies throw TNT (damage-only).

- Ender Zombies teleport using ender pearls.

- Jumpers leap at you and onto blocks.

- Crawlers crawl through small spaces.

- Revealers scream to expose your location, then flee.

- Spitter - shoots spit from a distance.

Horde Leaders

- Giant jumps and summons fangs.

- Tank incredibly tough.

- Necromancer summons more zombies.

- Zombies can also spawn with various potion effects like Speed or Fire Resistance.

- All vanilla hostile mobs are disabled, but their loot is obtainable by killing zombies.

Horde & Blood Moon System

- Bloodmoon occurs every 8 days

- Hordes spawn randomly 15 blocks away, always aware of your location.

- Horde size scales with difficulty (customizable in settings).

Blood Moon:

- Sleep disabled

- 100% chance of 12 horde events

- Zombies always know where you are

World Interaction

- Using horns or bells attracts zombies instantly.

- Taking damage releases blood scent (detectable up to 10 blocks).

- Faster Java-style regeneration included.

- Craft-able gunpowder (coal + iron nugget).

- High quality structures with loot.

Infection System

- Zombies can infect players on hit.

- Infection chance customizable (0100%).

- Infected players will collapse after the timer.

- Golden Apple = the only cure.

Visual Enhancements

- Blood screen effects (with compatible visual packs).

- New zombie skins & animations (Fresh Animations inspired).

- Realistic block-breaking particles.

- Glowing eye effects when using Vibrant Visuals.

New Blocks & Defenses

- Wired Fence

- Cheval Fence

- Wired Wall

- Chain fence (they can't break it until difficulty: 75.0)

- Spiked Plate

- Spiked Fence (zombies cant break the reinforced one)

- These blocks damage zombies and help control hordes.

New Weapons

- Bolo chops wood

- Katana fast slashes

- Dagger rapid attacks

- Saber huge sweeping damage

- Longsword wide sweep radius

- Hammer powerful crit hits

Rotten flesh becomes useful:

- Raw Flesh Cooked Flesh Leather

If you want a hardcore, cinematic, mechanically deep zombie apocalypse, this is the add-on youve been searching for. Everything evolves. Everything hunts you. Every day is harder than the last.
