DONT CRY+

Dont Cry+ is a Minecraft Bedrock horror add-on designed to make your world feel like something is always watching you.

This add-on combines multiple horror encounters into one pack while keeping their unique behaviors, animations, sounds, and stalking mechanics.

FEATURES

- Multiple terrifying stalker encounters

- Creatures that can appear outside your windows

- Random and unpredictable appearances

- Hostile entities that can chase the player

- Different behaviors and horror events

- Custom animations

- Horror sounds and atmosphere

- Randomized encounters so you never know when something will appear

- Made for Minecraft Bedrock Edition

You may be exploring, mining, building your house, or simply looking through a window when something appears.

Sometimes it watches.

Sometimes it waits.

And sometimes... it comes after you.

IMPORTANT

For the best experience, play in Survival mode with your sound turned up.

Don't Cry. Don't look away. And whatever you do... don't let it find you.
