Do you have trouble replanting your crops? Want to easily replant? Try this add-on! This add-on can be easily re-planted!

If you destroy a crop, it will automatically replant it.

When you destroy a crop with a hoe, the surrounding (3x3) crops are automatically replanted.

This will make it easier to replant crops.
