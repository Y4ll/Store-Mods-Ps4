Advance Tactical Gear : Modular System is a modular tactical loadout system addon for Minecraft Bedrock. Every piece of gear is built to be customized. Swap attachments, manage battery life. Adjust your protection setup one part at a time.

Modular Helmet

Seven attachment categories: Battery Pack, Face Shield, Gas Mask, Goggles, Headset, Lights and NVG (GPNVG-18 or PVS-31). Every slot can be set up independently using the loadout menu.

Modular Plate Carrier

Six attachment categories: Mag Pouch, Radio, Medkit, Back Panel, Utility and Other. Each category has real options. Open the loadout menu and mix and match without ever leaving your inventory.

Functional NVG System

This is not a cosmetic goggle model. It runs on a battery. NVGs drain power over 60 minutes of active use. Recharge instantly with an NVG Battery item. Flip the visor up or down on demand (Sneak + Jump). Ingame battery percentage readout is available while active. Full Features mode adds a realtime glow overlay that can be seen in firstperson while the NVG is down and powered

Real Damage Protection

The plate carrier and helmet are not visual. They actively absorb damage, from melee, projectiles and explosions. The protection stacks up to a capped maximum so it remains meaningful and does not turn you into invincibility.

This pack ships with two selectable subpacks so it fits your setup

Compatible Mode

Doesn't have NVG overlay

Safe to run alongside other addons all armor, attachment, battery, and protection systems work identically

Full Features

Have NVG overlay

May conflict with other addons that also modify the player.json

How to use:

-

- Equip the Plate Carrier or Helmet

- Use the matching Loadout Tool item to open the configuration menu

- Pick your setup from each category dropdown changes apply instantly

- For NVGs: equip a GPNVG-18 or PVS-31 in the Helmet's NVG slot, then Sneak + Jump to flip the visor down
