The add-on also adds Dead Leaves, which spawn randomly on trees, all trees now have their own variant of leaf litter!

The Add-on also adds 2 new WEAPON TYPES and 2 new TOOL SETS

Scythes: Scythes deal damage overtime, up to 4 more than the sword it came from, however since it deals damage overtime, its less effective than a sword against enemies with armor

Pikes: Pikes have an interactive ability via right-click that deals a ton of damage to anything 3-4 blocks in front of you, however, its only 3-4 blocks in front of you, nothing in-between, so anything directly in front of you wont be affected

Resin-Dipped Items: Resin Dipped items are upgraded versions of iron, they do as much damage as diamond items but the durability is the exact same as iron:
