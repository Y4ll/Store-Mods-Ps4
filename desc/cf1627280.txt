Skyfall Visuals is a simple, smooth lighting pack for Minecraft Bedrock. It keeps the vanilla look and only cleans up the lighting behind it.

What this changes

Foliage and grass

Grass and leaves get a small lift so they look healthy instead of dull, without going neon. Fields stay dense and green across every biome.

Sky and clouds

Clean blue sky with soft wispy clouds. They stay light and thin instead of turning into hard blocks overhead.

Water

Oceans and lakes go deep blue and reflect the sky above them. Shallow water stays clear enough to see the floor through it.

Cherry groves

Pink petal ground cover under the blossom trees, kept soft so it does not fight with the rest of the world.

Villages

Warm, cozy light on village houses and farmhouses. Nothing dramatic, just enough to make them feel lived in.

Point lighting

Every light source gives off its own colored light instead of everything glowing the same orange.

- Redstone torch glows red

- Soul torch and soul lantern glow cyan

- Lantern glows warm orange

- Campfire glows orange

PBR

This pack comes with PBR, so blocks react to light with real depth and ores glow on their own underground.

PBR is also the heaviest part of any Vibrant Visuals pack, so it is not forced on you. It ships as a subpack, which means you can switch it on or off yourself. When you activate Skyfall Visuals in Global Resources, pick the version you want. Turn PBR off if your device runs hot or drops frames.

Requirements

THIS PACK IS FOR VIBRANT VISUALS ONLY
IT DOES NOT WORK WITH MB LOADER

Vibrant Visuals must be ON. This pack is built for Bedrock's Vibrant Visuals renderer. The pack will not do anything if it is off.

Where to turn it on: Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals

If that toggle is not there, your device or version does not support Vibrant Visuals yet.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Skyfall Visuals

- Pick the subpack you want. PBR on for the full look, PBR off for better performance.

- Turn on Settings > Video > Vibrant Visuals

- If the lighting still looks the same, leave the world and load it again once
