VERSION 3.1 FULLY OVERHAULED

New progression system, four unique origins, clan bloodlines, nine cursed techniques with ultimates, and a brand new item system. This is the biggest Sorcerers Beyond update yet.

PROGRESSION & LEVELING

Earn Cursed Energy XP by fighting hostile mobs, exorcising curses, and using abilities in combat. Maximum level cap: Level 30.

Key Milestones:

-

Level 5 Minor passive physical boosts

-

Level 8 Clan Admissions unlocked. Open your Jujutsu Manual to join Zenin or Kamo

-

Level 10 Technique Awakening. Permanently select your Innate Cursed Technique (options depend on your Origin and Clan)

-

Level 20+ Ultimate Abilities unlocked. Charge and unleash Domain Expansions, Maximum Techniques, or Awakenings

-

Level 30 Peak Jujutsu. Maximum base stats and CE reserves. Exclusive techniques like Angel's Technique become available

THE FOUR ORIGINS

Choose your origin when you first spawn. This choice is permanent and dictates your entire playstyle.

Modern Jujutsu Sorcerer (Cursed Energy)
The standard and most versatile path. Start as a clanless sorcerer with full access to Cursed Energy.

-

Level 8 Join Zenin or Kamo clan

-

Level 10 Technique Pool Boogie Woogie - Divergent Fist - Cursed Speech - Yuta's Katana (+ clan technique if applicable)

Zenin Outcast (Heavenly Restriction)
Born with zero Cursed Energy. In exchange, your body is pushed to absolute physical perfection.

-

Permanent Speed + Strength + Resistance + Jump Boost

-

Completely immune to guaranteed-hit effects of Domain Expansions

-

No Cursed Techniques or Domains relies entirely on physical combat and Cursed Tools

Incarnated Heian Sorcerer
An ancient sorcerer reborn in the modern era. You do not regenerate power naturally you must hunt and consume Cursed Relics dropped by hostile mobs and JJK curses.

-

Level 10 Technique Pool Idle Transfiguration - Sky Manipulation - Cursed Energy Discharge

-

Consume 5 Cursed Relics + reach 20 CE Output unlock Cursed Spirit Manipulation

-

Level 30 unlock Angel's Technique

Cursed Spirit
A manifestation of negative human emotion. Power scales heavily off Cursed Energy Output.

-

Weakness Takes massive, fatal damage from Reverse Cursed Technique (RCT) output

-

Level 10 Technique Pool Idle Transfiguration - Cursed Speech - Cursed Energy Discharge

CLANS & BLOODLINES

-

Zenin Clan (Join at Level 8) Adds Projection Sorcery to your technique pool

-

Kamo Clan (Join at Level 8) Adds Blood Manipulation to your technique pool

-

Cursed Womb: Death Painting Eat a Death Painting item before Level 10 to mutate your biology and instantly gain access to Blood Manipulation

CURSED TECHNIQUES

Hit Level 10 to permanently select your technique. Sneak to cycle moves | Right-click to cast.
To charge your Ultimate, deal 100 damage (50 hearts). A tracker appears on screen and alerts you when ready.

Blood Manipulation
Control blood for immense offense and defense. Taking damage builds Blood Heat when full, gain massive Speed + Strength buffs.
Moves: Piercing Blood - Supernova - Blood Edge
Ultimate: Awakening Slicing Exorcism

Projection Sorcery
Move at blinding, hyper-fast speeds and trap enemies in 2D space during your frames.
(If you suffocate inside a wall due to speed, the game automatically teleports you to safety)
No Ultimate all abilities unlock immediately at Level 10. Infinite stacking system.

Boogie Woogie
Instantly swap positions with any entity by clapping.
No Ultimate all abilities unlock immediately at Level 10
