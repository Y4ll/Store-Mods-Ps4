Venom is an alien creature from the planet Klentar. There are 3 symbiotes in the addon.The addon even gives you the ability to turn into symbiotes with cool abilities.

The addon contains 3 symbiotes: Venom, Riot, and Carnage.

And 3 carriers: Eddie Brock, Carlton Drake, Clessedus Cassidy.

You can interact with each of the symbiotes.

When each character is killed, he will turn into a symbiotic version, after defeating it, a symbiote will crawl out of him, which must be destroyed in order to gain the abilities of the symbiote.
