Overview

Leave Earth behind.

Wan's Moon Dimension brings a fully procedural Moon to Minecraft Bedrock, combined with cinematic space travel and immersive gameplay systems.

Build, explore, and survive in a completely new environment designed to feel alien, desolate, and real.

Gameplay Systems

Low Gravity

- Enhanced jump and slow falling for players and entities

Footprints

- Dynamic footprints based on movement and direction

Lunar Mobs

- Astronaut variants (Zombie & Creeper)

Commands

Emergency Return

/moodim:return
Teleport back to your original dimension if something breaks.

Spawn Lunar Mobs

/moodim:spawnmobs
Spawn astronaut mobs around you (Moon only).
