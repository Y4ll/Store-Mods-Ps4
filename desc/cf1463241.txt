Addon Uang Rupiah Indonesia MCPE | Real Money IDR

Bring the official currency of Indonesia into your world! This addon adds 7 rupiah denominations that you can use in your Minecraft world.

Perfect for roleplay, economy servers, or just flexing your wealth in multiplayer with that authentic Indonesian vibe!

What's New in This Update?

- New Texture! : Fresh and hyper-detailed textures! Looks way more like the real deal now.

- Varied Sizes : Just like IRL100k is the biggest and 1k is the smallest. No more one-size-fits-all!

- Stackable! : You can actually stack your money now. Time to build that money mountain!

- New Animations : Holding money feels way more dynamic and natural.

- Money ASMR : Added custom paper sounds! Its super satisfying to hear while youre counting your cash.

Cool Features

- Authentic Look: 7 different denominations with textures that look just like real Indonesian Rupiah.

- Spawnable: All cash can be spawned in your world as entities.

- Easy Pickup: Once spawned, just tap and hold or right-click to pick up your money!
