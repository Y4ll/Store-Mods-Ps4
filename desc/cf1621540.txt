((This pack only affects background music))

Transform your Minecraft world into a surreal, nostalgic, and slightly unsettling space. This resource pack completely replaces the default background music with a curated soundtrack inspired by dreamcore, weirdcore, and liminal space aesthetics.

The music that will be played is only of a very similar aesthetic, because the most popular songs are copyrighted and I am not allowed to add them to my pack.

All the songs will be in a .txt file that gives credit to these authors, even though they were royalty-free songs.
