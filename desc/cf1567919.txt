Project Description:

The Actual Vein Miner Add-On significantly speeds up your survival gameplay by allowing you to mine an entire ore vein by breaking just a single block. Instead of tedious block-by-block harvesting, breaking any connected ore triggers a chain reaction that instabreaks the entire cluster. It reduces early-game grind, saves tool durability management time, and seamlessly integrates into vanilla mechanics.

Important Information:

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Real-time multiple block breaking.

- Sneak toggle on & off system.

- Vein guide for multiple settings.

- Behaviour & Resource packs are both required for the project to work.

- Have something to say? Join the Discord.

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

Sneak while holding a pickaxe to activate the vein miner ability and sneak again to turn it off before you start mining standard stone.

Once the Vein Miner is active, you will be able to mine any matching ores within range all at once, triggering a recursive chain reaction that instantly shatters the entire connected vein.

Craft a comprehensive Vein Miner Settings book to effortlessly navigate and customize your ore-harvesting options.

Using the Vein Miner Settings book, you can easily toggle XP drops and Mixed Veins on and off to customize how you want to play the add-on.
