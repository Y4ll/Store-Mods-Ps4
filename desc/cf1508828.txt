Phases

Phase 0

- The unknown worm could appear naturally

Phase 1

- The mob called parasite will appear naturally

- Parasites need 500 evolution points to reach this phase

Phase 2

- Infected parasites will be generated naturally

- Infected mutations will be able to generate growing flesh every time they kill 10 mobs

- Parasites need 2,000 evolution points to reach this phase

Phase 3

- Infected mutations can be generated naturally

- When the parasites die they will have a chance of a nucleus appearing

- Infected parasites will have a small chance to spawn with red particles that give them more health and do more damage.

- Parasites need 10,000 evolution points to reach this phase

- When a parasite kills a creature, the creature won't drop anything

Phase 4

- Mutated parasites will appear naturally

- Infected mutations will have a small chance to spawn with red particles that give them more health and do more damage.

- Parasites need 60,000 evolution points to reach this phase

Phase 5

- Fleshy parasites will appear naturally

- Mutated parasites will have a small chance to spawn with red particles that give them more health and do more damage.

- Parasites need 200,000 evolution points to reach this phase

Phase 6

- Meat bomb will appear naturally

- Fleshy parasites will have a small chance to spawn with red particles that give them more health and do more damage.

- Parasites need 3,000,000 evolution points to reach this phase

Phase 7

- Parasites will ignore light levels to occur naturally.

- Parasites need 20,000,000 evolution points to reach this phase.

Phase 8

- The hunter will appear naturally.

Every time they change phases the game sends a message to the players
