Welcome to TerraSphere, the ultimate world generation overhaul for Minecraft Bedrock Edition! This addon completely reimagines the default Minecraft terrain engine, transforming your blocky worlds into breathtaking, lifelike ecosystems.

If you are tired of flat landscapes and predictable terrain, TerraSphere is here to bring real-world geography right into your game.

Key Features

Expanded World Height Limit: Experience massive, towering mountain ranges that pierce the sky, utilizing the maximum world height limit for unparalleled verticality.

Hyper-Dense Forests: Explore deep, thick, and immersive woodlands. The forests are packed with rich vegetation, fallen logs, and realistic canopy structures that make survival truly challenging and beautiful.

Real-Life River Systems: No more disconnected puddles! TerraSphere introduces realistic, winding river networks that carve through valleys, connecting lakes and oceans just like in real life.

Breathtaking Valleys & Landscapes: Smooth transitions between biomes, deeper ravines, and realistic erosion patterns that make every viewpoint look like a photograph.

How to Install & Play

Download the .mcaddon or .mcpack file.

Open the file to automatically import it into Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

Create a new world or go to your existing world settings.

Activate TerraSphere under both Resource Packs and Behavior Packs.
