This addon adds all the mobs that lost the votes in the old BobiVotes from Bobicraft.

Encloser

End Shrimp

When he's angry, he destroys everything in his path.

End Squid

A walking beacon.

Absorbs the effects of any potion you throw at it.

Giant Birch

Wake him up with a dandelion.

It destroys logs in its path.

Hasp

Use the hasp wings in your offhand to get a boost when using elytra.

Use the hasp stinger in your offhand to inflict poison on the mobs you attack.

Hay Golem

Summon it by placing a carved pumpkin on top of a hay block.

When you get too close to this mob, it will grab you and throw you towards the nearest hay bale.
