Arabic Font & RTL Fix

A high-quality Minecraft Bedrock addon that fully repairs Arabic text rendering across chat, menus, UI, and gameplay. Fixes disconnected Arabic letters, reversed sentences, and broken formatting by adding proper Right-To-Left (RTL) support and an optimized Arabic font for a smooth and readable experience. Designed to make Arabic writing look natural and clean inside Minecraft.

Features:

Fixes broken Arabic letters

Correct Right-To-Left text rendering

Improved Arabic chat readability

Better Arabic UI and menu support

Lightweight and compatible with most addons

Optimized for Minecraft Bedrock Edition
