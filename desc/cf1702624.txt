Verxhade Client is a clean PvP pack. It helps you fight and practice better with clear menus, helpful screen info, fast kit loaders, arena map spawn, and smart training bots.

Features:

Durability Tracker

Item Counter

Player Radar

Kill & Death (K/D) Tracker

Killstreak Tracker

Hit Damage Indicator

Fight & Potion Timer

Crystal-Style Health Hearts

Clean Armor Shield Icons

Golden Food Icons

1-Pixel Crosshair

Clear Menus & Chests

No Dark Background

4 Bot Difficulties

Practice Dummy

Bot Enhancements Switch

5 Custom Kit Slots

Save & Load Inventory

Ready-to-Use Kits (Sword, Bow, Mace, Spear, Crystal, Anchor, Soup)

Auto-Enchanter

Two-Player Spawns

Arena Map Spawner & Reset

How to Open the Menu

- Triple-Sneak: Tap your sneak button 3 times quickly.

- Heavy Core: Hold a Heavy Core and click or tap your screen.

- Chat Command: Type /scriptevent verxhade:toggle in chat.

"Turn on Beta APIs in World Settings under Experiment Tab(Optional if non problems occur)"
