HOW TO INSTALL

- Download this skin pack.

- Rename the file extension from ".mcpack" to ".zip".

- Launch Minecraft on your device.

- Download the Cute Kitty HD Skin Pack from the marketplace to generate the cache.

- Clear out all existing folders inside your premium_cache/skin_packs directory.

- Unzip this file and move the extracted folder (named "Vague Memories") into that premium_cache/skin_packs folder.

- Restart Minecraft to apply the changes.

- Have fun with your new aesthetic look!
