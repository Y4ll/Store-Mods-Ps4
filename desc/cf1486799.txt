Iron Golem Girl - Cute 3D Transformation

Ever wanted a more aesthetic and cute protector for your village? The Iron Golem Girl resource pack is here! This pack replaces the vanilla Iron Golem with a beautiful 3D anime-style girl model that still packs a punch!

Features:

-

High-Quality 3D Model: Custom-made anime girl design with smooth textures.

-

Vanilla Friendly: Works in Survival, Creative, and Realms.

-

No Lag: Optimized for mobile (MCPE) and Windows 10/11.

-

Latest Support: Compatible with Minecraft 1.21, 1.22, up to 1.26.

How to Use:

-

Download the .mcpack file.

-

Open it to import automatically to Minecraft.

-

Activate the pack in Global Resources or World Settings.
