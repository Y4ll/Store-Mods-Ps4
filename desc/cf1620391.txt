Simple Danger Sensor

Simple Danger Sensor is a Minecraft Bedrock add-on that automatically detects nearby hostile mobs and warns you in real time. It displays the closest hostile mob, its approximate distance, and a color-based danger indicator to help you react before enemies get too close.

Designed to feel like a natural part of Minecraft, this add-on requires no setup, commands, or custom items. Simply install it and start playing.

How It Works

The add-on continuously scans for nearby hostile mobs around the player. Whenever a hostile mob is detected, the system automatically displays the closest threat along with its approximate distance.

If another hostile mob becomes closer, the warning automatically updates to the new nearest target.

When no hostile mobs are nearby, the warning disappears automatically.

Designed for Survival

Simple Danger Sensor is designed to improve player awareness without changing vanilla gameplay. It does not modify mob AI, combat mechanics, loot tables, or world generation. The add-on simply provides real-time warnings, making exploration, mining, and survival safer while preserving the original Minecraft experience.
