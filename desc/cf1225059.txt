The Weapon Combiner addon revolutionizes Minecraft gameplay by introducing a system that allows players to merge standard weapons with various materials, resulting in enhanced weaponry with unique abilities. Central to this system is the Materia Conflux, a custom crafting block where these combinations are forged.

Players can infuse their weapons with materials like TNT, Redstone, Amethyst, and more, each imparting distinct abilities. For instance:

FEATURES:

- Custom Crafting Block - The Materia Conflux serves as the core for combining weapons with special materials.

- Weapon Infusion System - Combine weapons with materials to unlock special abilities.

- Upgrade Mechanic - Enhance existing special weapons to add new effects.

- Randomized Abilities - Each crafted weapon gains unique abilities from a predefined set.

- Dynamic Gameplay - Custom weapons provide buffs, special attacks, and unique interactions.

How It Works

- Players use the Materia Conflux block to fuse specific materials with weapons.

- Upon fusing, weapons gain unique properties, including passive, active, and on-hit abilities.

- Players can further enhance weapons by adding additional abilities, up to a maximum of six per weapon.

- If a player removes their weapon from the crafting block, the original material is returned.

Combinable Item:

Amethyst Weapons Grants Regeneration on right-click. Duration scales with the base weapon.

Beacon Weapons Provides passive effects: Jump Boost, Speed, Resistance, and Strength.

Camouflage Weapons Enables Invisibility on right-click, with a cooldown.

Crying Weapons Hitting mobs collects tears, granting temporary buffs.

Deepdark Weapons Passively grants Speed and Resistance.

Dragon Egg Weapons Enables flight on right-click.

Ender Pearl Weapons Shoots Ender Pearls on right-click, with a cooldown.

Froglight Weapons Passively provides Jump Boost and Speed.

God Apple Weapons Grants passive effects: Absorption, Regeneration, Resistance, and Fire Resistance.

Magma Weapons Inflicts damage over time when hitting enemies. Intensity scales with the base weapon.

Redstone Weapons Passively increases Speed, based on the base weapon.

Sea Weapons Passively grants **Conduit Power

Spore Weapons Increases damage while poisoned and spreads poison through hits and damage taken.

Clock Weapons Slows enemies over time, can freeze them briefly, and rewinds some taken damage.

Soul Weapons Absorbs souls from enemies to heal and steal health, with souls dropping on hurt or kill.
