Description

Loot Beam adds a cinematic loot beam system to Minecraft Bedrock, making dropped items easier to identify and giving item drops a more rewarding RPG-style appearance.

Each loot rarity has its own unique beam color and visual effect, allowing players to quickly recognize the value of dropped items from a distance. Higher rarities feature stronger and more noticeable effects to make rare loot feel more special.

The beam uses a smooth vertical gradient, remaining brighter and denser near the item while gradually fading toward the top. Additional particles complement the beam without overwhelming the screen, creating a clean but visually impressive effect.

Loot Beam is designed for survival worlds, RPG addons, dungeon maps, adventure maps, boss fights, and any Minecraft world where discovering valuable loot should feel more satisfying.
