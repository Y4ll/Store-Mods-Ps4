Project Description:

Breaking open a lucky block is a chaotic gamble where you never know if you'll be rewarded with legendary loot or shocked by a hostile mob. With bated breath, you smash the glowing golden cube, knowing that the next split second will either grant you a diamond or unleash an angry Creeper.

Important Information:

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Vibrant Visuals will need to be activated for some features.

- Mine to find lucky blocks

- Good and bad items from the blocks.

- Behaviour & Resource packs are both required for the project to work.
