The ambience of Minecraft is pretty empty and lonely, but this resource pack will fill your Minecraft worlds with relaxing ambient sounds and also improves some of the in-game sounds. This is not an addon and can be used in your survival worlds!

If you are wondering how it works, it works by REPLACING some of the sounds that mobs make. They make ambient sounds according to their biomes and time at which they spawn.

Following sounds are made by following mobs:

- Birds chirping: chickens, pigs, cows

- Rustling of leaves: chickens, sheeps, cows

- Plain wind sounds: sheeps

- Mountain wind sounds: goats

- Hawk and desert wind sounds: husks

- Tropical jungle sounds: ocelots

- Owl and ambient night sounds: zombies

- Wolf howl: zombie villagers, wolves

- Water surfing and seagull sounds: turtles

- Water dripping and echoing bat sounds: bats

- Ambient swamp sounds: frogs

Other sounds/improvements:

- Improved rain sounds

- Improved water flowing sounds

- Echoing water and lava drippings sounds from dripstones

- Echoing walking sounds when walked on following blocks:

- stone block

- deepslate

- dripstone block

- You can also disable the scary vanilla cave sounds by downloading the version with no cave sounds!

More info:

- Animals still have some of their respective sounds, not all of their sounds have been replaced.

- Even if you kill the mob which makes sound, the sound will stay until it is finished(some are 2-3 min long).

- Even if you walk on stone in broad daylight, it will still have an echoing sound.
