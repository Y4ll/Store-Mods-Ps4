Sky Drop - Battle Royale Simulator

Sky Drop is a Minecraft Bedrock add-on that brings a fast-paced Battle Royale experience into your world.

Start a match, drop into the arena, collect loot, survive against enemies, and fight to become the last player standing. The add-on includes a full match controller system, spectator mode, player profile stats, air drops, custom fake players, and survival-based gameplay designed to feel like a Battle Royale inside Minecraft.

Features

- Battle Royale match system

- Match start countdown

- Spectator Mode

- Player Profile Status

- Win, match, kill, death, and quit tracking

- Air Drops with useful loot

- Fake players that simulate opponents

- Custom controller menu

- Clean chat during match startup

- Improved survival effects during the countdown

- Manual match cancel/end system

- English and Portuguese support

Gameplay

Use the Sky Drop controller to start or manage a match. Players are prepared during the countdown with temporary effects, then the battle begins. Explore the map, find loot, survive encounters, and eliminate opponents until only one player remains.

The add-on is designed for players who want a Battle Royale-style minigame directly inside Minecraft Bedrock, without needing external servers or complicated setup.
