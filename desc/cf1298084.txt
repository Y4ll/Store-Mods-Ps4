Survival helicopters is an addon for minecraft bedrock and windows (pc) that adds more than 12 different types of helicopters, from civil helicopters to cargo helicopters and defense helicopters. You can create all of them and the mod doesn't use player.json

In this update I added a new helicopter, the uh60 black hawk, and I also made some changes to the crafting and also added a small tilt animation when turning to all helicopters.

The tilt animation in "third person forward" can cause various problems and not play properly, please while flying the helicopters only play in first or second person not in third

In addition, now all helicopters are compatible with deferred shaders (PBR)

Helicopter list

Below the list of helicopters available in this update:

---------- NEW UH1 ----------

The UH1 is a classic helicopter prior to the UH60, with a capacity of 6 passengers including the pilot and co-pilot, it can be painted with basic paints, it does not have camouflage or exclusive paints (for the moment), you can upload vanilla villagers and simple comes alive

----------UH-60 Black Hawk helicopter ----------

This medium-sized helicopter is designed to carry up to 8 passengers, including the pilot and co-pilot. It is fully customizable with paint and camouflage options, making it an excellent addition to your adventures. Its versatile design makes it perfect for any situation in your Minecraft world.

IMAGES with shaders PBR

Exclusive Features

This helicopter includes an exclusive paint and a unique camouflage that you won't find anywhere else. Additionally, you can transport not only other players but also Minecraft Vanilla villagers and the villagers from the "Simple Comes Alive" addon of Furnicraft, adding an extra layer of utility and fun.

rendered images of the luxurious painting

---------- H130 Airbus helicopter ----------

It is a medium-sized helicopter with a capacity of up to 4 passengers, it also provides its own inventory and this helicopter can be painted in all colors available to date, and uses hydrogen as fuel.

---------- apache helicopter ----------

It is a medium-sized attack helicopter for 2 passengers, pilot and co-pilot, and it can be equipped with camouflage, but it cannot be painted in base colors, and uses hydrogen as fuel.

!! In this small error correction update, the helicopter submachine gun was eliminated and now 2 passengers, pilot and co-pilot can also travel !!

---------- bell 47 helicopter ----------

It is an old helicopter for 2 passengers, pilot and co-pilot, it cannot be painted. and does not provide, and uses hydrogen as fuel.

---------- chinook helicopter ----------

It is a large helicopter for up to 22 passengers, between players and villagers, it can be painted in all the colors available to date, 9 color variants in total, and it provides its own inventory, and uses hydrogen as fuel.

---------- fachero helicopter ----------

the fachero, is a small personal helicopter for a single passenger the pilot, does not provide inventory, and cannot be painted, and uses hydrogen as fuel.

---------- littlebrid helicopter ----------

This helicopter is very popular in different video games and is now available in Survival Helicopters, this helicopter has the capacity to transport up to 4 passengers, it cannot be painted and provides its own inventory, and uses hydrogen as fuel.

---------- mi-35 helicopter ----------

It is a large attack and troop cargo helicopter, it can carry several passengers between players and villagers, it can be painted in all colors available to date, it provides its own inventory, and uses hydrogen as fuel.

!!You already have this helicopter crafted, you can now manufacture it in your survival world!!
