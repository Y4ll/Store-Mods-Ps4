Pale Mangler is a relentless predator that can appear at any moment, turning even the most ordinary exploration into a sudden fight for survival. Fast, aggressive, and unpredictable, it doesnt give warningsit attacks.

This add-on introduces a new hostile creature designed to keep players on edge at all times. The Pale Mangler is built around pressure and unpredictability, forcing you to stay alert no matter where you are or what time it is. Encounters are meant to feel sudden and intense, not scripted or avoidable.

Defeating it rewards you with its severed head, a cursed item that grants increased strength and speed. However, this power comes at a costyour hunger drains rapidly while using it, making it a risky tool rather than a permanent advantage (only drop from pursuit mode).
