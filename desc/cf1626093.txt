This AddOn is achievements friendly!

Are you ready to explore countless new structures?

Add the add-on to your Bedrock world and get ready for an adventure into the past.

This add-on brings all kinds of old, forgotten ruins, temples, pyramids, and dungeons into the game.

Currently you can discover 127 different new structures!

Different custom dungeons with spawners and loot generating underground!

All of these dungeons feature different mob spawners

In the files tab you'll find the version v1.3 Advanced Edition - With this version you'll also get some new mobs and items!

Mummy - desert mob which can spawn at day time (faster at night)
Ancient Mummy - stronger & rarer variant of the mummy

Skeleton Knight - skeletons with swords and different armor

Parched Knight - parched with swords and different armor (causes weakness when hit)

Bogged Knight - bogged with swords (causes poison when hit)

Stray Knight - strays with swords and different armor (causes slowness when hit)

Ancient Emerald Warrior - stronger & slightly bigger parched mob with a custom emerald sword and armor

New items:

Ancient Emerald Sword - Only obtainable by defeating the Emerald Warriors
Ancient Emerald Armor - Only obtainable by defeating the Emerald Warriors

Emerald swords and armor pieces can be repaired with emeralds.

If you're wearing a full Emerald Armor set, you'll get the Hero of the Village effect.
