A modScript/addon that will drop items, place blocks or spawn random mobs by breaking any block, dropping items like enchanted apples, nether stars and many more.
Compatible with any Add-On/Mod in any world.

How to play
Simply break any block and random items will be dropped, mobs will appear, or blocks of any kind will be placed.
The only purpose is to have fun getting random items and survive the enemy mobs that might appear.

Crouching will temporarily disable the addon.
Mobs like dragon, wither, warden, and the elder guardian will die instantly to avoid disturbing players.
