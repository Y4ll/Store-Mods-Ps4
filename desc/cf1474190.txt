Comforts Bedrock Edition (Unofficial Port)

Comforts adds Sleeping Bags and Hammocks to Minecraft Bedrock just like beds, but without changing your spawn point.
Perfect for adventurers who want portable rest options and builders who enjoy functional decorative furniture.

Both items come in 16 colors, matching the full Minecraft dye palette.

This is an unofficial Bedrock port of the original Java mod.

Original Java Mod:
Comforts Minecraft Mods CurseForge

Features

Sleeping Bags: Portable beds that do NOT reset your spawn point.

Hammocks: Inverse beds that let you skip the day instead of the night.

16 Color Variants: Fully dyeable and redyeable.

Adventurefriendly: Sleeping bags stack and are easy to carry.

Sleeping Bag

Recipe: 3 wool of the same color in a straight line.

You can dye a White Sleeping Bag into any color and redye colored ones too.

Hammock

Recipe: Colored wool in the center, sticks above and below, strings in the remaining slots.

Just like sleeping bags, hammocks can be dyed and redyed.

Rope & Nail

Rope & Nail is used exclusively for building hammocks.
They can be placed on any side of a block.

Recipe

Strings in the top-left, top, left, and bottom-right slots, with an iron ingot in the center.

Building a Hammock

To build a hammock, you need:

1 Hammock Cloth

2 Rope & Nail sets

Find two blocks exactly four spaces apart.

Place Rope & Nail on each side, facing each other.

Rightclick either Rope & Nail with the Hammock Cloth.

Done! Enjoy naps even under the bright sun.
