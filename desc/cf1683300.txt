Kaleidoscope Chinese Food (Unoffical)

Requires Minecraft Version 26.3+

Port Permission This add-on is an unofficial Bedrock port of Kaleidoscope Chinese Food, originally developed for Minecraft Java Edition.

Ported to Bedrock under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International License, which permits adaptation and redistribution with attribution for non-commercial use, provided derivative works are shared under the same license. Special thanks to the Kaleidoscope Chinese Food team and the Kaleidoscope Cookery team for creating the original mods!

Kaleidoscope Chinese Food expands Kaleidoscope Cookery with additional Chinese-inspired foods, ingredients, cooking methods, kitchen equipment, drinks, placeable dishes, and decorative content.

It adds new recipes and preparation methods using equipment such as the Wok, Stockpot, Steamer, Teapot, Pickle Jar, and Mooncake Mold, along with new crops, preserved foods, specialty dishes, and decorations.

For recipes, preparation instructions, farming information, equipment usage, and explanations of the add-ons features, check the Chinese Food section of the in-game Guidebook.

REQUIRED DEPENDENCY and PACK ORDER

You need Kaleidoscope Cookery for this add-on to work, v1.0.3 or Newer is required!! Download it HERE.

Please put Kaleidoscope Chinese Food ABOVE Kaleidoscope Cookery for best results!

**YOU MAY HAVE TO CREATE THE WORLD, THEN LEAVE AND GO BACK TO WORLD SETTINGS TO GET THIS ORDER TO WORK CORRECTLY**
