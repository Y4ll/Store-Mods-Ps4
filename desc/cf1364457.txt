After the success of the Nautilus of Mayhem Add-On, and by popular demand, the Spears of Mayhem Add-On is here! :D Wield the mighty spears from Minecrafts latest game drop Mounts of Mayhem each crafted for precision, power, and chaos. Master their charge attacks, strike with speed, and unleash true mayhem across your world!

Spears

Designed to match the first Java Edition snapshot, Spears introduce two core attacks: the jab a fast thrust with greater reach than a sword (perfect for controlling distance) and the charge hold to channel and release a powerful lunge whose damage scales with the attacker's velocity (relative to the target), making mounted and high-speed charges especially lethal. Charges can hit multiple entities along their path and cycle through visual stages (Engaged Tired Disengaged) that change effects like knockback and dismount chance.

Give them a spin in survival: experiment with mounted charges, jab vs. charge timing, and extended reach and share what you discover. Read more on the Minecraft Wiki for exact numbers and mechanics. Enjoy it! :D

Installation:

- Click the Download button, somewhere on this page. After a few seconds, a file called Spears of Mayhem Add-On.mcaddon will appear in your download history. Click this file and open it with Minecraft Bedrock, and both the resource and behavior packs will be installed automatically!

- After a notification saying both packs were imported appears, activate the behavior or resource pack in your world settings, and both will be applied automatically.
