-- Introducing Solace RTX --

Get ready to see Minecraft in a whole new lightliterally! Were thrilled to announce the launch of Solace RTX, a beautifully crafted PBR (Physically Based Rendering) pack designed for Minecraft RTX. Solace RTX will transform your experience with stunning visuals, textures, and immersive lighting effects powered by RTX!

-- What to Expect in Solace RTX --

Legacy-Like Textures Reimagining Minecraft with beautiful visuals while preserving its classic aesthetic.

High-Resolution Details: Beautiful 96x normal maps, making every block satisfying to look at.

True-to-Life Lighting GI, real-time shadows, and reflections bring depth and atmosphere to your world.

Even better with Solace Better RTX Get a beautiful warm atmosphere by using Solace BRTX!

Built in dynamic lights Torches and lanterns when held will light your way

This requires an RX 6000+, ARC A+ or RTX 2000+ GPU to function.
