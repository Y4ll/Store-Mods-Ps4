How It Works:

- Mine Iron Ore, Copper Ore, Gold Ore, or Ancient Debris

- Watch it instantly smelt into ingots (or netherite scrap)

- AutoSmelt works with any pickaxe (or ends with _pickaxe) and any shovel (or ends in _shovel)

Auto-Setup on World Load:

No setup needed it installs itself automatically when the world first loads. Youll see:

Simple AutoSmelt - Enabled

Config Item:

Crafting Recipe for Config

How to Install:

1 Download the .mcaddon file
2 Open it to install in Minecraft
3 Add it to your world in Behavior Packs
4 Load the world and enjoy auto-smelting!
