Tired of mining for hours just to get a few diamonds? This add-on fixes that.

Now, every single mob you kill drops god-tier, max-enchanted items instead of their usual boring drops. Kill a sheep, a pig, or a zombie, and they will instantly drop overpowered loot to help you beat the game in minutes.

What drops from mobs?

- God-Tier Gear: Netherite armor, swords, and pickaxes.

- Max Enchantments: All weapons and armor come pre-enchanted with Sharpness V, Protection IV, Efficiency V, Infinity, etc.

- Speedrun Items: End Portal Frames, Eyes of Ender, Blaze Rods, and Obsidian.

- OP Survival Drops: Enchanted Golden Apples, Totems of Undying, Elytras, and Water Buckets.

Why you should try it:

- Works on ALL Mobs: You don't need to find specific monsters. Even a simple chicken drops OP loot.

- Super Lightweight: It is just a behavior pack. No heavy resource pack needed, meaning zero lag for your world.

- Perfect for Videos: If you make YouTube or TikTok videos, this setup gives instant, fun gameplay moments.

Works perfectly on Minecraft Bedrock 1.21 and above. Just activate it in your world settings and have fun!
