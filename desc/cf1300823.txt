Glacier Client (Minecraft Bedrock Edition)

Glacier Client is a lightweight, UI-focused texture client designed to improve your Minecraft Bedrock experience without sacrificing performance.

Features

Custom-designed user interface.

Built-in mod menu with essential tools.

Full customization for every module.

Draggable Modules for endless custom positions.
