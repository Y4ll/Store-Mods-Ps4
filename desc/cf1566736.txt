Metaanie: Vanilla (FPS+ Optimized)

Welcome to the ultimate and fully modernized version of the legendary Metaanie Resource Pack! This version has been meticulously reworked, fixed, and highly optimized for Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26+.

If you are looking for a pack that maintains the beautiful aesthetic of vanilla Minecraft while giving you a massive boost in performance and cool custom weapon variations, this is the perfect pack for you!

Key Features & Enhancements

Pure Vanilla Essence: Retains the core textures and nostalgic feel of official Minecraft, making it perfect for everyday survival, building, and PvP.

Extreme FPS+ Optimization: We have completely cleaned up, rewritten, and streamlined the backend UI files (hud_screen, progress_screen, etc.). This reduces memory lag, stabilizes your frame rate, and ensures butter-smooth gameplay even on low-end mobile devices!

Built-in Selectable Subpacks: No need to download multiple files! You can easily switch between different weapon styles directly inside the global resource settings.

What's Inside the Subpacks?

This pack comes equipped with highly detailed and sleek custom models that replace standard weapons into epic spears:

Meta Spear (Memory Tier 1): The classic, sharp, and aggressive design originally envisioned for the Metaanie community. Perfect for dominant PvP and looking badass in survival.

Kira Spear (Memory Tier 1): A futuristic, clean, and ultra-sleek alternative spear variant with smooth lines and a unique color palette for a refreshing look.

Installation Guide

Download the .mcpack file.

Click or open the file to automatically import it into your Minecraft Bedrock game.

Go to Settings > Global Resources > My Packs.

Click on Metaanie: Vanilla (FPS+ Optimized) and press Activate.

Click on the Gear Icon (Settings) on the pack to slide and choose your preferred Subpack (Meta Spear or Kira Spear).

Restart your world/game and enjoy the smoothness!
