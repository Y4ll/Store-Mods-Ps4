Add-On Overview

This add-on introduces the AIOT (All-In-One Tool), a versatile tool that combines multiple functions into one. The AIOT offers the following capabilities:

Key Features:

- Sword: Use the AIOT as a weapon for combat.

- Pickaxe: Mine ores and blocks with the AIOT.

- Axe: Chop wood, break wooden blocks, unwax copper variants, and remove oxidation from copper variants.

- Shovel: Break blocks like dirt, gravel, and sand, and create grass paths while sneaking.

- Hoe: Cultivate soil and create farmland.

Simplify your gameplay and free up inventory space with AIOTs, making tool management easier and more efficient.

AIOT Netherite Upgrade

You need an AIOT Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template to upgrade your Diamond AIOT to Netherite AIOT.

You can enchant and repair all AIOTs
