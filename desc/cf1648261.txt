WIZARD CRAFT ADDON

Transform Minecraft Bedrock into a fully realized world of magic.

Have you ever wanted to become a wizardsoaring on a broom through open skies, summoning meteors, calling down lightning, or riding a dragon? Wizard Craft AddOn introduces a comprehensive magic system to Minecraft Bedrock, opening a fresh and immersive way to play.

Magic System

Master a wide variety of spells using customizable wands. The Spell Select Book lets players learn and equip up to three spells at once, then switch between them instantly.

Featured spells include:

Fire Spell unleash a torrent of flames to incinerate foes.

Freeze Spell immobilize nearby creatures with freezing effects.

Lightning Spell call down lethal bolts of lightning.

Meteorite Spell rain meteors down from the sky.

Healing Spell restore health to nearby entities.

Shockwave Spell create a concussive blast that knocks back enemies.

Soul Spell lift targets into the air and apply levitation effects.

Knight Spell summon armored knights to fight and defend.

Setback/Rewind Spell return to your position from several seconds earlier.

Time Spell manipulate time within the world.

Wand Mechanics

Wizard Craft goes beyond point-and-cast. Build your own spell loadout using the Unbound Wand and Spell Select Book. Equip up to three spells and toggle between them with sneak + interact. Hold the interact button to charge powerful effects, then release to cast.

Alchemy & Potions

A dedicated potion-crafting system uses the Kettle and Wizard Bottle. Discover and brew potions with unique, gameplay-changing effects:

Bunny Potion increased speed and jump height.

Freezing Potion freezes targets on hit.

Magnetism Potion attracts nearby mobs and items.

Plant Growth Potion accelerates crop growth.

Power Potion increases damage output.

Restoration Potion restores health and hunger.

Shield Potion repels nearby creatures.

Teleport Potion teleport to the target location.

Stun Potion slows entities.

Life Steal Potion siphon health from enemies.

Each potion requires distinct ingredients, making exploration and resource gathering essential to progression.

Flying Brooms

No wizard is complete without a broom. Wizard Craft adds multiple broom types with varying performance:

Default Broom

Iron Broom

Gold Broom

Diamond Broom

Nether Broom

End Broom

Control flight by holding the jump button to ascend, steering with your look direction, and sprinting to reach maximum speed. The End Broom provides the highest speed boost.

Mystical Creatures

The world now hosts many magical creatures beyond the usual mobs:

Dragon a powerful, fire-breathing mount; tameable and rideable.

Hippogriff a tameable flying mount.

Unicorn grants substantial EXP when encountered.

Phoenix a legendary, tamable bird that perches on your shoulder.

Owl can carry spells and be tamed.

Troll a dangerous, goldloving night spawner.

Additional creatures include Knights, Magic Foxes, and Wizard Traders, expanding the addons ecosystem and gameplay.

Mounted Dragon Combat

After taming a Dragon, equip a saddle and soar across the world. Use the Dragon Whistle to command your mount to breathe fire on enemies while you rideturning your dragon into both transport and a formidable weapon.

Wizard Table

All magical systems interconnect through the Wizard Table. Use it to craft signature items such as:

Wands

Spell Select Books

Brooms

Wizard robes and apparel

Dragon Whistles

Other magical artifacts

Guides are included to teach players how to use spells, brew potions, and pilot brooms.

A Fully Realized Fantasy Experience

Wizard Craft is more than a collection of new items. It builds a cohesive fantasy layer on top of Minecraft with magic, alchemy, mythical creatures, mounts, and bespoke gameplay mechanics. Seek out Wizard Traders, learn your first spells, craft your personal wand, tame a mystical creature, and begin your journey to becoming a true wizard.
