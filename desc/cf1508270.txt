Welcome to the ultimate Ben 10 Classic experience for Minecraft Bedrock Edition! This addon brings the classic universe of Ben 10 straight into your survival world. Get ready to wield the Omnitrix, fight epic bosses, and save the world!
Main Features:
The Classic Omnitrix: Discover the Omnitrix and use it to transform into your favorite classic aliens.
Leveling System: Progress and get stronger! You can level up your Omnitrix and individually level up your aliens to unlock more power and longer transformation times.
Epic Boss Fights: The world isn't safe! Face off against powerful bosses from the Ben 10 universe.
Dynamic Difficulty: Survival just got harder. Every 8 in-game days, the difficulty level increases, bringing new challenges and tougher enemies your way. You will need to level up your aliens to keep up!
How to Play:
Start your survival world and begin your journey to find and unlock the Omnitrix.
Defeat enemies, survive the 8-day difficulty spikes, and upgrade your gear and aliens to face the ultimate bosses.
