The Twixxel's Stalkers

Inspired by the unsettling world of Twixxel's videos, The Twixxel's Stalkers transforms Minecraft Bedrock into a psychological horror experience where you are never truly alone.

As you explore your world, strange humanoid creatures known as Stalkers begin to appear. They don't behave like normal Minecraft mobs. They observe. They wait. They learn.

At first, they seem harmless.

But the longer you survive...

The worse things become.

Features

- Encounter mysterious Stalkers with unpredictable behavior.

- They may appear behind doors, windows, trees, and other unexpected places.

- They never intentionally spawn directly in front of your view.

- Multiple unique Stalker variants with different appearances and skins.

- Random ambient sounds, footsteps, cave noises, bone cracks, or complete silence.

- Unsettling encounters hidden throughout the world.

- Strange environmental events that make your world feel... wrong.

- Rare aggressive encounters that can quickly turn into terrifying chases.

---

How It Works

The world starts out almost normal.

For a while, you'll only catch brief glimpses of something watching from afar.

Sometimes it's standing behind a tree.

Sometimes it's staring through a window.

Sometimes you'll hear footsteps directly behind you...

Only to find nothing there when you turn around.

The Stalkers rarely behave the same way twice.

Some disappear the instant you notice them.

Others continue staring.

Some remain completely silent.

Others announce their presence with disturbing sounds that echo through the world.

The longer you continue playing, the more confident they become.

What begins as an occasional sighting slowly evolves into constant psychological pressure.

---

They Are Watching

Not every encounter ends peacefully.

Some Stalkers become increasingly bold.

If you get too close...

If you're unlucky...

One of them may reveal its true nature.

The aggressive variant is rare, relentless, and far more dangerous than the others.

Once it begins hunting you, running may be your only option.

Recommended Experience

For the intended experience:

- Play with headphones.

- Keep your volume turned up.

- Explore during the night.

- Avoid spoilers.

- Never assume you've seen everything.
