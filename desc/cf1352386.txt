Light up your world like never before! With the Dynamic Light Addon, torches, lanterns, and even glowing items shine in your hand or on the ground. No more dark cavescarry light wherever you go!

This dynamic light add-on is not just a normal dynamic light.

For example:
If you hold an Ender Eye, you will get the Night Vision effect.
If you hold a Nether Star, you will receive curse/blessing effects for a fairly long duration.
