Mobs Overview

Summary:

This plugin introduces a thrilling variety of new mobs from brave soldiers and powerful guardians to mischievous goblins, magical villagers, and even quirky, cute companions . Whether youre seeking allies in battle or unpredictable enemies to challenge your adventure, these unique creatures will make every encounter exciting and memorable!

Soldier Villager

- Health: 40

- Damage: 5

- Attack Mode: Melee

- Attack Description: Very fast attack time, only 0.1s

- Weapon: Iron Sword

- Behavior: When mobs spawn near a village, they will automatically move towards the village to protect it.

Bow Soldier Villager

- Health: 35

- Damage: Same as arrow damage

- Attack Mode: Ranged

- Attack Description: Shoots arrows every 1 ~ 1.5s

- Weapon: Bow

- Behavior: When mobs spawn near a village, they will automatically move towards the village to protect it.

Axe Soldier

- Health: 40

- Damage: 5

- Attack Mode: Melee

- Attack Description: Half-second attack time

- Weapon: Iron Axe

- Behavior: When mobs spawn near a village, they will automatically move towards the village to protect it.

Mage Villager

- Health: 35

- Damage: Same as small fireball

- Attack Mode: Ranged

- Attack Description: Casts 5 small fireballs with 1 ~ 3s cooldown

- Weapon: None

- Behavior: When mobs spawn near a village, they will automatically move towards the village to protect it.

Chain Golem

- Health: 125

- Damage: 12

- Attack Mode: Melee + Shockwave

- Attack Description:

- Attack cooldown: 0.3s

- Attacks within 3.8 blocks (x & y)

- Shockwave Damage: 6

- Shockwave Range: 4 blocks

- Weapon: None

Stone Guardian

- Health: 100

- Damage: 7 ~ 25

- Attack Mode: Melee

- Attack Description: Attacks only after locating a target

- Weapon: None

- Behavior: When mobs spawn near a village, they will automatically move towards the village to protect it.

Goblin

- Health: 30

- Damage: 1

- Attack Mode: Melee

- Attack Description: Seeks the most recent attacker (player, snow golem, iron golem, villager). After being hit, it looks for a hiding spot or flees if none are available.

- Weapon: Goblin Short Sword

Dungeon Watcher

- Health: 40

- Damage: 5

- Attack Mode: Ranged (Laser Beam, similar to Warden)

- Attack Description:

- Attack cooldown: 0.5s

- Beam Damage: 1

- Beam Range: 20 blocks

- Duration: 3s

- Knockback: 0.5 blocks

- Height: 2.8 blocks

- Weapon: None

Minecart Shooter

- Health: 20

- Damage: Same as arrow damage

- Attack Mode: Ranged

- Attack Description: Shoots with 0.8 ~ 1.5s cooldown

- Weapon: None

Shadow Hound

- Health: 24

- Damage: 3 (effect damage)

- Attack Mode: Melee

- Attack Description: Inflicts poison when attacking

- Weapon: None

Pallet Truck

- Health: 40

- Damage: None

- Attack Description: None

Rubble Sprite
