How to Use:

- Open the Morph Menu:,

- Grab the "DG MENU" from your inventory.

- Use it to open the morph menu.

- Choose a category, then select any option to morph instantly.

- Return to Player Form:

- Use the "DG Player Morph" item to revert back to your original self.

- Blend In Naturally (Solidifier):,

- Use the "DG Solidifier" item to lock your position, making you look like a real block or furnitureperfect for hiding.
