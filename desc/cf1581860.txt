A&S View Model by VanceMc

MPORTANT REQUIREMENT: This pack is designed to be used on top of the original Action & Stuff pack. Please ensure you have Action & Stuff installed and active beneath this pack in your loading order for everything to function properly!

Fully adjust your view model's position, scale, and rotation with added armor sleeve support.

Supports Actions and stuff 1.5 - 1.11

Features

Custom Positioning, Rotation & Scale Control: Fine-tune the X, Y, and Z coordinates of your view model.

Armor Sleeves: Added support for Vanilla armor sleeve.
