Lucky Block + Unlucky Block is a unique add-on featuring two types of blocks: Lucky Blocks and Unlucky Blocks. Lucky Blocks reward you with valuable items, while Unlucky Blocks give you useless items and turn your world into a haunted nightmare.

You don't need to craft these blocks, they cannot be crafted. Instead, you'll receive 3 Lucky Blocks or 3 Unlucky Blocks every hour.

Additional info about the Unlucky Block: it will spawn ghost creatures that will haunt you.

The block textures are exactly the same as shown in the images.
