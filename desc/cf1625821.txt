MoreCommands || By Creator Studios

MoreCommands is a lightweight scripting add-on that adds a huge set of quick chat commands gamemode shortcuts, teleport tools, quality-of-life utilities, and instant mob/item spawning and much more!

Features

40+ handy utility commands (teleport homes/warps, fly, god mode, vanish, freeze, particle effects, and more)

No Experimental Mode Needed!

Compatible with Minecraft Version 1.20.70 upto latest 26.40!

Command List

1. /gs - change gamemode to survival

2. /gc - change gamemode to creative

3. /gsp - change gamemode to spectator

4. /ga - change gamemode to adventure

5. /npc - summon an NPC at your position

6. /heal - restore your health to full

7. /feed - restore your hunger to full

8. /fly - toggle flight on or off

9. /enchants - open a menu to enchant your held item

10. /repair - repair the item in your hand

11. /kit - give yourself a starter kit

12. /day - set the time to day

13. /night - set the time to night

14. /tpa - request to teleport to a player

15. /tpaccept - accept a pending teleport request

16. /god - toggle invulnerability

17. /jump - set your jump boost level (0-10)

18. /vanish - toggle invisibility to other players

19. /warp - teleports players to a saved warp and can used to save a position as a warp with a custom name

20. /setwarp - save your current position as a warp

21. /home - teleport to your home

22. /sethome - set your home to your current position

23. /back - teleport back to your last location

24. /rename - rename the item in your hand

25. /stack - auto-stack your inventory

26. /explode - trigger a safe explosion effect at your feet

27. /freeze - freeze or unfreeze a player in place

28. /spawnmob - spawn several mobs at once

29. /biome - show the biome you're standing in

30. /pe - spawn a particle effect, optionally looping at a position

31. /pestop - stop your active looping particle effect

32. /nick - change your display nametag

33. /invsee - view another player's inventory (read-only)

34. /sit - sit down (or stand back up)

35. /lightning - summon a lightning bolt at your position or a given location

36. /repairall - repair every item in your inventory and armor

37. /mobkill - clear all mobs of a given type in the loaded area

38. /nametag - rename the mob you're looking at

39. /guide - get the More Commands guide book

40. /entitycount - report how many entities are loaded nearby

41. /cdlist - list all commands added by this addon

42. /nether - tp to nether dimension

43. /End - tp to end dimension instantly

44. /Spawn - tp to world spawn

45. /rain - changes weather to rainy

46. /Sunny - clears the weather

47. /Tag - Give permissions to whom can use what Commands, Owners have all commands access, Admins have access to all the admin only commands and Player tagged ones have access to limited commands and not game breaking commands like /mobkill and /invsee.

48. /Warplist - Tells the list of saved warps.

Mob & Item Shortcuts

All mobs and items in the game are available as their own instant one-word commands (e.g. /zombie to summon, /diamond 10 to get 10 diamonds). Run /cdlist in-game for the complete, current list of commands and also a guide containing all commands information is given to player at world creation.
