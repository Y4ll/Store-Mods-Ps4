Custom Loot Drops Breeze Addon v. 1.0.1

Custom Loot Drops Breeze Addon enhances your Minecraft gameplay by customizing the loot drops of the Breeze. All of that without losing your achievements! This addon lets you control and modify what the Breeze loots will drop upon defeat, making it more rewarding for combat/pvp, farming, or progression-based gameplay. Whether you want better resources, rare items, or custom drops tailored to your world, this pack gives you the flexibility without using scripts or commands. Perfect for loot-focused survival, pvp/combat, or mob farms, this pack is designed to work seamlessly with Minecrafts vanilla mechanics. It's ideal for players who want to improve the reward system of the Breeze mob and will use it for pvp/combat with mace.

You can customize the drop loots of a breeze. These are the following:

1. 1 to 10 Drop Loots

2. 11 to 20 Drop Loots

3. 21 to 30 Drop Loots

4. 31 to 40 Drop Loots

5. 41 to 50 Drop Loots

6. 51 to 60 Drop Loots

7. 61 to 70 Drop Loots

8. 71 to 80 Drop Loots

9. 81 to 90 Drop Loots

10. 91 to 100 Drop Loots
