The Swift Minecraft Sift Dimension Add-on

The Swift is CreativeMode's fan-made take on the Sift for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Open a musical portal and explore
pink meadows, white willow forests, rainbow ichor and aurora skies. Includes behavior and resource packs; no
experimental toggles are required.

Explore the Swift

- Seven landscapes: Singer's Meadow, Wild Meadows, Lullaby Hills, Sifter Canyon, Humbler Huskland, Ichor Pools and
the underground Ravines. Discover floating islands, white willow forests, deep chasms and towering rock formations.

- 27 creatures: befriend blubs, carry a tuner, tame a prickle and meet the sifters and sculkers. Challenge the Monarch,
Harmonizer and Twisted Warden, plus the Dartback mini-boss.

- Six encounter structures: Lullaby Circles, humbler husks, Echo Dens, Sculker Nests, Illager Soul Excavations and
Dartback Arenas. The Twisted Warden guards ancient-city portal frames in the Overworld.

- Three tides: Thrive recharges placed Drained Soul Blocks and speeds up golem pulses; Flow is the quiet transition;
Endure stops the pulses and brings soul-corrupted monsters. In the Swift, dormant golems wake only during Thrive.

- Build with white willow, Swiftstone, Tidestone and blush sandstone. Earn the Lullaby music disc and turn a diamond
spear into a Spectral Spear by dropping it into ichor.

Getting there and back

In the Overworld, play five different note-block pitches near a reinforced deepslate frame within 12 seconds.
Awake echo golems can contribute notes, and a nearby Singer can open the portal too. A return gate waits in the Swift;
if it goes dark, gather four awake echo golems nearby during Thrive to relight it.

Use a Soul Block or give a Singer an Echo Shard to open a temporary rift. Natural rifts also appear in the Overworld
at night. A rift home remembers where you entered through a rift, or returns you to your Overworld spawn if you came
another way. Beds and respawn anchors do not work in the Swift.

Install for Bedrock Edition

- Download swift_bedrock.mcaddon for Bedrock 26.50 or a newer compatible version.

- Open the file with Minecraft to import both packs.

- Activate The Swift (Behavior) for your world and check that The Swift (Resources) is active too.

The release was tested in the Windows 1.26.52 client. Vibrant Visuals is optional.

Bedrock terrain generates as you explore; very fast flight can briefly outrun it. Ichor does not flow or support
swimming. Portal melodies use player interactions with note blocks; redstone-played notes cannot be detected.
The Swift shares the Overworld's time of day. White willow signs, hanging signs, shelves and boats are Java-only.

Updating an existing world

Back up your world before updating, import the new mcaddon, and activate the updated behavior and resource packs.
New terrain and structures need unexplored chunks or a new world. The Sculk Monstrosity, Sculk Cube and their spawn
eggs are removed without a replacement migration. The Monstrosity Lair and Harmonizer Grove no longer generate;
Harmonizers now spawn naturally. Soul Blocks open rifts instead of granting the old personal soul powers. Renamed
content that remains in the add-on keeps its existing IDs.
