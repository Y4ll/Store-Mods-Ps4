Her role was "Black Agent" to locate and eliminate individuals who had seen too much, thereby maintaining global ignorance of the anomalous world.

Her operational record was flawless, marked by high efficiency and a noted lack of emotional compromise.

But after the "OPERATION CODENAME: WHISPERING HEARTH" Agent-7 began exhibiting signs of psychological deterioration. Subject's personal logs indicate profound regret and traumatic stress related to the elimination of a family unit with no prior anomalous affiliation, She filed an official resignation and intent to extract herself from all operations.

The Sanction Division's oversight committee assessed Agent-7 as a Priority-1 Anomalous Information Hazard. Her knowledge of operational protocols and anomalous events made her a threat to the Division's secrecy. Her resignation was denied. Standard procedure was initiated: Termination.

OPERATIONAL HISTORY

Standard Black Agent teams were dispatched for her elimination.

Result: Total team loss. Agent-7 survived through superior fieldcraft, utilizing her superior knowledge of Division's tactics against them.

Teams with experimental anti-anomaly weaponry were deployed.

Result: Catastrophic failure. Agent-7 demonstrated unprecedented adaptability.

After Two Failed Operations against Black Agent-7, Committee frustration peaked. The cost of eliminating her now outweighed her value as a target. A new assessment was made, Agent-7's firsthand combat experience against numerous anomaly types, coupled with her preternatural survival skills, Directive was changed from TERMINATE to ACQUIRE LIVE SUBJECT.
