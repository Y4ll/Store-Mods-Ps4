Create fully customizable potions in survival! Choose any effects, combine them however you want, and set any duration to build the perfect potion for any situation.

The addon turns potions into a customizable toolset. Instead of relying only on vanilla brewing recipes, players can create specialized potions for combat, exploration, mining, parkour, or roleplay by combining effects into a single brew.
