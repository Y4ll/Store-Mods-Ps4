Features & Abilities

Equip specific items and trigger actions by using them (Right-Click/Long Press) or sneaking (Shift) in specific situations.

Ground & Parkour Actions

Ledge Grab / Mantling (NEW!): Falling just short of a block? Sneak while near the upper edge of a block to smoothly pull yourself up! Perfect for saving those near-miss jumps.

Sliding (NEW!): Sneak while sprinting on the ground to perform a fast forward slide! You can even seamlessly slide through 1-block high gaps. Complete with custom camera FOV effects, friction sounds, and horizontal sliding animations!

Wall Jump: Sneak while falling next to a wall to kick off it and jump in the opposite direction.

Aerial Actions

Double Jump (Item: Ghast Tear): Use the item while in mid-air to perform a second jump.

Air Dash (Sneak in mid-air): Sneak while falling to violently dash forward in the direction you are looking.

Glide / Hover (NEW!) (Item: Phantom Membrane): Use in mid-air to catch the wind! Grants you a forward boost and temporary slow-falling to safely cross massive gaps.

Tactical Actions

Grappling Hook (Item: Fishing Rod / Tripwire Hook): Shoot a hook at any block within range and pull yourself toward it like a ninja! (Works flawlessly in survival without accidentally placing blocks!)

Blink / Teleport (Item: Amethyst Shard): Instantly teleport a short distance forward.

How to Get Items & Commands (NEW!)

We've completely overhauled how you get your action items. You no longer need cheats enabled!

Just type the following native custom command in the chat:

/ma:get all - Gives you all ability items and the Settings Book.

/ma:get jump - Gives only the Double Jump item.

/ma:get grapple - Gives only the Grappling Hook item.

/ma:get glide - Gives only the Glide item.

(Note: The legacy !get_actions chat trigger still works for older players!)

Fully Customizable (The Settings Book)

Use the Action Settings (Book) to open an intuitive UI menu. Every single action can be customized to your liking!

Toggle specific actions ON or OFF.

Adjust Cooldown times (in seconds).

Adjust Strength / Distance.

Toggle whether using the ability consumes the item.

Global Fall Damage Toggle (NEW!): Easily enable or disable fall damage prevention globally for all aerial actions (Double Jump, Grapple, Air Dash, Glide) with a single click!
