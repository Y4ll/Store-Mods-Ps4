Whats New in Plagues & Masquerades V2.1

Welcome to Version 2.1! Darkness, pestilence, and tactical siege warfare have descended upon the realm. Here is a showcase of the major features, mechanical overhauls, and additions waiting for you in the latest update.

Heavy Armaments & Flail Combat

Upgrade your arsenal with specialized gear, protective gear, and sweeping AoE attacks.

New Gear & Wearables

-

Executioner's Garb & Plague Mask: Equipping the Plague Mask grants complete immunity against the pestilence.

-

Shrouded Coat: Defeat the Plague Doctor to claim this coat and unlock its special ability.

Shield & Weapon Reworks

-

Shield Bash: Crouching with a fully charged large round shield and punching flings you forward into the enemy line.

-

Flail Special Abilities: Holding interact with the War Ravager or Steel Flail unleashes devastating AoE damage to surrounding targets. The War Ravager scales up to 3x damage the longer you charge it.

-

Bleed Stacking: Repeatedly striking a bleeding target with a bleed weapon now stacks and intensifies the effect over time.

Plagues, Bio-Warfare & The Doctor

Pestilence now spreads across entities, clogs player inventories, and reshapes kingdom defense.

Infection & Remedies

-

Inventory Mucus: Plagued players will quickly find their inventory filled with mucus; fail to clear it in time and up to 5 inventory slots will lock completely.

-

The Nighttime Doctor: Track down The Doctor in spooky biomes at night. He is your sole source for herbal seeds, which are grown and brewed to craft the plague cure.

Siege Bio-Warfare & Health Crest

-

Seeping Bag & Grotesque Siege: Slay a zombie with a Seeping Bag, load it into a catapult, and launch a grotesque payload into enemy strongholds to infect their forces. (Can only be cleansed with fire!)

-

Crest of Health: Protects your kingdom from rat outbreaks, enables automatic healing for your citizens, and unlocks a one-time offensive rat attack against rival factions.

Overhauled Sieges & Dynamic Events

Siege warfare has transformed into a multi-phase tactical battle requiring strategy and vigilance.

-

Siege Requirements: Laying siege now requires functional siege engines (trebuchets, catapults, siege towers, battering rams) or a dedicated force of 10+ troops positioned directly outside enemy walls.

-

Supply Lines & Sally-Outs: Stop enemy supply carts from reaching the garrison to keep the starve-out timer moving, and defend your engines against illager sally-out attacks.

-

Storm the Castle: Sound your Goat Horn once the starve-out phase finishes to launch the final assault on the garrison.

-

Ominous Siege Difficulty: Drink an Ominous Bottle before placing your siege weaponry to ramp up the challenge.

Kingdom Management & The Jester

Manage unit movements, kingdom morale, and economic balances.

-

The Jester: Hire the Jester with emeralds to entertain your villagers, or give him an Emerald Block for a major morale surge. If total kingdom collapse threatens, the Jester can spend coins from his own wallet to prevent it (up to 3 times per kingdom).

-

Patrols & Formations: Units now walk to assigned positions in commander view. Set up custom patrol paths around your walls and take advantage of an added 30 seconds to the commander timer.

-

Taxation System: Adjust tax rates high or low to balance kingdom income against citizen morale.
