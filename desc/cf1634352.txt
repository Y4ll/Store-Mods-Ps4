Horizen Shader

Horizen Shader is a lightweight Minecraft Bedrock shader focused on soft lighting, gentle sunlight, natural colors, beautiful skies, and a smooth visual atmosphere.

The shader now includes three built-in quality presets, which can be selected directly in-game

Default Balanced visual quality with soft lighting, natural colors, beautiful skies, and the main shader effects enabled.
