Introduction

Absolute Guns 3 3D is a sequel to a dead add-on with the same name. The add-on focuses on being able to use simple 3D weapons in any add-on! Supports realms, multiplayer worlds, and dedicated servers. Fully made with scripts, allowing easy modification!

Reloading your weapons

The addon only supports full reloads (ammo must be 0). Ensure you have the correct ammo in your inventory, then 'Eat' or use the weapon to reload as shown below:

Note: special weapons such as Flamethrower & MGL take the ammo component is inside their crafting recipe, that being the TNT and Blaze Powder.

Crafting your weapons

Toggle the uncraftable icon in the crafting table and you'll see all weapons that can be crafted.

Damage System

From the get-go, your weapons use Actual Guns damage system; bypassing Minecrafts 10-tick immunity frames and supporting customizable features like damage drop-off, armor penetration, and knockback.

Customization

Speaking of customization, the system also uses Keyyard's Gun System which allows you to customize the fireRate, ammo count, velocity, reload time, shooting modes, animations, recoil, and so on.
