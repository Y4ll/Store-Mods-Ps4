More Vanilla Bows+ Complete

Elevate Your Vanilla Survival Experience

More Vanilla Bows+ Complete is the perfect minimalist bow-and-arrow expansion for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. No new entities or fancy effectsjust pure, resource-based progression that seamlessly integrates with your vanilla gameplay.

What's New in Version 1.5.0

- Quartz Bow & Quartz Arrow - Nether-forged weaponry with fire immunity

- Lapis Lazuli Bow & Lapis Arrow - Enchantment-focused archery with enhanced experience rewards

- Improved Organization - New creative inventory categories for easy access

11 Vanilla Bows

Tier 1: Early Game

- Stone Bow - Your first upgrade over wood. Balanced durability and power for early survival.

Tier 2: Mid Game

- Iron Bow & Iron Arrow - Sturdy and reliable craftsmanship. Perfect for precision shots and consistent performance.

- Copper Bow & Copper Arrow - Mid-tier alternative with a unique patina aesthetic. Great balance between cost and effectiveness.

Tier 3: Advanced

- Gold Bow & Gold Arrow - Lightning-fast draw speed with moderate damage. Perfect for quick combat situations.

- Diamond Bow & Diamond Arrow - High durability meets powerful impact. The classic endgame choice for serious archers.

- Emerald Bow & Emerald Arrow - Rarest materials yield the finest precision weapon. Exceptional performance for the dedicated collector.

Tier 4: Specialized

- Amethyst Bow & Amethyst Arrow - Shiny new gemstone tier for stylish combat. Unique visual appeal with solid stats.

- Lapis Lazuli Bow & Lapis Arrow - Enchantment-focused archery. Enhanced experience rewards make this perfect for leveling up while fighting.

Tier 5: Endgame

- Obsidian Bow & Obsidian Arrow - Exceptional toughness for ultimate endgame builds. Maximum durability for extended adventures.

- Netherite Bow & Netherite Arrow - Maximum strength and endurance. The pinnacle of archery equipment.

- Quartz Bow & Quartz Arrow - Nether-forged weaponry with fire immunity. Perfect for exploring the dangerous Nether dimension.

10 Matching Arrow Types

Each bow (except Stone) pairs with its corresponding arrow materialIron, Gold, Diamond, Obsidian, Netherite, Amethyst, Copper, Emerald, Quartz, and Lapis Lazuligranting damage and durability proportional to resource rarity.

Special Features:

- Quartz Arrows - Immune to fire and lava damage, making them ideal for Nether exploration

- Lapis Arrows - Enhanced experience rewards when defeating mobs, perfect for leveling up
