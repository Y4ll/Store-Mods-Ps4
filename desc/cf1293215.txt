Custom Enderman Loot Drop

Custom Enderman Loot Drop Addon gives the Enderman a rewarding loot! This addon modifies the default behavior of Endermen to drop custom ender pearls, enhancing the excitement of every encounter. Whether you're farming resources, hunting rare items, or spicing up your survival gameplay, this tweak adds a meaningful reward. Perfect for players who want more utility out of the End, or for creators designing maps with specific progression goals, this addon is fully vanilla-compatibleno scripts required! It keeps the core Minecraft experience intact while adding a new layer of strategy and reward to your world. Ideal for survival enthusiasts, custom adventure builders, or anyone looking to refresh their loot tables with simplicity and style.

There are options when you want to customize the loot drop of Enderman:

1. 1 to 10 Loot Drops

2. 11 to 20 Loot Drops

3. 21 to 30 Loot Drops

4. 31 to 40 Loot Drops

5. 41 to 50 Loot Drops

6. 51 to 60 Loot Drops

7. 61 to 70 Loot Drops

8. 71 to 80 Loot Drops

9. 81 to 90 Loot Drops

10. 91 to 100 Loot Drops (Default)
