Capacity:

- Dyed Backpacks & Default Backpack: 27 Slots

- Copper Backpack: 36 Slots

- Iron Backpack: 54 Slots

- Golden Backpack: 63 Slots

- Diamond Backpack: 72 Slots

- Netherite Backpack: 88 Slots

-

To open the backpack, simply hold it in your hand. On mobile, the 'Open' button will appear, and on PC, just right-click.

-

If you're holding the backpack and you go to another dimension, simply take it out of your hand and put it back in so you can open it again.

- When the backpack is in your hand, it locks and prevents it from being moved or put inside itself.
