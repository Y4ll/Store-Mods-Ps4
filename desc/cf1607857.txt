Quick Totem Equip Your Totem of Undying in Seconds

Tired of digging through your inventory mid-fight to grab your Totem of Undying? Quick Totem adds a fast, touch-friendly button system that lets you equip your totem in just a few taps no commands, no behavior packs, no scripting required. Pure resource pack magic.

How it works:

Tap the totem icon on your HUD to open your inventory.

Tap the totem icon again to auto-equip it to your offhand.

Tap the X to close your inventory done.

Features:

Touch-optimized built specifically for mobile/touch controls

5 selectable button positions via in-game subpacks: Top Right, Top Left, Bottom Right, Bottom Left, and Hotbar Left (Java-style)

No lag, no scripting, no behavior pack just JSON UI

Smart detection the button automatically shows the right icon depending on whether a totem is already equipped

Requirements:

Minecraft Bedrock Edition

Minimum engine version 1.21.0

Choose your favorite button position from the pack's subpack settings and never fumble through your inventory during a fight again.
