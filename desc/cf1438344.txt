Imagine a Skeleton shoots an arrow at your face, and instead of taking damage, you simply raise your hand and freeze the arrow in mid-air. Or imagine pausing time right before a TNT block explodes.

This is the RR Time Matrix Addon.

While there have been "Time Stop" mods in the past, most of them were fake. They only gave slowness to mobs. This addon is different. I used the Script API to hack the game's physics engine.

Main Features

I have split the addon into two modes to keep it optimized for mobile devices.

1. Basic Freeze (Default)

This works immediately when you install the addon.

Mob Freeze: All living creatures (Zombies, Creepers, Cows) freeze in place.

Sound FX: Includes the iconic "Time Stop" heavy bass sound effect.

Limitation: In this mode, arrows and TNT will still move normally.

2. Matrix Physics (Advanced)

This is the real power. It stops non-living entities.

Projectile Stop: Arrows, Snowballs, and Fireballs freeze in mid-air. You can walk around them.

TNT Suspension: If you light a TNT, the fuse timer stops. It will not explode until you resume time.

Gravity Defy: Falling blocks and items will hang in the air.

Controls: How to Use

The controls are specific to prevent accidents. Please read carefully.

To Stop Time: Hold a Clock and Long Press (Right Click).

To Resume Time: Hold a Clock and Long Press again.

How to Open Settings Menu?

To enable the Advanced Matrix Physics, you need to open the hidden GUI.

Hold the Clock in your hand.

Crouch (Sneak) on the ground.

While crouching, Long Press the screen.

How to Unlock Matrix Physics?

By default, the Physics Stop is disabled to save performance. To unlock it:

Open the Menu (Crouch + Use Clock).

Enter the System Key in the input box.

Submit the key to unlock "Projectile Freezing".

Installation Requirement

This script manipulates entity velocity vectors. You MUST turn on Beta APIs in your world settings under Experiments. If you skip this, the physics will not stop.
