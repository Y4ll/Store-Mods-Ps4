AI MULTIPLAYER

AI Multiplayer brings your single-player Minecraft world to life by adding AI players that act, work, and survive just like real people. Instead of following you around mindlessly, these bots have their own inventories, personalities, and goals.

Here is what they can do:

Work & Mine: Give a bot a job, like chopping a specific amount of wood or mining ores. They use smart tacticslike felling entire trees or digging clean 45^\circ staircasesand deliver the exact items to a chest when done.

Survive & Travel: Bots follow real player physics, eat when hungry, sleep through the night, and build quick dirt shelters to stay safe. They can swim, ride animals (horses, camels, donkeys), and dig through blocks if their path is blocked.

Smart Combat: They fight dynamically using swords, bows, tridents, shields, and mace slams. They dodge attacks, flee from creepers, avoid lava, and retreat to heal when low on health.

Social Connections: Bots talk in chat, offer quests, trade, and request friendships. Help them out to build trust, or hit them to start a feud. You can easily adjust their behavior, chat frequency, and pranks through an in-game settings menu.
