Welcome to the new version of the Family Life addon! This addon replaces Minecraft's villagers with NPCs, allowing you to chat, interact, and start a family with them.

What's New?

This is an improved version of the original addon, with many changes:

- Artificial Intelligence (AI): Almost all interactions now use AI, from dialogues to NPC reactions when receiving a gift.

- Multi-Language: Talk to NPCs in any language (English, Japanese, Russian, etc.); the AI can communicate in all of them.

- More Skin Variations: Tens of thousands of skin combinations (over 16,000!), a big step up from the original addon's fixed 30-something skins.

- Family Life+ Client: A dedicated client that handles all AI communication, acting as the NPCs' "brain."

Features

The addon offers a wide variety of interactions. You can chat idly, tell jokes, or even flirt with the blacksmith's wife! With AI, the possibilities for interacting with NPCs are virtually limitless.

Features also include:

- Intimacy Levels: This system measures how close you are to an NPC and is stored individually, allowing for multiplayer. The current levels are:

- Friendship: Increases by doing friendly activities with the NPC.

- Romance: Increases with romantic gestures, flirting, etc.

Family Life+ Client - IMPORTANT

To use the Family Life+ addon's AI in Minecraft Bedrock, follow the steps below:

- Go to this link: https://bit.ly/49jrKt4 or https://family-life-plus-api-production.up.railway.app/api/auth/login

- Log in

- Copy the green command that appears.

- Paste the command into your Minecraft world (with the addon active).

Mandatory Configuration

Before entering the world, go to your General Settings and adjust the following:

- Enable: Websocket

- Disable: Encrypted Websocket

Important: Only the world's host needs run the /connect command. Other players don't need to do anything. From now on, a client is no longer required to use Family Life+
