Eclipse Deferred is a next-generation visual experience created by AztralMC64, leveraging the power of deferred rendering combined with physically based rendering (PBR) to bring an unmatched level of realism and immersion. Every element in the world reacts naturally to light, with accurate material definitions that allow metals to shine, rough surfaces to scatter light, and emissive objects to glow convincingly in darkness.

Built for those who seek both visual fidelity and performance, Eclipse Deferred offers dynamic global illumination, soft shadows, volumetric fog, and advanced atmospheric effects that respond to time and weather. The system enables complex lighting interactions and multiple light sources without compromising quality, thanks to its efficient deferred pipeline.

Eclipse Deferred isn't just about lightingit enhances the entire visual language of the world. Colors feel deeper, surfaces appear more lifelike, and environments gain a cinematic tone that adapts naturally to your surroundings. Whether you're exploring dark caves or standing under a glowing sunset, every moment is elevated with depth, realism, and artistic expression.

Crafted with precision and creativity, Eclipse Deferred represents the evolution of Minecraft visuals through the lens of modern rendering technology, offering an experience thats both beautiful and technically impressive.
