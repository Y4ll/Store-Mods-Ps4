-Update V1.6

-Wyvern Shields

In this update, wyvern shields were added, a total of 6 shields, one for each wyvern variant. These shields have effects that are inflicted on enemies when they hit the shields.

These shields have a total of 600 durability points and can be repaired at an anvil using scales from the corresponding type of wyvern the shield is made from. The shield appears on the player like this.

To craft these shields, you'll need to use the crafting table and arrange the following materials in the specified order. The wyvern scales will vary depending on the type of shield you want to make.

The special abilities of each shield vary depending on the type of wyvern shield you have. Some may be better than others, while others may be less effective it all depends on your preferences and needs at the time. The effects are as follows:

Fire Wyvern Shield: When hit, it will burn the mobs that struck the shield.

Ice Wyvern Shield: Applies Slowness level 5 to the mobs that struck the shield.

Poison Wyvern Shield: Curses mobs that struck the shield with the Poison effect.

Ender Wyvern Shield: Teleports mobs that struck the shield 10 blocks upward.

Wither Wyvern Shield: Inflicts the Wither effect on mobs that struck the shield.

Ground Wyvern Shield: Deals 5 damage to the mobs that struck the shield.

Update V1.4

Wyvern Chest Mechanic

Starting from this version, the mechanic to add chests to the wyvern's inventory will be available. They must be placed in the second slot of the wyvern's inventory. This core will add a total of 9 slots for storing items. In future versions, methods to increase the number of slots will be added.

Wyvern Structures

Wyvern structures have been added and will generate throughout the world. In these structures, the wyverns will remain inactive until you approach them, at which point they will rise and attack you. There will also be interesting loot that you can find in the chests.

Wyvern Egg Models

In this update, the models and textures of the wyvern eggs have been improved.
