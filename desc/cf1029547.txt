Un-Craftable is an add-on that introduces new crafting recipes for previously uncraftable items and blocks. With this add-on, you can craft items like the totem of undying, saddle, horse armor, saplings, grass blocks, podzol, and even spawn eggs. Additionally, it allows you to create normally unobtainable blocks such as bedrock, mob spawners, end portal frames, and chemistry equipment. You can even craft creative-only blocks like the command block and barrier.

Some Examples:

With this add-on, you can now craft items and blocks that were previously unavailable through crafting. Such as spawn eggs, creative-only blocks, and even the Enchanted Apple, whose recipe has been restored. Note that recipes involving a filled bucket or glass bottle will return the container to you after crafting.

Spawn Eggs

If mobs drop two different types of loot, they are typically crafted using those specific loot items, with this pattern.

If a mob only drops a single type of loot, it is usually crafted with this pattern.

Sometimes, they can be crafted by combining another spawn egg with another spawn egg, and these recipes are shapeless.

Spawn Zombie + Spawn Pig = Spawn Zombie Pigman

Certain spawn eggs can combine with specific items to create other spawn eggs. Here's an example:

Coal + Spawn Spider = Spawn Cave Spider

Where can you see all recipes?

The recipes provided above are just a few examples. You can find all blocks and items you can craft in your recipe book. Simply toggle off the filter button to view them all.
