Combat Mechanics/Gameplay

[Fatigue | Cursed Energy | Output]

The core gameplay of the add-on focuses on these three stats.

[Fatigue]

- Fatigue starts from 0% and increases each time a technique is cast. (Stronger Technique = Higher Fatigue)

- Fatigue scales technique cooldowns (Higher Fatigue = Longer Cooldowns)

- Fatigue scales technique Cursed Energy costs (Higher Fatigue = Techniques Require More Cursed Energy)

- Reaching 100% and above leads to [Burnout] which disables your innate Cursed Technique

(Burnout lasts for 8 seconds)

[Cursed Energy]

- Essentially your "mana", casting techniques require different amounts of Cursed Energy

[Output]

- Output scales your technique damage. (Higher Output = More Damage)

- Output decides Reverse Cursed Technique strength. (Higher Output = Stronger Healing)

- Output decides how fast you regenerate Cursed Energy (Higher Output = Faster Cursed Energy Regen)

- Output scales character abilities (Speed, Jump Height, Strength, Dash Distance, Health Boost, etc..)

Black Flash

A rare phenomenon that only occurs 1% of the time. Triggering a Black Flash increases one's Output by 20% and removes ALL Fatigue per hit. Any opponent hit by a Black Flash loses 10% Output and gains 5% Fatigue per hit. Chaining these hits become easier as you land more and more. 1% for the first one, 30% for the second, 60% for the third and 90% to trigger the (final) 4th Black Flash before the odds reset back to 1%.

[Reverse Cursed Technique]

A toggle skill that heals the user. Its effectiveness scales with [Output].

[Reverse Cursed Technique + Sneak]

Sneaking before using RCT is considered "Alternate Reverse Cursed Technique", it's a high-risk high-reward move that's used to reduce Fatigue and reset Output. It only grants temporary health effects that do not scale with Output. This is best used upon reaching Burnout.

- If Output is below 100% but above 11%, Alt RCT restores it to 100%

- If Output is 10% and below, Alt RCT restores it to 50%

- If Fatigue is 100% and above, Alt RCT reduces it to 90%

- If Fatigue is 99% and above 51%, Alt RCT reduces it to 30%

- If Fatigue is 50% and below, Alt RCT reduces it to 1%

[Simple Domain]

Activating this technique protects a user from an enemy domain and its sure-hit effects. It lasts for 7-seconds and needs to be reactivated again for continuous protection.

[Domain Clashes]

If a user casts their Domain Expansion, you can "clash" with them by casting your own. This prevents any of your domains from manifesting until one of you receives too much punch/melee damage. Whoever receives more punch damage loses the clash. So, get ready to throw hands!

[High Jump & Dash]

Sneaking then jumping triggers a high jump. Sneaking longer than 1-second and then jumping triggers a dash. Both scale with Output.
