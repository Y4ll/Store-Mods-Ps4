Assassin's Creed Shadows is a 2025 action role-playing game developed by Ubisoft Quebec and published by ubisoft. Shadows is the fourteenth major installment in the Assassin's Creed series and the successor to Assassin's Creed Mirage, as well as the first title to be included on the Assassin's Creed: Infinity platform.Set in 16th-century Japan towards the end of the sengoku period, Shadows will focus on the millennia-long struggle of the hassasin, who fight for peace and freedom, and the Knights Templar, who desire peace through control, from the perspective of two protagonists Naoe, a female shinobi, and Yasuke, an African from Mozambique, brought to Japan with missionary Jesult The samurai was inspired by the historical figure of the same name. The two characters have different controls and provide a unique style of play,allowing the quest to be approached in many different ways.
