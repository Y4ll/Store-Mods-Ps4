Blocklings

Build Your Own Living Team

Blocklings brings a full collection of companion creatures to Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
Each Blockling has its own identity, behavior, visuals, and gameplay role, turning your world into something much more alive than a simple pet addon.

Tame them, travel with them, use their abilities, and build a team of companions that can help you farm, fight, explore, and automate parts of your survival experience.

What This Addon Includes

- 18 unique Blocklings with different themes, roles, and abilities

- Full taming, growing, breeding, sitting, and following systems

- Farming-focused Blocklings for crops, bamboo, berries, melons, pumpkins, and more

- Combat-focused Blocklings with knockback, crowd control, fire, support auras, and special attacks

- Gadget systems that unlock new powers on specific companions

- Custom particles, textures, models, sounds, and animations

- Advanced Bedrock scripting for smarter and more dynamic behavior

- Reworked spawn rules so Blocklings appear in more fitting biomes and environments

Meet The Blocklings

Nature and Farming

- Dirt Blockling grows saplings and plant-based gadgets

- Tundra Dirt Blockling focuses on sweet berries and colder environments

- Hay Blockling helps with wheat-themed farming

- Pumpkin Blockling is tied to pumpkin farming

- Watermelon Blockling supports melon farming

- Bamboo Blockling farms bamboo and controls enemies with powerful knockback and bamboo cages

- Amethyst Blockling grows crystal-themed plant gear

Utility and Special Systems

- Lapis Blockling stores experience and can provide XP support

- Iron Blockling can mine for you

- Obsidian Blockling works like a mobile forge and can smelt materials

- Portal Blockling gives your world teleportation-based utility

- Redstone Blockling uses a powered gadget core and glow system

- Shroomlight Blockling acts as a living light source

Combat and Support

- Diamond Blockling is a heavy damage companion

- Emerald Blockling uses an offensive aura ability

- Gold Blockling provides support and defensive value

- Honey Blockling summons help and supports the player with swarm-based combat

- Netherite Blockling unleashes fire-based attacks and area burn effects
