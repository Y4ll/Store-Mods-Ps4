Bare Bones PvP Remastered

The long-awaited remaster of the classic Bare Bones PvP Is Here! Packed with PvP utilities, visual clarity enhancements, and modern features, all perfectly styled to fit Bare Bones.

Small Tools & Potions

Downsized tools and potions to free up screen real estate and maximize your field of view during combat.

Transparent Lowered Shield & Totem

Lowered semi-transparent shield and totem models so your vision stays completely unobstructed.

Outlined Glowing Ores & Glowing Armor Trims

Outlined and glowing ores (supports Vibrant Visuals) plus glowing armor trims for instant identification. (Note: Glowing Armor Trims do not support Vibrant Visuals).

Low Fire & Particle Enhancements

Reduced fire height, clean semi-transparent potion particles, and an integrated Particles Core for optimal visual clarity.

Clear Tooltips, Clean HUD & Numbered Hotbar

Numbered hotbar slots for quick switching, clean tooltips, and removed Pumpkin/Spyglass sight overlays.

Shooting Level & Trap Chest Indicators

Visual indicators for bow/crossbow charge levels and distinct outlines for trapped chests to avoid ambush traps.

Dark UI Version (Optional)

An optional sleek Dark UI featuring Quick Craft functionality and a cleaner interface with the "How to Play" button removed.

Recommended UI: Glassy UI

Looking for maximum screen visibility? Pair this pack with GLASSY UI (CLICK HERE) a fully clean and transparent interface designed to boost your PvP awareness and FPS!

Note: If you choose to use Glassy UI, please download default UI option in this PvP pack (do NOT use the Dark UI version) to avoid interface conflicts. (Glassy UI already has its own Dark UI version!)
