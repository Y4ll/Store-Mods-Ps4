One addon, every tool you actually need. World Utilities packs 9 powerful utilities into a single clean menu teleportation, waypoints, weather, time, tool repair, auto collect, flight, and smart mining features. All accessible from one item, no cheats required.

What's Included

- Waypoints save any location with a custom name and teleport back anytime, even across dimensions

- Teleport Menu teleport to coordinates, to other players, bring players to you, or browse your full TP history (last 20 locations)

- Teleport to Last Death instantly return to where you last died, across any dimension

- Weather Control set Clear, Rain, or Thunderstorm instantly from the menu

- Time Control jump to Day, Noon, Sunset, Night, Midnight, or Sunrise in one tap

- Tool Repairer repair any damaged tool or armor piece instantly, one by one or all at once

- Auto Collect automatically pull item drops into your inventory within range; toggle Ores, Crops, Logs, or build a custom list

- Flight Feather a craftable item that launches you into the air with right-click and glides you safely down

- Connected Glass all glass types automatically connect seamlessly when placed next to each other

Feature Settings (Toggleable)

- Tree Capacitor axe fells the entire tree in one break

- Vein Miner pickaxe mines entire ore veins at once with Fortune support

- Grid Miner pickaxe area-mines in a 33 to 99 grid (adjustable)

- Offhand Torch dynamic light from any light-emitting item in hand or offhand

- Auto Totem automatically equips a Totem of Undying to your offhand when available

- Harvest Hoe any hoe harvests and replants an entire crop patch at once

- Auto Smelt & Cook all raw ores and food automatically smelt the moment they drop, world-wide

Commands

- /wu:menu or /wu:m open the main World Utilities menu

- /wu:get get the menu item and Flight Feather directly

- /wu:waypoints open your waypoints list

- /wu:teleport open the teleport menu

- /wu:back teleport to your last death location

- /wu:weather open the weather menu

- /wu:time open the time menu

- /wu:repair open the tool repairer

- /wu:settings open feature toggles

How to Use

PC: Right-click the World Utilities menu item to open, or type any /wu: command in chat.
Mobile: Hold screen with the menu item to open, or type any /wu: command in chat.

Achievement Friendly?

Yes fully achievement friendly. Use it without any worries.
