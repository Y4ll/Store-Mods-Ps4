Minecraft But Chests are OP makes every chest worth opening by replacing ordinary loot with powerful, tier-based rewards. Chests are divided into different tiers depending on the structure you find them in, with better structures offering increasingly powerful loot. Even early-game chests can now give you lightly enchanted iron or diamond gear, while higher-tier chests can contain netherite, diamond blocks, enchanted golden apples, totems, and other rare items.

Village chests are also more unique, with each villager profession having its own signature loot pool. If you're extremely lucky, any chest, barrel, or vault has a 10% chance to trigger the Jackpot, instantly giving you two distinct pieces of fully max-enchanted gear plus a bonus reward.

Features

- Tiered Chest Loot - Loot gets better depending on the structure and chest tier.

- OP Rare & Epic Loot - Find netherite ingots, diamond blocks, enchanted golden apples, Totems of Undying, shulker shells, and more.

- Village Signature Loot - Every villager profession has its own dedicated chest loot pool.

- More Reasons to Explore - Structures become much more rewarding and exciting to discover.
