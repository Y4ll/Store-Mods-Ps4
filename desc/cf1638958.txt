Solaris Visuals is my golden hour pack. Warm light at both ends of the day, and every biome takes it differently.

What this changes

Golden hour

Sunrise and sunset both get real attention here, not just dusk. Warm light comes in low across the ground twice a day, and the pack is tuned around those two windows.

Savanna

Deep orange sky against blue at sunset. The contrast is strongest here, which is why savanna is where most people notice the pack first.

Jungle

Golden sunrise with warm tones flooding in over the canopy. Mornings in a jungle are the best moment in this pack.

Cherry groves

Pink petal ground cover under a clear blue sky. The grove stays pink here rather than being pulled warm along with everything else.

Plains

Bright green meadows with soft pink tinted clouds sitting above them. Open, light, easy to look at for a long time.

Snowy villages

Crisp clean blue sky over white snow. This is the one place the pack goes cool instead of warm, so snow reads as actually cold.

Swamps

Dark rich water with vivid green foliage over it. Swamps feel heavy and full rather than washed out and grey.

Requirements

THIS PACK IS FOR VIBRANT VISUALS ONLY
IT DOES NOT WORK WITH MB LOADER

Vibrant Visuals must be ON. This pack is built for Bedrock's Vibrant Visuals renderer. The pack will not do anything if it is off.

Where to turn it on: Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals

If that toggle is not there, your device or version does not support Vibrant Visuals yet.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Solaris Visuals

- Turn on Settings > Video > Vibrant Visuals

- If the lighting still looks the same, leave the world and load it again once

FAQ

The pack is not working. Nothing changed after I installed it.
I get this comment a lot, and almost every single time the reason is the same. Vibrant Visuals is turned off.

This pack only works when Vibrant Visuals is ON. That is written clearly at the top of this page. With it off, the pack loads fine but nothing on your screen changes, because there is no renderer for it to work with. The pack is not broken.

Go to Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals and switch it on. If you cannot find that option at all, your device or Minecraft version does not support it yet.

Please check this before commenting that the pack does not work.
