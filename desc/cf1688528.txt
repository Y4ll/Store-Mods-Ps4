Mining OP Addon

This addon adds a new mining mechanic where breaking stone can randomly drop super overpowered loot including rare minerals, enchanted gear, valuable resources, and many more surprises!

Features

-

Mine stone to trigger random OP drops

-

Obtain rare minerals and valuable resources

-

Receive enchanted items with powerful effects

-

Many more hidden rewards to discover!
