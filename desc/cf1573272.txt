Flatworld Villages [Bedrock]

Bored of the endless empty flat world? Flatworld Villages completely changes the rules of survival! This addon adds lively, well-crafted villages that generate directly in flat worlds and at almost any height.

Each settlement is assembled using the jigsaw system. Roads, buildings, decorations, and loot are distributed randomly. No two villages are the same every single spawn feels completely fresh!

Key Features

- Lively Flat World: True survival experience instead of empty space.

- Jigsaw Generation: Unique structure and layout for every settlement.

- Vanilla Loot: Balanced random items hidden inside chests.

- Custom Flat Worlds: Works perfectly even in TNT worlds or custom layouts.

- New Progression: Engaging goals for exploration and development.

- Structures: 21 structures enabled.

- [NEW] Custom Generation Height: Set it manually or let default AI handle it.

Spawn Frequency Settings

The addon features 5 generation modes to fit any playstyle. You can easily switch between them in the pack settings:

- Extreme Rare ultra-rare and high-value discoveries.

- Rare challenging search for hardcore survival fans.

- Default classic and most balanced experience.

- Frequent high density of villages for fast-paced gameplay.

- Infinite an almost endless world made of settlements.

How to Install

- Download and install the behavior pack.

- Activate the pack in your world settings.

- Enable all options in the Experimental Settings toggle.

- Click the gear icon next to the pack to select your preferred spawn mode.

- Recommended: Set your simulation distance to 812 chunks for optimal performance.
