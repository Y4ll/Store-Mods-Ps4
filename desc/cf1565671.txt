Creative Wand [Survival/Creative/Spectator Toggle]

Tired of typing long, annoying commands to switch gamemodes or manage your multiplayer server? This addon gives you a simple, craftable magic wand to instantly toggle between Creative and Survival mode. No commands, no cheats, and no experimental toggles required.

How to Use

Simply Long-Press (Hold screen/Right-click) for 3 seconds with the wand equipped. A clean timer will appear, and your gamemode will switch instantly. That's it.

To change another player's gamemode, simply Attack (Hit) them with the wand. It deals zero damage and switches their mode instantly!

Crafting & Auto-Unlock

Place 1 Log (any wood) and 2 Sticks diagonally in a crafting table. The recipe auto-unlocks the moment you pick up a stick in your world, so there's no confusion.

Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.22+

- Works on Android, iOS, Windows, Console

- Compatible with other addons

Installation

- Download the .mcaddon file

- Tap/double-click to auto-import into Minecraft

- Apply both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack to your world settings

- Play - The wand is ready to craft and use immediately!
