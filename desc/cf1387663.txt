Useful Emerald V1.5

Make Emerald more useful in Minecraft Bedrock!

Useful Emerald expands the uses of Emerald by adding powerful tools, armor, weapons, horse armor, and special Emerald-based equipment.

Emerald is normally most useful for trading with Villagers. This addon gives it a much bigger role in Survival by allowing you to turn Emeralds into powerful equipment.

Emerald Horse Armor

Protect your horse with Emerald Horse Armor.

Emerald Horse Armor gives your horse additional protection while matching the Emerald equipment set.

Off-Hand Support

Keep your main hand free.

Emerald tools can be placed in the off-hand.

This allows you to keep another item in your main hand while still having access to your Emerald equipment.

Achievement Friendly

No Experimental Features Required

One of the main goals of Useful Emerald is to keep the addon suitable for normal Survival gameplay.

Experimental Features are NOT required.

You can use the addon without enabling Experimental Features.

Installation

-

Download the latest .mcaddon file.

-

Open the file with Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

-

Wait for Minecraft to import the addon.

-

Create a new world or edit an existing world.

-

Activate Useful Emerald.

-

Enter your world
