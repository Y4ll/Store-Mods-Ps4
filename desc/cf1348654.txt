Enhance your resource gathering in Minecraft with this all-in-one addon that combines Tree Capitator and Vein Miner. Instantly chop entire trees with one swing and mine full ore veins effortlessly, saving time and effort. It also has leaf capitator with loot system.

Key Features:

- Tree Capitator: Instantly fell entire trees.

- Vein Miner: Mine connected ore veins in one go

- Leaf Capitator also gives loot like apple , sticks ..

Showcase

How To Use:

- Just Crouch and Break tree or mine ores to use this

- Take your Minecraft experience to the next level with the ultimate resource-gathering addon.

Note: To activate pack , Edit or Create your world and tap on the behaviour Section , Then Activate
