VENOM Client PvP

VENOM is a PvP texture pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition, designed to deliver a clean, smooth, and competitive gameplay experience while maintaining excellent performance on all devices.

PvP Optimized

Every texture has been carefully designed to improve combat visibility and provide a more comfortable PvP experience. From weapons to the user interface, everything is focused on gameplay.

A New Visual Direction

VENOM is currently moving toward a softer and more cartoon-inspired art style. Future updates will continue to improve textures with cleaner colors, smoother details, and a more polished overall appearance while preserving PvP visibility.
