Custom Damage Sounds+ V1.5

Tired of the same old, boring damage sound every time you take a hit? Custom Damage Sounds+ is the ultimate audio overhaul for Minecraft Bedrock! This pack allows you to fully customize your "Hurt" sound through an easy-to-use in-game slider.

Whether you want a competitive advantage, a nostalgic trip, or just want to laugh while falling into the void, this pack has something for you!

Key Features

- 40 Unique Sound Modes: From classic sounds to the wildest memes.

- In-Game Slider (Subpacks): Switch sounds instantly without leaving the game (requires world restart to apply).

- Optimized for PvP: Includes "CLEAN" sounds that are short and sharp, perfect for competitive play.

- The CHAOS Update: Introducing a randomized mode for the truly brave!

Featured Sound Categories

CUSTOM Mode

Varied sounds specifically edited to fit perfectly with Minecraft's atmosphere.

- Includes: Thorns, Classic Hurt, Enderdragon, Villager, and more!

CLEAN Mode

Specifically designed for PvP and Competitive players. These sounds provide clear audio feedback without cluttering your game.

- Featured: Sharp Sword hits and Pickaxe impact sounds.

MEME Mode

Make your deaths legendary with the internet's most iconic sounds.

- Highlights: Vine Boom, Roblox Oof, GTA V Wasted, Metal Pipe, Among Us Kill, Duck Toy, and the legendary Fahh.

CHAOS Mode (NEW!)

Can't decide? Let the game choose for you! Every time you take damage, a random meme sound will play. No two fights will sound the same! It'll feel like you're in a viral video!

Some New Sounds

- Originals: Minecraft Default, Legacy Minigames (Old School).

- Creatures: Villager, Dolphin, Enderdragon, Enderman, Wandering Trader, Pufferfish Sting.

- Effects: Explode, Pop, Shield, Critical Hit, Broken Potion, Orb.

- Memes: Cat Laugh, Pluh, Gawddamn, Lego Break, among many others!

How to Install & Use

- Download the .mcpack and open it with Minecraft.

- Go to Settings > Global Resources.

- Activate Custom Damage Sounds+.

- Click the Gear Icon () next to the "Deactivate" button.

5. Use the slider to change the sound and enjoy!
