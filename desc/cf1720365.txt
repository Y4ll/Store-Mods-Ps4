Also this remake adds 4 new structures: satelite dish, big silo, abandoned factory and landmine trap

-

Abandoned factory:

In mainly this structure hasn't good loot, but there's has one thing which you should visit it, and this is a geiger counter. It's really important thing which shows statistic about radiation level.

And yes, don't stay here for a long time, because this place consists a radiation, which will kills you in ~5 minutes

2. Landmine trap

How to detect it? Always look at the tallgrass count in one point. If you see an unnaturally large clump of grass, so proably it's a landmine

It will appears in the world pretty often, so be attentive

3. Big missile silo

This is not usual missile silo. First of all, this is a bunker with protect from small and middle projectiles. The only ones weapons can hit the bunker in - ballistic missile and nuclear weapons.

Also this building has 6 missile silos, which have ability to speed up missiles flight in 1.5x

4. Satelite dish

In mainly this structure serves a decorative function. BUT also this structure has steel blocks, very needed material for creating nuclear bombs, missiles and etc.

Also i added two secret chests in two structures - one in the old, and other in the new. Good luck to find them all!
