FreeCam Minecraft Bedrock

FreeCam is a camera addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition that allows you to freely explore your world from a different perspective without moving your player.

Whether you're creating content, recording cinematic shots, exploring builds, or simply want a better view of your world, FreeCam gives you more freedom over your camera.

Commands

FreeCam can be controlled directly from the Minecraft chat.

Activate FreeCam

/free On

Deactivate FreeCam

/free Off

Configuration

/config

Use /config to access the available settings and customize your FreeCam experience.

For a complete explanation of all features, settings, installation and commands, check the tutorial video.

https://youtu.be/bjsKom70yr0

Mobile & PC

FreeCam is designed to work on both mobile devices and PC, making it accessible regardless of your preferred way to play Minecraft Bedrock.

Multiplayer

FreeCam includes a dedicated multiplayer section, allowing you to configure and use the addon in multiplayer worlds.

You can also configure FreeCam depending on how you want players to access its features.

Achievements

FreeCam does not disable Minecraft achievements.

You can use the addon while playing Survival, Hardcore and other game modes without having to sacrifice your achievements.

Important

Always make a backup of your world before installing any addon, especially when using it in an important survival or hardcore world.
