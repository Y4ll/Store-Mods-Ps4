This Minecraft Bedrock script adds functional Portal Gun mechanics inspired by the Portal series to the game

Key Features:

Dual Portal Guns: Shoot blue and orange projectiles to create linked portals on walls, floors, or ceilings

Momentum Teleportation: Instantly teleports players and entities between active portals while preserving velocity, impulse, and rotation.

Long Fall Boots: Special boots detect high-velocity falls and dynamically grant resistance right before impact to eliminate fall damage

Smart Validation & Cleanup: Automatically checks for valid air space before placing portals, cleans up orphaned entities on player respawn or disconnect, and features custom audio and HUD elements
