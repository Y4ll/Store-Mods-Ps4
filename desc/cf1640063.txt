Ever wished the iconic Trailer look had just a bit more polish, color, and functionality?

Bare Bones but Better! takes the clean, vibrant aesthetic of the original Bare Bones pack and dials it up to eleven. Packed with community-driven enhancements, subtle 3D details, quality-of-life UI tweaks, and glowing elements, this is the ultimate way to play Minecraft with trailer-style visuals without missing out on modern pack features!

Key Features & Visual Upgrades

- Saturated & Vibrance Boost: Saturated blocks, vibrant foliage.

- Glowing Effects: Emissive textures for End Portals, torches, and armor trims for maximum ambiance in the dark.

- Clearer World Details: Seamless clear glass, glass doors, and refined redstone visual indicators.

- Quality of Life & UI: Numbered hotbars, 25-age kelp indicators, improved fence connections, new Bare Bones hearts and dirt variations.

- 3D Elements & Fixing: reworked bed models/textures and logged hanging signs.

- And more!
