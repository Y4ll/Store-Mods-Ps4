The Knights O'Plenty addon by Creator Studios adds many different types of Knights to the game. These Knights belonged to Minecraftia empire's different factions or clans but after the fierce clans hostile attempts to gain absolute power and summoning the ender knight commander to almost destroy the world has led to the downfall of Minecraftia.

Some of it's structures still remain inhabited by fierce clan knights and their heinous attempts still continue to destroy the world. Go on explore, fight and obtain the ultimate power and experience the remnants of the past.

This world has the ancient metal of forge named Orichalcum, five new structures and souls, a friendly companion mob summoned through special catalysts called charms.

The Orichalcum Ore is a new ore in the addon. It can found all over the Overworld in the surface or in the caves. It's actually a pretty common ore but it requires at least an Iron Pickaxe or higher to mine.

It can be smelted in a furnace to obtain the Orichalcum Ingot that can be used to craft Orichalcum tools and armor and much more.

The Orichalcum Block can be crafted with 6 Orichalcum Ingots and 3 Iron Ingots as Orichalcum requires iron to provide the grip.

The Orichalcum Block is used in creating the Orichalcum Armor and some tools and their recipes are unlocked from start in crafting table.

The Orichalcum Armor grants it's owner an extra HP when worn and provide more protection than a fully enchanted Netherite Armor.

The Orichalcum Tools And Weapons

The Orichalcum can be used to craft 7+ tools and weapons, each giving the user a different buff.

Like the Orichalcum Pickaxe that grants Haste when held or the Orichalcum Longsword that has a slower attack speed but damages nearby mobs with AOT or the Orichalcum Sharp sword made with iron and Orichalcum that lets you dash forward.

Each weapon has a distinct crafting recipe some easy while some hard. Download the addon to know them all.

Now let's talk about the knights that roam the world. Basically there are three types of Knights you can encounter throughout your journey the Good Knights , the Bad Knights and the Traders Knights. The Good Knights are the knights that roam the Overworld in broad daylight, defeating the hostile mobs and are considered players friends. They are mostly armed with Orichalcum Armor and Swords, but they are not hostile unless you attack first.

The Royal Knights

Spawn in plains and forest biome.

Often carry Orichalcum Swords and Shield to hunt hostile mobs.

The Paladin Knights

Spawns in Plains and Birch Forests.

Often in groups roaming the world.

The Masked Knights

Spawns in Desert and Nether Biomes.

Often fights the Fierce Knights Group.

The Female Knight

Captured by the Angry Witch Boss in her wood tower in Swamp Biomes.

Defeat the Angry Witch and befriend her with diamonds to make her your companion.

The Fat Pig

Not a Knight but rather a really fat pig with 100 HP.

Belongs to the Golds Clan but drops porkchop instead of Coins.

The Bad Knights are what their name suggests Bad and hostile towards the players. They don't spawn around the world much other than the nether and can be mostly found in their respective structures in the Overworld. They are faster and deal a lot of damage. If you see one either run or climb up two blocks.

The Smoked Knights

Spawn only in the Obsidian House or the Wither Ruins.

Have 100 HP and deal a lot of damage.

The Golden Knights

Spawn in the Nether naturally.

Drops gold ingots and Orichalcum when killed.

The Caped Knights

Spawn in the Brick House and Wither Ruins and sometimes in the Overworld during night time.

Slow but tanky with 120 HP

The Ruined Knights

Summoned by the Bosses, Ender Knight Commander and the Small Eye.

Bosses Allies, deal a lot of damage (5 hearts when wearing Iron Armor)

The Cherry Knights

Spawns in cherry biome and cherry towers.

Deals 2 hearts but has a speed of snail.
