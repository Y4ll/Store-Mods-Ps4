The past has not disappeared. It has left echoes.

on Structures is an addon for Minecraft Bedrock that focuses on rare structures inspired by forgotten eras, lost civilizations, alternate histories, and Minecraft's own past.

Scattered across your survival world are locations that should have been lost to time.

Ancient monuments. Forgotten strongholds. Fossilized ruins. Mysterious machines. And fragments of Minecraft's own history.

Explore them. Discover what remains

on Structures is built around exploration and discovery.

Structures appear naturally and rarely throughout the world, giving you a reason to travel beyond the familiar.

You may find:

Monumental ancient structures
Fossilized remains from a forgotten world
Forgotten castles and strongholds
Hidden structures inspired by Feudal Japan
Age Of Sail
Mysterious clockwork sites
- Echoes of Minecraft's own forgotten past

But these places are more than landmarks.

Some hide dangers. Some hide secrets. And some simply make you wonder how they got there.
