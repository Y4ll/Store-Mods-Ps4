Cromos Realistic is a Vibrant Visuals resource pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. It gives water a clearer, turquoise look, adds more noticeable animated ripples, and tunes volumetric fog to highlight sunlight between trees. It also includes underwater visibility adjustments, soft shadows, and custom colors for light sources.

Choose one preset: LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH. Enable Vibrant Visuals in the games video settings, then activate only one Cromos Realistic preset.

Created by Cromos: https://www.youtube.com/@cromosg

Note: The ripples are a visual effect; they do not physically raise or lower water blocks. The pack does not add handheld torch lighting or glowing mob eyes.
