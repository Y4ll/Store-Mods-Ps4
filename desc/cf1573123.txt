This mod adds Bon The Rabbit from The Walten Files into Minecraft, he can stalk you, jumpscare you, and much more.

Imagine yourself, wandering through a dense forest at night, you hear something behind you, "don't look back" the voice said, or maybe you're just hearing things, regardless you run back to the saftey of your house, you look outside, theres nothing there, you start to here the clanking of metal, you turn around, but it's already too late.

This mod is meant for singleplayer use only
