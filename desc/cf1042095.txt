Tired of losing your items when you die this addon is right for you!

This add-on adds 3 gravestones that spawns and picks up your items when you die.

They have their own inventory you can unlock and access it and retrieve all your items and pretend nothing happened.

This add-on can store your items when you die but it can't save your items from lava.

Grave Addon Command:

Using /scriptevent command

- grave:clean - use to remove all graves inside the world.

When you die you will receive a Grave Key.

You can use this grave key as a crafting recipe to craft the Instant Grave Key.

The instant grave key will let you teleport to your previous location where you died.

Use this key to access your grave.

I would not recommend dying from lava because your items might get burned before the gravestone can store your items.
