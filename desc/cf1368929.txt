Core Features & Capabilities

- 200+ Procedural Tree Profiles: Over 200+ handcrafted growth profiles covering Oak, Spruce, Birch, Jungle, Acacia, Dark Oak, Cherry, Mangrove, Pale Oak, Bamboo, Azalea, Crimson Fungus, and Warped Fungus!

- Massive Variety of Archetypes: Grow small detailed shrubs, twisted windblown trunks, weeping canopies with hanging vines, weeping willows, hollow ancient elders, and colossal multi-trunk titan behemoths!

- Stripped Log & Bark Blending: Natural trunks feature realistic stripped log cores, multi-layer bark tapering, and organic root flare networks embedded into the ground.

- Dynamic Seasons Engine: Foliage dynamically shifts with the active world season:

- Spring: Blooming flower canopies, cherry blossoms, and vibrant green buds.

- Autumn: Rich golden, red, and azalea-mixed leaf fall palettes.

- Winter: Snow-capped needle crowns and sparse winter canopies.

- Normal: Pure lush vanilla forestry.

- Player Favourites & Quick-Grow: Save up to 3 of your most-used tree designs per player for instant 1-click planting without scrolling menus!

- Instant Undo Protection (/scriptevent nasa:undo): Placed a giant tree in the wrong spot? Revert your last generated tree instantly and restore up to 300 blocks to their exact original state.

- Admin Forest Generator: Server admins can batch-generate full natural woodlands across configurable footprints: Small (8 trees), Medium (15 trees), Large (25 trees), or Massive (40 trees)!

- Fast Growth & Animated HUD Progress: Choose between animated real-time growth with custom sapling particles and action-bar progress bars, or instant Fast Growth for rapid building.

- Safe Placement Engine: Built-in safeguards check structural density, height caps, and block replaceability to ensure trees never overwrite player builds, chests, or valuable blocks.

How to Use

- Sneak & Place: In Survival or Creative mode, simply Sneak (crouch) and place any supported sapling, propagule, or fungus on the ground.

- Select Your Variant: The Grower UI will appear instantly, displaying all available designs, variant tags (Roots, Multi-Trunk, Vines, Snow, Fungus), your personal stats, and the Random roll option.

- Watch It Grow: Select your chosen tree variant and watch the fractal algorithm grow your custom tree before your eyes!

Quick Commands & Chat Shortcuts

- !trees or /scriptevent nasa:trees_menu Open the Player Tools & Preferences Menu.

- !undo or /scriptevent nasa:undo Undo your last generated tree.

- !treestats or /scriptevent nasa:stats View your lifetime trees grown and blocks placed.

- !stoptrees or /scriptevent nasa:stop Emergency cancel all your active growth jobs.

- /scriptevent nasa:season_<name> (Admin) Set world season (normal, autumn, spring, winter).
