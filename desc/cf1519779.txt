D Lucid Light is a professional-grade technical utility designed specifically for Minecraft Bedrock Edition players who want the benefits of a "Fullbright" experience without sacrificing their ability to earn achievements. Unlike traditional texture packs that can look washed out or experimental mods that disable progress, this addon uses a logic-based "Smart Toggle" system to provide high-intensity Night Vision only when you need it.

KEY FEATURES

Zero-Experiment Compatibility Runs entirely on the vanilla engine, meaning your world remains "pure" and trophies/achievements stay active.

Universal Off-Hand Activation No specialized modded items required; simply place any vanilla item (arrow, totem, shield) into your off-hand to trigger the effect.

Sensory Feedback System Features custom audio "clicks" and Action Bar notifications to provide instant confirmation when the effect is toggled.

Visual Clarity The Night Vision effect is applied with the "Hide Particles" flag, ensuring your screen remains clear of intrusive potion bubbles.
