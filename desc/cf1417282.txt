Better Structure Loot is a simple addon that attempts to balance the vanilla structure loot, while also not making it too overpowered. With this addon enabled, you should no longer find chests with just a couple pieces of wheat or string inside of them, making structure loot feel a lot more rewarding rather than just a small bonus. Most structure loot tables have recieved a lot of new items, however I have made sure that no items included in the vanilla loot tables were removed.

This addon is achievement friendly and does not require any experimental toggles to be enabled.

If you have any questions or find any issues with this addon, you can contact me in the comments or on Discord (lionhead07)

You can see exactly what changes were made to certain structure loot tables below, however pretty much all structure chests now contain:

- Double the amount of items

- Double the chance for rarer items (Diamonds, enchanted books and tools, armor trims, nautilus armor, etc.) to appear over less rare items

- Newer items and materials

- A lower chance to generate "worse" items like rotten flesh or wheat seeds
