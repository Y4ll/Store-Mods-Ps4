Spectral Horizons

What is Spectral Horizons

Spectral Horizons is a Vibrant Visuals Pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition that greatly transforms how your game can look with vibrant colors, unique PBR, and even colored fog. It can be used on its own or with other texture packs to even add to the experience.

What makes Spectral Horizons Unique?

There are a lot of things that make Spectral Horizons unique when compared to other Vibrant Visuals packs. While most packs aim to bring realism into the world of Minecraft, Spectral Horizons decides to do the opposite and brings bright, vibrant colors, colored fog, custom moon colors in select biomes, and a lot of extra glowing parts in blocks to make the world of Minecraft feel more like a cartoony world than a real one.

How to activate Spectral Horizons

Below are the steps to get Spectral Horizons working in your world.

Note: You will need a compatible Vibrant Visuals device to use Spectral Horizons, and if your device cannot run Vibrant Visuals normally, it cannot run Spectral Horizons as well.

- Download Spectral Horizons and import it into Minecraft

- Go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Spectral Horizons

- Turn on Vibrant Visuals in your video settings

- Load into a world and enjoy the visuals!

Spectral Horizons Subpacks

Spectral Horizons comes built-in with 3 different subpacks, with each one offering how you can enjoy Spectral Horizons.

- Subpack 1: 32x Normal Maps - This is the default for Spectral Horizons and offers the most detail and extra features compared to the other 2. This is recommended if you want the most out of Spectral Horizons

- Subpack 2: Height Maps - This is the subpack of the pack that changes PBR over from Normal Maps over to Height Maps. This subpack comes with fewer features, but is recommended to use if you want an experience that fits Minecraft more closely

- Subpack 3: Vanilla - This subpack completely removes all custom PBR from Spectral Horizons and is required if you want to use other Texture Packs alongside Spectral Horizons. This subpack offers the lowest amount of features but the most amount of compatibility.
