This is the classic "lite" v5 version of one of the biggest SCP add-ons of 2020/21! Even though it's called "lite" it actually has more features than v4, and is more optimised. It adds many SCP related aspects to your Minecraft world.

- SCPs/Entities- Structures/Biomes - Weapons - Armour/Morphs- Bosses

Attention!

This is a really old add-on! Some features may not work properly, but most features should still be fine. I've fixed up the blocks to show up in the creative menu and fixed up most of the items.
