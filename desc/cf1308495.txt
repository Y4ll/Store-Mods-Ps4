Super CPS Counter

See how fast you truly click with this minimal CPS (Clicks Per Second)!

Just go and do PvP as you usually do, and see your CPS at the actionbar! (You can see your final cps in chat)

You can also customize CPS test duration! By default, this is 1 second. Just run this command: /function cps/set. It will open a UI and you can adjust test length (from 0.5 seconds to 10 seconds).

NEW function cps/reset command to reset CPS and settings.

For best experiences, use it with Better Knockback. It allows you to customise knockback and get the best of PvP.

Key Features

- Works in Multiplayer

- Lightweight and Lag free

- Totally Error free, no bugs!

- Real time (resets per second or as defined by you) Counter

- Customize Length

- Multi version support

- Works with any Addon/Script

- Does not count wasted clicks.

- No player.json
