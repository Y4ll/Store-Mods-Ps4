Now to the basic features:

- If you got hit by a furry, there's a chance you'll get infect, this chance changes for each game difficulty. Your character will shake if you're infected, but not yet a furry. The furry that you'll eventually turn into is exactly like the one you got infected from. (If you don't like humanoid animal kind of furry, you can type "/customizefurry" command in the chat to only show the tail and ears and some other features of a furry that won't cover the player's skin)

- You can also infect other players and villagers once you became a furry, the infected player or villager also get the same kind of furry you are... Except some of them...

- To cancel the transformation progress, you can use Hexylium, the treatment to the infection, to go back to be a normal human. It won't work if you're totally transformed and you have to use Derrium - the cure, to go back being a human, just make sure that no one is coming after you.
