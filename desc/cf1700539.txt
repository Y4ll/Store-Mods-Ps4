Dream Valley is an add-on featuring otherworldly Dreamcore spaces places you feel you have seen before.

This add-on is inspired by the Dreamshift mod from the Java Edition.

For the best experience, it is recommended to play with shaders.

HOW TO PLAY?

Craks will appear in your world. You will enter to the Dream Valleys, you can play with friends if one enters the portal, everyone will be taken to the Dream Valleys.

Find the way out.
