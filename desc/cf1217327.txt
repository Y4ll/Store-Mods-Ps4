Note: This is an unofficial port of the Artifacts mod from Minecraft Java.

Everlasting Beef, Eternal Steak: a food item that doesnt get consumed when eaten, with a 15-second cooldown.

Umbrella: slows your fall when held.

Whoopee Cushion: Occasionally makes a fart noise when worn.

Night Vision Goggles: Grants night vision when worn.

Plastic Drinking Hat: Increases saturation.

Snorkel: Provides water breathing effect.

Superstitious Hat: Applies an extra level of looting to killed entities.

Villager Hat: Decreases trading prices with villagers.

Cowboy Hat: Increases the speed of ridden mounts.

Angler's Hat: Applies extra levels of Lure while fishing.

Necklaces Charm of Sinking: Conduict effect.

Flame Pendant: Chance to light attacking entities on fire

Panic Necklace: Grants a short speed boost after taking damage.

Lucky Scarf: Applies an extra level of fortune to mined blocks.

Scarf of Invisibility: Turns the player invisible.

Shock Pendant: Chance to strike attacking entities with lightning.

Thorn Pendant: Chance to damage attacking entities.

Charm of Shrinking: Shrinks the wearer (only visual).

Antidote Vessel: Reduces the duration of negative status effects.

Cloud in a Bottle: Allows double jumping.

Crystal Heart: Increases maximum health by 6 hearts.

Helium Flamingo: Allows swimming through the air for a short time.

Obsidian Skull: Grants a 30-second fire resistance effect after taking fire damage.

Universal Attractor: Attracts nearby items.

Chorus Totem: Teleports the player on a fatal hit.

Warp Drive: Ender pearls are not consumed but cost hunger instead.

Digging Claws: Increases mining speed.

Feral Claws: Increases attack.

Fire Gauntlet: Causes melee attacks to set entities on fire.

Golden Hook: Increases experience dropped.

Pocket Piston: Increases knockback dealt by the player.

Power Glove: Attacks deal an additional 4 damage.

Vampiric Glove: Heals the player for 20% of damage dealt by melee attacks.

Onion Ring: Grants a temporary haste effect after eating food.

Pickaxe Heater: Automatically smelts mined ores.

Withered Bracelet: Chance to apply a wither effect when attacking an entity.

Aqua-Dashers: Allows sprinting on lava.

Bunny Hoppers: Increases jump height.

Flippers: Increases swimming speed by 100%.

Kitty Slippers: Creepers run away from the player.

Running Shoes: Increases movement speed by 40%.

Steadfast Spikes: Increases knockback resistance.

Snowshoes: Allows walking on powdered snow.

Rooted Boots: Restores hunger when walking on grass.

Strider Shoes: Allows sprinting on lava.

Mimic: A new underground campsite structure has been introduced. Occasionally, Mimics spawn in these campsites instead of chests. These dangerous enemies attack players who get too close and always drop a random artifact when defeated.

What to do if the addon doesn't work?

-When creating the world, make sure this option is activated.

-Delete any previous versions of the addon and keep only the latest one.

-Stay on the latest official non-beta, non-preview versions of the game.

-If the addon still doesn't work for you, join my discord server so I can help you with your problem.

Note:

Don't forget to activate the beta-api from experimental options.
