GODZILLA MINUS ONE ADDON

This addon adds the terrifying creature from a movie called, Godzilla Minus One; Godzilla.

If you ever want Godzilla to exist in Minecraft, then this addon if for you. This addon adds Godzilla into your Minecraft world. The Godzilla in this addon has unique animations, and abilities.

GODZILLA:

- Godzilla has 100,000 health and 100,000 attack damage.

- Has 1 special attack which is the Atomic Blast
