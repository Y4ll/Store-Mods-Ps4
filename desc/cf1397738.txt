OverStack: Ultimate Stack Size & Inventory Manager :

Tired of your inventory filling up after 5 minutes of mining?

OverStack is a revolutionary Bedrock Edition Add-on that shatters the vanilla stack limit! Using advanced Scripting APIs, this add-on allows you to compress items into massive virtual stacks up to 1,000 items per slot keeping your inventory clean and organized.

How It Works

1. The Stack Regulator

When you load the world, the Stack Regulator item will appear in your inventory automatically.

Right-Click (Use) the Regulator to open the Upgrade Menu.

Sneak + Right-Click the Regulator to Pause/Resume the mod.

2. Upgrade Tiers

You start with a limit of 64. To hold more, you must upgrade your Regulator using Experience Levels and Chests.

Need to use your items? Sneak + Right Click while holding a stacked item to unpack it back into vanilla stacks.

Technical Info & Installation

Supported Version:

Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.120+

Requirements:

You must enable Beta APIs in your world settings for the scripts to function correctly.

Compatibility:

This add-on uses the Script API. It is generally compatible with other addons, but items with custom durability or enchantments will not be stacked to prevent data loss.
