Peterbilt Truck Addon
Bring heavy-duty haulage and realistic mechanics directly to your Minecraft world! The Peterbilt Truck Addon offers an immersive, hands-on experience for players who love engineering, customization, and realistic vehicles.

Key Features
Fully Interactive Assembly System: Say goodbye to simple spawn eggs! Build your truck step-by-step by interacting directly with the assembly entity.

Block-Based Truck Parts: Truck components function like placeable blocks, giving you complete control over how you store, display, and build your vehicle.

16 Custom Color Options: Personalize your rig using standard Minecraft dye colors to match your style or fleet branding.

Survival Friendly Crafting: Every part and component can be crafted in survival mode using standard recipes.

Upcoming Updates & Roadmap
Deep Customization System: Advanced modular upgrades, custom engine tuning, and expandable trailer attachments.

Refined Fuel & Maintenance Mechanics: Realistic fuel consumption and wear-and-tear repair mechanics.
