Cave Dweller

The deepest parts of Minecraft were never meant to feel safe.

As you descend into the darkness, strange sounds begin to echo through the caves. At first, they seem familiar... almost identical to Minecraft's normal cave ambience.

Then you realize something terrifying.

The sounds aren't coming from the cave.

They're coming from something inside it.

Features

- Encounter a mysterious humanoid creature lurking deep underground.

- Designed to make cave exploration tense and unpredictable.

- Advanced AI capable of navigating caves in unsettling ways.

- It may silently stalk you before revealing its presence.

- Intense chase sequences when the creature decides to hunt.

- Disturbing sounds inspired by Minecraft's classic cave ambience.

- Psychological horror that builds tension before striking.

- Every cave expedition becomes a risk.

---

How It Works

The surface remains peaceful.

The danger begins once you venture underground.

While exploring caves, you may notice strange noises echoing through the darkness.

A distant sound.

A silhouette disappearing around a corner.

A brief glimpse of something standing far away.

Sometimes it simply watches.

Sometimes it follows.

Sometimes it waits for the perfect moment.

And sometimes...

It starts running.

The creature knows the caves far better than you do, using the environment to stay hidden until it's ready to attack.

---

Explore at Your Own Risk

Mining is no longer relaxing.

Every tunnel could lead somewhere unknown.

Every dark passage might already be occupied.

The deeper you go...

The greater the chance that something else is exploring the cave with you.

Recommended Experience

For the intended experience:

- Play with headphones.

- Keep the volume turned up.

- Explore caves with minimal lighting.

- Avoid spoilers.

- Never ignore strange sounds.
