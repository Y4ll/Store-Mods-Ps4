Carry Light Anywhere in Minecraft Bedrock

System Dynamic Lights adds portable dynamic lighting to Minecraft Bedrock without requiring you to constantly place light blocks.

Hold supported items in your main hand or offhand, wear compatible lantern helmets, drop light-emitting items on the ground, or let supported entities and projectiles illuminate the world around them.

Full V3.3.2 Details

Custom Entity Lights

Operators can now give dynamic lighting to entities from other Add-ons.

You do not need to manually search through files just to find a mob.

Open Custom Entity Lights, move near a custom mob and select it directly from the nearby entity list.

For every registered entity you can choose a:

- Light Level from 1 to 15

You can register up to 24 custom entity light sources.

Custom Entity Lights work when Entity Lighting is set to:

All Supported Entities

You can also edit, remove or clear registered entity lights directly from the menu.

Special Vanilla Entity Lights

The Entity Lighting system now supports more special vanilla entities and projectiles.

Supported special sources include:

- Fireballs

- Small Fireballs

- Dragon Fireballs

- Burning Arrows

- Primed TNT

- End Crystals

- XP Orbs

These sources use different light intensities depending on how bright the entity should visually appear.

Brightest Entity Priority

When several entity light sources overlap, the system now processes them by light intensity.

This prevents a weak source from incorrectly replacing a stronger source.

For example, a nearby XP Orb should no longer override a much brighter entity or projectile light.

The brightest valid source keeps priority in overlapping areas.

TNT Lighting Adjustment

Primed TNT still produces dynamic light, but its light intensity has been reduced to better match its visual appearance and balance it against brighter sources.
