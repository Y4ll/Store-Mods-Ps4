This does support fortune, unbreaking, and silk touch enchantments.

This works with most custom tools based on their identifier.

There are pack settings to choose certain things like if you just want veinminer, or if you just want tree capitator.

This pack uses no experimental options, which means that it shouldn't break with future versions!
If this does well enough, i will continue to update it for future ores and logs.
