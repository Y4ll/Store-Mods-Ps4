A customizable Low Fire texture pack that lowers fire overlay on your screen & removes Lava Fog & Nether Fog

Features:

- No Lava fog.

- No Nether fog.

- Semi-Transparent Fire.

- Works with Vibrant Visuals, HIVE server and RTX.

Subpacks:

- Enhanced Fire - reduces transparency, keeps original textures.

- Low Fire - reduces fire overlay and fire, campfire textures.

- Better Low Fire - reduces fire overlay + transparency and fire, campfire textures.

Requires:

- PandaMine's Mip Mapper
