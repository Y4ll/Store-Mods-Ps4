Do you want your inventory screen to look like the Just Enough Items (JEI) mod from Minecraft Java? Well, this texture pack is for you.

This texture pack changes the appearance of recipe book to look like the Just Enough Items (JEI) mod from Minecraft Java. It's well optimized and gui scale friendly.
