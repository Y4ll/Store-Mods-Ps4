Undyne The Spear of JUSTICE (info)

Undyne The Spear of JUSTICE

Undyne is the leader of the newly formed Royal Guard, aiming to protect the villages from the illagers and the undead.

Not only does she have her spear magic, but she now has a trident as well! She got it from a Drowned that tried dragging her down when she was sitting beside a lake. She uses it a lot in combat, and since it has the loyalty enchantment, she also uses it to grab her enemies from afar! She grabbed things from afar with it too when feeling lazy but she rarely does that now since it caused a lot of destruction back then (and that said destruction was breaking Sans' new chair which was just a brick that he sits on).

Undyne's Stats

- Health: 500

- Attack: 10

- Defense: 2

- Chance of spawning: Rare

- Where she spawns: Spruce Forest and near Sculk Cave (she visits there to feel nostalgic because Sculk Caves looks like Waterfall)

- Can only spawn once; if you spare her she'll be back

Note: You can't be-friends with them yet; we will add this feature soon.

Hunting Phase

"HEY, YOU, RIGHT THERE!"

This is her phase when she's first spawned. She has Warden-like hearing and will attack zombies, illagers, and players that she hears and spots.

She is invulnerable in this phase. Illagers and zombies will try to take her down with anything they have just to ultimately fail. She is also after you for a reason...

Your only option is to HIDE and RUN. You can hide behind walls or in the tallgrass, and be quiet.

There is another option however, if you are brave enough...

To beat her you have to make her miss when she throws her Trident. After that you will see that little sword icon on top of her; when you see it ATTACK HER AS FAST AS YOU CAN. You have to hit her 10 times until she reaches her BOSS FIGHT PHASE. No need to worry about damage, just hit her 10 times.

Hunting Phase Abilities

- Spear Attack: Spears spawn in the air and aim at you. You can dodge them. When hit, it will cast Raining Spears, which are hard to avoid but possible.
