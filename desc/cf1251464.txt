This texture pack enhances Minecraft's block-breaking mechanics by replacing the default cracking animation with a dynamic loading bar. The loading bar provides a clear, visual progression as you mine, making it easier to gauge break timesespecially useful for multiplayer or resource-gathering tasks. The animation is designed to be smooth and unobtrusive.

By improving visual feedback, the pack helps players optimize their mining efficiency, reducing guesswork when switching between tools or blocks with varying hardness. The modernized aesthetic also gives the game a more polished feel without altering core gameplay. Whether you're strip-mining, building, or speedrunning, this pack ensures you always know exactly how much longer a block will take to break.
