About

Inspect blocks and entities instantly without opening menus or interrupting gameplay.

Inspect HUD adds a compact information panel for whatever is under your crosshair. Check the right tool before mining, see whether a crop is mature, inspect container space, read redstone strength, or check an entity's health with one quick look.

Features

- Localized names identifies blocks, mobs, players, and dropped items using their translated or custom names.

- Correct tool recommends a pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, shears, or sword through standard Bedrock tool tags.

- Crop maturity shows growth percentage and exact stage for vanilla crops and compatible custom crops.

- Container fill reports occupied slots for chests, barrels, hoppers, furnaces, and other inventory blocks.

- Special fill levels reads composters, cauldrons, beehives, bee nests, and respawn anchors.

- Redstone power shows the current signal strength from 0 to 15 when applicable.

- Entity details displays health plus baby and tamed states when available.

- Dropped items identifies the item and shows its stack count.

- Personal toggle /inspect or /inspect turns the HUD off or on for the player who runs it and remembers the choice.

- Add-on friendly supports compatible custom blocks and entities that use standard localization, components, crop states, and tool tags.

- Lightweight updates only the compact HUD payload when the inspected information changes.

How to use

- Import the .mcaddon file into Minecraft Bedrock.

- Activate both the Inspect HUD Behavior Pack and Resource Pack for your world.

- Look at a block, mob, player, or dropped item within seven blocks.

- Read the information panel at the top of the screen.

- Run inspect or /inspect whenever you want to hide or restore the panel.

Notes

- The toggle command does not require cheats or operator permission.

- Each multiplayer participant controls their own HUD independently.

- Both included packs are required: the Behavior Pack reads target data and the Resource Pack renders the custom HUD.

- No experimental toggles are required.

- Compatible custom content should use standard Bedrock components, localization keys, crop states, and tool-destructibility tags.

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.40 or newer

- Inspect HUD Behavior Pack and Resource Pack must both be active

- No external add-on dependency required
