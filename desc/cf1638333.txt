REQUIRES A SHADER LOADER TO WORK!!! CUSTOM SHADERS ARE NOT OFFICALLY SUPPORTED

This shaderpack aims to provide a dynamic stylized experience for bedrock edition. Most effects in this shader pack are dynamic. During Rain, sunrise sky wont look the same as noon sky. Fog brightness depends on the direction of the sun and its height, which applies to underwater effect. Details like these are what makes the shader truly dynamic.

It supports Android, Windows and ios.

Features:

stylized skybox

directional lighting

custom ao

Night

sunrise

Cloudy fog

beautiful underwater effects

puddles during raintime

water effects

bliss inspired end sky
