Netherite Armor Fix (v1.5.0+)

A lightweight Minecraft Bedrock script that fixes wind charge interactions with Netherite armor.

Feature List

- Wind Charge Fix: Wind charges affect Netherite armor normally instead of being blocked by knockback resistance.

- Vanilla Logic: Tuned to stay close to Bedrock Edition's core mechanics and physics.

- Enchantment Support: Compatible with Elytra flight and the Wind Burst enchantment.

- Universal Support: Works on mobs and non-mob entities, including armor stands, boats, and minecarts.

- Achievement Friendly: No experimental toggles required.

- PvP & Survival: Designed for PvP, survival, and multiplayer worlds.

Technical Overview

- Modern API Support: Updated for modern Minecraft Bedrock script APIs.

- Optimized Detection: Entity checks are optimized to reduce unnecessary performance impact.

- Plug and Play: Install the behavior pack and play normally without extra commands.

Compatibility

Supported: Netherite armor, Elytra, Wind Burst, mobs, armor stands, boats, and minecarts.

Best for: Survival, PvP, multiplayer, and achievement-friendly worlds.

Changelog
