Create: Synthetic Pressure Bedrock Port

A Minecraft Bedrock port by PanCake of Create: Synthetic Pressure, Angelic_Aether's updated remake of Create: High Pressure by Aweeri.

REQUIRED DEPENDENCY: Create Add-On by Vatonage

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.0 or newer

- Create Add-On 1.0.1 by Vatonage

- Both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack for Create and Synthetic Pressure must be active.

Overview

Create: Synthetic Pressure provides a balanced, automation-focused alternative for producing renewable diamonds without mining.

Originally created by Aweeri with survival progression in mind and later updated by Angelic_Aether, the addon is intentionally costly and challenging while avoiding unnecessary bloat.

This Bedrock port recreates the original processing chain and probabilities while integrating with Create Bedrock's machinery, visuals, sounds, and compatibility API.

New Items & Recipes

Graphite Powder

Process coal or charcoal using an Encased Fan and lava to produce Graphite Powder.

Graphite

Compact Graphite Powder beneath a Mechanical Press using a heated basin.

Each Graphite Powder has a 50% chance to produce Graphite.

Impure Diamond & Diamond Production

Compact Graphite beneath a Mechanical Press using a superheated basin.

Each Graphite independently has:

- 1% chance to produce a Diamond

- 80% chance to produce an Impure Diamond

Recycling & Multiplication

Process Impure Diamonds in Crushing Wheels to recover useful materials.

Each Impure Diamond independently has:

- 90% chance to return Gravel

- 50% chance to return Graphite Powder

- 50% chance to produce an Experience Nugget

Renewable Netherrack

Mix 1 Netherrack + 1 Gravel + 100 mB of Lava in a basin to produce 2 Netherrack.

This provides renewable Netherrack and makes Blaze Cake automation easier.

Bedrock Compatibility

Fan-blasting and crushing recipes register through Create Bedrock's public compatibility API.

Heated and superheated basin compacting are handled directly by this addon because the current public pressing API supports only belt and depot recipes.

Important: Keep unrelated items and native Create recipe ingredients out of Synthetic Pressure compacting basins.

A mixed basin is intentionally blocked until it is emptied. This prevents Create and Synthetic Pressure from attempting to process the same batch.

Diagnostics

For an in-game setup report, stand within 12 blocks of the machine and run:

/scriptevent create_high_pressure:diagnose
