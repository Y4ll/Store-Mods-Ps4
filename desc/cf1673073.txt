Economy System by Baku is a complete multiplayer economy addon designed for Minecraft Bedrock Survival worlds, SMPs, and multiplayer servers. It adds a secure virtual banking system where every player has their own persistent balance, missions, shop access, transaction history, statistics, and money transfer features.

Complete Bank System

- Persistent virtual currency: Dollars ($)

- Separate bank account for every player

- Balance survives death, logout, world reload, and server restart

- Secure server-side transaction validation

- Recent transaction history

- Player statistics and richest-player leaderboard

Bank Card

The Bank Card is the main controller of the addon. Use it to open the complete banking interface.

- Cannot be dropped

- Cannot be moved into chests or other containers

- Kept safely after death

- Automatically restored if missing after joining or respawning

- Your money is stored in your account, not inside the card

Mission System

Earn Dollars by completing different mission categories:

- Daily Missions smaller objectives with regular rewards

- Mega Missions difficult long-term challenges

- Monthly Missions major monthly objectives

- Yearly Missions extremely difficult high-reward challenges

Missions can track activities such as mining, harvesting crops, killing mobs, defeating bosses, travelling, dealing damage, placing blocks, collecting resources, playtime, and more.

Mission rewards include claim protection to prevent duplicate rewards and common farming exploits.

Buy Shop

Spend your Dollars directly through the built-in shop.

- Blocks

- Tools

- Weapons

- Armor

- Food

- Farming items

- Resources

- Mob drops

- Special items

Choose different quantities and purchase items securely using your bank balance.

Sell System

Sell supported Vanilla items directly from your inventory and receive Dollars.

- Inventory is checked before every sale

- Items are removed before payment is finalized

- Renamed, enchanted, or damaged items are protected from accidental selling

- Sell 1, 16, 32, 64, or Sell All when available

- Sell prices are normally lower than buy prices to prevent infinite money loops

Player Money Transfers

Send Dollars securely to other online players.

- Select an online player

- Enter a transfer amount

- Confirm before sending

- Sender and receiver both receive transaction records

- Self-transfer, negative values, duplicate confirmations, invalid amounts, and insufficient balance are protected

Security & Anti-Exploit Protection

The addon includes protection against common multiplayer economy exploits:

- Duplicate mission rewards

- Repeated transfer confirmation

- UI button spam

- Negative or invalid money values

- Integer overflow

- Buying without enough balance

- Selling items that are not owned

- Placed-block mining mission farming

- Several generated-block farming methods

- Duplicate Bank Cards

- World reload abuse

Multiplayer Ready

Every player's balance, missions, statistics, claims, and transaction history are stored separately. The system is designed for Survival multiplayer worlds and SMP gameplay.
