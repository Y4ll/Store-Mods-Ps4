Welcome to v1.5.1 release of Superhero Plus: Bedrock Edition, an official port of the original Java mod made by Kostina, currently the addon is still in development, but there are currently a decent amount of superpowers, which can be obtained in unique and fun ways

Powers:-

Electrokinesis:-

Can be obtained by getting struck by lightning while holding an experimental vaccine,

It gives you an item that allows you to summon lightning in the direction you are facing and a secondary ability that summons lightning around you

When you use the item, you have a 15 chance to get an electroball, when thrown, explodes violently.

Energy Constructs:-

Can be obtained by punching a training dummy with an electroball, it gives you the energy constructs item, when used has a chance to give the player, an Energy saber, Energy blocks and Energy nets.

-

- Energy Saber:- Is a powerful weapon that deals 11 damage, sets an entity on fire, but has 4 durability, its a strong but fragile weapon.

- Energy Blocks:- This block can only be broken by players with the energy construcs ability, it dissapears after some time.

- Energy Nets:- This item can be thrown, it electrocutes and damages the target.

Super Leap:-

Can be obtained by drinking a super leaping serum.

it gives you an item that when clicked dashes into the air, you have to be on ground for this to work.

Healing Factor

Can be obtained by drinking a Mirakuru serum, this ability grants you regeneration 3 every 10 secs

Super Strength

Can be obtained by punching a training dummy with a strength artifact scroll in your offhand while under the strength effect,

It gives you permanent strength 3

Super Speed & Dimension Travel

Edit :- Major super speed update

Can be obtained by injecting yourself with velocityX, and gettng struck by lightning within its time limit

Super speed works a lot differently, your speed isnt active by default, it can be triggered by running, which will put you at base speed,

Super speed works based on progressive speed, the longer you run, the faster you become

When you run for long enough, you hit sonic boom and break the sound barrier,

If you run for long enough after hitting the barrier, you will become fast enough to dimension travel, allowing you to travel between dimensions

After hitting the sound barrier, you will have infinite mass punch and be able to one shot anything, but doing so will reset your speed

**Water running** Water running can only be achieved by reaching a certain level of speed, with super speed active (about the time when the lightning trail comes out)

Flight

Flight can be obtained by drinking a flight serum
Edit :- Major flight update

--To levitate--, you need to put the angel wing in your offhand, hold the jump button while looking up to levitate, the higher you look, the higher you levitate

--To fly -- hold shift and jump with the angel wing in your offhand

Webswing

Webswing can be obtained by getting bit by a radioactive spider

On clicking the item while facing a block, you get pulled to the block, the farther you are to the block, the harder you get pulled to it

Pyrokinesis

Pyrokinesis can be obtained by using a binding agent while submerged in lava

It comes with two items, Fireball and Lava stream

**Fireball** Click to launch a fireball in your view direction.

**Lava Stream** Click to fire a jet stream of lava your view direction, setting anything in its way ablaze

Cryokinesis

Cryokinesis is obtained by drinking a serum base while submerged in powdered snow for 2 minutes

It comes with two items, Ice shard and Cryo freeze

Ice shard Shoot a singular ice shard that does heavy damage
Cryo Freeze AOE attack, its better explained by viewing it ingame
