Crafting & Use

Players start by mining raw ores, then smelt them in a furnace to create ingots, and finally use the ingots to craft powerful armor sets.

- Damonite Armor (Level 1) Starter OP armor with strong protection

- Bloodron Armor (Level 2) Advanced armor with higher durability and power

- Soulron Armor (Level 3) Ultimate OP armor with maximum strength
