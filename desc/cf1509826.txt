AFK Cinematic Camera

Let the world keep looking beautiful, even when you stop moving.

AFK Cinematic Camera is a Minecraft Bedrock add-on that automatically activates a stylish cinematic camera when your player stays inactive for a short time. Instead of standing still with a normal view, your character becomes the center of a smooth, dramatic orbiting showcase shot.

Features

Automatic AFK Detection
The cinematic camera activates after a period of inactivity.Smooth Orbiting Camera
The camera moves around the player with a polished, cinematic feel instead of staying static.

Dynamic Motion
Uses flowing movement, changing distance, and subtle height variation to create a more alive and professional presentation.

Automatic Exit
As soon as the player moves, looks around, or gives input again, the camera returns to normal gameplay view.

Built for Bedrock Atmosphere
Perfect for making your character, base, armor, biome, or world feel more dramatic while idle.
