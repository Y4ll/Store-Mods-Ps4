Buzzy Bees Panorama with Shaders (Bedrock)

This is a really simple Resource Pack that adds shaders to the original panorama.

The seed was discovered by the Minecraft@Home team.

- Seed: -4404205509303106230

- Coordinates: X=339.691 Y=65.546 Z=-1072.041

- Version: Minecraft Java Snapshot 19w40a
