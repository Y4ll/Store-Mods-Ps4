Dino Legends Addon

This addon brings a variety of dinosaurs to Minecraft, with custom structures, new items, and other stuff.

This Addon adds:

- 9 Different Dinosaurs

- 22 Items (Which includes armor and weapons)

- 2 proper structures

Mobs:

- Alvarezsaurus

A small mob that spawns in groups

- Ankylosaurus

A passive mob, and it spawns in 2-3 groups

- Spinosaurus

Spawns in beaches and in the ocean

- Tyrannosaurus Rex/Charged

Spawn everywhere and is very hostile towards players

- Tyrannosaurus Zilla

A boss version of T-rex, It spawns by activating the Pillar found in a structure

- Distortus Rex

A strong and giant mob that can be summoned by injecting a T-rex using a special injection

- Brachiosaurus

A passive mob, only attacking if provoked. this mob spawns in the world in groups of 3-5

- Ultimate Brachiosaurus

\

An ultimate form of brachiosaurus, the brachiosaurus can transform into this form if you inject it with a special injection. It has an ability to a cause a tremor, damaging any mobs in 30 radius

Items:

- Brachiosaurus DNA

can be obtained by killing Brachiosurus

- Injection (Brachiosaurus)

can be used for transforming a brachiosarus into an ultimate form

- Cooked/Dino Meat

Can be obtained by killing a dinosaur

- Blue Ember

- God Essence

Can be obtained by killing a Charged T-rex, Though it has low chance of dropping.

- Injection/Filled

- Mutated/Tyrannosaurus Rex DNA

The normal DNA can be obtained by killing T-rex And the Mutated DNA can be obtained by killing T-rex Zilla

- Tooth Knife

- Armor

- Tyrannosaurus Sword

- Tyrannosaurus Zilla's Presence

Can be used to summon Tyrannosaurus Zilla, Interact it with the pillar on the new structure.
