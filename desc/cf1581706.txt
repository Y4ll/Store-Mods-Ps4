Colorful Visuals is Minecraft cranked all the way up. colorful greens, deep purple skies, and saturation on everything with no exceptions. This one changes the textures too, not just the lighting.

What this changes

Textures

This is the one thing that sets this pack apart from everything else I make. My other packs leave vanilla textures alone and only touch the lighting. This one recolors the blocks themselves, so the color is in the world and not just in the light falling on it.

Saturation

Every biome is pushed to high saturation, no exceptions. There is no quiet corner of the map where the pack lets go.

Grass and leaves

Saturated green, well past anything natural. Plains and forests hit you from a distance.

Sky

Deep purple and blue gradients instead of a flat single tone. The sky is doing real work in this pack, especially at dawn and dusk.

Clouds

Kept crisp and white so they cut against the purple behind them rather than getting swallowed by it.

Contrast

Trees, grass and ground are separated harder than vanilla, so nothing melts into anything else. Without this the neon would turn the whole screen into one green smear.

Forests

Canopies read thick and full instead of flat, which keeps depth in the world even with the colors this high.

Requirements

THIS PACK IS FOR VIBRANT VISUALS ONLY
IT DOES NOT WORK WITH MB LOADER

Vibrant Visuals must be ON. This pack is built for Bedrock's Vibrant Visuals renderer. The pack will not do anything if it is off.

Where to turn it on: Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals

If that toggle is not there, your device or version does not support Vibrant Visuals yet.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Colorful Visuals

- Turn on Settings > Video > Vibrant Visuals

- If the lighting still looks the same, leave the world and load it again once

FAQ

The pack is not working. Nothing changed after I installed it.
I get this comment a lot, and almost every single time the reason is the same. Vibrant Visuals is turned off.

This pack only works when Vibrant Visuals is ON. That is written clearly at the top of this page. With it off, the pack loads fine but nothing on your screen changes, because there is no renderer for it to work with. The pack is not broken.

Go to Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals and switch it on. If you cannot find that option at all, your device or Minecraft version does not support it yet.
