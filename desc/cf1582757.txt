Infinite Health BP [Achievements Friendly]

Description

Stay alive no matter what with Infinite Health BP [Achievements Friendly]!

This lightweight Minecraft Bedrock add-on continuously grants powerful Regeneration and Resistance effects, allowing players to survive almost any situation while keeping gameplay simple and enjoyable.

Designed to work seamlessly in survival worlds, this add-on provides near-infinite survivability without requiring complex setups. Perfect for exploration, building projects, testing worlds, and casual gameplay.

Features

- Continuous high-level Regeneration effect

- Continuous high-level Resistance effect

- Simple and lightweight behavior pack

- Automatic operation using tick functions

- Great for survival, creative-style survival, and testing worlds

- Achievements Friendly version

Note

- The game may not accurately display the total amount of health available due to the way Minecraft handles health-related effects.

- While extremely difficult to defeat, certain situations or game mechanics may still affect players differently.
