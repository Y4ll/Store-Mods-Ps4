Smooth, Lag-Free & Stable!

Instead of rapidly flickering every single millisecond (which is super distracting and can actually hurt your game's performance), the numbers update smoothly once every second. This gives you a highly stable and readable frame rate count while being extremely lightweight for any device, from budget phones to high-end PCs!

Fully Compatible with Servers & Realms!

Since this is purely a resource pack (no behavior packs required), it is 100% server and realmsafe! You can join any of your favorite multiplayer servers or realms, and it will work perfectly. It is also completely compatible to run alongside any of your other favorite resource packs!
