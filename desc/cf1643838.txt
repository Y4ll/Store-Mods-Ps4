Swagcasters all new furniture pack brings all sorts of furniture to minecraft from vintage and antique furniture to retro appliances and street signs this addon brings what you may be missing in your minecraft world

So far this addon includes

A cast iron sink typical of a 1920s to 1950s bathroom/washroom

A late 1940s procelein enamel oven with a stove top available in White, Blue, Pink, Green and a Mystery finish

A 1940s Refrigerator with a bottom freezer drawer available in White, Blue, Pink and Green

The iconic big blue mailbox, a staple of American city streets since the 1970s

A Utilitub laundry sink typical of working basements

A wide cast iron kitchen sink with legs found in many homes from 1920 to 1940

All new street signage like a historically accurate yellow stopsign used from 1920 to the mid 1950s and of course a common red stop sign both built to be installed ontop of iron bar posts
