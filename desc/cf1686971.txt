Ever wondered what cats would do if they stopped being so peaceful?

Cat Fighting - Bedrock Edition adds hilarious cat quarrels and fights to Minecraft Bedrock. Cats can argue with each other, hiss, swipe, and occasionally get into full-blown feline brawls. It doesn't change their core behavior too much. It simply makes cats feel more alive, chaotic, and entertaining to watch.

CATS DO NOT TAKE DAMAGE WHILE FIGHTING! ITS JUST A VISUAL EFFECT!

Features

- Cats can argue with one another. (except for Cats that are sitting down)

- Hissing, growling, and aggressive animations.

- Cat fights that happen naturally.

- Works in survival worlds with naturally spawned cats.

- Adds funny and chaotic moments to your gameplay.

- Flatten cats with shovel or running them over with minecart.

Whether you're a cat lover or just enjoy seeing Minecraft mobs act a little more realistically (or dramatically), Cat Fighting brings extra personality to every village and home filled with cats.
