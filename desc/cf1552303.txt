Bored of the same old red health bar? Better Hearts: Variety Colors UI gives you complete control over your survival HUD! This aesthetic and utility resource pack allows you to swap the classic vanilla red hearts for 15 beautiful custom color variants.

Whether you are looking for a clean Black & White setup, a vibrant Cyan or Pink aesthetic, or a sleek Dark/PvP look, this pack has the perfect shade for you. It is fully compatible with any world and designed for casual survival players, builders, and PvP competitors who want a fresh, personalized screen view.

Available Colors (Subpacks Slider):

You can easily switch between 15 custom styles directly from the pack settings menu:

- White | Light Gray | Gray | Black

- Brown | Orange | Yellow | Lime

- Green | Cyan | Light Blue | Blue

- Purple | Magenta | Pink

Key Features:

- 15 Custom Variants: Choose your favorite color using the in-game subpacks slider.

- Clean & Readable: High-contrast textures that look perfect on any screen size or resolution.

- 100% Optimized: Vanilla-friendly textures with absolute zero FPS impact. Works smoothly on all Bedrock devices (Mobile, Console, and PC).
