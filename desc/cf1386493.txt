About This Pack

Hysteria Vibrate Shader is a dark, atmospheric, horror-themed shader created for Vibrant Visual. This pack transforms your Minecraft world into a tense and cinematic environment filled with deep shadows, eerie tones, and a heavy horror ambience. Its goal is not just to enhance visuals, but to completely shift the mood of your gameplayturning familiar landscapes into unsettling, unpredictable scenes.

Images

The shader is still in development, but the current version already delivers strong horror vibes with dramatic lighting, stylized darkness, and a unique visual style. Volumetric Clouds and several visual upgrades are planned for future updates to enhance the experience even further.

If you enjoy darker worlds, suspenseful ambience, and an intense, immersive atmosphere, this shader is made for

you.
