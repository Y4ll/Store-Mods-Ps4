Ever mined right past a diamond because it blended into the wall? Glowing Ores+ fixes that every ore now glows with a bright, distinct color so you can spot valuable blocks from across the cave without breaking Minecraft's classic look.

Features

Full ore coverage Coal, Copper, Iron, Gold, Redstone, Lapis, Diamond, Emerald, Quartz, Nether Gold, and Ancient Debris

Deepslate variants included every deepslate ore gets the same glow treatment as its stone counterpart

Clean, vanilla-friendly glow enhanced visibility without clashing with default textures or shaders

Works great with or without Vibrant Visuals looks fantastic on vanilla lighting, and glows even brighter with shaders on

High-contrast colors each ore type is easy to tell apart at a glance, even in low light

Lightweight pure resource pack, no behavior changes, runs smoothly on any device

Compatibility

Works on Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.80+, and plays nicely alongside other resource and shader packs.
