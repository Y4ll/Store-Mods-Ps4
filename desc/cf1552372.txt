How to Use?

- It's that easy! Just make sure you're inside a world. After that, press the inventory button and your Utility Inventory will be activated.

Features:

- Inventory Move, ability to move your player while you are using inventory.

- Armor Durability Display, this shows your armor durability to HUD.

- Offhand Slot, this shows item from your offhand to HUD.

- Offhand Swap, ability to move item from offhand slot to your inventory by double-pressing Offhand Slot.

- Slot Cycler, ability to move items from your hotbar to your inventory by simply double-pressing your hotbar.

- Tools Cycler, Adds an extra hotbar for tool items from your inventory to HUD.

- Fast Totem Button, Added new button to HUD to quickly move totems from your inventory to your offhand.

- Elytra/Chestplate Swapper Button. Added new button to HUD to quickly swap chestplate armor from the chestplate slot to elytra and vice versa.

- Extra Slot (Hack Feature), Adds 4 extra slots to inventory panel.

- Arrow Dupper (exploit a bug).

Subpacks:

- Inventory Overlay Mode (Only Support Touch), default subpack.

- Default Inventory Mode, this subpack makes Utility Inventory not use inventory overlay, this makes it compatible with mouse & keyboard users but only has Fast Totem button, Elytra/Chestplate swapper button and Armor Durability Display features in inventory screen.

Questions:

- How does the Extra Slots feature work? They're essentially crafting input slots. You can store items in crafting input slots, but if you close your inventory (which means turning off Utility Inventory), the items from the Extra Slots (crafting input slots) will go into your player inventory, or be dropped if your player inventory is full.

- How do you even duplicate arrows using just a texture pack? Well, it uses a bug I discovered while testing this pack. Here's how: make sure Utility Inventory is enabled and you have a bow and arrows (you can have as many arrows as you want, as long as they only fill 1 inventory slot), then open the inventory panel, charge your bow, while your bow is charging, move your arrows to Extra Slot, then unmount your bow and you can take arrows from your bow without using arrows from Extra Slot.

- Why can't I open the chest screen, villager trade screen, and so on when this pack is activated? I don't know, it's a bug from Minecraft that I can't fix (it's impossible to fix). You can disable this pack if you want to open those screens.
