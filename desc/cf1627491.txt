Dynamic Garage Doors Add-On for Minecraft Bedrock

Enhance your builds, modern houses, fire stations, and car repair shops with fully functional, animated garage doors. Unlike static block designs, this add-on introduces dynamic block entities with realistic sliding mechanics, custom sound effects, and customizable collision hitboxes.

Key Features

- Realistic Animations & Sounds: Smooth opening and closing transitions accompanied by custom mechanical audio.

- Dynamic Collision System: When open, the doors collision box is completely disabled, allowing players and large vehicles to pass through effortlessly. When closed, it functions as a solid protective wall.

- The Wrench Tool: Your main instrument for adjusting and dismantling.

-

- Dismantle: Simply click the garage door with the wrench to safely break and collect it.

- Configuration Menu: Hit the door block with the wrench to open an advanced settings UI.

In-Game Configuration UI

Using the Wrench, you can access a dedicated settings menu for each door to adjust the following:

- Slide Direction: Choose whether the door slides to the left or right upon opening.

- Grid Centering: Toggle a 0.5-block offset to make the door align perfectly with adjacent wall blocks.

- Rotation Menu: Rotate the door hitbox and model to 0, 90, 180, or 270 degrees to fit any architectural layout.

How to Get Started

- Obtain the Garage Door Item from the Creative inventory.

- Place the door on the ground. Its initial direction will align with your player's orientation.

- Click the door to open/close it.

- Use the Wrench to configure slide direction, centering, or rotation.
