Take a Seat Addon lets players sit naturally on stairs and slabs in Minecraft, perfect for immersive roleplay, creative builds, or simply relaxing in your world. It works with any addon.

This addon allows you to sit on slabs, stairs and logs, simply by right clicking them. It works using stable script apis, so no experimental features are required.

What interactions will let me sit on a block? Simple, just avoid a vanilla block interaction if you intend to sit down on a block, such as an axe on copper or wood, or placing down a block on another block.
