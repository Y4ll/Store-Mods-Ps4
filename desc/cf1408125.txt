Simply RPG Statistics (Update 1.1.0)

Simply RPG Statistics turns your Minecraft Bedrock world into a classic RPG adventure. Now featuring the new Specialist Mode (Adaptive Stats), Mana, and Sword Skills!

Start your adventure, kill mobs to earn XP, level up, and build your character. Will you manually assign your points, or let your actions define your class?

New Key Features

- Specialist Mode (NEW): An optional progression where your stats increase automatically based on what you do (Fighting, Running, or Tanking). [ V1.1.0 ]

- Automatic Leveling: Kill mobs to gain XP! The stronger the mob, the more XP you get.

- Addon Compatibility: This system works with mobs from other Addons too! XP is calculated based on the entity's Max Health. [ V1.1.0 ]

- Mana System & Skills: Unlock powerful Sword Skills that consume Mana.

- Immersive HUD: A custom Actionbar UI displays your Level, XP, Health, and Mana.

- Interactive Menu: Use the Menu Item (Right-click) to manage stats (Classic Mode) and configure skills.

Specialist Mode (Adaptive Stats) [ V1.1.0 ]

Want a realistic challenge? Use the command /scriptevent rpg:mode to toggle this mode. Manual points are disabled, and your stats evolve organically:

- Offensive Focus: Attacking enemies raises STR but slowly lowers Defense/Vitality. (Glass Cannon playstyle).

- Defensive Focus: Taking damage or blocking raises RES/VIT but lowers Agility/Strength. (Tank playstyle).

- Mobility Focus: Sprinting raises AGI but lowers combat stats. (Speedster playstyle).

The 6 Statistics

- Resistance (RES): Increases your Health Regeneration speed. [ V1.1.0 ]

- Strength (STR): Increases melee damage. Required to unlock Sword Skills.

- Vitality (VIT): Increases your Max Health (Hearts). [ V1.1.0 ]

- Intelligence (INT): Increases your Max Mana and Mana Regeneration.

- Agility (AGI): Boosts movement speed.

- Luck (LUCK): Increases Critical Hit chance and dodge probability.

Sword Skills

- Thrust: A powerful dash attack.

- Activation: Sprint + Hit an enemy.

- Requirement: 15 Strength.

- Cost: 15 Mana.

Commands

- /scriptevent rpg:menu - Opens the main menu.

- /scriptevent rpg:mode - (New) Toggles between Classic Mode and Specialist Mode. [ V1.1.0 ]

- /scriptevent rpg:reset - Resets your character to Level 1.

- /scriptevent rpg:levelup - (Admin) Forces a level up.

- /scriptevent rpg:points <amount> - (Admin) Gives free stat points.
