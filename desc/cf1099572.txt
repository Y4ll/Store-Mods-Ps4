Requirements:

- Beta APIs experimental toggle

- "Star's Centralized Menu" addon V-2.0

Setup:

- Download the StarBot V6 addon.

- Download the "Star's Centralized Menu" V-2.0 addon: MCPEDL Link, CurseForge Link

- Import both addons into Minecraft.

- Apply both addons to your world.

- Perform the wave emote to open the menu and perform the actions.

- Enjoy!

Notes:

- A player can only control those bots which they have summoned themselves and not the bots summoned by any other player, unless, the player has "Star:admin" tag which allows them to control all bots inside the world.

- A player with the "blocked" tag is blocked from using starbot.

- Experiments cannot be turned off once they are activated.

- If you close the world without removing the Bot first then it will be automatically removed without being killed and any items in its inventory will be lost.
