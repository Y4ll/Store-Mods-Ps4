Project Description:

Why play a Pokemon game without the tools to look like a real Pokemon Trainer? The Pokemon Backpack add-on will make you look the part whilst running around capturing powerful creatures and exploring untamed biomes.

Important Information:

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Actual pokemon style backpack.

- Inventory storage and on back features.

- Behaviour & Resource packs are both required for the project to work.

- Have something to say? Join the Discord.

Sneak and interact to place the Pokemon Backpack on your back then Sneak and Jump to get a prompt to place it back down.
