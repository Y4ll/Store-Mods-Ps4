Features:

- Creaking Heart: Needs to be placed between two aligned pale oak logs and/or its variants to be activated. Creaking Hearts have three states: disabled, dormant, and active. Removing one of these blocks keeps the heart in its dormant state if it hasn't already spawned a creaking. Unlike the block in the preview/snapshots, creaking hearts can be moved by pistons but are destroyed immediately after moved. Creaking hearts are dropped only when mined by a tool enchanted with Silk Touch.

- Creaking: The creaking is naturally spawned by an active creaking heart when at nighttime. A creaking spawns within 16 blocks horizontally and 8 blocks vertically from the creaking heart, and it is linked to the heart. You can easily find the creaking heart a creaking is linked to by hitting the mob, because they emit particles directed to their linked creaking heart when dealt damage! If a creaking moves more than 32 blocks from its heart, it despawns and another creaking will be spawned linked to that creaking heart. Creakings always despawn during daytime. It can also be spawned with a spawn egg or command, not despawning during day and being vulnerable to all types of damage. When a creaking within approximately 12 blocks enters a player's field of vision, it becomes alerted. Alerted creakings require no player to be looking at them to move, and to be within 24 blocks of them to become unalerted.

- Pale Hanging Moss: Emit erie noises randomly while hanging from a pale oak log or pale oak leaves. It drops itself only when mined with shears or a tool enchanted with Silk Touch, otherwise it drops nothing.
