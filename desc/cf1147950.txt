As assumed by the title this is a texture pack that changes Minecraft Items and block to look like things from both Neon Genesis Evangelion and The Rebuild of Evangelion Movies.

DLC/Addon Textures:

These are option addons to the texture pack that can be used with or stand alone:

The Third Impact Addon:

Changes the overall general overworld look to a Post Near-Third Impact style changing things from grass to leaves to even the water. (More and better images will come out with Minecraft Evangelion: The Rebuild Edition Version 4)

Blocks:

TNT:

TNT has been replaced with a N2 Mine Texture:

Armor:

Diamond and Netherite armors have been replaced by the Evangelion Units each subpack representing a different Unit out of the three main units: Unit 00, Unit 01, and Unit 02.

Gold has been replaced JSSDF Special Forces.

Iron with the Eva Unti Plug Suit.

Leather with the NERV Operator Outfit.
