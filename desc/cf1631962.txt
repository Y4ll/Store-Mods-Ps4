Bob will guide you through a sequence of building lessons, resource-gathering objectives and increasingly dangerous visitations. Follow his instructions, prepare your shelter and find The Visitor before it finds you.

The Story

When a new world begins, Bob The Toolbox appears near the players. At first, he may seem helpful: he gives instructions, provides materials and teaches you how to build.

Complete his lessons by placing the requested blocks and gathering the required resources. As the story progresses, Bob begins warning you about something called The Visitor.

When visitation begins, The Visitor will hide somewhere nearby. You have only ten seconds to find it. If you succeed, it will flee. If you fail, the visitation becomes a deadly chase.

Build carefully, listen to Bob and prepare before night arrives.

Some later events and appearances are intentionally left unexplained.

Features

- A complete story campaign featuring Bob The Toolbox.

- Original voice lines and sound effects.

- Normal, talking and crushed appearances for Bob.

- Bob watches nearby players and reacts when approached.

- Pick up Bob, carry him in your inventory and place him back into the world.

- Building objectives.

- Multiple construction lessons using different wood and building materials.

- Resource-gathering objectives.

- Crawlspace and basement construction challenges.

- Timed visitation events.

- The Visitor can hide in randomly selected locations.

- The Visitor can pursue players, climb and break through certain obstructions.

- Multiple story stages with different rewards and outcomes.

- commands for starting, testing and controlling campaign events.

- Multiplayer support.
