Immersive Combat is part of the TerraDraconis Addon Series a collection of addons that come together to create a vast, living world of dragons. Inspired by the monster-hunting experience, this series lets you hunt or tame dragons, trade and interact with the ecosystem, and even build your own settlement of hunters to defend against wild dragons that dare to attack your home.

This addon transforms the way you fight. With a toggleable Combat Mode, you gain access to:

Attack Combos for dynamic weapon play

Parries to deflect incoming strikes

Dodges to evade devastating blows

Consumables & Magic to give you an edge in battle

Cinematic Camera Shots for a more immersive and dramatic combat experience

Voice Line System with customizable settings, making battles feel more personal and realistic

One of the standout features is that the entire combat and animation system is fully script-based meaning no player.json is used. This makes it highly compatible with other animation packs or addons, such as Action and Stuffs, or any custom weapons and armor packs you want to bring into the fight.

Take on dragons with a fresh combat experience, seamlessly blending into the TerraDraconis world or enhancing your own custom setup.

Important Notes

This addon is not a weapon+ or item+ addon that adds new swords or weapons.
Instead, this is a core system addon a base framework that adds the mechanics and combat systems that other addons can build upon.

Think of it like a foundation or engine for combat not the content itself.
Its designed to work together with other addons, much like how addonpacks or modpacks work.

In fact, this addon serves as a core combat module for the TerraDraconis Series, including the main TerraDraconis Addon.
