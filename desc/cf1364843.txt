Discover with this Add-On a lot of construction blocks originally used in medieval France, that left their trace until this day!

Blocks will be added progressively through my own experiences and your suggestions.

To this day, this Add-On contains:

- Tiles roofs

Tiles roof are traditionally used in France because they resist well to heat, allow water flow. Plus, their rounded shape keeps air flowing in the roof, helping avoid overheating. In this Add-On, tiles can be crafted by placing a brick in a stonecutter. With this brick, you can craft 3 kinds of roofings: roofs, corners and roof tops, which can respectively be crafted this way:

-Cobblestone variants

You can fill traditional tile roofs with cobblestone to blend them better into your builds. To do so, simply place the roofing block together with a cobblestone block in the crafting table.
