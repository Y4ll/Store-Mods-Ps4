Someone's Dynamic Lighting

The ultimate, lightweight Dynamic Lighting add-on for Minecraft Bedrock Edition!

Features

This pack brings smooth and realistic Dynamic Lighting to your Minecraft world without the need for complex resource packs, experimental toggles, or heavy rendering.

- True Dynamic Light: Hold any light source (Torches, Lanterns, Glowstone, Campfires, etc.) and it will instantly illuminate the area around you!

- Native Inventory Offhand Support: You no longer need to use weird workarounds or right-click to hold a torch in your offhand. You can open your inventory and drag Torches and Lanterns directly into your offhand slot, just like a shield! (They can also still be placed normally).

- Smart Block Logic: Intelligently places built-in light blocks without breaking or replacing existing blocks like water, tall grass, doors, or custom blocks.

- Multiplayer Friendly: Fully compatible with multiplayer realms and servers. You and your friends can all explore caves together, each with your own dynamic light!

- Extremely Lightweight: Uses highly optimized, non-obfuscated Bedrock @minecraft/server scripting to keep performance high and lag practically nonexistent. No heavy resource pack neededjust load the behavior pack and play!

Supported Items

- Torch (Level 14) [Offhand Dragging Supported]

- Lantern (Level 15) [Offhand Dragging Supported]

- Campfire (Level 15)

- Glowstone (Level 15)

- Sea Lantern (Level 15)

- Shroomlight (Level 15)

- Beacon (Level 15)

- End Rod (Level 14)

- Glow Berries (Level 14)

- Soul Torch (Level 10)

- Soul Lantern (Level 10)

- Soul Campfire (Level 10)

- Redstone Torch (Level 7)

- Magma Block (Level 3)

- Amethyst Cluster (Level 5)

(To hold the other items in your offhand, simply right-click with them in the air, and they will automatically swap to your offhand slot!)

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file.

- Double-click it to install it directly into Minecraft.

- Apply the Behavior Pack to your world.
