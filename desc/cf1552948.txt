PvP Practice Elite Edition v2.6.10 is a ready-to-play Minecraft Bedrock PvP practice world/add-on setup made for duels, queues, and kit-based training.

The world already includes 2 normal PvP arenas for duels and queue matches, plus separate training arenas for each kit. Players can start practicing right away without needing to build or configure arenas themselves.

It is designed for players who want to improve in different PvP styles, including Sword, Axe, SMP, UHC, Crystal, and Mace PvP. The setup includes duels, queues, training arenas, custom kits, stats, leaderboards, parties, safe arena boundaries, and a clean custom UI.

Player Commands

!menu

Opens the main menu.

!queue

Opens the queue menu for normal PvP matches.

!duel

Opens the duel menu.

!training

Opens the training menu.

!kits

Shows available kits.

!kit

Opens kit options.

!kit save <name>

Saves your current inventory as a custom kit.

!kit load <name>

Loads a saved custom kit.

!kit delete <name>

Deletes a saved custom kit.

!kit list

Lists your saved kits.

!stats

Shows your personal stats.

!leaderboard

Opens the leaderboard.

!party

Opens party options.

!settings

Opens player settings.

!spawn

Teleports you back to the lobby/spawn.

Admin Commands

Most players do not need these commands because the world already comes with arenas set up. These are mainly for world owners who want to edit or add arenas.

!admin

Opens the admin menu.

!setspawn

Sets the lobby spawn.

!spawnradius <1-1000>

Sets the safe spawn radius.

!arena list

Lists normal duel/queue arenas.

!arena create <name>

Creates a new normal arena.

!arena spawn1 <id>

Sets player spawn 1 for an arena.

!arena spawn2 <id>

Sets player spawn 2 for an arena.

!arena radius <id> <blocks>

Sets the arena boundary radius.

!arena kit <id> <kit>

Sets the kit for an arena.

!arena ranked <id> <on/off>

Enables or disables ranked matches for an arena.

!arena enable <id>

Enables an arena.

!arena disable <id>

Disables an arena.

!arena delete <id>

Deletes an arena.

!trainarena list

Lists training arenas.

!trainarena create <name>

Creates a new training arena.

!trainarena spawn1 <id>

Sets training arena spawn 1.

!trainarena spawn2 <id>

Sets training arena spawn 2.

!trainarena radius <id> <blocks>

Sets the training arena boundary radius.

!trainarena kit <id> <kit>

Sets the kit for a training arena.

!maintenance

Toggles maintenance mode.

!resetplayer <player>

Resets a player back to spawn.

!forceend

Force ends an active match.

How To Play

Join the world and use:

!menu

From there, players can open the duel menu, queue menu, training menu, kits, stats, leaderboards, and party options.

Installation

- Download and import the world/add-on files.

- Open Minecraft Bedrock.

- Import the world.

- Add the BP and RP to the world (if not included)

- Enable cheats and required experimental/script options if needed.

- Join the world and use !menu to start.
