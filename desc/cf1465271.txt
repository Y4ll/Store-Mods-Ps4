Armors & Tools Expansion adds a wide variety of new armor sets and tools to Minecraft, ranging from basic materials like Dirt and Wood to powerful Netherite-tier sets with unique abilities. Each set has its own characteristics, enchantability, durability, and special effects that enhance gameplay.

Whether you're looking for early-game survival gear, mid-game utility sets, or end-game powerhouses, this addon has something for every playstyle. Many sets offer unique mechanics such as item attraction, lifesteal, teleportation, fire immunity, area damage, and more. The addon is designed to be balanced while still providing exciting new options for players.

All sets are fully compatible with Minecraft's vanilla enchanting system, and each tool type (Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel, Hoe, and Sword) is available for every material. Some sets also grant fire immunity or other status effects, making them particularly useful in specific dimensions like the Nether.
