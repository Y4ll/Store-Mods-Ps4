SCP: CLASSIFIED is a high quality vanilla styled add-on for the bedrock edition of Minecraft that aims to seamlessly implement content from the ever expanding SCP universe that contains the creative works of thousands of writers and artists.

SCP: CLASSIFIED in its current version (1.2.4) adds over 60+ anomalies with unique behaviors and designs that make them fit right in the vanilla game. CLASSIFIED also adds a mountains worth of blocks for the people who want to build large expansive facilities to contain the many SCPs we have added to the add-on. However do not fret, 1.3.0 is currently in development which will be our biggest update yet. Adding tons of new content but also reworking and polishing the older stuff such as full SCP reworks.

As mentioned in the previous paragraph, every SCP is getting a rework. As 1.3.0 was being developed we also started developing 1.2.4 as a gift to our very patient community, so because of that we have added a few reworks into this update. The reworked SCPs will be bolded. But there is one new anomaly for this update, SCP-500.
