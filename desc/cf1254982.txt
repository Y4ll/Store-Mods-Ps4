After version 1.5.0, this addon now requires an API to work!

Dynamic Lighting API: MCPEDL | CurseForge Page

Simple Dynamic Lighting is an optimized addon that adds light sources when the player holds an item that normally emits light.

Items that don't break when underwater, will emit light underwater as well.

You can swap dynamic lighting items between your offhand/mainhand.

You do this by crouching twice within half of a second.

Enchanted items will give off light.

Entities that are on fire, will give off light.

Some entities will give off light by default.

Achievements do not get disabled with this addon enabled.

Have fun with your new dynamic lighting!
