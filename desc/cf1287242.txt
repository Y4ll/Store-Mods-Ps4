LazShaders is a vibrant visuals shader pack made for bedrock/pocket edition

This pack doesn't need patch and works for versions 1.21.90 and above

This pack offers a beautiful viewing experience while keeping the original texture and feel of Minecraft bedrock and gives it a Special touch

In order for this pack to work "vibrant visuals" must be turned on when creating a world and your device must have support for vibrant visuals.
