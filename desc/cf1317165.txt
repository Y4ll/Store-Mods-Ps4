Keg

The Keg is an essential workstation in this mod, responsible for producing most of the food and drinks. The Keg has four input slots, one container slot one liquid slot, and one output slot. The container slot is also used to transfer liquids into the bliquid slot. The Kegr requires the output slotr to be empty or contain the same item as the output product, and the liquid slot must be full to function. Each fermentation recipe has different temperature requirements and fermentation time. You can check the fermentation progress by aiming at the keg.
