About

Potato Shader reduces visual clutter in Minecraft Bedrock with calmer particles, fog, weather effects, and animated textures.

Features

- Cleaner atmosphere in the Overworld, Nether, and End

- Reduced smoke, flame, portal, and enchantment particles

- Softer rain and water splash effects

- Slower animations for water, lava, fire, kelp, portals, and other animated textures

- Balanced, Ultra Potato, and Vanilla Fog presets

How to use

- Import the pack into Minecraft Bedrock.

- Activate the Potato Shader Resource Pack in your world.

- Open the pack settings and choose a preset: Balanced for the default look, Ultra Potato for more aggressive fog changes, or Vanilla Fog to keep the usual fog while cleaning up particles.

Notes

Performance gains depend on your device, render distance, world complexity, and entity count. The pack changes visual effects and does not reduce world simulation work. Other packs that change the same particles, fog, or animations may override its effects.

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.50 or newer

- The Resource Pack must be active

- No Behavior Pack or external add-on required
