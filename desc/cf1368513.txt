How It Works:

Advanced Bag:

Advanced Bag. Is a high tier storage item which you can use to store your items and take it out when ever you want. You can hold this item in your chest slot to keep it on you without any consicuence. Having the item in your inventory will slow you down.

Mini Bag:

Having the item in your inventory will slow you down. You can hold this item in your legs slot to keep it on you without any consicuence. Its has 18 less slots then advanced bag.
