REMASTERED

This addon comes with 5 naturally occuring events that happen around your world!

This addon is also very configurable via the Guide Book for operator players.

In here, you can find this menu.

You can then find all the events, their names, and descriptors.

For anyone with operator, a settings button will appear.

This hosts different settings depending on the event, as well as duration.

I shouldn't need to list out all the event descriptions, cause they are in the addon. :3

But i will list out their names.

- Tornado

- Acid Rain

- Severe Thunderstorm

- Meteor Shower

- Blood Moon

All of this works on 1.21.100 AND above.

None of this requires experimental features.
