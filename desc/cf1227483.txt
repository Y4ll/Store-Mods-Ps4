Mythical Biomes is an add-on that introduces new biomes to your world, creating a fantasy-like environment. It includes new mobs, features, and blocks. Mythical Biomes currently replaces some vanilla biomes due to a Minecraft bug (MCPE-100700) that prevents custom biomes from generating alongside vanilla ones. As a workaround, some biomes had to be replaced to ensure the new biomes generate properly.
