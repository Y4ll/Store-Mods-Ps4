Tables Obtention & In-Game Wiki

The core evolution of your empire revolves around three major workstations: the Army Table, the Infirmary Table, and the Dashboard Table.

How to get them: Craft a CraftStation block using iron and logs, then link a citizen to it. Bring the required resources and currency to your Craftsman NPC, and he will build your specialized tables!

NEW: In-Game Wiki Book: Lost in the mechanics? We have added a dedicated Wiki Book item directly in-game to guide you through every feature on the fly!

Centralized Economy & Resource Crates

Managing your civilization requires strict logistics. Resources are automatically tracked and stored into four main strategic categories:

- Minerals: Used for industrial growth, heavy weapons, and military equipment.

- Organic: Essential for feeding your citizens, funding healthcare, and medical treatments.

- Construction: Required to build infrastructure, fortify walls, and expand towns.

- Technological: Needed for upgrading advanced tables and training elite Knight classes.

Resource Crates: Specialized storage blocks that link directly to your colony treasury. Each crate holds up to 1,000 points of its specific resource type.

Banking & Coin Exchanges: Your colony runs on actual currency (Copper, Iron, and Gold coins). Trade your emeralds with the Banker NPC at the Bank to fund your Treasury.

New Economic Features:

- Convert All Button: A brand new button allows you to instantly convert your massive pile of resources into treasury coins.

- Diamond Conversion: You can now exchange high-value Diamonds directly into colony coins through the Banker!

- Banker Range Fix: Increased the Banker's detection radius so the withdrawal buttons always display correctly for colony leaders.

Advanced Military Logic & Regiments

Defend your borders and showcase your supremacy with a heavily reworked tactical combat system!

Military Classes & Ranks:

- Soldier: Cheap, basic baseline unit. Weak without custom gear but perfect for early defense.

- Archer & Arbalestier: Ranged defenses. Handing a bow or crossbow to a standard soldier upgrades them for free. (Ranged AI has been buffed to target and shoot enemies from a much longer distance!)

- Assassin: Extremely fast and deals lethal damage, but highly vulnerable with only 15 HP. Use tactically!

- Knight: Well-balanced elite units that earn combat XP. Level them up by interacting with them using Paper to permanently boost their stats.

- Tank: Massive health pools, slow movement, built to hold choke points and absorb damage.

- Royal Guard: Elite multi-role protectors with incredibly high stats, trained via the Barracks Trainer NPC.

- General: The supreme commander. Only ONE General is allowed per colony. He provides passive Strength and Resistance buffs to all surrounding troops. Protect him at all costs!

New Army Mechanics & Balancing:

- Aggressive Combat AI: Soldiers and Knights are now highly proactive! They will take the immediate initiative to target and attack enemy mobs entering the territory without waiting around.
