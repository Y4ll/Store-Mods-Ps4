My Totem Doll

This resource pack transforms the regular totem into a 3D Totem Doll, where you can even apply your own custom skin!

This pack is inspired by the original Java Edition version made by LopyTwich. You can check it out here: My Totem Doll (Java)

How to add your own custom skin:

- Download and extract the pack.

- Go to the folder: NeBux/Totem/

- Inside, youll find a file named skin.png.

- Replace this file with your own Minecraft skin.

After that, the Totem Doll will appear with your custom skin in-game!
