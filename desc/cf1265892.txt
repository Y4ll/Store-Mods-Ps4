What is Anime Waifus?

Anime Waifus adds more than 57 collectible companions inspired by anime, video games, and many other popular franchises.

More than simple pets, each waifu is a fully interactive companion capable of following you, protecting you, equipping exclusive armor, picking up weapons, and adapting to different situations thanks to customizable behaviors.

Whether you're exploring caves, fighting bosses, or building your dream world, your favorite waifu will always be by your side.

This addon does NOT disable achievements.

All Available Waifus

How to Obtain a Waifu

Getting a new companion is now easier than ever.

First, craft a Waifu Core.

Then simply use it.

A menu will automatically open, allowing you to choose any available waifu.

Once you confirm your choice, the Waifu Core will be consumed and your new companion will appear immediately.

How to Tame a Waifu

All waifus can be tamed using only a Poppy.

Once tamed, they can:

Follow you.

Sit whenever you want.

Protect you during combat.

Waifu Wand

The Waifu Wand is the main tool used to manage and customize your companions.

When interacting with a waifu, a menu will open where you can view all of her information.

There you can see:

Her health.

The armor she currently has equipped.

General statistics.

From this same menu, you can also access the customization options.

There you can configure:

Animations

Six different sitting styles.

Three dance animations.

Movement

Follow Owner.

Random Walk.

Combat

Attack Monsters.

Defend Owner when attacked.

Once you've finished making your changes, simply press Submit to apply them.

Equipment

Waifus use exclusive armor sets specially designed for them.

Each armor set provides a different level of damage reduction, significantly increasing their survivability during combat.

Equipping Armor

To equip armor, simply interact with a waifu while holding an armor piece.

If you wish to remove it, use Shears.

Additionally, waifus can automatically pick up nearby weapons and tools, such as swords, allowing them to improve their equipment throughout your adventures.

Features

Passive regeneration.

Can be healed using different types of food.

Sleep in nearby beds like a player.

Have a chance to celebrate by dancing after defeating enemies
