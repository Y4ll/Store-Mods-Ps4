Ore Details:

Aluminum Ore:

- Can be found from y=64 to y=-64

- Diggable with Iron Pickaxe and above

- Normally in clumps of 1-6

- Clumps of 1-10 in these biomes:
jungle, savanna, lush caves, mangrove swamps, and mesa/badlands

Titanium Ore:

- Can be found below y=0 only

- Diggable with Diamond Pickaxe and above

- Normally in clumps of 1-3

- Clumps of 1-5 in these biomes:
extreme hills, desert, beach, and mesa/badlands
