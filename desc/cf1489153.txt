Haebi's Enhanced Arsenal

Machete (The Base Weapon)
Equipped with Java Edition's "Sweeping Edge" mechanic, this is the core weapon of Enhanced Arsenal.

Damage: +1 Attack Damage compared to Vanilla Swords.

Sweep Attack: Upon hitting a target, it deals splash damage to all enemies within a 3-block radius. (Cooldown: 0.6 Seconds)

Smart Safety: The sweeping edge effect will NOT damage tamed wolves, cats, or villagers. Swing your blade safely in your base!

2. Longbow

A true archer's weapon that completely upgrades your ranged combat.

Powerful Damage: Deals 1.3x amplified damage compared to the Vanilla Bow.

Attack Speed: Features the exact same draw speed (1.2s) as the Vanilla Bow, making it a pure, direct upgrade.

3. Shield Coating System (Custom Shields & Mini-Gacha)

Turn your boring vanilla shield into a piece of magical gear!

How to use: Equip a shield in your Off-hand, hold a Coating Item in your Main-hand, and Right-Click (Use). Each shield has a maximum of 4 slots.

Risk & Return: There is a 70% chance of success and a 30% chance of failure. Success grants a random buff from Lv.1 to Lv.3. If it fails, the coating shatters and you lose that slot!

The Coating Lineup:

Magic Defense: Reduces Magic / Poison / Wither / Dragon Breath / Warden Sonic Boom damage. (Reaching Lv.3 has a 50% chance to grant Weakness Immunity)

Lightweight: Increases movement speed and gives a chance to dodge physical attacks. (Reaching Lv.3 has a 50% chance to halve fall damage & grant Slowness Immunity)

Luminous:

Provides dynamic lighting around the player. (Reaching Lv.3 grants infinite Night Vision)

Grants continuous Regeneration.
(Reaching Lv.3 grants a Max Health Boost of +4 Hearts!)

Mining: Grants Haste and immunity to Blindness/Darkness. (Reaching Lv.3 grants Fire Resistance)

Tip: How to replace a coating when all 4 slots are full?

Are your shield slots fully packed, but you want to switch to a different coating?

For example, if you want to replace a [Luminous] coating with a [Fortitude] coating, just try applying another [Luminous] coating!

If the coating process fails (30% chance), that specific slot will be emptied, allowing you to apply a new one.

Alternatively, you can just grab a new vanilla shield and build your very own custom shield collection!
