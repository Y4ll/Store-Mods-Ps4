This addon tries to update every part of the game. During your survival you will find new mobs, new food, new structures, new biomes.

The updates of this addon are based on vanilla concerts and fan and author's own ideas.

Throughout your survival you will be able to find new items or find uses for old ones, mainly by learning new crafts in the workbench or finding hints on what can be smelted into a new item.
