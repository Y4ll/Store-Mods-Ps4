Author: Baku_Studio

Baku X RTX Shader is an original Minecraft Bedrock Edition resource pack built for the official Vibrant Visuals graphics mode. It delivers an ultra-bright, high-key cinematic look with extreme daylight exposure, pale luminous skies, vivid green vegetation and a deep cyan underwater atmosphere while fully preserving Minecrafts classic block style.

Pack Information

- Pack Name: Baku X RTX Shader

- Author: Baku_Studio

- Type: Vibrant Visuals / PBR Resource Pack

- Supported Versions: Minecraft Bedrock & Pocket Edition 1.21.120 and newer (1.21 1.26 generation)

- Required Mode: Vibrant Visuals

- Platforms: Android, iOS, Windows, Xbox, PlayStation (devices that support Vibrant Visuals)

Visual Style

The pack focuses on a clean, extremely bright and luminous atmosphere. Expect high-key daylight with pale white horizons, soft blue upper skies, vivid fresh green grass, soft lifted shadows and a strong cyan-blue underwater look. The overall mood feels premium and cinematic while still looking unmistakably like Minecraft.

Note: This is a Vibrant Visuals pack. It is not a true hardware RTX / ray-tracing pack.

Features

Daylight

- Extreme high-key exposure

- Pale luminous white horizon

- Soft light-blue upper sky

- Bright white Minecraft clouds

- Vivid fresh green grass and foliage

- Soft shadows with lifted detail

- Strong ambient sky fill

Underwater

- Deep saturated blue/cyan atmosphere

- Soft volumetric-looking light scattering

- Depth-dependent blue haze

- Soft caustics

- Readable geometry with progressive blue attenuation

Color Grading & Post

- High-key filmic tone mapping

- Soft shadow lift

- Controlled highlight compression

- Natural but vivid greens

- Controlled cinematic bloom

Materials

- Default PBR material response

- Original Minecraft textures and geometry fully preserved

Installation

- Import the .mcpack file

- Activate Baku X RTX Shader in Resource Packs (place near the top)

- Go to Settings Video Graphics Mode Vibrant Visuals

- Enter any world

Performance

Lightweight configuration-only pack. Runs well on mobile and higher-end devices that support Vibrant Visuals.
