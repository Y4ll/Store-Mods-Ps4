THIS ADDON DOES NOT DISABLE ACHIEVEMENTS.

IMPORTANT. TO USE THE ANCIENT TABLE YOU MUST INTERACT IN THE CENTER AT THE TOP OF THE TABLE (PAPER).

Most players skip the first armor in the game because it takes too long to get the resources and they get very weak armor. This add-on adds:

- Leather pieces. Leather items recipes reworked.

- New Reinforced Leather Armor (13 defense points full armor).

- New Ancient Table. Enchant the Lether armor and Wood/Stone tools.

- New items and blocks: Barks, cellulose pulp, flint and bone daggers, firewood ember, nests and more.

- Greater spawn rate of basic animals (Cows, sheeps, chiken, horses, pigs).

BONUS: TEXTURE COMPATIBLE WITH ACTIONS AND STUFF.

1. LEATHER

Use the leather to divide it into pieces.

You can attach the leather pieces to recover the leather.

You now only need x3 leather for a full set of armor. And you still have some pieces left over.

All leather uses have been reworked. (Custom recipes will replace vanilla ones).

Use the leather pieces to create leather strings

You can also get leather strips by cooking rotten meat.

The uses of the leather pieces are similar those vanilla recipes.

2. REINFORCED LEATHER ARMOR

New armor recipes.

3. Ancient Table.

Improves game progression and provides advantages for crafting leather and wood gear early in the game with this new block.

To interact correctly with the ancient table you must click in the middle of the upper face of the block, where the paper is. Selecting another face will not work.

Ancient Table recipe. You can use any type of log and coal.

The table works as a trading system, you only need two items to produce a new, enchanted one.

As a balance, the enchantments the Ancient Table offers for wooden tools are far superior compared to the enchantments for stone tools.

TIP: You can break the table and put it back to change the enchantments.

4. NEW ITEMS AND BLOCKS

Cellulose pulp. This item is useful for obtaining paper in places where there is no sugarcane.

Use any type of debarked log and water in a bucket or bottles.

Alternatively, you can also use seaweed, bark, and bone meal.

Firewood ember. This item will help you make torches or campfires without using coal.

2 new weapons. Bone dagger and flint dagger.

The bone dagger has a high damage of +10 but a durability of 2. Yes, you only have 2 hits. So you'll need more than one to fight enemies. Flint dagger has a high damage of +12 and a durability of 6.

New blocks. In the overworld you will find nests with 0-3 eggs.
