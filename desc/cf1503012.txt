The Bloody Flesh Parasite Addon

A brutal survival experience where a mysterious parasite infection evolves over time, turning your world into a hostile environment where resources vanish and enemies grow stronger.

---

Entity System

- TypeA Family Standard Infected Parasites

- TypeB Family Large Parasites

Each type has unique behaviors, abilities, and increasing threat levels as the infection progresses.

---

Optimization & Game Modes

Yes, the addon is optimized performance depends on the selected subpack:

Performance Mode (Low-End Devices)

- Phase system removed

- Infection spreads through worms

- Infected mobs transform into Living Flesh

- 4 Living Flesh merge into a Large Parasite

Normal Mode (Mid-Range Devices)

- Balanced gameplay experience

- Phase system limited up to Phase 5

- Includes core features from version 0.0.4

Nightmare Mode (High-End Devices)

- Full gameplay experience

- Higher spawn rates

- Increased difficulty

- Starvation mechanics enabled

---

Phase Progression System

The infection evolves based on points and time survived without sleeping.

---

Phase 0 0 Points

- Worms spawn naturally at night

---

Phase 1 6,000 Points (~5 Days Awake)

- Infected parasites begin spawning at night

- Spawn rate remains low

---

Phase 2 8,400 Points (~7 Days Awake)

- Parasites now spawn during daytime

- Day spawn rate becomes higher than Phase 1 night

---

Phase 3 14,400 Points (~12 Days Awake)

- Parasites gain block-breaking abilities

- Limited to wood-type blocks:

fences, gates, logs, planks

---

Phase 4 18,000 Points (~15 Days Awake)

- Passive mobs that provide food begin to despawn:

cows, sheep, pigs, chickens, fish

- Players must rely on farming

- Parasites become more focused on players

---

Phase 5 30,000 Points (~25 Days Awake)

- Large parasites begin spawning naturally:

Charger, Arachnid, Bloater, Walker, Slasher

- Can destroy blocks in a 33 area instantly

- Strong blocks take longer:

obsidian and similar blocks break in 510 seconds

---

Phase 6 36,000 Points (~30 Days Awake)

- A reddish fog appears, reducing visibility

- Natural food drops are disabled:

wheat, beetroot, potato, carrot, apple, berries, fish

- Food can only be found in structures or villages

---

Phase 7 42,000 Points (~35 Days Awake)

- Parasites receive major buffs:

- Attack Damage: 15

- Health: 35

- Increased speed

- Unarmored players can be defeated in ~2 hits

- Large parasites can eliminate players instantly

---

Important Notes

- Designed for challenging survival gameplay

- Best experienced in multiplayer or long-term worlds
