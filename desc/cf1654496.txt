Vantage

A natural, full-body first-person experience for Minecraft: Bedrock Edition!

- Full body visible in first person (legs, torso, arms, armor, capes, held items).

- Fully compatible with other addons whenever possible.

- Compatible with texture packs without causing visual issues.

- Works correctly with both touch controls and mouse & keyboard/controller.

- Hit detection stays aligned with your crosshair interactions still come from your real eye position, so you won't notice any offset.

- Smooth animations without affecting gameplay vanilla still drives the skeleton, Vantage moves the camera.

- Multiplayer-friendly and survival-friendly no operator permissions, no cheats, and other players see you completely normally.

- Lightweight and optimized for performance.

- Compatible with the latest Bedrock version (built for 1.21.100 and up, including 1.26).

- Configurable body visibility, with three body distances (Close / Normal / Far).

- Adjustable camera position on all three axes.

- FOV compatibility, with an optional FOV override.

- Crawling, swimming, sneaking, and riding all handled sneaking and swimming are compensated automatically, and riding keeps your body visible.

- Automatically steps aside for anything that needs the real first-person view: maps, spyglasses, gliding, crawling, and sleeping.

- Support for custom player animations without conflicts.

Controls

- /vantage opens the main menu (toggle, settings, reset). Real custom slash command, permissionLevel: Any with cheatsRequired: false, so survival players without operator rights can use it. No experimental toggles required.

- /vantage:toggle flips Vantage on/off directly. Bind it to a Command Macro under Settings Keyboard & Mouse for a true PC hotkey.

- /vantage:config opens the settings form directly.

- Vantage Settings item (vantage:config_tool) hold use for about a second to open the same menu. Works identically on touch (tap and hold), mouse (hold right-click), and controller (hold the use trigger). Obtainable from the creative Items tab or /give.

- Sneak + Jump quick-toggle on by default; switch it off in the settings menu if you sneak-jump often enough to trip it by accident.

Config options

- First-person body master on/off. When off, Vantage clears the camera entirely and you get the engine's normal first-person view back exactly as if the pack weren't there, perspective key included.

- Body distance (Close / Normal / Far) how far your torso and legs sit from the camera.

- Camera left / right (-0.5 to 0.5 blocks) shifts the camera sideways.

- Camera up / down (-0.5 to 0.5 blocks) raises/lowers the camera.

- Camera forward / back (-0.5 to 0.5 blocks) pulls the camera slightly behind or in front of your eye point.

- Override field of view off by default; leaves your video settings alone unless you turn it on.

- Field of view (30 to 110) only applies while the body is actually being rendered, so spyglass zoom and the map view aren't affected.

- Quick toggle with Sneak + Jump see above.

- Vanilla view while gliding (on by default) a horizontal body sits between the lens and the horizon exactly when precision matters most.

- Vanilla view while crawling (on by default).

- Vanilla view with spyglass (on by default) the zoom overlay is drawn for a true first-person frustum.

- Vanilla view while riding (off by default) your body stays visible on mounts unless you turn this on.

Maps are not configurable: a held map always hands the camera back, because the first-person map view doesn't render under a camera preset. Happy Ghast mounts always hand back too.

All settings are saved per player and persist across sessions.
