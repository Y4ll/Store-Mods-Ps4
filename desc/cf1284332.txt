Lovely Robots is an addon designed to let you enjoy your adventures alongside loyal robotic companions.

Each robot has its own role, behavior, and combat style.
You can tame them, level them up, customize their colors, and activate advanced modes like BaseDefense and AutoAttack to adapt them to your playstyle.

Fight alongside 8 unique robots, each one created for a different purpose and strategy.

Each robot can reach up to 16 levels.

Features:

You can paint them: use the colors below the image of the robots in the frames.

Domestication: Use apples, carrots, golden apples, or golden carrots to tame them.

Passive Regeneration: All heal slowly over time.

Accelerated Healing: Use apples, carrots, golden versions, or even Redstone to heal them quickly.

Standby Mode: You can leave them still when you don't want them to move.

BaseDefense Mode: Protects an area automatically.

AutoAttack Mode: They chase and attack enemies on their own.

Sample statistics: Use a book in them to see their information.

Glow in the Dark: Her eyes and weapons have a glowing nighttime effect.
