Sword Add-on is a simple add-on for Minecraft Bedrock, which adds the Minecraft Dungeons sword to the game.

This sword behaves like an iron sword in every way: same damage, durability, and crafting method. However, it has one key difference:

It performs a sweeping attack similar to Minecraft Java!

With each hit, you not only damage the main enemy, but also all nearby mobs. There are no cooldowns or waiting times:
You can attack as much as you want, whenever you want!

Installation:

1. Download the .mcaddon file

2. Open it directly in Minecraft

3. Activate the behavior and resource pack

4. Make sure to enable experiments
