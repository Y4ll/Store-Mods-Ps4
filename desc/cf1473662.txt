Ranzies Overgrown Cities brings a haunting new life to your world by generating massive abandoned cities reclaimed by nature, where vines, trees, and decay have overtaken once-thriving streets. Explore detailed buildings filled with valuable gun loot, fully compatible with EAC Reboot, Aplok Guns, Krep's tacZ Port and Advance Tactical Gear turning every structure into a high-risk, high-reward scavenging zone. Designed to pair perfectly with zombie apocalypse gameplay like Rise and Survive, this addon creates an immersive survival experience where every ruined block tells a story and every corner could mean danger.
