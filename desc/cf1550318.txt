Ore Vein Miner Fast Mining Addon

Welcome to Ore Vein Miner by BAKU, a lightweight Minecraft Bedrock addon designed to make ore mining faster and more efficient .

With this addon, breaking a single ore block automatically mines the entire connected ore vein, helping you collect resources quickly while keeping your survival gameplay smooth and enjoyable .

Addon Features

- Instant Ore Vein Mining Mine an entire connected ore vein by breaking one ore block.

- Fast Resource Collection Spend less time mining and gather ores much faster.

- Survival Friendly Perfect for survival worlds without changing the vanilla feel.

- Optimized Performance Lightweight and designed for smooth gameplay.

Gameplay Benefits

- Faster Ore Collection Collect diamonds, iron, gold, redstone, emeralds, and more in seconds.

- Better Mining Experience Reduce repetitive mining while exploring caves.

- Exploration Focused Spend more time exploring instead of mining every block individually.

- MCPE & Bedrock Optimized Works smoothly on Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
