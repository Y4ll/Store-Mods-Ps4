Fancy Visuals is my sunset pack. Purple and pink skies at dusk, an ocean that catches all of it, and cherry petals drifting past while it happens.

What this changes

Sunsets

This is the reason the pack exists. Dusk pulls purple and pink across the entire sky, not just a band near the sun, so the color is over your head as well as in front of you.

Ocean at dusk

The water surface picks up the sunset and glows with it. Standing on a beach facing west is the single best shot in this pack.

Cherry petals

Petals drift through the air on the wind, with a soft pink haze sitting between the trees.

Color grading

Warm tones across every biome so nothing goes cold or grey, even at midday. It keeps the whole world sitting in the same mood as the sunsets.

Day to night

Ambient light shifts gradually instead of snapping over. Evening actually takes its time.

Bark and leaves

Birch and oak bark and leaves get real depth up close, so trees hold up when you stand right next to them instead of going flat.

PBR

Blocks react to light with real depth, which is where the detail on bark and leaves comes from.

PBR is also the heaviest part of any Vibrant Visuals pack, so it is not forced on you. It ships as a subpack, which means you can switch it on or off yourself. When you activate Fancy Visuals in Global Resources, pick the version you want. Turn PBR off if your device runs hot or drops frames.

Requirements

THIS PACK IS FOR VIBRANT VISUALS ONLY
IT DOES NOT WORK WITH MB LOADER

Vibrant Visuals must be ON. This pack is built for Bedrock's Vibrant Visuals renderer. The pack will not do anything if it is off.

Where to turn it on: Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals

If that toggle is not there, your device or version does not support Vibrant Visuals yet.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Fancy Visuals

- Pick the subpack you want. PBR on for the full look, PBR off for better performance.

- Turn on Settings > Video > Vibrant Visuals

- If the lighting still looks the same, leave the world and load it again once
