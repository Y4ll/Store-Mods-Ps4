PROGRESSION & LEVELING

Choose your race at the start: Soul Reaper, Hollow (Arrancar), Quincy, or Fullbringer (Patreon exclusive see below).

Level up by earning XP the amount needed scales per level (e.g. Level 1 = 100 XP, Level 2 = 200 XP). General cap is Level 20 (Kenpachi path pushes to Level 40).

Ways to earn XP:

-

Slaying mobs Base: 5 XP | Menos Grande: 45 XP | Adjuchas: 85 XP | Vasto Lorde: 200 XP | Bosses: 300 XP

-

Meditation/Focus (Levels 119 only) Sneak for 3 consecutive seconds with your race's specific item for +20 XP

Spiritual Power is earned separately by killing mobs. Used to meet requirements for heavy weapons and abilities not for leveling.

THE KENPACHI PATH (Soul Reaper Secret Route)

The most brutal progression route in the addon. The only path that reaches Level 40.

Level 10 The Refusal
Instead of choosing a Shikai, select "... (Keep your Asauchi)" in the Progression Manual.
You'll receive a warning: "Your blade refuses to reveal its name. Walk the path of raw strength your Asauchi will awaken as Nozarashi at Level 20. This choice is permanent."
Confirm it and you're locked in. You get no Shikai powers. Fight on with your bare Asauchi.

Levels 1020 The Grind
No powers. No Shikai. Just raw combat and Kido until you hit Level 20.
At Level 20, instead of a Bankai challenge, you receive: "Your Asauchi trembles with untamed power. It is ready to awaken!"

Level 20 Nozarashi Awakens
Open your Progression Manual and confirm "Awaken Nozarashi."
Your Asauchi shatters and is permanently replaced by Nozarashi (The Wild Cleaver).
Your level cap breaks from 20 40 and you enter a specialized XP tracking system.

Levels 2040 The Real Grind
While other Soul Reapers are done at Level 20 with Bankai, you grind 20 more levels with only Nozarashi's Shikai. Requires massive amounts of Hollow slaying.

Level 40 Yachiru Nozarashi (Bankai)
No quest. No spirit battle. The Bankai forces itself out automatically.
"BANKAI UNLOCKED: YACHIRU NOZARASHI! The savage blade has fully awakened. You now wield Kenpachi's ultimate power!
