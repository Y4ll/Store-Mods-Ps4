GAVTI RGB HITBOX

RGB JAVA-STYLE HITBOX VISUALIZER BEDROCK EDITION

Gavti RGB Hitbox adds a precise Java Edition-style hitbox around the player using clear red, green and blue lines. Enjoy a clean technical visual that makes the player boundary easy to identify.

[ FEATURES ]

- RGB Hitbox Design the old single-colour outline has been upgraded with distinct red, green and blue edges for a cleaner Java-style appearance.

- Dynamic Player Hitbox the outline adjusts according to supported player poses, including standing, sneaking, swimming, crawling and sleeping.

- Clean and Precise thin, clearly visible lines show the player boundary without covering the skin or making the screen feel cluttered.

- One-Tap Toggle switch the hitbox ON or OFF directly through the pack settings slider without removing or replacing the pack.

- Resource Pack Only no behavior pack, scripts, commands or additional downloads are required.

[ HOW TO INSTALL ]

- Download the .mcpack file and open it Minecraft will import it automatically.

- Apply Gavti RGB Hitbox to your world from the resource pack list.

- Open the pack settings and use the slider whenever you want to switch the RGB hitbox ON or OFF.
