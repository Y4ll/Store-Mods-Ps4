The Wild Lands is Add-on that adds several prehistoric animals that lived on earth
There are Shantungosaurus, Tarbosaurus, Oxalaia, Ornithomimus and more
You need to survive in this dangerous wild lands
Can You Survive?

The Tyrant of Mongolia, Tarbosaurus
The most dangerous dinosaur in this land..... Watch out for its theeth. It's very sharp.....
Health: 190
Damage: 9
Tame: Use your shield and block the Tarbosaurus' attack. Then it will sometimes get stunned, and when it get stunned feed it meat, tendon, or ornithomimus' drumstick
Spawn: Mesa

The Gentle Giant, Shantungosaurus
Shantungosaurus is large hadrosauridae. It's very peaceful when normal, but be careful, if you provoke it, it will stomp you with its giant feet
Health: 300
Damage: 5
Tame: Feed it sweet berries
Spawn: Plains

The Ostrich like dinosaur, Ornithomimus
Ornithomimus is Ostrich like dinosaur. It's quite fast, and will attack you if you attack it
And it loves to eat some seeds
Health: 20
Damage: 4
Tame: Feed it wheat seeds
Spawns: Plains

The Spiked Lizard, Styracosaurus
Styracosaurus is spiked lizard which have a lot of horns. It's very peaceful when it's normal, but don't you dare provoke it first, or, There will be consequences....
Health: 100
Damage: 8
Tame: Feed it some sweet berries
Spawns: Plains

The Ancestor of Whale, Pakicetus
Pakicetus is ancestor of whale, who really likes to eat fish. It's really cute animal, and if you tame it, it will become your one of the most cute and trustful pet.
Health: 10
Damage: 3
Tame: Feed it any kinds of fishes
Spawns: Plains

The Dangerous giant sailed predator, Oxalaia
Oxalia is unique dinosaur which really similar to Spinosaurus. It's spawns in swamp and don't get close to it, it can attack you with its really fast attack speed...
Health: 200
Damage: 6
Tame: Feed it any kinds of fishes
Spawns: Swamp

The Hunter with unique sail, Concavenator
Concavenator is middle sized hunter that preys on any animals that smaller than it. It appears in taiga biome, and if you encountered with it, run fast as possible as you can, cause it will hunt you down with its really fast speed.
Health: 35
Attack: 5
Tame: Feed it with meat, tendon, or ornithomimus drumstick
Spawns: Taiga

The Dangerous Ocean predator, Liopleurodon
liopleurodon is middle sized predator that lives any kinds of ocean except for cold ocean. It will hunt down any animals that in the ocean and it will prey on player as well. So... good luck if you go to the Ocean
Health: 50
Damage: 4
Tame: Not Tameable Currently
Spawns: Any kinds of ocean except for cold Ocean
