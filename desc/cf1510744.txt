DESCRIPTION

Expand your adventure with BackpacksPlus, a handy addon that introduces stylish backpacks in a variety of colors! Whether you're exploring caves, building massive structures, or going on long journeys, these backpacks help you carry more and stay organized.

Each backpack comes with an interesting and unique mechanic that makes inventory management more immersive and fun. Choose your favorite color and take your storage to the next level!

FEATURES

- Multiple backpack colors to match your style

- Regular and Large Backpack variants

- Large Backpacks provide expanded storage

- Simple, clean design that fits Minecraft Bedrock

- You can not have more than 2 backpacks in Survival Mode

- No backpack limit in Creative Mode

- You can wear backpacks using your off-hand slot

- Backpacks keep their stored items when broken and picked up

- Dye system for recoloring backpacks

- Custom crafting recipes and organized creative inventory
