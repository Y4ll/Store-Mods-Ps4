# More Body Animation and Effects

**By Lumenx2009**

### Summary (short tagline, ~1 sentence)

Replaces 20+ vanilla player animations (idle, walk, run, swim, climb, and more) with smoother custom ones, plus ambient particle effects glowing items, critical hit sparks, sleep/water splashes all in one lightweight add-on.

---

## Description (main body)

**More Body Animation and Effects** brings extra life to your world with two systems working together:

** Body Animations**

- 20+ custom replacement animations that play automatically based on what you're doing: Idle, Walk, Run, Sneak, Jump, Crawl, Swim, Climb, Fall, Bow, Fishing Rod, Eat, Drink, Minecart, Melee, Sit, Hurt, Crossbow, Map, and Fly

- These are passive/contextual they trigger on their own, there's no emote wheel or menu to "play" one manually

- An in-game info panel lists what each animation does, plus admin controls to enable/disable animations globally or for specific players

- No changes to your player skin/model works with any skin, persona, or cape

** Particles & Effects**

- Glowing particle effect on dropped items (Overworld, Nether, and The End)

- Critical hit particle effects

- Sleep and water splash particle effects

- Custom glow-item render effects

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock **1.26.20+**

- No experimental toggles needed runs on stable scripting APIs

- Enable both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack in your world

### Installation

1. Download the `.mcaddon` file

2. Open it (or double-click) Minecraft will import both packs automatically

3. Go to your world settings **Behavior Packs** activate "More Body Animation and Effects"

4. Repeat for **Resource Packs**

### Notes

- Version: 1.0.0

- No changes to vanilla player model/skin

- Compatible with most other add-ons; if you run into script errors, check for conflicts with other scripting-based add-ons in the same world
