Mob Spawn Multiplier increases the number of mobs when they spawn in the world

Features:

- Multiplies mobs when they spawn in the world.

- Supports mobs from other addons

- Does not require any Experimental Features

- Compatible with achievements.

Available Versions

-

Hostile Mob - Multiplies all hostile mobs when they spawn (excluding bosses)

-

Normal Mob - Multiplies all passive and neutral mobs when they spawn.

OPTIONS

- You can configure the multiplier value in the behavior pack settings. There are currently five options: x1.5, x2, x3, x4, and x5.

Notes:

- Choose the multiplier carefully. High multipliers can cause heavy lag or even crash the game, depending on your device and the addons installed.

- The Normal Mob pack and the Hostile Mob pack can be used together.

- This addon is pure fun only not designed for balanced gameplay
