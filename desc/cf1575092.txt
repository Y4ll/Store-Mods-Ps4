Sun Man Ultra Series V3.2

Sun Man Ultra Series V3.2 is a major upgrade of the original Sun Man addon, bringing a darker atmosphere, improved visuals, enhanced animations, custom UI, and many bug fixes. Updated and improved by GG Studios this version focuses on making Sun Man feel more mysterious, immersive, and terrifying while preserving the original concept.
