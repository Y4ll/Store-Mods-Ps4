Addon inspired by the anime Black Clover, bringing to Minecraft Bedrock the experience of becoming a true Magic Knight. Inspired by the world of magic, grimoires, and intense battles of the Clover Kingdom, this addon introduces systems based on the anime, including magical grimoires, missions, and the Magic Knights entrance exam.

Prepare to awaken your power, evolve your magic, and forge your path to becoming one of the strongest mages in the kingdom. Train to master your abilities, complete challenging missions, and prove your strength in magical combat. Only those with determination and skill will rise through the ranks and earn their place among the elite Magic Knights.

Are you ready to begin your journey in magic? Choose your grimoire, improve your abilities, and grow as a true Magic Knight.
