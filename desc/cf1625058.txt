Pokemon Expansion

Bring a Pokemon-inspired creature capturing experience to Minecraft Bedrock Edition with Pokemon Expansion by FleetMc Studio. Capture your favorite mobs, carry them inside reusable Orbs, and release them whenever you need. Whether you're collecting rare creatures, transporting animals, or creating your own monster battles, this addon makes mob capturing simple, fun, and rewarding.

Three different Orbs are included, each with its own capture power and supported mob types. Upgrade your Orbs through crafting and become the ultimate creature collector.

Available Orbs

- Capture Orb Captures passive mobs with a 92% success rate.

- Hunter Orb Captures both passive and hostile mobs with a 90% success rate.

- Elite Orb Captures almost every mob in Minecraft (except players) with a guaranteed 100% success rate.

How to Capture Mobs

- Craft or obtain an empty Orb.

- Hold the Orb in your hand.

- Use or attack a supported mob.

- If the capture succeeds, the mob is instantly stored inside the Orb.

- Your empty Orb immediately becomes a Filled Orb.

- If the capture fails, simply wait for the 3-second cooldown and try again.

Cooldown System

After every capture attempt, your Orb enters a short 3-second cooldown. During this time you'll see an "Orb Cooling Down..." message on your screen.

Once the cooldown finishes, you'll receive an "Orb Ready!" message and can attempt another capture.

Release Your Mobs Anytime

Simply use a Filled Orb to release the captured mob a short distance in front of you.

After releasing the creature, the Orb automatically becomes empty again and is ready to capture another mob.

Mob Information is Saved

Captured mobs keep important information while stored inside the Orb. Depending on the mob type, the addon saves:

- Current Health

- Maximum Health

- Custom Name

- Baby or Adult State

- Tamed Status

- Color

- Variant

- Mark Variant

- Skin ID

These details are displayed directly inside the Orb's tooltip (Lore).

Installation Guide

- Download the addon.

- Open it using ZArchiver or any file manager.

- Select Open With Minecraft.

- Wait for Minecraft to import both packs.

- Enable both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack.

- Create or open your world and start capturing mobs.
