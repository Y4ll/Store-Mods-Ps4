Tremors Addon: Underground Terror

Immerse yourself in the Tremors universe with this Addon that brings the complete lifecycle of the desert's most feared creatures to Minecraft.

Evolution & Ecosystem

Creatures don't just spawnthey live, hunt, and evolve!

- Dirt Dragons: The beginning of the danger, burrowing through the sand.

- Graboids: Blind apex predators that detect every vibration and sound.

- Shriekers: Fast, hungry, and capable of multiplying after every meal.

- Ass-Blasters: The final evolution that takes the terror to the skies.

Key Features

- Heat Vision: Experience the tension with proximity alerts and heartbeats when a predator has you in its sights.

- Tamable Mounts: Tame and ride your own Shriekers! Train them to be your loyal battle companions.

- Realistic Mechanics: Advanced sound detection system, dynamic egg incubation, and custom cinematic animations.

The Ultimate Challenge: White Graboid

A rare, massive, and lethal evolution. An epic Boss with a dedicated health bar and powerful breach attacks that will test your survival skills to the limit.

Will you conquer the desert, or will you become worm food?

[!WARNING] BETA DISCLAIMER: This addon is currently in a beta state. You may encounter bugs, unfinished features, or performance issues. Please back up your worlds before use. Feedback and bug reports are welcome to help improve the experience!
