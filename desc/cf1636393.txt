Black Hole PvP

Enter a dark cosmic battlefield with Black Hole PvP, a Minecraft Bedrock texture pack designed for players who want a unique and immersive PvP experience. This pack transforms the game's atmosphere with stunning black hole-inspired visuals, custom skyboxes, enhanced menu panoramas, and carefully optimized textures that maintain smooth performance across a wide range of devices.

Featuring multiple subpacks, players can customize their visual experience with different fog settings to improve visibility and match their preferred playstyle. The pack focuses on delivering a clean and competitive environment while maintaining an eye-catching space-themed aesthetic. Whether you're battling in servers, dueling friends, or exploring your worlds, Black Hole PvP adds a fresh and futuristic look to Minecraft without sacrificing performance.
