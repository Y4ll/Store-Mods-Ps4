What is Vein Miner?

If you have ever mined a single diamond ore and then had to manually hunt around for the rest of the vein, you know how tedious that gets. Vein Miner solves that completely. The moment you break one ore with a pickaxe, the entire connected vein breaks with it all at once, no extra effort.

It supports every ore type in the game including deepslate variants, nether ores and ancient debris. The items go straight to your inventory, and if you have auto smelt turned on, iron, gold and copper come out already smelted so you do not even need a furnace for basic processing.

If you want to mine normally at any point, just hold sneak while mining and vein miner stays disabled for that block. Everything is configurable max vein size, XP multiplier, auto smelt, sounds all through an in-game settings panel.
