Adds pigeons that can send items to players. Part of Billey's Mobs now available as a standalone add-on.

Unlike Billey's Mobs, this add-on doesn't require the Beta APIs experimental toggle. However you should enable it anyway as it might be needed for other add-ons, and there's no downside to having it turned on.

Pigeons

Viewing This Info In-Game

- You can view this information in-game by using a book or a feather on a pigeon.

General

- Pigeons spawn in plains and are especially common in flower forests and birch forests.

- They can be tamed with any kind of seeds, carrots, or bread.

Pooping

- Pigeons poop randomly.

- Their poop can be used to:

- Craft bone meal.

- Boost pollinating mobs from the Billey's Mobs add-on (e.g., pill bugs and stick bugs).

- Their poop shouldn't be an issue since it turns into its item form and then despawns quickly

Finding Apples

- Tamed pigeons will find apples in trees if they are not made to sit.

Sending Items to Players

- Pigeons can deliver items to other players. Here's how:

- Craft a pigeon backpack:

- Recipe: 2 string, 1 wool, and 2 leather.

- Equip the backpack:

- Use it on the pigeon.

- Using the backpack:

- Pet the pigeon (sneak + long tap, right-click, or use the pet button) while holding an item to store it in the backpack.

- If the pigeons backpack is full, petting it while holding an item will make it drop its contents.

- Pet it with an empty hand to select the player to send the pigeon to. (Players must be in the same dimension and not right next to you.)

- Customization:

- The pigeon backpack can be dyed by applying a dye to the pigeon wearing it.

Wandering

- Use a stick on a pigeon to make it wander around instead of following you.

Get More Useful Pets

Get the Billey's Mobs Add-on for free on MCPEDL or CurseForge for:

- More useful pets like this one.

- The ability to untame (give away) pigeons, put hats on them, and place them on your shoulder

If you download Billey's Mobs make sure to remove this add-on since they add the exact same pigeon mob and issues may occur.
