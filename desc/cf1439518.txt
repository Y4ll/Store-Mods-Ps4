After Gojo Satoru is freed from the Prison Realm cube, he fights zombies and skeletons and wants to quickly face the Ender Dragon.

Challenge for you: can you finish Survival in 1 hour with Gojo Satorus power?

How to activate the skill:

When you attack, you push mobs like a red orb.
When you use a snowball, you pull mobs.
When you use purple, you destroy and push mobs.
