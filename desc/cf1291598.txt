A deadly ancient virus that has been trapped in ice for billions of years, only to be released after the global warming melts down the ice-caps. This virus spreads over the entire planet and infected a lot of people.

This virus loves cold places, so when winter comes it will mutate and will be harder to resist.

For those that are still alive must now navigate a frozen wasteland, where resources are scarce and the remnants of civilization fight for survival in a harsh, unforgiving world.

=-Description-=

The Zombies are almost 2x slower than vanilla zombies, which makes it easier to be dodged, but they can spot you from a longer distance.

Bloodmoon will also occur in random nights. 5% per night and will increase by 10% every 10 days.
