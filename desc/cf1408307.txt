This shader introduces shadow-synced animated grass, where vegetation moves in harmony with light direction and intensity instead of waving independently. As the sun rises and falls, shadows stretch, soften, and shiftand the grass responds naturally, grounding every scene in physical realism.

From golden-hour forests with long, warm shadows...
to calm oceans reflecting the midday sun...
to soft pink biomes filled with drifting petals and living foliage...
each environment feels cohesive, atmospheric, and alive.

Lighting is carefully balanced to enhance depth without overpowering Minecrafts identity. Volumetric sunlight, realistic water reflections, smooth shadow transitions, and subtle ambient effects work together to create a cinematic yet believable visual experience.
