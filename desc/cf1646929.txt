Glacier Visuals is my cold tone pack. Deep blues, clean water and frosty skies. I wanted something that feels icy and quiet instead of warm and vibrant like the rest of my packs. Auroras over still water, misty cherry groves under a purple sky, that kind of thing.

What this changes

Color grading

Blue sits on everything. Nothing in the world is allowed to go warm, which is what keeps the whole pack feeling cold instead of just having a blue sky.

Water

Shallow water is clear enough to see seagrass and the ocean floor through it. Open ocean stays deep blue with clean reflections running along the horizon.

Night sky and auroras

Auroras at night, and they read best over calm water where the surface picks them up.

Cherry groves

Cherry groves get a cool misty purple instead of the usual warm pink. This is my favorite part of the pack and the biggest single difference from anything else I have made.

Rain and fog

Rain is heavier and comes with more fog behind it. Storms actually feel like storms instead of a texture falling past the camera.

Villages

Villages get a soft cold light at dusk and through the night, not the warm orange glow most packs give them.

Reflections

Blocks and armor pick up real reflections, which is where the ice look comes from up close.

Nether portals

Portals still glow properly and throw their own light, even with cold grading sitting on everything around them.

Requirements

THIS PACK IS FOR VIBRANT VISUALS ONLY
IT DOES NOT WORK WITH MB LOADER

Vibrant Visuals must be ON. This pack is built for Bedrock's Vibrant Visuals renderer. The pack will not do anything if it is off.

Where to turn it on: Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals

If that toggle is not there, your device or version does not support Vibrant Visuals yet.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Glacier Visuals

- Turn on Settings > Video > Vibrant Visuals

- If the lighting still looks the same, leave the world and load it again once
