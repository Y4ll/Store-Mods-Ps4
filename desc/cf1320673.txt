With this texture pack you can make all armor invisible, so you can always see your own skin and that of other players. This works on All Armor (helmets, chestplates, leggings and boots) and, if you want, also the Turtle Shell and Elytra.

TEXTURE SETTING:

You can choose to have:

- All Armor (all types of helmets, chestplates, leggings and boots) Invisible;

- All Armor Invisible but only Helmets Visible;

- All Armor, Turtle Shell, and Elytra Invisible;

- All Armor and Elytra Invisible;

- All Armor and Turtle Shell Invisible;

- Only Elytra Invisible;

- Only Netherite Armor Invisible;

- Only Diamond Armor Invisible;

- Only Gold Armor Invisible;

- Only Iron Armor Invisible;

- Only Copper Armor Invisible;

- Only Chain Armor Invisible;

- Only Leather Armor Invisible.

If you want to share the texture, be sure to send the MCPEDL or CURSEFORGE texture link.
You can also find this content on the official ADDONS UNIVERSE App!
Remember, this content is strictly exclusive to these 3 platforms.
