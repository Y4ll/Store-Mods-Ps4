How to get started?

First of all, TURN ON BETA API IN EXPERIMENTAL GAMEPLAYS!!!
Also, you need to activate extra behavior pack to make addon work.

How to get my stand?

To get your stand, you need to have an arrow first. To get your arrow, you need to find meteor on plain biome. Then use meteor to craft one.

There are also 2 different types of stand arrow, special arrow and requiem arrow. You can find heart father and Polnareff to get these 2 arrows.
After using the arrow, you need to go through trials. Including fighting and so on. If you don't like these trials, you can turn them off in settings. By sneaking twice with the golden slot.
You can also check your stand's introduction in settings like this(If you have one).
