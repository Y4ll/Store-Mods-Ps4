Take your Minecraft Bedrock SkyBlock experience to the next level with this feature-rich addon! Start on your own island and expand it into a thriving empire using an advanced economy, island upgrades, custom shops, generators, and powerful management tools.

Build, farm, mine, trade, and progress through a fully customizable SkyBlock experience designed for both single-player and multiplayer worlds. Manage your island with easy-to-use GUIs, invite friends, earn money, unlock upgrades, and grow your island's value as you work towards becoming the richest player.

Features

- Create and manage your own SkyBlock island

- Invite friends and manage island members

- Complete economy with balance, bank, and player payments

- Buy and sell hundreds of items

- Custom generators and resource progression

- Farming, mining and mob farming support

- Island leveling and upgrade system

- Island value and progression tracking

- Island bank with shared storage

- Player shops and Auction House support

- Daily rewards, quests, and achievements

- Island protection and permissions

- Modern GUI menus for all major features

- Optimized for Minecraft Bedrock Realms, Worlds, and Dedicated Servers

- Easy to configure and expand

Whether you're playing solo or with friends, this addon provides a complete SkyBlock experience packed with progression, automation, and endless opportunities to grow your island.

Compatible with Minecraft Bedrock Edition and designed for long-term survival gameplay.
