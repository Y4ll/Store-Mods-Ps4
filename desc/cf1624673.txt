# Acuity Horror Addon

Acuity is a psychological horror mod that adds a lost cousinwhom Verity cannot rememberto the game.

### Features:
- **New Entity:** Custom Acuity monster model and behavior.
- **Atmosphere:** Eerie visual blur effects and green-themed horror mechanics.
- **Custom Audio:** Atmospheric sound effects and scary custom music.
- **Survival Challenge:** Try to survive against the entity!
