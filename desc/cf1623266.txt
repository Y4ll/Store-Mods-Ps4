Control Overhaul v1

Tired of clunky touch controls clogging up your screen while playing Minecraft Bedrock/Pocket Edition? Control Overhaul v1 redesigns your mobile HUD into a streamlined, high-efficiency interface optimized for fast-paced PvP, End Crystal fighting, and overall smoother gameplay.

Key Features

1. Cleaner On-Screen Action Buttons

Hitting & Placing Refined: Replaces bulky action icons with transparent, minimalist, high-contrast buttons for hitting and block placing.

Unobstructed View: Maximizes screen visibility so you never lose sight of your target or crosshair in high-stakes fights.

2. Custom Subpack Configurations

Customize your HUD according to your preferred layout directly from the Resource Pack settings:

Default Resolution: Keeps intuitive, clean icon designs for quick touch targets.

Clean v2 Mode: Replaces standard button icons with crisp text labels (LEFT, RIGHT, CTRL, SHIFT, SPACE) for a distinct, minimal aesthetics layout.

3. Built for PvP Performance

Perfect for Crystal PvP, Anchor PvP, and fast-paced mobile combat.

Provides immediate visual clarity when swapping items, placing crystals, or pearling under pressure.

Lightweight resource size with zero impact on frame rates.
