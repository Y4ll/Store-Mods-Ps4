Terror Ambient V2 Horror Atmosphere Add-On for Minecraft Bedrock Turn your Minecraft world into a dark and terrifying experience with Terror Ambient V2. This add-on adds immersive ambient horror sounds that play automatically depending on where the player is, creating tension, suspense, and a creepy atmosphere while exploring. Perfect for horror maps, survival worlds, cave exploration, and scary multiplayer experiences. Features Automatic ambient sound system Creepy horror sounds and dark piano effects Different sounds for caves and surface areas Immersive exploration atmosphere Lightweight and optimized Works in survival and multiplayer Compatible with Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21+ Included Sounds Horror Ambience Disturbing ambient effects Sudden scary sounds Dark environmental atmosphere Piano Ambience Multiple eerie piano tracks Suspenseful background music Psychological horror feeling Cave Sounds Special underground ambience Creepy cave echoes Exploration tension Default Ambient Sounds Ambient effects for normal world exploration Dynamic atmosphere while traveling How It Works The add-on automatically detects the players environment and plays different ambient sounds depending on the location: Surface ambience Cave ambience Horror events Random atmospheric effects No commands are required after setup. Installation Import the .mcaddon file into Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Activate both: Behavior Pack Resource Pack Enable: Holiday Creator Features (if needed) Enter your world and enjoy the horror atmosphere.
