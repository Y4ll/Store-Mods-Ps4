A collection of sleek cyberpunk LED lights designed to illuminate your builds with vibrant neon energy. Perfect for sci-fi bases, neon cities, and high-tech interiors.

Tutorial:
The Panels and Blocks are placed like normal. Panels are mounted to the surfaces of blocks.
The LED Rods are directional. Look in the direction you wish to place them.
You can place them under blocks, to choose if it is standing up or against the bottom of the block; Simply Crouch and it will change direction.
