How to Install & Activate

1. Install the Add-on

Download the add-on and open the .mcaddon file. Minecraft will import it automatically.

2. Activate the Pack

Create a new world or edit an existing one.

Go to:
Behavior Packs & Resource Packs Activate "One Click PvP Kit Equip"

3. Enable Required Settings

Before entering the world, enable:

Beta APIs
Cheats

These settings are required for the add-on to work correctly.

4. Join Your World

Enter your world after enabling the pack and required settings.

5. Initialize the Add-on

Open the chat and run:

/function kit/all/<kit>
