Crosshair Indicator is a resource pack/pvp texture pack/pvp packs that changes crosshair color to red (indicator) when player or mob is reached to be hit.

Features

- Change the crosshair color to red when looking at mobs or players, very useful for knowing when to attack.

- Automatically changes the crosshair if you use a custom crosshair without having to edit the crosshair indicator.

- Highly compatible with other packs.
