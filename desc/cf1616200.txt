Better First Person lets you see your own body in first person for a more immersive Minecraft experience. It's vanilla-friendly, supports multiplayer, and works with most texture packs, animation packs, and addons.

Installation
Simply place the addon's Resource Pack above any other texture packs you're using to ensure everything displays correctly.

For Addon Creators
Better First Person continuously updates the camera to maintain the first-person view. If your addon needs temporary control of the camera, add the player tag "none_constant_cam" before setting your own camera, then remove the tag once you're finished. This temporarily disables Better First Person's automatic camera updates.
