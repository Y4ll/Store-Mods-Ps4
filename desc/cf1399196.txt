Outlined Grass

Have you seen those Minecraft Java shaders where grass and other blocks have a clean outline? This pack adds a similar not identical, but close outlined effect to your world.

It adds white outlines to grass, wheat, flowers, potatoes, carrots, planks, and other vegetation, making everything look cleaner and more defined. The effect looks especially good when playing with shaders.

Feedback & Suggestions

I created these outlines based on my own style, but if you think I should improve anything or adjust the look, feel free to leave your suggestions in the comments. Your feedback will help me make this pack even better lets improve it together!
