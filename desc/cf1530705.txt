Sands Superheroes V5.5 is the final version of the original Sands Superheroes add-on.

Everything is now back together as one add-on, so you don't need to download separate packs for every hero anymore. Just install Sands Superheroes V5.5 and you're good to go.

Heroes included:

The Flash
Superman
Iron Man
Spider-Man
Invincible
Homelander
Conquest

There's a bunch of stuff to mess around with including custom suits, abilities, flying, super speed, lasers, webs, heavy attacks, custom sounds and more.

This will be the final major update for Sands Superheroes. I'm now moving my focus over to its replacement...

Sands Superheroes Remastered

We're currently getting ready for Sands Superheroes Remastered, a complete remake of the add-on and by far the biggest project I've worked on.

The goal is to make it one of the biggest and best superhero add-ons for Minecraft Bedrock, with much better heroes, abilities, models, animations, systems and a LOT more.

So while V5.5 is the end of the original add-on, it's definitely not the end of Sands Superheroes lol.
