3D My Totem Doll

Bring your Minecraft skin to life as a 3D Totem Player!

This resource pack turns the Totem of Undying into a 3D player model using your Minecraft skin. It now also includes a 2D model, blinking eyes, size adjustment, and different model options.

Features

- Custom 3D Totem Player

- Uses your Minecraft skin

- Supports Slim (Alex) and Wide (Steve)

- New 2D Totem Model

- Animated blinking eyes

- Adjustable Totem size

- Hide the Player and only show the Bucket/Totem

- Foot Model option

- Supports multiple animation systems

Model Options

Hide Player

Hide the Player to show only the Bucket/Totem.

Default 3D Totem Player

The default 3D player model using your Minecraft skin.

2D Totem

A 2D model for a simpler Totem look.

Foot Model

Shows only the player's legs and feet without the full player body.

Animation Support

Supports:

JUSTANIMATION+ 1.4

HMI

XNOVA 1.2

Action & Stuff

Choose the animation style that matches your setup through Resource Pack Settings.

Skin

To use your own skin, put it in:

Slim (Alex):

- textures/entity/totem [ Skin Player ] By Ancxliy/alex

Wide (Steve):

- textures/entity/totem [ Skin Player ] By Ancxliy/steve

Then select Slim [Alex] or Wide [Steve] in Pack Settings.
