Minecraft, But There Are Custom OP Pickaxes!

This mod adds custom super pickaxes into Minecraft.

There are total 5 overpowered (OP) pickaxes, and each pickaxe has its own unique ability.

These pickaxes are not normal tools they give OP powers, make mining easier, survival faster, and gameplay more fun.

1. Dirt Pickaxe

Ability :

Gives 8 bread when mining or even while holding it.

Converts stone, deepslate, and other underground hard materials into haybales.

Recipe :

3 Dirt at the top

2 Sticks in the center

2. Emerald Pickaxe

Ability :

All villager trades become extremely cheap.

For 15 emeralds, you can trade for anything the villager offers.

You also get emeralds while holding or mining with this pickaxe.

Recipe :

3 Emeralds at the top

2 Sticks in the center

3. Sword Pickaxe (Most Deadliest Pickaxe)

Ability :

Deals 75 damage.

Mines stone and deepslate easily.

Gives Water Breathing and Regeneration while holding it.

Recipe :

3 Iron Swords at the top

2 Sticks in the cente

4. X-Ray Pickaxe

Ability :

Turns stone, deepslate, and all underground hard materials into glass.

Makes all ores clearly visible.

Recipe :

Diamond on the left

Redstone in the middle

Diamond on the right

5. Flying Pickaxe

Ability :

Allows the player to fly.

Works in Education / experimental gameplay.

Recipe :

3 Feathers at the top

2 Sticks in the center

These custom super pickaxes are extremely overpowered and designed to make gameplay faster, easier, and more fun. Each pickaxe has a unique ability that completely changes how you play.Enjoy!
