Early access / previews available via Discord by Server Boosting

Better Bedwars is a texture pack that simplifies your experience while still keeping the original look and feel of Minecraft. The aim is to simplify your Bedwars experience by making stuff less distracting.

If you ask a question that's already answered in the spoiler below, I will most likely ignore it.

Frequently Asked Questions:

Q: What features from Better Bedwars are missing from Better Bedwars (Bedrock)?

A: Better Bedwars and Better Bedwars (Bedrock) are nearly identical, however Better Bedwars (Bedrock) is missing the custom creative inventory tabs.

Q: Can you add _________ to Better Bedwars (Bedrock)?
A: Most likely not. I work and add new stuff to Better Bedwars (Bedrock) at my own pace and when I feel that it is necessary. However, if you do have any request or suggestions, feel free to leave a comment or say something in the Discord.

Q: How do I get rid of the weird missing texture that shows up when opening my inventory?

A: Go to Video Settings and disable Screen Animations

Q: How do I fix the weird overlapping and offset issue that happens when the recipe book is fully or partially visible?

A: Unfortunately, there was nothing we could do to fix this, as this seems to be caused by something that Mojang did. So, it is recommened that you just simply switch your inventory view to inventory only.

Q: I found a bug/issue with Better Bedwars (Bedrock). What should I do?

A: Create a ticket on the Discord server
