VISIBLE OG ORES PLUS

ENGLISH

Visible OG Ores Plus brings back the classic Minecraft ore textures, giving your world a nostalgic look while making ores easier to see underground. Find diamonds, emeralds, ancient debris, and many other ores more easily, even in the darkest caves.

Features:

Glowing effects for ores
Classic ore textures from older versions
Ores that are much easier to spot
Perfect for bringing back the OG Minecraft style

Requires Vibrant Visuals Graphics to enable the glowing effect.

How to activate it: https://youtu.be/h0CrQ_0gDaw?si=KMnd8kJoJCap0jHU
