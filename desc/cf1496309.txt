Features

- Adds 9 new furnaces, ranging from Copper to Rainbow.

- Adds 4 augments: Blasting, Smoking, Speed and Fuel.

- The furnaces contain 2 interfaces: one for heating items and another for augments.

- Click the button in the corner of the interface to switch between them.

- The Blasting and Smoking augments go in the red slot, while the Speed and Fuel augments go in the green slot. The blue slot doesn't have an augment yet.

Add-On Compatibility

- Added compatibility with other add-ons.

- Items, ores, and other contents from other add-ons that can be smelted in the furnace are now supported. On first use, wait about 10 seconds for them to be registered.

REQUIRED: Install both the RP and BP files. Pay close attention to this step!
