Shield Indicator shows the current state of your shield directly on your screen.

It lets you instantly know whether your shield is active, inactive, or on cooldown, so you can react faster during combat.

The indicator changes color depending on the shield state:

Green Shield is active (blocking)

Normal Shield is ready but not being used

Red Shield is on cooldown
