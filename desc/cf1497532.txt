AFK CHUNK LOADER BOT

The Ultimate Survival-Friendly Chunk Loader.

The AFK BOT Mod by @Sycure_Gaming is a lightweight, survival-ready solution for keeping your farms active when you aren't even around all while keeping your world Achievement Friendly.

Crafting & Setup

Crafting an AFK Bot Remote is simple and accessible for early-game survival:

Ingredients - Redstone Torch : 2, Compass : 1

How to Use : Stand at your desired location and interact with the remote. An AFK Bot will spawn on your exact position, ensuring the surrounding chunks remain loaded and active.

Important : Farm Compatibility Please note the distinction between chunk loading and player proximity requirements:

- Works : Iron Farms, Crop Farms, Sugar Cane/Bamboo Farms, and most automated machinery.

- Won't Work : Hostile Mob Farms (Creeper, Zombie, Skeleton, etc.). These farms require a physical player within a specific radius to trigger mob spawning mechanics.

Key Features

Achievement Friendly : Enhance your world without disabling trophies or achievements. Permanent Loading : Keeps chunks active even when you are in another dimension or away from the area.

- Easy Removal: To stop loading an area, simply kill the bot.

- Performance Optimized: Designed to run smoothly without causing significant lag.

Recommendations & Limits

To ensure world stability and optimal performance:

Bot Limit : It is highly recommended to have a maximum of 10 bots active in a single world at one time. Placement : The bot spawns exactly where you are standing; make sure you are in the center of the area you wish to keep loaded.
