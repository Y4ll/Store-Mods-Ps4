MY DISCORD

Vibrant Pack changes the visuals and view of your Minecraft world to be more vibrant and dynamic. This is a texture pack (RP), not a behavior pack (BP).
So it can also be used on Minecraft Bedrock servers or globally, as long as all requirements are met.

This add-on does not require experimental features.
If the add-on does not work, please report it on Discord.
If you want to request an add-on, please make a request on Discord.
If you want to collaborate or work together, please contact me on Discord.
