How to make a Dinosaur

First you need to find a Ground fossil they spawn on any stone related block

then you need a spectrometer

Use the ground fossil on the spectrometer

and boom dino dna!

now use the dna in a crafting table with a normal chicken egg

Now you have yourself a little dinosaur be careful because there's no taming!

if you have a carnivore you must feed it live animals do they get full and don't eat you (Don't worry there's no starving)

some dinosaurs can also climb or step up 2+ blocks depending on their size
