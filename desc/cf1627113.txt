MINERPLUS - 3X3, VEIN MINER & THE EMERALD EXPANSION

ONE PICKAXE BECAME A WHOLE KIT.

MinerPlus has always been about not mining one block at a time. Version 2.5.0
keeps that pickaxe exactly as you know it - and gives it a crew: a helmet that
lights your tunnel, a backpack you actually wear, and a guardian with a pistol
who walks behind you and shoots what tries to kill you.

AT A GLANCE

Emerald Pickaxe 3x3 mining + automatic vein miner, 2031 durability
Miner's Helmet Wearable headlamp, light level 10, toggles on and off
Emerald Backpack 27 slots, custom interface, contents follow the item
Magnet Totem Pulls every dropped item within 20 blocks
Furnace Totem Smelts ore inside your backpack while you walk
Guardian Totem An armed bodyguard with 40 HP and its own storage
Languages English, Francais, Espanol

THE EMERALD PICKAXE

The original, untouched.

- 3x3 area mining. One swing clears a full slice. Tunnels in a fraction of
the time.
- Automatic Vein Miner. Hit one ore, the whole connected vein comes out,
up to 64 blocks.
- 2031 durability, and Unbreaking works exactly as on a vanilla tool. Every
extra block costs one point of wear - powerful, never free.

A 3x3 tunnel being carved, then a large iron vein vanishing in one hit.

EMERALD MINER'S HELMET

Stop placing torches.

Sneak + right-click while holding it to switch the lamp on. It projects light
level 10 around you as you move, and burns one durability point every 10
seconds while lit - light you manage, not an infinite floodlight.

407 durability, 2 armour points, repairable with emeralds.

Pitch-black cave, the lamp switches on, the player walks and the light
travels with them.

EMERALD BACKPACK

27 extra slots, worn on your chest. Sneak + right-click with empty hands to
open it.

No plain menu list here. MinerPlus ships a custom interface built from the
backpack's own texture - the same canvas and leather you see on your back -
with a real grid: your 27 slots, three upgrade sockets, and your full
inventory underneath. One click moves an item either way.

The contents belong to the bag, not to you. Drop it, die with it, trade it,
leave it in a chest - everything inside travels with the item. Hand your
backpack to a friend and they open it to YOUR loot. That is what makes it safe
on servers and Realms.

240 durability, 3 armour points. Wear it out completely and it tears open,
spilling everything at your feet instead of deleting it.

Opening the backpack UI, moving a few stacks in and out. Show the grid
- it is the most striking new thing in the addon.

THE THREE UPGRADES

Three dedicated sockets in the backpack. Each totem has its own slot, and each
works only while the backpack is worn.

Magnet Totem
Every dropped item within 20 blocks flies to you. Vanilla pickup takes over
once they arrive. No more sweeping a cave floor after a creeper.

A scattered pile of drops rushing toward the player.

Furnace Totem
Smelts raw ore sitting in your backpack - one unit every 4 seconds,
entirely on its own. Iron, gold, copper, ancient debris and their raw forms
only; your food and building blocks are never touched. Smoke drifts from
the bag while it works.

THE EMERALD GUARDIAN

Forty hit points, a pistol, and one job.

- Walks beside you and keeps pace. It only teleports if you genuinely
outrun it.
- Fires on any hostile within 16 blocks that it can actually see. It will
not lock onto something behind a wall.
- Shoots while walking. It escorts and covers you at the same time, and
plants its feet to fire when you stop moving.
- 27 slots of its own. Right-click to open its backpack. It picks loot up
off the ground for you.
- Immune to fall and void damage. Following you off a cliff will never
kill it.
- Three-layer gunfire audio that shifts with distance, muzzle flash, and a
health bar above its head.

And it can die. If it falls, its totem is consumed and vanishes from your
backpack - craft another one. The guardian is an investment, not a permanent
buff.
