HitRange Circle Bedrock PvP Indicator

A lightweight resource pack that displays a range circle on the ground around nearby players, helping you visualize melee hit distance in real time.

How it works:

Red circle Enemy is out of your hit range

Green circle Enemy is within hit range, you can land a hit

Features:

Its a texture pack (:

Works on any server or world

See-through subpack option included ( esp )

Compatible with any skin or armor

Notes:

Works on 1.21.130 to 1.26+ any versions
