Transform your Minecraft world into an experience of constant terror and uncertainty! This add-on introduces stalker entities, supernatural events, and an oppressive atmosphere that will completely change the way you explore and survive. Main Features Unique Entities and Stalkers: Add terrifying creatures (such as the Stalker, long-limbed creatures, and entities with disturbing faces) that will actively stalk you from the shadows. On-Screen Messages and Threats: Disturbing floating text ("Hello," "RUN," "Give me your things," "I see you") appears, warning of danger or conveying hostile intentions from the entities. Dynamic Events and Jumpscares: Random events where entities can suddenly appear in front of you, disappear in the blink of an eye, or relentlessly pursue you. Psychological Horror Atmosphere: Integration of disturbing sounds, whispers, distortion effects, and moments where the environment seems to become hostile or stalker-like.
