This pack changes your 2D/Outer layer to 3D, which adds more depth while making your character feel more alive. The pack now has more options to choose from with each being unique. The pack even has a cosmetic option, so everything supports 128x128 skins and with other models in the pack support 128x128.

Compatible

The pack is compatible with Complex Left Hand, just make sure CLH is on top.

Pictures

The pack also works on other skins and other players.

It also works on mobs, entities, and npc that uses your skin.

Issues

The pack uses player.entity.json, so expect some compatible issues.
