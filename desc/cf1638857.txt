Damage Gives OP Items

Turn every hit into a reward with Damage Gives OP Items by FleetMc Studio! Instead of only losing health, every time you take damage you'll instantly receive random Minecraft loot that can help you survive, fight back, and progress faster.

Whether the damage comes from mobs, fall damage, lava, fire, explosions, or other survival hazards, every hit has the chance to reward you with useful resources, powerful equipment, and valuable treasures. Sometimes getting hurt is actually a good thing!

What Can Drop?

- Max Enchanted Armor.

- Powerful Weapons.

- Enchanted Tools.

- Totems of Undying.

- Elytra.

- Shulker Boxes.

- Diamonds & Diamond Blocks.

- Netherite Items.

- Rare Building Blocks.

- Useful Survival Resources.

- Food and Valuable Items.

- ...and hundreds of other random Minecraft items!

Features

- Receive random loot every time you take damage.

- Works with all normal damage sources.

- Huge variety of possible rewards.

- Receive both common and extremely overpowered items.

- Perfect for Survival challenges and lucky worlds.

- Lightweight and optimized.

- Achievement Friendly.

- No Experimental Gameplay required.

How to Use

- Download and import the addon.

- Enable the Behavior Pack.

- Join your world.

- Take damage from any source.

- Collect your random rewards!

Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

- Achievement Friendly.

- No Experimental Gameplay Required.

- Singleplayer & Multiplayer Supported.
