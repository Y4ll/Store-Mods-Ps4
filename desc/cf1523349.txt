WDP-4D Train Mod (MCPE)

WDP-4D is a train addon for Minecraft Pocket Edition that brings realistic rail-based gameplay to your world. Travel faster, transport in style, and give your builds a whole new level of immersion with fully functional trains.

Whether you're creating a modern city, a metro system, or long-distance railways, WDP4D lets you build and ride like never before.

Requirements

- Requires MTC Addon to function

- Make sure it is installed and activated before using WDP-4D

Features

- Functional trains you can use in your world

- Smooth movement designed for MCPE

- Great for city builds, rail networks, and roleplay

- Adds a realistic transport system to your gameplay

Installation (MCPE)

- Install the MTC Addon

- Import the WDP4D addon file (.mcaddon / .mcpack)

- Enable both packs in your world settings

- Start your world and enjoy your trains!

Important

- This addon will NOT work without MTC Addon

- Make sure both Behavior Pack and Resource Pack are enabled

- Compatible with latest versions of Minecraft PE
