What Is Altar SMP? Altar SMP adds powerful Legendary Weapons that can only be crafted using special Altars placed around the world by admins. How Altars Work: Admins place Altars throughout the world. Each altar contains custom recipes for Legendary Weapons. Crafting requires the correct materials. Every legendary weapon has a unique ability set. Use the /altarsmphelp command ingame for more information.
