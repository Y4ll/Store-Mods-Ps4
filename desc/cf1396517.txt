Project Description:

Dynamic Light allows you to hold an Illuminating item or block in your off hand as in Hytale leaving your main hand free to hold more important items like a Sword, illuminating the path as you walk. No extra steps are required, just place an Illuminating item or block in either hand and play as usual.

Important Information:

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Lightweight script.

- Dynamic Light features.

- Sneak to switch system.

- Have something to say? Join the Discord.

Any item or block that looks like it should give light will now illuminate around you when held in either hand.

Any block or entity that is on fire will now illuminate until the fire goes out.

Any entity that looks like it should give light will now illuminate as they move around.

Any item that give light in your hands will also give light when placed on the floor.
