Creeper Request is a Minecraft Bedrock behavior pack that brings consent to chaos before any creeper can explode, it must first politely ask for permission.

We've all been there. You spend hours building your dream base, mining deep into a cave, or simply enjoying a peaceful sunset and then boom. A creeper you never even saw coming reduces it all to a crater. No warning, no mercy, no apology. Just rubble and regret.

When a creeper gets close enough to a player, it freezes in place, turns to face them, and opens a dialog: "Can I boom?" The player then has a choice:

- Go ahead the creeper unfreezes and detonates normally (or with double the power if charged)

- Not today the creeper lets out a defeated poof of smoke and vanishes without a trace

Charged creepers are flagged clearly in the prompt, so you always know exactly what you're consenting to. A cooldown system prevents the same creeper from spamming the dialog, and a gentle cat purr plays when you spare one.

Perfect for servers, minigames, or anyone who has ever lost a hours worth of progress to a creeper that had absolutely no business being there.

Requires: Minecraft Bedrock 1.20.0+
