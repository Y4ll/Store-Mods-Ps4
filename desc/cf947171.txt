Chisel:

The Chisel is a tool that converts natural blocks or blocks used in construction into their brick variants. Once transformed, a natural block cannot revert to its natural form but can be converted into its crafted variants.

Paint Brush:

The Paint Brush! A tool for coloring blocks without having to do it manually. Use your imagination! The paint will run out, but the brush won't break, so just keep your paint pot full and get painting! HAHAHA
