One Shot Weapon

Turn every fight into a high-risk, high-reward battle with One Shot Weapon. This addon brings a collection of powerful custom guns designed for fast combat, strong hits, and satisfying weapon gameplay.

Each weapon has its own Minecraft-style 3D design while still feeling clean and recognizable. The focus is on powerful shots, quick reactions, and choosing the right weapon for every situation.

Features

Powerful custom weapons
Detailed Minecraft-style 3D gun models
Different weapon designs and colors
Fast and intense combat gameplay
Great for PvP and multiplayer
Perfect for custom maps and survival worlds
Simple but stylish weapon designs

Whether you're fighting friends in PvP, defending yourself in survival, or building your own combat map, One Shot Weapon gives every battle a more exciting and dangerous feeling.

Aim carefully. Make the shot count. One hit can change everything.
