Dynamic Crosshair is a resource pack for Minecraft Bedrock to hide or change your crosshair depending on the context.

Showcase

Features

- Hides the crosshair when not looking at anything and not holding any items.

- The crosshair will change or hide depending on the context you are looking at.

- Change the crosshair style for various situations.

- highly compatible with other packs.

Reference: https://modrinth.com/mod/dynamiccrosshair
