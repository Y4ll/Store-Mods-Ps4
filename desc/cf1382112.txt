Do you want Kaijus roam on your Minecraft world? well Godzilla Legacy of Titans is a Titan/Kaiju focused Minecraft Bedrock add-on for you! Encounter various Titans like Godzilla, Kong, Mothra, King Ghidorah and many more came from different Monsterverse Movies. Each Titans have different attacks, behaviors, and Features like using Blue Dye to Evolved Godzilla will make him turn into a new Godzilla Variant. Not only that, you can control them using "End Crystal" so you can cause destruction on your world and fight against other Titans.

Features

- Some Low ranked Titans will submit to Godzilla, once submitted will turn into peaceful titans, However a complete opposite when Titans submit to Ghidorah.

- Evolved Godzilla and King Kong will stop fighting when Mothra starts to roar or defeating Skar King.

- You can control each Titan using "End Crystal".

- Skar King can command Red apes & Shimo to attack.

- Skar King can make the great apes chant of bow.

- Mothra's death essence can revive Godzilla.

- Thermonuclear Godzilla will turn into normal state once Ghidorah is defeated.

- Kong's alpha roar will turn Suko into a mighty ape.

- Abaddon can command spiders to attack.

- You can Free Shimo once defeating Skar King.

- Godzilla will turn into Supercharged once absorbing "Blue Radioactive Source", And Supercharged Godzilla will turn into Evolved Godzilla once Absorbing "Purple Radioactive Source". (Long press to activate the item and be sure to activate it close to Godzilla!)

- You can check the titan stats using the "Monarch Titan Stats Info" Item. (Long press to activate the item)

- You can trigger the super charge mode on H.E.A.V using lever to revive Kong. (Trigger the supercharged mode on HEAV using lever)

- You can use the "Project Powerhouse" item to revive King Kong. (Long press to activate the item and call the BEAST Glove)

- Goodluck finding some hidden features!
