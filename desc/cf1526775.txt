NoierUi - Noier_x7

A visual and thematic texture pack for your Minecraft game, it changes the entire interface of the start screen and loading screens. It also adds a dark mode for better viewing.

Modified

- start_screen

- proguess_screen

Compatibility

- It works perfectly on mobile devices. / It may exhibit visual bugs on tablets.

- It may display a very small interface on a PC.
