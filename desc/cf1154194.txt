RPG Damage Indicator Addon Minecraft Bedrock 1.21

Transform your Minecraft Bedrock experience with the RPG Damage Indicator Addon! All mobs and enemies now display a health bar in true RPG style. This addon is fully compatible with custom mobs and other addons, ensuring seamless integration with your creations.

Key Features:

- Dynamic Health Bars: Easily monitor enemy health with visible bars that adapt to their remaining HP.

- Color-Coded Health Levels:

- Green: High health.

- Yellow: Moderate health.

- Red: Low health.

- Night Visibility: Health bars remain visible even in the darkest environments, perfect for nighttime adventures.
