This addon is NOT suitable for low or lower-medium range mobile devices, as it incorporates a large number of elements that may cause lag or FPS drops on devices with limited resources. It is recommended to use it on mid-high range, high-end devices, or on PC for optimal performance.

I changed the name of the addon from "expansive nature" to "expansion of nature" because the previous name was very similar to another addon.

Expancion of Nature Rediscover Nature in Minecraft Bedrock

Are you ready to transform your Minecraft world? Expancion of nature immerses you in a renewed natural environment, bringing a wide variety of plants and flowers that beautify and energize every biome. Dive into vibrant, lively landscapes where every corner holds a special touch.

In order to obtain some of the plants, you will need to use a tool that has the silk touch enchantment on it.

- In this version 1.3 of the mod, more plants were added, some changes were made to the display of some existing plants, 3 insects and a reptile were added, and now some plants can be put in pots.

- Furthermore, snow no longer accumulates on the plants, and most plants can now be placed in water.

- Now some plants adapt to the color of the vegetation in the biome

- The giant trees were temporarily removed because they have glitches when generated on the terrain.
