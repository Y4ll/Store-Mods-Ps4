Villager Trades Are Super OP!

Transform every village into the ultimate trading hub with Villager Trades Are Super OP! by FleetMc Studio. This addon upgrades the trades of every vanilla villager, allowing you to obtain powerful equipment, enchanted gear, rare resources, food, blocks, and other valuable items for incredibly affordable prices.

Instead of spending hours grinding for resources or waiting for perfect villager trades, simply visit your nearest village and start trading. Every profession has been upgraded with much better rewards, making progression faster and far more enjoyable.
