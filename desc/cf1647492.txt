Project Description:

Transform combat feedback with real-time, dynamic floating numbers directly above affected mobs and players. Every strike is visualized, removing the guesswork from battles and boss fights.

Important Information:

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Real time damage indicator.

- Color indicator for damage values.

- Behaviour & Resource packs are both required for the project to work.

- Having something to say? Join the Discord.

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

Now you will always know precisely how much damage you are dealing to any entityincluding custom mobs from other add-ons and boss fights. Whether you are testing high-tier weapons against vanilla zombies or seeing how your gear holds up against heavily modded custom bosses, every hit is tracked instantly with crystal-clear visual feedback. No more guessing if your custom enchantments or stat tweaks are actually making a difference.
