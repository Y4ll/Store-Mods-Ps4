The "Dynamic Lighting" addon for Minecraft Bedrock brings a dynamic and immersive feature: when you hold a torch in your left hand, the path around you is automatically illuminated. This system improves the gaming experience by allowing you to explore caves or dark environments without having to constantly place torches, providing practicality and a more realistic look.
