YGP! Minecraft But Leaves Give OP Items

Every leaf could hide something insane.

YGP! Minecraft But Leaves Give OP Items transforms supported Minecraft Bedrock leaves into a randomized reward and adventure system.

Break a supported leaf and receive exactly one outcome from valuable loot and YGP equipment to treasure, enemies, traps, teleport events, adventures, the YGP Immortal Dog, and rare special events.

One leaf. One outcome.

Created by YGP Studio / YGP Minecraft.

ONE LEAF = ONE OUTCOME

Every supported leaf break rolls one controlled outcome.

TREASURE SYSTEM

Treasure is divided into four tiers:

Rare more valuable resources and equipment.

Epic powerful end-game rewards.

Legendary extremely valuable and rare discoveries.

Possible treasure includes diamonds, emeralds, diamond blocks, netherite, ancient debris, nether stars, beacons, Elytra, tridents, enchanted golden apples, End items and more.

YGP IMMORTAL DOG

A rare 2% leaf outcome can trigger the YGP Immortal Dog.

The integrated system provides:

Player-specific ownership

Multiplayer-safe ownership

No separate taming for the leaf event

YGP Immortal Dog integration

The dog is associated with the player who triggered the event.

FOREST GUARDIAN

A rare leaf event can summon a Forest Guardian.

The current implementation uses a named husk guardian with additional effects.

Defeating it can provide a reward to the player who lands the final hit.

ENEMY ENCOUNTERS
