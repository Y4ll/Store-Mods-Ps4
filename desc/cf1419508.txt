Particles Core is a lightweight, highly configurable texture pack that gives you full control over Minecrafts particle system.
Designed for players who want cleaner visuals, better performance, or custom ambiencewithout breaking the vanilla feel.

Key Features

- Individual particle toggles enable or disable particles one by one

- Clean & consistent visuals that blend seamlessly with vanilla

- Performance-friendly for low to high-end devices

- Enhances combat feedback, ambience, redstone, magic, and world interactions

- Built to work alongside other packs with minimal conflicts

Customization

Particles Core includes an in-game configuration menu, allowing you to fine-tune each particle according to your preferencefrom subtle ambience to a fully minimal experience.

Ideal For

- Performance-focused players

- PvP & competitive gameplay

- Clean UI / visual overhaul setups

Enjoy a smoother, more controlled particle experience. **Build your world, your way.**
