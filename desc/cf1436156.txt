Rain destruction from the sky with the ultimate "God Rod"!

This addon transforms the humble Fishing Rod into a devastating Orbital Strike Cannon. Inspired by the technical "Stab Shot" cannons seen in Wembu's videos, this tool uses TNT Minecarts for instant, lag-friendly, and massive terrain destruction.

Features

Instant Detonation: Uses Tnt that explode on impactno waiting for fuses!

2 Destructive Modes:

DRILL MODE: Fires a stream of TNT straight down to bore a hole to bedrock (Wembu Style).

Nuke mode : fires tnt like the wembu does. This may cause lots of lag.

Water Breaker: Automatically clears water at the target zone so explosions actually destroy blocks.

Multi-Name Support: Works with names like "Orbital Canon", "God Rod", "Space Laser", and more.

How to Use

Grab a normal Fishing Rod.

Rename it in an Anvil to "Orbital Canon" (or "OC", "God Rod", "Strike").

Right-Click (Use Item) to fire at a target.

Sneak + Right-Click to switch between Drill and Scatter modes.

Or just rename nuke for nuke

Stab or stabshot for stabshot

Warning

This addon is extremely destructive. Do not use near your base.
