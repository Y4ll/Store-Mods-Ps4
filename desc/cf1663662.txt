BMW M3 GTR (E46)
Bring one of the most iconic racing legends directly into your game! The BMW M3 GTR (E46) is famous for its dominant performance in the American Le Mans Series (ALMS) and its legendary status in automotive and gaming culture. Equipped with a high-revving V8 engine and aggressive race-bred aerodynamics, this car is built for speed and precision.

Real-Life Overview
The real-world BMW M3 GTR E46 was created in 2001 specifically to compete in the GT class of the American Le Mans Series. Unlike the standard road-going M3s, which used an inline-six engine, the GTR featured a custom 4.0-liter P60B40 V8 engine producing over 440 horsepower. Combined with an ultra-lightweight carbon fiber body and track-tuned suspension, it dominated the racing circuit and etched its name into motor racing history.
