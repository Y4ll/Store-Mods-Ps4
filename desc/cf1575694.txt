About

Miner's Helmet adds the classic Terraria utility headwear to Minecraft Bedrock.

This item supports two ways of being equipped:

- As Armor: Wearing it in the helmet slot renders a custom 3D model of a miner's hard hat on your head and emits a dynamic light beam.

- As an Accessory: Equip it through the Accessory Pouch to gain the dynamic light beam without using your head armor slot or showing the model.

Features

- Adds the Miner's Helmet item

- Beautiful custom 3D model when worn in the helmet slot

- Dynamic, smooth light beam emitting in the direction you look (acts like a headlamp)

- Supports equipping through the Accessory Pouch

- Only replaces air/cave-air blocks (liquids and blocks are perfectly safe)

- Automatic cleanup of light blocks on logout, dimension change, or death

- Inspired by Terraria's accessory system

How to use

Craft the Miner's Helmet at a crafting table. Equip it directly as a helmet to display the model, or open your Accessory Pouch and place it there to use it as a silent helper.

Open the Accessory Pouch with the pouch item or /accessories. For the optional reactive menu, use /accessories-ddui.
