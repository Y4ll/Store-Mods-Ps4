Immersive Rain + Snow

Immersive Rain + Snow enhances Minecraft Bedrock's weather with more realistic rain and snow effects while keeping a clean, vanilla-friendly look.

Designed to feel natural, subtle, and consistent with Minecraft's art style.

Features

Realistic Rain Effects

- Enhanced rain splash particles with animated ripple rings on impact

- Wet rain puddles that darken the ground realistic water pooling effect

- Wind-driven rain drops that fall at natural angles, not straight down

- Layered splash system every raindrop spawns both a splash burst and a spreading ripple

Immersive Wind

- Thin wind streaks that drift through the air during storms

- Bidirectional wind movement with natural drag and deceleration

- Wisps spawn randomly around the player, so gusts feel unpredictable instead of looping

Improved Snow Atmosphere

- Detailed snowflake textures with 4 unique crystal variants

- Gentle floating motion with realistic wind drift and flutter

- Soft gravity and gust physics snowflakes tumble, not just fall

- Smaller, natural-scale flakes that blend with the environment

Balanced Weather Sounds

- Improved rain ambience that feels immersive without being overpowering

- Optional sound subpack choose between custom or vanilla rain audio

Carefully Tuned Particles

- Every particle is fine-tuned for size, lifetime, speed, and transparency

- Puddles use ground-darkening blend (not white overlays) for a true wet look

- Flipbook sprite animations for smooth, natural particle transitions

- Vibrant Visuals compatible with full texture set + MERS support

Compatibility

- Resource Pack Only no Behavior Pack, no commands needed

- Works in Singleplayer, Multiplayer & Realms

- Compatible with Vibrant Visuals

- Safe to use with shaders and other visual packs

- Performance friendly optimized particle counts and lifetime

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it Minecraft will import the pack automatically

- In your world settings, go to Resource Packs My Packs and activate Immersive Rain + Snow

- Pick your rain audio in the subpack slider: Vanilla Rain Sound or Custom Rain Sound
