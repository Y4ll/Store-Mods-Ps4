Now compatible with version 1.21.51. Are you tired of having lag and want to increase the fps of your game for a greater experience? Don't worry because I already have the request for you. After a great analysis and study of Minecraft Bedrock related to its performance, I have discovered which sections of the game consume the most resources on our devices, thanks to all this I have developed a kind of optimization tool. Below will be more details of my studies with explanations for those users who are interested in this.

Changes

- Performance has been improved.

- Now compatible with version 1.21.51+

- Fixed the bug with clouds as some of them had errors with them.

- Improved the appearance in the new pale garden biome.

Screenshots

A brief explanation so that you understand the context better, the lag is mainly produced by the particles and some textures of the default game, with this in mind I made important changes to optimize your game.
