About

No Fog FPS Booster clears distance, weather, and underwater fog, with lighter particles and calmer animated textures for low-end devices.

It uses vanilla textures while reducing particle density, motion, and visual noise.

Features

- Fog Removal: Distance, weather, and water fog are pushed to the edge of the render distance across Overworld, Nether, and End dimensions.

- Particle Optimization: Reduced visual noise and lighter overrides for performance-heavy particles (rain splash, water splash/wake, smoke, flame, portal, explosion, and bubble column particles).

- Calmer Fluid Animations: Slower texture animations for water, lava, fire, portals, sea lanterns, and kelp, reducing animation activity.

- Performance Focus: Lighter particle effects and slower animations may help on low-end devices; FPS gains vary by device and scene.

How to use

- Import the .mcaddon file into Minecraft Bedrock.

- Activate the No Fog FPS Booster resource pack for your world. No behavior pack or experiments are required.

- Put the No Fog resource pack above other visual packs to ensure custom fog settings and particles load correctly.

Notes

- This is not an RTX shader.

- Performance gains depend on your device, world complexity, active entities, and render distance.

- Air, weather, and underwater fog remain only at the render-distance boundary. Lava fog is retained.

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.50 or newer

- No external dependency required
