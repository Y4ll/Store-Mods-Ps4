Requirements

How to Enter

When the addon is active, you will receive a Sculk Block.

Build a 44 portal using Sculk Blocks, just like a Nether Portal.

Hold Flint and Steel and click the bottom portal block to activate it.

Beautiful Sky (Old Version Look)

(New Version Look)

Want a truly dark experience?

Disable Beautiful Sky to hide the bright sky and experience the full darkness of the Darken Dimension.

Dimension environments

Natural Structures

Quest Hunt

You will receive a Quest Book.

Complete the quests by collecting the required items and progress through the Darken Dimension.

Natural Mob Spawn

Darken Pigs and Darken Frogs can naturally spawn when new chunks generate.

Be careful while exploring.

Powerful Armor

Craft two powerful armor sets:

- Darken Pig Armor

- Darken Diamond Armor

Boss Altar

The Darken Dimension contains 2 powerful bosses:

- Demonic VEX

- Darken Count

Craft their specific Boss Altar, place it in the dimension, and wait a few seconds for the boss to spawn.

Home Key

The Home Key is your way out.

Complete all quests and defeat the Darken Count to obtain the key. Use it to escape the Darken Dimension without dying.

Can You Escape?

Complete the quests, defeat both bosses, collect the Home Key, and survive the darkness.
