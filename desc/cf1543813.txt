Newb x Dawn is my favorite shader of all time, but like a lot of other players, I can't actually use it because we need alternative tools like materia.bin loader to load it, which not all people can use, just like me.

Since then, I have been trying to make a graphic pack that gives similar visuals of newb x dawn, and after months of work, with helps from my friend, I am happy to introduce to you:

Newb x Dawn Recreate

There are totally 3 skies of different time

- Sunset

- Night

- Day

- Glowing Ores Recreation (Visuals)

- Water Recreation

Featured:

- NO patch/external loader needed for this to work.

- Day, Sunset and Night sky visual of Newb x Dawn

- Glowing Ores & Blocks recreated

- Clean Fog that gives shader atmosphere

- Saturated Blocks (Subpacks)

- Has good performance on low-end devices

- Support iOS, Android and Windows, and any device that can use texture packs

Notes:

- There are 9 subpacks for only sky & fog, sky with saturated blocks (Glowing blocks are included) and sky with glowing blocks

- Glowing Blocks are not actually glowing, it's just illusion trick to make it looks like glowing

- The Day, Sunset and Night sky are not animated, they are fixed cubemap and cannot change time. Day time is recommended to give the best visuals for all sky

- Put this pack on top of all pack is recommended
