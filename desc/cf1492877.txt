What is This Addon?

Files Ultimine brings Ultimine-style mining mechanics to Bedrock Edition.

Instead of breaking blocks one by one, this addon lets you mine entire groups at once whether it's ores, tunnels, or full mining patterns.

Perfect for survival, grinding, or just making mining feel insanely satisfying.

How to Use

- Sneak Un-sneak Sneak again

- Ultimine activates

- Switch hotbar slots to change mining modes

- Look at a block to preview the area

- Break once... and everything goes

Tip: Different hotbar slots = different mining shapes

Mining Modes

- Shapeless (Vein Mining)

- Small Tunnel

- 3x3 Mining

- Large Tunnel (3x3)
