Better Connected Glass

Tired of glass blocks looking like a grid of segmented panes? Better Connected Glass merges adjacent glass blocks into smooth, seamless surfaces giving your builds clean glass walls, greenhouses, and windows without the ugly grid lines.

Two Modes, Your Choice

- Borderless Glass removes the connecting borders for a sleek, seamless look while keeping the glass's natural texture detail.

- Clear & Borderless Glass goes a step further with fully clear, crystal-like glass plus the seamless borderless connection, for maximum transparency and visibility.

Switch between the two anytime from the pack's subpack selector in-game no need to download separate packs.

Achievement Friendly
This is a pure resource pack with no behavior pack, Script API, or experimental toggles so it won't disable achievements. Drop it in and keep earning your trophies.

Includes

- All standard glass blocks and glass panes

- Full color variant support (all 16 dyed glass/pane colors)

- Tinted glass support

Compatibility
Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80+
