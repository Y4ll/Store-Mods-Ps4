Ore Vein Miner

Crouch + break an ore to vein mine connected ore blocks

Now supports Silk touch and Fortune

Installation

- Double-click the file, or move it manually into your Minecraft

- Open your world settings Behavior Packs Enable Ore Vein Miner

UI Menu

- Mobile: Sneak and hold while holding gunpowder

- PC: Sneak and right-click while holding gunpowder
