Description:

Are you looking for an add-on that allow you have to stack your totems of undying without lost slot in your inventory? You are in the correct place. :3

Showcase

How works

Stacking

only interact anywhere with a totem of undying, select "stack more totems" and you can stack all totems in your inventory (No detect totems in your offhand).

Unstacking

Only interact anywhere, select "Unstack totem", after you choose what amount you want Unstack and spawn a totem ahead you. (Please, no make this action in top of the dimension, or you lost your totem unstacked).
