Pokesnacks Addon (SERP PokeDrock Complement) is an unofficial addon designed to enhance your adventure by adding a brand new system of special snacks inspired by Pokemon types.

This addon introduces 18 unique Poke Snacks, each one representing a different type, giving you new ways to interact with your world and improve your gameplay experience.

Features

18 Poke Snacks (one for each type)

Each snack has unique effects

Custom mechanics for better gameplay

Designed to support your adventure and progression

Compatible with SERP PokeDrock

How it works

Craft and use different Poke Snacks to gain special effects that can help you in battles, exploration, and survival. Each type offers a different advantage, so choose wisely depending on your situation.

All items are crafted using the addons custom crafting table called the Molcajete, which is required to create every Poke Snack.

News

- Added crafting recipes for the Enchanted Golden Apple and Honeycomb (both are crafted using the Molcajete).

-

Introduced a new mechanic for Serp Pokelitos. Placing Pokelitos into a Basket allows them to spawn Pokemon. 1 Pokelito = 1 Pokemon.

Pokelito Spawn Types

- pokelito_citrus Grass, Fairy, Bug, and Flying

- pokelito_mint Water, Ice, Electric, and Normal

- pokelito_mocha Fire, Rock, Ground, and Dark

- pokelito_spice Fighting, Poison, Ghost, and Dragon

- pokelito_sweet Psychic and Steel

Multiple Pokelitos can be placed in the Basket (up to 10), and the Basket can be refilled as needed.

- Fixed various bugs and minor issues.
