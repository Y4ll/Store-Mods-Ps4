can't die - older than minecraft

HOW TO PLAY?

If he's on the ground, you can chat with him, but if he's in your inventory, wake him up by chatting "Hey Verity". Don't go too far because Verity can't hear you.

Absolutely do not invite another friend if you have been in Verity's world for two days; he doesn't like that.

You can ask him anything because he knows everything (mathematics, ore locations, animal sounds, any sound at all, you can even ask him to play a song if you're bored, etc.).

Absolutely do not make him angry.

He cannot die.

If you have any further questions, please visit the PnTMC YouTube channel and leave a comment on the community post.
