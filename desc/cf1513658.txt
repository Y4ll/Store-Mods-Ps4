APOCALIPTYC ILLAGERS - Survive the Apocalypse in Style
Take your Minecraft world to the next level with a dark, technical, and unique aesthetic! This texture pack has been specifically designed for fans of extreme survival and apocalyptic environments, focusing on enhancing the atmosphere without sacrificing performance.

What makes this pack different?

This project isn't just a texture swap; it's a complete visual overhaul that utilizes a high-contrast design style with defined lines (Sticker Style). This allows objects and characters to stand out even in the darkest nights of the apocalypse, giving them a depth you won't find in the base game.

Key Features:
Unique Illager Designs: The Illagers have been completely redesigned to fit into a devastated world. Prepare to face enemies that truly command respect.

High Contrast Aesthetics: Inspired by the style of high-quality stickers, each texture features defined edges that enhance visibility in fast-paced combat.

Optimized for Realms: Tested and tuned to run smoothly on servers and Realms, reducing unnecessary visual load to maintain stable FPS on consoles and mobile devices.

Immersive Atmosphere: Ideal for content series like The Walking Craft Zero or zombie adventure maps.

Installation Instructions
Download the .mcpack file from the "Download" button here on CurseForge.

Run the file to automatically import it into Minecraft Bedrock.

In your world or Realm settings, go to Resource Packs and enable APOCALIPTYC ILLAGERS.

(Optional) For a superior visual experience, it is recommended to use it with dynamic lighting shaders.

This pack is constantly being updated. If you find any bugs or have a suggestion for a new mob design, please leave it in the comments! Your feedback helps this project continue to grow.
