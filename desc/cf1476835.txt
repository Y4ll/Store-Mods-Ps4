Immersive Ambient Sounds

Bring your Minecraft world to life with a fully dynamic, biomeaware soundscape.

Immersive Ambient Sounds enhances your gameplay with rich environmental audio that adapts to your biome, elevation, and time of day.
Forests feel alive with birds and deer, deserts echo with distant coyotes, jungles vibrate with exotic wildlife, and caves surround you with deep, echoing ambience.
Every biome becomes a unique auditory experience.

Key Features

Day & Night Soundscapes Distinct ambience for daytime and nighttime, creating natural atmospheric shifts.

BiomeSpecific Ambience Forests, deserts, jungles, swamps, oceans, snowy regions, mountains, and more.

Cave Ambience Below Y=40, ambience transitions to echoing drips, rumbling stone, and underground resonance.

Smart Detection System Automatically identifies your biome and environment to play the correct ambience seamlessly.

Supported Biomes

Plains, Forest, Desert, Savanna, Jungle, Swamp, Beach, Ocean, Taiga, Snowy biomes, Mountains, Mesa, Nether, End, and related variants.

Installation

Install both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack.
Once you enter your world, the addon automatically begins playing ambient sounds based on your biome and the time of day.

Compatibility

Compatible with Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.60+.
Works alongside most addons, resource packs, and sound packs.
