Inspired by the viral Invincible Wobbly Edit meme trend, this mod brings a humorous and dynamic twist to combat in Minecraft. Whenever an entity gets hit, it triggers a smooth wobble animation-mimicking the iconic back-and-forth motion seen in edits of Mark Grayson (Invincible).

The meme originated from tweened animations of Invincible and gained massive popularity in late 2025, resurging again in early 2026 alongside the release of Season 4. This mod captures that same chaotic and funny energy, making every hit feel expressive and alive.
