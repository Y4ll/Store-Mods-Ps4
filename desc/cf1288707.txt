An QoL Texture Pack that turn Item frames & Glow frames invisible, useful for large World / Map decorations.

Works on 1.16-1.26+, supports Vibrant Visuals, Actions & Stuff and RTX.

Includes 3 subpacks:

- Invisible Item Frames (Default & Glow)

- Invisible Item Frame

- Invisible Glow Frame

Requires:

- PandaMine's Mip Mapper
