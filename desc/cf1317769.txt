The Wither Equipment by Tiffer5999: This Addon adds a upgrade of netherite tools and armor thats much stronger

The Wither tools and armor are crafted with a Netherite tool/armor piece and a Wither upgrade template in a Crafting table

To get the Wither Upgrade template you have to craft it with a Netherite upgrade template and a Nether star

And don't worry, you can duplicate the Wither upgrade template with the Wither upgrade template, 7 diamonds, and a Wither skull
