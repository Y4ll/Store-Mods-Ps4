Friendly Mobs

by Gavti

Friendly Mobs turns every hostile and neutral mob in the game peaceful. Zombies, skeletons, creepers, endermen, the Warden, wolves none of them will ever attack you, even after you hit them first. Bosses (Wither, Ender Dragon) are intentionally left alone so boss fights still work.

Where you can use this pack

- Zoos & wildlife parks house real mobs safely, visitors can walk right up to them

- Museums & exhibits display dangerous mobs like the Warden or Wither Skeleton with zero risk

- Machinima & cinematic builds film scenes with mobs as extras, no combat interruptions

- Screenshots & renders get close-up shots of mobs for thumbnails and artwork

- Relaxed / kid-friendly worlds explore without combat stress

- Adventure & roleplay maps use mobs purely as scenery or NPCs, not threats

Mobs covered (40)

Overworld: Zombie, Husk, Drowned, Zombie Villager, Skeleton, Stray, Bogged, Spider, Cave Spider, Creeper, Witch, Slime, Enderman, Silverfish, Endermite, Pillager, Vindicator, Evoker, Ravager, Vex, Phantom, Guardian, Elder Guardian, Warden, Shulker

Nether: Zombie Pigman, Wither Skeleton, Blaze, Ghast, Magma Cube, Hoglin, Piglin, Piglin Brute, Breeze

Neutral (normally turn hostile if hit): Wolf, Bee, Polar Bear, Llama, Iron Golem, Goat
