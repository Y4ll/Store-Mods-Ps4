The Sapphire Addon

Overview

Dive into a gemstone-powered gameplay loop built around Sapphire.
Mine the ore, craft advanced gear, summon a companion, and get utility that changes how you fight and mine.

What you get

- New Sapphire Ore chain with custom drop behavior

- Sapphire Toolset: sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, and a 3x3 hammer

- Sapphire Chestplate with advanced combat reactions

- Sapphire Amulet ritual item

- Blokkit summon companion with passive/powerful support mechanics

Combat

- Sapphire chestplate features projectile-interaction logic with cooldown control.

- Sapphire tools provide higher-tier utility and custom behavior through gameplay scripting.

Mining & Progressionn

- Added mining identity around Sapphire materials and their crafting uses.

- Includes custom drop/repair/buff behavior for deeper progression than vanilla-only play.
