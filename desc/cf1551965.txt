In-Game Settings

Command:

/mop

Supports English and Thai

Operators can adjust server-wide options Drop Merging, Diagonal Mining, Durability Loss, and Max Blocks Per Vein or disable the addon entirely.

Every player can toggle Require Sneaking to suit their playstyle.

Add-on Compatibility

Works with custom pickaxes and ores from other add-ons
even across different add-ons, so a pickaxe from one add-on works correctly with ores from another, including tier requirements.

(Requires that each add-on defines its items correctly)
