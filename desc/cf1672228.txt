Super Duper PvP is a resource pack designed for Minecraft Bedrock Edition, built for competitive PvP servers and custom maps.

Key features:

- Custom SuperPvP title screen and logo

- Enhanced night skybox with dense stars and atmospheric effects

- Immersive medieval-style hub and castle environments

- Optimized visuals for PvP arenas, shops, and outdoor areas

- Clean, high-contrast look suitable for fast-paced combat

Perfect for players who want a sharp, competitive atmosphere with a strong SuperPvP identity.
