Night Vision Plus is a pure resource pack designed to make dark environments completely readable without touching gameplay mechanics. It uses a fullbright lighting preset to push ambient cave and night brightness to its maximum, so torches, glowstone, and sea lanterns are optional rather than essential. Cave fog the pitch-black obscuring effect that limits vision underground is removed entirely, giving clear long-distance sight lines through any mine or cavern. The vignette overlay is also disabled for a wider, cleaner view.

When Vibrant Visuals is active in Minecraft Video Settings, the fullbright lighting preset reaches its full effect and caves become as bright as the open surface. Without Vibrant Visuals the pack still provides fog removal and vignette clearing.

Night Vision Plus contains no behavior pack, JavaScript, commands, functions, or gameplay scripting. It does not intentionally disable achievements.

Features
Fullbright Lighting: Ambient cave and night brightness maximised no more dark corners.
Clear Fog: Pitch-black underground fog removed for clear long-distance visibility.
No Vignette: Dark screen edges removed for a wider, cleaner view.
Achievement Safe: Pure resource pack no scripts, commands, or behavior pack.
Global Resource: Apply once in main menu settings to use on every world, Realm, and server.
Ultra Lightweight: Tiny file size absolute zero performance impact.
Stack Friendly: Combines cleanly with Xray Plus, Glowing Ores, and any other resource pack.
Requirements
Enable Vibrant Visuals in Minecraft Video Settings for the fullbright lighting effect.

Installation
Download and double-click Night Vision Plus.mcpack.
Go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Night Vision Plus, or add it in your world settings.
(Recommended) Enable Vibrant Visuals in Video Settings for the full fullbright effect.
Enter any cave or dark area it will be clearly lit.
