The Minecraft Custom Punch Addon introduces two unique punches: the Water Punch and the Fire Punch. Players can acquire these punches by crafting special items called the Book of Flame and the Book of Water.

To craft the Book of Flame, players need to venture into the Nether and collect lava in a bucket. They then place the lava bucket in a crafting table to convert it into the Book of Flame.

Similarly, to craft the Book of Water, players must place a bucket of water in the center of a crafting table.

Once players have obtained these books, they can use them to craft the respective punches in-game. This adds an exciting new element to gameplay, allowing players to wield elemental powers and enhance their Minecraft experience.
