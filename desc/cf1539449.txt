Hidden Helmet

Keep your armor protection while showing your Minecraft skin clearly.

Features

- Hides the equipped helmet visually

- Helmet remains equipped and still provides protection

- Works perfectly with armor sets like Diamond and Netherite

- Lets your character's head and skin stay visible

- Lightweight resource pack with no unnecessary features

- Great for survival, PvP, screenshots and multiplayer

How It Works

Simply activate the pack and wear your helmet normally. The helmet becomes invisible on your character while keeping the equipped armor slot unchanged.
