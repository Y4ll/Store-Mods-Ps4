Sit On Player [1.1.2]

Sit on Player is an unofficial Minecraft Bedrock Edition port of the popular Java mod Player Ladder by ForwarDNerN.

It allows players to sit on top of each other, forming fun and chaotic player towers perfect for multiplayer

Created with full permission from the original mod author

How to Use

To ride another player:

- Make sure the player you want to ride is doesn't sneaking

- Use right-click (mouse) or tap the Use button (mobile)

To stack more players:

- Each new rider must interact with the topmost player in the stack

- You can keep stacking as high as you can!

To dismount or drop riders:

- Any player who is being ridden can sneak/crouch to remove the passenger(s)

- Riders can also dismount manually (with sneaking too)

Don't forget turn on Experimental Beta-APIs

What's New?

- Supported minecraft version 1.26.51.1

Compatibility Note

- This add-on using player.json, It may conflict with other behavior packs that also using player.json

- Place this add-on at the top of the behavior pack list

- Avoid combining with other packs that include their own player.json

This add-on was inspired by the Java mod Player Ladder, developed by ForwarDNerN.
