This texture pack is a Bedrock version of Bare Bones PBR x128 !

It adds PBR to Bare Bones, which makes the game look like the Minecraft trailers

I took the screenshots with Vibrant Visuals and some with Trailer Visuals

Place it above the default Bare Bones in the texture pack list

To properly update the texture pack, I recommend deleting the old version in game, then download and import the latest one

- About emissive blocks brightness :
I know it can be very bright when using Ray Tracing or any other shader
Emissive blocks in this texture pack are meant to work with Vibrant Visuals (the default shader)
Here is a small texture pack to place above BBPBRx128 that reduce the brightness for emissive blocks :

- Bare Bones PBR x128 Low Brightness
If you are playing with Vibrant Visuals, do not download this pack

If you play with Actions & Stuff, here is the pack order :
-Bare Bones & Stuff
-Bare Bones PBR
-Bare Bones
-Actions & Stuff
