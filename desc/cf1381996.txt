Warblocks: Warthunder is a Minecraft Bedrock addon that brings unprecedented realism to ground and air combat. Inspired by War Thunder, this mod adds 17 fully functional vehicles with advanced controls, realistic animations, and authentic behavior.

Works on 1.21.+ Minecraft Bedrock + All Experimental On

10 realistic combat tanks

7 fully armed and flyable aircraft

Each vehicle faithfully reproduces real-world behavior: movement, turret rotation, gun elevation, sound effects, shooting particles, explosions, and more.

During combat with any vehicletanks or aircraftplayers see:

HIT when a target is struck

TARGET DESTROYED when a target is destroyed

Revolutionary Damage System

This is the first mod in the world to feature a complete weakspot and strongspot system on tanks:

Front armor is heavily reinforced and resists most shots.

Sides and rear are vulnerable: a well-placed shot can destroy a tank in a single hit.

Each tank has its own weak points:

M4A1, M26 Pershing, M4A3(76)W weakspot on the hull machine gun

T-34/76 and IS-1 weakspot on the driver hatch

This system makes combat tactical, strategic, and immersive, like a true war simulator.

Aviation and Armament

Aircraft are fully flyable and equipped with functional machine guns.

Some models also carry droppable bombs for ground attacks.
