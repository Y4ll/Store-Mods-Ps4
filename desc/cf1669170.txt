All the information you need to use this Add-On is listed below!

Requirements

- The Education Edition slider (found in settings under cheats) must be turned on in order for this Add-On to work!

How to use this Add-On

- Look in the recipe book to find what you need to craft Spider-Man's mask! Wear it like a normal helmet to become Spider-Man. You are given two different items and a set of different animations:

- The three different web items can be right-clicked to fire webs. The first one places a cobweb where a mob stands to "capture" them. The Web Pull item can be used to pull a mob towards you! Web Tornado is an area of effect attack.

- When you are Spider-Man, you can

- Climb walls,

- Emit spider-sense particles when hostile mobs nearby,

- "Swing". You control the swinging by flying as if you are in creative mode but the animation is changed so that it looks like you are swinging or hanging on a web. Here is a list of animations that are changed:

- Flying - Hanging animation (upside down)

- Sprinting while Flying - Swinging animation

- Sneaking - Superhero landing pose

- Sneaking while Falling - Falling animation (sneaking while you are falling to the ground makes you transition instantly from the falling animation to the superhero landing pose)
