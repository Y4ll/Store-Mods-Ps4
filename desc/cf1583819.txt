Bring Java Combat to Bedrock!

Ever miss the sleek combat cooldown indicator from Minecraft Java Edition while playing on Bedrock? The oSkullo Java Attack Indicator bridges that gap! This clever resource pack adds a fully functional, real-time attack cooldown indicator to your screen, helping you time your strikes perfectly.

Features

Two Custom Modes: Choose your preferred playstyle using the built-in subpack slider!

Crosshair Indicator: Places the dynamic cooldown animation directly under your crosshair, keeping your eyes on the action.

Sword Indicator: Places the indicator down by your hotbar, giving you that classic vanilla Java feel.

Smooth Animations: Accurately tracks your attack time with a clean, multi-frame animation that syncs perfectly with your swings.

Lightweight & Optimized: Built specifically for Bedrock engine limits to ensure zero lag and seamless UI integration.
