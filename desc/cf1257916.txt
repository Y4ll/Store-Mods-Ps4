NOT AN OFFICIAL MINECRAFT PRODUCT.

The classic Minecraft sounds you know and love! Mojang is constantly updating the game with new and diverse sounds, but if you prefer more of a retro flavor to your Minecraft, this pack is for you! Also, newer content have received the same treatment for consistency sake!

Subpacks

Options labelled under 'Classic' removes sounds to match the atmosphere of certain historical versions of Minecraft.
