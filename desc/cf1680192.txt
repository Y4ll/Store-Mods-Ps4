YGP! Dirt Gives OP Items

What if breaking ordinary dirt could give you anything from normal loot to extremely powerful YGP equipment?

YGP! Dirt Gives OP Items turns Minecraft Bedrock dirt breaking into a randomized survival experience where supported dirt-family blocks can produce different rewards, special events, enemy encounters, or extremely rare YGP outcomes.

BREAK DIRT GET A RANDOM OUTCOME GET OP SURVIVE

Created by Yojitgamingpro / YGP Studio / YGP Minecraft.

RANDOMIZED REWARD SYSTEM

Breaking supported dirt-family blocks can produce different reward tiers:

Common 55%

Rare 25%

Epic 12%

Legendary 5%

YGP / OP Tier 3%

The rarer the outcome, the more valuable, unusual, or powerful the reward can become.

The 3% YGP / OP outcome guarantees the YGP V2 Blade, followed by an additional OP loot reward.

YGP V2 BLADE

The YGP V2 Blade is one of the addon's special high-tier items.

It includes two custom abilities.

YGP Surge

Activate Surge to receive powerful temporary effects including:

Strength

Resistance

Regeneration

Speed

Absorption

Night Vision

Cooldown: 8 seconds

YGP Pulse

Use Pulse while sneaking to activate a powerful support effect.

Pulse can:

Heal the player

Provide Fire Resistance

Provide Saturation

Extinguish fire

Affect nearby non-player entities

Cooldown: 12 seconds

OP ENEMY REWARDS

Defeating certain OP dirt enemies can provide additional high-tier rewards.

The OP enemy reward system includes a 35% chance to receive the YGP V2 Blade, with other powerful rewards available through the same reward path.

YGP IMMORTAL DOG

The previous Loyal Dog system has been replaced with the YGP Immortal Dog.

The Immortal Dog is automatically associated with the player as their pet.

No separate taming is required.

The system is designed for player-specific ownership, including multiplayer worlds.

The Immortal Dog brings the YGP companion system into the Dirt Gives OP Items experience.

MULTIPLAYER

YGP! Dirt Gives OP Items is designed with multiplayer gameplay in mind.

Player-specific state is used for relevant cooldown and event handling so that one player's interaction does not unnecessarily overwrite another player's state.

The YGP Immortal Dog is also associated with its individual owner.
