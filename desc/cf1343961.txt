Modular Fences is a Minecraft Bedrock Add-On featuring over 10 fully functional modular fences, these fences are part of my other Add-Ons, but now they come with extra features and brand-new content!

For now, Modular Fences includes only a few variations and fence models. The available designs are:

Glass Panel Fence:

The glass fence comes from the Modern Furniture Add-On. It currently includes 1 model: a modular glass fence with black or silver aluminum joints.

Detailed Wooden Fence:

This wooden fence comes from the Feudal Furniture Add-On and includes 12 variations (one for each wood type). Unlike regular fences, this one features a more medieval design, with cut log details and a more intricate structure.

Outdoor Fence:

This fence comes from the Modern Furniture Add-On. It features a tall geometry and is designed to block visibility from one side only. Its style comes in a pale black finish.

White Fence:

This fence replicates the classic American-style white picket fence, featuring the traditional upward-pointed tips.

Metal Fence:

This fence comes from the Modern Furniture Add-On. As its name suggests, its made of metal, featuring a simple, arcade-style design with straightforward slats. For now, it includes only one variant.

All of these fence types are modular and, most importantly, fully connectable with each otherallowing you to mix and match different styles seamlessly.
