Progressive design, cutting-edge technology, and precision performance brought to Minecraft Bedrock.

Experience the innovation and power of Audi vehicles inside Minecraft. This addon introduces a lineup of iconic Audi models, combining sleek design, responsive handling, and advanced engineering inspired by the legendary Quattro heritage perfect for players who want modern performance and refined driving on city streets and highways

MECHANICS:

- SPEED SYSTEM: The addon now features a gradual acceleration system that simulates an automatic transmission, providing smoother and more realistic vehicle behavior.

Start Neutral

Neutral Drive

Drive Neutral (only when stopped)

- IGNITION SYSTEM: Vehicles can only be started from inside the car using a key.

- FUEL SYSTEM:Each vehicle can be powered by different fuel types: Coal (Combustion), Redstone (Electric) and Hybrid: Uses both coal and redstone.

Each fuel type has its own maximum capacity. Players must manage fuel carefully to keep their vehicles running efficiently.

- SPEEDOMETER: A functional speedometer shows your current speed in km/h and the remaining amount of fuel, allowing precise control while driving.

- EMISSIVITY: Simulates the cars headlights.

- COLORS: Paint your car using any Minecraft dye.

- SHOP: Shop system using iron ingots + the required engine.
