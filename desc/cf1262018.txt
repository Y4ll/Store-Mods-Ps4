Compost Barter

Compost Barter lets you barter for bonemeals instead of composting it in the composter. Through one (1) material that uses in the barter (like potato) can be immediately be exchanged for bonemeal. Designed for survival worlds, hardcore worlds, resource-focused worlds, and farm (crop farm). This addon is compatible to the players/devices that play Minecraft:Bedrock/MCPE. If you're tired for time-consuming composting and then ran out of compost materials, then this addon is for you!

In this addon, the entity will let you barter compost items in exchange for bonemeals. All of the items that can be compost are available to barter. This is affordable if you have compost materials. This entity can barter for 0.5 second/half a second. This entity is also fast, what I mean fast I mean FAST. The entity spawns to the overworld with the surface grass block.
