Better Biomes Add-On

Welcome to Better Biomes, an expansive add-on that completely reimagines your Minecraft world!
This add-on introduces brand-new biomes and overhauls existing vanilla biomes and caves, filling them with vibrant life, new natural decorations, and unique mechanics.

Whether you're exploring frozen depths or relaxing under a palm tree, the world will feel more alive than ever!

Brand New Biomes

Azalea Forest

A lush forest filled with azalea bushes, towering azalea trees, mossy meadows, and abundant rivers. Underground, massive Lush Caves thrive beneath the surface.

Coralrest Cove

A breathtaking aquatic biome overflowing with marine life. Discover new aquatic blocks, colorful corals, and vibrant underwater vegetation.

Jacaranda Grove

One of the most gorgeous biomes in the game. Wander through purple-leafed trees, lavender fields, and patches of podzol that enrich the terrain.

Lushy Desert

A fresh twist on the classic desert! Green vegetation, hidden lush caves, algae, and a surprisingly lively ecosystem thrive in the heat.

Sulfur Wastes

Inspired by sulfur caves, this biome brings toxic beauty to the surface. Beware bubbling sulfur pools and jagged sulfur spikes made with the newest Minecraft blocks.

Vanilla Biome Overhauls

We havent just added new biomesweve breathed new life into the old ones!

Beaches

Completely redesigned with dry grass, new blocks, and beautiful Palm Trees. Harvest coconuts and crack them open with an axe for a tropical snack!

Deserts & Mesas

Discover new cacti, scattered ancient bones, and fresh vegetation. Mesa caves now feature unique eroded terrain, colorful terracotta layers, and new underground dangers.

Ice & Snow Biomes

Surface: New winter plants and small leaf-hanging stalactites.

Caves: Massive icicles and solid ice structures.

Frozen Ores: Break the ice layer before mining the ore inside!

Savanna

New dry grass, improved terrain layers, revamped acacia trees, and towering Giant Baobabs dominating the horizon.

Swamps & Swamp Caves

Dense lilypads, cattails, rich vegetation, and underground swamp caves filled with vines, exotic mushrooms, and new natural materials.

Natural Decorations & Features

The world is now packed with tiny details that make a huge difference:

Crystals growing naturally in caves

Small rocks & pebbles across terrain

Sandwaves & realistic terrain details

Hundreds of new natural decorative blocks

Unique Interactive Mechanics

Better Biomes is fully interactive thanks to custom scripting!

Pearl-bearing Clams: Crack them open for a chance to find a White Pearl.

Harvestable Coconuts: Knock them down, chop them open, and enjoy a Split Coconut.

Frozen Solid Ores: Break the ice layer before mining the ore.

Realistic Leaf Decay: Custom leaves decay naturally when trunks are chopped.

Lost Treasure Boxes: Found in shipwrecks and ruinscrack them open for loot!

Explore, survive, and get lost in the beauty of Better Biomes!
