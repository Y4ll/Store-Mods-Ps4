Saturation Enhanced

Description: This Minecraft pack enhances the game's saturation and makes every element look visually vibrant and appealing. Colors pop naturally without being overwhelming.

What makes this pack different from other saturated packs? This pack covers every single texture in Minecraft, including UI, items, entities, blocks, and more. The entire game achieves the same level of saturation, providing a consistent and balanced vibrant experience throughout.

Pack size: Around 3840 MB. Since this pack enhances saturation for every single texture, the size is slightly larger than usual.

If you prefer a pack with slightly lower saturation, you can check out this one: Saturation Balanced.
