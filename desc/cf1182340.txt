Do you want to know how much damage your weapon does? Then don't wait any longer and attack a target dummy.

You can place it like any other entity in the game, just make sure you have the item in your hand. The Target Dummy has 8 different rotations, and depending on the direction you place it, its rotation will change, similar to the Armor Stand.

Main Features

The Target Dummy can be placed anywhere in the world or in any dimension. When you hit it, it will perform a dynamic animation. In other words, if you hit it, it will lean in the opposite direction. Additionally, it will display the exact amount of damage it received.

To remove it, crouch and right-click (interaction button on mobile).

- Compatible with any Add-on or Texture pack

- You can use it to train and refine your skills.

- It is useful for knowing the exact damage your weapons deal.

- It does not replace any Vanilla Minecraft entities.

- It's easy to craft.

Installation Notes

To enjoy this add-on comfortably, make sure you have correctly downloaded the following files in their corresponding versions:

- TargetDummy [Bp].mcpack

- TargetDummy [Rp].mcpack

Both files are required for the add-on to function correctly. (One handles all behaviors and scripts, while the other provides the textures, so be sure to download both)

Experimental settings

This add-on does not require any experimental settings to be enabled. You can enjoy all of its content without disabling achievements in your world.
