Makes Sense aims to add some basic features that could be in the game, without adding any new blocks or itemsonly improving what already exists. It is a branch of Essence++ and works perfectly with it.

Dynamic Light & Off hand

Holding a luminous item
now emit light.

Drop a luminous item
now emit light.

Burning Mobs
now emit light.

Interacting with a glowing item while aiming in the air will transfer the item to your off-hand. This does not work with blocks.

Attacking or interacting with mobs using a torch sets them on fire for a few seconds, depending on the torch type.

View Visual Dem

Double Door:

When opening or closing a door, if there is another door nearby, it will also react to the action.

"Realistic" Breeding:

Breeding mobs can result in a chance of more than one being born. This does not happen with all mobs.
