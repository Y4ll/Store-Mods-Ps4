The Auto Miner is a machine that will dig for you. It can also place planks, rails, powered rails, and red-stone torches as it mines.

- The miner will pick up items as it mines. It won't pick up anything that doesn't stack to 64.

- It will use planks to bridge so that it doesn't fall. It can use any kind of planks, including planks from other add-ons.

- If it has rails, it will place them as it goes.

- If it has powered rails, it will place one every 16 blocks.

- If it has red-stone torches, it will place them next to the powered rails.

- To power it, use Coal, Charcoal, Blaze Rods, or a Lava bucket.

- It can also be powered with Ender Plasma and Nether Coal from my other add-on MineRe.

- The drill breaks after 6000 blocks are broken. It can be repaired with another diamond drill or by using diamonds on the miner.

- Durability and fuel are remembered when the miner is broken into item form.

- The miner can be started and stopped by hitting it. It will start when given fuel.

This add-on also includes the Enchanted Minecart.

A Minecart upgraded with Amethyst that can go up to 10x the speed of a vanilla minecart.

It's recommended to add guardrails to your tracks so that it doesn't fly off.

It has to be placed on rails like vanilla minecarts.
