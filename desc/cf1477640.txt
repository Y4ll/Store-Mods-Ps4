Project Description:

Are you ready to bring your Minecraft world into the modern era? After all, nothing says modern quite like spending all your time staring at a mobile phone. Text your friends with real-time notifications, share updates on the go, and stay connected no matter where your adventures take you.

Important Information:

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Actual message sending.

- Action bar notifications.

- Sound notifications.

- Offline memory.

- Behaviour & Resource packs are both required for the project to work.

- Have something to say? Join the Discord.

Change the dye to create any colour you want.

When you use your Mobile Phone will will get a UI pop up showing you everything your phone can do, before sending a Message to someone you will have to send a Friend Request. If your friend accepts your request you can then send messages to each other. If you or your friend are offline when a message is sent they will receive a notification when they next log in.

You can check your inbox to see if anyone has messaged you when you were offline.

Use your Social App to check Friend Requests, Contact List and a Block list to make sure only people you want messages from come through.

You can transfer items to friends in need, this will cost you one level of XP for each transfer.
