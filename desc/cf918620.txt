Gravel's Hammers Add-On for Minecraft Bedrock Edition

Overview

Gravel's Hammers is a Minecraft Bedrock Add-On designed to align closely with the vanilla mechanics and values of vanilla pickaxes. This add-on enhances your ability to create large tunnels or to clear areas while maintaining the game's balance.

Hammer Mining Modes

- Wooden, Stone, and Gold Hammers: Perform 331 mining when breaking a block.

- Iron and Diamond Hammers: Perform 332 when interacting and 331 when breaking.

- Netherite Hammer: Performs 332 for block breaking and 333 when interacting.

Note: You must sneak/crouch to activate any 33 hammer mode!

Dynamic Durability System

Hammers only lose durability when they actually break a blocksaving resources and improving longevity.

Enchantment Support

Gravel's Hammers supports all standard pickaxe enchantments:

- Fortune

- Unbreaking

- Silk Touch

- Mending

- Efficiency

- Curse of Vanishing

Experimental Features

This add-on does not use experimental toggles, ensuring full stability with future game updates.

Dedication to Vanilla Mechanics

- All hammer types match pickaxe crafting rules (e.g., wooden hammers use any wood type).

- Mining speed and block compatibility mirrors vanilla pickaxes.

- Repairing uses the same materials and amount.

- Progression follows pickaxe tiers (e.g., wood cant mine diamond ore).

Recipes

Wooden Hammer

Crafted using:

- 5 Wooden Planks (any type)

- 2 Sticks

Stone Hammer

Crafted using:

- 5 Stone/Deepslate

- 2 Sticks

Iron Hammer

Crafted using:

- 5 Iron Ingots

- 2 Sticks

Gold Hammer

Crafted using:

- 5 Gold Ingots

- 2 Sticks

Diamond Hammer

Crafted using:

- 5 Diamonds

- 2 Sticks

Netherite Hammer

Crafted using:

- 1 Diamond Hammer

- 1 Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template

Workbench

Crafted using:

- 1 Log (any type)

- 5 Planks (any type)

- 1 Iron Ingot

You craft all hammers on this special workbench!
