Milking Gives You Random Effects

Give milking a whole new purpose with Milking Gives You Random Effects by FleetMc Studio! Every time you milk a cow using an empty Bucket, you'll instantly receive a completely random effect or special ability. Some powers will make you nearly unstoppable, while others will challenge your survival skills in hilarious ways.

With over 140 different random powers and effects, every bucket of milk becomes a surprise. You never know whether you'll become incredibly powerful or receive a funny curse that changes the way you play.

Features

- Receive a random effect every time you milk a cow.

- Over 140 different powers and effects.

- Includes One Punch-style powers and many unique abilities.

- Contains both powerful buffs and funny curses.

- Every milking experience is completely random.

- Lightweight and optimized.

- Achievement Friendly.

- No Experimental Gameplay required.

How to Use

- Download and import the addon.

- Enable the Behavior Pack.

- Join your world.

- Craft or obtain an empty Bucket.

- Milk any Cow.

- Instantly receive a random power or effect!

Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
