How to use:

- Take 3 wands (Team A, Team B, Battle Wand) from creative.

- Select 1st mob by interacting with the Team A Wand (right click on PC, long click on mobile).

- Select 2nd mob by interacting with the Team B Wand (right click on PC, long click on mobile).

- Team A & B Wands mob record range is 7 blocks.

- When mob is recorded, it will send you feedback.

- After all of this, use a battle wand.

Items:

Mob Stop Clock:

- You can stop the mob when you set it up so you dont worry about distance.

- Stop mob Just hold the clock and tab on the screen. It will give you feedback so dont worry.
