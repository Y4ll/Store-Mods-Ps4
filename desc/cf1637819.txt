Tree Physics

A small Bedrock Add-On that causes trees to fall with realistic, interactive physics: leaves and branches can break away, entities can be crushed and knocked back, fragile blocks can shatter, and other physical structures can be pushed around.

Original Tree Physics Mod: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/tree-physics

(The Java mod inspired the gameplay design only, all other aspects were developed independently)

Tips and tricks:

-
Chop a naturally generated or sapling-grown tree to make it fall. Crouch while breaking a log to bypass tree physics and chop it normally.

-
Tree Physics relies on special roots generated by the Add-On. A new world is recommended. Trees generated before the Add-On is enabled, along with trees containing player-placed logs or leaves, remain ordinary blocks.

-
Some trees from other Add-Ons are supported through an optional expansion, see below for details.

-
Fallen trees interact with terrain, water, lava, entities, and one another. Attack a fallen tree to push it, or use an item while looking at it to grab and drag it. Use the item again to release it.

-
Leaves, attachments, branches, and large trunks may break away on impact. After coming to rest, a fallen tree will gradually break down and drop its normal vanilla loot.

-
World operators can configure physics simulation quality, breakdown rate, impact fracturing, and whether movement counts toward breakdown time. Settings are saved separately for each world.

-
The settings screen can be opened from the axe button in the chat screen. If another resource pack conflicts with this interface, select the Compatibility subpack and use /tree_physics:settings instead.

Optional Expansion

Tree Physics: Timber Tumble

Extends Tree Physics to support conventionally generated trees from other Add-Ons. It is not needed when playing with vanilla trees only.

Known limitations and risks:

-
Only trees in newly generated chunks can be recognized, and some supported trees may still be missed.

-
Enabling the expansion will increase world generation load and slow chunk generation.

-
Exceptionally large trees may cause severe performance loss or crashes when becoming physical.

-
Tree-like natural wooden structures may rarely be misidentified as trees.
