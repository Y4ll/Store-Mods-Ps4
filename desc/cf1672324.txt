A Touch of Medieval brings a subtle medieval makeover to Minecraft with detailed doors, trapdoors, iron bars, ladders, lanterns, torches, new 3D fire, and decorative glass blocks and panes, adding an old-world feel while keeping the vanilla style.

Features

New door and trapdoor textures

New Glass Block and Panes Textures

New Torch Texture and New Flame Effect!

Black Iron Themed Door,Trapdoor and Iron Bars

New Lantern and Ladder Texture
