Hardcore Hearts Texture Pack

(Bedrock Edition)

This Hardcore Hearts is a texture modification for Minecraft Bedrock Edition designed to bring the iconic Java Edition "Hardcore Mode" aesthetic to your heads-up display (HUD)

This resource pack replaces the standard survival heart icons with the stylized "Hardcore" versions, featuring the classic red texture with a more intense, outlined design.

Standard Hearts: Includes full and half hearts, as well as their flashing animations for when you take damage.

Status Effects: Custom hardcore-styled textures for Poison and Wither effects.
