Arduous Journey is a real addonpack for the Bedrock version of Minecraft that works on ALL platforms and includes custom content, not just mashing addons together.

Playing this pack will bring you into a challenging dark fantasy world with new things such as bosses, mobs, dungeons, armors, tools and much more then you can imagine like the world lore & its story!

The custom mods include various stuff made by me such as bosses, performence tweaks, custom fixes to make addons work together, custom edits to most structures and etc. Read the installation instructions below please.

Installation Guide (Must read or watch installation video above)

Step 1: Download the latest Arduous Journey Mcaddon file in the Files tab! Then double click the downloaded mcaddon file itself, doing so will make it auto install all the addons into your game.

It will take a minute for all the addons to install, please wait until the game is done.

Step 2: The Better On Bedrock addon is the only addon that didn't give permission to include their addon in this pack. Click this link and download their addon!: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft-bedrock/addons/better-on-bedrock

Step 3: Click to create a world and go to the "Experimental" world settings, then toggle on Upcoming Creator Features and "Beta APIs". Some addons will fail to work unless these are on!

Step 4: The Resource Packs must be organized exactly like this image below for the first 10 Resource Pack addons. The rest of the addons do not matter in the way they are organized.

Here is the addon list if you unsure which addons belong to this pack THIS LINK IS NOT HOW TO ORGANIZE, THE IMAGE IS BELOW: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1V3ckoFrT_vWib3vIXfbpLKKZZtgOoHdPuGWyxjcxXRw/edit?usp=sharing

Step 5: Recommended settings!

Difficulty: HARD!

Render Distance 12 or lower
Higher may cause lag or crashes due to a Mojang bug unrelated to my pack: Mojang Bug Report Link

Vibrant Visuals - Does not matter
