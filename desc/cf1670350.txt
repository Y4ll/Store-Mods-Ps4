Basic Visuals is a lightweight resource pack for Minecraft Bedrock that brings clean, balanced colors, lighting, skies, and atmosphere to the game a simple, vanilla-inspired upgrade with no shader pack or Vibrant Visuals toggle required.

Reimagined Visual Style

- Clean, balanced color identity that stays true to vanilla Minecraft

- Simple and readable, without heavy stylization or oversaturation

- Designed to feel refreshed and immersive without overwhelming builds

- Subtle atmospheric depth added across all vanilla biomes

Advanced Atmospherics System

- Custom sky zenith and horizon color tuning for a smooth day-night gradient

- Warm golden-orange sunrise and sunset horizon tones with strong blending

- Rich blue-violet daytime zenith for added sky depth

- Tuned Rayleigh scattering to prevent washed-out or overly dark skies

Pink Atmosphere Variant

- Alternate atmosphere option with soft magenta-to-lavender sky tones

- Same clean lighting and horizon blending, reimagined in a dreamier palette

- Easy to swap in for a different mood without changing anything else

Aurora Effect

- Subtle blue aurora that activates naturally during nighttime hours in the Overworld

- Adds a quiet extra layer of atmosphere without distracting from gameplay

Requirements

Basic Visuals requires Minecraft Bedrock/PE version 1.21.80 or higher. No Vibrant Visuals or PBR support needed works on any device that runs vanilla Bedrock.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open to import into Minecraft

- Enable Basic Visuals in Global Resources
