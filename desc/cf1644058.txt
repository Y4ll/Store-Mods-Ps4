Lynx Quality Tools Addon

Every item. Every quality. Every choice.

Lynx Quality Tools adds a clean RPG-style Quality System to Minecraft Bedrock.

Weapons, tools, shields, and armor can receive different Quality Tiers with effects designed around their purpose.

Quality Tiers

Poor 35% 1 Negative Effect

Bad 25% 2 Negative Effects

Common 20% No Effects

Rare 12% 2 Positive Effects II

Epic 6% 3 Positive Effects II

Legendary 2% 3 Positive Effects III

The higher the tier, the stronger the potential.

Item-Based Effects

Effects are designed around each item's role:

Swords Combat

Bows Ranged Combat

Spears Melee & Mobility

Tridents Aquatic Combat

Shields Defense

Tools Mining & Utility

Fishing Rods Fishing & Aquatic Utility

Armor Defense & Mobility

Quality Reset

Want to reroll your gear?

Rename a normal Diamond to Reset using an Anvil.

Slot 1 Reset

Slot 2 Quality Item

Interact/Use the Reset Diamond to reroll the item in Slot 2.

One successful reset consumes one Reset Diamond.
