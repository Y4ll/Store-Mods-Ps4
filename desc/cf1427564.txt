- HOW DO YOU EVOLVE THE STORM MANUALLY? -

To do so, you must ensure certain stages are active, for example.

COMMANDED WITHER:

/event entity @e[family=main_wither_storm] stage.....

WITHER STORM (Destroyer):

/event entity @e[family=main_wither_storm] phase.....

(after you explode the formidibomb on phases bigger than 5.1) >

WITHER STORM (Devour):

/event entity @e[family=main_wither_storm] devour_phase....

(you CAN do a "devour_phase1" on destroyer to omit the wither storm play dead, however, it MUST be devour_phase1, going to another devour phase won't summon severeds.)

(also, if the Devour Wither Storm is active, doing once again "devour_phase1" will duplicate the severeds!)

ENABLE VORTEX DEBRIS:

/event entity @e[family=main_wither_storm] devour:debris_vortex_level...

KEEP IN MIND that these stages (except debris vortex level) are only a temporary change! the storm will still evolve to the phase it was supposed to.

The Basics.

The Wither Storm begins as a modified Wither fused with a Command Block.

you must PERSONALLY summon it to bring this creature to the world.

As the Storm evolves, it gains new abilities such as tentacles, tractor beams and charged abilities, Destroying it's heads is not always the correct solution, as doing so can trigger accelerated evolution for a short period of time.

The Storm actively tracks players (specially groups of players), adapts its altitude, regenerates health over time, and responds differently depending on player actions. Ignoring it or allowing it to feed uncontested can quickly turn the world into a wasteland.
