A Bare Bones-style visual adaptation of SpectralPumpkin A&S, bringing that clean, trailer-inspired look to every animated tool frame made with permission from JustPals.

This pack replaces the tool textures across all 12 animation frames per tool, matching the original SpectralPumpkin color-cycle effect while keeping the Bare Bones aesthetic: softer shading, defined outlines, and a more natural color palette. All 8 subpacks (Default All Tools + 7 "Only Netherite" color variants) are supported.

Requirements pack order matters! (top to bottom):

Bare bones X spectral pumpkin

Bare Bones & Stuff

Bare Bones Texture Pack

Actions & Stuff

Original SpectralPumpkin A&S by JustPals used with permission

This pack requires Action and Stuff 1.11 and any of the dependencies mentioned above need to be in the most recent version to function.

WARNING SECTION

Without the required packs installed and in the correct order, your tools will be invisible in-hand.
