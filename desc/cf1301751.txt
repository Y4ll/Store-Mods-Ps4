Miraculous Adventure Addon

Welcome to Miraculous Adventure, a Minecraft Bedrock addon inspired by the universe of Miraculous: Tales of Ladybug & Cat Noir.

Explore the world of Miraculous in Minecraft, discover powerful abilities, transform with different Miraculous, and experience a growing system designed around the adventures, characters, and powers of the series.

Each Miraculous is connected to a mystical creature known as a Kwami, granting its holder unique abilities and allowing them to transform into powerful heroes or villains.

Miraculous

The addon currently includes a variety of Miraculous, each with its own abilities, transformations, and gameplay mechanics:

-

Ladybug Miraculous Harness the power of creation and restore what has been destroyed with the Lucky Charm and Miraculous Ladybug.

-

Rabbit Miraculous Master the power of time and travel between different moments.

-

Snake Miraculous Use Second Chance to return to a previous moment and try again.

-

Rooster Miraculous Choose a unique special ability and adapt your powers to different situations.

-

Dragon Miraculous Harness the elemental powers of wind, water, and lightning.

-

Butterfly Miraculous Grant special powers to others through Akumas and influence the course of events.

-

Bee Miraculous Paralyze your enemies with the power of Venom.

-

Goat Miraculous Use the power of creation to bring objects and ideas into reality.

-

Bull Miraculous Harness incredible strength and become resistant to supernatural effects.

-

Peacock Miraculous Create Sentimonsters and give life to beings shaped by emotions.

-

More Miraculous coming soon.

Compatibility

Miraculous Adventure is compatible with Novelty API, allowing it to work alongside systems and content that use the API.

For the addon to function correctly, you must enable Education Edition and the required Experimental Features, including Beta APIs.
