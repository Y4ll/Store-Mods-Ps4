A New Era of Bedrock Graphics

Complimentary Vibrant Visuals is a premium graphics overhaul engineered specifically for the Minecraft Bedrock Render Dragon engine. It transforms standard, flat environments into deeply atmospheric, physically accurate landscapes.

Whether you are capturing cinematic screenshots or building your ultimate survival base, this shader provides a definitive visual upgrade without compromising the beloved aesthetic of vanilla Minecraft.

Next-Gen Capabilities

PBR Texturing

Complete integration of normal and specular maps. Experience realistic material roughness, depth, and reactive light reflections across terrains.

Color Grading

Eliminates the washed-out tone of the base game. Biomes feature rich, carefully calibrated color palettes that dramatically enhance environmental mood.

Optimized Tiers

Includes highly optimized memory tiers via subpack configurations (Low Featured & Default Featured) to ensure maximum stability.

Atmospheric Lighting

Stunning sunrises, glowing ores, and deep shadows completely change the way you explore the world at night and in caves.

Installation & Setup

- Download the .mcpack file and open it to import into Minecraft.

- Navigate to Settings > Global Resources and activate the pack.

- Click the Gear Icon on the pack to select your performance tier (Low Featured or Default Featured).

- Crucial: Ensure that Deferred Technical Preview is enabled in your Video settings!
