Bring the terrifying Tripods from the 2005 War of the Worlds movie starring Tom Cruise into your Minecraft world! The invasion has begun, and humanity is no longer at the top of the food chain. Take control of powerful Martian machines and experience the invasion from the perspective of the Tripods themselves. This add-on is still in early beta and is highly experimental, so expect bugs, unfinished features, and changes as development continues. For now, the add-on features three unique controllable Tripods: the Exterminator, the Harvester, and the Uberpod. Each Tripod has its own purpose, abilities, weapons, and style of destruction. Your goal is simple: SURVIVE FOR 30 DAYS WITHOUT DYING. Whether you decide to fight alongside the Martians, take control of their machines, or simply try to survive the invasion, the world around you will never be the same. Can humanity survive the arrival of the Tripods?

Add-On fully compatible with Vibrant Visuals!

THE EXTERMINATOR

THE FIGHTING MACHINE

Built for combat and destruction, the Exterminator is the primary fighting machine of the Martian invasion. Armed with its devastating Heat Ray, the Exterminator can eviscerate anything that stands in its way. Its weapon is designed to turn enemies into nothing more than ash, making it one of the most dangerous machines on the battlefield. But the Exterminator isn't defenseless. An energy-based defensive system protects the Tripod from incoming projectiles, allowing it to withstand attacks from enemies attempting to fight back from a distance. Take control of the Exterminator and become the Martians' ultimate combat machine. Heat Ray. Energy Protection. Pure Destruction.

THE HARVESTER

THE HANDLING MACHINE

The Harvester was not created simply to destroy. It was created to harvest. Using its powerful machinery, the Harvester can capture and harvest mobs throughout the world, turning living creatures into resources for the Martian invasion. But the harvesting is only the beginning. The creatures taken by the Harvester help spread the Martians' influence across the world. Red Weed begins to infest the environment, slowly transforming the landscape into something resembling the Martians' own world. As the infestation spreads, the world becomes less and less recognizable. Harvest the creatures. Spread the Red Weed. Transform the world.

THE UBERPOD

THE WAR MACHINE

The Uberpod is a Martian war machine created for one purpose: OVERPOWER EVERYTHING. Built with the highest level of firepower and durability, the Uberpod represents the next stage of Martian warfare. It was designed to command the battlefield, overwhelm resistance, and destroy anything powerful enough to challenge the invasion. Unlike the other Tripods, the Uberpod relies on overwhelming force and advanced weapons to dominate its targets. Its arsenal includes chemical warfare designed to weaken and destroy its enemies, turning the battlefield into a deadly environment for anything caught in its path. Heavy. Powerful. Durable. Unrelenting. When the Uberpod arrives, the battle is no longer about survival. It is about domination.
