This add-on will have the Plant Vs Zombies 2 plants to help you out in your survival world and defeat zombies for you. The add-on can be used in maps as well. If you want to have a Plant Vs Zombies 2 map, then this add-on is for you. (This is not really the best in survival, because some plants can one shot vanilla zombies, and some zombies can kill you on one hit. I am planning to have it survival friendly and fit in a survival experience)

There are currently 76 plants in the add-on so far, and 35 zombies from the actual game.
