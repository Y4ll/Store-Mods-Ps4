Goatman

The forest is silent.

Too silent.

The deeper you wander into dense woods, the more the feeling grows that something else is moving between the trees.

You never hear it for long.

You never see it clearly.

But it always seems to know exactly where you are.

Features

- Encounter the mysterious Goatman hidden within dense forests.

- Advanced AI that reacts to your actions and knows when you've spotted it.

- Unpredictable stalking behavior that keeps every encounter unique.

- Strange footsteps and unsettling ambient sounds throughout the woods.

- Terrifying chase sequences when the Goatman decides to hunt.

- Psychological horror focused on tension, uncertainty, and atmosphere.

- Every trip through the forest becomes a dangerous gamble.

---

How It Works

At first, the forests seem no different from any other.

Then the strange sounds begin.

Branches crack.

Footsteps echo between the trees.

Something moves just beyond your vision.

Occasionally, you'll catch a glimpse of a tall figure standing perfectly still in the distance.

Unlike most creatures...

The Goatman knows when you've seen it.

Sometimes it disappears before you can get closer.

Sometimes it refuses to move at all.

And sometimes...

It starts moving toward you.

Once it decides you're no longer just another traveler, escaping the forest becomes far more difficult than finding your way in.

---

The Forest Remembers

The deeper you venture into dense forests, the less predictable your surroundings become.

Every shadow could be hiding something.

Every strange sound could mean you're being followed.

And every time you look behind you...

You may not like what you find.
