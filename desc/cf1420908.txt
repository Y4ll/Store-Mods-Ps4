Bedrock ChunkLoader

Your farms work brilliantly until you walk 30 blocks away and everything grinds to a halt. ChunkLoader adds placeable blocks that keep chunks loaded and ticking, so your contraptions finally work without a babysitter.

Key Features

- Persistent Chunk Loading - Keep farms and contraptions running while you're away

- Farm Mode - Mob farms keep producing drops even when no player is nearby

- Looting Sword Support - Hold a looting sword while your farm runs to boost drop amounts, just like a real trident killer

- 12 Supported Farm Types - Iron, Gold, General Mob, Creeper, Witch, Enderman, Blaze, Guardian, Drowned, Slime, Fish & Ink, Magma Cube

- Redstone Control - Power a chunk loader with redstone to pause chunk loading

- Configurable Chunk Radius - 2-6 chunks (5x5 to 13x13 chunk areas)

- Visual Status Display - Animated textures and floating text show loader status

- Three-State Display - Active (green), Inactive (red), Redstone Off (orange)

- Setup Validation - Ensures correct placement above a hopper or water stream

- Owner Protection - Only the owner or admins can remove a chunk loader

- Player Limits - Configurable max loaders per player (1-20)

- Admin Panel - Full management via /mygen:chunkloaders with global controls

- Admin Delegation - Operators can grant trusted players access to the admin panel

- Floating Text Behind Walls - Raycasting hides text through blocks

- Craftable - A Redstone Block ringed with a Diamond, Resin Clump, Amethyst Shard, and Lapis Lazuli (recipe unlocks on obtaining a Redstone Block or Resin Clump)

- No Beta APIs Required - Achievement friendly

- Multiplayer Ready - Works on realms and servers

How to Use

Getting a Chunk Loader

Craft one at a crafting table a Redstone Block in the centre with a Diamond, Resin Clump, Amethyst Shard, and Lapis Lazuli around it (the four corners are left empty). It's an overworld scavenger hunt rather than a boss grind: deep caves for the diamond, an amethyst geode to crack open, a mining trip for lapis, and a trek into the Pale Garden for the resin clump. The recipe unlocks the moment you pick up a Redstone Block or a Resin Clump.

You can also grab one from the creative inventory under Equipment if you're not feeling the grind.

Placing & Managing

Place the block wherever you want chunks to stay loaded. It'll activate automatically and show its status with floating text above the block green tick means working, red cross means off. Right-click (interact with) it to open the management menu where you can toggle it on/off, set a farm type, and check its status.

Only you (or an admin) can break your chunk loaders. Nobody's griefing your setup.

Farm Mode

Looting Sword Support

Want more drops? Hold a Looting-enchanted sword while your farm is running and the looting level applies to the farm's drop rates automatically just like a real trident killer. No extra setup, just hold and profit.
