Overview

Welcome to Apocalypse, the Add-On version. This add-on brings the Map's experience into your own worlds.

If you are looking for the map, it is here

Slay zombies for Experience Levels, and use them to buy weapons, utility or boosters to aid you in your survival in this apocalypse! Oh and you can CHAINSAW zombies for ammo.

This add-on also has a custom music system. The music changes according to the time of day and number of zombies. The World Host can always turn on / off the music.

Host System

This add-on features a Host system. The very first player to join a world with this Add-On active will be the World Host.
All players will receive the following items when they first join the world:

- Full Iron Armour

- Chainsaw

- P250 Pistol

The World Host will receive the following additional items:

- Safehouse

Custom Damage System - No Hit Immunity

This add-on also has a custom gun damage system using scripting that removes hit immunity, meaning fire rate actually matters and shotguns work like shotguns.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Safehouse

There can only be 1 safehouse in any world. Choose your location wisely! The safehouse as a chest with supplies, a crafting table and a furnace.
The safehouse also contains Chuck, the friendly shop keeper. He will load the chunks around him and he cannot be removed.
Right click/tap the Highscore Block or the Stats Block to view the World Highscores or World Stats respectively

New Safehouse - Stronghold

It has many starter items, lava generators for fuel (using dripstone) and even a small potato farm on the roof! Oh and it's no longer flammable.

Old Safehouse (Now called "Shed")

The old safehouse isn't deleted! The world host can still craft it using Spruce Planks and an Iron Block. You can view all the new custom recipes in the recipe book.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Shop

Use Experience Levels to buy items! Some special items such as Boosters may cost different items such as Diamonds or Lapis Lazuli Blocks.

Just simply right click / tap & hold Chuck to open up the shop menu!

<figure class="image"></figure>

Host Menu

Here you can change some settings like Difficulty, Music, Hordes, Display, etc.
You can access this menu anywhere, use the Host Menu item or type !host in chat. If it breaks try using the fix item.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Personal Menu

You can access this anywhere, use the Personal Menu item or type !personal in chat. Here you can see the Credits, buy boosters, look at your stats, as well as learn about every zombie type!

<figure class="image"></figure>

Prestige

Use the Prestige item or type !prestige in chat to access this menu. Unlock all weapons & abilities first to prestige, which will give you permanent buffs.
