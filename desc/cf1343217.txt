Have you ever imagined farming experience in a new way in Minecraft? With this addon, youll discover the XP Berry Bushes, a special type of bush that spawns in your world and produces glowing berries filled with energy.

Main Features: Works similar to the Sweet Berry Bush, but with a magical twist. Harvest the bushes to collect XP Berries. Eating one grants 25 experience points instantly. A fun and unique way to get XP without grinding mobs or mining endlessly.

How it works: Find or plant the bushes in your world. Wait for them to grow naturally. Harvest the berries and eat them for instant XP.

The seed can be planted on grass and dirt blocks, after being planted it will have 4 stages of growth

Compatibility: Fully compatible with the latest version of Minecraft Bedrock. Works in both Survival and Creative.

Perfect for players who enjoy adventure, survival, or just want to speed up enchanting without massive XP farms
