Performance+ | FPS Optimization Pack

Official Performance Resource Pack for Minecraft Bedrock/Pocket Edition.

Performance+ is a carefully optimized resource pack for Minecraft Bedrock, designed to reduce lag, lower rendering load, and improve overall gameplay smoothness on low-end and mid-range devices.

Instead of flashy visuals, Performance+ focuses on clean textures, reduced visual noise, and smart feature optimizations that directly improve performance. Every change is made to keep Minecraft looking vanilla, readable, and lightweight, while delivering a noticeably smoother and more stable experience.

Key Features

-Clear water and clear fog in all 89 biomes

-Lower fire texture for improved combat visibility

-Fast & optimized full grass rendering

-176 particles removed, with potion and spell effects kept but trimmed

-42 animated block textures frozen, including water, lava, kelp, bubble columns and portals

-Cleaner block visuals with refined 16 textures

-Reduced rendering overhead for smoother frame pacing

Best Use Cases

-Smoother gameplay and reduced lag

-Low-end phones, tablets & PCs

-Older hardware or weak GPUs

-Survival, PvP, and long gameplay sessions

-Reduced device heating and stutter

Compatibility

-Minecraft Bedrock Edition

-Supported versions: 1.21+

-Works in single-player & on servers

-Compatible with most resource packs & shaders

-Works with Vibrant Visuals

-Supports Pocket Edition

Installation

1.Download the .mcpack file

2.Open the file Minecraft will import it automatically

3.Go to Settings Resource Packs My Packs

4.Activate Performance+
