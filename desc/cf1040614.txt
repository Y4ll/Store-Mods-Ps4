This addon will no longer receive updates and has been replaced by Fused's Better Falling Leaves Texture Pack

Forests in Minecraft don't have much ambience, but this small addon fixes that by adding leaf particles which fall from trees, adding ambience to your worlds! Each tree has its own type of falling leaves and the particles have some cool physics, animations and varieties and also interact with blocks!
