ABOUT THE ADD-ON:

PEPPA PIG DWELLER is a Minecraft horror add-on designed for survival gameplay. The add-on introduces two new characters, a toy box, and a dinosaur toy.

1) Peppa Pig Dweller: the only hostile mob added by this addon; the pig has 500 health and 3 different types of attacks.

- Right-hand attack deals 5 damage.

- Left-hand attack deals 5 damage.

- Jumpscare attack instantly kills the player. Peppa attacks only the player and George. In survival mode, she spawns only after George cries.

2) George: a friendly character who does not attack the player or other mobs. He has only 50 health points. In Survival Mode, how quickly you encounter Peppa depends entirely on his condition. George has three meters you need to monitor; if any one of them drops to zero, George will start crying, and Peppa Pig will appear.

- HUNGER METER: George is a very picky eater, so he can only be fed carrots.

- FEAR METER: Like all small children, George can get scared. The fear meter depletes much more slowly than the others during gameplay, but try to avoid or skip thunderstorms. To lower George's fear level, you need to give him his favorite dinosaur toy, which can be found in toy boxes.

- SLEEP BAR: Like all young children, George needs plenty of sleep, so you will need to build a special bed for him. His hunger and fear levels continue to decrease while he sleeps. Also, to move around the world faster, I recommend picking George up, as he does not follow the player on his own. That said, Peppa Pig may also appear if you kill George.

3) Toy Box: An ordinary box containing various loot, similar to "Lucky Blocks." The box can yield bad itemssuch as sticks or bonesas well as good ones, like a dinosaur toy for George, copper, iron, or a carrot (also for George).

4) Dinosaur: A standard dinosaur toy that can be given to George to reduce the fear meter.

5) Bed: George needs his own personal bed for sleeping; to craft it in Survival mode, you only need sticks and blue wool. To get George to lie down on it, you need to push him as close to the bed as possible.
