Wheel of Creation: Geographic Mining

A Minecraft Bedrock Edition Behavior Pack

English

Wheel of Creation: Geographic Mining completely reimagines how mineral ores generate in your Minecraft Bedrock world. Forget the old vanilla system where every ore appears randomly everywhere underground. Now, each ore is tied to specific biomes, making exploration not just rewarding but necessary.

- Diamonds: Exclusively found in the Deep Dark.

- Iron: Forms the bones of the Mountains.

- Gold: Buried deep in Deserts and Mesas.

- Emeralds: Hidden beneath the Jungle floor.

- Farms Nerfed: Iron Golems and Zombie Piglins no longer drop ingots/gold. You must mine for your wealth!

This transforms the geopolitical landscape of any server. Borders, trade routes, and alliances will form naturally based on who controls which biome.
