IMPORTANT:

- Does NOT require experimental features.

- Requires version 1.21.60+ or newer.

- Achievements are enabled.

- Make sure you download and install both packs Resource and Behavior.

HOW TO WEAR:
- To wear the backpack place the backpack item in the off-hand slot.
- If the backpack is placed on the ground hit the backpack and this will auto
equip it to your off-hand slot, if you are already wearing a backpack it will
drop the item.

HOW TO UPGRADE:
- To upgrade your backpack there are two ways to access the menu.
- Metho 1: Place down the back pack sneak and interact with it, this will
open the upgrade menu.

- Method 2: Hold the backpack in your hand and interact with the item this
will also open the upgrade menu.

Upgrade: Extra Storage
Not enough room? Get the Storage Upgrade!
This upgrade adds extra pages to your backpack,
letting you carry even more items on your adventures.

How it works:
When Placed: You'll see arrows inside the backpack.
Click them to flip between your inventory pages.

Upgrade: Magnet
Tired of running to pick up items? Let them come to you!
This upgrade automatically pulls all nearby items right to you,
so you don't have to pick them up one by one.

How it works:
- Purchase: Buy the Magnet upgrade from the backpack's
upgrade menu.
- Toggle: After buying it, you can turn the magnet ON or OFF
from the same menu.

Upgrade: Security Lock
- Keep your items safe and sound!
- This upgrade locks your backpack so only you, the owner, can open it
or pick it up. No one else will be able to get inside.

How it works:
- Purchase: Buy the Security Lock from the backpack's upgrade
menu. This will also set you as the owner.
- Toggle: After buying it, you can make your backpack LOCKED or
UNLOCKED from the same menu. (Be careful! Unlocking it lets anyone
get inside!)

Upgrade: Auto-Sort
Is your backpack a mess? Clean it up with one click!

This upgrade gives you a new button in the upgrade menu that letsyou
instantly sort all the items on your backpack's current page. It will
stack everything together neatly.

How it works:
Purchase: Buy the Auto-Sort upgrade from the backpack's menu.
Sort: Go back into the menu and choose how you want to sort: by
Name or by Amount.
Note: This upgrade only works on a backpack that has been placed in
the world.

Void Filter
This upgrade deletes selected items from the backpacks inventory.

Auto Totem
This upgrade allows the player to use the totem of undying directly
from the backpacks inventory

Tombstone

This upgrade automatically places your offhand
backpack at your death location and collects your
dropped inventory items directly into its storage pages.

- When the player spawns they will also reive a paper with the coordinates of where they died, and when held in hand the paper will show a waypoint to the location of the backpack.

Upgrade: Dynamic Lights

The Dynamic Lights upgrade causes the backpack to emit full brightness (light level 15) around the player
while held or equipped in hand/offhand, illuminating the surrounding area as they move.

CRAFTING RECIPES:

HOW TO CHANGE BACKPACK COLOR:

- For now only the first backpacks color can be changed but others will be added in the future.

- To change the color simply place the backpack and hold any dye in your hand and interact with it.
