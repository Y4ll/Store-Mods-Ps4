Beachside update

Normal Beach

The beach now has its own grass set!

-Both Tall and Normal grass variants including wheat tipped variants! Tall wheat-tipped grass can be harvested for wheat, while short beach grass needs to be broken to get wheat from.

Seashells from Natural Structures original returns!! Seashells can be turned into bonemeal through the andesite mortar.

Kelp Clumps drop a decent amount of kelp and can be found in all beach biomes.

Beach Pebbles can drop an assortment of polished stones!

Succulents (only found at normal beaches) can be stacked in a single block. Found in your worlds on beach sides they can be crafted into cactus and used as such.

Driftwood can spawn on the beach, it can generate on beaches of all kinds and on the ocean floor.

Bioluminiscent Moss can sometimes generate on side of the beaches! Will glow and provide a little light during the night.

Theres also a rare beach biome variant!

Stone Beach

Stone Beaches have new foliage

Stone beaches have new bushes that generate exclusively here. Also very increased rock generations, so all your pebbles needs are fulfilled.

Ice succulents generate in stone beaches too! Head to your local stone beach to gather some.

Tuff generates on the outer layer too!

Ocean Floor

Disclaimer: I do plan on adding a lot more things to underwater areas with waterlogging!!!

Shells, Pebbles, Driftwood and Bones can spawn on the bottom of the ocean.

River

Disclaimer: Rivers will be updated more thoroughly!

River generation has two new flowers and a water plant.

Water Lillies are more commonly found on the sides of the river. Can be found fairly commonly.

Primroses can be found generated either on land or 1 tile deep water.
