What is this?

This is an addon of the famous Lifesteal where if you kill someone you get +1 Heart, and if you die you lose 1 heart.

Recipes.

The Heart has 3 recipes:

The Beacon of Life:

Items

The Heart:

Gives you +1 extra heart.

The Beacon of Life:

Revive banned players after they lost all their Hearts.

Commands:

- /lifesteal withdraw <amount>: Withdraw hearts.

- /admin-lf: Operator only command that opens Admin Menu where you can edit Config and Player Hearts.
