Simple Waypoint System

A super light weight, super simple waypoints system. Save locations you've been, teleport back to them later! Yes, it's honestly so simple I haven't much more to say about it. I made one of these a couple of years ago as part of a larger unreleased project, so I thought I'd try my hand at it again in a sprint.

Gamemodes:

Good for Survival: The "Waypearl" item can be crafted using obsidian, ender pearls, and a compass.* The Waypearl gives you access to the whole simple waypoints system. The fastest form of travel in the game.

Good for Creative: The simple waypoints system can be accessed via the /waypoints command (or the aliases /waypoint and /wp), if you have cheats enabled on the world. No more flying to and from your impressive builds.

* Screenshot of the recipe included, although the game will guide you as the recipe will be unlocked if you obtain any of the three items required.
