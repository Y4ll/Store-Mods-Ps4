About

Welcome to Happy's Magic Mirrors, an Add-On that adds Magic Mirrors from the legendary game Terraria, but with a minecrafty twist!

This Add-On adds 10 items.

Recall Potion

The Recall Potion is a single-use item that teleports the user to their spawn.

It can be brewed in a brewing stand using a Dandelion Potion as the input and Specular Fish as the reagent.

Dandelion Potion

The Dandelion Potion is an item used to brew Recall Potions.

It can be brewed in a brewing stand using a Bottle of Water as the input and a Dandelion as the reagent.

Specular Fish

The Specular Fish is an item used to craft Recall Potions. It can also be cooked.

It is rarely obtained through fishing.

It replenishes 1 hunger.

Cooked Specular Fish

Cooked Specular Fish is a food item.

It is obtained through cooking Specular Fish.

It replenishes 6 hunger.
