This mod turns your Minecraft experience upside down by making every single crafting recipe completely random! Every time you start a new world, the game generates a fresh set of combinations. This means a recipe that gives you a diamond sword in one world might give you a dirt block in the next. No two playthroughs will ever be the same!

How it works:

- Every world has its own randomized logic.

- Don't worry once you discover a recipe in your world, it stays the same for that specific world. You can craft it again and get the same item every time.

Can you actually beat Minecraft when you have no idea how to make a pickaxe? Give it a try and have fun!

Attention: To use this mod, please enable the Beta API in your world settings.
