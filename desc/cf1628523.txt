In Your World

It starts like any other Minecraft world.

Trees. Rivers. Caves.

Nothing feels out of place.

Until you notice something that shouldn't exist.

A structure where there was nothing before.

A silhouette standing impossibly high above the clouds.

You look away

It's gone.

Maybe you imagined it.

Maybe not.

Features

- Unexplained structures that appear where they shouldn't.

- A mysterious presence that is never where you expect it.

- Strange translucent figures wandering the world.

- Unsettling ambient sounds and unexplained noises.

- Psychological horror focused on atmosphere and uncertainty.

- Random events that make every world feel different.

---

What Happens?

No one really knows.

Some players report seeing impossible structures in the sky.

Others claim they heard footsteps while completely alone.

Some have found strange constructions that seem to have no purpose.

Others insist they were being watched.

Most never see the same thing twice.

Some things disappear the moment you notice them.

Some things don't.

Recommended Experience

For the intended experience:

- Play with headphones.

- Keep the volume high.

- Avoid spoilers.

- Don't increase the game's brightness.

The less you know...

The better.
