Mine an entire vein with a single block and automatically smelt raw materials into ingots!

To use this feature, you have to sneak while mining an ore. If you don't sneak, you can mine ores just like normal!

This add-on keeps achievements enabled!

NEW v1.1: Sneak while breaking stone to get stone instead of cobblestone!
This feature also works for deepslate!

Works with all ores and pickaxes; enchantments like Fortune or Silk Touch are also applied!
