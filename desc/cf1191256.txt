7 commands in the mod:

/jdl disable

/jdl enable

/jdl inc <num>

/jdl dec <num>

/jdl reset

/mode mobile

/mode default

Light Levels are now shown in items as lores

<num> can be any number

inc is to increment the light levels

dec is to decrement the light levels

To use mobile mode: click the interact button 2 times to swap offhand
