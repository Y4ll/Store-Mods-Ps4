Random Tick Speed Controller

This add-on is simple but powerful tool that allows players to control the Random Tick Speed of their Minecraft world in real-time and you can get achievements.

Want crops to grow faster?
Need leaves to decay instantly?
Want to slow down time for farming or mob farms?

Now you can do it with just one item the TickSpeed Setting.

Features

- Custom Tick Speed Menu Easily adjust the world's tick speed using a clean UI.

- Boost Mode Temporarily increase tick speed for a few seconds, great for farming!

- Auto-Revert System Automatically restores previous tick speed after boost ends.

- Smart Memory Remembers your last used tick speed for convenience.

- Achievement You can earn Achievement

Compatibility

- Supports Bedrock Edition 1.20.x+

- Works in Survival, Creative, and Adventure Mode

How to Use

- Equip the TickSpeed Setting item.

- Right-click / Use it to open the control menu.

- Slide to set your desired Random Tick Speed.

- (Optional) Enable Boost Mode to set a temporary high-speed burst.

TickSpeed Controller is perfect for players who love automation, farming, redstone builds, or just having more control over their Minecraft worlds rhythm.

Enjoy a smoother and smarter survival experience!
