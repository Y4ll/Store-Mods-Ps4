"More TNT (rework)" is a rework for update "More TNT Addon" by CodanRaigen.

More TNT add-on adds more than 20 new explosives to the game! Molotovs, grenades, missiles, and even a black hole!

This add-on finally adds new explosive blocks that range from twice the power of your regular TNT to the massive nuclear blast.

Crafting

You can craft the new items using the recipe tab. Due to a bug, custom blocks recipes can't be viewed (Hopefully, this will be fixed in the future).

Blocks

There are a total of 12 blocks of new TNT added by this add-on. Some of the newly added TNT have differing fuse duration so keep that in mind. You need flint and steel to trigger all of them (except for the C4).

TNT x2, x4, x8

These are pretty self-explanatory, the power is multiplied by two, by four, or by eight.

Bedrock buster

This TNT has the special ability to blow up bedrock!

Cryocharge

This TNT has the special ability to freeze nearby entities, as well as freeze surrounding water into ice and lava to cobblestone.

Dynamite

This TNT has the special ability to turn into a cluster and destroy an even larger area

Napalm

This TNT is the polar opposite of the Cryocharge (mentioned above). It has the ability to set an area ablaze and continuously explode its area.

C4

This TNT cannot be lit by flint and steel, instead it can be activated by interacting with its detonator. The range of the detonator is 60x60x60 blocks, you can detonate this bomb from a safe distance, it will activate any C4 placed within that area! After a C4 is activated, it will sound an alarm to anyone nearby. The more C4 activated, the louder the alarm becomes.

## Nuke
This is by far the most destructive bomb in the previous version. Complete with a radioactive sickness effect from within the range of the blast and a dark mushroom cloud.

## Hydrogen bomb **not fully implemented**
The utmost destructive bomb that this add-on has to offer! It's three times more destructive than the nuke, it also has a white-out mushroom cloud. No radioactive sickness from this bomb but a 20-second fuse should allow you to escape in time

Sticky bomb

This is a normal TNT that is stuck to a cobweb when triggered. It sticks to any surface and won't be coming down.

Tesla Bomb

This is a powerful bomb that can generate lightning bolts in a medium-sized area. The destructive explosion will burn an area and the lightning bolts won't be helping either.

Throwable bombs

These are bombs that you can throw for yourself.

Throwing them

To throw them, simply aim and interact with the item as if you were throwing a snowball. Note that some of these bombs have varying weights so be careful when using them.
