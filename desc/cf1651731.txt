Mike Shader is a Render Dragon style shader for Minecraft Bedrock. Waving vegetation, a choice of custom skies, real fog and brighter fire.

No Vibrant Visuals needed. No MB Loader. No patched game. Import it and it works.

Requirements

NONE. THIS ONE RUNS ON PLAIN MINECRAFT BEDROCK.

This is the only shader on my page with no requirements at all. You do not need the Vibrant Visuals toggle, you do not need MB Loader, and you do not need a patched or modified game.

If Vibrant Visuals is not available on your device, or you did not want to patch your game just to run a shader, this is the one for you.

What this changes

Waving vegetation

Leaves, grass, flowers and crops all move in the wind with smooth animation. This is the biggest single change and the reason the GIFs below exist, because screenshots cannot show it.

Custom skies

You choose what goes above you. Aurora, soft clouds, or leave the vanilla sky alone. See the subpacks below.

Fog

Real atmospheric fog that gives distance actual depth instead of terrain just ending.

Fire

Fire burns brighter and moves more. Campfires, torches and lava all read as a live flame rather than a looping texture.

Wind

The whole world moves together rather than each plant animating on its own, so nothing looks like it is twitching in isolation.

Subpacks

Pick one when you activate the shader. You can switch any time.

- Aurora puts an aurora in the night sky.

- Clouds gives you a custom cloud layer.

- No custom sky keeps the vanilla sky and only applies the waving, fog and fire.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Mike Shader

- Pick your subpack. Aurora, Clouds, or No custom sky.

- That is it. Nothing else needs turning on.
