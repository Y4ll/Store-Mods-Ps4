Full Bright

Full Bright is an essential utility pack that completely eliminates darkness in Minecraft! This pack enhances visibility in all dark areas, letting you explore caves, mines, and the night with perfect clarity. No more struggling to see in dark environments - everything is bright and visible!

Important: You must enable Vibrant Visuals in your video settings for this pack to work properly!

Features

- Complete darkness removal

- Clear visibility in caves and underground areas

- Bright nights for easier exploration

- No need for torches to see

- Lightweight and performance-friendly
