Cataclysmical is a large content Add-On that adds various blocks, items, weapons, mobs, dungeons, bosses and even a new trinket mechanic. Cataclysmical aims to add a fun and hard challenge to Minecraft. Some mobs and bosses are intended to be hard and challenging.

## How to Install:
1. Download the .mcaddon file
2. Import into Minecraft
3. Activate both packs (behavior + resource)

experimental toggles are not required
