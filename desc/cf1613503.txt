## Ultimate Vein Capitator (Veinminer & Treecapitator)

**Ultimate Vein Capitator** is a highly-optimized, lightweight, and vanilla-friendly script-based addon for Minecraft Bedrock. It makes resource gathering a breeze by allowing you to mine entire veins of ores, chop down whole trees, or clear blocks instantlyall while simply sneaking!

Designed with performance in mind, this addon removes unnecessary custom mod blocks, focusing purely on vanilla compatibility with seamless integration for custom additions.

---

### Key Features

* **Treecapitator (Axe):** Chop down entire trees in one go! Fully supports all vanilla logs, leaves, giant mushrooms, and the brand-new **Poplar trees** (both logs and leaves).

* **Veinminer (Pickaxe):** Mines whole veins of vanilla ores (Coal, Iron, Lapis, Gold, Emerald, Diamond, Copper, Redstone, Nether Gold, Quartz, and Ancient Debris). Supports Fortune and Silk Touch enchantments perfectly!

* **Subpack Support:** Comes with 3 pre-configured subpacks**Treecapitator** (only axes), **Veinminer** (only pickaxes), and **Veincapitator** (all tools enabled: axe, pickaxe, shovel, hoe, shears, sword).

* **Performance First:** Re-written from scratch to fix recursive loop-break bugs, preventing game lag or freezes during massive chain-destructions.

* **Clean Vanilla Database:** No bloated custom mod/addon blocks. Purely optimized for vanilla Minecraft plus select custom tree variants (Poplar).

---

### How to Use

1. Equip the appropriate tool (Axe, Pickaxe, Shovel, etc.).

2. **Hold Sneak (Shift)** and break the block.

3. Watch the entire tree or ore vein break instantly and teleport the drops directly to you!

---

### Subpacks Included

* **Treecapitator Mode:** Active only for Axes (efficient woodcutting).

* **Veinminer Mode:** Active only for Pickaxes (efficient mining).

* **Veincapitator (Ultimate Mode):** Active for all tools, including Shovels (gravel/clay), Hoes, Swords (webs), and Shears (leaves/webs with Silk Touch).
