YGP! True Offhand Functionality

TRUE OFFHAND. TRUE POWER.

YGP! True Offhand Functionality brings a more powerful and flexible offhand experience to Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

Use Minecraft's real offhand equipment slot, quickly swap items between hands, carry supported items, use offhand Wind Charges, and boost while Elytra flying with Firework Rockets in your offhand.

Created by YGP Studio / YGP Minecraft.

FEATURES

Real Offhand

This addon uses Minecraft Bedrock's actual offhand equipment slot.

It is not a fake inventory slot or visual-only system.

Put supported items into your offhand and use the addon systems to manage them.

Quick Hand Swap

Quickly swap your selected hotbar item with your offhand item.

Double Sneak / Crouch quickly Swap hands

You can also use:

/scriptevent ygp:swap

The swap system works with swords, tools, food, fireworks, Wind Charges, torches, and many other supported items.

ELYTRA + OFFHAND FIREWORKS

Java-Inspired Firework Boosting

Fly with your Elytra while keeping Firework Rockets in your offhand.

You do not need to manually move the rocket into your main hand for the addon-supported boost system.

Survival How-To

-
Wear an Elytra.

-
Put Firework Rockets in your offhand.

-
Press jump two times.

-
Look up.

-
Press jump again to fly/boost.

-
You must already be gliding for the boost.

One activation = one Firework Rocket used.

This provides a Java-inspired offhand Firework experience using Bedrock-compatible functionality.

OFFHAND WIND CHARGE

Use Wind Charges from your offhand.

Put a Wind Charge into your offhand and activate the offhand-use system.

One activation one Wind Charge thrown one charge consumed.

You can also use:

/scriptevent ygp:use

The system is designed to prevent accidental repeated use or infinite consumption.

DYNAMIC OFFHAND LIGHTING

Carry supported light-emitting items in your offhand and get a Java-inspired handheld-light experience on Bedrock.

Supported light sources include:

Torch

Soul Torch

Lantern

Soul Lantern

The moving light follows the player while the supported light item remains in the offhand.

When the item is removed, the extra light is stopped.

Bedrock does not provide exactly the same dynamic-light system as Java Edition. YGP uses Bedrock-compatible functionality to provide the closest reliable experience.
