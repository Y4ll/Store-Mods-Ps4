System Requirements

RTX (BetterRTX mod):

Just an RTX graphics card is required to run this texture pack smoothly.

Deferred shaders on PC:

A GTX 1050 is recommended but not required. Performance depends on your world type and shader settings.

Deferred shaders on mobile:

A mid-range device is recommended. YSS SE Shaders is the recommended deferred shader for mobile. Performance may vary by device.

Vibrant Visuals:

Works on any device that supports Minecraft's Vibrant Visuals mode Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, PlayStation 4/5, Android, iOS, and PC. No additional mods needed.
