So basically this pack just gets rid of a bunch of useless stuff that makes your phone lag. like it removes hidden particles you dont even see and makes the textures way simpler.

This means your phone has less work to do so the game can run faster. ig it just helps your phone focus on the important stuff :)

This pack uses a custom script I made called 'pixel skipping'. It basically tells your phone not to draw stuff that's completely hidden behind other blocks, which frees up a ton of power.

It also untangles the game's code a little, so your phone can process things in a smarter order and stop those annoying lag spikes :)
