This Add-On combines aspects of Batman from DC Comics and Minecraft. Complete with different suits for the Bat himself and his allies. Numerous gadgets AND vehicles. Oh yeah, enemies too.

TL;DR, I added Batman gadgets, weapons, vehicles, suits, sidekick suits, enemies and villains into the game.

New Update Showcase Video:

Before starting, be sure to select between Hero ID or Enemy ID

ID Cards

The Hero ID enabled you to fully utilize Batman's entire arsenal of suits, gadgets, weapons and vehicles.
The Enemy ID makes you susceptible to being affected by the gadgets and other weapons.

Bat Family Gear

The "Bat Family" also comes equipped with their unique weapon. Each weapon has its own unique attack.

Red Hood Deagles

(thank you to @TheF1rst on Discord for the texturing!)

Red Hood's Desert Eagles are easily one of the most dangerous weapons in the add-on. You have 10 shots before needing to reload.

Nightwing Escrima Sticks

Nightwing's Escrima Sticks can be thrown and retrieved for long ranged targets. They also have a chance to give slowness effects to targets hit with melee attacks.

Red Robin Staff

Red Robin's Staff is a decent weapon on it's own with a special attack. When the player right-clicks/interact tap, the player will slam the staff to the ground and knock back nearby enemies.

Robin Katana

A deadly sword given to Damien by his grandfather Ras Al Ghul. The sword itself does not have a special attack (for now) but it does deal high amounts of damage per hit.

Bat Family Offhand Gear Slots

You can place their gear in your offhand to keep them on the player body.
