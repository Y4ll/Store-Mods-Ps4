<mark class="marker-yellow">ADDON DESCRIPTION</mark>

Introducing AG's Morph Mobs, the ultimate Minecraft addon that unleashes your shapeshifting abilities like never before! With this groundbreaking addon, players can seamlessly transform into any Minecraft mob at their whim, opening up a whole new world of possibilities and adventures. <mark class="marker-yellow">OVER 70+ MOBS!</mark>

<mark class="marker-green">MORPH ITEMS </mark>

Morph Item

Hold OR right-click on a mob to morph into it! This item helps you to change into the mob by going near it and striking the item to transform into it. Take on the abilities and appearance of any creature!
