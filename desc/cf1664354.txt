Simplest Paxels combines the functions of a pickaxe, axe and shovel into one practical survival tool. Spend less time changing equipment and keep mining, chopping and digging with a single inventory slot.

The addon includes wooden, stone, copper, iron, golden, diamond and netherite paxels. Every tier follows familiar vanilla progression, with suitable durability, mining speed and enchantment support.

Paxels can mine the blocks handled by all three original tools. They can also strip logs and create dirt paths through normal item interaction, making them useful for building, exploration and everyday resource gathering.

Features

- Seven craftable paxel tiers
- Pickaxe, axe and shovel functionality in one tool
- Log stripping and path creation
- Vanilla-style survival progression
- Enchantment and durability support
