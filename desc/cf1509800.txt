OptiFPS is a next-generation FPS booster designed to give you maximum performance, smoother gameplay, and zero lag experience in Minecraft.

Built with inspiration from popular optimization mods like Sodium, OptiFine, and Lithium, OptiFPS combines the best ideas into one lightweight and powerful solution.

Features

Massive FPS Boost (even on low-end devices)

Smart optimization system (auto performance tuning)

Smooth gameplay for PvP & survival

Reduced lag spikes & stuttering

Lightweight & compatible with most mods

Perfect for low-end PCs and mobile setups
