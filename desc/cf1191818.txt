An addon that allows you to build structures with pixel-sized blocks in a single block.

How does it work?

To start, place one of the available voxel layers in the creative inventory.
Placing it on top of a block will bring up a grass block platform where you can start building with blocks of your choice from the creative inventory, including blocks from other addons.

Recommendations and other things you should know

- It works on mobile devices but can be very awkward to use (Split controls must be enabled in touch settings) (Crosshair)

- It is recommended to set the FOV to 50 or lower Settings/Video/FOV

- Lowering the camera sensitivity can improve accuracy when selecting blocks in the voxel.

- Some (uncommon) blocks and items will appear incorrectly rotated when placed (It will be fixed little by little in future updates)

- You can place anything even items like weapons and tools

- Some blocks such as plants and others may not be the same as the original and may be represented differently when placed.

- Water and lava blocks can be placed but they will not have physics.

- Cannot place mobs (For now)

- There are no limits when placing blocks in the voxel and you can go outside the blocks.

- If you place the voxel chunk underwater it will not represent the water blocks.

- Voxels can be pushed by water or pistons, if they get out of control remove them with the voxel eraser

- By looking at the voxel you will be able to see the selection box of the block you are selecting.

- Pay attention to the white cross in the selection box when trying to place a block as this will indicate where it will be placed.

Warning: This addon requires a high-performance device

This addon should only be used for entertainment purposes, the structures created should not be left as decoration as they can cause a lot of lag. Using the Voxel chunk may also cause lag.

Items:

- Voxel chunk: When placed, 16x16x16 blocks of the current chunk will be scanned and placed into the voxel.

- Small voxel layer: A platform of 8x8 pixel blocks will be placed.

- Medium voxel layer: A platform of 12x12 pixel blocks will be placed.

- Large voxel layer: A platform of 16x16 pixel blocks will be placed.

- Delete voxel: Interacting with a voxel will remove the entire voxel in the selected block.

- Delete all voxels: Using this will remove all voxels in the world.
