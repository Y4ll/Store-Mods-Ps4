Welcome to the world of Dear Family V3!

This custom life-simulation update brings your Minecraft Bedrock roleplays to life with new family dynamics, functional nursery furniture, and detailed character mechanics.

Reminder! This addon is still under maintenance; make sure to be alert for new updates ~ ~~

This addon includes 35 Items in total. Make sure to always have the Dear Phone Item in your inventory as it is essential for your roleplay to run smoothly.

- All entities are under the eggs arrays in minecraft

- All Items are under equipments array

- The rest can be search by names!

Family Life Cycle Life Stages: Experience family life with newborn babies, toddlers, children, teens, spouses, and grandparents!

Growth Timeline: Feed your baby milk to activate their growth system. It takes 2 full in-game days for a baby to grow into a toddler, 2 days to become a child, 2 days to reach teenhood, 4 days to grow into an adult, and 4 days to become a grandparent.

Growth Acceleration: Use growth pill to fasten their growth! Use stunted growth pill to pause their growth (Still under maintenance and is not working yet)

How to Tame Your Family?

- Taming Babies: Tame newborn babies using milk!

- Taming Other Entities: Use the Dear Phone item to bond with toddlers, children, teens, spouses, and grandparents.

-

Dear Phone Functions

- Teleportation: Instantly bring family members to your position.

- Health Check: Monitor the health status of your loved ones.

- Family Menu: Access special settings and interaction menus on demand.

Fashion customization

- 8 Clothing Styles: Dress your family in Casual, Academia, Sports, Pyjama, Costume, No Outfit, Dress, or Suits!

- Baby Wardrobe: Newborn babies feature 3 adorable outfits: Pyjama, Casual, and No Clothing.

- Spouse Customization: While clothes are fully functional for other entities, spouses feature full customization directly through the Dear Phone systemallowing you to change their skin tone, hair color, and outfits seam lessly!

Baby Care System Feeding & Movement:

Feeding milk is essential to initiate growth! Giving a baby their second bottle feed will allow them to crawl around (otherwise, they will start crawling naturally after 1 day).

Hygiene Care:

Babies get dirty very easily! Use Baby Wipes to wipe them clean and keep them happy.Work In Progress Items: Items like pacifiers, strawberry milk, stunted growth pills, and diapers are currently undergoing updates and do not function just yet.

Nursery Furniture Nursery Decor:

Set up your room with functional items like a Wardrobe, Crib, Baby Bathtub, Baby Table, Baby Highchair, Baby Twin Holder, and Baby Stroller! (Most furniture pieces are functional, while minor fixes are actively being worked on)
