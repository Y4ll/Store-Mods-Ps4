About DomeDrip

DomeDrip is a head cosmetic Add-On for Minecraft Bedrock, featuring a diverse collection of unique hats and accessories. From classic styles to legendary items, each cosmetic is designed to help players freely express their personal style.

With the Tier system (Basic, Plus, Epic, Artifact, Legendary), you can easily recognize how high the level of an item is.
