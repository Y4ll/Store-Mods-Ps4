Fantasy Ores is an addon for Minecraft Bedrock that incorporates real-life minerals into the game, featuring impressive 3D armor designs, each unique and different from the others.

Minerals will be generated starting from layer -20, except for aquamarine, which will be generated in the oceans.

- The diamond drill will be used to collect all the minerals from the addon.

- The crafting of the armors will be the same as in vanilla, but replacing the materials with those from the addon.

- Shields will be crafted as follows, and in the addon, there are spinel and opal shields.

- Both the trident and the spear will have the same crafting recipes with their respective minerals.

IMPORTANT

The add-on does NOT need experimental options, feel free not to activate them!
Means that it also works in preview versions.
