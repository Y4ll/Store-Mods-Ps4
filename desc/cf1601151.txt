Minecraft, But Block Drops are Random and Multiplied...

Break any block and receive a completely random item instead of its normal drop! Every new world generates its own unique block-to-item mapping, so you'll never know what you'll get until you start mining.

In one world, dirt might drop diamonds. In another, stone could drop boats, buckets, or enchanted gear. Once a block is assigned an item in a world, it will always drop that same item for the rest of your playthrough, allowing you to learn and adapt to your world's random loot table.

Perfect for challenge runs, speedruns, and chaotic survival experiences!

Features

- Every block has a random drop.

- Different random mappings for every new world.

- Consistent drops within the same world.

- Works in Survival and Multiplayer.

- Lightweight with no setup required.

Can you beat Minecraft when every block drops something completely unexpected?
