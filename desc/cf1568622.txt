Installation Guide

- Scroll down and download the Skin Pack.

- Open the downloaded file using ZArchiver or any file manager app.

- Tap Open With and choose Minecraft.

- Wait for Minecraft to import the Skin Pack automatically.

- Open Dressing Room or Change Skin.

- Navigate to the imported Skin Pack.

- Select any Spider-Man skin you want to use.

- Start your adventure and enjoy your new look.
