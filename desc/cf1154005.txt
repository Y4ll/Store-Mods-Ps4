When starting a new world you will be given a Guide Book to help you navigate your way through this new dead world, you can get useful information as well as change some settings to make your game as comfortable as possible.

Don't worry if you lose your Guide Book, you can easily craft a new one.

Zombies will grace every biome of your world, killing them will get you some well needed loot, when all Structures have been looted, killing Zombies will be your Survival.One of the items they can drop is Titanium Scrap, these can be crafted into Titanium Ingots which will be used to create an array of items later on.

As well as your hunger you will need to keep an eye on your Hydration and Energy levels, these will be shown on your action bar.
When you get attacked by a Zombie (which you will trust me) you will get a little Toxicity, this will also be shown on your action bar.

Hydration can be managed by drinking from Water Bottles or Cans of Drink found in Structures.

Fill your Water Bottle from any Water Source.

Purify your Water by heating it on a Campfire to make it safe to Drink.

Energy will fall faster then your Hydration or Hunger as your gonna be doing a lot of running. You can keep your Energy at a healthy level by sleeping but if that is not an option you can take some medication until you can find time to sleep.

Take care when jumping around as taking fall damage will hurt you legs, if this is to happen you can heal yourself by using a Leg Brace.

You can find Rags in Structures or craft them yourself

Eating is obviously one of the most important things we all need to do and being in a Zombie Apocalypse is no different, food can be found in all the usual ways like killing innocent animals but you can also find food in loot blocks in Structures. All food has an expiry time, raw food will go bad fast but cooking it will allow it to be kept for longer.

If you have played this add-on before you will know that inventory management is a nightmare, items that have no use right now will need to be sacrificed for more important items like food, medication and weapons but with a Backpack those decisions will no longer need to be made.

Backpacks can be found in loot blocks in Structures but can also be crafted.

When you die and trust me you will! Remember all your hard earned loot will be safe in a Gravestone marked with your name. The Gravestones are not locked so any player that comes across your Gravestone will be able to access it.

Structures will generate all over your world from small houses to massive factories, looting these Structures will be your main way of survival as they will contain tons of leftover loot, interact with all the custom loot blocks for a chance of food, weapons and other useful items.

When looting Structures you may come across a Movie Poster or two, these are collectable items. Movie Posters are like Music Discs rare to find and not much use but somewhere in the back of your mind you have the urge to find them all.
