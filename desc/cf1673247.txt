WHAT IS POKEFUN BEGINNER'S GUIDE?

POKEFUN Beginner's Guide is a community-focused guide addon for Minecraft Bedrock players who want to learn how to get started with a Pokemon multiplayer survival experience.

The guide provides information about important gameplay systems, server features, Pokemon-related mechanics, multiplayer activities and useful commands.

This project is designed to make it easier for new players to understand the available features and begin playing without needing to learn everything at once.
