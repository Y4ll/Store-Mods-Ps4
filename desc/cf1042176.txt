Torches but now in your favorite color!

With Vibrant Visuals enabled, the light color changes depending on the item. For example, a Redstone Torch emits red light.

This isn't limited to torches held in your hand, placed colored torches also emit their own colored light.

Dynamic Light also works underwater using the new Sea Torch and other light-emitting items such as lanterns and glowing blocks.

The Sea Torch is the only torch that can be placed underwater.
