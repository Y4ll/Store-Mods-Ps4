Orbital Strike Cannon: Authentic adds five devastating weapons to Minecraft Bedrock: the Nuke Shot, Stab Shot, Wolf Shot, Arrow Cannon, and Wither Shot.

Go check out the original: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft-bedrock/addons/nikolais-orbital-strike-cannon/comments

Or: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft-bedrock/addons/remote-stasis-chamber-addon

The Nuke Shot fires a precision orbital marker in the exact direction you're aiming. On impact, it detonates a massive ring explosion that obliterates everything in its path.

The Stab Shot sends a vertical column of destruction straight through the world from bedrock to the sky, destroying anything in its way.

The Wolf Shot calls down a pack of wolves onto your target customise how many, their effects, and even their names.

The Arrow Cannon rains a concentrated barrage of arrows from the sky, with adjustable concentration, spread, and range. Optional exploding arrows included!

The Wither Shot unleashes a massive barrage of explosive wither skulls that spread out into a ring formation and explode on impact - tearing through terrain, obsidian, and even water and lava. More destructive than Nuke, but less powerful.

Every weapon is fully configurable via the Settings item: control who can fire, toggle craftability, tune damage and concentration, and dive into a huge customisation menu. Try the built-in presets - Fun Mode, MAX Mode, and Optimised Mode - to reconfigure everything in one tap, and use the dedicated Optimisation menu to keep things smooth on any device. Or toggle new options like arrow audio or death sound effects.

Craft each rod at a crafting table, or use the Settings/Creative inventory item to give them directly. Requires Holiday Creator Features and Beta APIs enabled (on some devices)
