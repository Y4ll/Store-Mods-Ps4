Equinox RD

Equinox RD is a balanced shader for Minecraft Bedrock Edition designed to enhance the game's visuals while maintaining smooth performance on a wide range of devices.

Features

- 1.16-style dynamic shadows

- Volumetric clouds

- Realistic water

- ACES Filmic Tonemapping

- Balanced visuals and performance

- Highly optimized

- Smooth day and night lighting

- Beautiful sunrise and sunset colors

Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition

- Shader Editor App

- Android compatible

Installation

- Download the shader.

- Launch Minecraft Bedrock Edition (Using MB Loader or Any Mbl Launcher)

- Enable the shader and Enjoy!

Performance

Equinox RD is designed to provide high-quality visuals while remaining lightweight enough for everyday gameplay on supported devices.
