PVP ESSENTIALS

CLEANER GAMEPLAY BETTER VISIBILITY BETTER PVP

ALL-IN-ONE PVP TEXTURE PACK

PvP Essentials combines multiple useful PvP resource packs into one complete package.

Instead of activating many separate packs, you can use a single pack designed to provide a cleaner screen, better visibility and a smoother PvP experience.

The pack focuses on removing unnecessary visual clutter while keeping the important parts of Minecraft easy to see during combat.

INCLUDED FEATURES

Custom Crosshair
Clean crosshair designed to make aiming easier during PvP.

Java-Style Hitbox
Java-inspired entity hitboxes with a clean white appearance.

Low Fire
Reduces the amount of your screen covered while burning.

Small Totem
Smaller Totem of Undying for improved combat visibility.

Small Shield
Reduces the first-person shield size so it blocks less of your view.

Reduced Particles
Removes unnecessary particles for cleaner fights and better visibility.

Clear Water & Lava
Improves visibility while looking through water and lava.

Transparent UI
Transparent inventory and container interfaces for better awareness.

White Block Outline
Cleaner white selection outline when targeting blocks.

No Pumpkin Blur
Removes the pumpkin overlay and keeps your screen clear.

Enchantment Hider
Reduces distracting enchantment-related visual elements.

HUD Hider
Helps remove unnecessary HUD elements for a cleaner screen.

Fast Craft + Quick Loot
Useful PvP-oriented interface improvements for faster interactions.

MADE FOR PVP

PvP Essentials does not try to completely redesign Minecraft. Its purpose is to improve the parts of the game that matter during combat.

Low fire keeps your opponent visible, smaller items free up screen space, transparent interfaces improve awareness, and the custom crosshair and hitboxes help keep your focus on the fight.
