Monster Tank WW2 Addon

Welcome to the Monster Tank WW2 Addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition! This add-on brings highly detailed, realistic World War II heavy armor and immersive combat mechanics straight into your world.

Note: This project is currently Under Active Development. Features and content will continue to be added and polished in upcoming updates.

Features & Highlights

- German Heavy Tanks: Features iconic WWII German heavy tank models, including the new Auff. C and the powerful Sturm Tiger heavy mortar tank (other factions, light, and medium vehicles will be added in future updates).

- Universal Armament: Every tank series now comes equipped with an integrated Machine Gun (MG) for anti-infantry and secondary defense.

- Advanced Aiming Systems: Integrated Laser Assist and Aim Assist targeting systems to enhance precision firing while on the move or at long range.

- WOTB-Style Driving: Vehicle movement and driving mechanics inspired by World of Tanks Blitz.

- Realistic Tank Mechanics: Advanced reloading systems, ammunition swapping, dynamic target locking, and tactical adrenaline capabilities.

- Custom Animations: Detailed and realistic animations for tracks, gun barrels, and vehicle movement.

- Multiplayer Ready: Test and engage in armored combat scenarios with friends on your server.

Work in Progress (WIP) Status

- Audio System: Custom sound effects (engine, firing, track sounds) are actively being refined and will be updated continuously.

- More Factions & Vehicles: Additional nations, vehicle classes, balance tweaks, and armor mechanics are currently under development.

How to Use & Controls

-
Mounting the Tank:

- Enter the tank to take control. Driving mechanics function similarly to World of Tanks Blitz.

-
Targeting & Assistance (Laser & Aim Assist):

- Laser Assist: Use the visual laser guide to line up precision shots over distance.

- Aim Assist & Target Lock: Lock onto moving targets easily using the Target Designator to keep your main gun on target while driving.

-
Loading Ammunition (AP / HE):

- Keep either AP (Armor-Piercing) or HE (High-Explosive) ammo in your inventory.

- Hold the ammo item to start reloading. An action bar notification will automatically display your reload progress.

- Switch between AP and HE shells anytime (triggers corresponding reload time).

- Adrenaline Rush: A built-in feature that speeds up reload time during critical combat moments!

-
Firing the Main Cannon / Mortar:

- Once fully loaded, switch to the Fire item and hold it to unleash your main cannon or deliver devastating high-explosive mortar shells (Sturm Tiger).

-
Secondary Armament (Machine Guns):

- Available across all tank series. Use the Machine Gun item to unleash continuous suppressive fire.
