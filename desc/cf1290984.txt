This bike models are evolutions of my modeling style, choosing less cubes and more detail to be portrayed through the texture. This Add-On is well optimized, using only around 100 cubes per bike. Thank you to JannisX11 for entity wizard base vehicle code. The bikes fit one driver, and the speed is low enough to be controlled on mobile easily.

This Addon utilizes my most up to date code, with working sounds and bike climbing animations, alongside fork steering angle corrections that I was previously unable to make work (Thank you to sk8torad3: https://mcpedl.com/user/sk8torad3/ for helping me fix the angles)
