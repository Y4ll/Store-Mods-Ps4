X-Ray With Night Vision

X-ray With Night vision is the second variant of the Advanced X-Ray Mod, created based on community demand for an integrated Night Vision feature. This version enhances the original X-Ray experience by allowing players to clearly see ores and underground structures even in dark environments.

This mod is designed to provide a clean, efficient, and achievement-friendly X-Ray experience while maintaining stable performance in your game.

Features

Integrated Night Vision

- Easily see ores and underground caves even in complete darkness.

Enhanced Ore Visibility

- Blocks surrounding valuable ores are hidden to make mining more efficient.

Optimized Performance

- Lightweight design ensures minimal performance impact.

Clean Visual Experience

- Clear underground view with minimal visual clutter.

Important Settings (Required)

To ensure ores are visible while using the mod:

Turn OFF Smooth Lighting

If Smooth Lighting is enabled, ores may not render correctly and could become invisible.

Important Note - You must use mbLoader/Minecraft Patch/BetterRenderDragon In order to make Night Vision work.

How to Disable Smooth Lighting ?

- Open Settings

- Go to Video / Graphics Settings

- Find Smooth Lighting

- Set it to OFF

After changing the setting, reload the world if necessary.
