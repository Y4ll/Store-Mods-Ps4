Economy & Shops

Latest Version: v1.3

Economy & Shops adds a complete economy system to Minecraft Bedrock, designed for survival worlds, multiplayer servers, roleplay and custom economies.

Manage your money through a banking system, use physical cash, create shops, run your own Player Shop, transfer money between players and manage loans, bills, debts and more.
