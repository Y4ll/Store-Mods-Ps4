but fully original this addon does NOT use or copy anything from the original mod. Only the portal concept is inspired; the rest uses completely different blocks, mechanics, and bosses

Quest Book

When you join the world, you will automatically receive a Quest Book.

The book guides you through the addon and explains how to Play

New Quest Advancements Pop-up

Twilight Guardian

Your journey begins by crafting the Twilight Guardian Altar.

Place the altar and offer a Diamond to summon the Twilight Guardian.

Defeat the Guardian to obtain the Twilight Abyss Diamond.

Twilight Gurdian Armour & Sword

Portal Light Up

Defeat the Guardian to obtain the Twilight Abyss Diamond

- Dig a 22 hole (4 blocks deep), fill it with water

- You can decorate it using flowers

- Then craft the Twilight Abyss Diamond

- Hold it and click ONLY on the water bottom block (not the sides!) to activate the portal

Twilight Abyss Biomes

The dimension features infinite world generation with no limited islands.

Umbra Birch Biome

A mysterious forest biome filled with strange creatures and dark exploration areas.

Natural mobs:

- Big Sheep

- Deer

- Blue Goblin

Maple Biome

A colorful maple-themed biome with its own unique wildlife.

Natural mobs:

- Wild Pig

- Chill Mouse

- Brahma Chicken

Natural Structures:

Explore 12 unique structures across the two biomes.

- 6+ structures in the Umbra Birch biome

- 6+ structures in the Maple biome

These structures contain dangerous encounters and special rewards.

New Hostile Mobs

The addon introduces 3 new hostile mobs and 2 custom Zombie variants.

These enemies can be found spawning from Structure Spawners, making exploration much more dangerous.

Crypt Weaver

A strange one-eyed spider-like creature with a funny but creepy chasing animation.

Spectral Seeker

A ghost-like enemy capable of throwing dangerous projectiles at players.

Forgotten Knight

A powerful armored knight equipped with a shield. Be careful when fighting it!
