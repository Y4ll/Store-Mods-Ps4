DG Chunk Loader Keep Your Farms Running Anywhere (Supports Iron Farm, Gold Farm)

Have you ever built a farm, planted your seeds, then gone mining or exploring... only to return and realize that nothing has grown and your farms didnt work at all?

Well, that problem ends here

Introducing the DG Chunk Loader Addon. a simple but powerful tool that keeps your farms active even when youre far away

Special Thanks To @tunekeshi2304 For Letting Me Use His Codes.

Key Features:

Keeps crops growing and farms working even if you're not nearby.
Unique Feature: Works with Iron Farms, which most other chunk loader addons dont support.

How To Use:

1. Activate the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack
2. In world settings, enable "Beta APIs" under Experiments:

3. Open an existing world or create a new one
4. Craft the DG Chunk Loader using the recipe shown below:

5. Place it near any build or farm that you want to stay active

Thats it! Now your farms will keep working, and seeds will continue growing no matter where you go

Important Note:

Do not place too many chunk loaders doing so may cause glitches or lag

How To Use With Iron Farms:

Just place the DG Iron Farm Loader block 3-4 blocks above water inside your iron farm
For extra safety, place a leaf block on top sometimes iron golems may spawn on it

Now, even if youre far away, your iron farm will still produce iron

(Make sure you place the Chunk Loader near the farm first)
