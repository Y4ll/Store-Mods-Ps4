Skylix Visuals is my open sky pack. Warm sunlight, teal water and haze in the distance that makes the world feel like it keeps going past what you can see.

What this changes

Atmospheric depth

Soft haze builds up over distance, so far hills sit further back than near ones instead of everything stacking at the same depth. This is what makes the world feel big and it is the whole idea behind the pack.

Open sky

The sky is given room to breathe, with sharp cloud rendering that keeps clouds defined even far overhead. Standing on a hill and just looking out is what this pack is for.

Sunlight

Warm natural light across terrain and buildings. Nothing is tinted or graded hard, the sun just behaves the way you would expect it to.

Water

Clear water in natural teal tones rather than the deep blue most of my packs go for. Shallows and rivers especially look different here.

Foliage

Lush greens with detail held in the grass, so open fields do not flatten out when you look across them.

Block contrast

Wood, stone and sand are separated properly from each other. Builds read clearly instead of blending into the terrain around them.

Requirements

THIS PACK IS FOR VIBRANT VISUALS ONLY
IT DOES NOT WORK WITH MB LOADER

Vibrant Visuals must be ON. This pack is built for Bedrock's Vibrant Visuals renderer. The pack will not do anything if it is off.

Where to turn it on: Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals

If that toggle is not there, your device or version does not support Vibrant Visuals yet.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Skylix Visuals

- Turn on Settings > Video > Vibrant Visuals

- If the lighting still looks the same, leave the world and load it again once

FAQ

The pack is not working. Nothing changed after I installed it.
I get this comment a lot, and almost every single time the reason is the same. Vibrant Visuals is turned off.

This pack only works when Vibrant Visuals is ON. That is written clearly at the top of this page. With it off, the pack loads fine but nothing on your screen changes, because there is no renderer for it to work with. The pack is not broken.

Go to Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals and switch it on. If you cannot find that option at all, your device or Minecraft version does not support it yet.

Please check this before commenting that the pack does not work.
