Maces Overhaul: Legendary Impacts

Elevate your combat experience with Maces Overhaul, a premium Bedrock Addon that introduces five powerful thematic maces. Each weapon features the "Smash" mechanic, where damage, knockback, and special abilities scale dynamically based on your fall height!

The Legendary Arsenal

Thunder Mace: Summon delayed lightning bolts that strike the ground around your target.

- Blaze Mace: Incinerate enemies with scaling fire duration and explosive fiery blasts.

- Demon Mace: A dark-themed weapon that inflicts Wither II and releases dense, dark-gray smoke.

- Diamond Mace: The ultimate defensive weapon. Grants the wielder Resistance and Absorption based on impact intensity.

- Rascal Mace: Steal the momentum! Inflicts heavy Slowness on enemies while granting you Speed II.

Advanced Mechanics

- Always-On Abilities: Unlike vanilla, unique effects trigger on every hit, but their potency and duration scale massively with your fall distance.

- Cinematic Visuals: Each mace features custom, high-quality geometry particles designed for maximum spectacle.

- Impact Physics: Area-of-effect knockback pushes nearby enemies away from the impact site.

Mace Records System

Craft the Mace Records Book (1 Book + 1 Gold Ingot) to access a custom UI menu.

- Global Records: Track the highest "Power Intensity" ever achieved in your world.

- Detailed Stats: View the player name, exact damage, fall height, and even the target type of the world-record hit.

- Announcements: Breaking a record triggers a global server-wide celebration message!
