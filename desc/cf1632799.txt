Flashlight: Dynamic Beam

Bring realistic portable lighting to Minecraft Bedrock with Flashlight: Dynamic Beam. This addon adds a fully functional flashlight that produces a dynamic light source, making caves, abandoned structures, forests, and nighttime exploration far more immersive.

Unlike simple decorative flashlights, this addon features a complete battery system that encourages strategic use while keeping gameplay balanced.

Features

- Functional flashlight with ON/OFF toggle

- Dynamic light that follows the player while holding the flashlight

- Rechargeable battery system

- Real-time battery indicator displayed on the action bar

- Automatic recharge when the flashlight is turned off

- Cooldown after the battery is fully depleted

- Realistic click sound effects when switching modes

- Survival crafting recipe included

- Compatible with modern Minecraft Bedrock versions

- Perfect for caves, mining, exploration, horror maps, adventure worlds, and survival gameplay

Battery System

The flashlight uses energy while it is turned on.

- Battery slowly drains during use.

- Turn the flashlight off to automatically recharge it.

- When the battery reaches 0%, the flashlight shuts off automatically.

- After completely draining the battery, a 30-second cooldown prevents immediate reuse.

- The action bar displays battery percentage, recharge progress, and cooldown status in real time.

This creates a balanced experience that feels immersive without being overpowered.

Crafting Recipe

Craft your flashlight using simple survival materials:

- Glass Pane

- Glowstone Dust

- Iron Ingot
