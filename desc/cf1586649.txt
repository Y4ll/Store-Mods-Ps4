Welcome to the mod update! Do you hear the whistling?

Terrifying music tracks and scary foggy atmospheres have been added, along with the bloody fog. Some new creatures have been added, and each one has its own unique terrifying behavior which has become even more horrifying. Its no longer limited to just chasing; theres also psychological terror and mysterious atmospheres before the chase. You might be deceived by some creatures that look like the player but are infected and can kill you, so stay careful!

The Whistle Mod adds a variety of unnatural and disturbing creatures that will haunt your world. You will encounter heavily glitched and broken cows with strange behavior, along with horrifying entities that stretch, deform, and move in unnatural and terrifying ways.

Every sound and every shadow might hide something dangerous. Are you brave enough to explore your world after hearing the whistle?

This is a true analog-style horror experience for Minecraft Bedrock.

This is The Whistle Mod!
