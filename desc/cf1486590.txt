Content Overview

Mining & Smelting

Begin your Titanium journey by exploring the Overworld and discovering a new high-tier resource.

- Titanium Ore generates naturally throughout the Overworld.

- Deepslate Titanium Ore generates at lower depths.

- Mine Titanium Ore and Deepslate Titanium Ore with a Netherite, Titanium, or Titanium+ Pickaxe to obtain Raw Titanium.

- Process Raw Titanium and related materials into Unrefined Titanium Scrap, then smelt it in a Blast Furnace into Refined Titanium Ingots.

- Titanium Shards are rare Epic boss drops featuring an enchanted glint and a stack size of 16.

- Titanium Ore and Deepslate Titanium Ore no longer have direct crafting recipes.

This creates a meaningful post-Netherite progression path instead of allowing high-tier materials to be obtained instantly.

Titanium Workbench

The Titanium Workbench is the heart of Titanium progression.

It is a dedicated crafting station a custom crafting table with its own tag that emits light and produces a weak redstone signal.

Use the Workbench to craft and process advanced Titanium components and equipment while keeping Titanium progression separate from ordinary vanilla crafting.

Titanium is designed to feel like a genuine progression system not a collection of instantly obtainable overpowered items.

Titanium Spear Jab & Charge

The Titanium Spear introduces a new combat style to the Titanium arsenal, combining precise close-range attacks with powerful high-speed charges.

Its custom 3D model, held-item attachable, inventory icon, localization, and crafting integration are designed to make the spear feel like a native part of the add-on.
