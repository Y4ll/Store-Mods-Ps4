Copper Coil

Copper Coil is a block that, when powered with redstone, generates an electric field that deals damage to any nearby entity.

Water Conductivity:If in contact with water, it energizes the liquid in a radius based on redstone strength.

Conductive Damage:Entities in electrified water wearing metallic armor (Iron, Gold, Netherite) take 50% increased damage.

Overcharge:Can be used to charge Creepers.
