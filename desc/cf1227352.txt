Nico's Item Expansion is an add-on that focuses on adding useful, convenient, and suitable items for your survival game. It introduces more food, weapons, armor, and recipes for previously uncraftable items. Additionally, this mod adds a new crafting table, evaporator, and pots.

(v1.14.5)
