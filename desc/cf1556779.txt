Survival Mode 32k Gear

This add-on adds fully enchanted netherite level, diamond level, and other tools/armor to your world through a simple craftable item.

Best use case (imo): Download, apply to your world or realm to gear up, then remove the addon so no one else can use it.

The Guidebook is used to obtain the items:
Crafting Recipe: Book + Diamond

Open the guidebook to choose a kit:

- 32K Illegal (Netherite) full netherite gear with incompatible enchant combos at level 32,767

- 32K Illegal (Diamond) full diamond gear with incompatible enchant combos at level 32,767

- Max Vanilla Illegal (Netherite) full netherite gear with incompatible enchant combos at normal vanilla max levels

- Max Vanilla Illegal (Diamond) full diamond gear with incompatible enchant combos at normal vanilla max levels

- 32K with Thorns (Netherite) full netherite 32K gear with Thorns 32,767 on all armor pieces

- 32K with Thorns (Diamond) full diamond 32K gear with Thorns 32,767 on all armor pieces

- 32K Enchanted Books every enchantment available as a book at level 32,767

- 32K Doors 32K enchanted doors

- Modified 32K Tools selectively tuned 32K gear: bows with reduced Punch (Level 2), a diamond sword with no Knockback, and a diamond pickaxe with Fortune 100 (all other enchants at 32K)

FYI: Crossbows do not work with high levels of Quick Charge so those enchants have been limited to 3

A chest spawns at your feet containing the items. The enchantments are at level 32,767 for most options and are the real values, not engine-capped. Avoid spawning the new chest next to an existing chest (It will make a double chest with no items!)
