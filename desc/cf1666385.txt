ChainBreaker: Ultimate Vein Miner & Tree Capitator

A modern, lightweight, and highly optimized Minecraft Bedrock addon that brings seamless Vein Mining and Tree Capitator mechanics to your world, without disabling Xbox Achievements or Trophies.

Mine entire ore veins, chop down massive trees, harvest crops, and clear webs in a single strike, powered by a custom lag-free queue engine designed for peak performance.

Achievement Friendly: does not require any experimental toggles. Keep earning Xbox Achievements and PlayStation Trophies in your survival worlds.

Key Features

- 100% Achievement Friendly: no experimental toggles needed, works out of the box in survival worlds.

- Vein Mining: mine entire connected ore veins instantly with any pickaxe.

- Tree Capitator: fell whole trees, including leaves, roots, and mushroom stems, with a single axe chop.

- Per-Player In-Game Config: each player can toggle features and customize block limits individually.

- Interactive GUI Settings Menu: accessible anytime through the custom Settings Stick.

- Multi-Tool Support: works with pickaxes, axes, swords, hoes, shovels, and shears.

- Enchantment Compatible: fully supports Fortune and Silk Touch, with drops and XP teleported straight to you.

- Tag-Based Auto Detection: native vanilla support plus generic support for custom modded trees/ores via block tags.

- Zero Lag Architecture: built on a Breadth-First Search queue engine that processes blocks smoothly per tick without crashing or lagging servers.

How to Use

Activate Chain Mining

- Hold any supported tool (pickaxe, axe, etc).

- Sneak (crouch) and break a block.

- All connected matching blocks break automatically, with loot and XP teleported straight to your location.

Open the Settings GUI Menu

- New players automatically receive a ChainBreaker Settings stick on first join.

- Right-click/long-press/use the stick to open the interactive UI menu.

Creating or Renaming the Settings Stick

Lost your stick, or playing on a dedicated server? You can make a new one anytime:

- Place a regular Stick into an Anvil.

- Rename it to ChainBreaker Settings (or any name containing "ChainBreaker").

- Cost: 1 XP level.

- Right-click/use the renamed stick to open the menu.

In-Game Configuration Options

In the Settings Menu, each player can independently customize:

- Vein Mining Toggle: enable or disable pickaxe vein mining.

- Tree Capitator Toggle: enable or disable axe tree cutting.

- Block Limit Slider: set the max blocks broken per chain (16 to 512 blocks).

- Tool Toggles: enable/disable secondary tool support (sword, hoe, shovel, shears).

- Reset Defaults: restore all settings to default values.

Installation

- Download ChainBreaker_v1.0.0.mcaddon and open/double-click it to import into Minecraft Bedrock.

- Go to World Settings, then Behavior Packs, and activate ChainBreaker.

- No experimental toggles required. Uses the Bedrock Stable Script API 1.11.0+, keeping your world achievement friendly.

Compatibility

- No experimental toggles needed, safe for achievement-enabled worlds.

- Works with vanilla ores/trees natively, plus custom modded trees/ores through block tags.

- BFS-based queue engine processes chains per tick to avoid server lag, even on large veins or forests.
