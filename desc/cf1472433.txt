KeepInventory - by Mikayil022

Activates the vanilla Keep Inventory mechanic without enabling cheats. Fully survival-compatible and achievement-friendly.

- Once the add-on is activated in your world, Keep Inventory will be permanently enabled.

- Use /ki:keepinventory <true/false> in-game to enable or disable Keep Inventory.

- Only operators or players tagged "admin" can use the command

- Compatible with Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.100 and all newer versions.
