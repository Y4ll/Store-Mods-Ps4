Whatever you do in one chunk gets mirrored to the others!

Place one block. Every chunk in the world places it too. Break a tree, and it falls in every chunk. Light a nether portal, and the whole world lights up. Blow a crater with TNT, and the same crater appears everywhere, forever, in every direction.

Every change you make repeats across the world every 16 blocks and it never stops. Travel 10,000 blocks and the terrain there is already yours.

Techical informations:

Recommended: set Simulation Distance to 812 in world settings. Minecraft can only place blocks in chunks that are loaded, so a higher setting means more copies appear around you at once. (higher value may lag your phone or device, high-end phones or devices can set to max for better visibility)

Good to know

- Copies in far chunks appear as you travel to them that is the engine's limit, not the pack's. Nothing is lost; it is stamped in the moment the chunk loads.

- Chest contents do not copy. Chests replicate as empty blocks. This is deliberate: syncing inventories across thousands of copies duplicates items.

- Copies never drop items. Only the chunk you are standing in gives you blocks back, so the pack can't be used as an item duplicator.

- Naturally generated chests and structures are never copied only what you place, break or change.

- The pattern remembers the most recent 12,000 changes. Past that the oldest are forgotten.

- Paintings and armour stands are entities, not blocks, so they stay local.

Commands (Cheats must be on.)

Command - What it does
/scriptevent wce:status How many changes are remembered
/scriptevent wce:pause Stop copying (build freely)
/scriptevent wce:resume Start again
/scriptevent wce:verify Clean up stale records around you
/scriptevent wce:reset Forget the pattern blocks already placed stay
