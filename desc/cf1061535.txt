This addon adds a feature where, if you chop the base of a tree while crouching/sneaking, all the logs above it will fall down.

As you chop the lower part of the tree, it gradually thins out until it finally breaks.

Works with any type of trees.

The bigger the tree, the longer it takes to chop down.

Added Poplar Trees Support.

Tree Chopper only works with trees.

However, if the addon is added to an existing world, it may affect pre-existing buildings. To prevent any accidental damage, a protection system has been implemented.

After 5 seconds, cut log layers will start to regenerate, restoring 1 layer every 5 seconds until they return to full normal logs.

This addon does not disable achievements
