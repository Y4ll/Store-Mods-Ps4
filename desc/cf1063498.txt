Beharvior & Resources Packs Needed

This addon does not disable achievements.

Sculkite gear is slightly weaker and slower than diamond, but very useful for mining and XP farming.

Full Armor: Sneaking reveals ores in a 4x4 radius for a few seconds, cooldown and hunger cost in negative layers.

With 2 or more pieces: Grants immunity to Darkness and Warden sonic Attacks.

Effect on hit: Weakness.

On kill: Grant 1-4 extra XP orbs.

Speed: Mine deepslate and sculk faster than any tier.

On mine: Chance to grant XP.

Sculkite ore found mostly in the Deep Dark biomes and low Y-levels;
Must be smelted to obtain its ingot.

Breezine gear is nearly as fast as gold and moderately stronger than iron.

Any Piece: Reflect projectiles, with damage scaling to the amount of pieces equipped.

On Hit: Breezine Tools perform a wind charge attack with a 0.6-second cooldown.

Enderian gear is a good alternative to diamond.

Full Armor: Jump and keep sneaking until it summons an Ender Eye;
Do it again to teleport to its location.
Stopping sneaking cancels both timers.

With 2 or more pieces: Grants immunity to Slowness effect.

Effect on hit: Slowness.

Bundle: Enderian Tools can store items like bundles.

Dragon Forge gear is a solid end-game alternative to Netherite.

Full Armor: Grants a double jump in midair and prevents fall damage.

With 2 or more pieces: Grants fire resistance.

On Hit: The Dragon Forge mace is fiery, burning nearby mobs.

Shulkite gear is moderately better than iron and has even more durability than netherite!

Armor texture by Torrezx: CurseForge Shulker Armor Texture Pack

For every 2 pieces equipped: Summon a friendly Shulker bullet that will defend you.

With 2 or more pieces: Grants immunity to levitation effect.

On use/Right Click: Tools can manually summon Shulker Bullets to defend you

Bundle: Shulkite Tools can store items like bundles.

Beehive gear is moderately better than iron.

Any Piece: For every armor piece summons a bee to defend you.

With 2 or more pieces: Grants immunity to poison effect.

On Kill: Grant e few seconds of Regeneration.

On Mine: Chance to grant saturation.

With any piece: 15% chance per piece to deal damage like Thorns, damage increases with more pieces

With 2 or more pieces: Grant water breathing;

Each armor piece grants 2 extra hearts underwater, up to a full bar.

On Hit: Prismarite tools deal triple damage to mobs in water.

On Underwater: Prismarite tools grant conduit effect for mining and exploration!

Item: Prismarite gear is fire-proof, just like Netherite

Crafting follows the standard vanilla patterns for tools and armor.

English
Spanish
Portuguese

Current Limitations / Issues:
Animated items like breezine cannot display the glint effect when enchanted.
Tools that function like bundles may lose stored items when used in an anvil, so its recommended to empty them first.
The Shulkite Bundle can only be used comfortably from the inventory due to its use cooldown.
