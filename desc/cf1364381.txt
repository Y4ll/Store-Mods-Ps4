Enhanced FX is a resource pack that adds many different visual improvements, particle effects, and general quality of life changes, while still keeping the vanilla style of Minecraft. With this pack, many mobs recieve entirely new damaged textures, damage particles, ambient particles, glowing eyes, idle animations, sound effects, and even name tag easter eggs!

If you have any questions or find any issues with this pack, you can contact me in the comments or on Discord (lionhead07)
