Newb X Stars shaders are back. It's a new, customized variant of the Newb shader created by VeteranDev. This shader is based on the concept of the universe, which is why it's called Newb X Stars, and this is also reflected in the cool tones of the universe. The best thing about this shader is that it can run on low-end devices. Check it out! I promise you won't regret it.

Changes

- New Realistic auroras have been added to the sky.

- Better Clouds Reimagined and Clouds Lite.

- Stars identical to those in the Complementary Shader have been implemented.

- The white pixels in the textures have been removed.

- The lava has been improved for a more realistic appearance.

- Need Minecraft v26.20+ and MBLoader.
