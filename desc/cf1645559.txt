How To Use

Download the .maddon file

Tap/double-click it Minecraft will open and import it automatically

Wait for the "Successfully imported" message

Open Minecraft and go to Play

Create a new world or edit an existing one

Scroll down to Behavior Packs

Find Tree Capitator and tap the + to activate it

Achivement Friendly

Launch your world!

Features

- Works on any tree type oak, spruce, dark oak, jungle, acacia, birch, crimson/warped stems, even stripped logs

- Smart detection: only chops connected logs, leaves your builds and neighboring trees untouched

- Realistic durability: your axe takes damage per log chopped, just like breaking them normally

- Capped at 128 logs per tree so massive jungle/dark oak trees don't lag your world

- Creative mode friendly no drops, no durability loss

- Fully script-based, no behavior changes to vanilla trees when not crouching
