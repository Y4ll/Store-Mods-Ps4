000.jar

At first, everything seems normal.

You create a new world, start playing, and everything looks exactly as it should.

Until something appears that wasn't there before.

A structure in the distance.

A strange figure between the trees.

A house where there shouldn't be one.

And then you realize...

Something has entered your world.

Features

- Mysterious structures that can appear throughout the world.

- A bizarre red village unlike anything found in normal Minecraft.

- Supernatural entities that appear without warning.

- Strange dungeons hidden across the world.

- Abandoned and mysterious houses appearing in unexpected locations.

- Strange balloons with unsettling smiles that can be spotted while exploring.

- Encounter the mysterious smiling entity known as 000.

- An ARG-inspired experience filled with unexplained events and disturbing discoveries.

- A world filled with secrets, mysteries, and events that may not have an obvious explanation.

---

How It Works

There is no obvious beginning.

The world simply exists.

You can build, explore, mine, and play normally.

Then, somewhere in the distance, you notice something strange.

A red structure.

A house standing alone.

A dungeon hidden underground.

Or perhaps a balloon floating where no balloon should be.

Some encounters may be completely harmless.

Others may leave you wondering whether you actually saw anything at all.

As you continue playing, more unusual things can begin appearing throughout the world.

And among all of them...

There is one presence that seems different.

000.

A strange creature with an unsettling smile, surrounded by mysteries that may never be fully explained.

You may see it.

You may find traces of it.

You may discover something it left behind.

But you will never really know what it wants.

---

An ARG Inside Your World

000.jar is designed to feel less like a traditional horror addon and more like discovering an ARG hidden inside Minecraft.

Not everything has an explanation.

Not every structure has a purpose you can immediately understand.

And not every strange event is meant to be solved.

Sometimes, the most disturbing question isn't "What is that?"

It's...

"Why did it appear in my world?"

Recommended Experience

For the intended experience:

- Play with headphones.

- Explore without spoilers.

- Don't immediately assume everything is random.

- Pay attention to strange structures.

- Take screenshots when something feels wrong.

- Keep exploring.

You never know what might appear next.
