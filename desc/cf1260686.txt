Gun Classic Beta is an in-development add-on that adds popular weapons to Minecraft Bedrock. It offers a good variety, and all weapons have unique animations when the character is standing still. You may encounter some bugs as it is still in beta, which adds a unique realism to the game.

Now you can enjoy an improved Minecraft with new weapons, leaving behind the classic Minecraft tools. Fighting Minecraft's most feared bosses has never been easier with this add-on. The damage of each weapon varies depending on its caliber, so you should consider what you plan to use to defeat the dragon or the guardian.

To enjoy this plugin, it is important to enable experiments for proper functionality and performance.
