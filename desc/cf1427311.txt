One Punch Tree (Tree Capitator) | V3.2.0

One Punch Tree is a Tree Capitator addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Chop the bottom log of any tree and the whole thing falls with full enchantment support, per-player settings, and zero lag.

The Ultimate Tree Capitator Experience for Minecraft Bedrock! (Compatible with 1.21.300+ and Realms)

One Punch Tree V3.2 adds four distinct breaking modes with real tick-accurate animations, a streamlined single-screen settings menu, and internal performance improvements.

WHAT'S NEW IN V3.2.0

Choose how your trees fall from instant destruction to a cinematic falling animation all configurable per player.

Breaking Modes (New!)

- Instant: All blocks break as fast as possible. Classic behavior, zero delay.

- Chopping: Blocks break one by one, bottom to top. Satisfying and immersive.

- Layer: Breaks floor by floor with a short pause between each Y level.

- Falling Tree: Leaves strip away first (top to bottom), then the trunk collapses simulating a real tree falling.

In-Game Settings Menu

- Breaking Mode: Select your preferred mode from a dropdown no submenus needed.

- Animation Speed: Control how fast animated modes play out (120 ticks per block).

- Toggle Options:

- Break Leaves: Turn automatic leaf destruction ON or OFF.

- Particles: Turn chopping particle effects ON or OFF.

- Silk Touch Leaves: Choose if leaves drop as blocks or disappear naturally.

- Configurable Limits:

- Max Horizontal Spread: 116 blocks

- Max Blocks Up: 164 blocks

- Max Blocks Down: 010 blocks

- Max Total Blocks: 1002000 blocks

- Reset to Defaults: One toggle at the bottom of the menu restores all settings instantly.

Toggle Mode

- How to Toggle: Hold an Axe, Sneak + Right-Click in the air.

- Visual Feedback: An Actionbar message tells you if the Tree Capitator is ENABLED or DISABLED.

- Freedom: Once enabled, chop trees standing up, running, or flying!

Advanced Durability & Enchantments

- Silk Touch: When active, leaves drop as blocks instead of disappearing configurable via the Silk Touch Leaves toggle.

- Unbreaking IIII: Fully calculated to match vanilla formulas.

- "Save My Axe": The addon automatically stops chopping if your axe has only 1 durability left.

Core Features

- ZERO LAG Technology: Powered by the Job System (system.runJob) breaks trees in batches of 16 blocks per tick.

- Smart Item Attraction: Dropped items are gradually pulled toward the base of the stump for a smooth, natural collection effect.

- Universal Compatibility: Works with ANY Axe (Vanilla, Netherite, Custom Addons) and ANY Tree (Cherry, Mangrove, Nether Stems, custom addon trees).

How to Use (V3.2)

- Get the Config Item: Received automatically on first join.

- Toggle Mode: Hold your Axe, Sneak + Right-Click. You will hear a sound and see "Enabled".

- Chop: Break the bottom log of any tree. The whole thing falls!

- Configure: Right-Click the config item to adjust your breaking mode, speed, and limits.
