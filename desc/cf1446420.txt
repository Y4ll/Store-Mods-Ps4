A resource pack that adds dynamic, trailer-inspired animations for villagers, players, and more. This pack does not require any additional mods, as it is a texture pack.

Supported Entities
- Players (Jolly walk & run)
- Villagers (including Wandering Traders) (Expressive movement)
- Iron Golems (Dynamic idles)
- Pillagers (Combat poses)
- Ravagers (Improved movement)
- Witches (Expressive movement)

There are more animations to come! This project is designed to be texture-friendly and works with any pack that doesn't alter the base entity geometry.
Key Information
- Platform: Bedrock Edition (Win10, Mobile, Console)
- Achievements: Safe (Client-side only)
- Multiplayer: Compatible with Realms and Servers
