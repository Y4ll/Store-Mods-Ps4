The Locust

Something is wrong.

When you create a new world, everything feels... empty.

Animals are gone.

Villagers have vanished.

The world is almost completely silent.

For a while, nothing happens.

The silence makes you believe you're alone.

You're not.

Features

- Experience a world where almost every living creature has mysteriously disappeared.

- Unexplained sounds and disturbing ambience that grow more frequent over time.

- Witness the appearance of an impossibly tall, unnatural creature.

- Encounter a slow but relentless entity that can follow you across enormous distances.

- Survive terrifying chase events that can begin without warning.

- Psychological horror focused on isolation, tension, and suspense.

- Random encounters that ensure no two playthroughs feel the same.

---

How It Works

The first days feel... empty.

No animals.

No villagers.

Almost nothing moves.

You begin to wonder if something is broken.

Then, strange sounds start echoing across the world.

Sometimes they're distant.

Sometimes they're impossibly close.

Soon after, you may notice a towering figure standing silently on the horizon.

Watching.

Waiting.

Most of the time, it disappears before you can get close.

But as time passes...

The sightings become more frequent.

Another creature may eventually appear.

It moves slowly.

Painfully slowly.

Yet no matter how far you travel...

It never seems to stop following you.

And one day...

The giant figure may finally stop watching.

Instead, it starts running.

When that happens, there are only two possibilities.

Keep running...

Or find somewhere to hide before it loses interest.

---

The Silence Isn't Empty

The world may seem abandoned.

It isn't.

Something has taken its place.

Something that doesn't belong.

And the longer you survive...

The more certain it becomes that it has always known where you are.

Recommended Experience

For the intended experience:

- Play with headphones.

- Keep the volume turned up.

- Avoid spoilers.

- Explore without knowing what comes next.

- Don't trust the silence.
