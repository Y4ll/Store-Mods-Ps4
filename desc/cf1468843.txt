Hello, and welcome to another pack of mine! This pack was originally made by Boombo8455 for Java Edition and was ported by me to Bedrock Edition. Flaming Netherite is a pack that aims to make netherite look a little less metallic. Therefore it is on literal fire...

This Pack Re-textures:

- Netherite Armor (Helmet, Chesplate, Leggings, Boots)

- Netherite Tools (Sword, Axe, Pickaxe, Hoe, Shovel)

- Netherite Block

- Netherite Scrap & Ingot

- Ancient Debris
