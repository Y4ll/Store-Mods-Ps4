Time Stop Addon (Script API)

Ever wanted to freeze the world and move freely while everything else stands still?

The Time Stop Addon allows you to control the flow of time in Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Using the modern Script API, this addon offers a smooth, lag-free experience that freezes mobs, projectiles, and physics instantly.

Key Features

Matrix-Style Projectiles: Arrows, snowballs, and tridents freeze in mid-air! When you resume time, they continue flying exactly where they left off with their original speed.

Complete Mob Freeze: Mobs are locked in place. They cannot walk, run, or pathfind.

Invincibility Logic: While time is paused, mobs cannot deal damage and deal zero knockback. You are safe to walk right up to a Creeper's face!

Day/Night Cycle: The sun and moon stop moving, and the weather freezes.

Multiplayer Friendly: The player who stops time can move, while entities and projectiles remain frozen.

How to Use

Craft or obtain a vanilla Clock.

Right-Click (PC) or Long Press (Mobile) while holding the Clock.

To Pause: You will hear a sound and see a message "Time has STOPPED."

To Resume: Use the clock again. Everything will snap back to action instantly.

Installation & Requirements

This addon requires the Beta APIs experimental feature to work because it uses the powerful GameTest Script Engine.

Download the .mcpack file.

Open Minecraft and go to your world settings.

Activate the Behavior Pack.

IMPORTANT: Go to "Experiments" and turn on Beta APIs.

Start your world!

Notes

This addon uses the @minecraft/server module for high performance.

Unlike old command block systems, this does not spam your chat log or cause massive TPS lag.
