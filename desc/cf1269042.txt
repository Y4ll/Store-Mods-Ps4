Minecraft Bedrock Edition with New Bows Overhaul, the definitive bow-and-arrow mod designed for survival, PvP, and adventure. Dive into a world of elemental power and fearsome foes:

Eight Elemental Bows & Arrows

Lightning Bow & Lightning Arrows: Strike enemies with devastating lightning bolts that chain across the battlefield.

Earth Bow & Earth Arrows: Trap foes in 3x3x3 dirt cages, immobilizing them instantly.

Water Bow & Water Arrows: Drench targets and extinguish flames with aquatic projectiles.

Fire Bow & Fire Arrows: Incinerate mobs and terrain with blazing projectiles that leave trails of destruction.

Frozen Bow & Frozen Arrows: Freeze enemies solid and paralyze them with icy power.

Ender Bow & Ender Arrows: Teleport your arrows through walls for surprise assaults from impossible angles.

Powered Bow & Powered Arrows: Unleash explosive energy with electrifying force.

XP Bow & XP Arrows: Absorb experience from every hitlevel up instantly while fighting.

Five Unique Spider Mobs

Harvest precious Strings from fearsome elemental arachnids:

Lightning Spiders: Storm-charged arachnids that call down lightning on their prey, dropping Electric String

Earth Spiders: Stone-armored spiders that encase victims in dirt prisons, yielding Earthen String.

Fire Spiders: Lava-infused crawlers that ignite everything they touch, providing Molten String.

Frozen Spiders: Ice-cloaked hunters that paralyze with freezing venom, dropping Glacial String.

Ender Spiders: Shadowy weavers from the End, teleporting in combat and dropping Ender String.

Each spider spawns naturally in biomes matching their element, creating dynamic hunting challenges!
