Requirements (Bedrock RTX)

- Platform: Minecraft for Windows (Bedrock)

- GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX (RTX 2060 or better recommended)

- OS: Windows 10/11 (64-bit)

Note: RTX toggles are part of Bedrocks Windows ray-tracing features.

Quick install (Bedrock/Windows)

- Download the .mcpack for your chosen tier (Demo / LOW / HD / FHD).

- Import: Double-click the .mcpack to import into Minecraft (Bedrock).

- Activate: Go to Settings Global Resources and enable the pack.

- Enable Ray Tracing: Settings Video Ray Tracing RTX: ON.

Performance tips

- Start with LOW or HD; move to FHD if frame rate is stable.

- Reduce render distance for heavier scenes.

- Keep GPU drivers up to date and close background apps before high-res sessions.
