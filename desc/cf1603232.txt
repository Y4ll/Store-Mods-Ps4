Advanced Vein Miner

Make mining faster and more efficient with Advanced Vein Miner by FleetMc Studio. Instead of breaking every ore block one by one, simply mine a single ore and the addon will automatically detect and break the connected ores in the entire vein.

Unlike many basic vein miner addons that only focus on quickly breaking connected blocks, Advanced Vein Miner is designed with survival gameplay and tool mechanics in mind. Pickaxe durability, Fortune, Silk Touch, and Unbreaking are all supported, giving you a more complete and balanced mining experience.

How Does It Work?

Using Advanced Vein Miner is simple. Find an ore vein and break one of its blocks with a pickaxe. The addon will automatically detect the connected ores and mine them together, saving you from having to break every block individually.

This makes large ore veins much faster to collect while still maintaining important survival mechanics such as tool durability and enchantment support.

Pickaxe Durability Support

Advanced Vein Miner doesn't give you unlimited mining for free. When connected ores are broken, your pickaxe durability is consumed accordingly, helping the addon remain useful while still respecting survival progression.

This means you should always keep an eye on your pickaxe durability before mining large veins, especially during long underground adventures.

Fortune, Silk Touch & Unbreaking Support

One of the biggest advantages of Advanced Vein Miner is its support for important Minecraft enchantments. Your enchanted pickaxes continue to provide their intended benefits while using the vein mining system.

- Fortune Support Using a Fortune-enchanted pickaxe gives you the opportunity to receive increased ore drops.

- Silk Touch Support Mine supported ores with Silk Touch to collect the ore blocks themselves instead of their normal drops.

- Unbreaking Support Unbreaking enchantments are supported, helping your pickaxe last longer while mining connected ore veins.
