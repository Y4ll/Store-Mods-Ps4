Documentation is used in the crafting table for crafting and further modification of the car; it is not consumed upon use.

Documentation #... - crafted from a book and iron nuggets.

Modernization Documentation - restyling.

Sport Modification - converts the car into a sports or semi-sports model (coupe, rally, hot rod, lowrider).

Increased Capacity Modification - converts the car into a station wagon, minivan, or pickup truck.

It can be recycled back into an empty book using a piece of resin (but other materials will be lost).

All cars can be repaired using a copper ingot, iron ingot, gold ingot, netherite ingot, netherite scrap, iron nugget, gold nugget, and redstone dust.

Repair kit - allows you to repair a car without consuming materials (for several cars at the moment).

Iron golem sound effects are used.
