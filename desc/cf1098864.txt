How to get it?

To craft a jetpack, you must wear this like a chestplate. All the crafting recipes for the required items are unlocked. Combine these items and craft your Jetpack.

All Items List: PVC, Battery, Circuit Board & Remote

Combine them all and craft a Jetpack.

How to use?

- Equip the Basic Jetpack in the chestplate slot.

- Look up to start flying (Levitation effect).

- Look down to slow fall (Slow Falling effect).

This addon is perfect for exploration, building, and survival-friendly flight without making Elytra obsolete.
