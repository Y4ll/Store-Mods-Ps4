Project Description:

Say goodbye to tedious, block-by-block logging. The Actual Tree Capitator add-on streamlines resource gathering in survival mode by instantly felling whole trees when you break the bottom log. It handles massive oak canopies, towering mega-spruces, and jungle trees in a single swing, saving valuable tool durability and hours of manual chopping.

Important Information:

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Simple sneak on & off system.

- Realistic above only breaking.

- Capitator guide for leaf decay.

- Behaviour & Resource packs are both required for the project to work.

- Have something to say? Join the Discord.

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

To prevent unwanted timber falls during building, Tree Capitator is toggleable. Simply sneak (crouch) while chopping the base log to trigger the automated tree-felling system.

Craft a comprehensive Tree Capitator Settings book to effortlessly navigate and customize your wood-harvesting options.

Using the Tree Capitator Settings book, you can easily toggle leaf-decay, leaf drops and Beehive breaking on and off to customize how you want to play the add-on.
