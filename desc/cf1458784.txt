Overview

Age of Dragons is a Minecraft addon featuring powerful elemental dragons. Currently includes Fire, Ice, and Lightning Dragons, each with unique models, animations, abilities, and combat styles.

Get Started

If you're looking for a dragon-themed addon and want to conquer them, this is the addon for you.

Age of Dragons features 3 types of dragons:
- Fire,
- Ice,
- Lightning.
They can be found in the following biomes:
- Fire dragons in plains.
- Ice dragons in cold regions like ice plains.
- Lightning dragons can be found in the Sanvana region.
- You will find them in nests, and inside there will be loot.
- If you're lucky, you'll find a dragon nest underground; it's the nest of an old, elder dragon. When you capture them, you have a chance to get an egg.
- There are also dragon scales and bones to craft armor and weapons.
- How to hatch eggs:
- Fire dragons: inside a flame.
- Ice dragons: inside a block of water, they will freeze.
- Lightning dragons: wait for a thunderstorm, the eggs will begin to hatch.
- The whole process takes 5 minutes. Feed them fish to tame and stimulate growth. Use meals to speed up the growth process. Eat any kind of meat to restore health.

You can craft armor for your dragon and equip chests. Craft a dragon stick to see who owns it and breath in ride. Craft golden horns to store your dragon.

There are still some things you can discover for yourself. XD
