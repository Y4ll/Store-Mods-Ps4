Full Description:

Through the world of Minecraft will generate the Mansion X, inside you can meet Charles Xavier in which he will sell you the powers of a mutant.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Charles Xavier will sell you the powers of a Mutant for vials of Gen X, to get the vials X-Men boxes will be generated by the world, commonly will grant you the Gen X and there is a chance to get costumes and accessories of an X-Men character. There are two types of boxes.

Beast:

It is generated in a part of the Mansion X, he will grant you the costumes of the members of the X-Men.

Skills and controls:

Melee Attack:

As long as you have Wolverine's claws you can perform melee attacks with a damage of 10.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Claw ability:

While on the ground you can use Wolverine's claws to use his ability which deals 11 area damage and destroys blocks around you.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Jump and attack:

After jumping use Wolverine's claws to perform a ground attack that will deal 16 area damage and destroy blocks in area.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Climb walls:

As long as you have Wolverine's claws in your main hand you can use the ability to climb up walls. While you are touching a wall press the crouch button to hang on the wall and press the jump button to climb. To stop climbing release the crouch button.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Smell:

Wolverine's powers will grant you the ability to smell that will allow you to detect nearby entities, a symbol will be created that will indicate where the entity is located and you will be able to see it through the blocks. The blue symbol indicates nearby players, the red symbol indicates aggressive creatures and the green symbol indicates harmless creatures or items.
