The Dark Infection

A parasitic Minecraft add-on inspired by Scape and Run: Parasites

The world is no longer safe.
A strange biological infection spreads across blocks, creatures, and entire environments. As the parasite evolves, it becomes smarter, more aggressive, and harder to survive against.

Survival Mode is now fully supported.
The infection now progresses naturally through gameplay, allowing players to experience a complete survival-horror evolution system.

Core Progression System

The infection evolves through three major systems:

- Environmental Points controls world corruption and spawn behavior

- Capability Points controls parasite intelligence and combat evolution

- Transmission Points controls infection and spreading abilities

Environmental Points

Environmental Points represent how heavily the world has been corrupted.

How to Earn Environmental Points

- Every infected block grants +1 Environmental Point

Environmental Stages

1000 Environmental Points

[Environmental Stage 1]

- Infected mobs begin spawning during nighttime

100000 Environmental Points

[Environmental Stage 2]

- Infected mobs begin spawning during daytime

- The infection becomes impossible to fully avoid

Capability Points

Capability Points represent the parasite hives evolution and combat intelligence.

How Capability Points Work

- Every infected mob spawned: +3 Capability Points

- Every infected mob killed: -5 Capability Points

Players can temporarily slow parasite evolution by eliminating infected creatures.

Capability Stages

0 Capability Points

[Capability Stage 0 Passive Adaptation]

- Infected mobs attack only certain common creatures

- Parasites avoid players whenever possible

- Detection range limited to 25 blocks

- Early infection behavior remains unstable

500 Capability Points

[Capability Stage 1 Hostile Evolution]
Infected mobs begin attacking:

- Players

- Overworld creatures

- Sea creatures

- Modded entities

- Nearly all living mobs

Additional Changes:

- Detection range increases to 50 blocks

- Parasites become significantly more aggressive

1500 Capability Points

[Capability Stage 2 Combat Mutation]
All infected mobs receive major combat upgrades:

- Increased maximum health

- Attack damage increased from 68 15

- Movement speed increased by 10%

- Improved chase behavior

4000 Capability Points

[Capability Stage 3 Apex Intelligence]

- Parasites prioritize players above all other targets

- Normal mobs are ignored unless necessary

- Detection range exceeds 200 blocks

- Parasites can detect players through walls

- Escaping becomes extremely difficult

8000 Capability Points

[Capability Stage 4 Environmental Collapse]
All infected mobs gain:

- Wooden block breaking abilities

- Improved pathfinding

- The ability to invade player bases more effectively

No shelter remains completely safe.

Transmission Points

Transmission Points determine how efficiently the infection spreads between organisms.

Transmission Stages

0 Transmission Points

[Transmission Stage 0 Dormant Infection]

- Parasites cannot infect living mobs

100 Transmission Points

[Transmission Stage 1 Explosive Mutation]

- Parasites explode upon death

- Explosions release additional parasitic worms

- Infection spread becomes more unpredictable

500 Transmission Points

[Transmission Stage 2 Limited Infection]
Only these variants can infect mobs:

- Parasitic Larvae

- Parasite Spiders

The infection is still partially controlled.

2000 Transmission Points

[Transmission Stage 3 Full Transmission]

- All parasite variants can now infect mobs

- Infection is no longer limited to specific parasite types

- Outbreaks spread rapidly across the world
