How to put the backpack on your back?

If you want to see the backpack on your back, you have to place the backpack item in your inventory, NOT in the hotbar.

If you can't deselect your backpack, stop holding it for a moment to put it in your inventory.

Now that you have your backpack in your inventory it will now be visible!! Try all the designs you want and choose the one you like the most. Have fun!!
