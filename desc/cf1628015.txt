Never Lose Your Hardcore World Again!

The ultimate lifesaver addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Automatically refilled to your offhand in milliseconds!

Why You Need ZenXveda Auto Totem

Tired of dying in boss battles, raids, or PvP because you couldn't open your inventory fast enough? ZenXveda Auto Totem instantly equips a Totem of Undying straight from your inventory into your offhand the exact moment one pops or your offhand becomes empty. Available in two specialized flavors: Pro Edition (full-featured, stats, streak rewards, shield support) and Lite Edition (zero scripts, maximum FPS, pure auto-swap).

Pro Edition

The Ultimate Experience Tracking, Rewards, Shield Support & Custom Crafting!

Core Features

- Instant Auto-Swapping: Seamlessly detects when your totem pops and equips a replacement from your inventory in milliseconds.

- Smart Shield Support: Hold a shield to temporarily pause auto-swapping to block oncoming attacks, then automatically resumes the moment you finish!

Progression & Stats

- Live Stats Dashboard: Rename any ordinary book to totem on an anvil to check your deaths, totems used, and active survival streaks!

- Survival Streak Rewards: Earn bonus Golden Apples and Diamonds as rewards for staying alive!

Custom Totem Crafting Recipe

Craft Totems directly on your crafting table with:
2x Emeralds | 1x Diamond | 6x Gold Ingots

Lite Edition

Pure Auto-Swapping. Zero Scripts. Zero Overhead. Maximum FPS!

- Continuous Auto-Swapping:
Always ready! Automatically swaps totems to your offhand non-stop with zero delay.

- Zero Scripts & Maximum Performance:
Built with 100% vanilla Bedrock animation systems. No JavaScript runtime overhead, zero memory lag, and buttery smooth 60+ FPS.

- 100% Achievement Compatible:
Does not require experimental gameplay toggles. Perfect for pure survival worlds and Realms where achievements matter!

Quick 3-Step Installation Guide

- Download: Grab your chosen .mcpack file (Pro or Lite).

- Import: Double-click or tap the .mcpack file to automatically launch and import into Minecraft Bedrock.

- Activate: Go to your World Settings > Behavior Packs, activate ZenXveda Auto Totem, and hit Play!

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does this work on Minecraft Bedrock Realms and Dedicated Servers?

A: Yes! Both Pro and Lite editions work seamlessly on singleplayer, LAN, Realms, and Bedrock Dedicated Servers (BDS).

Q: Will activating this disable Xbox achievements?

A: No! Both editions are built to be 100% achievement friendly without disabling achievements on your survival worlds.

Q: How do I view my stats in the Pro Edition?

A: Simply rename any book to totem on an anvil and hold it to view your deaths, totems saved, and active survival streaks!

Q: Which edition should I choose?

A: Choose Pro if you want shield pause/resume support, stats, streak rewards, and custom recipes. Choose Lite if you want pure unpaused auto-swap with zero script overhead!
