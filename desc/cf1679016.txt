Gojo Satoru Infinity Barrier Script gives your player a powerful protective aura that automatically repels nearby monsters. Any monster that enters the aura's range will be pushed away, preventing them from getting close to you.

The script is designed to simulate Gojo Satoru's Infinity Barrier, keeping hostile mobs at a distance without requiring you to attack them.

This is a script, not an addon. You don't need to install a Resource Pack, enable Experimental Features, or configure any additional files. Simply install the script and it will work in your world.
