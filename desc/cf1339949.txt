Welcome to THE PVZ PLUS Addon

Strange Zombies are invading your minecraft world, and the only defense is your arsenal of plants! Armed with an alien nursery-worth of zombie-zapping plants like peashooters and cherry bombs, you'll need to think fast and plant faster to stop dozens of types of zombies dead in their tracks. CAN YOU SURVIVE!

Plants

[More Plants will be added in the future]

Each Plants have their own abilities like solarpea, ghostpepper, and jack-o-lantern when close range

However, like Peashooter and Sunflower, they have plant food abilities to be stronger

Also, some plants only get the plant food ability because the others are in WIP.

Zombies

[More Zombies will be added in the future]

Don't think it's going to be that easy because each zombie has

advantages when they are low health or taking damage.
