This Addon adds 16 different colored Philippine Jeepneys to your world and can be ride by up to 8 Players!

Hop on, roam your city builds and join the road trip with your friends with this Colorful Jeepneys along the way!

Basic Informations:

- Jeepneys, sometimes called simply "jeeps", are buses and the most popular means of public transportation ubiquitous in the Philippines. They are known for their crowded seating and kitsch decorations, which have become a wide spread symbol of Philippine culture and art. Learn more....

Features:

Menu for easy access of information about the Addon:

Place the jeepney in your world!

To do this, find the Jeepney in the "Nature" section, and scroll down to the "Mob Eggs" category.

Customize their colors!

By using colored dye, you can use it by holding it in your main hand and interacting to the Jeepney.

You can use all 16 dye colors to the jeepneys to change their colors and design!
