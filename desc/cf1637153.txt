Cursed Peppa

Cursed Peppa is a story-driven horror add-on for Minecraft Bedrock. Survive five terrifying nights, protect George and his family, complete missions, and prepare for a final confrontation with Cursed Peppa.

As each night passes, new scripted events, warnings, sounds, and encounters unfold. You only have five liveslose them all, and you will become a spectator.

Features

- A five-night survival horror experience

- Cursed Peppa with multiple phases and attacks

- George, Daddy Pig, and Mummy Pig as custom entities

- Story events and mission objectives

- Custom models, textures, animations, voices, and sound effects

- Dynamic nighttime encounters and weather changes

- Georges craftable toy dinosaur

- A five-life system and final battle

- Behavior and resource packs included in one .mcaddon

How to play

Create a new survival world and activate both included packs. The story begins automatically, and the current objectives will appear on the screen.

Georges toy dinosaur can be crafted at a crafting table using four pieces of white wool.

For the intended experience, we recommend playing in Survival mode without combining this add-on with other projects that heavily modify hostile mobs, time, weather, or world events.
