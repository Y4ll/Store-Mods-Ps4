This Minecraft Blacksmith Mod lets you craft up to 3 custom blacksmiths in your world, each offering powerful loot. All blacksmiths can be crafted using basic items found in villages, making your gameplay more exciting and rewarding.

This mod adds three custom blacksmiths: Dirt Blacksmith, Iron Blacksmith, and Diamond Blacksmith. Each blacksmith has its own basic crafting recipe, letting you obtain powerful loot and items using simple village resources.
