What are you waiting for? The Locust addon has been updated now!

Terrifying features and sounds have been added, along with terrifying events every night it gets more terrifying, and cursed skeletons much weirder!!

The Locust mod is a mod that adds a creature called The Locust, inspired by Dr. Noig's YouTube channel.

The mob spawns in caves and dark places. At night, it becomes very fast, strong, and breaks blocks quickly.

It also adds blood-stained cross pillars that represent a curse in the world.

Additionally, there is a TV that hints at how this creature entered the real world.
