FPS Booster Pack | No Lag + Performance

Enhance your gameplay with FPS Booster Pack, a lightweight optimization resource pack designed to reduce lag, improve stability, and deliver consistently smooth FPS across all devices, including low-end hardware.

This pack focuses on performance-first adjustments while maintaining a clean and visually pleasing experience, ensuring smoother gameplay without compromising core aesthetics.

What's New

- In-game settings menu tap the gear on the pack to tune it, no reinstall

- Effect Density slider scale how many particles each effect spawns

- Burst Limit slider cap any single effect, aimed at splash potions

- Three effect toggles grouped by where the particles come from

- Fixed auto-selection phones and tablets now default to Turbo instead of the lighter mode

- Renamed modes Turbo and Lite, both now fully self-contained

In-Game Settings

Tap the gear icon on the pack to open the settings screen. Every control works in both modes, not just one.

Key Features

- 171 particle effects cut in Turbo mode, 45 in Lite the largest single source of GPU overdraw on Bedrock

- Per-biome fog tuning across 79 biomes, with two completely different fog profiles to pick from

- Clean and minimal UI 224 interface textures flattened, 42 of them down to a single solid colour

- Simplified block textures 43 water, bubble-column and fire textures reduced in Turbo mode

- Flat cloud layer clouds reduced to two colours in Turbo mode

- Grass and nylium side-overlays removed 6 block types render one texture on every face instead of compositing a tinted overlay

- Desaturated 6464 colormaps less VRAM and more natural foliage tone

- Quieter ambient mix 85 mobs plus 44 individual sound events pulled down, and 39 sound definitions overridden

- Sky and weather textures reduced end sky, end flash and rain splash cut to 11

- Fully compatible with the Render Dragon engine

Subpacks

Pick one from the subpack list when you activate the pack. On low-memory devices Turbo is now selected automatically.

- Turbo Short Fog + Max FPS fog closes in at 60 blocks so the game stops drawing distant geometry, 171 particle effects silenced, flattened block and cloud textures. The heaviest optimization.

- Lite Clear View + Light Touch fog pushed past the horizon for maximum visibility, 45 particle effects silenced, vanilla block textures. For when you want the view back.

Both modes share the settings menu, the flattened UI, the colormaps and the quieter sound mix.
