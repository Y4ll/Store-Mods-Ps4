Aqua Item Upgrader

Turn any item into a better one... or lose it all. A high-stakes item upgrade gamble for Minecraft Bedrock.

>Small note: this addon is made with $0 budget. The logo was made with AI, so please be nice about it!

How it works

1. Hold the item you want to upgrade.

2. Sneak and right-click.

3. Pick the amount, the prize you want, and a multiplier (x1, x2, x3, x5, x10, x20).

4. Confirm and watch the chances spin. Win the prize... or lose your item.

The probability depends on the value of your item vs. the prize:

- Same value: ~50%

- Cheaper prize: up to 95%

- More expensive prize: from 0.25%

Higher multipliers give a bigger prize but a much lower chance.

Features

- Upgrade from any item in your inventory.

- 130+ curated items with automatic values for every other item (no exploits).

- Win streaks: each consecutive win adds +0.25x to your prize (up to 3x). Lose and the streak resets.

- Search any item by ID (e.g. `minecraft:diamond`) and try to win it.

- A guide book given on first join (open it anytime with the `aqua:guide_book` item).

- Bilingual: Spanish and English.

- Flashy progress-bar animation, sounds and particles on every spin.

Multipliers

| Multiplier | Prize | Chance |

|------------|-------|--------|

| x1 | 1x | Highest |

| x2 | 2x | High |

| x3 | 3x | Good |

| x5 | 5x | Low |

| x10 | 10x | Very low |

| x20 | 20x | Extreme |

Combo system

Win twice in a row and you start a streak. Every consecutive win raises your multiplier by +0.25x:

- Streak 2: x1.25

- Streak 3: x1.50

- ...

- Streak 9: x3.00 (max)

Lose and the streak resets to zero.

Commands

- `/give @s aqua:guide_book 1` get the guide book.

Installation

1. Download the `.mcaddon` file.

2. Open it Minecraft imports both packs automatically.

3. Create a world (or edit an existing one) and enable **Aqua Item Upgrader** under Behavior Packs and Resource Packs.

4. Play. Sneak + right-click with any item to start upgrading.
