Liminal Craft is a Minecraft Bedrock Add-On that adds different Entities, Blocks and Items for Building a Backroom World, Apart from its Compatibility with the New Vibrant Visuals, Try to make each entity have its meaning in the Add-On and its Function, and apart from trying to have a style as Vanilla as possible.

The Entities that the Add-on contains are mainly from Roblox Games, Wiki Pages or Custom, mainly from the Adysync, Reysync and Limbo, others would be like the Kane Parsons series, It also uses the original Backrooms Wikidot as a base for some ideas, because it is obviously the basis for everything.

This Add-On is created mainly for the player to create maps, so it can be called Sandbox but in future versions a natural generation is planned, it mainly has 1 level blocks (+Biome) That its sections are under construction category.
