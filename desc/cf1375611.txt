There's not much to describe, the name is self-explanatory: This add-on adds glass with support for texture packs (but not all textures work well).

When you place a Glass block, it automatically transforms into Connected Glass with seamless textures.

Supported Blocks

Settings Menu:

While sneaking and holding any Glass Block, interact with the Air to open the Settings Menu fast.

You can also open the menu using the /kwz:glass_config command.

Only player with Host perm. can open it.

Features

- Does not replace Vanilla Glass, only intercepts placement

- Uses Vanilla textures - compatible with any texture pack (but there's no guarantee it will work smoothly)

- Works in existing worlds (break and replace glass to update)

- Do NOT modify "player.json"

- No experimental features required

- Achievement Friendly
