The Furniture Expansion - Vanilla Styled Addon brings furniture and decorations to Minecraft Bedrock without interfering with the original experience. Built to be lightweight and survival-friendly, it works perfectly in singleplayer or multiplayer worlds with no lag or changes to world generation.

Lightweight & Performance Focused: Extremely lightweight, no crashes, and doesn't alter core game mechanics. A true vanilla-friendly expansion.

Supported Languages: pt-BR, en-US, de-DE, ru-RU, fr-FR, it-IT, es-MX, es-ES, pt-PT, zh-CN, ja-JP. Report errors on Discord.
