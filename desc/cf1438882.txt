A resource pack that overhauls the look of Minecraft mobs while keeping a cute, but mostly vanilla feel. Includes remodels and textures! This pack was originally created by emberjams for Java Edition and was ported by me to Bedrock Edition

Changes so far to:

- Cows

- Sheeps

- Squids

- Glow Squids

- Pigs

- Chickens

- Ghasts

- Foxes

- Mooshrooms
