Sleeping OP

Make sleeping actually worth it with Sleeping OP. Every time you wake up, you can receive random rewards ranging from common building blocks to rare items, powerful gear, and heavily enchanted equipment.

Sleep & Get Rewards

Simply sleep in a bed and receive a random selection of rewards when you wake up. The rewards are randomly chosen from different tiers, so every sleep can give you something different.

Reward Tiers

- Common: Building blocks, wood, plants, food, and other useful items.

- Uncommon: Valuable resources, decorations, tools, armor, and special items.

- Rare: Diamonds, emeralds, Netherite items, Ender Pearls, rare loot, and more.

- Legendary: Elytra, Totem of Undying, Mace, Dragon Egg, Nether Star, powerful gear, and other extremely rare rewards.

Sleep Streak System

Keep sleeping on consecutive in-game days to build your sleep streak. As your streak increases, the reward chances become better and you can receive more rewards.

Enchanted Gear

Some tools and armor can come with powerful enchantments such as Protection IV, Efficiency V, Fortune III, Sharpness V, Unbreaking III, Mending, and more.

Features

- Random rewards every time you wake up.

- Four reward tiers.

- Sleep streak system.

- Higher streaks improve reward chances.

- Reward quantity increases with your streak.

- Rare and legendary loot can be obtained.

- Powerful enchanted tools and armor.

- Works with survival gameplay.
