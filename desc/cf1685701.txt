Verity

Enter a disturbing Minecraft horror experience with Verity, where creepy creatures, strange characters, and unsettling environments turn every moment into something unpredictable.

This addon is designed to create a darker atmosphere around your world, with mysterious entities and horror-inspired encounters that make exploration feel much more tense.

Features

- Creepy and mysterious characters

- Horror-inspired creatures

- Dark and unsettling atmosphere

- Great for horror maps and survival worlds

- Works well for multiplayer and roleplay

- Designed to make exploration feel more intense and unpredictable

Whether you are exploring alone or with friends, Verity adds a creepy new mood to Minecraft and makes even familiar places feel dangerous.

Stay alert. Keep moving. Something may already be watching you.
