SCARY FRIEND BERITY

Enter a darker Minecraft experience with Scary Friend Berity by FleetMc Studio. Verity is a mysterious creature that can naturally appear underground in dark areas, turning ordinary cave exploration into an unpredictable survival experience.

With its unique appearance, aggressive behavior, taming system, and powerful attacks, Verity can become either a dangerous enemy or a powerful companion. Explore carefully, because you never know when Verity might be waiting somewhere in the darkness.

Meet Berity

- 60 Health Verity has a large health pool, making it difficult to defeat.

- Powerful Attack Wild Verity can attack nearby players.

- 25 Attack Damage Its attacks can be extremely dangerous in survival.

- Underground Spawns Verity can naturally spawn underground in dark areas.

- Rare Encounter It appears as a hostile monster, making cave exploration more unpredictable.

Tame Berity

Verity doesn't always have to be your enemy. Feed it suitable foods and you have a chance to tame it. Once tamed, Verity becomes a powerful companion that can protect you from nearby threats.

A successful tame changes Verity's behavior completely. It can follow its owner, teleport back when it gets too far away, and help defend its owner during combat.

Tamed Berity Features

- Can be tamed using various food items.

- Follows its owner around the world.

- Can teleport back when it gets too far away.

- Attacks targets that threaten its owner.

- Can be healed using supported food items.

- Can be bred when tamed.

- Can be leashed.

What Can Berity Drop?

Defeating a wild Verity can reward you with useful resources. Its drops include Redstone, Iron Ingots, Diamonds, Cooked Beef, and Bread, giving you another reason to take the risk and face this mysterious creature.

Healing & Taming

Berity can interact with a variety of Minecraft foods. Foods such as apples, bread, carrots, cooked meats, golden apples, golden carrots, honey bottles, pumpkin pie, suspicious stew, and sweet berries can be used with Verity for taming, healing, or other interactions depending on its state.

How to Play

- Import the addon into Minecraft Bedrock.

- Enable the required packs in your world.

- Explore dark underground areas to encounter Verity naturally.

- Fight it or attempt to tame it using suitable food.

- Once tamed, keep Verity as your companion and let it help protect you.

- Heal and interact with your tamed Verity using supported foods.

Compatibility

- Survival gameplay.

- Multiplayer supported.
