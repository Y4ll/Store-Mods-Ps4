What Is this Shader?

SmooTea Shader is a charming aesthetic shader pack designed to transform your world with a soft, pastel-inspired color palette. It features smooth, subtle clouds and calming water animations that create a peaceful atmosphere for your gameplay.

If youre looking to give your world a cozy and "cute" vibe, this shader is perfect. It emphasizes a cleaner visuals and gentle lighting, making every biome feel more inviting and aesthetically pleasing for players who love a softer look.

Is This Shader demanding?

Most of the time No. Since SmooTeaShader prioritizes smooth-end graphics and cozy details, it is mostly not that resource-intensive. It is recommended for low-end devices.
