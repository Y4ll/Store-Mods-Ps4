Newb X Daydream

A fantasy shader for Android, iOS, and Windows designed to make every Minecraft world feel peaceful, immersive, and comfortable.

This is a variant shader made from Newb Shader by Devendrn

Features

- Soft Cozy lighting

- Reflective ground

- Beautiful sunrise and sunset colors

- Colorful night sky with shooting stars

- Aurora Borealis (Northern Lights)

- Fluffy Clouds

- Glowing Ores

- Enhanced atmospheric fog

- Balanced color grading

- Realistic Moon

- Enhanced End Atmosphere

- Yellow fireflies

Subpacks

Choose the visual quality that best suits your device. Rounded Clouds and Realistic Clouds are available in supported quality levels.

- Lite / Default - Performance-focused experience designed for smoother gameplay, with reduced effects and lighter cloud rendering.

- Medium - A balanced preset offering improved visuals while maintaining good performance. Choose between Rounded Clouds or Realistic Clouds.

- Wisteria Edition - Adds Wisteria Trees for a unique fantasy-inspired look, available with both Rounded and Realistic Clouds.

- High - Enhanced visuals with reflections and improved cloud quality, offering a more cinematic Minecraft experience.

- Ultra - The ultimate visual preset featuring ground reflections, galaxy effects, and high-quality cloud rendering for the most immersive experience.

Compatibility

Newb X Hearth only works with MB Loader (Android) or Wyvern Loader (Windows) or Minecraft with Hynis (iOS).
