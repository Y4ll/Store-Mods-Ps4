Full Description:

Throughout the world of Minecraft will appear the boys' van, along with Frenchie, Hughie, Mother's Milk and Kimiko.

<figure class="Z--PZ" data-hook="figure-IMAGE">

Frenchie will sell you the temporary Compound V which will give you temporary powers. He will also sell an arsenal of weapons and ammunition.

<figure class="Z--PZ" data-hook="figure-IMAGE">

<button class="vB0h2" type="button" data-hook="image-expand-button" aria-label="Expand image"></button>

</figure>

Mother's Milk will sell you items to make the super powers.

You will be able to recruit the boys, for that you will have to give the temporary compound V to Kimiko, Frenchie and Hughie, while with Mother's Milk you recruit with a bucket of Milk.

<figure class="Z--PZ" data-hook="figure-IMAGE">

The boys will defend you from enemies using their weapons or powers. They will follow you or you can ask them to stop following you.

You can ask them not to follow you and be in surveillance mode so they can walk around you, for that you must interact with them while crouching, this will also allow you to interact with them to exchange items. To make them follow you again, interact with them while you are crouched.

Temporary Compound V Powers

Temporary Compound V will allow you to obtain temporary super powers that will last for 64 seconds. To use it, interact with the temporary Compound V.
