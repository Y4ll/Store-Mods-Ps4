Six beautiful butterfly species, a craftable net to catch them, and a passive emitter for your gardens. Vanilla-friendly, lightweight, and survival-ready.

Six Unique Butterflies

- Monarch - Spawns across all Overworld surface biomes in daylight

- Blue Butterfly - Found in flower forests, plains, and meadows

- Red Butterfly - A fiery flash of color among the greenery

- Pink Butterfly - Delicate and elegant, drawn to flowers

- Cave Butterfly - A mysterious glowing variant from deep underground

- Golden Butterfly - Rare meadow dweller; pulses Regeneration to nearby players

Butterfly Net

Catch any butterfly to add it to your collection.

- Recipe: 3 cobwebs on top + 2 sticks in the handle

- Right-click a butterfly with the net to catch it

- You receive the matching spawn egg as a drop

- Use the spawn egg on any block to release the butterfly back

- Spawn eggs display perfectly inside vanilla item frames

Butterfly Emitter

Place an emitter to create your own butterfly hotspot.

- Recipe: 8 poppies around 1 dirt

- Periodically spawns random butterflies in a small radius

- Emits soft light level 6, perfect for gardens

- Spawns all 6 variants with weighted randomness
