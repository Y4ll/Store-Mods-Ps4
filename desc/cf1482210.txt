PVP Essentials

A Bedrock resource pack built for competitive players.

No fluff. No distractions. Every texture decision exists to give you an edge.

Features

- Clear Glass Plain glass is almost entirely see-through, and all 16 stained colours are cut to roughly half opacity. Enough tint left to read the colour, not enough to hide behind.

- Ore Highlighting High-contrast retextures for all 10 overworld and nether ores plus all 8 deepslate variants.

- Vibrant Visuals Ready 20 PBR texture sets with metalness/emissive/roughness maps, so ores stay readable with VV switched on.

- Distinct Potions 63 potion textures. Every drinkable and splash potion has its own readable colour.

- Minimal Fog All 89 biomes individually remapped, plus 8 direct overrides of vanilla's own fog identifiers. Air, water and weather fog all pushed past 1000 blocks, so underwater visibility is instant. None of the 102 fog definitions carry a volumetric block, so the expensive raymarched fog under Vibrant Visuals never runs.

- Numbered Hotbar Slot numbers on your hotbar for instant inventory reads under pressure.

- Durability Viewer Durability on the HUD, in your inventory, in hover text, in chests and on armor. No behaviour pack needed.

- Clean Weapons & Armor All 7 sword tiers retextured, plus both layers of all 6 armor model sets (chain, copper, iron, gold, diamond, netherite) for fast identification of what your opponent is wearing.

- Shield Rework Custom shield geometry and a low-shield animation that keeps the shield out of your sightline while blocking. Both override the assets vanilla already loads, so no attachable setup is needed.

- Quick Craft One-tap output collection on the crafting table, stonecutter and villager trading. All six screens covered, desktop and pocket.

- Optimized HUD Chat, inventory and status effect icons tuned for competitive play. Help buttons removed throughout.

- 177 particle types stripped Only the eight that carry real combat information are kept potion swirls, splash bursts, tipped arrow trails, cauldrons, witch and evoker magic and those are trimmed so they never bury the fight.

Subpack Options

Two crosshair modes. Tap the pack after activating and pick from the subpack list:

- Default Crosshair vanilla crosshair

- PVP Crosshair high-contrast crosshair, plus flattened clouds and weather textures

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it, Minecraft imports it automatically

- Activate in Global Resources or World Settings

- Tap the pack to pick your crosshair subpack

Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.0+

- Android, iOS/iPadOS, Windows 10/11, Xbox, PlayStation

- Works in single-player, on servers and on Realms

- No experimental features required
