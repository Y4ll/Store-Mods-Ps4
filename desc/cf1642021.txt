Day Zov Core is a comprehensive addon that completely overhauls Minecraft Bedrock, introducing post-apocalyptic survival mechanics, weapons, vehicles, and immersive content.

The World:

Dead cities, scorched wastelands, swamps, and dense taiga

New mutants and radiation hazards

Realistic survival demands the right tools find or craft an axe to chop down trees

Lore & Backstory

The story places you in a world after a nuclear war, triggered by an outbreak of an unknown disease in the 2000s. The devastating effects of radiation and bacteria gave rise to horrifying monsters that now roam the wastelands and city ruins. The war didn't end even after nuclear warheads destroyed the world, and the last survivors remain hostile toward you.

Your journey begins as you emerge from the house where you weathered the apocalypse until supplies ran out, forcing you to finally step outside.

General view of the modified generation

Version 1.0 Features

Not everything listed below is fully implemented; many features are still under development.

Weapons & Combat:

Realistic 3D firearms and 2D melee weapons

Weapon customization: attach suppressors, scopes, and under-barrel grenade launchers

Vehicles:

Military and civilian vehicles

Survival Mechanics:

Updated damage system (tracking specific body parts and overall health)

Bleeding

Bone fractures based on player weight (carry items in a backpack, not in your main inventory)

Food spoilage

Wounds

Concussions

Armor penetration

Thirst and body temperature (you can drink directly from water sources with your hands no bottle needed)

Hunting requires sneaking up on animals so they don't flee

Player may cough at night and when diving

Environment & Tools:

Wood and stone can only be gathered with tools (axe, pickaxe)

Multiple zombie types

New structures

New iron tool forging system

Immersion & Realism:

Sanity level affecting disease susceptibility

Realistic lighting (torches emit light when held in hand)

Night blindness due to vitamin deficiency (find and consume vitamins to see beyond 4 blocks at night)

Coordinates removed from the HUD (use the compass it now shows coordinates)

Torches are not eternal (burn out within 1060 minutes)

Water in this world is contaminated drinking a little from a river won't kill you, but consuming too much can lead to fatal poisoning

And much, much more!

new wound treatment system (currently being refined)

You can fill bottles and flasks with water. (The custom interface is currently being refined.)

In winter, the temperature drops very low; you will need warm clothing.
