Ever wanted to show your pets a little extra love in Minecraft? With this simple Bedrock add-on, you can! By sneaking and right-clicking, you can pet any mob in the game whether its your loyal wolf, a cute cat, or even a wandering villager.

This add-on doesnt change gameplay balance or add complicated features its just a wholesome way to interact with your world. Perfect for roleplay, casual play, or simply making your adventures feel more alive.

Give your pets (and everything else) the love they deserve!
