Kaiju Craft is a Minecraft Bedrock add-on that adds giant kaiju bosses, powerful weapons, and rare materials. Players can battle massive creatures like Wardenzilla and Golem Kong, collect their drops, and craft destructive gear and unique items. Some kaiju can even be calmed and tamed using special items, allowing players to turn these powerful monsters into allies. With new crafting materials, boss fights, and abilities, Kaiju Craft transforms Minecraft into a world of epic monster battles and kaiju adventures!
