Real Vanilla Sit

Seamless Sitting on Stairs, Slabs, and Carpets The Vanilla Way

Real Vanilla Sit is an optimized addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition that lets players sit seamlessly on any vanilla stairs, slabs, and carpets.

Trailer

Features

- Sit on any vanilla block of the following types:

- Stairs (as long as theyre not upside down)

- Bottom slabs

- Carpets

- Sit by interacting with supported blocks using an empty hand no sneaking, no items required. Its that simple.

- Two players cannot sit on the same seat at the same time, ensuring realistic multiplayer behavior.

- Sit perfectly aligned with the stairs direction for natural posture.

- Prevents sitting on upside-down stairs and top slabs to ensure logical behavior.

- No commands needed just sit and play.

- Optimized by design with no interval loops and powered by the official Minecraft Scripting API v2.0.0 (@minecraft/server).

Perfect For

- Survival and SMP servers

- Roleplay and furniture builds

- Quality-of-life enhancements

- Addon packs that need a clean sitting system

How to Sit

- Right-click or tap on supported blocks with an empty hand to sit.

- Press jump, sneak, or break the block to stand up.

Compatibility

- Multiplayer worlds

- Realms support

- Dedicated Servers

Installation

- Download the Real Vanilla Sit.mcaddon file.

- Open it to automatically import into Minecraft Bedrock.

- In your world settings, activate both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack.

- No experimental toggles required.

- Play and sit!
