Are you a true adventurer? RUINS adds a variety of forgotten and ruined structures to your Minecraft world! From mysterious ancient temples and trap-filled dungeons to crumbling fortresses and desolate abandoned settlements, each structure holds secrets and valuable loot waiting for you to discover. The RUINS addon is an unofficial port of the classic Java mod "Ruins (Structure Spawning System)" by atomicstrykergrumpy. My goal is to bring the original mod's fun exploration structures and years of great community contributions to Bedrock Edition.

- The addon is now Achievement Friendly

- This addon brings a great sense of adventure to Minecraft by introducing a variety of randomly generated structures such as houses, temples, and even castles in the sky, along with a variety of loot.

- The amount of ruins available in this addon is staggering and will never leave you bored. Each structure is meticulously designed and has many different elements for you to explore.

- Some ruins can be dangerous or seemingly random...treat them wisely!
