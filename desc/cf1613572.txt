Capes VM | Cosmetics Bedrock

Redefining cosmetics in Minecraft Bedrock.

Capes VM is a lightweight and optimized cosmetics pack that adds 67 unique capes to choose from, while keeping 100% vanilla style and without breaking any player animations.

FEATURES:

67 Unique Capes - From Minecon classics to exclusive designs like Lunar Client, Akatsuki, Totoro and more.

Vanilla Movement - Designed to move naturally with the player. Does not affect Elytra or animations.

Lightweight & Clean - Low file size, no lag, and everything perfectly organized in subpacks.

Easy to Use - Just select your favorite cape from the subpacks settings.

Includes popular capes like: Allay, Axolotl, Migrator, Minecon, Warden, Cherry Blossom, Discord, Hero, Optifine Remastered, Kirby, Lunar, and many more.
