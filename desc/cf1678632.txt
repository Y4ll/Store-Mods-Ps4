EARDING WEAPON V4.0

EARDING WEAPON is a Minecraft Bedrock combat addon that adds powerful weapons with unique abilities, custom effects, cooldowns, dashes, projectiles, and special domains.

Each weapon has its own playstyle and 4 unique skills, allowing you to create different combos and fight enemies in your own way.
You can explore the world and discover unique structures in specific biomes, such as the Taiga.

Skill System

Every weapon has 4 unique skills with individual cooldowns.

Skills can be activated using different combinations of:

-

Use Item

-

Sneak + Use Item

-

Sneak + Jump

-

Sneak + Swing

A cooldown display helps you keep track of your abilities during combat.
