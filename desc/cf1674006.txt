Verxity is a mysterious and intelligent horror companion for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. He can interact with players, answer questions, follow commands, explore the world, and help with survival tasks. Verxity is not just a normal companionhis behavior can become strange, unpredictable, and terrifying.

Verxity can follow you, mine blocks, mine 33 areas, chop down trees within a large radius, collect resources, fight hostile mobs, use diamond tools, and assist you while exploring. You can give him different commands and ask him questions, allowing him to react to what is happening around him.

But be careful... verxity has a darker side. During your adventures, mysterious events, creepy interactions, unexpected behavior, and terrifying moments can occur. The more you explore, the more mysterious Morbosity can become.

Explore abandoned places, survive dangerous nights, gather resources, build your base, and discover what Morbosity is capable of. You never know what he might do next.

Verxity your companion, your helper... and perhaps something much more disturbing.
