Expedition Blueprints adds blueprint items that allow you to place prebuilt expedition structures in a survival-friendly way. Instead of building everything block by block, you obtain blueprints through villager trading, gather the required materials, and place the structure directly in the world.

All placements follow strict rules to prevent floating structures and keep everything aligned with vanilla gameplay.

How It Works

- Craft a Basic Blueprint (paper + feather + ink sac).

- Upgrade it to:

- Refined Blueprint (Basic + emerald)

- Polished Blueprint (Basic + redstone)

- Use the Drafting Table to craft structure blueprints by combining the correct blank blueprint with required materials.

- Hold a blueprint, look at a solid block, and use the item.

- A confirmation prompt appears before placement. If the ground is flat and you have the materials, the structure is built and materials are consumed.

Features

- Survivalfriendly structure placement

- Craftable blueprints with a Drafting Table

- Villager trading integration

- Automatic structure rotation

- Footprint preview before placement

- Placement confirmation

- Ground support validation

- No floating or partially placed structures

Starter Items

On first spawn, players receive a small starter kit:

- Tent Blueprint

- Campfire

- 12 white wool

Blueprints From Villager Trades

Blueprints can also be obtained through vanilla villager trades:

- Tent Blueprint Shepherd (Level 1)

- Villager Camp Blueprint Farmer (Level 3)

- Hunters Hut Blueprint Fletcher (Level 3)

- Bonfire Blueprint Cartographer (Level 2)

- Wayside Shrine Blueprint Cleric (Level 2)

- Watchtower Blueprint Mason (Level 4)
