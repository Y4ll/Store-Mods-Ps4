Custom Blaze Loot Drop

Custom Blaze Loot Drop addon tweaks the loot of the Blaze, allowing them to drop custom number of blaze rods upon defeat. Whether you're looking to speed up potion brewing, reward Nether exploration, or build a more balanced progression system, this addon brings more purpose to every smoky battle. No need for scripts or complex commandsthis addon is fully vanilla-compatible, seamlessly integrating into your world without breaking core mechanics. Its perfect for map makers, survival players, or creators building custom challenges, quest systems, or unique economies. Make every Blaze drop count, and bring a new spark to your Nether adventures!

There are options when you want to customize the loot drop of Blaze:

1. 1 to 10 Loot Drops

2. 11 to 20 Loot Drops

3. 21 to 30 Loot Drops

4. 31 to 40 Loot Drops

5. 41 to 50 Loot Drops

6. 51 to 60 Loot Drops

7. 61 to 70 Loot Drops

8. 71 to 80 Loot Drops

9. 81 to 90 Loot Drops

10. 91 to 100 Loot Drops (Default)
