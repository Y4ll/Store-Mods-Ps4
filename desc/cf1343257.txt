LumaShade is a soft cinematic bloom shader pack built for the new Vibrant Visuals rendering mode in Minecraft Bedrock. Designed to deliver warm lighting, gentle fog, and atmospheric godrays for a cinematic experience.

LumaShade A soft cinematic bloom shader pack for Minecraft Bedrock, wrapping every scene in smooth bloom, gentle haze, and warm atmospheric glow.

Luma Shade is made for new Vibrant Visuals and will work on IOS, Android, Xbox, PlayStation and Windows.

For the best experience, it's suggested you use the following brightness.

What LumaShade Changes:

Sky & Atmosphere - Zenith and horizon colours hand-tuned across sixteen points of the day cycle Rayleigh and Mie scattering shaped separately for sun and moon, so dawn and dusk each get their own character Sun glare shaping for soft, wide godrays through gaps and foliage

Fog - Seventeen fog profiles mapped across all eighty-eight biomes Birch, cherry, jungle, taiga, savanna, mesa, desert, swamp, snow and pale garden all read differently Dedicated fog for the Nether's crimson, warped, soul sand and basalt regions, plus the End

Lighting - Seven lighting, one global, tuned passes for every Nether variant and the End Soft shadows Coloured local lights, so torches, lanterns and fire each cast their own warm tone rather than one flat glow

Colour - Separate highlight, midtone and shadow grading with per-channel contrast, saturation, gain, offset and gamma Its own grading pass for the End Custom colourmaps for grass, foliage, birch, evergreen, swamp and mangrove

Extras - PBR fallback values for blocks, entities, particles.
