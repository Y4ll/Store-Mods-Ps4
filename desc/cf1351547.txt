Craftpunk is an alternative-futuresinspired Minecraft add-on that blends futuristic technology, robotics, and structures into survival gameplay. Build with advanced materials, craft electronic components, and encounter new robotic mobs while exploring the Overworld.

This add-on is still a work in progress and was created for participation in CurseForge ModJam 2025. It currently brings a mix of cyberpunk technology and solarpunk design into your world. Since the project is still under development, not all planned features are included yet but new content is steadily being added.
