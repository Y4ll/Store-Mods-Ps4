MY DISCORD

In Flora Shader, you can experience magical nights filled with a beautiful atmosphere and a strong, radiant sun that lights up the world with vibrant colors. Every moment feels more alive, creating a more immersive and visually stunning Minecraft experience but with Flora Theme.

I used the name from an old game, just like Lumina Visual Shader.

This add-on does not include player.json.
This add-on does not require experimental features.
If the add-on does not work, please report it on Discord.
If you want to request an add-on, please make a request on Discord.
If you want to collaborate or work together, please contact me on Discord.
