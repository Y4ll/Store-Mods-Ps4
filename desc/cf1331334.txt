Are you the type of modded Minecraft player that makes a beeline for the designated "end game" and then gives up on ever playing again once you get there? Do you wish there was a way to make the process take significantly longer? Do you love Decayed Reality, but wish it weren't so short? Do you sit down on your chest full of Compressed Ores and wish there was an addon that didn't just hand things to you on a silver platter?

This might be the addon for you!

-------------------------------------

Start off by crafting Crystal Matrix for a couple diamonds and slowly upgrade to Neutronium and after getting materials from around the whole world.. craft them into Infinity Ingots, The Most EXPENSIVE and also powerful item you can get... Use these to craft the New Most OVERPOWERED armor, tools and even bows and crossbows you can get your hand on.. After all that, Forget worrying about dying, forget worrying about bossfights, as now you are eternal.. aside from that cringey description, enjoy the addon!
