This small quality-of-life Minecraft mod helps players avoid losing valuable tools and armor by providing automatic low durability warnings for both inventory items and equipped gear. When an item reaches a critical durability level, players receive an on-screen alert along with a sound notification, making it easier to repair or replace gear before it breaks. Simple, lightweight, and highly practical, this mod enhances everyday survival gameplay without changing core mechanics.

Attention: To use this mod, please enable the Beta API in your world settings.
