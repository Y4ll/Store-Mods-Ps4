About Twisted Lands

A fantasy addon filled with adventure, exploration, challenges, and magic. While some features are based on and inspired by the old Orespawn mod, it retains its original essence, standing out with new mobs, dungeons, biomes, dimensions, and equipment. This addon will be notable for its vast number of new additions to the game; many may seem crazy or over-the-top, but it brings a completely new experience to Minecraft, and perhaps for some players, something reminiscent of Terraria.

In this add-on you will have an incredible experience fighting against strong monsters, giant kaiju, exploring new biomes and dungeons.

IMPORTANT NOTICE! - THIS ADDON IS STILL IN ALPHA STAGE, SO MANY FEATURES HAVE NOT YET BEEN ADDED

This addon is not a port of Orespawn. All resources are originally ours, however we use Orespawn as a reimagining.
