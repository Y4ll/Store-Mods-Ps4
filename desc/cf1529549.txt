Capytator is a feature-rich QoL add-on for Minecraft Bedrock that automates tedious manual tasks from felling a tree to integrated auto-smelting. Spend less time grinding and more time building.

Designed for survival. Built for solo or multiplayer.

What's new in v1.7.0?

Added new features:

Fluid Collector (Empty Buckets)

Collect any fluid source within an 16-blocks radius at once using empty buckets.

Works for Lava, Water, and Powder Snow.

Fluid Collector toggle can be found in Any Tool menu.

Moss Clearer (Hoe)

Basically it works like Snow Clearer but for Moss Block and Moss Carpet, pale variant is supported.

Natural Trees Only (Axe settings)

Toggle it on to restrict Tree Capitator to only breaking logs connected to natural leaves.

Leaf Decay also no longer break other trees' leaves.

Snow Clearer can now clear the cluster of Powder Snow using a shovel.

You can now trigger Snow Clearer for Snow Layer by interacting with the Snow Layer while holding a shovel.

HOW IT WORKS

Most miner features require crouching (sneak) to activate for example, sneak + break an ore block to mine the entire ore vein.

Hammers are the exception they work normally, sneaking switches them to single-block mode instead.

If you don't like sneaking, toggle "Activate by Sneaking" off in Capytator > General to flip this; bulk actions trigger normally, and sneaking becomes the single-block override.

On First Join

If the add-on was activated correctly, you'll receive a message and "Capytator" item when you join the world for the first time.

Interact or use the item to open your personal settings, where you can enable/disable individual features. Sneak and interact to instantly disables everything for yourself.

Craft with one stick (shapeless) if you lose the item.

MAIN FEATURES

Smart Tool-Switch

Automatically swaps to the best matching tool in your inventory for whatever block you're mining. When you hit a mob, it immediately swaps to a sword (or axe as fallback), ensuring you're always ready for combat.

Prefers higher-tier tools and skips tools with fewer than 5 durability remaining.

Works seamlessly while mining and switches your tools instantly when the block type changes without stopping mid-mine.

[!] This only works when you're holding a tool to prevent it from messing up your hotbar.

Axe

Tree Capitator

Chop down an entire tree in one swing. Supports all wood types, including Nether stems and their stripped variants.

[*] Leaves are decayed immediately. No more floating canopies.

Auto-Replant

Chop at the base of the tree to automatically consume a sapling from your inventory and replant it. This is skipped if you don't have any.

[*] Only works if the logs have natural leaves.

Mushroom Capitator

Basically just a Tree Capitator, but for Huge Mushrooms. Silk Touch is supported.

Leaf miner

The manual version of Leaf Decay. Clear connected leaves in one go with an Axe, or Shears.

All leaf or tree types are supported, including the custom ones from other add-ons*

Pickaxe

Vein Miner

Breaking an ore will also break the connected ones the entire vein at once. Compatible with Fortune & Silk Touch enchantments.

[*] Tool tiers are respected Vein Miner won't work if you use an insufficient tool tier.

[*] Works for all ore types and some custom ores.

Auto-Smelt

Automatically smelt raw ores into ingots when broken. Works with Vein Miner and grants XP (from smelting) directly to the miner.

[!] Auto-Smelt (Pickaxe) is OFF by default. You have to enable it in Capytator > Pickaxe.

[!] This won't work if your Pickaxe (or Hammer) has Silk Touch enchantment.

Stone Miner

Mines connected decorative stones, including Magma and Obsidian.

Supported blocks for Stone Miner:

1. Granite

2. Andesite

3. Diorite

4. Blackstone

5. Gilded Blackstone

6. Tuff

7. Calcite

8. Magma

9. Obsidian

10. Crying Obsidian

11. Glowstone (Silk Touch supported)

Ice Miner

Basically a Stone Miner, but for ice blocks. Silk touch is supported.
