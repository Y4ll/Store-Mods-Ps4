The Forgotten Legends: Age of Renewal

JOIN NOW! to my Discord server! :D

TFL: AoR is a Dark Fantasy themed addon for MCBE, here you'll find a great variety of creatures, structures, items, weapons and more!

ENTITIES

It is dangerous out there! but we encourage you to explore and go on an adventure encountering a lot of new creatures, from undead to fire breathing drakes!

UNDEAD

Some of the most common foes out there, Undead or Nekros, are formidable and appear in many presentations, from basic units such as the Ochi Nekros to a Nekros Colossus, exploring their dungeons reward you with brass equipment and if lucky enough, the Necromancer armor set, which allows you to use the Necromancy magic!

ORCS

The green skins, brutal enemies which can be found in their villages or scouting, sometimes they can ambush you!, inside the villages, in the Ringleader's hall, you may find a "Warlords Effigy", this totem allows you to summon a horde that spawns a ton of orcs near your position, if you killed enough before you summon a horde, you will encounter the Warlord and another surprise at the final wave, killing the Warlord will give you some of it's equipment, it can be it's sword, or helmet, both giving you some pretty cool abilities, that if mixed together, makes you a very dangerous warrior.

I also wanted to include Wargs in this category, some of them can be tamed using rotten flesh, they are giant wolves.

GREAT SPIDERS

Probably your worst nightmare, Great Spiders are simple creatures that can spawn in the roofed forests, spruce or underground, the boss of this race is the Spider Matriarch, a creature from the depths of hell that drops spider sacks, using that item allows you to summon the spiderlings, small spiders that will fight for you.

HUMANS

Humans appear as bandits inside structures or Mercenaries, people that appear accross your world that can be recruited with gold ingots, pretty useful!

WYVERNS

Medium sized venomous winged reptiles, Wyverns are the most efficient traveling method, fighting one may not be easy, specially because they are never alone, when you kill a Wyvern it may drop an egg, place it and wait 10 minutes to spawn a baby wyvern, which can be tamed using salmon or any other fish, make it grow and it will be able to be used as mount, it is the fastest creature!.

Fire Drake

The most attractive and dangerous creature out there (by the moment...), Fire Drakes are feral creatures that destroy anything with their fire breath, these cannibalistic creatures can detect the player at very far distances, so if you notice a nest and you are not ready, avoid it! BUT! you can tame these! kill one first, get their meat and now, resist another's attacks while you feed it with the same meat to tame it, ue a saddle and the drake will be ready to be ridden, using a normal stick will trigger their fire breath, be carefu, it is super destructive!

STRUCTURES

In your adventures you'll encounter different and interesting structures!

NECROMANCY

It is a pretty simple magic system, interacting with the necromancer scythe will summon some bone jaws around you, BUT, if you ware holding one of the necromancy spell books and sneaking, when you'll interact undead will spawn depending on the book you're using!

SILVER EQUIPMENT

Silver ore is a very special one, it allows you to craft a whole set of armor and tools, each tool can deal +4 extra damage to undead enemies IGNORING ARMOUR, and the armor can make extra protection against undead attacks!
