Transform your Minecraft Bedrock world with the Falling Meteors Addon, introducing thrilling new gameplay elements:

Features:

-

New Armors with Abilities:

- Meteorite Armor: Grants item magnetization, attracting nearby items.

- Kreknorite Armor: Provides fire resistance, keeping you safe from flames.

- Frezarite Armor: Offers underwater breathing for deep-sea exploration.

-

New Tools and Weapons:

- Tools crafted from meteorite materials have item magnetization abilities.

- Kreknorite Sword: Ignites enemies on hit with its fire-based attack.

-

Random Meteor Generation: Watch as meteors crash into your world, creating unique challenges and opportunities. There are 3 types of meteors:

- Meteorite: Basic yet powerful, with magnetization properties.

-

- Frezarite: Perfect for aquatic adventurers.

-

- Kreknorite: Ideal for fire-dominant combat scenarios.

-

Meteor Shield: A crucial item crafted using the Meteor Creeper's drops to protect your base from meteor crashes. This shield can be activated using a Red Meteor Gem. When activated, it generates a light beam similar to a beacon, creating a protective aura to safeguard against meteor crashes. The Meteor Shield protects an area within a 100-block radius when activated.

-

Meteor Creeper: A new hostile mob that explodes faster than a regular creeper and drops materials necessary for crafting the Meteor Shield.

-

Meteor Summoning Items: Summon meteors at will for a more action-packed experience.
