Are you the contents of your chest of drawers in disarray? Is sorting a pain in the ass? Try this addon! This add-on is sorted by simply tapping on the chest!

Blocks supported

Sneak for 3 seconds and you will get an indication that it is on.

If you press a chest while holding an item while it is on, it will be sorted.(Any item.)

When off, sneek for 3 seconds again to turn it off.
