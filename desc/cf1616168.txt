Chroma Visuals is my colored light pack. Every light source in the game gets its own color, and the rest of the pack is built to show that off after dark.

What this changes

Colored light sources

This is the pack. Nothing glows the same generic orange any more. Each source throws its own color onto the blocks around it, which is why caves and night bases look completely different here.

- Redstone torch glows red

- Soul torch and soul lantern glow cyan

- Lantern glows warm orange

- End rod glows cool white

- Campfire glows orange

Northern lights

Auroras run across the sky at night. Away from any light source they are the brightest thing in the world, which is exactly the contrast the pack is after.

Oceans and water

Deep blue open water with the sky sitting on the surface, and shallows clear enough to see through.

Forests

Lush and full greens with proper shading under the canopy, so woods stay dense instead of going flat at midday.

Cherry groves

Soft pink landscapes that hold their own against the rest of the pack rather than getting washed over by it.

Villages at night

Lanterns and torches light the buildings and paths around them, so a village after dark reads as lived in rather than as a dark shape.

Requirements

THIS PACK IS FOR VIBRANT VISUALS ONLY
IT DOES NOT WORK WITH MB LOADER

Vibrant Visuals must be ON. This pack is built for Bedrock's Vibrant Visuals renderer. The pack will not do anything if it is off.

Where to turn it on: Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals

If that toggle is not there, your device or version does not support Vibrant Visuals yet.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Chroma Visuals

- Turn on Settings > Video > Vibrant Visuals

- If the lighting still looks the same, leave the world and load it again once

FAQ

The pack is not working. Nothing changed after I installed it.
I get this comment a lot, and almost every single time the reason is the same. Vibrant Visuals is turned off.

This pack only works when Vibrant Visuals is ON. That is written clearly at the top of this page. With it off, the pack loads fine but nothing on your screen changes, because there is no renderer for it to work with. The pack is not broken.
