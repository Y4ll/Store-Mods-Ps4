X-Ray Ultimate

Take your mining and exploration to the next level with a powerful, clean, and efficient X-Ray experience. Designed for players who want instant access to ores, hidden caves, and underground structureswithout ruining the natural feel of the game.

Gravel is replaced with redstone torch indicators, making unstable blocks instantly visible. Avoid sudden cave-ins and mine safely with clear, easy-to-spot warnings.

Easily spot hidden caves, mineshafts, and underground structures with clear visibilityno more blind digging or missed discoveries.

Key Features

- Night-vision style visibility for all ores in every graphics mode (Simple, Fancy, etc.)

- Outlined and highlighted ores for instant detection, even in dark caves

- Gravel replaced with redstone torch indicators for safety from falling blocks

- Structure visibilityeasily locate caves, mineshafts, and hidden areas

- Clean and minimal designno clutter, only what matters

- Performance-friendlysmooth gameplay with no lag

Recommended Settings

- Turn off Smooth Lighting for the clearest ore visibility

- Use Simple or Fancy graphicsfully supported

- Increase brightness for maximum clarity (optional)

Installation

- Place the resource pack in your resource_packs folder or import it directly into the game

- Open or create a world go to Resource Packs

- Enable Texture Pack

- Start your world and enjoy full underground visibility
