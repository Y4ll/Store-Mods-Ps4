This addon, based on popular Java Hammers mods, adds Hammers and Excavators that breaks blocks in large areas while keeping it balanced and vanilla-friendly.

Hammers and Excavators mine blocks in a 3x3 area.

(To mine single blocks, simply crouch/sneak.)

You can preview the mining area with an outline before breaking any blocks.

Specialized for stone, deepslate, netherrack, end stone, and similar blocks.

Designed for dirt, gravel, sand, and other soft materials.

Individual blocks are mined slower than Pickaxes or Shovels to keep gameplay balanced.
Each mined block reduces durability(Unbreaking Enchantment Works).

RESOURCES PACKS AND BEHARVIORS PACKS NEEDED

This Addon does not disable achievements.
