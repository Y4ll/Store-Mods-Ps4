Special Villagers

Special villagers are a new variant of villagers who work in special jobs; you can convert any unskilled villager into a special villager using their corresponding job block

-Beekeeper:

It can spawn naturally in any village, builds a work place only once the first time it is generated, its trades are based on anything related to honey and flowers, uses the new block "Apiary" to work.

-Pyrotechnic:

It can spawn naturally in any village, builds a workspace only once the first time it spawns, its trades are based on anything related to gunpowder and fireworks, uses the new block "Pyrotechnic table" to work.

-Architect:

It can spawn naturally in any village, he builds a great house with 4 beds once the first time he spawns (this is him work area and also a house for himself and any other villagers that don't have a bed) his trades are based on anything furniture related, he uses the new block "Blueprint table" to work.

-Baker:

It can spawn naturally in any village, builds a workspace only once the first time it spawns, its trades are based on anything related to food (not meat as that is the butcher's job, the cook is mostly in charge of buying and selling the new foods introduced in the addon), uses the new block "Cooking table" to work.

-Carpenter:

It can spawn naturally in any village, builds a workspace only once the first time it spawns, its trades are based on anything related to wood and axes, uses the new block "Wood cutter" to work.
