Addon Lore

Of course we've added lore to this addon, we're over-the-top! Enjoy!

Thousands of year ago, a powerful being descended. With him, he brought 7 powerful swords, one for every race of mobs. After blessing the races with the swords, he left. For some time, everyone was content and happy with their blessed swords. However, one race wanted them all...

The Players wanted to collect every sword for themselves, so that they could become the strongest race. They started raiding everywhere, looting whatever swords they could find. Unfortunately for the Players, the creator of the swords came down from where he once was. He saw the chaos and death spread by the players in their power hungry raiding. He decided he needed to punish the Players for what they had done. With his powerful blade, he rained down lasers from the very heavens, striking all the players down. Once the players were defeated, he collected his swords once again, and left...

Thousands of years have past, and it appears the mysterious person returned. With him, came a challenge...

A trail to see if the new generation of Players are worthy of his powerful swords. Are you ready to accept the trail, and gather all of his swords?

The holograms and guardians in this are complete opposites to the Dreadful Horrors.

Every sword has a custom ability. Don't worry, using the ability does NOT take up durability.

Recipes

The Swords

Starlight Sword

The Starlight Sword, made from a Star of a Wither. This is the only sword that can be crafting in the addon. The Starlight sword has 1,000 Durability, and does 8 attack damage. When you use its ability, you get every Beacon effect for a few seconds.

Echo Claymore

The Echo Claymore, made from Echo Shard and multiple other precious minerals. It has 1,000 Durability, and does 9 attack damage. You obtain this sword in Ancient Cities. It has the same chance to be in a chest as a Ward Armor Trim. Its ability is summoning Sonic Shrieks from the sky. Its cooldown is very small, so you can spam the airstrikes.

Eternal Blade

The Eternal Blade. Made from long forgotten materials, its purple blade shines in the Desert Heat. It has 1,000 Durability, and does 8 attack damage. You find the Eternal Blade in the Desert Temples chests. When its ability is activated, the Eternal Blade will raise Pressure Plates from the Ground. Do not step on them, they will explode instantly upon contact.

Firebrand Blade

The Firebrand Blade, made from Rubies heated in a Blast Furnace. It has 1,000 Durability, and does 8 attack damage. You have a chance to find the Firebrand Blade in a Nether Fortress chest. When its ability is used, it will summon a Fire Twister that will aggravates mobs. After a few seconds, it will explode in fire.
