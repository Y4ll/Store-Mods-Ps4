********************** OTG BACKPACK **********************

OTG Backpack is a modular survival backpack addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

Start with a simple Basic Backpack and upgrade it through multiple tiers as your adventure grows:

Basic > Copper > Iron > Gold > Diamond > Netherite

Each tier increases your storage potential and unlocks more backpack utility, turning the backpack into a portable survival system for exploring, mining, building, traveling, and long-term worlds.

OTG Backpack is designed to be more than just extra storage. It includes portable workstations, fluid tanks, modules, cosmetics, placement support, quick transfer tools, mobile-friendly controls, and world settings for admins.

********************** MAIN FEATURES **********************

Upgradeable Backpack Tiers

Upgrade your backpack through six tiers:

Basic Copper Iron Gold Diamond Netherite

Higher tiers provide more storage and unlock additional backpack systems, including:

- Portable workstations

- Fluid tanks

- Built-in crafting and smelting

- Module slots

- Advanced utility features

Netherite Backpack

The Netherite Backpack is the highest backpack tier and provides the largest storage capacity.

Netherite features include:

- 126 storage slots

- Support for the Overpack Module

- Access to advanced portable workstations

- Stronger effects from selected modules

- Improved large-storage UI

- Fire resistance when dropped as an item

Overpack Module

The Overpack Module is an endgame upgrade available for Netherite Backpacks.

It adds a second storage page containing another 126 slots, making it useful for:

- Long mining trips

- Large construction projects

- Exploration

- Resource gathering

- Adventure supplies

Page 1 and Page 2 remain separate and can be switched from inside the backpack.

Stockpile Module

The Stockpile Module provides bulk storage separate from normal backpack slots and Overpack Page 2.

Features include:

- Two item filters

- Automatic storage of matching items

- Large virtual item counts

- Individual withdrawal controls

- Magnet compatibility

Stockpile is useful for carrying large amounts of frequently collected resources without filling the main backpack inventory.

Built-in Crafting Table

Craft directly inside your backpack using the built-in 33 Crafting Table.

The Crafting panel supports compatible vanilla and OTG Backpack recipes.

You can also deploy a normal Crafting Table into the world when needed.

Portable Workstations

OTG Backpack provides portable workstation access depending on backpack tier, installed modules, and World Settings.

Available utilities include:

- Built-in Crafting Table

- Virtual Furnace

- Stonecutter

- Anvil

- Max Enchanting Setup

- Brewing Stand

- Smithing Table

- Ender Chest

- Rest Campfire

- Sorting

Successful workstation deployment automatically closes the backpack.

If the area is blocked or unsafe, the backpack remains open so you can reposition and try again.

Max Enchanting Setup

Netherite Backpacks can deploy a complete temporary level-30 enchanting station.

The setup includes:

- A functional Enchantment Table

- A complete Bookshelf arrangement

- Maximum enchanting power

- Automatic rotation so the entrance faces the player

Players do not need to carry or spend Bookshelves.

The setup can automatically clear harmless ground cover such as:

- Grass

- Tall Grass

- Flowers

- Ferns

- Snow Layers

- Bushes

- Firefly Bushes

- Leaf Litter

The area must still be flat, supported, and free from solid blocks, liquids, carpets, or other obstacles.

The temporary structure:

- Cannot be mined for free materials

- Does not drop Bookshelves or the Enchantment Table

- Is protected against explosions and pistons

- Disappears when the owner moves too far away

- Disappears after death, teleporting, portal travel, or changing dimensions

- Is replaced when another portable workstation is deployed
