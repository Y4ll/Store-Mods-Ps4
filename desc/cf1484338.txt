Scaramouche Blue

is a cosmetic resource pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition that replaces the standard helmet with a Scaramouche-style hat featuring a mystical blue theme.

The hat has a floating puppet-like design with blue energy details and hanging ornaments, inspired by fantasy aesthetics.

Pick the color you want for the subpack :

- blue

- yellow

- green

- red

- purple

- black

Perfect for players who want a unique cosmetic look while wearing a helmet.
