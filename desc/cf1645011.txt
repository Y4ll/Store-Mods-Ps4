Ultimate Treecapitator

An achievement-friendly, lag-free treecapitator addon that chops down whole trees instantly, auto-collects drops, and features an advanced build-protection system. No experimental toggles required!

100% Achievement Friendly & No Beta APIs!

Unlike most scripting addons, this pack is designed to be completely Achievement Friendly. You can keep earning your Xbox achievements in your survival worlds while enjoying the lumberjack life!

Even better: NO Experimental Toggles or Beta APIs are required! Just apply the behavior pack to your world, and it works instantly right out of the box.

Stop Destroying Your Wooden Builds!

Most treecapitator addons have a massive flaw: if you accidentally use your axe on your wooden house, the whole house collapses.

Not this one!
The Ultimate Treecapitator is built with a highly advanced Smart Build Protection system. It uses custom script logic to scan for valid soils (dirt, grass, podzol) and natural leaf blocks. If a log is placed by a player or is part of a building, the script simply ignores it.

Try to chop a wooden build while sneaking, and you will get a safe, real-time warning on your screen: [ Breaking not for build ]. Your starter bases and survival houses are 100% safe!

Key Features

- 100% Safe For Builds: Completely ignores player-placed logs and structures. It only works on naturally generated trees attached to valid soil.

- One-Block Chop: Destroy massive trees, including all connected logs and leaves, just by breaking the bottom block.

- Auto-Collect Loot: No more running around to pick up items. All wood, saplings, apples, and XP are automatically pulled to your exact location the moment the tree falls.

- Vanilla Durability & Unbreaking: Fully respects vanilla mechanics. Chopping a 20-block tree removes 20 durability points, and it calculates the Unbreaking enchantment perfectly.

- Real-time Actionbar UI: Sneak with an axe to see [ Treecapitator Active ] in green. Stand up to see [ Treecapitator Disable ] in red. You always know when it is on!

- Fast Leaf Decay: Leaves break instantly alongside the logs, keeping your survival world clean and lag-free.

How to Use

- Equip any Axe (Wood, Stone, Iron, Gold, Diamond, or Netherite).

- Sneak (Crouch) while looking at a natural tree.

- Wait for the green [ Treecapitator Active ] message on your screen.

- Break the bottom log.

- Watch the tree fall and all items teleport directly to your inventory!
