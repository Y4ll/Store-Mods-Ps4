BedrockWorldBorder

Java got world borders. Bedrock didn't. So we fixed that.

Ever wanted to stop your players from wandering off into the infinite void of chunks you'll never use? Maybe you're running a survival server and someone's decided to walk 50,000 blocks in one direction "just to see what's out there." Or maybe your Realm is getting a bit too spread out and the lag is starting to show. Either way, you need a wall a big, beautiful, impossible-to-miss wall.

BedrockWorldBorder gives you exactly that. Proper Java Edition-style world borders, complete with towering animated particle walls that stretch 192 blocks into the sky. They look fantastic, they work in every dimension, and your players will absolutely know when they've reached the edge of civilisation.

What Can You Do With It?

Set up independent borders for the Overworld, Nether and End each with its own size, centre point and look. Pick from 11 animated particle styles (each with a little preview icon in the dropdown, because guessing is for chumps), choose a colour, dial the opacity up or down, and make each dimension look exactly how you want it.

When a player hits the border, you've got options. Teleport them smoothly back inside (without that annoying camera snap you're welcome), or give them a physics-based knockback that sends them bouncing off the wall. Both feel great. Knockback is particularly fun for minigame arenas.

You can also lock down the outside entirely. Turn on interaction prevention and players can't break, place or interact with anything beyond the border. No sneaky chest raiding in the wilderness.

Custom Areas

New in 4.0, and the thing people kept asking for. Your survival world is a 10,000-block border around spawn but your PvP arena is a few hundred blocks wide and 10,000 blocks away. Previously, the moment you teleported someone there the border dragged them straight back. Not ideal for a fight.

Now you create a Custom Area. It's a second, completely separate border with its own full set of settings, so the arena can be knockback with red hearts while the main world stays teleport with classic blue stripes. Players are free inside any border, and the addon remembers which one they were last in step out of the arena and the arena pushes you back, never the main border 10,000 blocks away. Make as many as you like. Overlap them if you want. Disable the main border and keep the areas, and you've got an arena-only setup where players roam freely until they're inside one.

Automation

Want the border to shrink by a chunk every in-game day? Want another addon to move it? Everything the menu can do, a command block can do too, via scriptevents:

/scriptevent worldborder:shrink overworld 1
/scriptevent worldborder:area create pvp_arena overworld 10000 0 12 PvP Arena
/scriptevent worldborder:style pvp_arena hearts

Setting It Up

- Download the addon and open the .mcaddon Minecraft imports it for you

- Add both the behaviour pack and resource pack to your world

- Run /worldborder:menu

- Pick your dimension, set your size, choose a style, and you're done

Everything's configured through a clean in-game GUI with tooltips on every field. No config files to edit, no JSON to mess with.

One important thing: operators are not exempt from the border. Realm owners asked for a border that admins can't wander through by accident, so passing through is a strict whitelist. If you want to cross it yourself, open the menu, go to Bypass Whitelist and add yourself. Takes five seconds.
