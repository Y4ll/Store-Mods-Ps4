OP Death Drops

OP Death Drops is a fun and rewarding Minecraft Bedrock addon that completely changes what happens when a player dies. Instead of death being only a punishment, every player death can now reward you with powerful and valuable OP items.

Features

- OP rewards drop whenever a player dies.

- Multiple random rewards can drop from a single death.

- Powerful enchanted Netherite weapons and tools.

- High-level enchanted Netherite armor.

- Chance to obtain Elytra.

- Totems of Undying can appear as rewards.

- Enchanted Golden Apples included in the loot pool.

- Diamonds, Diamond Blocks, Netherite Ingots and other valuable resources.

- Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic-style reward progression.

- Randomized drops make every death different.

- Multiplayer compatible.

- Lightweight and simple to use.

Reward System

Whenever a player dies, OP items are dropped directly at the player's death location. Each death provides multiple rewards, with an additional chance to receive even more loot.

The reward pool contains everything from valuable resources to extremely powerful equipment, meaning every death has the possibility of giving you something special.

Possible OP Rewards

- Enchanted Netherite Sword

- Enchanted Netherite Pickaxe

- Enchanted Netherite Axe

- Enchanted Netherite Armor

- Powerful Bow

- Enchanted Trident

- Totem of Undying

- Elytra

- Enchanted Golden Apple

- Netherite Ingots

- Diamond Blocks

- Other high-value rewards

How To Use

- Import the addon into Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

- Activate the Behavior Pack in your world.

- Enter the world normally.

- When a player dies, random OP rewards will appear at the death location.

Perfect For

- Survival Worlds

- SMP Servers

- Challenge Worlds

- Multiplayer Survival

- Fun OP Gameplay

Important Note

This addon is designed to make player deaths more rewarding and entertaining. Because the rewards can be extremely powerful, it can significantly change normal survival progression.
