Zombies are one of the oldest entities in Minecraft. They're the most common undead mob and probably the first monster you encounter at night or in caves.

This skinpack lets you use textures from all zombie variants, faithfully copied from their original models. It includes the classic Zombie, Husk, Drowned, and even rare versions like the Poisonous Potato Zombie from snapshots! We've also added textures inspired by Minecraft Dungeons and Minecraft Earth.

The pack features authentic zombie arm animations for both idle and attack poses. And for a legendary touch, we've included exclusive Herobrine zombie variants with glowing eyes. Perfect for bringing new life to your undead encounters!
