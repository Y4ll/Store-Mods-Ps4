Minecraft Fortify Reworked

Minecraft Fortify Reworked is a large Minecraft Bedrock equipment and combat expansion. It adds ten themed armor sets, complete tool collections, specialized weapons, custom crafting materials, unique abilities, new combat mechanics, and a hostile Reaper Skeleton encounter.

Both downloads are required. Install and activate the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack together.

Included equipment sets

- Nexus equipment and bow a green-themed armor and weapon set with custom projectiles.

- Reaper equipment and scythes fear-based combat abilities and a Reaper Skeleton enemy.

- Aetherite equipment and greatswords powerful gear crafted from Aetherium ore.

- Warden equipment and echo staff darkness resistance, mob tracking, echo tools, and a directional staff attack.

- Phantom equipment and whip invisibility-focused armor and a distinctive whip weapon.

- Glowing Obsidian equipment and battleaxe glowing armor, dynamic light, strength effects, and heavy weapons.

- Gilded Netherite equipment and morningstars upgraded Netherite gear, resistance, durability damage, and stun attacks.

- Copper equipment and hammers enchantable copper armor, tools, and specialized hammer attacks.

- Amethyst equipment and spear a complete Amethyst set with a first-person spear animation.

- Emerald equipment and dagger a complete Emerald set with its own specialized weapon.

Featured armor

Warden Glowing Obsidian Gilded Netherite Phantom

The addon also includes custom smithing templates, recipes, first-person weapon animations, directional sounds, durability-aware abilities, recipe unlocking, multiplayer-aware PvP behavior, custom blocks, and the Ability Damage enchantment system.

Reworked scripting and performance

Version 1.0.2 rebuilds the equipment abilities with Minecraft's scripting APIs. The rework reduces command and entity usage, improves sound positioning, updates item formats, fixes portal-related problems, and makes ability durability costs scale with the number of targets hit.

Minecraft Fortify does not replace player.json, making it easier to combine with other compatible Bedrock addons.

Requirements and installation

- Download both the MC Fortify Reworked Behavior Pack v1.0.2 and MC Fortify Reworked Resource Pack v1.0.2 from this CurseForge project.

- Open both .mcpack files to import them into Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

- Apply both packs to the same world.

- Enable the Custom Biomes and Beta APIs world experiments.

- Place the Behavior Pack above conflicting gameplay packs if another addon changes the same items, entities, or recipes.

Required experiments

The pack manifests require Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.0 or newer. The CurseForge file tags should be treated as the author's current compatibility declaration.
