This add-on will add different types of crates that generate naturally in your world. By using keys and other equipment, you can unlock the crates to obtain random loot, ranging from low-tier to high-tier items.

(v1.16)

NEW UPDATES:

Version: 1.16

Version: 1.16

NOTE:

- This update only works with Minecraft version 26.40 and higher.

STRUCTURES:

- Reworked structure generation to prevent structures from generating too close to or colliding with other structures.

MOBS:

- Piglin Crates Trader:

- Can now pickup and store Golden Dandelions in its inventory.

- Updated its animation and model to match the vanilla Piglins animations.

BLOCKS:

- Custom blocks now have assigned instrument sounds when played by a Note Block.

ITEMS:

- Fixed a bug with the Flint Axe when stripping logs and wood.

TECHNICAL CHANGES:

- Updated several files to 26.40 version.

Version: 1.15

Version: 1.15

NOTE:

- This update only works with Minecraft version 26.30 and higher.

TECHNICAL CHANGES:

- Updated several files to 26.30 version.

BLOCKS:

- Fixed a bug where Wooden Crates could drop multiple loot items from a single interaction.

- Added 5 Crate Trophies and 5 Crate Key Trophies.

- Trophy tiers: Stone, Copper, Iron, Golden, and Diamond.

- Stone Crate Trophies and Stone Crate Key Trophies have a chance to be obtained from any type of Crate.

- Higher-tier trophies can be crafted by combining lower-tier trophies.

Version: 1.14
