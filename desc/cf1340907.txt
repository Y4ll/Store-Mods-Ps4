DRILLS ADDON (Bedrock) Heat Modes Balance Boom! Stop wasting pickaxes and start drilling smarter. This nerfed-for-survival addon adds 11 drill tiers with a heating/cooling system, 4 switchable modes, smart drop toggle, flavorful particles/sounds, and clean English in-game messages. Built for performance, balanced for long worlds.

Core Features

Heating & Cooling System manage heat and cooldowns

4 Modes: Precision Standard Excavation Tunnel

Drops Toggle: Double-sneak or type !!drops (starts OFF for performance)

Balanced (Nerfed): Survival-friendly progression

Stats & HUD: !drillstats shows ratings, heat & speed

Entity-Safe: Warnings if misused as a weapon
