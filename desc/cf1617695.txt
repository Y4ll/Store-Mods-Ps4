Glowing Ores Resource Pack for Minecraft Bedrock

Visible Ores makes every ore glow in dark areas and underground, so you can spot them instantly without missing a single vein. Each ore glows in its own unique color gold, diamond, emerald, redstone, iron and more all clearly visible even in complete darkness.

Features

- All ores glow brightly in dark areas and underground

- Each ore has its own unique glow color for easy identification

- Ores emit actual light that illuminates the surrounding area

- Two variants included Outlined Ores with colored borders and Normal Ores without outlines
