Four years since the first release back in 2022, I am absolutely thrilled to bring this iconic project back with a completely new look: Ruins Reforged.

This is not merely a bugfix update. This is a rebuild from scratch, taking advantage of the latest addon development features (Jigsaw structure) along with all the experience I have accumulated over the years. My goal is to deliver a ruinexploring experience that is more authentic, lively, and surprising than ever before.

FEATURES

Achievement-friendly

STRUCTURES REDESIGNED

All structures from the original version have been revised, using the newest blocks from recent Minecraft updates. The result is a much more authentic, weathered, ancient look.

WORLD GEN

Structures naturally bend to the terrain, halfsink into the ground, and blend their foundations with rubble no more floating builds or cliffs cutting awkwardly through them.

-Ruins chests have been updated with items from the latest versions and feature a randomisation mechanism no two chests will ever be the same.

-Structural rarity customization options: Rare | Medium | Common

IF YOU NEED TO LOCATE A STRUCTURE

Simply type /locate structure rf:<structure_name> to quickly find any ruin you wish to explore.
