Its so sad that Minecraft Dungeons is not recieving new updates, isn't it? Well, it's your lucky day, since I present you, the Dungeons Content Plus Add-on.

This add-on expands the universe of Minecraft Dungeons on Minecraft Bedrock Edition. This pack works as a complement for my Dungeons Content Add-on, so they must be used together. The project has been under development for 2 years now, and more updates will be released in the future.

How does this addon works? It works the same way like the Mojang Devs used to add DLCs to Minecraft Dungeons, each Major Release of this Addon will be a new custom DLC, new mobs, artifacts, pets and gear, the first release of this addon is The Frozen Adventures DLC

This first DLC adds a new set of illagers, 2 pets and 1 artifact.

- Eskologers, an ancient clan of illagers living in the cold biomes of the overworld (These mobs originally come from a Java mod "Pyrologer and friends".

- Twisted Captain, an ancient rampart captain with the thorns enchantment, has 1 enchanted redstone golem as minion.

- Tiny Glacier, a small creature made out of ice living in cold biomes, can be tamed.

- Mini Wricked Wraith, a mini scaled version of the Wretched Wraith, can be tamed.

- Snow Golem Kit, an artifact that can summon a snow golem which shoots flaming snow balls at monsters and stuns them.

- Hammer of Frost, a hammer who deals knockback damage and freezes the target.

This second DLC adds a new set of monsters found in the Nether

- Soul Blaze, a soul variant of the Blaze that deals more damage.

- Soul Wildfire, a soul variant of the Wildfire that deals more damage.

- Armored Wither Skeletons, an armored variant of the wither skeletons, they have both melee and ranged variants with 2 different armor types, Golden and Netherbrick.

- Mini Soul Wildfire, a pet version of the soul wildfire.

- Mini Wildfire, a pet version of the wildfire.

- Netherite Ministrosity, a pet version of the Netherite Monstrosity.

- Baby Wither, a pet version of the wither.

This third DLC adds 3 new armors, 2 artifacts and 2 new illagers.

- Pumpkineer, an illager disguised as a pumpkin. This mob originally comes from a Java mod "Pyrologer and friends".

- Pyrologer, an illagers living in the nether with the power of fire. This mob originally comes from a Java mod "Pyrologer and friends".

- Nightmare bone, an item that summons the black wolf as an ally.

- Delicious bone, an item that summons the skeleton wolf as an ally.

- Pumpkin Horror, a new type of armor.

- Nightmare Horror, a new type of armor.

- Phantom Wolf Armor, a new type of armor.

This fourth DLC adds 4 new mobs, 11 blocks and 1 music disc.

- Easterloger, an illager disguised as an Easter Bunny which shoots easter eggs that spawn little surprise on impact. This mob originally comes from a Java mod "Pyrologer and friends".

- Chickenger, an illager disguised as a chicken. This mob originally comes from a Java mod "Pyrologer and friends".

- Chickager, a baby variant of the Chickenger. This mob originally comes from a Java mod "Pyrologer and friends".

- Easter Eggs, these are blocks that gen generate in the Flower forest biome, and drop a little surprise when broken.

- "Easter Eggs" Music Disc, a rare item that obtained from the easter egg blocks.
