Items:

Strength in a Bottle: Consumable item which gives you +1 strength.

Revive Beacon: Revive a banned player.

Config: Edit the mod's settings.

Commands:

- /strength withdraw: Withdraws your Strength level into a usable item

- /strength help: Shows help text

How it works:

This is S1 Strength.

When you die you lose 1 strength. When you kill someone you get 1 strength. Getting very low strength will get you banned

For items that deal 1 damage (sticks, blocks, fist) it will deal 1 * strength level damage, so if you are on strength level 0 or lower, you will not deal any damage.

For items with damage (swords, axe, pickaxe) it will deal item damage + strength level
