Frequently Asked Questions

Q. How to fix the inventory ,that items are dropping after touching any on mobile ?

Go to settings/video/ui profile and turn it to classic from pocket ,If this doesn't work then switch to disable inventory overlay in pack settings .

Q. How to turn off the PVP sky ?

Go to settings/video/graphics/beautiful skies and turn it off
