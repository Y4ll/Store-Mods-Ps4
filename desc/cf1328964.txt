My Mini 3D Totem

This pack replaces the boring default Totem of Undying with a fun 3D mini version of your player! You can even use your own custom skin to make the totem look like a tiny version of you.

How to add your custom skin:

- Download and extract the pack.

- Open the folder: NeBux/Totem/

- Find the file skin.png.

- Replace it with your Minecraft skin.

Now your Totem of Undying will appear as a mini 3D version of yourself in-game!
