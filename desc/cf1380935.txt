Baby Mode Addon

Baby Mode makes your Minecraft experience easier, safer, and more enjoyable! Designed for beginners, casual players, and anyone who prefers a relaxed survival adventure, this addon provides powerful buffs that help you stay alive while still keeping the core survival gameplay intact.

Instead of making you completely invincible, Baby Mode gives you extra protection and quality-of-life improvements that make exploring, building, mining, and fighting much less stressful. Whether you're learning the game for the first time or simply want a more forgiving survival experience, Baby Mode has you covered.

With permanent Night Vision, you'll never struggle to see in caves or during the night. Strong damage resistance helps you survive dangerous encounters, slower hunger drain means less time worrying about food, and Keep Inventory ensures your hard-earned items stay safe even after death.

Perfect for solo worlds, family-friendly gameplay, casual survival adventures, and players who want a smoother Minecraft journey.

Features

Approximately 80% Damage Reduction

Permanent Night Vision

Slower Hunger Drain

Keep Inventory Enabled

Works in Survival Mode

Lightweight and Easy to Use

Compatible with Most Worlds

Great for Beginners and Kids

Enjoy a safer and more relaxing Minecraft adventure with Baby Mode!
