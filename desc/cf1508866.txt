True Java Full Bright Clear Vision for Bedrock!

This addon removes all dark lighting in your world, giving you full visibility everywhere without needing night vision potions.

Installation & Compatibility

- Download the BP and add it to your Minecraft world.

- No experiments required for this addon.

- Works on the latest stable Minecraft versions (not beta or preview).

- Does not use player.json, so it's compatible with most other addons and keeps achievements enabled.

Achievement-Friendly & No player.json!

This addon is inspired by Java Edition's fullbright setting and rebuilt for Bedrock. Here's a quick look at what it does:

True Java Full Bright

This addon brings full brightness to Bedrock, removing all dark areas from your world.

[How It Works]

- No more darkness Caves, oceans, and nighttime are fully lit without any torches or potions.

- Achievement-friendly Unlike texture pack methods, this keeps your achievements enabled.

- No resource pack needed Works purely as a behavior pack, no need to change your textures.

- Simple and lightweight Just add it to your world and enjoy clear visibility everywhere.
