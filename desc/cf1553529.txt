How to activate Sea-Through X-Ray in Worlds

To activate Sea-Through X-Ray in Worlds using the new World Settings menu:

Open the World Settings screen > click Resource Packs > open the Available menu > select Sea-Through X-Ray > click Activate > open the Active menu > select Sea-Through X-Ray > click Settings > choose your X-Ray Version.

X-Ray Results

- Turn off Smooth Lighting and use Night Vision Potions for better visibility in dark areas.

Other Features

High-Contrast Outlined Ores

- Makes ores easier to spot.

Pumpkin Indicator

- When wearing a pumpkin, the full pumpkin overlay is removed and a small pumpkin icon appears in the upper-left corner. This is useful when traveling in the End.

Spyglass Indicator

- When using a spyglass, the spyglass overlay is removed and a small spyglass icon appears in the upper-left corner.

- The Pumpkin, Spyglass, and Burning indicators can all appear at the same time.

Burning Indicator

- When you are on fire, the fire overlay is removed and a small burning icon appears in the upper-left corner.

Suspicious Sand & Gravel Indicators

Suspicious sand and suspicious gravel are marked with barricade tape on all sides, making them easier to find.

Powdered Snow Indicator

Powdered snow is marked with barricade tape on all sides, making it easier to spot before you fall in.

Infested Stone & Deepslate Indicators

Infested stone and infested deepslate are marked with barricade tape on all sides, making them easier to avoid while mining.

Trapped Chest Indicators

Trapped chests, including double trapped chests, are marked with barricade tape on all sides so they are easier to identify.

Translucent Rain, Water, Lava, and Snow

- Improves water clarity.

- Replaces lavas block shape with end rods for better visibility.

Lowered Shields, Totems, and Fire Overlays

Lowers shields, totems, and fire overlays so they take up less space on your screen.

Brighter Tripwires

- Changes tripwires to a bright red color, making them much easier to see.
