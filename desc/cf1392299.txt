Drop Multiplier increases the number of items dropped from almost any source, including mobs, ores, blocks, chests you break, players dying, or even items thrown on the ground

Features:

- Multiplies the drop quantity of every item

- Supports items from other addons

- Does not require any Experimental Features

- Compatible with achievements

Available Versions

-

X2 Multiply every item drop by 2

-

X10 Multiply every item drop by 10

-

X100 Multiply every item drop by 100

-

No Player Drop - Multiply every item drop except Item drop from Player (Player drop item, Player death)

-

Mob Only Only mob drops are multiplied (Doesnt work with Knockback II enchantment)

- Ore Only Only ore drops are multiplied (Only works with vanilla ores and tools without Silk Touch enchantment)

- Crop Only - Only crop drops are multiplied (Only works with vanilla crop)

- You can now configure the multiplier value in the behavior pack settings

Notes:

- Choose the multiplier pack carefully. Extremely high multipliers can cause heavy lag or even crash the game, depending on your device and addons installed.

- The Mob Only, Ore Only and Crop Only packs can be used together. However, please use the No Player Drop pack and the default pack separately.

- This addon is pure fun only not designed for balanced gameplay
