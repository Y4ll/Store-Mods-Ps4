Clean Visuals is my lightest pack. No fog at all, maximum clarity, and built to run well on weaker devices while still looking like a proper shader.

What this changes

No fog

There is no distance fog in this pack at all. Terrain stays sharp all the way to your render distance instead of fading into haze. This is the main thing the pack is built around.

Clarity

Everything holds its edge. Blocks, treelines and horizons stay crisp rather than softening off, which is why this one reads as cleaner than anything else I make.

Lighting

Smooth natural light that shifts properly through the day cycle. Nothing snaps or jumps between times of day.

Shadows

Real shadows with soft edges, so they sit under objects instead of looking painted on.

Water

Clear water with proper reflections on the surface. You can see through it and see the sky in it at the same time.

Sky

Rich color through sunrise and sunset without the rest of the day getting tinted along with it.

Contrast and color depth

More separation between biomes so they do not all sit at the same flat brightness, but kept restrained. This is not a saturated pack.

Performance

This is the lightest pack on my page. Dropping the fog and keeping the effects restrained means there is simply less for your device to do every frame.

If Vibrant Visuals runs at all on your device but my other packs feel heavy, start here. You still get real lighting, shadows and water, just without the expensive extras.

Requirements

THIS PACK IS FOR VIBRANT VISUALS ONLY
IT DOES NOT WORK WITH MB LOADER

Vibrant Visuals must be ON. This pack is built for Bedrock's Vibrant Visuals renderer. The pack will not do anything if it is off.

Where to turn it on: Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals

If that toggle is not there, your device or version does not support Vibrant Visuals yet.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Clean Visuals

- Turn on Settings > Video > Vibrant Visuals

- If the lighting still looks the same, leave the world and load it again once

FAQ

The pack is not working. Nothing changed after I installed it.
I get this comment a lot, and almost every single time the reason is the same. Vibrant Visuals is turned off.

This pack only works when Vibrant Visuals is ON. That is written clearly at the top of this page. With it off, the pack loads fine but nothing on your screen changes, because there is no renderer for it to work with. The pack is not broken.
