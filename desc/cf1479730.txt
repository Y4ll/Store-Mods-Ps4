Siren Head: Stranded Add-on

Turn on Experimental (Beta APIs)

Java version: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/siren-head-stranded

This add-on brought the Siren Head from Hex Core's horror game Siren Head Stranded: https://hexcoregames.itch.io/siren-head-stranded into Minecraft.

Features:

Siren Head will emit various sounds (alarm sirens, conversations, laughter, etc.).

Siren Head has 10000 health and deals 100 damage.

Siren Head moves very fast (sometimes faster than players).

Siren Head's attack targets are players and villagers.

When attacking, Siren Head will lift its target into the air and bite it.

Siren Head can disguise itself as a lamppost to trick players.

Siren Head will naturally spawn.
