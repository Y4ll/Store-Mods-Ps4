Block Display brings display-entity style building to Minecraft Bedrock. Spawn a block as a display, then move it, spin it and scale it freely rotated stairs, tilted logs, giant cubes, tiny detail blocks, floating decorations all with handles you click in the world, no commands to memorise.

What it does

- Any block, anywhere. Displays float exactly where you put them, even inside other blocks. No gravity, no collision, nothing pushes them, nothing hurts them.

- Move, rotate, scale with gizmos. Arrows, ring knobs and a corner handle appear around the selected display. Click to nudge by a step, sneak-click to go the other way, or switch on drag mode and pull them around by looking.

- Exact values. Type the position, rotation and size when you need precision.

- Real block models. Full cubes, slabs, stairs and fences (about 650 vanilla blocks) are drawn with their real textures at true block scale. Slabs, stairs and fences have shape variants: top/bottom, inner and outer corners, upside-down, fence post / panel / cross.

- Everything else too. Panes, doors, anvils, plants, items any item can be shown.

- Sizes from 1/16 of a block to 16 blocks.

- Group Tool. Select many displays and move, rotate or resize them together as one object.

- No shadow blobs, no drift, survives restarts. Displays are saved with the world.

Getting started

- Add the behavior pack to your world (the resource pack is attached automatically). Works on standard worlds no experimental toggles.

- Run /scriptevent bd:wand for the Block Display Wand and /scriptevent bd:group for the Group Tool.

- Use the wand on empty air and pick Spawn a display here.

Controls

Wand on empty air

Wand on a display

Group Tool on displays
add / remove them from the selection

Group Tool on air

Steps for moving (1/32 ... 1 block), rotating (1 ... 90) and sizing are in Settings.

Commands

/scriptevent bd:wand bd:group bd:tools bd:spawn [block] bd:mode move|rotate|scale bd:list bd:settings bd:help

Good to know

- Modeled blocks always show their default look (a stair's direction comes from rotating the display). Animated blocks such as magma and sea lanterns fall back to the held-item look.

- Held-item blocks (panes, doors, anvils...) use a one-time alignment that ships pre-set. If one ever looks off-centre, Calibrate in the display menu fixes it for the whole world.

- Requires Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.40 or newer.
