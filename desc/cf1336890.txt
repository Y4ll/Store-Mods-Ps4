Are you looking for glow ore resource pack for Minecraft bedrock?

I present, Vibrant Emissive.

Vibrant Emissive is a resource pack that makes ore glow in the dark, not only ore but entities, items and some blocks also glow in the dark.

Select the option you want.

Don't forget to enable Vibrant Visual graphics in settings to use it.
