New Shields

Beharvior & Resources Packs Needed

This addon does not disable achievements.

Variants of the base shield for each type of wood have been added!

(You can still craft a standard shield with mixed wood types)

Every 3 blocked hits, performs a sonic attack similar to the Warden.

(Can block the Wardens sonic attacks)

On Melee: On contact burns with Dragon's Fire.

On Projectiles: Shoots a fireball infused with Dragon's Fire.

On Melee: Every 3 blocked hits stun nearby mobs with a vibration.

On Projectiles: Sends a vibration back to the attacker.

On Melee: Burns on contact.

On Melee: Launches mobs into the air.

On Projectiles: Fires a wind charge back to the attacker.

Every 2 blocked hits summons a shulker bullet that protects you.

On Melee: On contact deals damage.

Copper Shield oxidize as durability decreases. Use honeycomb to prevent oxidation.

(Blocking axe attacks remove oxidation, like on copper blocks)

On Wield: lights up the surroundings.

(The higher the oxidation level, the lower the light intensity)

On Melee: When oxidized applies the Oxidation, increasing the attackers damage taken for a few seconds.

(This scales with the oxidation level, boosting damage from 45% up to 75%)

Every 3 blocked hits grants Absorption for a few seconds.

(Also makes piglins neutral, like gold armor)

On Wield: summons an Emerald Vex to protect you.

(Can block magic attacks)

On wield: Increases max health by 2 hearts.
On block: Every 3 hits regenerates health for a few seconds.

On wield: Increases max health by 4 hearts and temporary protects from fire.

On melee: On contact wither for a few seconds.

The addon is compatible with Actions & Stuff, no setup needed!
