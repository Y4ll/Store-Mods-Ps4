Make combat feel more dynamic with Real Ragdoll, a physics driven death overhaul for Minecraft Bedrock.

Instead of playing the standard death animation, supported mobs collapse into simulated ragdolls. Humanoid mobs use six part rigs, while animals, creepers, spiders, and cave spiders use custom rigs. Each body reacts to the direction and strength of the killing blow, tumbles across the environment, collides with blocks and its own limbs, and eventually settles into a natural resting position.

Ragdolls can be pushed by nearby players, and optional experimental features let you drag bodies with leads, ragdoll the player, and show the armor a body died in. Bodies automatically disappear after a configurable period 30 seconds by default while the Settings menu also lets you keep bodies forever or clear them all at once.

Features

- Physics driven death reactions

- Directional knockback from attacks and explosions

- Terrain, limb, and self collision

- Mob specific ragdoll models, textures, proportions, and variants

- Bodies can be pushed by nearby players

- Optional lead dragging for ragdoll bodies (experimental)

- Optional player ragdoll, with falls, explosions, and the void (experimental)

- Optional armor display on bodies (experimental)

- Vanilla loot and supported player kill experience rewards

- Configurable ragdoll toggle, corpse cap, despawn time, and quality presets

- Optional Keep Bodies Forever setting

- Automatic cleanup after a configurable duration, from 5 to 120 seconds

Commands

- /realragdoll:settings or /settings. open the Settings menu

- /realragdoll:experimental or /experimental. open the Experimental menu

- /realragdoll:clearall. remove every ragdoll in the world
