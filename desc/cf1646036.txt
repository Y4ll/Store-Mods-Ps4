How To Use

Download the .maddon file

Tap/double-click it Minecraft will open and import it automatically

Wait for the "Successfully imported" message

Open Minecraft and go to Play

Create a new world or edit an existing one

Scroll down to Behavior Packs

Find Vein Miner and tap the + to activate it

Achivement Friendly

Launch your world!

Features

- Detects the full vein: only connects matching ore types, so mixed veins (iron next to diamond) won't cross-chop

- Drops the correct resource per ore raw iron, raw gold, diamond, redstone dust, lapis, coal, emerald, copper, quartz, gold nuggets, ancient debris with vanilla-accurate quantities (e.g. 49 lapis, 45 redstone)

- Works across overworld, deepslate, and nether ore variants

- Realistic durability: your pickaxe takes damage per ore mined, same as breaking manually

- Capped at 64 blocks per vein to keep large deposits lag-free

- Fully script-based, no changes to vanilla mining when not crouching
