Better Trees transforms Minecrafts forests into lush, detailed landscapes. Each tree is redesigned to look more natural, with improved shapes, thicker leaves, and realistic branches. Explore your world and see classic biomes come to life with more variety and beauty, without losing the original Minecraft feel.

Whether youre exploring jungles, oak forests, or birch groves, every tree feels fresh and full of life. Perfect for builders, explorers, and anyone who wants a more immersive world.

How to Use:
Just create a new world with the add-on activated, and the new trees will automatically generate in your world. Make sure your world type is set to Infinite, as the add-on only works there.

There are two download options available: Better Trees and Instant Trees.

Better Trees

- Removes vanilla trees and replaces them with Better Trees, including custom sapling generation.

- Designed mainly for Survival and Hardcore gameplay.

- Achievement-friendly.

Instant Trees

- Does not replace vanilla trees or vanilla saplings.

- Adds Instant Sapling items that let you spawn trees instantly in Creative mode with a single tap.

- Perfect for builders.

Note: Do not combine the Better Trees add-on with the Instant Trees Only version. The full Better Trees add-on already includes Instant Tree items along with world generation changes. If you want both features, only install Better Trees.
