Hitbox Visualizer (v1.1.0)

See what the game sees! Hitbox Visualizer allows you to see the exact collision boxes of every entity in your Minecraft world. Whether you are a technical player testing farm mechanics, a map maker ensuring your mobs are perfect, or just curious, this pack gives you the vision you need.

Features

- See Hitboxes: A clean white box appears around every mob, showing exactly where they can be hit or collide.

- View Direction: A blue arrow shows exactly where a mob is looking.

- Lag Free: Unlike older packs that use particles, this uses modern game features to draw shapes instantly without slowing down your game.

- Toggle On/Off: The pack is disabled by default so it doesn't clutter your view. Turn it on only when you need it!

How to Use

- Install: Add the Behavior Pack to your world.

- Activate: Make sure "Beta APIs" is enabled in your world settings experiments.

- Play: Join the world.

- Toggle: Open the chat and type:

- !hitbox

- ...or simply !hb

- Done! You will see a message saying visualization is Enabled. Type it again to turn it off!

Notes

- Works in Singleplayer and Multiplayer.

- Required "Beta APIs" experiment enabled.

- Works in the Overworld, Nether, and End.
