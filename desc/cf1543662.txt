Better Grass: Full Organic Sides is a massive visual improvement for your Minecraft landscapes. Instead of seeing the brown dirt sides on every hill, this pack extends the top texture to all sides of the block, creating a seamless and immersive environment in every dimension.

From the overworld plains to the alien forests of the Nether, your world will look significantly cleaner and more professional.

Whats Included?

This pack provides a Full Side overhaul for all of these blocks:

- Overworld: Grass Blocks, Snowy Grass, Podzol, and Mycelium.

- Paths: Full Dirt Path sides (perfect for realistic roads!).

- The Nether: Crimson Nylium and Warped Nylium.
