Transform your Minecraft experience into a whimsical journey with the Ancxliy UI! This resource pack breathes new life into your game by redesigning the interface with a soft, warm color palette and beautifully illustrated paper-style menus.

Key Features:

* Cute Cat Companions: Custom feline illustrations throughout the crafting, smelting, and inventory screens.

* Hand-Drawn Aesthetic: Every menu, from the Enchanting Table to the Brewing Stand, features a unique, artistic sketch style.

* Clean & Readable: Despite its artistic flair, the UI remains intuitive and easy to navigate for survival and creative players alike.

* Themed Icons: Includes custom icons for various workstations like the Smithing Table, Cartography Table, and Loom.

Perfect for players who love "Aesthetic" or "Kawaii" setups and want their Minecraft world to feel more like a cozy sketchbook.
