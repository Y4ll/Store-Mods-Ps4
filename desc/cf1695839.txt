Noite Produtiva makes sleeping actually useful.

Normally, when you sleep in Minecraft, the night simply skips ahead. With this mod, that skipped time now affects the world around you too.

While you sleep:

- Crops continue growing

- Saplings keep progressing

- Furnaces continue smelting

- Smokers and blast furnaces keep working

So instead of waking up to a world that feels frozen in time, your farms and machines actually make progress during the night.

The goal is simple: make sleeping feel like real time has passed, without changing the core Minecraft experience.
