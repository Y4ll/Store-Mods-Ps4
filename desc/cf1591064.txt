A lightning-fast, script-based add-on that brings true dynamic lighting to Minecraft Bedrock [ any version 1.20 to 26.50+]. Hold any light source and watch your surroundings illuminate in real-time!

Choose Your Edition

LITE For Low-End Devices

Hand-held dynamic lighting with zero lag. Perfect for mobile.

PRO The Full Experience

Everything in Lite + Flaming Arrows, Ghast Fireballs & Burning Mobs emit light!

Features

- Dynamic Lighting smooth, real-time light as you move

- Offhand Support hold lights while mining or fighting

- Underwater Glow Sea Lanterns, Glowstone, End Rods & more work underwater

- Multiplayer Ready works on Realms & multiplayer worlds

Offhand Usage

- Hold a light source in your Main Hand

- Double-Crouch (sneak twice within 1.3s)

- Item swaps to your offhand automatically!

To remove: empty your main hand and double-crouch again.

Supported Items

Bright (12-15)

Torches, Copper Torches, Lanterns, Copper Lanterns, Glowstone*, Sea Lantern*, Shroomlight*, Jack o'Lantern*

Medium (7-11)

Campfire, Soul Campfire, Soul Torch, Soul Lantern, Froglights*, End Rod*, Lava Bucket, Redstone Torch, Amethyst Cluster*, Beacon*, Sea Pickle*

Faint (3-6)

Blaze Rod*, Glow Berries*, Glow Lichen*, Magma Block*, Crying Obsidian*, Glow Ink Sac*, Amethyst Buds*, Enchanting Table*, Firefly Bush*
