JM Land Claim Protect Your Builds, Trust Your Friends

JM Land Claim is a lightweight, powerful land protection addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition, designed especially for survival multiplayer (SMP) worlds and realms. It gives players a simple, intuitive way to claim land, prevent griefing, and control who can interact with their builds all through clean, in-game menus.

Features

Stick-Based Claiming

Use a stick to claim a 50x50 area centered on your position no commands needed.

Full Block Protection

Prevents others from placing, breaking, or accessing containers inside your land.

Trusted Player System

Add or remove friends who can interact within your claimed areas.

How It Works

Hold a Stick

Right-click or tap while holding a stick to open the claiming system.

First Use = Claim Land

If you havent claimed land yet, youll be given the option to claim a plot.

Manage Your Claims

If youve already claimed, access a full menu to:

Add or remove trusted players

View all players you trust

Delete your claims

Protection Events

Once claimed, others cant build, break, or open containers on your land unless you trust them.

Installation Instructions

Download the .mcpack and import it into Minecraft.

Create or edit a world and activate the behavior pack.

Enable Beta APIs in the Experimental Features tab.

Start the world and begin claiming using a stick.
