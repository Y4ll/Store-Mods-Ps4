Sulfur FPS Booster is built to enhance your Minecraft performance without completely removing the visual experience. It focuses on optimizing in-game elements that commonly cause lag, resulting in smoother and more stable gameplay.

This pack reduces heavy particles, simplifies animations, and cleans up unnecessary visual effects to boost FPS, especially in laggy environments like dense forests or high-entity areas.

Key Features:

Improves FPS stability

Reduces lag spikes and stuttering

Provides smoother gameplay experience

- Optimized animations to minimize lag

- Clean and minimal visual style

- Improved FPS in heavy biomes (Jungle, Dark Oak Forest, etc.)
