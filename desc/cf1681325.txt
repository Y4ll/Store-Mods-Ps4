CheatCore is an all-in-one utility and Quality of Life add-on for Minecraft Bedrock, combining many useful gameplay tools into a single pack.

Main Features

Vein Miner Break connected blocks at once with adjustable mining modes and block limits.

TreeCapitator Cut down entire trees quickly without breaking every log manually.

FarmCapitator Till large areas, plant multiple crops at once, harvest crops, and automatically replant them.

Swift Place / Swift Bridge Place blocks rapidly for building bridges and large structures.

Agglomerate / Mass Stack Combine dropped items into stacks to reduce entity clutter.

Mob Stacker Combine nearby mobs of the same type into stacks.

Inventory Sorter Automatically sort your inventory and containers.

Multi-Container Organizer Scan and organize multiple nearby chests or containers at once.

Super Speed Hopper Transfer items much faster than a normal hopper.

Super Speed Furnace Smelt items at a much higher speed.

Enchant Manager Add, remove, and configure multiple enchantments from an in-game menu.

Item Spawner Quickly get items through an easy-to-use interface.

Effect Manager Apply and manage status effects directly from the menu.

Teleport & Waypoints Save locations and teleport between them quickly.

Freecam Explore the surrounding area using a detached camera.

Chunk Border Display chunk boundaries for farms, redstone builds, and technical projects.

Magnet Pull nearby dropped items toward the player.

Forcefield Control or push nearby entities away from the player.

Custom HUD Display useful information such as mining mode, block count, selected block, and other status information.

Backpack & Inventory Utilities Extra tools for easier item and storage management.

In-Game Settings Menu Enable, disable, and configure features directly in-game.

Thai & English Support Full support for both Thai and English.

CheatCore is designed to reduce the need for multiple separate add-ons by bringing many useful systems together in one complete package.
