Bring Your Minecraft Cities to Life with City Streets+

Turn your Minecraft Bedrock worlds into vibrant, realistic urban landscapes with City Streets+ the ultimate addon for city builders. Packed with over 60+ unique blocks, you can create authentic streetscapes complete with traffic cones, bollards, fire hydrants, trash cans, signs and a huge variety of road tiles featuring arrows, crosswalks, center lines, manhole and more.

Whether you're building a bustling downtown, a suburban neighborhood, or a detailed highway system, this add-on replaces plain concrete with rich, high-detail infrastructure. Your roads won't just connect places they'll feel alive.

More block types will be added in future updates, so your city can keep growing with us.

Start building with details and realism because your Minecraft city deserves more than flat gray streets.

What's New in v1.4 (V1.4 will be release on Moday 14th sep 2026)

In Jist this major update brings a bunch of new street light related blocks, New signs and a Brand new Crash Barrier

New Features

- Iron Sheet - Its a new item that can be crafted from a iron ingot in Stone cutter. This item is now necessary to craft the furniture and sign related blocks.

- Asphault Mix Black - Previously called 'Raod block' is now renamed and a new crafting recipe is added to make future colorful road expansion easy and digestible and the name was somewhat missleading and would have conflicted with other furntiture related items.

- New signs - Sign blocks for [ Roundabout/Square about, U Turn, Dead End and Crossing ] were added.

- Street Light Bocks - [Street light Middle(non interactive), Street light Electric Box/Utility Box, Top Light and Side light]

- Crash Barrier - A new crash barrier block.

- Added 5 new translation files bringing the number to 18 translated languages. ( New languages Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Ukrainian, Danish, Swedish )

Improvements

Creative Menu Overhaul
Restructured item categories for better organization. Finding the right block is now faster and more intuitive.

More Generous Crafting
Increased Asphault Mix Black recipe yields, craft more blocks with the same resources, making large road projects less grindy.

Bug Fixes
