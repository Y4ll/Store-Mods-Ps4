Tired of manually chopping every log block by block? This addon lets you fell an entire connected tree instantly, no commands, no crafting, no extra items.

-How to Use

Simply Sneak + Break any log block to instantly chop every connected log in the tree. That's it. Works with your axe exactly as normal.

- Features

- Instant tree felling Sneak + Break a log to chop the full connected tree

- All wood types Oak, Spruce, Birch, Jungle, Acacia, Dark Oak, Cherry, Mangrove, Pale Oak + all stripped variants

- Nether support Works with Crimson and Warped stems too

- Natural drops Every log drops exactly as if broken normally

- All dimensions Works in Overworld, Nether, and The End

- Achievement-safe Does NOT disable achievements or trophies

- Multiplayer ready Works independently for each player

- Zero lag Lightweight 26-direction BFS script, no tick-heavy loops

- Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21+, 26.0+

- Works on Android, iOS, Windows, Console

- Behavior Pack only, no Resource Pack needed

- Compatible with other addons

- Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Tap/double-click to auto-import into Minecraft

- Apply the pack to your world under Behavior Packs

- Play, Sneak + Break any log to fell the whole tree!
