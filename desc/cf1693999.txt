Glowing X-Ray v1.0

Tired of squinting at dark cave walls hoping you spotted a diamond? Glowing X-Ray makes every ore block emit a soft glow, so valuable resources stand out clearly against stone and Deep slate no more digging past hidden treasure in the dark.

This pack adds emissive, PBR-ready textures to every ore in the game: Coal, Iron, Gold, Redstone, Lapis Lazuli, Diamond, Emerald, Copper, and Quartz, plus their Deep slate variants, along with Nether Gold Ore and Ancient Debris. Each block uses custom MER (Metallic/Emissive/Roughness) maps, so the glow effect activates automatically when Deferred/PBR rendering is enabled giving your mining runs a clean, clutter-free way to spot ores at a glance.

Features:

Emissive glow on all overworld and Nether ores

Full Deep slate ore variant support

Ancient Debris included

PBR/MER texture maps for realistic lighting response

Simple,clean visual style no clutter, just clarity

new particles added

Requirements: Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.80+, with Deferred Technical Rendering (PBR) enabled for the glow to appear.
