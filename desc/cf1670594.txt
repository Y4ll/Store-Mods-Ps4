G-Flow PvP

A competitive-focused reasorce pack for Minecraft Bedrock PvP

G-Flow PvP is a carefully tuned reasorce pack designed specifically for competitive Minecraft Bedrock PvP. It reduces visual clutter, enhances combat clarity, and streamlines on-screen elements so you can focus on what matters winning fights.

This pack reworks shields, fire, weapons, particles, ores, glass, water, and UI elements to give you a cleaner, more responsive PvP experience without changing the core gameplay.

Features

Shield repositioned down shrinks further when blocking

Fire and soul fire squeezed smaller still readable, just less intrusive

Carved pumpkin screen blur completely removed

Swords shortened across all materials cleaner weapon profiles

Axes shortened across all materials cleaner weapon profiles

Bow & crossbow pull stages recolored: green > yellow > red

Crit & sharpness particles smaller tinted random red/green/blue per hit

Ores with thin natural-color outlines

Clear glass with visible frame colored glass variants flattened

Water: vanilla texture with increased transparency

Inventory, slots, hotbar: dark teal translucent fill with grey/white border

Recipe book backgrounds translucent

Loading screens: dark, translucent, vanilla beveled shape kept
