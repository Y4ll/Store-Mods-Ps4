The go-to 64x resource pack for Minecraft.

The logical next step in upscaling Minecraft textures. Just like Faithful 32x, this pack is based on the default artmasterfully remade in quadruple the resolution and adding just the right amount of extra detail for the perfect vanilla-style Minecraft experience.

By basing its textures directly on its 32x counterparts, it achieves great consistency between packs and is always improving in quality. If you're into detailed resource packs but still want that vanilla feel, this pack is for you!

Additional Information:

If you have any feedback, want to report a bug, or generally need help, join our Discord server above, make a GitHub issue, or comment below. For any commercial or distribution-related questions, refer to our license.

All textures and add-ons are provided by contributors volunteering in their free time. Anyone can submit textures through our Discord server linked above, or make an add-on to officially list on our website. Before you begin, make sure to read our texturing guidelines which precisely outline the art style of the pack.

Each pack has listings on our website, CurseForge, Planet Minecraft, and Modrinth, and some packs are additionally listed on MCPEDL and the Minecraft Forums. Downloads from any other sites are unofficial, unsupported and could be dangerous! Remember to stay safe on the internet.
