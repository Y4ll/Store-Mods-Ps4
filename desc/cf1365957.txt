Here's a quick video showing some structures and Dungeons!

This addon adds new dungeons and over 20+ structures with unique content such as food, weapons, armor, and more! Explore these randomly generated dungeons filled with unique blocks such as trial spawners and vaults! There's other features such as coins found in dungeons and structures to be able to trade with a new trader, there's trinkets that can be used with a trinket bag to give you various effects and abilities, new ingredients for new potions, and more!
