- Tired of driving cars made out of wool?

- Tired of anyone being able to steal your car at any time?

- Tired of car addons that don't work properly in survival?

I am too! And it was out of this frustration that I've spent over a year developing CMDBlocc's Vehicles! As of version 1.2, this pack features 15 distinct vehicles; from sports cars, to a family minivan, and even a moped! All cars are dyeable and have a built in ownership system that allows you to not only keep your car secure, but also allows you to sell and trade vehicles with other players!

Here are some other features of CMDBlocc's Vehicles:

- All cars are dyeable by the owner, allowing you to change the color whenever you please!

- All vehicles have a maintenance and repair system, and will break down more often as they age.

- All applicable vehicles also have an inventory system with a working trunk, allowing you to securely store items and make them publicly visible whenever you want!

- All vehicles have a simple damage and collision repair model, allowing them to appear dented when damaged.

- Pack is fully functional in survival mode, with all vehicles available from specialized traders!

- If you aren't able to purchase a new vehicle, you can purchase a Mystery Lemon from a trader who definitely isn't inspired by Spamton G. Spamton from Deltarune.

Along with all this, even more features are planned in future updates!

For now, however, no fuel system is planned. I'm sorry for any confusion!

What are you waiting for? Bring the fun of highly-detailed vehicles to your Minecraft: Bedrock Edition game!
