The Harvester Grim Reaper Plague Doctor Boss

The Harvester is a soul-fire boss for Minecraft Bedrock: half Grim Reaper, half Plague Doctor. His skull burns like a torch, teal soul fire pours off his robe and scythe, and every skill he casts is telegraphed so you can read it, dodge it, and fight back.

Requires Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.50+. No experimental toggles needed.
Built on the latest stable Script API (@minecraft/server 2.10.0).

How to fight him

- Summon him with /summon pa:harvester, or find him in The End.

- 550 health, 3 phases. He grows faster and fiercer as his health drops.

- Every skill shows a warning first (sprouting soul wheat, an opening coffin, lit candles, glowing footprints, clock-dial rings), and he stands still while casting.

- His skills hit every creature in their area, not just players: villagers, animals and golems get caught too.

- Enrages after 5 minutes of fighting.

Skills

Phase 1 Doctor of the Dead

- Reaping Arc: a wide scythe sweep that leaves the ground burning.

- Field of Souls: rows of soul wheat sprout around you, then he reaps the whole field. Stand in the bare furrows.

- Will-o'-the-Wisps: ghost fires drift after you. Sprint to outrun them.

- Candles of the Dead: soul candles light up around you. Stand on one to snuff it, or it erupts and heals him.

Phase 2 Epidemic

- Plague Pyre: soul fire erupts on the target and spreads to anyone standing nearby. Spread out!

- Footsteps of the Dead: your own footprints ignite. Keep moving and never retrace your steps.

- Buried Alive: a coffin opens beneath you. Step out before the lid slams shut.

- Plague Aura: standing next to him infects you.

Phase 3 Final Harvest

- Death Sentence: an hourglass runs down over your head. Drink milk to cleanse your plague first.

- Danse Macabre: hooded dancers circle you and close in. Slip out through the gap.

- The Black Death: a killing miasma floods the arena. Hide in a soul lantern's light.

- Final Harvest: the ground fills with soul wheat before a full 360 spin. Stand right next to him, or get out of the field.

The Plague

Most skills add plague stacks (shown above your head). At 5 stacks you burst for extra damage. Stacks fade over time, and milk cures them instantly.

Rewards

Every kill drops:

- 1 Reaper Skull

- 812 Souls

- 500 XP

Use them to craft the Harvester Scythe: right-click for Shadow Reap, use it on a mob for Soul Harvest, and it has passive Death Mark, Soul Explosion and Dark Blessing. Also included: the Dark Knight armor set, apples, ingots and steel gear.

Features

- 18 custom animations and 48 hand-drawn particles in a memento mori woodcut + soul fire pixel-art style

- Custom soul-fire effects attached to the boss that never stop burning

- No on-screen spam: no action bar HUD and no phase-change titles. Only short warnings for the three skills that need a specific dodge

- Stable APIs only: no Beta APIs or other experiments

Overview

The Harvester adds a terrifying new end-game boss and a full custom progression tier to your Bedrock survival world. Grind through Steel, forge the Dark Knight set, then challenge the Harvester itself a monster powerful enough to kill a Netherite-geared player in seconds.

Everything in this addon connects together in one progression chain, from your first iron ingot all the way to the Harvester Scythe.
