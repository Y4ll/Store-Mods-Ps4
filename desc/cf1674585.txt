The Nether Has More to Hide

Some places are better left buried.

Echoes of the Nether is a large survival-focused add-on that pushes the Nether far beyond what you already know, introducing new biomes, structures, creatures, equipment, progression systems and a story hidden inside the world itself.

Explore forgotten regions. Search abandoned ruins. Recover technology that should have disappeared long ago.

And find out why nothing here looks the way it once did.

Two New Nether Biomes

Venture into two completely new environments, each with its own identity, resources, structures and dangers.

Blackstone Desert

A vast land of ash, blackstone and ruined architecture, now inhabited by Piglin outcasts who have learned to survive where others would not.

Crystal Mine

A pale and mysterious mineral biome filled with enormous crystal formations, strange vegetation and the remains of something much larger.

Both biomes are fully integrated into Survival and contain unique resources, exploration opportunities and progression.

Explore Forgotten Structures

Scattered throughout the new regions are remnants of a civilization that vanished long ago.

Discover:

- Ancient ruins

- Foundries

- Camps

- Towers

- Arenas

- Mining sites

- Cranes

- Drills

- Industrial structures

- Hidden loot

Every structure is built to feel like part of the world rather than a standalone dungeon.

Some contain valuable resources. Some contain answers. Some only create more questions.

Face the Piglin Outcasts

Not every Piglin follows the same rules.

The Blackstone Desert is now home to a group of outcasts who have adapted to the ruins around them and learned to use what was left behind.

Encounter 3 new Piglin enemies:

- Grunts

- Bulwarks

- Warlords

Fight them, raid their strongholds and recover rare materials used to craft powerful new equipment.

They may not have built the ruins. But they have made them their home.

Recover Lost Technology

Exploration unlocks access to a completely new set of tools and equipment:

- Piglin Drill tear through blocks and connected veins

- Hoarder Arm pull nearby dropped items toward you

- Furnace Backpack process materials while exploring

- Piglin Jetpack take ancient technology into the air

- Piglin War Gear a full equipment set built from rare recovered materials

The strongest items cannot simply be crafted from common resources. You will need to explore, fight, experiment and interact with multiple systems to obtain everything.

More Than Crafting

Echoes of the Nether introduces several new ways to progress.

Discover and experiment with:

- Crystal Resonance

- Ancient Reliquaries

- Sifting

- Rare structure loot

- Unique mob resources

- Ancient materials

- New crystal resources

- Survival events

Different systems connect together, encouraging you to explore both new biomes instead of farming a single resource forever.

Piece Together What Happened

The story is not given to you in a quest log.
It is hidden throughout the world.
Recover lost mural fragments.
Decode damaged Ancient Tablets.
Explore structures left untouched for ages.
Pay attention to what the environment itself is telling you.
The deeper you explore, the clearer the picture becomes.
But some things were forgotten for a reason.

New Blocks & Building Sets

Both biomes introduce complete new block families for builders, including Ancient Blackstone and Quartzstone variants.

Salvage materials directly from the world and turn them into:

- Bricks

- Chiseled blocks

- Slabs

- Stairs

- Walls

- Pillars

- Tiles

- Decorative variants

Everything is designed to fit naturally alongside vanilla Nether building palettes.
