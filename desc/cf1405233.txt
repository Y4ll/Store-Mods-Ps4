Are you frustrated when managing or looting items is difficult and slow? Well, this resource pack is for you!

This resource pack adds new buttons to the inventory screen and chest screen to make it easier for you to manage items. This resource pack is also server-friendly! You don't have to worry about getting banned when playing Bedwars or PvP, which requires speed when looting items.

Features

1. Quick Items

Added 4 new buttons: Auto Place, to move items from inventory to chest or vice versa with one click, either all items (64x) or just one item (1x). Drop, to drop items, either all items (64x) or just one item (1x). Destroy, to remove items from existence (only available for inventory screen), and Default.

2. Quick Craft

Added new button to crafting screen to craft all items with one click. Quick Craft button on crafting player will only appear if there is item in crafting input slot!

3. Spam Craft

Now you can spam click to CRAFT ITEMS! which you couldn't do before for some reasons, like there is delay after craft an item.

More features are coming soon...
