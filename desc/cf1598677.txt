Nokia Armor + Nokia Tool

Turn your Netherite Armor and Netherite Tools into the legendary Nokia style! This resource pack gives your equipment a unique Nokia phone-inspired look while keeping the original Minecraft gameplay unchanged.

Whether you're mining deep underground or fighting powerful mobs, your gear will now look stronger than ever. This pack is lightweight, optimized, and works smoothly on Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

Features

- Nokia-themed Netherite Armor

- Nokia-themed Netherite Tools

- High-quality textures

- Optimized for better performance

- Works in Survival & Creative

- Compatible with Mobile, PC & Console

- Multiplayer friendly

- Easy to import and use

How to Install

- Download the .mcpack file.

- Open it with Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

- Wait for the import to finish.

- Go to Settings Global Resources or apply it to your world.

- Activate the resource pack and enjoy!
