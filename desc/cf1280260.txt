Colorful Bricks Expansion adds 16 new brick blocks, each in one of the classic Minecraft colors. Whether you want to build a modern house, a vibrant wall, or add accents to your creations, this pack gives you endless creative possibilities!

Features :

- 16 new colored brick blocks one for every dye color in Minecraft!

- Perfect for decoration, builds, and pixel art

- Craftable in survival using 8 bricks + 1 dye of your choice

- Works in both Creative and Survival mode

How to Craft Colored Bricks :

Simply place 8 regular bricks and 1 dye of the color you want in the crafting table to get 8 bricks of that color.

For example:

- 8 Bricks + 1 Red Dye = 8 Red Bricks

- 8 Bricks + 1 Blue Dye = 8 Blue Bricks

- ...and so on for every color!
