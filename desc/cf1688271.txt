Project Description:

Mining a single block at a time can quickly become a tedious chore, turning long resource-gathering sessions into a test of patience. This tool changes the entire rhythm of the underground by letting you carve through stone, ore, and dirt in a sweeping 3x3 radius.

Important Information:

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Mine blocks in a 3x3 radius.

- Lightweight project.

- Vanilla style crafting recipe.

- Behaviour & Resource packs are both required for the project to work.

- Have something to say? Join the Discord.

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

Craft an Iron Hammer to mine in a 3x3 radius.

Tired of chipping away at dark caves one stone at a time? Equip the hammer and smash your way through the underground. Carving out a massive 3x3 path with every single swing, this tool turns exhausting excavation into an effortless sweep.
