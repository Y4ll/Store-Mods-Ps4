Installation

1. Download the ".mcaddon" file.

2. Open it with Minecraft Bedrock.

3. Activate Special Forces Equipment in your world.

4. Make sure both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack are enabled.

5. Enter your world and equip your tactical gear!
