Hide Armor v1.3.1

Hide Armor (v1.3) is a modern resource pack that allows you to hide armor visually while keeping all gameplay mechanics intact.

Designed for players who want a clean look without sacrificing protection, this pack introduces a full in-game configuration system.

Compatible with Minecraft 1.21.130+

Basic Information

Hide Armor

A lightweight and optimized texture pack that hides armor pieces individually, without affecting stats, enchantments, or effects.

Perfect for:

- Roleplay servers

- Cinematic content

- Skin showcases

- Thumbnails and screenshots

How it Works

By default, all armor becomes invisible while still being equipped.

You can still use the classic feature:

- Crouch preview can be enabled to temporarily reveal armor.

This allows quick previews without changing settings.

Configuration System

The pack includes a native configuration system, fully integrated into Minecraft's resource pack settings.

Now you can choose exactly which parts of the body remain visible:

- Head (Helmet)

- Chest (Chestplate)

- Left Arm

- Right Arm

- Left Leg

- Right Leg

- Elytra

- Shield

- Armor Trims

Each part can be toggled independently.

Settings System

No subpacks. No external files. No commands.

Everything is handled directly in the Resource Pack Settings menu:

- Show or hide armor per body part

- Toggle Elytra and Shield visibility

- Show or hide Armor Trims

- Configure crouch preview behavior

- Combine partial visibility with crouch preview

All changes apply instantly.

New in v1.3

Version 1.3 expands the customization system and improves compatibility with newer Minecraft versions.

- Rendering system adjusted for newer game versions, improving compatibility before and after the Tiny Takeover update (26.10).

- Shield visibility can now be controlled independently:

- Show the shield permanently through settings

- Or display it only while blocking

- Armor Trims can now be shown or hidden separately from the armor itself.

- Crouch preview behavior is now configurable:

- Enable crouching to temporarily reveal armor

- Or keep armor hidden even while sneaking

This update gives even more control over how your equipment appears in-game.

Versions & Migration

Legacy Version (v1.1)

The legacy version only supported full armor hiding and basic crouch preview.

It may be included separately for compatibility purposes.

Current Version (v1.3.x)

This version adds a complete settings system and granular visual control.

More flexible, more stable, and future-proof.

In v1.3.1, compatibility with Adaptive Cape v1.5 or later was added.

Also in v1.3.1, the resource pack import was fixed, allowing it to be installed and imported without problems.

Notes & Compatibility

Technical Notes

- This pack is purely visual.

- Does not affect gameplay balance.

- Fully compatible with multiplayer servers.

- Works with most modded armors that use standard rendering controllers.
