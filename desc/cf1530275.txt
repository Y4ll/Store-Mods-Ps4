RAGDOLL FOR BEDROCK !

CAPE PHYISC redefines how movement feels in Minecraft Bedrock. While standard cape packs use a single, stiff bone, this project uses a manually crafted 24-segment chain to deliver the smoothest physics possible . We might implement 32 bones in later updates .

Features:

True Fluidity: A 24 bone skeleton allows the cape to curve, ripple, and flow naturally rather than bending at sharp, ugly angles .

Nested Geometry: Using custom-sized "nested" segments, the cape maintains a solid, seamless look even during extreme rotations or sprinting.

Dynamic Molang Logic: Custom math strings ensure the cape reacts differently to:

Sprinting: Added lift and drag.

Sneaking: Proper draping that respects the player's crouch height.

Jumping/Falling: Realistic vertical momentum.

Swimming: Fluid, water-resistant sway.

Technical Specifications:

Bone Count: 24 High-Fidelity Segments

Compatibility: Works with standard 64x32 cape textures.

Optimization: Tested and optimized for mobile performance

Installation:

Download the .mcpack.

Double-click to import to Minecraft.

Apply to your Global Resources.

Note: Ensure your skin has a cape enabled to see the custom physics.
