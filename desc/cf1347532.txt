Installation & Support:

Compatibility: Only for Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.80 - 26.0+ and Minecraft Preview/Beta.

Supported Devices: Available on iOS, Android, Windows, Xbox, and PlayStation.

Activating:

1. Download and open the .mcpack file.

2. Activate it in Settings > Global Resources.

3. Enable the "Vibrant Visuals" option in your Video Settings.

Hardware Requirements:

For Windows PCs

DirectX: Requires a graphics card that supports DirectX 12. Older cards that support DirectX 12 maybe compatible.

For Mobile Devices

Android: Requires a graphics processor such as the Adreno 640, Mali-G68, Mali-G77, or Xclipse 530, or a higher model.

iOS: Requires an Apple A12 chip or a newer and more powerful processor.

For Consoles

Vibrant Visuals is supported on Xbox Series X|S, Xbox Series X, Xbox One, PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 4.
