Bring the chaos of Brainrot memes into Minecraft!
This addon introduces a variety of ridiculous, meme-inspired creatures known as Brainrot mobs. They're weird, they're chaotic, and they're here to mess with your world in the most unexpected ways.

From derpy faces to unhinged behavior, these mobs will turn your survival experience into a living internet fever dream. Whether you're looking to spice up your gameplay or just want a laugh with friends, this addon delivers nonstop absurdity.

Features:

- Unique Brainrot-inspired mobs with wild designs

- Random and unpredictable behavior

- Perfect for meme-themed worlds or chaotic playthroughs

- Constant updates with new creatures and references

- Install now and embrace the Brainrot!

TungTungTung Sahur

This unhinged mob attacks everything in sight with pure meme-fueled rage. It uses two brutal attacks:

- Bat Swing A quick, standard swing with its bat used in close combat.

- Bat Smash Charges up to 3 times, growing its bat larger with each stage before slamming it down, cracking the ground and causing area damage.

TralaleroTralala

This absurd aquatic menace is a three-legged shark wearing shoesand it's every bit as ridiculous as it sounds. Powered by chaotic energy and questionable science, TralaleroTralala brings mayhem to land and sea with these two unholy abilities:

- Bite A vicious snap of its jaws that deals heavy damage to anything foolish enough to get close.

- Dash A high-speed burst that sends enemies flying on impact. Blink and youll miss itand possibly get launched into orbit.

- Tame & Ride Can be tamed using Brainrot, a rare drop from TungTungTung Sahur or TralaleroTralala itself. Once tamed, it becomes a rideable mount that can walk on water, making it perfect for fast aquatic travel.

Balerina Capuchina

This deranged dancer twirls straight out of a fever dream imagine a coffee cup with a human face, topped with a frothy capuchina swirl, perched on a ballerina body in a frilly tutu. Yep, its as horrifyingly graceful as it sounds.

Spinning with manic elegance, Balerina Capuchina turns the battlefield into a caffeine-fueled ballet with her signature move:

- Spin She pirouettes at breakneck speed, dealing area-of-effect damage every second to anything caught in her swirl zone. Get too close, and youll be sent flying with a side of emotional damage.

Asasino Capuchino

Brewed in darkness and trained in the forbidden arts of caffeine-fueled combat, Asasino Capuchino is a ninja espresso shot with a vengeance. With a capuchino head, glowing eyes, and two razor-sharp katanas, this stealthy menace turns every encounter into a high-speed coffee massacre.

This jittery juggernaut comes armed with three deadly abilities:

- Slash A rapid katana strike that hits with the force of a double shot. Fast, brutal, and surprisingly quiet.

- Spin Attack A whirlwind of blades and steam, slicing everything around him in a wide radius. Dont blink or youll be diced and decaffeinated.

- Clone Vanishes in a puff of steam and reappears alongside three identical copies. Good luck figuring out which ones real before youre overwhelmed.

Lirili Larila

A cactus-bodied elephant with just one front leg and one back leg each ending in sandal-wearing cactus feet. Lirili Larila is as unbalanced as it is unstoppable.

- Cactus Pierce When this bizarre beast attacks, sharp cactus spikes erupt from the ground, piercing any unlucky player hit by its strike. It's like being body-slammed and impaled desert style.

Frigo Camelo

A desert camel with the body of a refrigerator and two legs in sneakers. Frigo Camelo is the unholy fusion of kitchen appliance and desert wanderer, built to chill literally.

- Ice Ball Breathes a homing ball of freezing energy that locks onto targets. Victims are hit and slowed, caught in a frostbitten nightmare.

- Freeze Unleashes an AOE burst of cold, freezing everything nearby in a glacier of pain. Perfect for stopping mobs and friends in their tracks.
