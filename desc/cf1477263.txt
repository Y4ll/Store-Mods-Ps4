Adopt Pokemon Addon Take care of your best friend!

Tired of just battling? The Adopt Pokemon Addon transforms your journey into an experience of friendship and responsibility! Now, your main objective is to build genuine bonds with the creatures in your world.

Key Features:

Selective Capture: Encounter wild Pokemon, but remember: they are looking for a place to belong!

Feeding System: Use specific berries and foods to keep your Pokemon healthy, energized, and happy.

Daily Care: Well-cared-for Pokemon are more loyal and can assist you with various tasks during your adventure.

Friendship Evolution: Show love and attention to watch your partner grow and evolve naturally through the power of your bond.

Because being a Master is about more than just winning; it's about caring.
