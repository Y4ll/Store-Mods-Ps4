Introduction

Bring Minecraft's flat items to life with handcrafted 3D models.

This resource pack transforms vanilla tools and weapons into detailed 3D models while preserving Minecraft's original look and feel. Every model is carefully handcrafted to blend seamlessly with the vanilla style, adding depth, personality, and immersion to your gameplay without changing how the game plays.

Features

- Handcrafted 3D models

- Vanilla-friendly design

- Visual-only resource pack

- Built using Minecraft Bedrock's attachables

- Regular content updates

- All vanilla weapons
