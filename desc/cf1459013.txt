New Feature: Throwables Troves

Note that these are not blocks, but entities!

Throwables Troves are crates that contain a small set of throwable items, and can come with rarer items. These spawn on the surface at night and despawn during the day. These cannot be destroyed unless interacted by the player.

Red troves spawn as usual; blue troves have a higher chance to spawn in snow biomes; and green troves have a higher chance to spawn in jungle biomes.

Loot tables may be changed in another update.

Currently, there is no item to spawn them, but can be done with cheats by typing any of these commands below. Note that they despawn quick on daytime.

/summon kmcg:throwables_trove
/summon kmcg:throwables_trove_spruce
/summon kmcg:throwables_trove_jungle

Ninja Stars (Shurikens)

This addon adds ninja stars (shurikens) in 6 different materials - gold, copper, iron, diamond, blaze, netherite, breeze, frost, poison and wither. These can be thrown at any target and can be picked up when it hit the ground. They also have the ability to break all kinds of glass, normal ice, and even bamboo.
