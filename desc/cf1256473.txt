Nightmare Mode!

Morellagers now has a Nightmare mode subpack, that works for all 3 Minecraft difficulties. Its basically an Ultra Modded Raid, but with a single addon. PLEASE DO NOT USE THIS AS YOUR BASE SURVIVAL! This raid is so incredibly overpowered and hard, it might be impossible in survival Minecraft. Please do not activate Nightmare Mode on a world you care about, it will end up in ruins.

Morellagers works for all Minecraft Difficulties.

Morellagers now works for all 3 Difficulties in Minecraft! AND its in 1 mcaddon pack? That is right, Morellagers is now 1 addon pack, with subpacks that work for all 3 difficulties that Minecraft has to offer. Morellagers Easy subpack is for the Easy difficulty, Morellagers Normal subpack is for the Normal difficulty, and Morellagers Hard subpack is for the Hard difficulty.

Make sure you put the correct Morellagers subpack with the Minecraft Difficulty you are using, or it will NOT work.

The Guidebook and the Record

Morellagers now has its very own Guidebook! By adding an emerald to a books crafting recipe, you can get this guide. It contains the whole Lore story, and some tips and helpful tricks, and even a settings tabs to turn on and off certain items!

Ever wanted to listen to the Morellagers OST, but just can't? Well, by beating Chapter 3, you can get a music disc that, when interacted with, opens a menu containing every song in the addon!

The Illager Structures

These structures generate randomly across your world, housing powerful and deadly Illagers with them!

Forging Outpost

Builders: DiZaRkY

You hear pounding in the distance, and you investigate. What you see, is the Forging Outpost! This Outpost spawns rarely in Savannas, and contains good loot, IF you can get past the Obsidian Golem they keep in a cage for construction. This Outpost has 3 Pillagers in it. The loot is good, and it looks pretty nice too. But tread carefully, for if you are not careful, you will be slain by the Obsidian Golem in the cage.

Frozen Outpost

Builders: TheEntityNul, DiZaRkY

In the freezing cold of the Frozen Plains, you see a tower looming in the distance, and at closer observation, you see the Frozen Outpost. This Outpost has Iceologers and Pillagers roaming the floors, guarding it. Within the chest, holds a Sentry Smithing Template buried under under Snow and Ice. Do you dare face the Iceologers to obtain this rare armor decor?
