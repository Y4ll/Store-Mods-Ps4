Crosshair Indicator!

Ever missed a hit because you weren't sure if your crosshair was actually on the mob? This resource pack fixes exactly that.

Crosshair Indicator is a simple but super useful UI tweak for Minecraft Bedrock. It keeps the classic 9x9 vanilla crosshair size that we all know, but adds a dynamic hit indicator. Whenever you aim directly at an entity (like a mob or another player), your crosshair will instantly turn red.

It's perfect for PvP, hunting, or just general survival gameplay where every hit counts. No more guessingjust aim, wait for the red, and swing!

Features:

- Clean, vanilla-style crosshair (9x9 pixels)

- Instantly turns red when targeting any entity

- Lightweight and works smoothly on Bedrock

- No experimental gameplay features required!
