Unleash the Power of the Wither!

Orbital Wither Skull Cannon brings the devastating orbital weapon inspired by Spokeishere into Minecraft.

Use the cannon to target a specific area and unleash a powerful barrage of Wither Skull projectiles from above. The attack doesn't just look impressiveit can also devastate the battlefield and affect a wide area.

How It Works

The weapon uses a fishing rod as its targeting device.

Simply aim at the location you want to attack and activate the weapon. Once activated, the cannon will launch a powerful barrage of Wither Skull projectiles toward the targeted area.

The projectiles spread across the designated zone, creating a devastating orbital attack from above.

Destructive Power

The Orbital Wither Skull Cannon is more than just a powerful attack.

- Destroys tough and highly durable blocks, allowing it to break through heavily protected areas.

- Applies the Wither effect over a wide area, affecting entities caught within the attack zone.

- Unleashes a massive Wither Skull barrage from above.

- Covers a large area, making the attack highly effective against groups of targets.

Inspired by Spokeishere

This addon recreates the concept of Spokeishere's famous Orbital Wither Skull Cannon, bringing its distinctive orbital attack style into Minecraft.

The goal is to capture the feeling of calling down a devastating Wither-powered attack from the sky while keeping the weapon simple and exciting to use.

Use With Caution!

The Orbital Wither Skull Cannon is an extremely powerful weapon. Its ability to destroy tough blocks and spread the Wither effect over a large area makes it especially dangerous.

Choose your target carefully before activating it! One wrong decision could turn the entire area into a disaster zone.
