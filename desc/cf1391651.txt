How It Works

Start by crafting the Farm Master Table.
Place it down, and an NPC Farmer will automatically spawn beside it. He offers 5 special farm blocks:

- Cobblestone Generator Block

- Sugar Cane Farm Block

- Cow Farm Block

- Wheat Farm Block

- Mob Farm Block

Place & Preview Before Spawning

When you place any farm block, a structure preview block appears.
It shows the exact area border so youll know if the space is occupied.

Dont like the location?

Just interact with the preview block a small UI will pop up.
From there, you can remove it and try again.

Once you find the perfect spot, confirm to spawn the full structure instantly!

Perfect for Survival & SMPs

This addon is designed to keep gameplay balanced while saving time. Build farms instantly without breaking immersion or using commands.
