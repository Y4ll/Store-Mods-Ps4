Myloveee Totem Skins 24 Action & Stuff Variants

Tired of looking at the same old vanilla Totem of Undying every time you escape death? Give your ultimate lifesaver a massive glow-up that matches your epic combat animations!

Myloveee Totem Skins brings 24 breathtaking, custom 3D animated skins to your Totem of Undying. Built to blend perfectly with the stylish aesthetic of the Action and Stuff framework, you can now hold mystical nether stars, ticking clocks, glowing campfires, or even cute little axolotls in a bucket instead of a boring golden doll!

Switch your totem's look instantly using the in-game settings wheel and match your totem to your current adventure style!

How to Use & Requirements

IMPORTANT REQUIREMENT: This pack is an add-on designed to complement the Action and Stuff animation pack.

To make the custom models and scaling display correctly, you must install Action and Stuff FIRST. In your Global Resources list, place Action and Stuff at the bottom and this pack directly on top of it!

-

Download & Import: Download the .mcpack file and open it to auto-import into Minecraft Bedrock.

-

Activate: Go to Settings > Global Resources and activate the pack.

-

Pick Your Skin: Click on Myloveee Totem Skins in your active list, click the Gear Icon (), slide the bar to your preferred totem, and confirm!
