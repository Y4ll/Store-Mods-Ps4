Adventure Backpacks

An unofficial Bedrock port of the original Adventure Backpacks mod by Darkona.

Adventure Backpacks adds 89 wearable and placeable backpacks to Minecraft Bedrock. Every backpack includes 39 storage slots, a 33 crafting grid, two tool slots, two fluid tanks, and a sleeping bag, while many designs also provide unique abilities based on Minecraft mobs, blocks, and materials.

Requires the Beta APIs experiment.

Two Versions

Both versions include the same backpacks, storage, and abilities. The only difference is how they're worn.

-

Compatible Worn in the chest slot as an attachable. This version doesn't modify the player entity, making it compatible with most other add-ons. The trade-off is that it replaces your chestplate.

-

Slot-Free Can be worn in hotbar slot 9, the top-right inventory slot, or the offhand, allowing you to keep your armor equipped. Because this version overrides the player entity, it must be placed above every other add-on in both pack orders.

Every Backpack Includes

-

39 storage slots that stay with the backpack

-

A built-in 33 crafting grid

-

Two dedicated tool slots

-

Two 4,000 mB fluid tanks

-

A portable sleeping bag

-

Wearable and placeable functionality

Backpacks store their contents internally, so items remain inside when they're dropped, placed, transferred, or recovered after death.
