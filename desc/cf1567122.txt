Required Settings

Before entering the Rotten Lands, make sure the following settings are enabled.

Existing World Warning

Rotten Lands is designed for new worlds.

Adding the addon to an existing world is not recommended. Any newly generated chunks may be transformed into Rotten Lands terrain, potentially causing visible transitions between old and new areas.

Recommended Audio Settings

Rotten Lands includes custom biome ambience and environmental audio designed to replace the default Minecraft soundtrack.

Explore five unique biomes transformed by the apocalypse, each with its own atmosphere, dangers and secrets waiting to be discovered.

Rotten Meadow

Once familiar and peaceful, the Rotten Meadow serves as the gateway into the Rotten Lands. Decayed vegetation and signs of collapse can be found everywhere, offering a glimpse of what happened to the world.

Infested Forest

Dense vegetation and limited visibility make the Infested Forest a dangerous place to explore. The infected can emerge from between the trees at any moment.

Wasteland

Dry terrain, thick fog and abandoned landscapes define the Wasteland. Zombie activity increases significantly at night, making every journey a risk.

Dead Ashlands

Covered in ash soil and scattered with towering stalagmites, the Dead Ashlands are among the most hostile environments in Rotten Lands.

Abandoned City

Massive ruined skyscrapers emerge from the fog, creating an overwhelming sense of scale. Every floor has been manually decorated and filled with loot, rewarding players willing to explore the city's dark interiors.
