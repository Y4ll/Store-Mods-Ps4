Fantasy Villagers Remake 2.0 Part 1

Tired of villagers just standing around trading wheat and emeralds? With Fantasy Villagers Remake 2.0, your villagers finally fight back! This add-on introduces new classes of warriors, mages, and golems to protect your village and challenge hostile mobs in epic battles.

Features

- Infantry Units: Scout, Bowstraton, Arbalistas, Swordsman, Spearman, Town Guard, Knight, Vanguard, Guardian, Chief, Royal Guard, Royal Reinforcer.

- Magical Villagers: Enchanted Librarian & Thermochanter.

- Golems: Great Guardian, Golembreaker, Furnace Golem.

Gears are still a work in progress and will be added in future updates!

How to Install

- Download the .mcaddon file

- Open it and select Minecraft

- Create a new world enable all Experimental & Creator Options

- Enjoy your brand-new fantasy army!
