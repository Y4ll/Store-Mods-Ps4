This addon introduces a terrifying new entity called "Anguish" to your Minecraft world. Unlike regular monsters, Anguish is designed to stalk, torment, and psychologically intimidate players before taking any action.

Features

Stalker Behavior: Anguish quietly follows and watches you from a distance, creating a constant sense of dread.

Look-At Mechanic: Your worst mistake will be looking directly at Anguish. Maintaining eye contact or turning your camera toward it triggers aggressive behaviors.

Custom Models & Animations: Features unique entity models, smooth camera-tracking animations, and immersive custom sounds designed for survival horror.

How to Play

Download and install both the Behavior and Resource packs.

Enable Experimental Features in your world settings for full functionality.

Enter your world... and keep your camera away from the shadows. Good luck!
