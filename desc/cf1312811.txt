EXPERIMENT 8:

This HORRIFY addon allows you to WITNESS the end of time.

The beginning of the infestation:

These parasites will begin their lives in these disgusting meat eggs.

NOTE:

These nests now appear only in the new infected biomes, such as Tissue.
Be careful: when they capture prey, they can explode and release parasites.

Experiment 8 (PARASITES)

These apparently WEAK and DANGEROUS parasites are slow and die in one hit, but IF THEY TOUCH YOU, YOU ARE DEAD.

NOTE:

These little monsters now spawn naturally at night.
They are harmless in the dark, but they HATE THE LIGHT and become AGGRESSIVE when exposed to it.

If they touch you, you will die.

NEW BIOME: TISSUE

A grotesque biome made of living flesh, organic growths, and unstable infected matter.
This is one of the first places where the infestation begins to spread, and where some of its most dangerous structures can generate naturally.

If you see this biome, youd better turn back... or prepare for the worst.

Mobs List:

Living Flesh Nest:

Parasite Nest Pig

These parasites are born inside disgusting LIVING nests, formed from infected pigs that were lucky enough to become part of the infections reproduction cycle.

NOTE:

These nests no longer spawn naturally.
They can only be generated from pigs attacked by Experiment 8.

If you do not destroy them in time, parasites will continue to spawn and the infestation will grow out of control.

( weather-based variants. )

Human Nest

This unfortunate person has become part of a grotesque nest.
It no longer spawns infected, but drops Human Fat and Human Oil when destroyed.

Note:
Weve only seen it appear when the parasite is trying to establish a larger nest or has already succeeded.

Mini Boss:

Infected Iron Golem

This grotesque construct appears when a Corruptor infects an Iron Golem, triggering an unnatural fusion between metal and parasite.
The result is an Infected Iron Golem, a towering abomination that delivers extremely powerful blows and can grab its victims and violently throw them, causing massive damage.

NOTE:
Its strength is overwhelming, and being caught by it often means certain death.

Rare Infected List:

Corruptor

A strange entity that emerges from the corpse of an Infected Human.
It seeks out living beings to merge with such as normal spiders, infected humans, and Iron Golems twisting them into new grotesque forms.

Note:
Spawns only when an Infected Human dies.
It actively searches for compatible hosts to infect and mutate.

Infester

This grotesque creature appears when a Corruptor infects an Infected Human, triggering an unnatural fusion.
The result is an Infester, a disturbing entity that helps spread the infestation around it.

Note:
The Infester infects blocks beneath it, accelerating the spread of the infestation.
It doesnt seem aggressive, and light has no effect on it.

Infected Mooshroom

This creature behaves exactly like a normal Infected Cow, but it is considered rare due to the biome it inhabits, making it difficult to encounter.

NOTE:
Its danger does not come from new abilities, but from how uncommon it is to find.

Snitcher
