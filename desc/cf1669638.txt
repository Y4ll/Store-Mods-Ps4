Key Features:

- Custom Magic Damage System: Direct damage that ignores shields, armor, and vanilla status effects.

- Spell Altars and Rituals: Craft scrolls, imbue weapons, and upgrade stats using glowstone dust, redstone, and gunpowder rituals.

- Scrolls and Spells: Cast offensive, defensive, and utility spells, such as spawning compressed air platforms to walk on air or summoning blood spikes.

- Legendary Weapons and Staffs: Magic swords and staffs that reduce Spells casting time up to 75%.

- Elemental Mastery System: Increases specific elemental damage types as you defeat mobs and progress.

- Addons Compatibility & DLCs: Work with every Addon, and have Full Integration with the How Many Maids addon, allowing maids to wield magic swords and cast spells.

Quick Start Guide

1. Spell Altar Activation:

- Activation: Place the Spell Altar block and interact with Glowstone Dust to turn it on.

- Setup: Spell Altars can link together when placed within 6 blocks of each other.

- Check Stats: Interact with an redstone on the Altar to view your current status.

2. Ritual Mechanics:

Scroll Creation:

- Place 2 Glow Ink Sacs, 2 Echo Shards, and 4 Diamonds on the Spell Altars.

- Interact with a Blank Scroll to finish.

- Optional: Add 1 to 3 Upgrade Shards to increase rarity, or specific elemental shards (Light, Blood, etc.) to guarantee the scroll type.

Creating Magic Swords:

- Place a spell scroll and a diamond sword on the Spell Altar.

- Interact while holding an Upgrade Shard.This creates a permanent spell sword with half the original casting cooldown.

Stat Modification:

- Place up to 12 Stat Shards (Critical Damage, Critical Rate, or Defense) on the Altar and interact with Redstone to overwrite your stats.

- Interact with Gunpowder to craft or tier up Stat Shards.

3. Mastery and Magic Damage:

- Ken Maho damage ignores armors, effects, and shields, can be blocked with the stat Defense. It scales as you play by defeating monsters.
