Ores Dimension

Ever wondered what a world made entirely around one ore would look like? Ores Dimension adds 9 unique ore-themed dimensions to Minecraft Bedrock, each with its own terrain, blocks, trees, and atmosphere based on a specific ore.

9 Ore Dimensions

- Iron Dimension

- Gold Dimension

- Copper Dimension

- Coal Dimension

- Redstone Dimension

- Lapis Dimension

- Diamond Dimension

- Emerald Dimension

- Netherite Dimension

Build Your Ore Portal

Build a portal frame using the corresponding ore block and light it with Flint & Steel. The portal will open into that ore's own dimension.

For example, build an Iron Block frame to enter the Iron Dimension, or a Diamond Block frame to enter the Diamond Dimension.

Unique Worlds

Each dimension generates its own terrain with ore-based surfaces, underground layers, hills, trees, ponds, and natural structures. The terrain is procedurally generated, so you can explore a large world instead of a small fixed area.

Explore & Survive

These dimensions are not just empty ore worlds. Explore their terrain, collect resources, discover different landscapes, and build your own base in whichever ore dimension you like.

Animals such as Cows, Sheep, Pigs, and Chickens can also spawn naturally in the dimensions.

Portal Travel

- Build an ore-block portal in the Overworld.

- Light it using Flint & Steel.

- Enter the portal to travel to the matching ore dimension.

- Return through the portal to go back to the Overworld.

- Portal locations are linked to make travelling between worlds easier.

Features

- 9 custom ore dimensions.

- Iron, Gold, Copper, Coal, Redstone, Lapis, Diamond, Emerald, and Netherite worlds.

- Custom procedural terrain generation.

- Ore-based terrain and vegetation.

- Custom portal system.

- Ore block frames for each dimension.

- Flint & Steel portal activation.

- Natural ponds, hills, trees, and structures.

- Natural animal spawning.

- Linked portal travel.

- Designed for Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
