Addon Developed by

@PnTMC

Supports versions from 1.21.90 to 26.40 & above

This add-on removes the hunger bar and changes how health regeneration works. Instead of regenerating health naturally, you must eat food to restore health instantly. The amount of health restored depends on the type of foodand consuming harmful items, such as Rotten Flesh, may even cause you to lose health.

In addition to food, Potions can also restore your health instantly. However, the amount of health recovered depends on the quality of the Potion.
