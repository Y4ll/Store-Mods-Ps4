The Supreme Anti-Cheat Solution for Minecraft Bedrock

Introduction

We offered a totally free anticheat services for realm and bds server.

What we're special is we don't rely on player.json and compatible with different addon.

We have slash command and UI design, aim to provide a lite, easy-to-use anticheat.

You can do /commandlist to gain anticheat's command list.

Detection

Tired of useless detection which cannot patch most of the cheater? Matrix anticheat is your best choice.

You can do /detection <detection> true to enable the detection you want.
