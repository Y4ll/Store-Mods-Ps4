Ray Tracing like Graphics without a Ray Tracing Card!

You'll be amazed at how much better the graphics look just after installing this shader. The lighting, reflections, everything becomes more realistic and visually stunning. And all of this without needing an RTX GPU! A strong device is still required, of course... it's a shader, after all. But the performance cost is much lower.

The Beatutiful And fantasical Skies!

Minecraft's new deferred rendering technique gives shaders much more control over the environment and graphics. With this shader, you can see how dynamically the sky changes its color and appearance. Each time of day has its own unique sky and lighting. Lunac shaders Version 2 also bring dynamic Skies and lighting. Each Biome has it'a own unique atmosphere and lighting presets.

Lighting & Reflections!

Lighting is an important part of any shader or the overall aesthetic of games, and Lunac shaders truly excel at it. They provide precise control over lighting throughout the day. Reflections are handled in a way that ensures no object looks dull or lifeless from low reflections, and no object looks plastic from overuse. Striking that balance has always been my main goal.

Recommended Video Settings:

- First, activate the Lunac shaders in your world.

- Make sure you're inside the world, then go to Settings > Video Settings.

- In Graphics Mode, make sure "Vibrant Visuals" is selected.

Note: If You are not satisfied with predefined brightness of the shader. You can adjust the brightness in the video settings for the game.

Use "Favor Performance" mode is the recommended over "Favor Visuals" mode unless you have a powerful machine that can easily handle it

Set The Resolution as "Native" by moving the slider. Or else Quality becomes really bad. (Keep it Auto if you have performance issues)
