Commands And Configs

Others Configs:

Cloud Commands:

Teleportation between players

/tpa (steve) request to teleport to a player
/tpahere (steve) request a player to teleport to you
/tpaccept accept a teleport request
/tpdeny decline a teleport request
/tpas (steve) teleport to a player without asking for permission (admin)
/tpashere (steve) teleport a player to you without asking for permission (admin)

Chunk Configuration

/claimchunk claim a chunk
/claimchunk_expand expand a chunk
/unclaimchunk unclaim/refund a chunk
/claimlist list claimed chunks

PvP

/claim_pvp (on/off) enable/disable PvP
/claim_damage_status show current status of these rules

Spawn points

/sethome set your home point
/home teleport to your home point
/delhome delete your home point
/homes list your home points

Warp points

/warp (warp name) teleport to a set warp (has a cooldown)
/setwarp (warp name) set a warp point
/delwarp (warp name) delete a set warp point
/warps list all set warp points

Teams

/maketeam (team name) create a team
/invteam (steve) invite someone to your team
/accteam accept a team invitation
/leaveteam leave your current team
/listteam list all teams
/delteam delete your team
/teamchat (hello world) send a message only to your team
/tkp teleport to the team
/vcaptain vote to change the team captain
/nchange (other team name) change the team name

Miscellaneous utilities

/back return to the last place you died
/banish (steve) kick/ban someone temporarily
/thru pass through walls (admin)
/pulloot pull nearby items/loot to you
/chestsort sort a chest
/playerinvsort sort your own inventory
/moveinv move items from chests to your inventory
/rtp teleport to a safe location
