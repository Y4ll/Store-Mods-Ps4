Survival Reworked is an addon that completely reshapes the survival progression making it longer, harder, and far more exciting!
Dive into a world full of mystery and challenge, where every biome hides secrets and every artifact are unique.

A new way to fish :

To make the game longer and more fun, this add-on gives players a new way to progress.

Discover a brand new artifact mechanic that changes the way you fight, explore, and survive.
Each artifact comes with unique powers and abilities collect them, master them, and become unstoppable!

From simple monsters to massive bosses prepare for a real challenge!
