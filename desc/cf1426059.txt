Villager Soldier 2 Version 3: The Rise

If you want to experience try-hard gameplay and love armies and Minecraft-style battles with Villagers and Illagers, then try this addon. It will bring many new experiences in gameplay and new weapons, armour, and golems.

The addon will not lock achievements when installed in the world.

Since the addon is based on Villager Soldier Vanilla V6, most of the villagers are similar to the Villager Soldier Vanilla V6 addon.

League Generals:

Villager Generals are units subordinate to the alliance of villager armies. Units subordinate to the alliance cannot be bought (tamed) by the player; they can only be summoned using command flags.

How to have League Units, please watch my tutorial videos.

Clan Units:

Clan units are typically lower-level units within the alliance. Clan Leaders, however, are key figures in the League. You need to meet all Clan Leaders to exchange Clan medals to obtain the General's badge.

Tanker and Champion:

In this new version, the Villager Melee unit has been split into two: Vanguard and Tanker. Vanguard remains the same, only they can no longer carry shields and cannot level up. Tankers will be the only unit that can level up to Champion. Tankers have 4 levels; reaching level 4 and accumulating enough XP will allow them to become a Champion. Champions will serve as a transitional unit between the militia and the general.

They can be tamed by the player.

Note: Once your tanker has been tamed and identified, when it levels up to Champion, you should re-identify it to continue using the team system's functions. Since the code only transfers name data and not tag data, please re-identify it immediately after your tanker levels up to Champion.

For more details, please watch the tutorial video.
