JOIN DISCORD

With this addon, you can play the characters from the anime Solo Leveling.
Some mobs will be added in future updates.
If you find any bugs, please report them on Discord.

Sung Jin-Woo now commands an army of undead, summoning shadow soldiers to aid him.
Choi Jong-In unleashes powerful fireballs to incinerate his enemies.
Cha Hae-In excels at eliminating undead and taking down powerful bosses with ease.
