Simple Vein Miner | V2.1.0

Simple Vein Miner is a Vein Miner addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Break one ore and the entire vein mines itself with full enchantment support, per-player settings, and zero lag.

The Most Lightweight & Efficient Mining Addon for Minecraft Bedrock! (Compatible with 1.21.120+ and Realms)

Simple Vein Miner V2.1 brings stability and UX improvements on top of the fully featured settings system introduced in V2.0.

WHAT'S NEW IN V2.1.0

Stability fixes and a cleaner in-game experience better toggle feedback, a fixed Silk Touch behavior, and the removal of an unintended server-wide gamerule change.

Stability Fixes

- Fixed Silk Touch drop duplication: blocks now drop correctly with no chance of generating duplicate items.

- Removed unintended gamerule showtags false that was being applied server-wide on every new player join.

Improved UX

- Clearer welcome message: new players now receive three lines explaining how to use the addon right away.

- Distinct toggle sounds & icons: enabling plays a unique sound and shows Enabled; disabling plays a different sound and shows Disabled.

Custom Config Item

- Replaced the vanilla book with a proper custom item (svm:config).

- Right-Click it to open the Settings Menu.

- Given automatically on first join and kept on death.

In-Game Settings Menu

- Toggle Options:

- Magnet XP & Items: Gradually pulls dropped items and experience orbs toward the mined vein.

- Particles: Turn mining particle effects ON or OFF.

- Silk Touch Support: Choose whether Silk Touch drops the ore block instead of the resource.

- Configurable Limits (Sliders):

- Max Horizontal Spread: 116 blocks

- Max Blocks Up: 132 blocks

- Max Blocks Down: 032 blocks

- Max Total Blocks: 8512 blocks

- Reset to Defaults: One toggle at the bottom of the menu restores all settings instantly.

Toggle Mode

- How to Toggle: Hold a Pickaxe, Sneak + Right-Click in the air.

- Visual Feedback: An Actionbar message tells you if the Vein Miner is ENABLED or DISABLED.

- Freedom: Once enabled, mine ores standing up, running, or flying!

Advanced Durability & Enchantments

- Fortune IIII: Fully calculated to match vanilla drop formulas. You get the extra resources you deserve.

- Unbreaking IIII: Fully supported each block has a reduced chance of damaging the tool.

- Silk Touch: Drops the ore block itself instead of the resource (toggleable per player).

- "Save My Pickaxe": The addon automatically stops mining if your pickaxe has only 1 durability left.

Core Features

- ZERO LAG Technology: Powered by the Job System (system.runJob) breaks veins in batches of 16 blocks per tick.

- Smart Item Attraction: Dropped items and XP are gradually pulled toward the origin block for a smooth, natural collection effect.

- Universal Compatibility: Works with ANY Pickaxe (Vanilla, Netherite, Custom Addons) and ANY Ore (Overworld, Nether, Deepslate, custom addon ores).

How to Use

- Get the Config Item: Received automatically on first join.

- Toggle Mode: Hold your Pickaxe, Sneak + Right-Click. You will hear a sound and see "Enabled".

- Mine: Break any ore. The entire vein mines itself!

- Configure: Right-Click the config item to adjust options and limits.
