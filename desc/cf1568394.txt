# Arkcraft

Arkcraft is a Minecraft Bedrock add-on inspired by ARK-style survival gameplay, focused on dinosaurs, prehistoric creatures, taming mechanics, special tools, saddles, creature variants, bosses, and unique survival systems.

This update reworks the add-on almost entirely: many creatures were visually improved, several outdated mobs were repaired or removed, and the gameplay was rebuilt to feel more stable, fun, and full of new content.

## Main Features

- Many prehistoric and fantasy creatures inspired by ARK-style gameplay.
- Knockout and passive taming systems.
- Narcotics, kibbles, narcotic arrows, bolas, bows, crossbows, rifles, clubs, and other taming tools.
- Rideable creatures with different saddles depending on size, diet, or creature type.
- Flying, aquatic, terrestrial, shoulder, boss, and utility creatures.
- Creature variants, including special glowing variants.
- Special tools such as whips, sickles, TEK tools, fishing tools, maces, sabers, grappling hooks, creature bags, platforms, and more.
- Custom food, armor, crops, crafting tables, NPCs, and survival items.
- Creature abilities, synergies, biome spawning, and special interactions.

## Versions

The Full Version includes all Arkcraft content, including ARK-inspired creatures, Atlas-inspired weapons, extra creatures, tools, items, and additional systems.

The Lite Version is currently paused.

## Important

This add-on is still being improved. Some creatures, mechanics, textures, animations, or systems may change between versions.

For detailed taming methods, creature stats, biome spawns, item explanations, and full gameplay guides, please check the official guide / Discord / wiki.
