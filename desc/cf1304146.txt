About the pack:

This PvP texture pack was designed specifically for Minecraft Bedrock Edition players who enjoy competitive modes like BedWars, SkyWars, and The Bridge. It offers cleaner visuals and improved item textures to help you focus during fights.

Main features:

Short swords for better screen visibility

Simplified block textures (wool, ores, etc.)

Vibrant item colors for easier identification

Highlighted hotbar and clean UI elements

Optimized for better FPS on low-end devices

Compatibility:

Minecraft Bedrock 1.20+

Works on popular PvP servers

Best for:

BedWars, SkyWars, Duels, The Bridge, and other PvP modes
