X-ray Glowy Ores: Outline X-Ray + Glowing Ores in one pack

Tired of digging blind? X-ray Glowy Ores turns the ground into a see-through wireframe world and makes every ore shine like a tiny lamp. It combines two popular Bedrock packs into one ready-to-use download, so you don't have to stack them, reorder them or fix conflicts yourself.

WHAT YOU GET

Outline X-Ray
Stone, deepslate, dirt, gravel, sand, sandstone, netherrack, basalt, blackstone, tuff, calcite, ice and many other common blocks become transparent and keep only a thin one-pixel outline. Each block family has its own outline colour: gray for stone, darker gray for deepslate, brown for dirt, pale yellow for sand, deep red for netherrack. You can still read caves, tunnels and walls at a glance, and the grid always shows how far away a block is and where the next one sits.

Glowing Ores
Every ore stays solid and fully visible, and the veins inside it glow. That covers coal, copper, iron, gold, redstone, lapis, diamond and emerald, their deepslate versions, nether gold, nether quartz and ancient debris. Diamonds shine aqua, emeralds green, gold yellow, redstone bright red, lapis deep blue and iron a warm peach. Only the ore pixels light up while the stone around them stays matte, so the veins stand out from the dark.

Together you get a clean, dark, gridded underground with glowing ore clusters floating inside it: the classic look of mining videos and screenshots.

KEY FEATURES
- Outline X-Ray and Glowing Ores merged into a single .mcpack
- Thin outlines on stone, deepslate, dirt, sand, netherrack and more
- Ores stay solid, with glow colours that match each ore
- Emissive glow maps and texture sets for all overworld and nether ores
- No overlapping files and no pack-order problems
- The original outline material and block settings are kept unchanged

REQUIREMENTS
Both parts of this pack have a requirement, and both matter.
1. X-Ray: the outline effect uses a custom RenderChunk material. You need a patched Minecraft, BetterRenderDragon or MaterialBinLoader on Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.30 or newer (minimum engine version in the manifest: 1.26.40). It will not work on an unpatched vanilla client.
2. Glow: ore glow uses Minecraft's PBR emissive maps. Turn on Vibrant Visuals in Settings > Video > Graphics Mode. Without it, ores look normal.

HOW TO INSTALL
1. Open the .mcpack file and Minecraft will import it automatically.
2. Go to Settings > Global Resources and activate X-ray Glowy Ores. Keep the two original packs switched off so they don't clash.
3. Turn on Vibrant Visuals in the video settings.
4. Load your world and start mining.

TIPS
The effect looks best in caves and the deepslate layers, where the dark background lets the glow stand out.

FAQ
Do I need the original packs as well? No. Everything is included in this one file, and the originals should stay switched off.
Why do the ores not glow for me? Ore glow needs Vibrant Visuals, which requires a device that supports it.
Why do I not see outlines? The X-ray effect needs a patched game, BetterRenderDragon or MaterialBinLoader.

FAIR PLAY
X-ray gives a big advantage. Please use this pack only in single player, on your own worlds, or on servers that allow it.
