Tired of chopping wood block by block? Say goodbye to tedious work with Ultimate Tree Capitator, a smart Behavior Pack and Resource Pack for Minecraft Bedrock (v26+) that lets you fell entire trees with a single swing! Feel the satisfaction of watching a whole tree crumble in seconds with 8 spectacular breaking modes!

What's Inside? The pack introduces a global "complete tree chopping" ability with an advanced in-game configuration menu. No new tools to craft! Simply use any axewood, stone, iron, diamond, netherite, or even axes from other addonsand customize your lumberjack experience!

Key Features:

8 Spectacular Breaking Modes:

- Instant Break - All blocks vanish at once for maximum efficiency

- Chopping Mode - Watch the tree fall progressively, block by block

- Vein Miner - Tree breaks from top to bottom in a satisfying cascade

- Spiral Mode - Blocks break in a mesmerizing spiral pattern

- Layer by Layer - Tree disappears one height level at a time

- Random Mode - Unpredictable and chaotic block breaking

- Inside Out - Breaks from the center outward

- Explosive Mode - Watch your tree EXPLODE dramatically with particle effects!

Advanced Customization Menu:

- 5 Speed Settings - From Very Fast to Very Slow, control the breaking animation

- Smart Leaf Removal - Automatically removes only the leaves from YOUR tree, not nearby trees

- Auto-Replant - Saplings automatically replant at the base after chopping

- Durability Control - Toggle whether your axe takes damage per block

- Visual Effects - Enable/disable particles and sounds

- Easy Toggle - Sneak + Use to quickly enable/disable the mod

- Jump + Use - Opens the full configuration menu mid-game

Smart Technology:

- Intelligent Tree Detection - Uses advanced BFS algorithm to find all connected logs

- Works with Any Axe - Vanilla or modded axes, no complex recipes needed

- Performance Optimized - Handles trees of any size smoothly

- Custom UI Icons - Beautiful custom textures for all modes and settings

- Tag-Based Config - Your settings persist across sessions

How to Use:

- Install both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack

- Equip any axe

- Jump + Use to open the configuration menu

- Choose your favorite breaking mode and settings

- Start chopping and enjoy the show!

Compatible with Minecraft Bedrock v1.20.30+

Transform your wood gathering from a chore into an spectacular experience!

Works with Any Axe:

No complex recipes. Grab your favorite axe and start chopping! It works seamlessly with vanilla axes and most axes from other add-ons
