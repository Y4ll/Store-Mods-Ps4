BSLD Shaders is a full PBR resource pack for Minecraft Bedrock that reworks lighting, skies, and atmosphere into a violet-magenta cinematic experience built to shine with Vibrant Visuals enabled.

Reimagined Visual Style

- Bold violet-magenta color identity carried through skies, fog, and lighting

- Consistent atmospheric styling applied across all 85+ vanilla biomes

- Adds mood and depth to builds while keeping vanilla terrain readable

- Cinematic ACES tone mapping with a warm 6400K color temperature for punchy, filmic tones

Advanced Atmospherics System

- Hand-tuned sky zenith and horizon curves for a smooth transition across the full day cycle

- Bold orange-gold horizon blend at sunrise and sunset for dramatic transitions

- Deep blue zenith through midday for contrast against the warm horizon

- Balanced Rayleigh scattering values to keep skies vivid without washing out or going flat black

Reworked Local & Global Lighting

- Retuned light colors for torches, lanterns, campfires, and other emissive blocks

- Accurate glow tones for soul fire, redstone, amethyst, and copper block variants

- Adds richer, warmer ambient lighting to interiors and builds with no gameplay changes

Full PBR Material Support

- Metalness, emissive, roughness, and subsurface values tuned separately for blocks, actors, particles, and items

- PBR detailing on all ore types, including every deepslate variant and ancient debris

- Custom still and flowing water with dedicated normal maps and MER maps, plus animated caustics for realistic underwater light

Violet Aurora Effect

- Custom curve-driven aurora particle animation drifting across the sky

- Adds a signature atmospheric touch without pulling focus from gameplay

Requirements
BSLD Shaders requires Minecraft Bedrock/PE 1.21.0 or higher, with Vibrant Visuals (PBR rendering) enabled in video settings.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it to import into Minecraft

- Enable BSLD Shaders under Global Resources

- Turn on Vibrant Visuals in video settings
