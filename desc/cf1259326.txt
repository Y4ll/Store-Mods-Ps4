Sniffer Custom Finder v. 1.0.2

Sniffer Custom Finder lets your Sniffer have a custom choice, whether you want them to dig ONLY torchflower seeds or pitcher pod, how about customizing your chances when finding the said loots. All of that without losing your achievements! Designed for survival worlds, hardcore worlds, and resource-focused worlds. This addon is compatible to the players/devices that play Minecraft : Bedrock Edition/MCPE. Whether you want them to dig certain items from the sniffer, this addon is for you!

In this addon, there are five (5) settings. These decides on what your sniffer digs. These are the following:

1. Normal

2. ONLY Torchflower Seeds

3. ONLY Pitcher Pod

4. DOMINANT Torchflower Seeds

5. DOMINANT Pitcher Pod
