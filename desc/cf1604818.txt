Real Vein Ore lets you mine an entire ore vein instantly, making mining much faster and more efficient.

Auto Smelt automatically smelts only compatible ores that normally require smelting, such as Iron Ore, Gold Ore, and other supported ores.

How to Use

- Mine an ore block using a Pickaxe.

- Breaking an ore with a pickaxe will automatically activate Real Vein Ore.

- If the ore is supported by Auto Smelt, it will drop its smelted version instead of the raw ore.
