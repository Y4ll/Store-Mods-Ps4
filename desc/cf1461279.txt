Natural Visual is a high-quality shader designed to bring realistic lighting, atmospheric depth, and cinematic visuals to Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Built with Vibrant Visual support, this shader delivers a smooth RTX-style PBR experience without requiring official ray tracing hardware.

With carefully balanced global lighting, enhanced atmosphere settings, and refined color grading, Natural Visual creates:

Realistic sunlight and soft shadow transitions

Cinematic sky tones and immersive fog

Enhanced PBR surface reflections

Smooth ambient lighting balance

Stable exposure between bright and dark areas
