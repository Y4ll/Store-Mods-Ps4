Use the power rings and become the lanterns in minecraft!

Currently the only rings that are obtainable in survival, the green, red and yellow rings.

FOR A VIDEO TUTORIAL:

To get the green power ring, you have to find this structure, near beaches, rivers and mangrove swamps:

Interact with the entity and get your ring

Functioning of the Ring

Upon shifting + interacting with the ring you equip the suit. Upon equipping the suit, the suit and ring are locked into your inventory and cannot be lost on death.

As you keep using your ring, the durability of the ring will go down, at a rate of 2 durability per 3 seconds and when it reaches one, the ring will stop working. To charge the ring again you have to take a charging lantern which you also get at the above structure and place it down

(COLORED LIGHTING ONLY FOR VIBRANT VSUALS)

You can put your ring in the slot in the lantern and it charges the ring at 4 durability per second, the total durabiltiy of the ring being 1000. You can pick up the lantern by shifting and interacting with the lantern with the ring of the same color as the lantern.

ABILITIES

Upon shifting and interacting with the ring again this menu pops up:

TOOLS:

The tools work as they do in normal minecraft, with each of them doing their normal functions as their diamond counterparts.

MELEE:

There are 3 melee weapons:
Sword: Does 7 damage
Boxing Glove: Gives the player extra reach
Bat: Does extra knockback

RANGED:

There are 2 ranged weapons:
Bazooka: Fires a missile which explodes on impact, has a cooldown, needs to be charged for 2 seconds by holding down interact and firing
Beam: No cooldown projectile

CONSTRUCTS

There are 2 constructs:
Grab: Shoot any entities within 3 blocks to move them around wherever you look, walking backward and forward also moves them, you can release them by interacting, or they will be let go on their own in 7 seconds.
Spyglass: A spyglass.

TRAVEL

There are 3 places to travel currently:
Earth: Takes you back to wherever you left from if on another planet
OA: Planet to go to get the yellow power ring
Ysmault: Planet to go get the red ring
