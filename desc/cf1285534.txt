Overview

HOW is Survival harder? Most monsters in the game have been changed in some way to make them tougher to deal with, Creepers are more precise before they try to explode, Zombies hit a bit harder, Skeletons are more accurate, Spiders come in different sizes, Witches apply Resistance to nearby Monsters, Husks will slow you down if your too close, the Enderman can warp you if your too close (only in the overworld) and anything tied to Progression/Bosses have been given movesets so they're more diverse.

Its not just Monster changes that make the game harder. You can't access certain parts of the game immediately due to progression. The big ones are the Nether and Deepslate regions. Completing the first 3 progression Bosses will unlock these areas (USE THE "Neo Elementals Current Progression" Item), and there are a total of 9 things tied to progression. The reasoning for Progression Blocks is to prevent going after the strongest items immediately and breaking the rest of the game. It's also there to tie parts of the game together instead of things just existing.

Night Skip: The first 3 Nights can't be skipped, and afterwards, whenever you do decide to sleep the next night won't be skipped. In the base game, you can skip the challenging parts of the game by just sleeping you can still do that just every other night. There's also Night Fog in the early game, done to obstruct your vision, maybe that'll keep you inside at Night. Comments talking about Herobrine?
