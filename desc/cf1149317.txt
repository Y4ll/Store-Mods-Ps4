In short:

Capture Villagers with the Villager Cage, trade from below, and free them by breaking the top. Ideal for organized villager management!

How to capture:

- Obtain the "Empty Cage" item by crafting.

- Stand close to a Villager while holding the Empty Cage.

- Right click/long press on screen (old button) / click eat button (new control).

- The "Empty Cage" will turn into a "Captured Villager" item.

- Click on ground to place cage block.

- Cage block down part is used for trading.

- Cage block upper part is used for breaking.
