Fly Wand
Given on world join unlocks Fly Mode!

Fly Mode:
Look up to fly up
Look down to fly down
Uses Mana while flying
Mana refills when grounded
Stops flying if mana is 0

Fun & balanced flight for epic exploring

Soul Wand

How it works:
Hold the Soul Wand
Hit any mob
The mob dies instantly
Drops a Monster Spawner
And its Spawn Egg

Requires Full Mana
Consumes the entire Mana bar!
Wont work if Mana is low.
