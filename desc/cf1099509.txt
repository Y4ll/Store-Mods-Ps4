The texture pack contains 9 custom skies or cubemaps that can be used in any of your Minecraft worlds. Each cubemap is designed for different environments, weather conditions, and styles.

Features

- Seamless and natural skies, thanks to the use of real 3D (HDRI) images for the sky.

- High resolution and looks stunning and crystal-clear.

- 10 New skies

- The textures have been carefully chosen for various styles and environments, including:

- Snowy weather

- Stormy weather

- Nebula galaxy
...and more.

- Dynamic Sky (default): unlike other skyies, this one only adds realistic looking clouds thus when it blends with the day/night cycle.

- Works with new Vibrant Visual mode.

- Compatible with other texture packs (unless there's any texture pack which has custom sky too).

- Works in Bedrock and Pocket Edition.
