Newb X Mike Shader makes Minecraft Bedrock look alive without changing the vanilla feel. Soft clouds drift across the sky and drag their shadows over the ground, sunrises and sunsets burn orange around the sun, and the night fills with stars that twinkle on their own.

Requirements

MB LOADER IS REQUIRED TO RUN THIS SHADER
THIS IS NOT A VIBRANT VISUALS PACK

You need MB Loader and a patched Minecraft. This is a real shader, not a Vibrant Visuals pack. Vanilla Minecraft cannot load it. Installing it on an unpatched game does nothing at all.

Do not turn Vibrant Visuals on for this one. It is a separate renderer and this shader is not built for it.

If you are looking for something that works on normal unpatched Minecraft, all of my other packs are Vibrant Visuals packs. Links are further down.

What this changes

Volumetric clouds

Soft, fluffy clouds that move across the sky and drag their shadows over the ground as they pass. This is the part I spent the most time on.

Sunrise and sunset

The sky turns orange in a band around the sun while the rest of it stays blue, instead of the whole sky changing color at once.

Night sky

Cyan night sky with stars that twinkle on their own rather than sitting flat.

Rain

The ground goes dark and soaked while rain is falling, and fog rolls in around you.

Thunderstorms

Grass and trees sway much harder than they do in normal rain, so a storm reads as a storm before you hear the thunder.

Waving plants

Grass, leaves, flowers and saplings all move in the wind.

Glowing blocks

Torches, lanterns, glowstone and ores give off light in their own color instead of everything glowing the same orange.

Colors and shadows

Stronger shadows and richer colors through the whole day, without pulling the world away from the vanilla look.

Subpacks

Pick a cloud style in the pack settings:

- Default

- Rounded Clouds

- Box Clouds

- Realistic Clouds

There are also two lighter options if you care more about frames than looks:

- No Fog drops the fog and the rain haze.

- No Wave stops plants and leaves from moving.

On a weaker phone, start with No Wave. It is the cheapest thing to give up and you keep the clouds and the lighting.

Installation

- Install MB Loader first. Without it nothing below will do anything.

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate the shader

- Pick your cloud subpack, or a No Fog / No Wave option if you want more frames

- Leave the world and load it again once if nothing looks different
