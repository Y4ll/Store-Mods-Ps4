PlayZer's Waypoints TP UI Fast Travel & Survival Teleportation Addon

Are you exploring a massive survival world, building across distant biomes, and tired of endless walking? PlayZer's Waypoints TP UI is the ultimate lightweight utility addon and fast-travel system designed to make teleportation effortless in Minecraft Bedrock Edition (MCPE / Windows / Console).

Save your favorite locations, teleport instantly across your world, and navigate like a proall without disabling Xbox achievements or needing cheats enabled! Perfect for pure Survival, Hardcore, Multiplayer SMPs, and Realms.

Key Features

- 100% Achievement Friendly (No Cheats Required): Progress through your survival world without limitations. All Xbox achievements and trophies stay permanently enabled.

- Intuitive Custom TP Menu UI: Receive an enchanted "TP Menu" item upon joining. Simply use/right-click to open a clean, modern, and lag-free navigation interface.

- Private & Multiplayer Ready: Every player has their own secure, personal waypoint list. Other players on the server cannot see or overwrite your saved locations.

- Smart Waypoint Management:

- Save New Location: Easily name and bookmark your exact XYZ coordinates (Bases, Mineshafts, Farms, Portals).

- Instant Teleport: Jump directly to any saved spot in seconds with a single tap.

- Delete Waypoints: Clean up old locations with clear coordinate displays for maximum accuracy.

- Ultra-Optimized & Lag-Free: Built with lightweight scripts to ensure smooth 60+ FPS performance on mobile devices (Android / iOS), low-end PCs, and servers.

How to Use

- Get the Item: Upon spawning or joining the world, you will automatically receive an enchanted Ender Pearl named "TP Menu".

- Open the UI: Hold the item and Right-Click (PC) or tap "Use" / Long-Press (Mobile) to launch the menu.

- Save Waypoint: Select "Save New Location", enter a custom name, and save your current coordinates.

- Fast Travel: Click "Go to Saved Location" to view your waypoint list and teleport instantly.

- Manage: Use "Delete Location" to remove any waypoint you no longer need.

Installation Guide

- Download the .mcaddon or .mcpack file.

- Tap/Open the file to automatically import it into Minecraft Bedrock.

- Apply the addon in your World Settings under both Behavior Packs and Resource Packs.

- Load into your world and enjoy hassle-free teleportation!

Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition: Fully compatible with Mobile (Android / iOS), Windows 10/11, Xbox, PlayStation, and Nintendo Switch.

- Game Modes: Works seamlessly on Singleplayer Worlds, LAN, Dedicated Bedrock Servers, and Realms.
