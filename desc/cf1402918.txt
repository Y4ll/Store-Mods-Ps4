This addon introduces a biological entity with supernatural powers, and the corruption Xadrier, is based on the analog horror series shadows in your eyes

Will you be brave enough to live being watched?

You will have to face this:

Around the entire overworld, corrupt cores will be generated, which will infect all types of entities, turning them into entity absorbers.

Entity Absorbers can infect other entities.

The only way to stop them is to destroy them, good luck with that. Due to corruption, the night cycle may not end, causing the night to often last several days... you won't be able to do much against that.

Xadurgie:

The main entity of the addon may appear in dark areas from time to time, can teleport and attack from a distance. He's always watching you

Xadurgie's behavior changes depending on the biome where it appears. If it is born in the XARD BIOME, it will be larger but will also disappear faster. Additionally, its eyes, which turn the world into night, cannot be generated in that biome because the Tree of Innocence has an influence over it.

Necrocordyceps Xadrier:

It is a creature made by the cordyceps fungus, corrupted and brought to this universe that will also torment the player.

Daico Hunger Xadrier:

A flying biological entity that will levitate the player when attacking them

Xard Skeletons

Many have tried to find the tree of innocence but have gotten lost in the Xard labyrinth, never returning home; now they are skeletons under the command of the corrupt entity.

Androids:

Simple puppets of Xadurgie, they will become hostile to the player if the evil entity is nearby, they will have an important role in the future

If you kill them, they will give you "Innocent Heart" which you can use to do two things

If you interact with a baby villager using the "innocent heart", it will drop a sack of meat

You must exchange this block with the Innocence Tree, which is located in the XARD MAZE biome.

By offering the sack of meat to the tree, it will give you one sprout for each body; this is its only form of reproduction.

XADURGIE IS AFRAID OF THIS TREE, you can use the sprout to create a safe zone and repel the entity

You must feed the tree "innocent heart" for it to grow; ONLY NATURALLY GENERATED INNOCENT TREES CAN GIVE YOU SPROUTS.

Make the most of the first day, as you may never see the sun again in your world...

Good luck with that
