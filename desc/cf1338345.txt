Step back into history and dominate the battlefield with this World War II Tank Add-on! This pack brings iconic WWII-era tanks to Minecraft, designed for both realism and fun gameplay. Whether you're storming enemy bases, defending your territory, or just exploring your world in style, these tanks pack heavy firepower and durable armor to change how you play the game.

Features:

Authentic WWII Tanks Classic tanks inspired by real-life vehicles from the Second World War.

Functional Cannons Blast through enemies, mobs, and terrain with powerful tank shells.

Custom Models & Animations Detailed, historically-inspired tank models designed to fit Minecrafts style.

Balanced Gameplay Tanks are strong but fair, making them perfect for both singleplayer adventures and multiplayer battles.

Custom Camera - Swich between third person and first person camera.

How to use the tanks

Opening the tank menu - To open the tank menu right click or hit the tank and the menu will pop up. In the menu you can turn on/off the engine, change camera perspective, change gun and switch cammos.
