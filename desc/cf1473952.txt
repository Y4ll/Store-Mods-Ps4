Siren Head: The Alternate Alarms

Turn on Experimental (Beta APIs)

Siren Head addon that introduces a terrifying mob. It stalks players in Survival.

Features:

- Health: 8000

- Damage: 6 (But there are reports that its damage is far more devastating than that)

- The speed is very fast, faster than the player

- Makes very loud noises when it has a target

- It can continuously combine wide-area attack skills.

- It can break many types of blocks

- Spawns in the Overworld
