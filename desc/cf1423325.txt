-->Gameplay & Movesets<--

Multi Moveset Moves

There are some moves that are available to multiple movesets.
Firstly, the first slot of every moveset will always contain m1s. You can crouch while m1ing someone to give them extra knockback.

Secondly, You can Block by crouching, allowing you to negate some attacks

Third, Black Flashes. You must build up 15 charges to be able to use a black flash, this is done by simply m1ing your opponents. To actually use the Black Flash, hold your m1 item, and right click. You will be put in a black flash state for 3 seconds, during which time m1ing someone again will activate the black flash. After this you are put in The Zone for 10 seconds, during which time if you m1 someone, you will gain double the usual charge.

Fourth and Fifth, Boost and Blocking.
Boost is a move which grants the user insane mobility for 2 seconds. Think of it like a way better dash.
These can be accessed by crouching with your cease cursed energy item. You will initially get boost, but you can crouch to swap between them.

There is a third skill in this crouch swap rotation however; the Misc Skills Menu, which contains even more UNLOCKABLE skills for you to use.
-REVERSE CURSED TECHNIQUE
-SIMPLE DOMAIN
-BINDING VOW
-CURSED ENERGY SENSING
-TAUNT

These can all be unlocked via defeating various bosses or buying them from the Yuta Okkotsu NPC.
Toji also possesses these skills but just rebranded to not be cursed techniques, lol
But instead of Simple Domain he gets Domain Nullification; spam this move and all domain effects will be neutralized.

There are three main moveset types: Blitz (extremely aggro), Combo (focuses on chaining moves together, can be aggro or passive), Ranged (extremely passive)
