About

- DynamicSurroundings-Remastered Sounds immerses you in over 800 dynamic sound effects and ambient sounds, revolutionizing the atmosphere of Minecraft. Experience a more realistic gameplay with just the power of sound as a ResourcePack! for BEDROCKKKKK

- This sounds pack is an official fork "DynamicSurroundings" sounds pack!

Features

- 800+ Sound effects in total.

- Biome-dependent ambient sounds such as Jungle, Forest, Ocean, Beach, Desert, Deep-dark Cave&Cliffs, etc...

- Unique entity ambient sounds depend on where they naturally spawn.

- Unique utilities sound effects such as picking items, bow & crossbow, combat, walking, running, wandering, explosion, etc...

- Unique and realistic weather sound effects such as rain, thunder, storm, wind, etc...

- Enhance overall Minecraft's soundscapes.

- Smooth overall fade in and fade out sounds.
