Mas Effects [Bedrock Remake]

Mas Effects is a Bedrock Edition remake of the original Java mod, designed to enhance your gameplay with visually appealing particles and immersive effects.

Features

Hitbox Indicator

Displays a dynamic hitbox that turns red when a player enters range, helping improve combat awareness.

Mace Soundwave Effect

Adds a powerful ground impact soundwave when a mace hits, giving attacks more weight and visual feedback.

Critical Particles

Enhances critical hits with a shockwave effect and clean white sparkles around the targets head.

Death Particles

Introduces unique skull particles along with a stylized and slightly goofy death animation.

Ender Pearl Trail

Ender pearls now leave behind smooth particle trails during flight.

Totem Particles

Totems emit star-like particles upon activation, making clutch moments feel more impactful.
