How To Use

1. First Collect & Find Unenchanted Leveled Books
2. Once You Want To upgrade and activate books into enchanted versions
YOU MUST CRAFT AN ACTIVATION BOOK
3. Hold activation book in hand with books you want to enchant ito usable versions inside your inventory, leaving an extra inventory space or 2 for conversions
4. use enchanted books inside an anvil on equipment!

Future Crafting Info

Keep Books Plain/Unenchanted to use in future crafting
if you want to upgrade them into Higher versions!
(I.E combining books/going from protection 9 to 10)
