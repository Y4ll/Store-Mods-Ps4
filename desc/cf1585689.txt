Tombstone Addon

Turns death into a clean, recoverable gameplay moment instead of a chaotic item explosion.

When a player dies, their inventory is safely stored inside a tombstone block placed in the world. Return to the grave, unlock it, and recover your items through polished menus, key mechanics, visual effects, and customizable grave styles.

Features

- Placeable tombstone blocks, not entities

- Graves store dropped player items after death

- Multiple grave models and material styles

- Key-based recovery system

- Grave claiming and interaction menus

- Visual effects, particles, and sounds

- Configurable tombstone appearance

- Multiplayer-friendly recovery flow
