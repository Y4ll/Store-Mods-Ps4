Intelligent Entity Management System

The optimizer incorporates a dynamic tagging system that allows you to classify mobs and dropped items in real-time, offering complete control over what stays and what gets removed.

Valuable Entity Protection Important entitiespets, villagers, named mobs, and mountscan be marked as protected, guaranteeing their permanent preservation during any cleanup process.

Automatic and Manual Cleanup Disposable elements, overflowing items, and non-essential spawns can be tagged for automatic or manual removal as needed, keeping your world free of unnecessary clutter.

Configurable Limits For environments with high entity density (mob farms, massive builds, crowded servers), the system allows you to set configurable limits that prevent world saturation.

Intelligent Auto-Optimizer Automatically activates when defined thresholds are exceeded, removing only permitted entities while respecting all established protections. Starts with a conservative approach that can be progressively adjusted based on gameplay experience.

Safety Reset System Allows you to revert deletion lists without affecting protected entities, offering complete control and peace of mind over the optimization process.

Visual and Performance Optimizations (FPS)

Low-impact visual adjustments calibrated to increase FPS without significantly altering the game's aesthetics, preserving critical milliseconds in combat and movement fluidity.

V-Sync Deactivation Releases additional frames on compatible devices, especially beneficial on low-end hardware. May generate screen tearing in some cases, but on older or low-performance devices, the FPS increase amply compensates for this visual effect.
