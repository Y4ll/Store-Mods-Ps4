Upon death, a grave will be automatically created at your location, and you will receive the coordinates so you can easily return and recover your items.

V1.0.6

-New Gravestone Texture

-Gravestone Key: teleports you to your last death location

Note: At the moment I have 2 versions of the addon, one with the gravestone key and another without. In a future update it can be configured in the game itself according to your preferences.

Don't forget to activate the beta-api from experimental options.
