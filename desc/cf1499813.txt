Auto Hold Totem Addon

Never worry about dying again! This achievement-friendly addon automatically keeps a Totem of Undying in your off-hand whenever you need it, giving you instant protection in survival, PvP, and adventures.

How to use

- Activate Auto Hold Totem

Equip a Totem of Undying in your inventory.

This addon will automatically place it in your off-hand for instant protection.

- Deactivate / Hold Shield

Equip shield in your off-hand

You can craft totems now

Notes

Works with all Totems of Undying in your inventory.

Compatible with survival, adventure, and PvP gameplay.

Lightweight, easy to use, and achievement-friendly.
