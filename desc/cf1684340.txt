Flux Dynamic Light

A lightweight, high-performance, and lag-free dynamic lighting addon built specifically for Minecraft Bedrock Edition (1.21.x+).

Tired of placing hundreds of torches while mining in dark caves? Just hold a torch, lantern, or any light-emitting item in your hand or off-hand, or toss it into a dark ravine, and watch the world illuminate around you smoothly in real time.

Important: no experimental toggles are required to use this addon. However, due to a recent Minecraft update, any behavior pack installed from outside the official Marketplace disables Xbox Achievements/Trophies in that world, regardless of experimental toggles. This addon is NOT achievement friendly. If keeping achievements matters to you or your players, keep this in mind before installing.

What's New in v1.0.1

- Added full dynamic lighting support for Copper Torch and Copper Lantern.

- Added full support for Copper Bulb (all oxidized and waxed variants) and Chemistry/Colored Torches.

- Enhanced the in-game Settings UI contrast for clearer readability on all screens.

- Cleaned up the config stick's tooltip text.

Highlights

- No Experimental Toggles Required: no need to enable Beta APIs, Holiday Creator Features, or any other experimental toggle. Just activate the packs and play. Note: achievements will still be disabled in that world since this is a non-Marketplace behavior pack.

- Centralized Lag-Free Engine: built with an optimized single-loop scheduler and smart player-priority suppression, so dropped items and multiplayer lighting won't cause block-update lag or stutter, even on large servers or Realms.

- 100% Vanilla Safe Menu: the settings menu is accessed with a regular vanilla stick named fluxmenu (or via /scriptevent flux:get_config), no risky custom items that break on game updates.

- Admin Lock Settings: server owners can lock the config menu to operators only, keeping settings consistent across Realms and SMP servers.

Features Overview

- Dynamic Hand Lighting: walk around dark caves with light seamlessly following you.

- Full Off-hand Support: hold a pickaxe or sword in your main hand while lighting up caves from your off-hand.

- Quick Off-hand Switch: right-click/hold a light source into the air to instantly swap it into your off-hand.

- Dropped Items Lighting: toss torches or glowstone down ravines or deep pits to inspect the bottom before jumping down.

- Smart Light Prioritization: automatically prioritizes the brightest source between mainhand and offhand, preventing flickering between nearby players and dropped items.

- Interactive 2-Level Settings UI: complete control over light settings, active dimensions, dropped item limits, and auto-offhand mechanics.
