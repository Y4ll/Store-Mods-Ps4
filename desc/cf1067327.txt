Do you want to get the dropped item instantly whenever you break a block or kill a mob? Well this addon is suited for you, this addon functions just like that.

The addon uses the non-experimental version of the gametest scripts meaning it does not break when minecraft updates. However I would still be updating it for further improvements.

How To Use:

- Import the addon in the behavior pack and then open your world.

- Break some blocks or kill mobs you will notice their loot will go to you.

This addon will not work for the Wither and The Ender Dragon.
