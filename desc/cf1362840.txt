Blue Netherite

Recolors Ancient Debris with a frosty light-blue hue, giving Netherite a cool, refined look unlike anything seen in the Nether before.

Features

- Recolors Ancient Debris to a glowing light-blue variant

- Keeps original block detail and texture style

- Fully compatible with Minecraft Bedrock v1.21x

- Wont interfere with item or entity textures.

Preview

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file.

- Open the file with Minecraft to import automatically.

- Go to Settings Resource Packs My Packs.

- Activate Blue Netherite and enjoy!
