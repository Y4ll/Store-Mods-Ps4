Beyond the Realms

Step beyond the Overworld and discover an entirely new way to explore Minecraft.

Beyond the Realms adds 55 unique dimensions and 200+ handcrafted structures, giving you countless new places to explore, loot, and conquer. Every dimension offers its own atmosphere, terrain, resources, and secrets waiting to be uncovered.

Create Your Own Dimensions

Traveling to a new world is simple:

-

Craft a Riftlighter.

-

Build a portal frame using any Minecraft block.

-

Light the frame with the Riftlighter (not Flint and Steel).

-

Enter a brand-new dimension themed around the block you used to build the portal.

Every block opens the door to a different adventure, encouraging experimentation and exploration.

Features

-

55 unique dimensions

-

200+ unique structures

-

Create portals from any block

-

Use the Riftlighter to activate portals

-

Discover new loot, secrets, and hidden locations

-

Endless exploration with countless worlds to uncover

Whether you're looking for new places to explore, mysterious ruins to uncover, or a completely fresh Minecraft adventure, Beyond the Realms offers a massive expansion filled with discoveries around every corner.
