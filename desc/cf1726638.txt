R&M (Beta) is a Minecraft Bedrock addon inspired by chaotic sci-fi adventures, portal technology, cloning systems, futuristic weapons, strange devices, and experimental machinery.

This project is currently in an early Beta stage. At the moment, the playable content included in this release is focused on the Phoenix Capsule and its related cloning/survival mechanics.

Current Beta Features

Phoenix Capsule

Cloning-related mechanics

Survival backup mechanics

Capsule interactions and systems

Planned Content

Future versions are planned to expand the addon with:

Portal technology

Futuristic weapons and gadgets

Omega-inspired experimental technology

Vehicles and advanced devices

Additional sci-fi tools and mechanics

Beta Status

This addon is currently in Beta. Features may be improved, adjusted, expanded, or added in future versions.

Compatibility

Designed for Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
