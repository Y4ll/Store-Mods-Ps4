Transform your Minecraft Bedrock/Pocket Edition gameplay with this powerful addon! Designed for adventurers, builders, and explorers, it brings features that make your world more immersive and visually stunning.

Key Features:

- Dynamic Lighting: Realistic illumination that lights up your surroundings as you move. Torches, glowstones, and other light sources now brighten nearby areas dynamically, making exploration safer and more immersive.

- Left-Hand Torch: Previously exclusive to Java Edition, this feature lets you hold your torch in your left hand, giving you better visibility while mining, building, or adventuring.
