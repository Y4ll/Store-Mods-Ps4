Bedrock Essentials+ adds popular addons like Tree Capitator & Vein Miner. This is an all in one pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition!

Tree Capitator (sneak to activate)

Vein Miner (sneak to activate)

Open Double Doors (interact with the bottom half)

Anvil Repair

Auto Tool Switch

Flashlight

Auto Replant

The coordinates where you died will be sent to you.

Silk Touch Breakable Mob Spawner ( Does not include the mob inside )

Auto Pickup, By default it is not activated.

to activate: /pickup enable

to deactivate: /pickup disable

Auto Tool Switch, By default it is not activated.

to activate: /ats enable

to deactivate: /ats disable
