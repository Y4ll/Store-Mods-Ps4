Giants of Cinema

Enter a world full of epic creatures and legendary beasts inspired by the movies! Face giant anacondas and now also the fearsome tripods. Explore and survive in this universe of titanic monsters and experience the excitement of the movies in your Minecraft world.

Good afternoon, night or day, I will give you a small explanation of what the addon is about. You see, since I was little I have liked movies about snakes, aliens, sharks, crocodiles, etc. and I was always looking for an addon that had all that but the years passed and I never found one, now that I learned how to make addons I decided that the addon that I was looking for so much I would create it myself and here it is, that's what the addon is about, bringing creatures from movies to the world of Minecraft that's why it's called giants of cinema, so I hope you have a good time. :)
