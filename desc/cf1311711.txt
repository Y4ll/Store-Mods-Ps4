**Hexbyte PvB Intelligence Add-on**

by Hexbyte

This is not your ordinary Minecraft Bedrock add-on. The Hexbyte PvP Intelligence Add-on brings a powerful, custom-coded enemy NPC that behaves almost exactly like a real player. This mob doesnt just walk around it **fights, dodges, builds, aims, switches weapons**, and even **talks trash in chat** like a PvP veteran.

Built for both **PvP training** and **survival challenge maps**, this AI enemy is equipped with advanced logic to respond dynamically to any situation. Whether youre battling it in the open field, underground, or underwater expect it to **chase you**, **use blocks**, **attack with perfect timing**, and even **climb up to reach you**.

---

**Key Features**

*Advanced PvP AI:* It behaves like a skilled player dodging, blocking, and attacking.

*Block Placer:* Can place dirt when Stuck

*Dynamic Weapon Switching:* Switches between a diamond sword, bow, and pickaxe based on the situation (melee, ranged, or block-breaking).

*4444-Block Detection:* Detects and follows players from far away.

*Realistic Movement:* Walks, swims, climbs, and jumps automatically.

*Chat Dialogues:* Sends randomized chat lines when attacking just like toxic PvP opponents.

*Combat Intelligence:* It attacks players only, prioritizes its last attacker, and adapts when stuck.

*Retreat Mode:* When HP is low, it flees and buffs itself with strength before re-engaging.

*Addon Format:* Uses no command blocks 100% behavior pack and resource pack add-on.
