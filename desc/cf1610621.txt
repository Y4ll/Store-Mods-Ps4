Better Flashlight Addon V2

The standalone horror/survival flashlight is back now with a headlamp, upgrade modules, a golden infinite battery, built-in Vibrant Visuals support, and still no experimental world toggles. A flashlight that actually feels like one: beam, battery drain, flicker, and sound. Works on its own on classic graphics; optional warmer cinematic beam when you turn on Vibrant Visuals in video settings.

Whats new in V2

- Headlamp hands-free light that follows where you look; Empty / OFF / ON / DEAD states, same battery loop as the flashlight

- Headlamp Remote keep it in inventory; right-click to toggle the worn headlamp; the bar is live battery while its lit

- Headlamp runtime about 5 minutes per charge (flashlight is still about 3)

- Upgrade modules Extended Cell (~1.5 runtime), Stabilizer (less flicker / hard breaks), Lens Normal+ / Wide / Narrow (Normal+ is the only lens that fits a headlamp)

- Golden Flashlight Battery doesnt run out; install like a normal cell; lore says Infinite

- New look new textures and 3D models for headlamp, remote, modules, and golden battery; corner marks on the icon show whats installed (green cell, red stabilizer, blue lens)

- Partial recharge any charged battery (not the golden one) + redstone at a table tops it back up

- Creative inventory flashlights, headlamps, batteries, and modules sit in their own groups

What you get

A full flashlight lifecycle

This isnt a single on/off item. You craft a shell, install batteries, run the light until it dies, eject the dead cell, recharge it, and do it again. The headlamp uses the same loop.

- Flashlight (Empty) shell with no battery; install a charged battery to use it

- Flashlight OFF / ON right-click to toggle; the beam follows where you look

- Flashlight DEAD battery died inside; right-click to eject it (sound short delay dead battery out empty shell again)

- Headlamp (Empty / OFF / ON / DEAD) wear it; lights when on your head; take it off and it goes out

Batteries

- Flashlight Battery charge on the durability bar; stacks to 16

- Dead Flashlight Battery what you get when a flashlight or headlamp dies; empty bar, same 3D model in hand

- Golden Flashlight Battery infinite charge; install like a normal cell

- Recharge: Dead Battery + Redstone at a crafting table full battery

- Partial recharge: charged battery (not golden) + redstone at a table tops it back up

- Hot-swap: Use a charged battery on a powered flashlight or headlamp; only 1 is taken from a stack; your old battery goes back to inventory, or drops at your feet if your inventory is full

Upgrade modules

Craft them, then combine with an empty flashlight or headlamp at a crafting table. One upgrade per type: power, circuit, or lens. Charge and whats already installed stay on the tool.

- Extended Cell Module longer runtime (about 1.5)

- Stabilizer Module much less flicker and far less chance of a hard break

- Lens (Normal+) a bit brighter; the only lens that fits a headlamp

- Lens (Wide) wider cone, flashlight only

- Lens (Narrow) longer, tighter beam, flashlight only

Already have a lens on an empty shell? Hold a different lens and use it they swap, and you get the old one back.

Crafting

- Empty flashlight: glass pane, iron, copper, redstone

- Battery: redstone, iron nuggets, coal

- Recharge: dead battery + redstone

- Quick revive: dead flashlight + battery at a table flashlight OFF (shortcut if you dont want to eject first)

- Upgrade modules: craft, then combine with an empty flashlight or headlamp at a table

Gameplay feel

Dynamic light beam
Light along your view direction up to ~18 blocks, fading with distance. Stays on in chat, inventory, or while standing still (no more beam dying when you pause to read).

Per-tool charge
Flashlights and headlamps keep separate battery levels swapping items doesnt sync them.
