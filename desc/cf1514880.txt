Project Description:

Actual Glowing Ores transforms every mining expedition into an exhilarating treasure hunt. By making ores brilliantly luminescent, this enhancement ensures you can spot even the rarest minerals deep within the darkest, most treacherous caves, so you never miss a valuable vein again.

Important Information:

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Vibrant Visuals will need to be activated for some features.

- Full glowing ore experience.

- Multiple subpacks to choose from.

- Have something to say? Join the Discord.

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

This distinctive style of glowing ore will be applied as your default variant, automatically giving your world a vibrant and cohesive look right out of the box. You can easily keep this as your go-to aesthetic or switch to a different style whenever you want to mix things up.

Variant Two outlines your ores with a vibrant, glowing border while leaving the inner texture untouched, creating a sleek and subtle look that makes valuable minerals stand out without overwhelming your screen.

Variant Three delivers the ultimate combination of both styles. Featuring a brilliant glowing border paired with a luminously detailed ore inside, this option ensures maximum visibility and a striking aesthetic for all your valuable minerals.

Variant four delivers the signature illumination you have come to rely on, now enhanced with a high-visibility white border for effortless navigation through dark caves.

Once you have successfully activated the pack in your world, simply open the settings menu to customize your experience and choose your preferred visual style.

Use the slider tool to effortlessly browse through the options and select the exact ore style that best fits your aesthetic preferences.
