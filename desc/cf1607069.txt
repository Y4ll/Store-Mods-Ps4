Unlimited Stacks by Baku

Unlimited Stacks is a Minecraft Bedrock addon designed to make inventory management easier by allowing you to carry far more items without constantly filling your inventory.

This addon uses a smart virtual stacking system that automatically stores extra items and refills your visible stack whenever needed.

Special Stack Support

- Totem of Undying Carry multiple Totems with automatic refill support.

- Water Bucket Extra Water Buckets are stored and automatically refilled after use.

- Lava Bucket Carry multiple Lava Buckets without using many inventory slots.

- Shield Multiple normal Shields can be stored using the virtual stacking system.

Main Features

- Smart Unlimited Stack System

How It Works

Minecraft normally limits how many items can exist inside a single visible stack. Unlimited Stacks works around this limitation by keeping the normal visible item while storing additional items inside a virtual reserve.

When the visible item is used, consumed or removed, the addon automatically refills it using items from the stored reserve.

Totem & Shield Support

Totems and Shields also work with the offhand slot. When a Totem activates or a supported Shield is removed or broken, another available item can automatically refill the offhand.

Special Shields with custom names, lore or enchantments may remain separate to help protect their custom data.
