Kaleidoscope Nether (Unoffical)

Requires Minecraft Version 26.3+

Port Permission
This add-on is an unofficial Bedrock port of Kaleidoscope Nether, originally developed for Minecraft Java Edition by BmtUltra.

The original Kaleidoscope Nether project is distributed under the MIT License, which permits modification and redistribution under its terms. Special thanks to the Kaleidoscope Nether team and the Kaleidoscope Cookery team for creating the original mods!

Kaleidoscope Nether expands Kaleidoscope Cookery with a large collection of Nether-themed foods, ingredients, crops, mob drops, cooking recipes, tools, furniture, and special items.

Explore the Nether for new ingredients from Piglins, Hoglins, Ghasts, Striders, Blazes, Wither Skeletons, Magma Cubes, and more. Grow new Nether crops such as Crimson Fruit, Warped Fruit, Poisonous Fruit, and Soul Peppers, then use Cookery equipment to turn them into a wide variety of dishes.

The add-on also includes unusual mechanics such as throwable Blaze Hearts, the Blowgun, Primitive Machete, Ghast Pendant, Star Dust, teleporting foods, placeable dishes and drinks, and Nether-specific cooking equipment.

For recipes, farming information, mob drops, special item behavior, preparation instructions, and explanations of the add-ons features, check the Nether section of the in-game Kaleidoscope Cookery Guidebook.

REQUIRED DEPENDENCY and PACK ORDER

You need Kaleidoscope Cookery for this add-on to work, v1.0.7 or Newer is required!! Download it HERE.

Please put Kaleidoscope Nether ABOVE Kaleidoscope Cookery for best results!
