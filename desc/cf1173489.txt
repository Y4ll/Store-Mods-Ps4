Dorios' Trinkets is a dynamic Minecraft addon introducing 80 unique trinkets that enhance your gameplay with exciting abilities and effects. With 13 customizable slotsincluding Head, Necklace, Body, Feet, Rings, Charms, and moreyou can tailor your character to suit your playstyle. Equip trinkets through a dedicated inventory system or directly from your hand for seamless gameplay.

Trinkets provide responsive stat boosts like increased health, damage, speed, water mobility, and magic protection, as well as unique abilities such as life steal, immunity to effects, and item repair. The addon is fully compatible with Archaism Talismans, expanding your options for ultimate customization.

Discover trinkets in various Minecraft structures, from Ancient Cities to Desert Pyramids, or craft them to unlock their full potential. Whether youre building a tanky warrior, a swift explorer, or a versatile adventurer, Dorios' Trinkets adds depth, variety, and excitement to your world.

How to equip my trinkets?

Equip your trinkets easily by using a dynamic system. Right-clicking the item or opening the inventory gives you instant access to a flexible and responsive system to manage your trinkets across. To open the inventory you need to interact while having the Scroll. Which can be crafted using paper.

With the right combination of trinkets, you'll benefit from both stat boosts and special effects. For example:

- Increase health, damage, speed, and water speed.

- Magic protection to safeguard against harmful spells.

- Immunity to negative status effects.

- Life steal to replenish your health during battle.

- Repair items to keep your gear in top shape.

- Double Jump to improve your agility.

Responsive Effects: If you equip multiple trinkets that modify the same stat (like health or speed), the effects will stack dynamically. For instance, a trinket might give +10 health, while another reduces -2 health. The result is a final health bonus of +8.

Trinket Tiers:

- Primitive: Basic trinkets with simple effects, ideal for early-game.

- Apprentice: Stronger bonuses and more complex abilities for mid-game players.

- Mastercraft: Advanced trinkets with powerful abilities for experienced players.

- Ascendant: The highest tier, offering game-changing boosts and effects. Ascendant+ means its even more powerful than Ascendant, representing the best of the best.
