This shader, created by SETBr0ken (SetDelta), is for Minecraft Bedrock Edition and uses BetterRedDragon or MB Loader.

This shader adds warm, saturated lighting during the day, a dark, bluish night at night, and in the End, a reflection on the ground and a black hole that contrasts well with the environment.

This is version V3 of these shaders.
