Introduction

The Slasher is a chainsaw-sword (chainsword) with spectacular combat.

Also:

- Very compatible with other addons that modify player.json, including gun addons!

- Doesn't need any experiment, so it should remain stable for a long time!

However, please accept the absense of your custom skin in first-person view. I had to sacrifice it to prioritize compability with other addons.

Abilities

Fast Attack

How: Start using the item (RMB), and stop before the charging completes. Just spam RMB.

Fires a beam made up of 3 parts, each dealing 1 armor-penetrating damage, for a maximum of 3 damage in total.

Charge Attack

How: Keep using the item (hold RMB) until the charging completes, then release it!

Damage:

- Direct hit: 14

- Beam: 8

- Combined: 24 (instantly kills most humanoid mobs)

You can dash by releasing charge while holding W (moving forward)!

Chainsaw Attack

How: Release the charge while sneaking.

It continously deals 1 armor-penetrating damage in a very fast cycle, until the user stops sneaking, or the target is dead.

Plunge Attack

How: Release the charge while mid-air, holding jump button and looking down.

The impact damage is calculated based on how high you fell from. Not so strong if you don't fall from high.
