Animated Menu Pack Description:

Transform your Minecraft experience from the moment you launch the game by replacing your static main menu with this high-energy Gojo Jujutsu Kaisen animated sequence.

Important Information:

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Real time animated menu.

- Having something to say? Join the Discord.

Installation

Once you have installed the pack, head to your settings menu and choose Global Resources.

From the "MY PACKS" menu choose this Texture Pack to activate.

Thats it, once the game has finished loading the Global Resourses you will have an Animated Menu Screen.
