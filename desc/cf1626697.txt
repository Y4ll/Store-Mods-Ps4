What's in my Shulker?

Preview the contents of your Shulker Boxes directly from your inventory.

This addon adds a tooltip displaying the items stored inside each Shulker Box.

How to use?

It works out of the box!

No setup or toggles required. However, Both the resource and behavior pack has to be applied to a given world for the addon to work.

The tooltip of the items appears as soon as you hover over the shulker!

Coloring Shulkers

To color shulkers interact with them while holding a dye!

Features!

Custom name!

Multiple preview colors!

Colors can be changed by renaming the shulker in the anvil.
