Adha Craft is a Minecraft Bedrock addon designed for players who want a butcher-style gameplay experience. It introduces realistic butchery features that make processing mobs more immersive and engaging. Equip your butcher knife, manage your own farm, and bring a new layer of survival gameplay into your world.
