Music Player

Music Player UI Lite adds an in-game music player to the emote screen. This menu allows you to play any song in Minecraft with the press of a button. It works in Survival and Creative Mode on any world, even without cheats enabled.

The music player works best after muting the default game music by setting the Music slider to 0. Using the music player while the default game music is turned up could cause multiple songs to play at once.

Selecting multiple songs from the music player will cause them to play at the same time. If you select an album, the game will play one random song from that album. You can stop any song by selecting the stop button next to the individual song entry.

Music player volume is controlled by the Musical blocks audio slider.

Custom Music

Music Player UI Lite also has support for custom music added by the player with the Custom Music Container pack. Custom songs can be played from the music player. Follow the tutorial below to learn how to add custom music to Music Player UI Lite.

Make sure to apply Custom Music Container above Music Player UI Lite.

If you're downloading an update to Music Player UI Lite, do not redownload Custom Music Container. Custom Music Container will never need an update, which keeps your custom songs safe.
