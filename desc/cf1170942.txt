Odyssey is a Vibrant Visuals / deferred pack designed to bring new life into the classic Minecraft visual experience. We've meticulously enhanced the vanilla aesthetic with vibrant colours, stunning lighting effects, and a host of visual improvements while maintaining the core Minecraft look and feel while making it feeling cozy and also realistic.

Experiance Minecraft with features like: volumetric fog specific to each biome, realistic lighting, bright and vibrant sky, PBR texture and more.
