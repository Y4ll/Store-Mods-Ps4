Do you want to see items in your inventory without having to open inventory screen? Well, this texture pack is for you!

This texture pack displays inventory slots for viewing items on the Hud screen. You can also see highlights of items in the inventory that you are currently holding.

Subpacks:

This texture pack contains subpacks to move the position of the Inventory Hud: Top Right, Top Left, Left Middle, Right Middle, Bottom Left, Bottom Middle, and Bottom Right (default)
