THIS ADDON IS ACHIEVEMENT FRIENDLY

Items Ragdoll replaces dropped items with versions that feature physics-based movement and rotation. These items can fall, roll, collide, be pushed by entities and pistons, float in liquids, and react to lava just like vanilla items.

Features
- Movement and collisions.
- Smooth, dynamic rotation when items are thrown.
- Support for both items and blocks.
- Items can be pushed by players, entities, and pistons.
- Compatibility with water, lava, and hoppers.
- Fire-resistant items survive in lava.
- Item pickup, stacking, and automatic despawning.
- Players in spectator mode cannot pick up items.
- Simple configuration to adjust physics behavior.

This addon is designed to enhance the visual appeal of items without altering their in-game utility.
