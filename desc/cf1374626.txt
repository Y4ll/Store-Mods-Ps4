TerraDraconis

The Heart of the Dragon World

TerraDraconis is the core addon of the TerraDraconis Series the foundation that ties together every other addon in the collection. It introduces the dragons, creatures, systems, and mechanics that bring this dark yet living world to life.

The vision of TerraDraconis is to create an immersive ecosystem not just filled with dragons, but with a whole web of species that evolved alongside them, including humans. Dragons here are more than enemies or pets: they are part of the worlds history and survival, shaping every culture, hunt, and battle.

Current Systems

Dragons & Creatures

- Multiple species of dragons, each with unique biology, behaviors, and roles in the ecosystem

- Dragon Level System similar to Hostile Scaling, but with extra attributes such as mutations

- Savage Variants: stronger miniboss versions of dragons (not naturally spawning yet) for added challenge

- Non-dragon creatures that add depth and realism to the ecosystem

Better World Generation

- Vast landscapes with larger terrain, massive trees, stone patches, and unique features

- A land slab system to smooth terrain and make the world feel more natural and realistic

- World design that feels ancient and full of history

Immersive Taming System

- Inspired by ARK: uses tranq and torpor mechanics for a more rewarding taming experience

- Taming feels like a true hunt instead of a simple interaction

Dragon Riding System

- Dragons have stamina and a forward-gliding flight system

- More realistic controls: dragons cant fly backward or strafe sideways in midair

- Planned improvements for long-range flight mechanics

Crafting & Progression

- Recipe Unlocking System: crafting recipes require specific player level + skill points to unlock

- Separate Crafting System: custom crafting grids allow for more complex and expensive recipes

- Includes a config file for adding custom recipes (compatible with other addons)

Weapon & Item Quality

- Weapons and tools can be upgraded at Reforge Stations to improve damage and durability

- Fully configurable other addons can register their own items into the reforge system

Planned Updates

Since TerraDraconis is still in its beta stage, many more systems are on the way:

- More dragon and creature species (currently only 2 creatures available)

- Settlement System with NPCs

- Bounty Hunting System

- Additional weapons and armor

- Enhanced dragon combat mechanics

TerraDraconis isnt just an addon its the living foundation of a dragon-filled world. As the series grows, so will this base, evolving into a complete survival ecosystem where every dragon, creature, and hunter has a role to play.
