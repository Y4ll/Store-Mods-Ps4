Easter Eggs Rename A&S

A resource pack that replaces the useless "How to Play" button in the pause menu with a complete guide to named easter eggs in the Actions & Stuff texture pack.

No more guessing what name to give a mob or which item to put on an armor stand! Just open the pause menu and get all the information you need: names, items, versions, and facts.

> Important: This pack does not add any new animations or easter eggs. It only displays what is already included in Actions & Stuff.

---

Features

- Full list of named easter eggs special mobs: snowy, cherry, sulfur, autumn, and baby variants.
- Named decorations custom decors, toys, gifts, stockings, rugs, flags, and blocks/cubes.
- Secret blocks, transformations, hats, clothing, and poses.
- One-click name copying just click on a name and paste it right into the anvil!
- Flexible customization hide unwanted tabs or entire categories to clean up your UI.
