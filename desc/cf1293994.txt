Minecraft But Structure Loots Are OP

Created by: GamerFile

In this addon, all structure chest loot has been modified to drop overpowered items!
With just a few loot chests from villages, desert temples, igloos, fortresses, bastions, jungle temples, strongholds, end citieseven ancient citiesyou can collect enough gear to defeat the Ender Dragon or complete any survival goal with ease.

Originally made for Java, this version is now available for MCBE. So go aheaddownload it and support me!
