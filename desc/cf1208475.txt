About JustHammers Add-on

Add hammers to the game that are able to mine in a 3x3, 3x3x3, 5x5x3 and finally a 5x5x5! They're pretty awesome.

This Add-on is an Unofficial port for Minecraft Bedrock! Do not bother the original creator with questions or bugs related to this Add-on.

Main Features

- Mine and destroy blocks.

- Finding ores is easier.

- Break all the blocks in your way.

- Herobrine will be scared when he sees how many blocks you can destroy.

- Iron, Gold, Diamond, Netherite Hammers.

- Has tiers of all of the above that increase the area of affect. Starting at 3x3x1 all the way up to 5x5x5. Durability scales with the tiers.

- Compatible with any Add-on.

Installation Notes

To enjoy this add-on comfortably, make sure you have correctly downloaded the following files in their corresponding versions:

- JustHammers [Bp].mcpack

- JustHammers [Rp].mcpack

Both files are required for the add-on to function correctly. (One handles all behaviors and scripts, while the other provides the textures, so be sure to download both)

Experimental settings

This add-on does not require any experimental settings to be enabled. You can enjoy all of its content without disabling achievements in your world.
