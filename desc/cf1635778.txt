Night Vision Universal

Explore dark caves and survive nights clearly without torches or potions using this client-side FullBright behavior and resource pack setup.

- No Effects/Commands Needed: Works without potion effects or commands.

- Universal Compatibility: Works in personal worlds, Realms, and most multiplayer servers on Windows, Android, and iOS.

- Enhanced Visuals: Improves cave, nighttime, and underwater visibility while reducing rain and distance fog without breaking block colors or achievements.
