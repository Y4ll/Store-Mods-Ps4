OP Librarian

Upgrade your Minecraft Bedrock survival experience with OP Librarian by FleetMc Studio. This addon completely overhauls the Librarian Villager, giving you access to powerful enchanted books, max-enchanted gear, rare items, and useful survival resources for just 1 Emerald.

Forget spending hours rerolling Librarian trades or grinding for enchantments. Simply find a Librarian, trade a few Emeralds, and instantly obtain some of the best equipment available in Minecraft.

Easy Emerald Farming

Getting Emeralds is easier than ever! Trade just 1 Paper to receive 1 Emerald, allowing you to quickly unlock the Librarian's powerful custom trades.
