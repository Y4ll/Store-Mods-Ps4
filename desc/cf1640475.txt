Pteranodon

Pteranodon was a genus of large pterosaurs that lived during the Late Cretaceous period, approximately 8684 million years ago. Despite often being called a "flying dinosaur," Pteranodon was actually a pterosaura group of flying reptiles that lived alongside dinosaurs but belonged to a separate evolutionary lineage.

Recognizable by its long, toothless beak and distinctive cranial crest, Pteranodon had a wingspan that could exceed 6 meters (20 feet), making it one of the largest flying reptiles of its time. Fossil evidence suggests it primarily fed on fish and spent much of its life soaring over ancient seas.

This addon brings the iconic Pteranodon into Minecraft Bedrock, recreating this remarkable prehistoric animal while preserving its recognizable appearance and aerial behavior.
