Visual Effects Log:

Atmospheric and Natural Effects:

- Deciduous Leaves: Trees drop withered leaves, varying by tree type, featuring realistic physics for block collisions, floatage, and combustion. Some trees also tend to drop small branches.

- Light snowfall: This type of snowfall occurs in snowy forest biomes, and freezing winds are generated in the presence of ice or snow. It can be replicated by covering tree leaves with layers of snow.

- Sand Clouds: These dust clusters form in deserts and badlands in areas with unstable sand; tumbleweeds can also be seen there. They cannot be replicated.

- Mushroom Spores: The small and giant mushrooms on the Mushroom Islands release spores into the air if they are situated on mycelium.

- Pale Garden: This biome features a dense mist that drifts through it, as well as glowing spores constantly released by Eyeblossoms at night. The mist can be replicated by placing Pale Moss beneath Pale Oaks.

- Caverns: Underground biomes feature their own unique atmospheric effects.

In Lush Caves, a humid mist forms due to the interaction between Spore Blossoms and moss.

In Dripstone Caves, a humid mist also occurs, caused by long stalactites dripping onto rocky ground.

In Sulfur Caves, a dense mist forms around pools of Potent Sulfur that have sulfur deposits beneath them; the toxic gases cause nearby lichens to release their glowing spores into the air.

In the Deep Dark, a very dense haze appears when Sculk Sensors detect movement, which in turn generates small, glowing, descending particles.

- Nether Mist: In areas of Netherrack where fire is present, this dense, reddish mist will appear.

- Magma Drip: Magma blocks with fire on top will drip lava from their sides.

- Resin Drip: The Creaking Heart and the Pale Oak containing it will ooze resin from their sides when the Creaking is struck.

- Leaf Drip: Tree leaves exposed to rain will drip water from below.
