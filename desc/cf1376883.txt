Feather FPS Boost Mod

Feather FPS Boost Mod is a lightweight, general-purpose performance optimization addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition, designed to improve frame rate stability and overall smoothness while keeping the vanilla look intact.

Works on existing worlds | No experimental features required | Achievement friendly | Bedrock 1.21.0+

Performance Improvements

Feather focuses on client-side rendering overhead and spawn density, the largest sources of lag on Bedrock devices.

Fog Modes

Pick one from the subpack list when you activate the pack, and switch at any time:

- Optimized short-range fog, best FPS. The original Feather look

- Balanced mid-range fog, softer horizon. More visibility, still culls geometry

- Clean no fog at all, with clear water in every biome. For strong devices or anyone who dislikes fog

Low-End Friendly Design

Built primarily for low-end and mid-range devices phones, tablets, and older PCs that struggle with vanilla Bedrock's visual load. Stuttering during mob spawns, lag in built-up areas, and thermal throttling during long sessions are the exact problems this pack targets.

- Reduced GPU load per frame

- Smoother frame pacing and less stutter

- Better performance in dense mob environments

- Reduced overheating during long play sessions

- Improved consistency in both singleplayer and multiplayer

What Is And Isn't Changed

Not touched: crafting, physics, progression, world generation, loot, mob AI, pathfinding, attacking, or any core game logic. Colormaps are desaturated for a more natural tone but do not affect block data or biome logic. Achievements stay enabled.

Changed, and worth knowing: the behaviour pack trims herd sizes, spawn weights and density limits on 35 mobs. Passive animals spawn in smaller groups than vanilla a cow herd is 2 instead of 4. This is where a large share of the FPS gain comes from, but it does mean mob density is visibly lower. If you want untouched spawning, run the resource pack on its own; it works standalone.

Installation

- Download the .mcaddon (both packs in one file), or the BP and RP .mcpack files separately

- Tap or double-click the file Minecraft imports it automatically

- Go to Settings Global Resources, or open a world Behaviour / Resource Packs

- Activate Feather FPS Boost v11 [BP] and [RP]

- Pick a fog mode from the subpack list

- Launch your world - no further configuration required
