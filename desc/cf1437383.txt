MobileFPS+ is a performance-focused Minecraft Bedrock resource pack made especially for mobile players and low-end devices. This pack improves FPS and reduces lag while keeping the game visually clean and close to vanilla.

Key Features

Add new stlyel ore

Real life custom sun

Real life custom moon

Quieter mobs, blocks, and ambient sounds for smoother gameplay

Removed cave sounds, Wither spawn sound, and Ender Dragon death sound

Clean and simple visuals (wool, armor, elytra, glass doors & trapdoors)

Full-sided terrain blocks (grass, snow, podzol, mycelium, nylium, paths) !

Useful visual utilities:

Ore borders

Budding amethyst borders

Visual waxed copper items

Optimized For

Mobile / Android devices

Low-end phones & tablets

Long survival and building sessions
