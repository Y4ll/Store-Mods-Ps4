Overview

Tired of oversaturated, vibrant visuals and dangerous third-party apps? This resource pack transforms your world into a cold, moody, atmospheric experience using purely vanilla JSON fog and colormap tweaks.

Engineered specifically for low-end mobile devices, this pack gives you a high-end shader feel without melting your phone.

Features

True Atmospheric Fog: Every single biome has custom-coded, seamless fog (including all 1.18 mountain/cave updates and the Nether/End).

Glassy Water: Custom 50% transparency on all water blocks to remove the chunky vanilla textures, leaving a smooth, glass-like surface.

Frosty Colormaps: Vanilla grass and foliage have been color-corrected to give the world a crisp, blueish-green, freezing morning vibe.

Compatibility

Version: Original Minecraft Bedrock 1.26+ (Render Dragon Supported).

Requirements: NO patched Minecraft needed. NO MB Loader needed. NO third-party clients required.

Performance: Extremely lightweight. Runs smoothly on 1GB/2GB RAM low-end phones.

Disclaimer

This is a standard vanilla resource pack that safely modifies fog, colormaps, and UI to create a shader-like atmosphere. It does not alter your game engine, contain mods, or bypass Render Dragon. Please ensure you are running Minecraft Bedrock version 1.26 or higher.
