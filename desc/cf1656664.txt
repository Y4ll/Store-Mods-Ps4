True Sky Shader

True Sky is a lightweight Minecraft Bedrock visual pack designed to make the sky, sun, moon, and atmosphere look more realistic and cinematic.

Features

Realistic Sun & Sunlight

Enhanced Moon & Night Sky

Beautiful Sunrise & Sunset Colors

Improved Sky Atmosphere

Atmospheric Fog

Cinematic Sun Rays

Better Horizon Lighting

Designed for Minecraft Bedrock Edition

Lightweight and performance-friendly

Realistic Visual Experience

True Sky adds a more natural atmosphere to Minecraft Bedrock without making the visuals overly complicated. Enjoy beautiful sunsets, realistic daylight, atmospheric nights, and enhanced sky colors while exploring your world.

Performance

The pack is designed to remain lightweight while improving the overall visual experience. Performance may vary depending on your device, Minecraft version, render distance, and other active resource packs.

Installation

-

Download the True Sky .mcpack file.

-

Open the file with Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

-

Minecraft will automatically import the pack.

-

Go to Settings Global Resources.

-

Activate True Sky.

-

Enter your world and enjoy the new sky!

Compatibility

Made for Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
For the best experience, use a supported/compatible Minecraft version and avoid using multiple packs that modify the same sky or lighting files.
