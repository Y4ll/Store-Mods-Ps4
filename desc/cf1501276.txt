How to use

Download the .mcpack file

Tap or double-click it and Minecraft will open and import it automatically

Wait for the "Successfully imported" message to appear

Open Minecraft and go to Play

Create a new world or edit an existing one

Scroll down to Behavior Packs

Find Choas+ and tap the + to activate it

Launch your world!

Every 10 seconds an event will happen
