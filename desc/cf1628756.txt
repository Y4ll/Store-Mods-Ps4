Nightmare Overload is a horror survival modpack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition that transforms the peaceful Overworld into a terrifying world filled with monsters, abandoned places, thick fog, and horrifying encounters. Every journey becomes unpredictable as danger lurks in forests, caves, oceans, and the darkness beyond your torchlight.

This modpack is designed to create constant tension without changing the core Minecraft survival experience. Explore ruined structures, search for valuable loot, survive terrifying nights, and face creatures that stalk, chase, and hunt you when you least expect it.

Features

Dense atmospheric fog for reduced visibility

Terrifying creatures with unique behaviours

Horror ambient sounds that make every cave and forest unsettling

Abandoned structures filled with mystery and loot

Expanded ocean horror, including the terrifying Bloop

Improved immersion while keeping vanilla survival gameplay intact
