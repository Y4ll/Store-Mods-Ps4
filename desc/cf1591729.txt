Lilium Backpacks - Bedrock Edition (V1.3.0)

Lilium Backpacks is a feature-rich, standalone addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition that introduces fully functional, wearable, dyeable and placeable backpacks with expandable storage tiers from 9 to 54 slots, in 102 variants.

Features

- 102 Unique Backpack Variants: The default leather backpack and 5 mineral upgrade tiers (Copper, Iron, Gold, Diamond, Netherite), each available undyed or in any of 16 vibrant colours, every pairing with its own hand-drawn item icon and worn model texture.

- Expandable Storage Tiers: Progress from a basic 9-slot backpack up to a massive 54-slot Netherite storage chest using upgrade kits in Survival.

- Wearable & Held 3D Renderings: Custom 3D geometric models (geometry.backpack) with 1st-person, 3rd-person hand, and 3rd-person back alignment animations powered by Molang.

- Material-Themed Interface: Every tier opens with its own colour scheme, from leather brown through copper, iron, gold and diamond to near-black netherite, using custom nine-slice panel and slot textures.

- Item Search: A search field in every backpack's title row dims the items that do not match what you type, so a full Netherite backpack stays readable at a glance.

- In-Interface Upgrade & Dye Slots: Every backpack interface carries two dedicated slots on the left. Drop an upgrade kit or a vanilla dye into them and the change is applied the moment you close the backpack.

- 16-Colour Dyeing System: Colour any tier by dropping a vanilla dye into the backpack's dye slot. The dye is not consumed: it stays in the slot as the colour marker, so taking it back out returns the backpack to its undyed look at any time.

- Adaptive Chest Screen UI: Dynamically adapts the in-game chest interface layout (chest_screen.json) to display 9, 18, 27, 36, 45, or 54 slots depending on the opened backpack tier.

- Crash-Safe Structure Persistence: Uses native world.structureManager snapshotting to save full inventory contents and NBT data safely without losing items.

- Ownership Security Guards: Built-in security prevents unauthorized players from opening or picking up backpacks owned by other players.

- No Experiments Required: Works out of the box in standard Survival worlds, Multiplayer, and Realms without needing experimental toggles.
