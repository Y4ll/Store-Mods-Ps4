A historical war addon which takes place in late 15th Century Europe. Join nations, raise armies, fight wars...

This addon aims to provide users a glimpse of what armies would look like in the 1470s...

Grab your weapon, don your armour, and rally up your men to fight!

Although research was done, this addon is far from being 100% historicaly accurate due to considerations on gameplay and lack of knowledge to implement such features...

This addon may not be 100% survival friendly, as some recipes might be available in the base addon...

It is still highly recomended to play with "TheEpicKnightsMod" and "EpicKnightsPlus" installed in order for the addon to work 100% in survival, you can download them through MCPEDL or Curseforge...

FEATURES:

Currently has 4 playable nations, "Kingdom of England", "Kingdom of France", "Kingdom of Spain", & "Kingdom of Portugal", more are planned to come...

To join any nation, you must become a citezen. You can do this by putting the "Accept Oath" in the offhand... Though take note, you are 'joining' a nation, it does not grand you any special abilities, they will not fight for you, you will just be a soldier... Joining a nation will make you allied with the nation, but beware as now enemies of the nation you joined will now attack you...

You can leave the nation by holding the "Refuse Oath" in the offhand. This makes you neutral to all nations again, but beware as you will be marked a traitor to the nation you leave, proceed with caution...

Soliers are bought through the "Army Table", reffer to instructions below...

Adds 7 Types of Soldiers: Archer, Pikemen, Billmen, Footsoldier, Knight, Cavalry & Standard Bearer... Each having their own stats and abilities, they can also be recruited. Reffer to the info given below...

Soldiers can be led using their nation's flag...

You can spawn Citizens using the Settlement Table, reffer to info given below...
