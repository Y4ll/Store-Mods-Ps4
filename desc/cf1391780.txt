Project Description:

Travel through your world where, if you're lucky enough, you'll find an abandoned vehicle. Craft fuel and fill it up to make exploration much easier. Featuring a rusty, abandoned aesthetic perfect for horror maps, these vehicles deal damage to any entity they crash into.

Important Information:

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Vibrant Visuals will need to be activated for some features.

- Actual real-time speedometer.

- Dynamic Light features.

- Custom sound effect features.

- Realistic gasoline crafting method.

- Behaviour & Resource packs are both required for the project to work.

- Have something to say? Join the Discord.

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

Don't leave those rusty wrecks behind! With a full can of gasoline in your inventory, you can easily refuel and restore an abandoned vehicle to get back on the road.

Jump in your Abandoned Vehicle to get around your world faster. Your fuel will start dropping as you drive. You will also see a realtime speedometer, no two vehicles are the same they will all have a different max speeds so keep filling up Abandoned Vehicles until you find a really fast one.

As soon as you slide into the driver's seat of an abandoned vehicle, the automatic headlights activate, casting a bright beam across the dark terrain to dramatically improve your nighttime visibility.

If you are searching for limestone, scour the arid sands and unearth it naturally generating underground in desert biomes between Y-levels 0 and 40.

Smelt Limestone to create Crude Oil.

Smelt Crude Oil to create Distilled Crude Oil.

Smelt Distilled Crude Oil to create Unrefined Bitumen.

Smelt Unrefined Bitumen to create Bitumen.

Craft Empty Jerry Cans.

Use a Brewing Stand to turn Bitumen into Gasoline.

If your Vehicles takes to much damage you can repair it with a Spanner.
