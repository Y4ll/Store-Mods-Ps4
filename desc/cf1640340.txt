This visuals pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition gives the world a soft sakura-pink atmosphere with bright turquoise water and richer colors overall.

Vibrant Visuals must be turned on to get the most out of the pack!

Note: This pack is still in early development. If you notice any bugs, please feel free to mention them in the comments or on my discord server!
