Star's FPS Counter

Star's FPS Counter allows you to see live FPS in game and it is as accurate as it gets!

And the best part? It doesn't disable achievements!

The people who don't have access to a reliable FPS Counter on their platform, like the people on mobile and consoles, can use this in game FPS Counter to make do!

Installation guide :

- Download the file and import it into Minecraft.

- Go to "settings/video" and turn off the "view bobbing" toggle like shown below:

- Apply the pack to your world or global resources.

- Enjoy!

Customize Size:

- You can customize the size of FPS counter by going to the resourse pack's settings and adjusting the slider.
