CREATE YOUR ARMY

You can read the introduction or watch this video, in which I teach you how to build your base step by step

To fight the cursed souls of ancient battles, you must build a base to create troops and fight the enemy

To do this you must summon a Master Buldier to help you set up a base to start creating your troops

Once you have the summoning pearl, throw it to summon the master builder (Note: if you put one very close to another, it will disappear automatically, so you can only have one of each faction or without faction)

Now choose your faction, so you can start fighting, to do this you have to give a distinctive ribbon to your Master Builder, so that he can give you the initial buildings of your base

When building your base and initial buildings, you must wait four minutes for initial villagers to appear, try to take care of them to advance quickly

ITEMS (New additions)

Hammer Tool

With this hammer you are able to repair any structure that has been destroyed, basically becoming a builder

Toolkit

With this toolbox you are able to repair any structure that has been damaged

Crossbow Blueprints

The blueprints of the crossbow can be obtained with the engineer, once you use them a crossbow will appear that needs to be armed, finish it with a builder's hammer, once it is ready you can get on it and shoot powerful arrows equal to the crossbows with wheel

Dark Diamod

A very difficult diamond to forge, this can be obtained with the engineer, merchant H and O, it will be used to forge the pearls of the crown

Crown Pearl

This pearl is made of a very resistant material, it will serve for the creation of the throne, the crown of the king, shield of firmness, loyalty katanas, valor sword and trident of justice

Crown

Made of quite strange materials, since it carries with it a great power to be able to invoke the king of Steeland

Trone

The Throne is the final piece to summon the king of the Steeland, once you have the throne, bring the king's crown closer to him to be able to summon him

Clothes

You can get this item with the merchant or with the tailor, this item is used to turn vagabonds into villagers, there are different factions, so you can choose which faction your villager will belong to

Box of breads

This item can be found at the merchant, it is used to completely fill an empty barrel in the barn

Humman flesh

This item is obtained by eliminating a corpse of any villager, these pieces of meat are used to tame the cannibals and other tings...

Death Champion Heart

Being so withered, it still beats, this heart of a former champion is very difficult to obtain, it has the same probability of being obtained as a wither head, and you must give the death blow to the Dead Champion, so that he can give it to you. Give them to your advisor

Nether Rift Shard

This can be obtained by giving a final blow to a Nether Rift. Give them to your advisor

Jewel of Hope

Obtaining it will allow you to forge an item to summon a champion

Aprobal scroll

This scroll is used so that your master builder can start giving you plans to improve your houses

Invasion maps

These maps can be found in different bandit buildings. Gather them this way to give them to your advisor

Invasion stick

It is achieved by giving a final blow to an invasion flag

Beacon torch

Used to light battle battlements. It is made with charcoal and an invasion stick

Champions items

Use them to summon the powerful champions: William, Lenora, Yasuke, Gareth and Cassandra

Beacons

Use them to place your battle battlements, you can place a maximum of four in a village
