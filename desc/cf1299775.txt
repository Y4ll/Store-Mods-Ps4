The CUTEST Add-on in Minecraft is here!

This Add-on is an authorized Minecraft Bedrock port of Popular Biology / Chiikawa Java Mod created by Dwinovo.

This mod brings some of the internet's most beloved and trending characters into Minecraft tameable little friends, a job system that lets them work on their own, and a music box that plays your very own songs(Coming soon!). These pets, complete with exquisite models and unique sound effects, can fight alongside their owners, adding a new layer of excitement to your adventures.

Key Features

Taming and Interaction: Each creature in this mod can be tamed using specific items. For example, "Hachiware" can be tamed with a "Cornflower," while "Chikawa" requires a "White Tulip." Once tamed, these pets will follow and assist you in battles. Author Note: In the recent update, creatures can be tamed with ANY COOKED FOODS, such as Bread and Cooked beef.

Job System:

1) Farmer: Equipped with a hoe, with any vanilla seeds in backpack. Pets will help you sow seeds and harvest mature crops.

2) Swordman: Equipped with a sword. Pets will help you automatically attack hostile mobs such as monsters, except creepers.

3) Archer: Equipped with a bow. Pets will help you automatically shoot hostile mobs with bows and arrows, except creepers.

Detailed Models and Exclusive Sounds: Every creature in the mod is carefully modeled and equipped with distinct sound effects, creating an immersive and visually pleasing experience.

Combat Companions: Your tamed pets arent just companionsthey are also powerful allies in combat. Each creature has unique abilities. For instance, "Usagi" inflicts a fire effect on enemies, while "Hachiware" adds extra knockback power.

Exclusive Weapons: Each pet comes with its own special weapon, which can be equipped by Shift + Right-clicking on the pet. These weapons enhance their combat abilities, like the "Hachiwares Hunting Stick" increasing knockback or "Usagis HuntingStick" adding fire damage and explosion.

Once tamed, pets can be commanded to sit, stand, and they will assist you in battle, making them both loyal companions and fierce fighters.
