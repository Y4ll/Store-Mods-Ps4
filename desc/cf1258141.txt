Custom Heart Nametags for Players and Mobs [Addon Script]

This script modifies the nameTag of players and mobs in Minecraft Bedrock to visually display hearts representing health, using animated and colorful effects.

final Version Features:

Displays hearts () to represent the current health of the player or mob.

Dynamic color changes:

Damage: Hearts flash dark red.

Healing: Hearts flash green.

Effect detection:

Wither: Hearts turn gray.

Absorption: Golden hearts for extra health.

Automatic heart adjustment:

High-health mobs: Shows up to 10 hearts with a fraction (current / max HP).

Low-health mobs (10): Shows proportional hearts, no fraction.

Fully compatible with all mobs that have health (zombies, bosses, animals, etc.).

Keeps the original entity name and adds the heart bar below it.

Optimized for performance: Updates every few ticks to reduce lag.

Now compatible with Action & Stuff (addon/script) for enhanced entity interactions.

What Makes It Special?
Hearts flash consistently when entities take damage or heal, producing a clean and clear animation.
It's a pure scriptno resource packs or NBT edits required.
