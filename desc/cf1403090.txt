An HQ, highly detailed Peugeot 207 (206+ France car) mod for Minecraft, features:

- Animated Spinning Wheels when driving

- The driver sits in the correct position

- Highly Detailed 3D Model exterior and interior

- Steering Wheel Animation Synced with Players Head Rotation

If you are using Vibrant Visuals, the lighting on the vehicles may not update correctly at first.
To fix this issue, simply exit the world once and re-enter it. The lighting will work properly afterwards.
