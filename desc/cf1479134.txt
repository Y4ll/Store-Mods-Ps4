Jujutsu Mechanics Addon

Jujutsu Mechanics adds a lore-accurate Jujutsu Kaisen power system to Minecraft Bedrock, featuring cursed techniques and cursed energy abilities designed to coexist naturally with Minecraft gameplay.

For Guidance on how to use the Systems Join the Official Discord Server, Jujutsu Mechanic Server

Core Systems

Output & Efficiency

Six Eyes

A powerful ability that gives the user unmatched perception:

-

-

Detect enemy state (e.g. in Zone, preparing CT)

-

Identify opponents cursed technique

DrawBacks
