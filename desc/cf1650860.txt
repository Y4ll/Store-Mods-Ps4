Cherry Visuals is a pink themed lighting pack for Minecraft Bedrock. Cherry groves, sunsets and night skies all get pulled toward pink and magenta.

What this changes

Color grading

Pink and magenta are pushed across the whole world, not just the cherry biome. Plains, deserts and mountains all pick up the tint.

Cherry groves

Blossom trees glow softly and the falling petals catch light instead of looking flat.

Sunsets

The sky burns pink at dusk with hard golden light coming in low across the ground.

Villages

Warm sunset tones stay on the buildings through the whole day and night cycle, not only at evening.

Night sky and auroras

Deep starry skies with pink and magenta auroras running across them. Best seen from above a village.

Nether portals

Portals throw real light onto the blocks around them, with heavy shadow contrast behind.

Point lighting

Every light source gives off its own colored light instead of everything glowing the same orange.

- Redstone torch glows red

- Soul torch and soul lantern glow cyan

- Lantern glows warm orange

- Campfire glows orange

PBR

This pack comes with PBR, so blocks react to light with real depth and ores glow on their own underground.

PBR is also the heaviest part of any Vibrant Visuals pack, so it is not forced on you. It ships as a subpack, which means you can switch it on or off yourself. When you activate Cherry Visuals in Global Resources, pick the version you want. Turn PBR off if your device runs hot or drops frames.

Requirements

THIS PACK IS FOR VIBRANT VISUALS ONLY
IT DOES NOT WORK WITH MB LOADER

Vibrant Visuals must be ON. This pack is built for Bedrock's Vibrant Visuals renderer. The pack will not do anything if it is off.

Where to turn it on: Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals

If that toggle is not there, your device or version does not support Vibrant Visuals yet.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Cherry Visuals

- Pick the subpack you want. PBR on for the full look, PBR off for better performance.

- Turn on Settings > Video > Vibrant Visuals

- If the lighting still looks the same, leave the world and load it again once
