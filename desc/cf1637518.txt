The biggest fear with TreeCapitator addons is accidentally destroying your own wooden house. We have put a massive effort into rewriting our system to make it as smart and safe as possible! While no system can be claimed as purely 100% foolproof (especially with how unpredictable Nether blocks can be), we have heavily optimized the script to protect your custom builds from accidental chain-reactions.

Here is how our 3-Step Build Protection tries its best to keep your base safe:

- The Root Scan (Soil Detection): Before breaking a tree, the addon scans the bottom block. If the log is placed on Planks, Stone, Glass, or anything artificial, it is instantly recognized as a "Build" and will NOT trigger the chain reaction. It only chops trees grounded on natural soils (Dirt, Grass, Podzol, Nylium, Netherrack, etc.).

- The Leaf Scan (Player-Placed Leaves Ignored): Overworld trees are checked for the "persistent_bit" state. If you harvest leaves with shears and place them manually for decoration, the addon knows it! It will completely ignore your decorative leaves and stop the chain reaction from destroying your custom bushes.

- Nether Tree Surround Check: Since Nether Wart Blocks don't have a player-placed state, our system scans the surrounding blocks. If your Nether build touches artificial blocks (like Chests, Glass, or Planks), the addon instantly cancels the chain reaction.

How to Keep Your Builds Safe (Do's & Don'ts):

DON'T build your wooden house directly on bare Dirt or Grass if the walls are made of raw Logs touching natural leaves. Replace the floor under your walls with Planks, Cobblestone, or any crafted block to activate the protection!

DON'T mix natural Nether Wart Blocks with other natural Nether blocks in your builds if you plan to chop them. (Always use artificial blocks for your Nether base floors/walls for safety).

DO use shears to place leaves for decorationthe system will safely ignore them!
