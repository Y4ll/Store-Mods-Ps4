Block ESP is a specialized utility resource pack designed to grant "X-Ray" vision solely for storage and utility objects, making them visible through solid walls and terrain.

Key Features:

Focused Detection: Highlights only Chests (including Trapped and sometimes Ender Chests/Barrels depending on the pack version) and Bells with bright, glowing outlines through obstacles.

Ore-Free: Does not highlight any ores (diamonds, gold, netherite, etc.) or building blocks, keeping the view uncluttered.

Loot Finder: Optimized for quickly spotting storage containers in dark caves, dungeons, bastions, and hidden bases.

Ideal For:

Efficiently raiding generated structures (Nether Fortresses, Bastions, End Cities).

Locating buried treasure chests.

Finding villages quickly by spotting bells.

Recovering lost storage within your own base.

> Note: This gives a gameplay advantage and may be banned on many multiplayer servers. Use responsibly.
