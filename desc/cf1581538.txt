Animated Glowing Ores

Squinting at dark cave walls trying to spot a diamond vein is over. Animated Glowing Ores makes every ore block glow with smooth, eye-catching animations visible from a distance, easy to spot, impossible to miss. With over 22 unique animation styles to choose from, you can pick the exact glow effect that fits your style.

Requirements

Vibrant Visuals must be enabled in your world settings for this pack to work correctly.

Features

- 24+ animated glow styles pick the one that fits your style

- Available in both Outlined and Non-Outlined versions same animations, your choice of look

- Each ore glows in its own natural color diamond blue, gold yellow, redstone red

- Adjustable glow strength Glow+ variants available for stronger intensity
