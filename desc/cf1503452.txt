This is a horror add-on that brings Siren Head (Creation of Trevor Henderson) to Minecraft, based on a horror game.

Siren Head:

- Siren Head has 80000 health and deals 700 damage.

- Siren Head can spawn naturally at night

- Siren Head's targets are players, villagers, and iron golems.

- Siren Head runs very fast.

- Siren Head has a very large damage range.

- Siren Head can lift its target into the air and bite.

- The Siren's Field is an extremely dangerous area.
