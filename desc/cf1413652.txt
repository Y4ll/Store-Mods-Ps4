One Punch Man Addon (Bedrock Edition)

Become the strongest hero in Minecraft! This lightweight script addon grants you the overwhelming power of Saitama, allowing you to defeat any enemy in a single punch and move at god-like speeds.

Unlike other overpowered mods, YOU are in control. This addon uses a unique Hotbar Scaling System that lets you adjust your power level simply by switching slots in your inventory.

Key Features

One Punch Kills: instantly kill any entity you hit. No potion effects neededjust raw power.

Invincibility: You are immune to all damage (Resistance 255).

Infinite Stamina: Never get hungry again (Saturation).

Super Speed & Agility: Run faster than a horse and jump over houses.

No Destruction: This version is "Safe Mode" friendly. You get all the physical powers without accidentally destroying your world or buildings.

How It Works (The Hotbar System)

Your power scales based on which Hotbar Slot you are currently holding. You don't need commands or menusjust scroll!

Slot 1 (Far Left): SERIOUS MODE

Speed: Maximum (God Speed)

Jump: High

Damage: Infinite (Instant Kill)

Defense: Invincible

Slot 5 (Middle): HERO MODE

Speed: Fast

Jump: Medium

Damage: Infinite (Instant Kill)

Defense: Invincible

Slot 9 (Far Right): NORMAL MODE

Power: OFF

Stats: Vanilla / Normal Player

Use this slot when you want to build, trade, or play survival normally.

Installation & Requirements

Download the .mcpack and open it to install.

Apply the Behavior Pack to your world.

IMPORTANT: You must enable Beta APIs in your world settings under "Experiments" for this addon to work.
