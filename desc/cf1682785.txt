What if every mob you killed had a chance to drop something actually worth getting?

Minecraft, But Mobs Drop OP Items changes the way mob drops work by giving mobs a chance to drop much more powerful loot than their normal drops. Zombies, skeletons, creepers, spiders, and other mobs can now give you useful gear, enchanted weapons, tools, armor, and rare items when you defeat them.

You might kill a mob and get a normal enchanted item, or you might get something much better than you expected. Some drops can have strong enchantments, making mob farms and random encounters much more rewarding.

The addon is especially fun for survival worlds because it gives you another way to find powerful equipment without simply handing it to you. Exploring at night, clearing caves, fighting mobs, or setting up a mob farm can all lead to something useful.

What's included?

- Enchanted armor, weapons, and tools can be found as drops.

- Rare items have much lower drop chances.

- Some of the strongest gear is extremely difficult to obtain.

- Loot is designed around different rarity levels, so common and rare drops feel different.

- Normal mob drops can still be obtained.

Kill mobs. Collect loot. Get lucky. Find something OP.
