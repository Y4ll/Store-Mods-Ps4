Full Description:

You will be able to get the powers and costumes of the Invincible characters in survival.

Manufacture costumes:

To make the costumes you can use the costume table using this recipe:

Place the table on the floor and interact with it to choose which suit to make.

It will show you the recipes you need to make the suit, you need to have the necessary items in your inventory if you want to make the suit, when you have the necessary items press the make button and it will give you the complete suit of the selected character.

Robot:

Robot will have the functions of an iron golem, i.e. it will guard the area by eliminating nearby enemies.

Robot will have the functions of an iron golem, i.e. it will guard the area by eliminating nearby enemies.

By interacting with it you will be able to build the powers of the characters added by the add-on.

Robot can heal itself using redstone or wait a while for it to heal itself.

Characters/Powers:

To use the powers place the powers item in the leg slot, while the costume place it in the foot slot and the mask in the head slot. Some Invincible costumes come with the mask already incorporated.

To use a character skin place the skin item in the leg slot. If you use the Invincible skin or another skin you can change its appearance depending on the costume you place in the leg slot.

When you have equipped the powers of any character it will grant you a variety of abilities that can be done depending on the action you do or use the items that the power grants you.

Enemies:

In the add-on includes a variety of new enemies, several of these enemies are generated by the Minecraft world and others will be able to be summoned.

Mauler Twins

They will appear randomly at night and will attack the player if you get close.

Several can appear in an area.

Statistics:

Health: 150

Fire Damage: 6

Angstrom Levy

Angstrom Levy has abilities of his own such as creating portals that will fire his drones at you.

Statistics:

- Health: 150

- Drone damage: 8

- Blast power: 1

Spawn: Anywhere in the world at night.

There is also a small chance that he will trigger a portal that will send you up or down.

Defeating Anstrom Levy will grant you his powers that will allow you to create portals to launch drones or summon variants of Invincible.

Invincible War:

You will be able to face the different variants of Invincible, in order to summon an Invincible use Angstrom Levy's powers to create portals and summon an Invincible. Defeating an Invincible will grant you its suit and Invincible's powers.

From Levy's powers you can summon a variant of Mark, you cannot summon more than one variant of Invincible in the same place, you must wait until that variant is eliminated or is as far away as possible.

Statistics:

- Health: 800

- Attack damage: 12

- Has 5 special attacks

- When defeated, he will release Invincible's powers and the suit that Mark's variant wears.
