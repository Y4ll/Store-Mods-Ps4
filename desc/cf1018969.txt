It adds a real rain sound pack replacing the default rain sound which was like FM radio. Also changes the texture of the rain making it look more real. decreasing the amount of rain which not just look good but also improves performance. It also disables Ground rain splash particle which improves performance drastically... Also it is fully compatible with all other packs just place it above all packs.

What It does?

Better Textures

To make rain look more realistic and better, I worked on the textures. It enhances the textures of rain particles and splash particles (raindrops). This makes the rain look more aesthetic.

Better Particles/Less Particles - Configurable

Not only are the particle textures enhanced, but their properties as well. To make the rain more pleasing, I had to make it less annoying. Unlike before, you won't feel irritated by the rain. In fact, I got the idea to make this texture pack because I used to change the weather whenever it started to rain on my server. Then I realized not only is that cheating, but it's also the opposite of how I behave in real life. I love rain in real life, so I thought if I made rain more realistic, it would feel better and it worked for me! ;)

Less Rain

Ahhh, why does it always rain so heavily that it feels like you're taking a shower in blue-colored water?? Well, I fixed it. The sea or ocean may look blue, but we know it's not definitely not while it's raining. Anyway, I've adjusted the color of the rain, and it looks great now!

Better Sound - Configurable

I think sound is a crucial part of enhancing the rain experience, so I added a real rain sound to the Minecraft sound effects. It gives off different vibes, light, aesthetic, and soothing. I hope you like it.

Player Skin Effects - Configurable

When you're in rain, you'll notice the player skin gets patches of water on it. This is the new we player skin feature. You'll also notice tiny droplets coming from the player skin, another new feature. both of these interesting features don't require behaviour and are fully covered by the resource pack.
