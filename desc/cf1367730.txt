This add-on adds a Redstone Bicycle! Each bicycle is powered by the infinite energy of a Redstone Torch, so players dont have to pedal!
The bicycle also comes with several useful features, including Sounds, Animations, and Dynamic Lighting. At night, youll be able to see the road more clearly thanks to the bikes bright headlight, which turns on automatically when you ride.
The bicycle is also Craftable, so you can use it in Survival Mode to travel faster and make your adventures even more exciting and fun.
