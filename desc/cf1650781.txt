YGP! Minecraft But Fishing Gives OP Items

Every catch could become an adventure.

YGP! Minecraft But Fishing Gives OP Items transforms Minecraft Bedrock fishing into a randomized treasure and adventure system.

A successful catch can give you valuable resources, rare treasures, powerful equipment, dangerous encounters, special events, YGP Signature Gear, or other unexpected rewards.

You never know what is waiting at the end of your fishing line.

Created by YGP Studio / YGP Minecraft.

ONE CATCH ONE REWARD/EVENT

The addon uses a catch-lock system designed to prevent one fishing catch from accidentally triggering multiple unrelated rewards.

1 successful catch 1 reward/event

Intentional item quantities and normal item stacks remain possible.

LOYAL PETS

A rare fishing event can provide a Loyal Pet.

The companion is automatically associated with the player who triggered the event.

No manual taming is required for the event.

The Loyal Wolf is one of the special companion events.
