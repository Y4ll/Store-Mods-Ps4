Round Nature Circular Sun, Moon & Nature Touch!

Bring smooth, natural vibes to your Minecraft world with Round Nature! This resource pack softens the sharp edges of Minecrafts environment, giving it a more organic and relaxing feel perfect for builders, explorers, and survival players who love nature aesthetics.

Features

Circular Sun & Moon a smoother, softer look for the skies.

Circular Log Tops realistic rounded trunks.

Fancy Sunflowers brighter and more detailed sunflower textures.

Circle Shield-custome 3d model with new texture.

Works perfectly on Minecraft Bedrock 1.21+.

Lightweight & Lag-Free optimized for mobile and PC.
