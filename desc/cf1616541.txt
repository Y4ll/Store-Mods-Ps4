Curse?

curse is a lightweight Minecraft Bedrock Resource Pack that enhances the game's interface while keeping the vanilla experience intact. It is designed especially for PvP players who prefer a clean, modern, and distraction-free UI.

Features

Clean and minimal interface.

Custom Crosshair.

Redesigned Hotbar.

Improved Hearts.

Custom Sword Colors.

Better visual clarity during combat.

Lightweight with no FPS impact.

Multiplayer compatible.

Compatibility

Minecraft Bedrock Edition

Installation

Import the .mcpack file into Minecraft and enable it from Global Resources or World Resources.
