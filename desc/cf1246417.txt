After a long time the trailer graphics have returned. Trailer Graphics has arrived after so many patches and bug fixes, but what are Trailer Graphics? Have you ever wanted to play Minecraft but with the graphics shown in its trailers? Don't worry because you have just found the perfect package to fulfill your desires, as the name says, this package is based on the graphics of the Minecraft trailers, the best thing about this graphics package is that it does not have any type of delay. performance and is compatible with all Minecraft Bedrock Edition compatible devices. I invite you to try this package, I am sure you will not regret it.

Featured

- Fancy mode for a brighter and more colorful gaming look.

- Cherry mode similar to the graphics of the Minecraft 1.20 trailer trails & tales.

- New deferred rendering mode.

- Shadow effect on earth blocks.

- Modified textures to simulate a double layer cloud effect.

- Beautiful and colorful setting, the closest thing to the Minecraft trailers.

- Colorful sky.

- Fog that blends with the sky.

- Blue and transparent water.

- Heavy fog in the rain.

- Fog in the nether and in the End.

- It does not affect the performance section.
