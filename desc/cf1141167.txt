You do not require any experimental toggles to play this Add-On

This Add-On works for any Minecraft Bedrock version above 1.21.100

With the addition of the amethyst geodes in the caves and cliffs update, I thought the addition of new geodes would be an interesting feature in addition to the amethyst geodes. With this add-on, you can find new geodes, each one from a mineral present in the game.

This add-on adds 12 new diamond, emerald, gold, iron, coal, red stone and other geodes to the game, which are randomly generated throughout the map at the same heights as the original geodes. In addition to these, quartz and netherite geodes have also been added, which can be found in the Nether.

Here are some of the geodes that have been added:

In the geodes, you can find two new blocks for each mineral, as well as buds and clusters. One of the added blocks generates sprouts, called budding. A feature of the add-on is the ability to collect the buddings with the enchanted pickaxe with a gentle touch, so that players can take them back to base and have a way of generating Minecraft ores.
