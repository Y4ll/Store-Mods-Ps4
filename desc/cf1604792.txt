Step through a Netherrack portal and enter a dimension corrupted by Herobrine. Nether-flavored biomes stretch across a fully generated world Crimson forests, Warped forests, Soul Valleys, and Nether Wastes all filled with Herobrine-corrupted versions of familiar animals, custom ores, and eerie abandoned structures.

Features

- Herobrine-corrupted mobs Cows, Pigs, Sheep, Chickens, Horses, Rabbits, Wolves, and Foxes all spawn as corrupted Herobrine variants that attack with lightning strikes

- 4 unique biomes Nether Wastes, Soul Valley, Crimson Forest, and Warped Forest all generate across the dimension

- Custom ores Amethyst, Aquamarine, Bloodstone, Bronze, Celestite, Citrine, Cobalt, Garnet, Jade, Onyx, Peridot, Platinum, Rose Gold, Ruby, Sapphire, Spinel, Titanium, and Topaz all with Deepslate variants

- Abandoned structures ruined houses and corrupted builds generate naturally across the dimension as you explore

- Netherrack portal build a Nether-portal shape out of Netherrack, light it with Flint & Steel, and walk through to enter

- Two-way travel build and light a portal inside the dimension to return to the Overworld anytime

- Chunk streaming terrain generates and saves around you in real time; revisit any area and it stays as you left it

Portal Setup

Build a Nether-portal shape using Netherrack (minimum 23 inside opening). Light it with Flint & Steel the opening fills with a ghost portal block. Walk through to enter the Herobrine Dimension.

Commands

- /voxen:herobrine_dimension open the travel menu to enter or return from the dimension

Requirements

- Beta APIs experiment must be ON World Settings Experiments Beta APIs

- Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.100 or higher

- Both packs must be active Behavior Pack and Resource Pack

Achievement Friendly?

Yes fully achievement friendly. Use it without any worries.
