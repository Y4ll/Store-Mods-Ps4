A simple RenderDragon shader for Minecraft Bedrock, built on Newb Shader with its own sky, sunrise, sunset and clouds.

What it changes:

- Beautiful yellow/orange sunrise and sunset

- Cool blue sky

- Fluffy smooth cloudy clouds, it moves smoothly across the sky

- Beautiful night with aurora in sky

- Beautiful water, its waves changes according to wheather.(you will see speed and huge waves during rain or thunderstroms)

- Wavey leaves, plants, crops ( waves strength changes based on weather)

- Glowing ores

- Every light source like torch, lantern etc emits more saturated color, making the villages look more lively

- Rain with dark clouds. Terrain/blocks reflects during rain and terrain looks wet

- End dimenions has purple auorura with cool starry cubemap

- Nether looks more bright and firey

Installation:

- Download pack

- Load the downloaded pack into your game

- Activate the pack from Global Resources and play

Since, it is a render dragon shader you will need mb loader or patched minecraft in order to use this shader

How to install in Windows: (easiest way)

Locate this folder C:\XboxGames\Minecraft for Windows\Content\data\renderer

Extract my pack, in pack there is a folder "renderer" copy it and past it at this location C:\XboxGames\Minecraft for Windows\Content\data\

Replace all files

But before doing this, i recommend you to have a backup of that renderer folder, so you can remove the shader whenever you want without any problem
