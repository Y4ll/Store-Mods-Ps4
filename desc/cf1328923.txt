Pack Description: This resource pack turns normal vanilla mobs into player models with female and male variants, you get the point.

Some of the designs of the mobs might not look like what you'd expect for packs like these, cuz unlike most of these "girl packs", the ones here are more integrated to nature, they dont have names, they are blank slates, they are humanoid animals basically, creatures made to live in the world than sticking out, unless to go to my Patreon or Deviantart and look at my renders. The rest of the platforms i'm in covers different things, so i wont mention them here.

Another thing, to make this pack more immersive than the 2nd paragraph, both the original mobs and the player model mob spawn each other instead of one replacing each other.

Instructions:

- To make a variant of a mob stick instead of changing for everytime to log in your minecraft world, craft a Name Tag using 1 paper and 1 iron ingot. Then go to the Anvil and rename them the following: male, female, mob, cold/warm male, cold/warm female, cold/warm mob. No captial letters. More Name Tag lists on here: https://bit.ly/47d5kbb

-

- To have the extra female part on the female models, go to Global Resources, tap on Human Player Mobs, the Setting icon should appear, tap on the Setting icon, and there should be a slider option that appear.

-

- To have the new humanoid model from Waifu Husbando Mobs, go to Global Resources, tap on Human Player Mobs, the Setting icon should appear, tap on the Setting icon, and there should be a slider option that appear. Please note that it's still on Beta phase, so you'll see both humanoid and player model spawning as some mobs havent been remodeled yet.
