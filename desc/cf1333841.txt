With this mod you can create golems with new blocks, almost any material with this you can defend your village from each threat and explore each golem with different abilities.

GENERATE GOLEMS IN THE WORLD, THERE IS NO LONGER NEED TO CREATE THEM BECAUSE NOW THEY HAVE A NEW FUNCTION, AND THAT IS THAT THEY CAN BE GENERATED BY STRUCTURE LIKE AN IRON GOLEM WITH ONLY ONE PUMPKIN AND THE CORRESPONDING MATERIAL FOR EACH GOLEM AND FORMING A CRUZ WE CAN GENERATE IT!.

IN ADDITION, THEY CAN ALSO BE HEALED WHEN RECEIVING DAMAGE, THAT IS, THEY WILL SHOW THEIR CRACKED TEXTURE AND HOW THE IRON GOLEM CAN BE HEALED WITH THE CORRESPONDING MATERIAL OF THE GOLEM THAT YOU WANT TO REPAIR!

IN ADDITION, GOLEMS CAN BE GENERATED WITH THE STRUCTURE OF A CROSS, THEY ALSO HAVE AN INCREDIBLE SPATTING EGG!

Ever get tired of plain old Iron Golems? So difficult to make, always look the same . . . Well, Extra Golems is here for you!

Now introducing 50+ golems made from Minecraft blocks, ranging from wood and glass to diamond and nether brick. Each golem has unique properties, as described below. Have fun!

To build a golem, start by placing blocks in a 'T' formation, similar to the Iron Golem layout. Finally, place a Golem Head on top. Once created, golems act like Iron Golems, killing monsters left and right and defending your fort / base / testificate prison.
