About Pie Shader

Pie Shader [RTX] is a high-quality Minecraft Bedrock shader pack designed to completely transform your world with stunning visual effects while maintaining smooth gameplay performance.

Built for Minecraft Bedrock 1.21 - 26.20+, this shader enhances:

- Lighting

- Night Atmosphere

- Water Reflections

- Sky Rendering

- Fog Effects

- Biome Atmospherics

- RTX/PBR Style Visuals

Whether you're exploring caves, building cities, surviving with friends, or creating cinematic content Pie Shader delivers a beautiful next-gen experience.

IMPORTANT Vibrant Visuals Support

For the BEST experience, enable Vibrant Visuals inside Minecraft settings!

Without Vibrant Visuals:

- Some effects may appear weaker

- Lighting may look less immersive

- RTX/PBR visuals may not fully render

With Vibrant Visuals Enabled:

- Stronger lighting

- Better atmosphere

- Enhanced reflections

- Improved RTX style visuals

- Cinematic environments
