Bare Bones Recolors is a lightweight, standalone resource pack made specifically for Minecraft Pocket Edition (MCPE / Bedrock). It fully alters the world on its own. It gives clean, vibrant, shader, and flat-colored look.

Why It Works Perfectly.

Performance Boost: Features a simple 16x16 pixel layout. This lowers hardware strain, reduces lag, and increases frame rates on mobile devices.

Trailer Aesthetic: Instantly swaps out noisy, gritty vanilla blocks for smooth, solid blocks right after activation.

Enhanced Visibility: Brightens dark caves, deep oceans, and nighttime environments, making it much easier to see on a small phone or tablet screen.
