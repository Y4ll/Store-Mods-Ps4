Baku's Carnival Shader
Author: Baku_Studio

Baku's Carnival Shader is an original Minecraft Bedrock Edition resource pack made for the official Vibrant Visuals graphics mode. It brings a powerful cinematic golden-hour look with strong glowing sunlight, soft volumetric rays, warm atmospheric colors and rich natural vegetation while fully keeping Minecrafts classic block style.

Pack Information

- Pack Name: Baku's Carnival Shader

- Author: Baku_Studio

- Type: Vibrant Visuals / PBR Resource Pack

- Supported Versions: Minecraft Bedrock & Pocket Edition 1.21.120 and newer (1.21 1.26 generation)

- Required Mode: Vibrant Visuals

- Platforms: Android, iOS, Windows, Xbox, PlayStation (devices that support Vibrant Visuals)

Visual Style

The pack delivers a warm, cinematic and immersive atmosphere. Expect strong golden sunlight with soft volumetric rays, warm peach-orange-pink skies, rich lush emerald green grass, deep natural foliage, soft readable shadows and calm reflective teal water. The result feels modern and cinematic while still looking completely like Minecraft.

Installation

- Import the .mcpack file

- Activate Baku's Carnival Shader in Resource Packs (place near the top)

- Go to Settings Video Graphics Mode Vibrant Visuals

- Enter any world

Performance

Lightweight configuration-only pack. Runs well on mobile and higher-end devices that support Vibrant Visuals.
