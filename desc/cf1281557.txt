Core Features

The Gaze Gloom Infection System

The horror begins with The Nesta grotesque, pulsating entity that spawns naturally in the world. This is the heart of the infection, constantly spreading its influence.

- Spreading Corruption: The Nest slowly converts surrounding blocks into a terrifying new biome, creating Gloom Blocks, flickering Catalysts, sticky Gloom Carpets, and twitching Gloom Sprouts.

- The Infectors: The true vectors of the plague are the nimble Cockroaches and mutated Bats. When a Nest is damaged, it unleashes a swarm of these creatures. A single attack from them is enough to permanently transform any vulnerable mob into an Infected monstrosity.

- Chain Reaction: Many infected creatures, upon death, spawn more Cockroaches, creating a devastating chain reaction that can quickly overwhelm an entire region, lives 30 seconds.

The Spoils of Horror

Defeating these nightmares yields gruesome but essential rewards.

- Heart of Gloom: A rare drop from The Nest. This pulsating organ is the key ingredient for the most powerful items and the summoning of the final boss.

- Gloom Clot: A foul, sticky substance dropped by infected creatures, used in various crafting recipes.
