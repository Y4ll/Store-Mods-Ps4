Mega TNT+

Mega TNT+ adds 61 unique craftable TNT blocks to Minecraft Bedrock, each with custom explosions, craters, particle effects, and distinct destruction behaviors based on their block material. Every TNT is fully survival-obtainable and crafted at a crafting table.

How It Works

All TNT blocks are crafted using the same base logic: 8 pieces of a specific block/material surrounding a TNT in the center (or in some special cases, a unique pattern). Each one explodes differently different crater sizes, particle effects, mob interactions, and special abilities.

Special TNTs

- Slime TNT Launches all nearby mobs into the air

- Bedrock TNT Massive explosion, destroys everything

- Black Hole TNT Creates a gravitational pull that sucks in mobs and items

- End Stone TNT Summons a particle dragon that lingers after the explosion

Features

- 61 TNT types, all fully survival-craftable

- Custom craters with unique shapes and sizes per material

- Distinct particle effects on ignition and explosion

- Special mob interactions (knockback, gravity, levitation, burning, slowing)

- Animated particle dragon (End Stone TNT)

- Black Hole gravitational pull with visible particle vortex

- Slime bounce launcher launches all mobs vertically within radius

- No experimental features required

- Compatible with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21+
