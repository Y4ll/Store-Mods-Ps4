A complementary add-on for SERP Pokedrock that adds various details to improve the experience of a Pokemon world in Minecraft.

The add-on is available in Spanish, English, Portuguese, and French (The translation is done by AI; please report any errors).

IT IS VERY IMPORTANT TO HAVE DETAILED ABOVE POKEDROCK

- Behavior

- Resourse

Mechanics

Roll: By crouching, moving forward, and jumping, you can perform a "Dodge" or "Roll"

Level Cap: This mechanic limits the Pokemon you can use based on their level; the level cap increases according to the number of badges you have

(It can also be modified or disabled with the command /dpk:options)

Ranch machine: This allows Pokemon to walk freely outside their Poke Balls without taking up space in your party; some even drop items after a while.

Pokemon Information: This allows you to see details such as a Pokemon's name, gender, and level without engaging it in battle.

(Can be disabled with the command /dpk:options)

PokeMenu: Allows you to pet, change the position of your Pokemon's attacks, and make it sit (Activated by interacting with your Pokemon with your empty hand)

Details

- Now status effects affect out of combat

- Pokemon can appear asleep at night.

- A notification will appear if a Hoopa ring appears.

- Each Pokeball has a different particle when releasing a Pokemon

- If the Pokemon is Shiny, it will have a particle effect and a sound when it appears.

- Mega Evolutions remain active in battle even if you switch Pokemon

- A notification will appear if the Pokemon can evolve.

- It adds some new animations for the player

- Some Pokemon grant you new abilities when you're on their shoulder (For example: Bidoof helps you chop down trees and Spinarak gives you the ability to climb)

New Commands

/dpk:alpha Turns a wild Pokemon into an alpha Pokemon

/dpk:levelcap Tells you your level cap

/dpk:options Allows you to modify addon options in your world

/dpk:regalo_misterioso Allows you to claim rewards, either time-based or via code

/dpk:fullreset Resets your progress upon starting Detailed Pokedrock

(example code: ZacekRules)

Pokemon with Skins

One of the systems in Detailed Pokedrock is Pokemon with Skins; these are usually obtained through rewards or codes.

New Items

- Alpha Capsule: Transforms your Pokemon into their alpha versions. (Not currently obtainable in survival mode)

- Shiny Capsule: Transforms your Pokemon into their Shiny versions. (Not currently obtainable in survival mode)

- Bottle caps: They are used to increase the IVs of your Pokemon. (Not currently obtainable in survival mode)

- Omni Ring: If your Pokemon has a Mega Stone equipped, it will function as a Mega Ring; if the Pokemon is Dynamax level, it will function as a Dynamax Band; if it has neither, it allows you to use the "Power-Up" ability. (Not currently obtainable in survival mode)

(Vitamins are created at the brewing table, using new berries along with a potion (the most basic one) with its opposite effect:
Health uses maximum health
Attack and special attack use strength potion
Defense and special defense use turtle master potion
Speed uses speed potion)

Important

This addon removes all other vanilla animals that Pokedrock does not, such as nautiluses, skeleton horses, and goats.
