Turn every encounter with mobs into an adventure filled with treasures and discoveries! This addon introduces a unique system of rewards and exploration, making each mob eliminated an opportunity to gain surprise loot and reveal structures throughout the world.

Key Features:

- Random Rewards for Eliminating Mobs (45%)

Each mob you defeat has a chance to drop special loot, ranging from essential resources to rare and valuable items. You never know what treasure you might find when facing the creatures of Minecraft!

- Generation of Mysterious Structures (15%)

With every elimination, there's a chance for a structure to appear on-site: houses, temples, mansions, and more.

This addon is designed for adventurers and explorers, ensuring that every battle in Minecraft becomes an unforgettable experience. Get ready to uncover rewards that will make your world a place filled with mysteries to solve!
