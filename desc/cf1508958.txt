Hacking addon lets you take full control of your world without needing to use commands or switch to creative mode. You can access powerful abilities and features that are normally restricted, making gameplay more flexible and fun while still staying in survival. Best of all, it is fully achievement-friendly, so you can continue earning achievements without any restrictions.

Important note: To obtain the hacking items, you need an iron ingot, a nether star, and a crafting table.
