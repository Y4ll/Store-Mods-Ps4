The Shiny Derps v3 skin pack comes with 48 shiny and colorful derp skins made by ExamineCat (previously known as Examinedcat669).

I was inspired to make these skins a few years ago when I saw the silver and gold derps with a creeper or pig face on Skinseed's skin search. I thought they were cool, so I decided to save them, turn them into bases, and recolor them to make the rest of the colors. Since then I have given them derp faces, and now I have improved them and put them together and into this skin pack.

Special thanks to Funiverse Minnesota for making a tutorial on how to make skin packs. This skin pack wouldn't be possible without him. Go check out his channel: Visit Funiverse Minnesota's channel.

Downloading (using the alternative download links):

- Click on the download link for the file you want to download.

- Click on the red Click on ad button and follow the steps to unlock the content. You will be prompted to visit a page with a list of articles. Select one, wait 15 seconds, then go back to the main Boostellar page. You may close the other tabs.

- Click on the purple Unlock content to be taken to Mediafire.

- Once you are on MediaFire, click on the blue DOWNLOAD (File Size) button. Make sure to close any additional popups that may show up.

- Wait for the file to download, then import it to Minecraft.

Importing:

- The .mcpack and .mctemplate files will automatically import into Minecraft when opened if your browser, file explorer, or device recognizes them as files that Minecraft can open.

- The .zip files must be extracted before being manually placed in your skin_packs folder.
