Let me introduce you to VOID-BORN CLIENT DEFINITIVE, already converted to black and red, varying view alternatives a client-style resource pack for Minecraft Bedrock created by TheKx. Designed to enhance your gameplay with visual improvements, quality-of-life utilities and performance options, while keeping the core Minecraft experience intact. Features:

Tool & Armor Durability Display

Enchantment Icons

Food Stats (hunger & saturation)

Inventory Counter

Available Inventory Viewer

Quick Buttons / Quick Crafting & Search

Improved Chat (ChatPlus)

Optimized & Custom Biome Fog Completely removed

Cleaner Water Improved Lava Particles

Enhanced Elytra & Mace Animations

4 Game Modes:

Default Pro: Full set of visual enhancements, utilities and HUD improvements.

Viewer Chunks: Displays chunks, slime chunks, light ranges and technical references.

Boosted FPS: Reduces particles, fog and visual effects for better performance.

X-Ray Pro: Ore and structure vision with configurable night vision.

Water arrows: Allows us to see which direction the water currents are flowing.

VOID-BORN CLIENT DEFINITIVE aims to deliver a more complete, optimized and customizable Minecraft Bedrock experience without changing the soul of the game.
