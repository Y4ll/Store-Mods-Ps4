There are now 300 textures that can be applied to 12 Framed Blocks.

(that's almost all of Minecraft's full block textures)

REQUIREMENTS: Enable "Upcoming Creator Features" in the world's experiments section for the multi-collision to work.

All blocks are texture pack compatible, so you can use whatever texture you wish and the framed textures will look what you want it to be!

To apply a texture, just interact with the block in hand you want to apply the texture of!

You will get back the block used when you break or replace the texture.

Framed Blocks:

- Tall Wedge

- Tall Corner Wedge

- Medium Wedge

- Medium Corner Wedge

- Small Wedge

- Small Corner Wedge

- Slabs

- Stairs

- Panel

- Pillar

- Corner Pillar

- Half Slabs

(Note: You can use any planks, including custom woods if it has the tag of "minecraft:planks")

The Framed Blocks add-on allows for you to expand your building capabilities by increasing your pallet and shape possibilities to have your builds look more exquisite than ever!

All blocks are crafted in the Stonecutter using any planks, including custom woods with the tag "minecraft:planks"
