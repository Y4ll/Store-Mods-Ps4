A Private One Block World

After activating the addon, a Register/Teleport UI will open when you are in the Overworld. The normal Overworld is completely restricted, and every player receives their own private One Block island.

Starter Resources & Quest System

Every player receives useful starter resources, including Guide book and quest book

Three Endless One Block Faces

Your One Block adventure has three different phases/faces: Surface, Underground and Hell, After Hell it will be repeat again form surface

Grow & Harvest Wheat Easily

Use Bone Meal to grow your wheat faster.

Even better, fully grown wheat can be harvested simply by clicking it with an empty hand. No tool required.

Animal Seeds

Craft special Cow, Sheep, and Pig Seeds and grow them just like crops.

Once they are fully grown, simply click the grown crop to harvest the animal and expand your resources.

Chicken Bosses

The modpack also introduces powerful Chicken Bosses, including: Evi Planks Chicken, Cobblestone Chicken, and Iron Chicken

Prepare your equipment before taking them on.

Resourceful Chickens

Chickens aren't just for food anymore.

Discover special Resourceful Chickens that provide materials such as: Cobblestone, Grave, Clay, Sand, Dirt

Aromic Villager Dimension

Build the Aromic Villager Dimension Portal to unlock a special dimension designed for villagers and the Wandering Trader.

Farm Master

Don't want to spend time building complicated farms?

Find the Farm Master and trade for instant farms.

Get your production started without building everything from scratch.

Monster Cages

Capture monsters using different Monster Cages and create your own controlled mob-resource system.

Skyblock Progression

The pack also includes many classic Skyblock-style progression systems and blocks, including: Clay Furnace, Sieve, Wooden Barrel, Crucible, And more Skyblock progression content

Turn basic materials into increasingly valuable resources and keep progressing.

The Quest Doesn't Really End...

Complete the One Block progression and eventually reach the final One Block quest: crafting the Darken Quest Book.

But that's not the true ending...

The moment you craft the Darken Quest Book, a new chapter begins.
