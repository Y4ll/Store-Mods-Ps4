Clear Water + Lava

Clear Water + Lava is a Minecraft Bedrock resource pack designed to greatly improve visibility while exploring underwater areas and lava-filled environments.

About the Pack

Minecraft's default underwater and lava fog can make exploration difficult by heavily limiting visibility. This pack reduces that visual obstruction, allowing you to see your surroundings much more clearly while swimming underwater or moving through lava.

Features

- Clearer underwater visibility

- Reduced water fog

- Improved visibility inside lava

- Reduced lava and lava resistance fog

- Better ocean and underwater exploration

- Useful for underwater structures, caves and mining

- Supports multiple Minecraft Bedrock biomes

- Clean and lightweight resource pack

- No gameplay mechanics are changed

Perfect For

This pack is ideal for players who frequently explore oceans, underwater caves, monuments, ravines, Nether areas or locations surrounded by lava. It gives you a cleaner view while keeping the original Minecraft experience recognizable.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file.

- Open it with Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

- Wait for the resource pack to import.

- Activate the pack from your World or Global Resources settings.

- For best results, keep this pack above conflicting visual or fog resource packs.

Compatibility

Designed for modern versions of Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Compatibility may vary when used together with shaders, Vibrant Visuals, custom biome packs or other resource packs that modify water, lava or fog rendering.
