Atomic Destruction: Nuclear Bombs Addon

Experience the ultimate destructive power in Minecraft Bedrock with Atomic Destruction: Nuclear Bombs Addon. This addon introduces four unique nuclear weapons, each with increasing levels of devastation, realistic explosion effects, powerful shockwaves, radioactive fallout, and massive mushroom clouds.

Whether you're creating apocalyptic worlds, testing large-scale destruction, or making cinematic videos, this addon delivers an immersive nuclear experience unlike anything in vanilla Minecraft.

Remote Detonation System

Each bomb has its own dedicated detonator.

Simply place the bomb, activate the corresponding detonator, and watch as an enormous nuclear explosion unfolds with realistic visual and sound effects.

Radiation Effects

The danger doesn't end after the explosion.

Radiation remains in the affected area, making it hazardous for nearby players and creating a realistic post-apocalyptic environment.
