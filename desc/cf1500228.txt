SmoothCraft FPS is a lightweight performance-focused resource pack designed to boost FPS and reduce lag in Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

This pack removes unnecessary particles and animations, simplifies visuals, and optimizes UI elements to deliver a smoother and more responsive gameplay experience especially on low-end devices.

Features:

- Optimized UI for better performance

- Removed unnecessary particles and animations

- Lightweight textures

- Reduced lag and stutter

- Improved overall FPS

- Clean and minimal design

Best for:

- Low-end devices

- PvP players

- Survival gameplay

- Smooth experience lovers

Recommended Settings:

- Render Distance: 48 chunks

- Graphics: Low / Medium

- Smooth Lighting: Off

- Fancy Graphics: Off

Performance Impact:

- Noticeable FPS boost

- Smoother camera movement

Reduced frame drops in heavy biomes (Jungle, Dark Oak, etc.)

Note:

This pack focuses on performance, so some visual effects and details are reduced.
