Imagine a texture pack that adds even more life to the game, while maintaining its simple essence...

What is this?

Is a texture pack with that enhances natural elements in the game, such as crops, vines, and more. To give a simple yet elegant look! It improves the appearance of your Minecraft Bedrock worlds.

Inspired by Farmer's Delight and Better Foliage (Minecraft Bedrock), to create a texture pack that enhances nature with unique characteristics, I have added my own design so you can get a better look with a texture pack that changes the vegetation of the game!

Feature list

- Grass:

Sometimes, grass has small stalks of wheat (just like the real life).

It's truly strange, with grass everywhere, but you can easily appreciate it every time you walk through the plains or other biomes.

- Crops:

Just like in real life, some crops can be grown with flowers when they are at their peak growth stage. Not all crops have flowers, but potatoes and carrots do, so I decided to add them.

The flowers of these crops have an appearance reminiscent of Farmer's Delight wild crops.

Melons and pumpkins can have yellow flowers when their stems are connected.

- Vines:

Vines are everywhere, these can grow with small red flowers, giving a nice look to the biomes.

They may have white/light flowers with a small chance.
