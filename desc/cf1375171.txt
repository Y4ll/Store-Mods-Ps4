Basic Information

Freight Train [GTA SA] Basic Specifications

Performance:

- Gibonz System: 42

Miscellaneous:

- Health: 20 Points.

- Seat count: Depends on vehicle.

Damage:

- The Vehicle will get damage animation if under max health condition and if the Vehicle in Critical Health [2 health] will release particles if the car moving. so fix your car using Wrench.

Wrenches:

- Wrench: Fixes 1 point to the vehicle.

Commands, Eggs and Colors available to change:

- /summon hp:freighttrain_locomotive [NONE]

- /summon hp:freighttrain_flatcars [NONE]

- /summon hp:freighttrain_flatcars_catalyst [NONE]

- /summon hp:freighttrain_flatcars_greengoo [NONE]

Special Features:

- [NONE]
