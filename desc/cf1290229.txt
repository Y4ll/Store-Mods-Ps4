Chase the Skies Panorama with Shaders (Bedrock)

This is a really simple Resource Pack that adds shaders to the original panorama.

The seed was discovered by the Minecraft@Home team.

- Seed: 8819392414030687460

- Coordinates: X=1266.666 Y=105.002 Z=-125.298

- Version: Minecraft Java 1.21.5 or Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.70

The shaders used are Mojang's Vibrant Visuals.

Note: GUI not included.
