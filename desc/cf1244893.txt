To get started, craft a wand from a stick. Next, craft a regular bubble solution from an empty glass bottle, sweet berries, and a dye of choice. Bubbles come in all 16 colors! What's more, bubbles also come in glow-in-the-dark variants! Simply substitute glow berries for sweet berries.

How to Use:

- Equip the bubble solution into your off hand, the wand in your main hand. When done correctly and the bubble wand is selected, the 'dipping' animation will play.

<figure class="image image_resized align-center"></figure>

2. The longer you hold, the bigger the bubble gets! But don't blow them too big, or they pop.

<figure class="image image_resized align-center"></figure>

3. Leash them to get balloons!

<figure class="image image_resized align-center"></figure>

4. Target an entity while blowing to have the bubbles swarm them relentlessly!

<figure class="image align-center"></figure>
