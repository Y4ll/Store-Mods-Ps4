Golden Mirror Update

This addon allows you to craft a magic mirror that allows you to teleport to your spawnpoint. It's easy to craft, but you can run out of iron quickly if you use it too often.

- You can repair ir with either iron blocks or diamonds.

- Don't forget to set a spawnpoint, or else it won't work.

- Have in mind that it's expensive to use it frequently.

- You can also use it in other dimensions. Just make sure you have enough free space around your portal

If you use a golden mirror, you will be teleported to the last place where you used a magic mirror.

You can repair it with a gold block
