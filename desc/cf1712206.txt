How to Use

Spawn and start your AI opponent using /scriptevent commands.

Spawn the default bot:

/scriptevent pvpbot:spawn

Spawn a bot at specific coordinates with a custom name:

/scriptevent pvpbot:spawn <x> <y> <z> <AshBlast0P_BOT_>

Start the AI Bot

/scriptevent pvpbot:start AshBlast0P_BOT_

Installation

Download the .mcaddon file.
Open it with Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
Activate the required packs in your world settings.

Enter your world and spawn the AI bot using the commands above.
