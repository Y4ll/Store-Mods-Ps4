TerraNova add-on works for Minecraft Bedrock version 26.10+ and higher.

The TerraNova add-on explores another world, which fantastical elements and many mysteries bloom in the world of Terra. This will focus on adding those things to the world of Minecraft by with new items, blocks, mobs, equipment, armours, world generation, structures, and more!
