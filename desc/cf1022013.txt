Fast Craft/Quick Craft:

Adds a button in the UI that lets you craft multiple items at once. Instead of cliking again and again for crafting a same item again, just click the fast craft button once and you're done! This Button is added to following UI screens: Player Inventory, Crafting inventory, Stonecutter inventory and Villager's trading UI. It is available in both and Classic and Pocket UI

Fast Loot/Quick Loot:

Adds a toggle button in the UI that lets you quickly move items between your inventory and containers. Instead of dragging and dropping items one by one, just turn on the toggle and click to instantly transfer them. This feature is available for all container screens in both Classic and Pocket UI.

This is one of the hidden feature of the minecraft bedrock, and this resource pack enables it. This texture pack is completely compatible with any other resource pack. Works on both pocket and classic ui And On mobile and PC.
