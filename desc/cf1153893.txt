Do you think that Armor Trims look very dull and non-shiny. As the name suggests 'Armor Trims', there should be some glow according to the material used to make the trim. Here it is! Glowing Armor Trims is a Resource Pack (Not Shader!) which makes every type of trim made with any material or template, have a glow! It is a better way to show off your good looking trims!

Not:

- Leather Armors do not glow because of some material glitch.

- Some armors trims may look dark because they use a darker color palette as their texture.

- Icons of armors with trims look same as the ones without trims, so please adjust with that!
