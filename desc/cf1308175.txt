This is a shaderpack that adds additional effects to vibrant visuals for Minecraft bedrock edition

Features include

Custom sky

chromatic aberration

fake depth of field

enhanced volumetric fog

aces tonemaping

rain drop on screen during rain [see subpacks]

Pixelation [see subpacks]

THIS REQUIRES A SHADER LOADER AND VIBRANT VISUALS TO BE ENABLED

examples of shaderloaders are: mb loader(android), betterrenderdragon/wyvern(windows)

Installation:

Follow this awesome guide on how to install shaders:
