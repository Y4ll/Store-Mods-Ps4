Small Tools

Subtitle: Clearer Vision, Better Performance!

Description:

Tired of bulky tools taking up half your screen? Small Tools 16x is designed specifically for PvP and Survival players who want a clean, minimalist look without losing the vanilla feel. This pack shrinks your tools to give you a massive visibility advantage during combat!

Key Features:

Minimalist Design: All tools (Swords, Pickaxes, Axes, Shovels, Hoes) are resized for maximum screen space.

Diamond Sword Focus: Specially crafted Short Diamond Sword for better hit-timing and visibility.

FPS Friendly: High-quality 16x textures that won't lag your game.

Perfect for PvP: No more getting distracted by large item models in the heat of battle.

Universal Compatibility: Works as an overlay on top of any other resource pack!
