Ever picked up a random food item and wondered if it's worth eating? Food Stats takes the guesswork out of Minecraft's food system by displaying hunger points, saturation values, and status effects directly in item names.

Just glance at any food in your hotbar or inventory, no need to pause the game or check the wiki. Everything you need to know is right there.

What it shows:

- Hunger points restored

- Saturation value

- Negative effects like Poison, Hunger, and Nausea with chance percentages

- Positive buffs like Regeneration, Absorption, and Fire Resistance

- Special info like Cake slices, Chorus Fruit teleportation, and more

Why use it:

- Perfect for survival players who want to make smart food choices

- Great for newer players still learning the food system

- Useful in hardcore mode where every food decision matters

- Clean and lightweight, uses only font and language files, no behavior packs required

New:

- Added thirst stat display with a custom water droplet icon

- Expanded compatibility with popular survival and food addons, stats now appear on supported custom foods and drinks too

- Cleaner language files and refreshed manifest

Compatibility note: For stats to appear on custom foods from other addons, place Food Stats above those packs in your world's resource pack list. Global resource packs are the lowest priority in Bedrock, so activating Food Stats in-world is required for addon foods to display correctly.

Supports 31 languages including English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Russian, Portuguese, and many more.

Works on Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Just drop it into your resource packs and you're good to go.
