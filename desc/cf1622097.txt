LEmote is a Minecraft Bedrock add-on that lets players browse and use custom emotes through an easy in-game menu. This add-on includes a clean UI system for opening emote categories, selecting emotes, and managing favorites or saved slots. It is designed to make emote access simple and practical during gameplay.

For users who want smoother-looking animation results, LEmote can optionally work alongside LModel. Using LModel is recommended for the best visual feel, but it is not required and LEmote still works without it.

Features:

- In-game emote menu

- Category-based browsing

- Slot management for emotes

- Add and delete emote options

- 100+ custom emotes

- Optional compatibility with LModel for smoother animation presentation

Dependency

- LModel by Levyyc

Notes:

- LModel is recommended but not required

- LEmote works normally without LModel

- Made for Minecraft Bedrock Edition
