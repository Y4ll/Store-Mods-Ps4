GlowCraft is a stunning Vibrant Visuals shader pack for Minecraft Bedrock that makes blocks, items, particles, & entities glow, bringing true illumination and best visuals to your game. It makes your world look cool and magical!

GlowCraft uses Vibrant Visuals and works on every platform that supports it!

To see what's new in GlowCraft version 2.7, scroll down to the changelog.

Warning: The GlowCraft background panorama on the title screen may not work on 26.50-52, note that this is a Minecraft problem and nothing to do with GlowCraft.

Minimum Required Minecraft version: v1.21.120

Recommended Minecraft version: v26.40 (26.50 works, but has broken UI issues)

Requires Vibrant Visuals to be turned ON to work, and it's highly recommended to have bloom turned on in settings.

GlowCraft UI is turned ON by default. It can be turned OFF in the pack settings.

Disclaimers:

There are some things which are not possible to glow/bloom in Vibrant Visuals, which is why they are not included in GlowCraft. I don't make netherite glow because it makes it look pink. I also tried to add many toggles in GlowCraft settings but it's not possible yet with the current Minecraft Bedrock.

The GlowCraft 2.0 video was recorded before I implemented the new colored lighting feature.

If upgrading GlowCraft has a duplicate error, make sure to delete the old files first.

There is a bug in Minecraft Bedrock v26.50-52 that has invisible and broken UIs, none of those is related to GlowCraft. The background panorama also may not work until Mojang fixes it.
