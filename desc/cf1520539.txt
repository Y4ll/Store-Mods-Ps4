Visible Debris

Visible Debris is a sleek, high-contrast aesthetic pack that re-imagines the toughest material in Minecraft. Say goodbye to the muddy browns of the Netherthis pack transforms the Netherite tier into a collection of glowing, electric blue artifacts.

Key Features

Vibrant Ancient Debris: Features a glowing blue center, making it significantly easier to spot while mining in the Nether.

Neon-Infused Equipment: All Netherite tools, swords, and bows feature a dark metallic base with detailing.

Lightweight Performance: Built to be resource-friendly, ensuring high FPS even on lower-end devices.

Installation Guide

-

Click the Download button on the right sidebar.

-

Once downloaded, double-click the .mcpack file to launch Minecraft and import automatically.

-

Navigate to Settings > Global Resources and move "Visible Debris" to the top of your Active list.
