For reminder, this addon isn't fully complete, there are still lots of feature that will be added soon, so let me introduce you about this addon. ACE brings a various fully animated weapon to your survival world, the weapon using a unique mechanic that very diffrent with other 3D gun addons, the gun using attach/detach, change mag reload system which is unique than just ordinary reload system before, Influenced by many Extraction shooter game such like Road to Vostok, Escape from tarkov, Arena Breakout, and Inspired by most popular bedrock shooter map "GET OUT"

Extraction In SURVIVAL?

Yes you can, find a structure with flare, interact then call it, wait for 60sec then a car will come, pay them, ride their car and they will get you to your last spawnpoint (bed) so make sure to get your spawnpoint not blocked first!

Forget about the terrible stamina bar, its now removed

The guns have a lots of features here, such a equip/unequip animations, attach/detach mag, checking mag bullets, hipfire, attachment, no hit immunity delay, and much more

Gun Mechanics

At first, your weapon doesnt have a mag, you need to attach it using your weapon's mag by right click/interact when holding the gun, make sure you have the right mag on your inventory

A mag with highest bullet will be chosed first, but if you want to attach particular mag, you can put em on your offhand slot.

Bullet counter UI is now removed, you can check your mag by sneaking + hit/attack while holding the gun

To detach the mag, just simply press hit/attack button or left click while holding the gun.. you can play the inspect animation when the gun has no mag attached by pressing hit/attach button or left click again since you didn't hit a entity or your gun will swing (gun attack animation) cuz the gun works like sword (has a damage)

Control

Sneak: Aim down sight

Right click/hold: Shoot / attach mag when the gun is empty

Sneak + Left click/hit: Checking bullet on attached mag

Hit air/block: Detach mag / play inspect animation when the gun is empty

Hit entity: Gun swing attack animation

Reloading Magazine

To reload the magazine, hold it then put the right ammo on your offhand slot, then right click/interact rapidly to attach the bullet one by one

This thing will become 3d animated and automaticaly in the future update, so stay tuned

Attachment mode

To enter attachment mode, you need attachment kit items then put it on your 9th slot hotbar while holding the gun, left click/hit to select and right click/hold to install/uninstall. You cannot shoot, aim and attach/detach mag while entering attachment mode, Sneak to go back and remove the attachment kit from your 9th slot hotbar to quit.

You can instal infinite attachment on creative mode, but in survival mode, you'll need the attachment items

Muzzle: Reduce Gun Shoot volume and radius

Laser: Give you crosshair assist

Scope: Zoom in your aim

Stock: Reduce weapon shake

Magazine: Increase bullet capacity but also increase attach/detach mag duration

Foregrip: Reduce bullet spread
