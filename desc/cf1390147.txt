What Is This

FirmamentCraft Shader is a stylized rendering shader developed for Minecraft: Bedrock Edition in China. It supports MCBE version 1.21.120+

Main Features

Terrain Rendering

-

Basic diffuse lighting and shadows

-

Sky lighting

-

Surface specular highlights and bump effects

-

Metal block reflections of the sky

-

Ore emissive effects

-

Water waves and surface reflections of the sky

-

Underwater lighting and color absorption effects

-

Underwater total internal reflection (TIR) effect

-

Translucent block reflections of the sky and simulated subsurface scattering

-

Plant sway and simulated subsurface scattering

-

Smooth transitional fog effects

-

Artistic rain effects on surfaces

Sky Rendering

-

Dynamic clouds

-

Sun, moon, and starry sky

-

Sky dome and horizon, simulating Mie scattering

-

Artistic representation of the End dimension sky

-

Artistic rain sky effects

Entity Rendering

-

Basic diffuse lighting

-

Dynamic ambient light color variation by biome in the hell

How to Use

On Android, use tools such as MB Loader or Minecraft Patch to load the shader. Alternatively, manually replace the shader files inside the game's installation package.
On Windows, it is recommended to use BetterRenderDragon to load the shader.
Please ensure to use the resource pack provided by the author, otherwise the sky will not be displayed.
