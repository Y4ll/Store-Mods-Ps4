APOCALYPSE

EL SANDO

The world as we knew ended when an ancient force opened the gates to a forgotten age. The dead returned, dinosaurs once again ruled the Earth, and the remnants of ancient civilizations emerged from giant forests, volcanoes, and ruined temples.

As zombie hordes corrupt the living, legendary warriors march to reclaim lost territories. In the skies, tales speak of the return of dragons; in the ruins, survivors fear awakening gods capable of deciding the fate of all creation.

You are one of the few who can still change the outcome of this war. Explore a transformed world, find nests, breed and tame dinosaurs, gather resources from prehistoric creatures, and fight against the armies of extinction. In this apocalypse, survival is not enough: you must choose which creatures to protect and which horrors to destroy.

Playable Content
The addon transforms Minecraft Bedrock survival with prehistoric wildlife, taming and growth systems, real hatching nests, zombified creatures, special threats, new loot, prehistoric fruits, equipment, and custom world generation.

Exploration: Discover castles, temples, coliseums, caves, fossils, waterfalls, natural formations, giant trees, and volcanoes generated by the addon.

Taming: Most dinosaurs can only be tamed as babies. Each group uses different foods and has its own taming rate.

Breeding and Eggs: Tamed adult dinosaurs can reproduce. Instead of generating an instant baby, they produce their corresponding nest or egg.

Apocalyptic Survival: Zombified versions attack living beings and turn the prehistoric world into a perpetual war between life and corruption.

Mounts and Combat: Many tamed dinosaurs can be ridden. Some small creatures can also jump on the player and must be expelled by crouching.

World Generation and Structures
Custom world generation modifies world exploration with epic structures and prehistoric natural elements. The file includes, among others:
Castle: A grand fortress representing the last strongholds of warriors.

Ruined Castle: A ruined version that reinforces the story of war and the apocalypse.

Ancient Temple: Sanctuary of a lost civilization, ideal for exploration and setting the scene.

Coliseum: Monumental arena related to warriors, combat, and ancient trials.

Volcano: A natural megastructure that alters the landscape and creates a dangerous and imposing area.

Prehistoric Cave: A custom underground formation to expand exploration.

Waterfall and Natural Bridge: Environmental formations that make the world feel older and wilder.

Fossils and Rock Formations: Remains of creatures and natural elements scattered throughout the terrain.

Custom Trees: Nine variants of giant and prehistoric trees that completely change the appearance of biomes. Chests and points of interest: Small reward structures integrated into the world.
