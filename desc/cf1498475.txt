PROJECT LUNA

Warning: This addon is still in development, but it is already very stable and has minimal bugs. If you find any bugs, please report them to me through the link below.

Project Luna is a Minecraft Bedrock addon focused on expanding the gameplay experience through new mechanics, entities, biomes, weapons, and structures all while keeping the vanilla feel intact. From a soul-based trading system to legendary weapons with unique skills, Luna aims to add depth and replayability to your world. The addon is actively developed and updated regularly, either monthly or weekly depending on the size of the update.

This addon features a soul collection system, passive player skills, custom entities, unique biomes, epic and legendary weapons with attack patterns and skills, craftable armor sets, and procedurally generated structures. More content is continuously being added with each update.

Special thanks to Expler HD for helping create this addon.
