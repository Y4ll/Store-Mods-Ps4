Luminous Dreams is being reborn with v1.0, embracing a unique style designed to make your world feel warm and welcoming. In the past, Luminous Dreams aimed for realism, but that approach never truly captured our vision, it just didnt feel right in Minecraft. Now, weve reimagined nearly every aspect of the pack to give it its own distinct identity.

Luminous Dreams is a stylized deferred graphics pack for Minecraft Bedrock, designed to bring warmth, depth, and character to your world. Moving beyond the pursuit of realism, it embraces a unique visual identity with soft lighting, glowing atmospheres, and rich environmental detailcrafted to feel truly at home in Minecraft.
