Real Money USD | US Dollar Currency Addon

Bring the world's most iconic currency into your Minecraft world! This addon adds 7 realistic US Dollar denominations from $1 to $100 that you can use to build a professional economy.

Perfect for city roleplay, global economy servers, or just flexing your billionaire status in multiplayer!

Visual & Feel

- 7 Authentic Denominations : Features realistic USD textures for $1, $2, $5, $10, $20, $50, and $100.

- High Quality Textures : High quality hyper-detailed textures for all money denominations that look professional in your hand.

- Holding Animations : Holding and using your money feels dynamic and natural in first and third-person views.

- Realistic Sound Effects : Custom paper crisp sounds added for a satisfying money counting experience.

Money Mechanics

- Spawnable Entities : All money can be spawned as entities for immersive visual roleplay.

- Stackable Money : All money is fully stackable. Perfect for building massive money piles in your vaults!

- Easy Interaction : Once spawned, just tap and hold or right-click to pick up your money!

Gamemode Ready

- Survival Mode : Craft or exchange your money using unique survival recipes!

Recipe: 1x Emerald + 1x Paper = $1

Recipe: 2x Emeralds + 1x Paper = $2

Recipe: 5x Emeralds + 1x Paper = $5

Upgrading Your Money (Combine)

Got a wallet full of small money? Combine them into larger denominations!

- Example: 2x $10 = $20 | 2x $50 = $100.

Breaking Big Money (Small Change)

Need small change for smaller transactions? Break your big money down easily.

- Example: 1x $100 = 2x $50 | 1x $10 = 2x $5.

- Creative Mode : All money denominations are easily accessible in the Items tab.

Find them in the Items tab.

Technical Deets

- Plug & Play: No "Experimental Toggles" required!

- Universal Compatibility: Works on Singleplayer, Multiplayer, Realms, and Dedicated Servers.

- COMPATIBILITY WARNING: This addon is NOT compatible with other Real Money Addons!

Quick Install

- Grab the .mcaddon file.

- Open it, and Minecraft does the rest.

- Enable Behavior + Resource Packs in your world settings.

- Start your global empire!
