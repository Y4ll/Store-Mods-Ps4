Visual Aspects is a resource pack, that improves Minecraft's visuals while mostly maintaining a Vanilla feel.

Features:

- Costum Totem

- Green Hearts, Salad Hunger Indicators

- Full Grass for normal grass, snow grass and paths and pink glass having a similar color to cherry wood

- Costum main menu panorama

- Costum Textures for Diamond Tools and Armor

- Extensions for the pack, changing slight stuff (available on my Discord Server)

For now that's it, but the pack will get lots more updates. Join my Discord Server to suggest new features or report bugs!

Follow my WhatsApp Channel to get exclusive access to Sneak Peeks and Images of upcoming updates.
