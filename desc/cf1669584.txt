How to use this Add-On

- Look in the recipe book to find what you need to craft Wolverines mask! Wear it like a helmet to become Wolverine. You can also craft it into Wolverine Mask - Player which lets you keep your skin though it doesn't have all of the same visual effects.

- While you are Wolverine you have a few "passive" abilities:

- You have a double health bar. When the first is reduced, you become an "adamantium skeleton", but you can heal yourself to return to normal.

- Mobs drop "foot-print particles" so that Wolverine can "track" them (it doesn't do anything special mechanically, just visuals).

- The Claw item has a few different functions:

- Select it to play the unsheath animation.

- You are given a bit of a strength boost while holding the claws and mobs you attack are damaged over time for a few seconds.

- Sneak while holding the claws to "block" which can deflect arrows.

- Right-click while holding the claws to insta-heal.
