IF YOU WANT TO ADD MONEY FOR ANY PLAYER JUST USE THE SCOREBOARD CALLED "MONEY", EXAMPLES:

/scoreboard players add <player> money <quantity>

/scoreboard players add @s money 1000

This addon brings a whole new level of immersion to Minecraft! It adds a fully functional cellphone with 18 different apps, ranging from useful tools to a complete economy system for your world or server.

Some initial screenshots

Main Features:

- Integrated economy: earn, transfer, and invest money directly through the phone.

- Customization: change background colors and phone cases to make it unique.

- Communication: send private messages, create groups, or even share posts like on social media.

- Entertainment & utility: listen to records with Craftfy, save coordinates with Noteblock, take selfies with the Camera, or bet against other players with Coinflip.

- Market & shops: buy and sell items in Saborreia (player shops) or send items to any player without being close.

- Teleport system: create teleport points with Waypoint or request TPs directly to another player with the Teleporter.

- Mini-games inside the phone: challenge your friends in Digitonildo (typing speed game) and bet real server money!

- Automatic investments: invest your balance with Bicraft and earn profits while you play.

- Smart management: organize notes, store extra items in the Backpack, and configure languages or notifications in Settings.

Example of Included Apps:

- Customize: personalize your phone.

- Craftfy: listen to all Minecraft discs.

- Camera: place a camera and see yourself in third person.

- Saborreia: buy and sell items with other players.

- Craftcord: create groups and send private messages.

- Waypoint: teleport to marked locations.

- Bank: check balance and make transfers.

- Digitonildo: typing game with money bets.

- Backpack: store extra items.

- Bicraft: automatic investment system.
