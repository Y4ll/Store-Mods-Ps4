Simple Gravestones does what it says. It simply adds gravestones.

A gravestone will spawn in the nearest open space wherever you die, it will also put a message in chat saying where you died. This can be toggled off with the command /ae_sg:toggle.

Once you find your gravestone, it will have your name above it and will look different depending on the dimension.

Then, simply right-click, or break your gravestone to recieve your items and experience back.

You can also craft each type of gravestone for decorational purposes.

No experimental options needed.

Works for 26.0 and above.
