Recreates OptiFine's Better Grass (Fast mode) for Minecraft Bedrock no mods, no external assets required. Every listed block wraps its top texture seamlessly around all sides.

Affected blocks

- Grass block

- Snowy grass block

- Dirt path

- Podzol

- Mycelium

- Warped nylium

- Crimson nylium

Subpacks choose your style

- Default faithful biome tints, matches vanilla color grading

- Bloomy vivid, saturated greens for a vibrant lush look, with a bright orange for Dappled Forests

- Autumn warm golden-olive tones for a fall season aesthetic

- Bare Bones Style flat grass in the trailer art style. Sides follow each biome's grass tint, so they always match the tops. Built to sit on top of Bare Bones-style packs, and works standalone too.

Compatibility

- No mods or external assets required

- Stacks with other texture packs

- Requires Minecraft Bedrock 1.16.0 or higher
