CRAFTABLE RARE ITEMS

Rare Minecraft items are now craftable with balanced survival recipes.

About This Pack

Craftable Rare Items expands survival crafting by adding recipes for valuable items that are normally obtained from rare loot, structures, mobs, exploration, or special events. The recipes are designed to stay useful without making rare items completely free.

Main Features

- 28 unique rare and normally non-craftable vanilla items can be crafted.

- 29 recipe definitions are included, with an additional fallback recipe for the Trident.

- Recipes use vanilla Minecraft ingredients and the normal Crafting Table.

- Includes combat, exploration, End, Nether, Trial Chamber, archaeology, music, nature and utility items.

- No custom replacement items are required for the recipe results.
