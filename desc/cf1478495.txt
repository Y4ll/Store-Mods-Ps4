Mechanics

The main mechanic of the addon is to add a bleeding system to Minecraft. This gives the game more realism because you can bleed out and die if you do not heal in time or if you do not have enough health to withstand the bleeding damage. This mechanic does not only apply to the player, but also to all Minecraft entities and entities from other addons.

What you should know is that the duration of the bleeding effect depends heavily on the amount of damage you receive. Receiving weak hits is not the same as receiving a very powerful hit.

The first bleeding case activates after receiving three weak hits in a row. In this case, the bleeding will last 15 seconds.

The second case occurs if you receive a hit dealing more than 4 damage but less than 9 damage. This creates a 60% chance of triggering bleeding from a single hit, activating the bleeding effect for 25 seconds, which has a high probability of becoming lethal and causing death by bleeding out.

The third case, and the worst one, happens if you receive more than 8 damage. This immediately activates bleeding with just one hit, lasting 35 seconds, making it almost certain death if it is not treated immediately.

To cure the bleeding status, there are several methods. The main one is using a bandage, which will give you regeneration and instantly remove the bleeding effect.

Another way is to heal yourself using any item that grants the regeneration effect, such as potions, golden apples, or items from other addons that provide the same benefit.

While you are in the bleeding state, blood will periodically drop onto the ground, and these blood stains will disappear after some time. This also happens when you die or when an entity dies, as long as the entity is not an undead mob.

Additionally, when you are hit by any mob through melee attacks or projectiles, blood particles will appear. This effect also applies to Minecraft mobs and mobs from other addons.
