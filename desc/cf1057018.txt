Preview 1.21.30.22 is required for wavy water!

You cannot use the stable version.

Experimental Toggles

- You can find the toggle in World Settings under the Game section.

- This toggle must be turned on:

- Render Dragon Features for Creators

- After entering your world, go to video settings. Scroll down to the bottom and set Graphics Mode to Deferred Technical Preview.

Lighting & Atmospherics

^ Sunrise at around 6:00 am in-game time.

^ Daytime at around 10:00 am in-game time.

^ Sunset (6:00 pm). Block surfaces facing the sun all have a golden tint to them. Light also reflects on water.

^ Nighttime.

Main Features

^ Some hostile mobs have glowing eyes. Charged Creepers also have a glowing charge effect.

^ Added volumetric point lights to some blocks.
