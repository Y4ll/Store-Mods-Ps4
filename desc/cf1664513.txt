Description

The Stalkers+ is a Minecraft Bedrock horror add-on designed to make you feel like you are never truly alone.

Different Stalkers can appear through a variety of creepy and unpredictable events. Some silently watch from a distance, peek through windows, hide around corners, appear near doors, or stalk you from the darkness. Others are far more dangerous and may begin hunting or chasing you.

The add-on includes multiple unique Stalkers, custom models, textures, animations, sounds, behaviors, and scripted horror encounters.

Features include:

Multiple Stalker entities

Random stalking events

Window and door encounters

Distant and close-up appearances

Cave encounters

Chase and hunting events

Creepy ambient encounters

Custom animations and models

Custom sounds and effects

Special event commands

Unpredictable horror gameplay

You never know which Stalker is watchingor when the next encounter will begin.
