Is an addon which gives you the possibility to make a Christmas tree to place it in your home, and that's not all, with the passage of time it will leave you gifts with incredible prizes for your survival, add it to your world and play!.

Version 1.1 | Bedrock 1.21.55

Update v1.3

ko-fi.com/realtern

Tree

Craft your Christmas tree that will take 20 real minutes to grow, or you can speed up the process by giving it bone meal. Gifts will start appearing every 5 or 10 minutes and will disappear after 20 minutes in your world. Christmas trees won't produce gifts if another one is nearby within a radius of 20 blocks.

Wrap your gift to place it in your friends' tree. More crafting for gifts and extra content will be included coming soon.
