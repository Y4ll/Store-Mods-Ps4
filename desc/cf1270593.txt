Hulk Add-on full description:

Gamma Block:

In your Minecraft world, gamma blocks will be generated in the mesa/badlands biomes.

<figure class="image"></figure>

The blocks are compatible with any RTX pack.

<figure class="image"></figure>

You can obtain the gamma sphere by heating the gamma block from the blast furnace.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Using this recipe you will be able to make the Gamma Orb.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Transform into the Hulk:

With the Gamma Orb you can use it to create Hulk transformations.

Hulk transformations are made from the stone cutter:

<figure class="image"></figure>

To transform into the Hulk you must place the item in the head slot.

<figure class="image"></figure>

When you transform into the Hulk you will be given an item with a red bordered icon, as you crouch down and interact with the item an interface will open allowing you to select an ability for the Hulk or change the game camera.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Controls:

Combat:

When you transform into the Hulk you will be given an item with a fist icon, while you have it in your main hand interact with the item or use the attack button to attack.

You can also place the combat item in the second hand and you can be in combat mode and attack using the attack button in any slot.

<figure class="image"></figure>

When you perform 2 hits in a row, the third hit will push your enemies in the area.

Jumping and Attack

While in the air use the attack button to perform an attack on the ground.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Super Jump

Hold down the crouch button, while looking up press the jump button to perform a super jump.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Clap:

In your inventory you will be given an item called smash, keep pressing the crouch button and use the item to perform a super clap attack that will deal damage to your enemies.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Hulk Smash:

By using the smash item you will be able to perform an attack that will produce huge area damage.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Super Smash

The second item called Super Smash will perform a super attack that will produce a huge damage to enemies in an area of 16 blocks.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Rock throwing

In the skills item you can select the rock throwing skill.

<figure class="image"></figure>

While you have it selected use the item while you are on the ground to grab a rock. To throw it interact with the item, use the attack button or switch slots.

<figure class="image"></figure>

While touching the ground on gamma rocks you can throw gamma rocks which will increase the damage on impact.

<figure class="image"></figure>

<figure class="image"></figure>

Grab:

While using the skill item you can select the ability to grab entities, to grab an entity use the grab item while you have an entity nearby.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Enemies:

Gamma Dogs:

Gamma dogs will spawn in tabletop biomes in places where gamma stone blocks are generated and are completely aggressive creatures and will attack anyone in their path.

<figure class="image"></figure>

During the night they will fall asleep and are completely harmless as long as you do not attack them.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Features:

- Health: 120

- Damage: 16

- Mobility: 0.35

Abomination:

Will appear in tabletop biomes in places where gamma stone blocks are generated. It will be a completely difficult boss to defeat only if you face it in your Hulk form.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Perform different special attacks and when you defeat him, he will release a great loot.
