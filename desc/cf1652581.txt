Build the defence line

LBR Defence: Missile Launcher & Turrets adds a complete, craftable defence system to Minecraft Bedrock.

Deploy a missile launcher, load its bay, lock target coordinates through the fire-control console, and launch either a ballistic missile or a multi-warhead strike. Protect your base with an automated machine-gun turret, ammo boxes, and interceptor rockets.

Features

Craftable missile launcher with an interactive fire-control console

Ballistic missile for a single long-range strike

Multi-warhead missile with nine dispersed warheads

Coordinate targeting and launch countdown

Automated MG turret for hostile targets

Ammo box system with 500 rounds per box

Two interceptor-rocket tubes for anti-missile defence

Crafting recipes for every core item

Wrench retrieval system for deployed equipment

Compatible with the Trooper 4x4 and RXII Spacecraft modules

Full in-game localization: English, French, and Spanish

How to use

-

Craft a Missile Launcher at a crafting table.

-

Deploy it on clear ground.

-

Interact with the launcher to open the fire-control console.

-

Load a ballistic or multi-warhead missile.

-

Set the target X and Z coordinates.

-

Sneak and interact with the launcher to begin the launch sequence.

To arm the Machine Gun Turret, interact with it while holding an Ammo Box. Load interceptor rockets one at a time into its rocket tubes.

Requirements

Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.0 or newer

No external dependency required

Import the .mcaddon file, then enable the included packs in your world

Languages

English, French, and Spanish are supported for item names, interactions, menus, HUD messages, and chat feedback.
