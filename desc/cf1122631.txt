Forget boring 2D sprites. Weapons+ 3D Reloaded completely overhauls your Minecraft Bedrock combat experience with 18 massive, hand-crafted 3D custom weapons AND a complete 3D overhaul of all vanilla swords. This isn't just a texture packevery custom weapon comes with unique stats, lore descriptions, and game-changing scripted abilities that bring your battles to life.

Whether you are smashing enemies with the ground-shaking Worm Hammer, pulling souls with the Dread Knight's Scythe, zipping across the battlefield with the Cybersword, or just swinging a classic 3D Diamond Sword, this addon gives you the power to conquer your world.

Main Features

- Vanilla 3D Overhaul: Say goodbye to flat pixels! All standard Minecraft swords (Wood, Stone, Iron, Gold, Diamond, and Netherite) have been completely remodeled into high-quality 3D assets while keeping their classic feel and enchantments.

- High-Quality Custom Models: Every new hammer, scythe, axe, and sword is a detailed 3D model that looks epic in your hand.

- Scripted Abilities: Custom weapons feature game-changing passive and active skills like Lifesteal, Sonic Booms, Neon Dash, Bloodlust Sprints, and Death's Grasp.

- Lore System: Hold a custom weapon to reveal its hidden lore and abilities in your inventory.
