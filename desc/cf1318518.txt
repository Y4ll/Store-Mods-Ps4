Introduction to the Jigsaw Structures City Mod Pack

This is a Minecraft BE module pack based on the jigsaw puzzle structure, bringing naturally generated random cityscapes to the game. Its core mechanism was inspired by the introduction of jigsaw puzzles in the Minecraft creator's documentation.

When you install this expansion pack, your rural village will transform into a modern metropolis spanning over 200200 meters.

The city features interconnected roads and pedestrian walkways, diverse apartment complexes with complete interior designs, and specialized infrastructure.

It also refreshes some reward chests and eliminates monsters that previously hindered your urban development.

You will see its huge outline at the edge of the city.

Current Status

The module remains actively updated, with plans to introduce a new version featuring a decaying urban environment. You can add other modules to the base of this module, such as car zombies, to enrich your game experience.

The massive city structure may cause block generation delays during gameplay keep an eye out for this potential issue.
