PREMIUM X-RAY VISION

AUTHOR: GGSTUDIOS

Premium X-Ray Vision is an optimized Minecraft Bedrock addon designed to make underground exploration faster, clearer, and easier. It removes unnecessary terrain from your view and lets you quickly spot important resources, caves, and underground areas without having to dig through layers of blocks.

-

Clear Underground View reduces visual clutter so ores and caves are easier to locate.

-

Ore Visibility designed to keep important ores clearly visible in dark underground areas.

-

Invisible Terrain & Glass Support removes unnecessary blocks from your view while keeping the important parts visible.

Lightweight & Optimized designed to provide a smooth experience without unnecessary features or heavy systems.

IMPORTANT REQUIRED SETTINGS

For the addon to work correctly, you MUST enable Experiments and turn OFF Smooth Lighting.

1. ENABLE EXPERIMENTS

Before entering your world:

-

Open your Minecraft world settings.

-

Go to Experiments.

-

Turn ON the required Experiments available for the addon.

-

Apply the settings.

-

Enter the world.

Experiments are REQUIRED for the addon to function correctly.

2. TURN OFF SMOOTH LIGHTING VERY IMPORTANT

Go to:

Settings Video Smooth Lighting OFF

DO NOT leave Smooth Lighting ON.

When Smooth Lighting is enabled, underground ores may become very dark or black, especially in areas with little light. Turning Smooth Lighting OFF allows the colored ore textures to remain clearly visible.

Recommended setting:

Smooth Lighting: OFF

INSTALLATION

-

Download the Premium X-Ray Vision .mcaddon.

-

Import the addon into Minecraft.

-

Create a new world or open your existing world.

-

Activate the addon/Behavior Pack and its linked Resource Pack if Minecraft does not enable them automatically.

-

Enable the required Experiments.

-

Make sure Smooth Lighting is OFF.

-

Enter the world and enjoy X-Ray Vision + Night Vision.

IMPORTANT

For the best experience, use the addon with Smooth Lighting OFF. This is especially important when searching for ores underground.

The addon is designed to provide a clear, simple, and optimized X-Ray experience without unnecessary settings or complicated configuration.
