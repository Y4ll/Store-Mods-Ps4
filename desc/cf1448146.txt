Smart Crop Growth Addon Sneak Boost Farming System for Minecraft Bedrock 1.21+

Smart Crop Growth Addon is a lightweight Minecraft Bedrock survival utility addon that introduces an automatic crop acceleration system triggered by player movement. By simply sneaking near crops, players can stimulate nearby plants to grow naturally, creating a magical farming experience that feels powerful while remaining fully balanced for survival gameplay.

If you are looking for a Minecraft Bedrock crop growth addon, farming helper mod, bone meal alternative, or survival agriculture utility, this addon is designed to improve farming efficiency without breaking immersion or server balance.

What This Crop Growth Addon Does
When a player starts sneaking, the addon scans a small area around them.
Nearby crops with valid growth stages have a chance to advance by one stage.
Growth is applied naturally using block states instead of replacing blocks.
Only real growable crops are affected, while unsafe or non crop blocks are ignored.

Intelligent Crop Detection
The system dynamically detects crop growth states such as age, growth, or stage.
Works with vanilla crops and most custom crops that use standard Bedrock growth components.
Excludes blocks like fire, kelp, vines, and special blocks to prevent glitches.
Automatically stops when a crop reaches its maximum growth stage.

Visual Feedback
Classic green villager happy particles appear when a crop grows.
Players receive clear visual confirmation of successful growth.
The effect feels similar to magical fertilization without using bone meal.

Balanced Growth Logic
Growth chance is randomized for each crop to prevent instant full farms.
Works in a short radius around the player for natural progression.
Triggers only when the player begins sneaking, not every tick of movement.
Prevents farming abuse while still saving time and effort.

Performance Optimized and Server Safe
Uses a small scan radius for low CPU usage.
No block replacement or chunk editing.
No heavy loops or constant global scans.
Safe for SMP servers and Realms.
Script based system with no resource pack dependency.

How to Use
Join the world with the addon installed.
Plant crops as normal.
Sneak near your farm to activate growth.
Watch nearby crops grow over time.
Harvest and repeat for efficient farming.
