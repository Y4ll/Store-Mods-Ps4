This is an add-on that adds a huge amount of content from Minecraft Dungeons into your world! From tiny weapons to HUGE bosses that make it worth while. There's a quest for everyone! (REMEMBER TO DOWNLOAD BOTH BEH AND RES .MCPACKS FOR IT TO WORK!) Also looks GREAT if you have Vibrant Visuals!

Every boss: Every single boss is included, including the Arch-Illager and the Heart of Ender!

Boss behaviors:

The Corrupted Cauldron: This boss will summon a variety of mobs, and every once in a while will summon fire to shield itself!

The Nameless One: The Nameless One summons Clones, and Vanguards and will shoot volleys of orb shots. So better be good at dodging!

The Redstone Monstrosity: This monster shoots bursts of projectiles out of its mouth as a ranged attack, summons Redstone Cubes, and has a HUGE slam attack!

The Tempest Golem: The Tempest golem cant walk, sp its attacks are to summon entities and lightning, such as Squall Golems, and has a hefty melee attack if you get too close!

The Wretched Wraith: The Wretched Wraith summons frosty mobs and teleports if you get to close!

The Ancient Guardian: This sufFISHant fish shoots a monsterous lazer beam attack and summons Drowned, Guardians, and its own signature Biomine!

The Arch-Illager/Heart of Ender: On the first stage, The Arch-Illager shoots orb projectiles and summons Pillagers and Vindicators. Then, when you defeat him, he transforms into the Heart of Ender. I'll leave those attacks for surprise!

The Vengeful Heart of Ender: The final battle to prove your legend. The Vengeful Heart of Ender shoots hundreds of projectiles, and whenever he does, he summons defective shields to make the fight even more chaotic. It summons endermites, and has a ram attack! Also this is the only boss that plays the theme. FOR NOW.....

The mob roster: The add-on contains every enemy mob exept the Enchanter and Geomancer which will be added soon!

The mini-bosses: Every single mini-boss is included with a new and clean experience!

ENCHANTED MOBS COMING VERY SOON!!!!!!!

The items: There are 26 melee weapons, 5 ranged weapons, 46 armor sets, and 2 artifacts from Minecraft Dungeons and more to come!

The hunt begins:

A new way to progress through the game. You have to hunt mini-bosses, kill them, and get special item drops such as Necromancer Orbs and Redstone hearts! Crafting recipes are shown below.

The easiest recipe for the easiest boss. The Corrupted Cauldron: You use redstone and some cauldrons to craft it's spawn item.

The Redstone Monstrosity: Kill 9 Redstone Golems and collect their hearts. Redstone Golems are tough. So you better have some good gear!

The Arch-Illager: Kill the Nameless One, Redstone Monstrosity, and Corrupted Cauldron.

The Wretched Wraith: Kill nine Wraithes and/or Tower Wraithes. Tower creatures spawn in cold biomes such as Jagged Peaks.

The Tempest Golem: Kill 9 Squall Golems and collect their hearts. Squall Golems spawn in cold biomes as well.

The Vengeful Heart of Ender: You must kill every eye and The Heart of Ender to unlock the ultimate battle!

The Nameless One: You must kill 9 Necromancers and collect their orbs.

SOULS:

Craft 6 souls at a time with one Soul Sand block to fuel your Corrupted Beacons!
