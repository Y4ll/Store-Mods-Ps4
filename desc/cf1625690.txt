Update 2.0

Yeti Boss Update

Note: this update changes the pack UUIDs please uninstall the previous version before installing this one to avoid conflicts.

Natural Spawning

- Yeti (Phase 1) can now spawn naturally in snow/ice biomes

- Extremely rare: low spawn weight, capped at 1 per area keeps it feeling like a real boss encounter instead of a common mob

New Skills: Ice Spike & Charge Attack

- Ice Spike: summons rows of ice spikes shooting straight toward the player

- Charge Attack: the boss dashes at high speed to ram into the player

- Combo mechanic: Ice Spike is prioritized; if it's on cooldown, the boss switches to Charge Attack if the charge misses, Ice Spike's cooldown resets instantly so it follows up right away, creating a hard-to-dodge chain of attacks

- Applied to all 3 phases, with damage and cooldown scaling up with each phase's difficulty

Damage & Armor Balancing

- Synced max health values across phases (previously mismatched internally, which caused healing/skill-trigger thresholds based on HP% to fire at the wrong times)

- Smoothed out the melee damage curve so it scales up sensibly across all 3 phases

- Added an armor mechanic: the boss now reduces incoming player damage by 2535% (scaling per phase) making fights more about strategy rather than just a health-pool race

Bug Fixes

- Fixed a script error/crash that fired every time the boss died (all 3 phases)

- Fixed abnormal knockback behavior caused by an incorrect API parameter signature

- Guarded particle/entity-spawn calls against unloaded chunks to prevent the fight from freezing mid-combat

Thanks for supporting the addon! Feel free to leave feedback or bug reports in the comments.

1.0

The eternal frozen wastes finally have a ruler. The Yeti a legendary creature from the coldest peaks is no longer just another mob. This is a full-fledged boss fight, evolving through 3 distinct phases, each one a different beast with its own skill set, stronger and more brutal than the last.

3 Evolution Phases

YETI (Phase 1) starts things off with the basics: throwing ice balls, freezing the ground beneath your feet, a slowing roar, a ground-pound jump attack, self-healing when low on health, and summoning ice minions once its HP drops far enough.

MATURE YETI (Phase 2) faster, stronger, and packing a whole new arsenal: Frost Spike, Blizzard Rain, Glacial Wave, a Polar Vortex that drags players toward its center, and the ultimate Absolute Zero a massive area freeze unleashed when the boss is on the ropes.

ANCIENT YETI (Phase 3) the final form. It summons an elite army (Wither Skeletons, Husks, Strays, and its own Pet Boss), locks you down with Ice Chains, rains crystals from the sky with Crystal Barrage, and wraps itself in Frost Armor a shield that reflects 30% of any damage dealt to it back at the attacker.

Ice Spike & Charge Its Signature Moves

Across all 3 phases, the Yeti has two standout attacks with their own dedicated animations: Ice Spike the boss winds up and slams the ground, sending jagged zigzag rows of ice spikes tearing straight toward you; and Charge the boss rushes you down at terrifying speed, and if it whiffs, it immediately follows up with an Ice Spike so you never get a moment to breathe.

Frozen Gear Set

Defeat the Yeti to collect rare materials: Yeti Fur, Yeti Core, Frozen Heart forge the Frozen armor set (Chestplate/Helmet/Legging/Boot), then upgrade it into the Frozemeral Chestplate, Frozpeed Boot, and Frozether Legging. Wield the devastating Frozen Sword and Frozen Scythe, tempered from Ice Steel and Frozen Steel mined right from the ice caverns.
