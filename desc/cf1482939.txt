Effortless Building

Achievements Friendly & Survival Friendly
Lightweight Performance - Full Custom Block Support - 13 Languages
Build smarter, not harder.

Effortless Building is a Bedrock addon inspired by the iconic Java mod of the same name by Requioss. Instead of placing blocks one at a time, you pick a build mode, click two or three times, and entire structures appear: lines, walls, floors, spheres, cones, diagonal ramps, and more, all while consuming the correct blocks from your survival inventory. A live Coach teaches you the addon step by step, a Tutorial Book opens right in your world, and all of it speaks 13 languages.

New in v5

- Floating Menus: the menu, settings, palette, modifiers, clipboard and help now hang in the air in front of you. Look at a button and click it.

- Quick Switch Wheel: hold right-click with the Building Book, aim at a shape on the wheel and let go. Your shape changes without opening a menu.

- Customize Wheel: choose which shapes sit on the wheel, slot by slot, or go back to the automatic layout.

- Live Coach: 15 short lessons that teach the addon while you play. A card beside you shows one step at a time and waits until you have actually done it.

- Tutorial Book: hold it and it opens in front of you as a real book with pictures of every shape, modifier and tool. Right-click turns the page.

- 13 Languages: English, Espanol, Deutsch, Portugues, Nederlands, Bahasa Indonesia, Shqip, , , , , and , for the menus, the coach, the tutorial book and every message.

- Hold to repeat: hold the click on a number in any menu and it keeps counting, faster the longer you hold.

- Comfort settings: menu size, which side the coach card hangs on, menu sound volume, and an optional aiming cursor for touch screens.

- Your choice of menus: prefer the game's standard menus? Set Menu Style to Plain. Admins can switch everyone to plain menus at once.

14 Build Modes

Every mode works in both Survival and Creative. 2-step modes take two right-clicks (anchor then build). 3-step modes take three (anchor, XZ footprint, then height + build). Each mode has its own options on the right of the menu.

2-Step Modes

- Line - Straight or Bezier curve

- Wall - Filled or Hollow

- Floor - Filled or Hollow

- Circle - Filled/Hollow, Even or Uneven (ellipse)

- Sphere - Filled or Hollow

- Semicircle - Horizontal, Arch, or Dome orientation; Filled or Hollow

- Diagonal Line - Straight or Bezier curve

3-Step Modes

- Cube - Filled, Hollow, or Skeleton

- Cylinder - Filled/Hollow, Even or Uneven (ellipse)

- Cone - Filled or Hollow; set the axis to Diagonal to lean the tip any way you like

- Diagonal Wall - Filled or Hollow

- Slope Floor - Filled or Hollow

- Copy / Paste - 3-click region selection

Special

- Air Build - Single right-click to place one block in midair, no surface needed

Bezier curves (Line & Diagonal Line): right-click to add waypoints one by one, sneak+click to confirm; the addon traces a smooth 3D Catmull-Rom spline through all your points.

Starter Shapes: new players can start with just Line, Wall, Floor and Cube, and open the full list with + More Modes whenever they are ready.

The Building Book

Everything starts from the Building Book. Hold it and the action bar shows its controls.

- Right-click: open the menu

- Hold right-click: Quick Switch Wheel

- Left-click: Block Palette

- Sneak + Right-click: Settings

- Sneak + Left-click: Build Modifiers

- Sneak twice: turn your build mode off and back on

Prefer the palette on right-click? Turn on Swap Book Clicks in Settings > Controls.

The Menu

Open with right-click on the Building Book or /menu.

- Shapes on the left, with the active one lit in gold. Pin a shape to keep it near the top.
