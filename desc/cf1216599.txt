Treecapitators adds 6 new Treecapitator tools to your game.

These treecapitators break wood slower than axes, but they trigger a treecapitate effect.

They can also be used in the off-hand.

For the effect to activate, the log must be isolated. To break a 2x2 tree, you need to mine 4 blocks.

They can be crafted with Copper, Iron, Gold, Diamond, and Netherite

There is also an Enderon Treecapitator for if you are using my other add-ons

The Treecapitator takes durability damage for each log broken.

The Treecapitators destroy the leaves after breaking the tree. They do not take durability for the leaves.

Treecapitators can be enchanted with Efficiency, Unbreaking, and Mending
