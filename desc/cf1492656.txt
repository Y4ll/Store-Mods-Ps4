[h1]Holdfast[/h1]
[b]WWII armor, squads, and arms for Minecraft Bedrock.[/b]

Holdfast turns a Bedrock world into a living front. Spawn armor, call a squad, pick a side, and give orders over the radio. The name is the watch on the line: steel in the field, and a voice that keeps the unit together.

[h2]Armor[/h2]
[list]
[][b]M4 Sherman[/b] for the Allies and [b]Tiger I[/b] for the Axis
[]Fire, impact, and explosion effects
[*]Sherman and Tiger wrecks after a vehicle is destroyed
[/list]

[h2]Infantry[/h2]
[list]
[]American and German soldiers, plus squads for both sides
[]Paratrooper, medic, mortar, and flamethrower roles
[]Anti-tank support: bazooka and Panzerfaust
[]BAR and MG42 among the unit loadouts
[*]Combat voice lines and grenade warnings
[/list]

[h2]The command wire[/h2]
[list]
[]Choose a side: [b]Allies[/b] or [b]Axis[/b]
[]Commander radio for field orders
[]Follow, hold, rally, dismiss, and check squad status
[]Medic that can heal allies
[/list]

[h2]Player arsenal[/h2]
[list]
[]Colt M1911
[]Thompson M1A1
[]Mauser C96
[]Karabiner 98k
[]MP 40
[]StG 44
[]Combat shotgun
[]Entrenching tool
[]Mk 2 "pineapple" grenade and Stielhandgranate M24

[i]An AK-47 is also included as an extra weapon. It is post-war (1947), not a World War II firearm.[/i]

[h2]How to install[/h2]
[list]
[]Download the [b].mcaddon[/b] file
[]Open it with Minecraft Bedrock, or import it from Settings > Storage
[]Enable both the behavior pack and the resource pack on your world
[]Turn on [b]Experiments / Beta APIs (Script API)[/b] this addon uses scripts
[*]Minimum game version: about [b]1.21.80[/b]
[/list]
