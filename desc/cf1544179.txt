Compound V transforms Minecraft survival into a full superhuman arms race inspired by The Boys. Build a Vought research operation, manufacture unstable serums, discover powerful abilities, fight naturally spawning Supes, raid Vought facilities, unlock anti-Supe technology, and survive in a world where powers are dangerous, unfair, and never fully safe.

Players can become Supes through Compound V, experiment with Temp V, risk V1, copy powers through Targeted Compound V, or stay human and fight back using uranium weapons, Power Dampening Cuffs, Hazmat gear, firearms, and the T.K. Combat Suit.

Every power has its own playstyle, strengths, weaknesses, passive effects, ability system, counters, and risks.

GETTING STARTED

When a world is first created, the host chooses how new players receive powers.

Compound V Item

Every new player receives one vial of Compound V. Players can drink it whenever they are ready to receive a random permanent power.

Compound V Chance

Each new player has a chance to be contacted by Vought International. Selected players receive a special offer that they can accept or decline. Accepting the offer gives the player Compound V, while declining leaves them human.

No Powers

All players begin powerless. Compound V must be discovered, manufactured, given out by an administrator, or obtained through gameplay.

Origin Modes

Servers can also use origin-based starting rules. Depending on the server config, new players may receive a random origin, a possible origin choice, a direct choice, or no origin at all.

Origin modes include:

- Random Origin

- Potential Origin

- Choose Origin

- No Origin

Rise of Viltrum Compatibility

Compound V can detect the Rise of Viltrum bridge when it is installed. Viltrumites are rejected by Compound V, Temp V, V1, and Purge V instead of being converted into Supes. This keeps Viltrumites and Compound V users as separate origin paths.

Compatibility also supports Viltrumite-specific developer menu hooks, origin rules, and flight animation support.

The selected starting mode can be changed at any time through the Developer Menu.

When you first become a Viltrumite, you can choose how you want to control your powers. Use Ability Items for hotbar-based combat and fast combo chaining, or switch to the Crouch System for a cleaner hotbar where double-crouch cycles abilities and crouch+jump activates them. You can change your control style later with the Power Switch.

Being a Viltrumite means your body is the weapon. Viltrumites are proud warriors who rely on raw strength, speed, durability, and combat skill instead of cheap tools or protection. Because of this, Viltrumites cannot properly use normal weapons or wear normal armor. Using external equipment is seen as weak and dishonourable to the Viltrumite Empire, so players who try to rely on weapons or armor will be heavily punished and weakened.

Unlock brutal Viltrumite abilities as you progress, including Dash, Grab, Flight, Thunder Clap, Hand Stab, Head Crush, Ground Pound, Super Sonic, Conquerors Rage, and more. Fight with your body as the weapon, build Dominance EP, complete rank-up trials, survive powerful enemies, and prove that Earth belongs to the Empire.
