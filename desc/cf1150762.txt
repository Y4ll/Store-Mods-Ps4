Ever wanted Dynamic Light just like Java's Optifine? Well this add-on is for you because it makes every light source emit real light in your hand. This makes cave exploration much more realistic, in fact you won't have to no longer have to constantly place torches.

HOW TO MOVE THE TORCH TO THE OFFHAND IN AUTO MODE:

In automatic mode the object will automatically move to your second hand if the object is held in your first hand.

HOW TO MOVE THE TORCH TO THE OFFHAND IN MANUAL MODE:

To move the Torch to the offhand you have to execute a simple command:

/function handtorch

Obviously, to be able to move the torch to the offhand, you must have a Torch in your inventory.
The items that can be moved in the offhand are all types of torches and lanterns.
