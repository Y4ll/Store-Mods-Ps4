OF FPS Booster The Ultimate Performance Solution for Minecraft Bedrock!

Say goodbye to lag and hello to smooth gameplay. OF FPS Booster is a lightweight, high-performance optimization resource pack specifically designed to maximize your frame rate without losing the classic Minecraft feel. Whether you are on a high-end PC or a low-end mobile device, this pack is built to squeeze every bit of power out of your system.

Key Features

Render Dragon Optimized: Fully updated for the latest Minecraft versions and the Render Dragon engine.

Reduced Particle Load: Removes heavy particles and unnecessary animations that kill your FPS during combat and farming.

Clear Visuals: Optimized fog, skybox, and water rendering for a cleaner look and better visibility.

Faster Chunk Loading: Streamlined texture processing to help your world load faster and reduce stuttering.

PvP Ready: Minimalist UI and clear particles make this perfect for competitive play and Bedwars/Skywars.

Universal Support: Designed for Windows 10/11, Android, and iOS.
