Bosya's Japan Katana v4.0.5 - Vanilla Sword

Japan Katana is a Minecraft Bedrock resource pack that transforms all vanilla swords into beautifully crafted 3D Japanese katana models. Each sword tier features a unique and highly detailed design.

Looking for Actions & Stuff Support?

This pack does NOT support the Actions & Stuff animation pack! If you want custom animations, unique craftable katanas, and Actions & Stuff support, please download Bosya's Japan Katana - Support A&S instead!

Key Features

- Full 3D Katana Models: Transforms all 7 sword tiers (Wooden Netherite) into custom 3D katanas.

- Achievement Friendly: Only changes the visuals of the swords. No behavior pack is required, keeping your survival achievements fully enabled.

How to Apply

- Download the .mcpack file.

- Open the file to import it into Minecraft.

- Go to Settings Global Resources and activate Japan Katana v4.0.5.
