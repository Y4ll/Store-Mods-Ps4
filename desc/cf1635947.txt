Solscape reimagines Minecraft Bedrock's atmosphere with warm, golden-hour lighting that turns every sunrise and sunset into a cinematic moment built for both stunning visuals and smooth performance.

From glowing caves to hazy sunlit horizons, every detail is tuned to give your world a warmer, more immersive feel without sacrificing the vanilla charm you know.

Features:

- Warm dynamic skies with custom sunrise/sunset gradients

- Golden volumetric fog for a sun-drenched, hazy look

- Cinematic ACES color grading with warm white balance

- Glowing PBR ores with full MER support

- Lightweight and vanilla-friendly performance

Requirements:
Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.80+ with Vibrant Visuals / RenderDragon enabled

Installation:

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open with Minecraft or place in games/com.mojang/resource_packs

- Activate in Settings > Global Resources, move to top

- Enable Vibrant Visuals in Settings > Video

- Apply under World Settings > Resource Packs
