Have you ever wanted to spice up Minecraft with some meme action or simply have a few laughs ? Meme death does just that! By switching up the death screen in many different aspects, this pack makes dying in Minecraft a bit more "interesting" (for some :D). This pack was originally made by noobysheep_ for Java Edition and was ported by me to Bedrock Edition.

FEATURES

- 15 custom death messages

- 6 player death sounds (picked at random on death)

- New player death subtitle

- New death screen GUI text
