Ancient Age

Ancient Age brings the prehistoric world to Minecraft, introducing a diverse collection of dinosaurs and other prehistoric creatures that transform the world into a living ecosystem full of discovery, danger, and adventure.

From massive herbivores roaming the plains to dangerous predators hunting their prey, Ancient Age aims to bring prehistoric life into Minecraft while maintaining the visual style and gameplay feel that make Minecraft unique.

A World Full of Prehistoric Life

Ancient Age focuses primarily on dinosaurs, but the prehistoric world was home to far more than dinosaurs alone. The addon will feature a variety of creatures from different prehistoric periods, including terrestrial, aquatic, and flying species.

Each creature is designed to have its own characteristics and behavior, making encounters with prehistoric wildlife feel different depending on the species you encounter.

Explore a Living Prehistoric World

Whether you are exploring forests, plains, mountains, rivers, or other environments, prehistoric creatures can become part of your Minecraft world.

Some species are peaceful and will simply roam their surroundings, while others are territorial or aggressive, turning certain encounters into dangerous situations.

You will need to learn which creatures are safe to approach and which ones are better avoided.

More Than Just Dinosaurs

Ancient Age is intended to grow over time, with new prehistoric species, features, and improvements being added through future updates.

The goal is to create an ever-expanding collection of prehistoric life that gives players more reasons to explore and interact with their world.

An Expanding Project

Ancient Age is an ongoing project developed by Dungeons Estudio. We are continuously working on new creatures, mechanics, animations, models, and improvements to make the addon more complete with every update.

Our goal is to create a prehistoric experience that can be enjoyed by players who love dinosaurs, paleontology, exploration, and Minecraft.
