Apex Visuals is a cold and clean lighting pack for Minecraft Bedrock. Clear water, thin fog, and a sky that stays sharp instead of hazy.

What this changes

Water

Shallow water is clear enough to see the floor through it, with caustics moving across the sand. Deep ocean goes dark blue, picks up real wave movement and reflects the sky above it.

Fog

Fog is pulled way back compared to vanilla. Distant terrain stays readable instead of fading into a grey wall, which is the main reason this pack looks colder and cleaner than my others.

Sky and clouds

Deep blue daytime sky with hard cloud edges, so clouds read as separate shapes instead of one flat band across the horizon.

Forests

Denser shading through birch and oak canopies. Leaves cast proper shadow instead of glowing flat white at midday.

Cherry groves

Pink petal ground cover with a warm glow at sunset, sitting against the cooler tone of the rest of the pack.

Swamps and jungles

Soft mist low to the ground with sunlight breaking through the canopy in patches.

Villages at night

Lanterns and torches throw warm light onto the buildings and paths around them, so villages stay readable after dark without going flat.

Nether portals

Portals give off real light onto nearby blocks, with a deep starfield sky behind them at night.

Mushroom islands

Purple sky tones over the island, kept strong so the biome feels separate from everything around it.

Point lighting

Every light source gives off its own colored light instead of everything glowing the same orange.

- Redstone torch glows red

- Soul torch and soul lantern glow cyan

- Lantern glows warm orange

- Campfire glows orange

PBR

This pack comes with PBR, so blocks react to light with real depth and ores glow on their own underground.

PBR is also the heaviest part of any Vibrant Visuals pack, so it is not forced on you. It ships as a subpack, which means you can switch it on or off yourself. When you activate Apex Visuals in Global Resources, pick the version you want. Turn PBR off if your device runs hot or drops frames.

Requirements

THIS PACK IS FOR VIBRANT VISUALS ONLY
IT DOES NOT WORK WITH MB LOADER

Vibrant Visuals must be ON. This pack is built for Bedrock's Vibrant Visuals renderer. The pack will not do anything if it is off.

Where to turn it on: Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals

If that toggle is not there, your device or version does not support Vibrant Visuals yet.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Apex Visuals

- Pick the subpack you want. PBR on for the full look, PBR off for better performance.

- Turn on Settings > Video > Vibrant Visuals

- If the lighting still looks the same, leave the world and load it again once
