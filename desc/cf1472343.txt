Tigrex Visual Shader provides a warm horizon and a cold zenith. It features very natural and clean water, like in the age of dinosaurs, making it look very pure. It creates a dynamic atmosphere during sunrise and sunset, where you can see the Tigrex color style. This shader is inspired by Tigrex from the Monster Hunter Freedom Unite, and it was made to honor Tigrex, the king.
