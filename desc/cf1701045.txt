CRAFT YOUR FIRST UPGRADER

Everything starts at the crafting table. You need 4 cobblestone, 4 iron ingots and 1 redstone dust to craft one Upgrader.

- Cobblestone: place one in each of the four corners.

- Iron ingots: place one at the top, bottom, left and right.

- Redstone dust: place it in the center.

Craft it, use it, and open the roulette! No special currency is required your inventory items are your offer.

YOUR OFFER. YOUR REWARD. YOUR CHOICE.

- Select your offer: choose the item you want to risk from your inventory.

- Choose your reward: browse the catalog and pick the item you want to win.

- Adjust the quantities: check how your offer and reward affect the displayed chance.

- Spin the wheel: watch the animation and reveal your result!

In this example: the player offers 1 diamond for a chance to win 2 diamonds. The wheel shows a 50% success chance, and the VIEW RESULT button reveals the outcome.

Use MAX, quantity controls, 2 / 4 / 8 multipliers and the 30% / 50% / 70% presets to prepare your offer. Chances follow the total addon value of the offered items and selected reward, with a 90% maximum.

WATCH THE WHEEL SPIN

The moving pointer, chance ring and reveal animation bring each upgrade attempt to life. The GIF below shows an example roll at 50%.

One attempt, one result. Closing the interface does not reroll the attempt. Each new attempt uses an independent random roll.

VICTORY COLLECT YOUR PRIZE!

Win the roll and your offered items are consumed while the chosen reward is awarded. A green thumbs-up effect appears above the player, and the chat confirms the victory.

This example: a 1-diamond offer wins a 2-diamond reward. The chat shows the round details and confirms the inventory total after delivery.

Inventory full? Rewards that cannot fit remain pending for later collection when space becomes available.

DEFEAT THAT IS THE RISK!

If the roll fails, your offered items are consumed and you receive no reward. A red thumbs-down effect and a defeat message make the outcome clear.

The chat still contains messages from an earlier win. The red DEFEAT message identifies the latest attempt.

Every upgrade has a risk. A high chance does not guarantee a win, and previous results do not determine your next roll. Only offer items you are willing to lose.

MORE THAN JUST A ROULETTE

- Browse & search: a paginated grid with 54 items per page and item details.

- Favorites: keep your preferred rewards easy to find.

- Round history: review your recent attempts.

- Player rankings: compare wins, profit and unlikely victories.

- Enchanted books & potions: select supported enchantments and potion variants.

- Optional Skyblock selection: explore progression resources in a separate menu.

- Pending rewards: collect prizes later when you need more inventory space.

- Three languages: English, Portuguese and Spanish.
