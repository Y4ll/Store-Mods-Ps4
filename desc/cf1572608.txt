Ethereal Shaders transforms Minecraft Bedrock into a living, breathing world where every biome feels alive, every sunset tells a story, and every night sky pulls you in.

What is Ethereal?

Ethereal is a Bedrock-exclusive shader pack designed to bring cinematic atmosphere to your world without sacrificing performance. From misty river valleys to vibrant crimson forests, every environment feels uniquely alive like the world itself has a pulse.

Features

Cinematic Sunrises & Sunsets Warm golden light bleeds across the horizon, casting rich color into the sky and water around it.

Vibrant Biome Colors Lush greens, deep reds, and vivid foliage make every biome feel distinct and full of life.

Atmospheric Fog & Depth Soft mist rolls through forests and valleys, adding natural depth and immersion to every landscape.

Stunning Night Skies A deep, star-filled sky with softly lit clouds makes nighttime genuinely beautiful instead of something to survive.

Saturated & Dreamlike Visuals Colors are rich but never overwhelming striking the balance between stylized and natural.
