This addon is designed for Minecraft: Bedrock Edition. In this addon, I have recreated the following characters:

- Verity

- Falsity

- Cruelity

- Lovity

This version includes a total of 35 characters, with more planned for future updates.

The characters are not individually named. Instead, they are labeled as "VERITY" from Phase 1 through Phase 27 and Verity Fusion series (Verity fused with Minecraft mobs) from Phase 1 through Phase 8.
