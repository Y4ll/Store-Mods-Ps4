Update V1.7.6

The Shield Configuration Book lets you customize how the custom shields work in your world. From this menu, you can enable or disable all shield abilities, change general settings, or configure each shield separately.

All Shield Abilities
Turns all custom shield abilities ON or OFF.
If this option is OFF, no shield ability will activate, even if each individual shield is enabled.

Individual Shields
Lets you enable or disable each shield ability one by one.
This is useful if you only want some shields to have special powers.

General Options
Lets you configure the general behavior of the addon, such as durability, sounds, particles, and shield hit durability.

Introduction / Help
Shows basic information about how the configuration menu works.

Close
Closes the configuration menu.

General Options

Durability
Controls whether shields and other durability items lose durability when blocking, breaking blocks, or using durability-based actions.

Shield Hit Durability
Controls whether custom shields lose durability when they are used to hit entities.

Sounds
Enables or disables shield sounds, block sounds, and break sounds.

Particles
Enables or disables visual particles created by shield abilities.

Reset All Settings
Restores all shield settings back to their default values.

Individual Shield Options

Magma Shield
Burns enemies that hit your shield.

Dripstone Shield
Deals extra spike damage to enemies that attack you while blocking.

Lapis Shield
Drops XP when you block an attack. This shield is useful if you want to get extra experience while defending yourself.

Slime Shield
Adds stronger knockback while blocking, pushing enemies farther away.

TnT Shield
Damages enemies and creates an explosion particle when blocking attacks.

Mushroom Shield
Poisons enemies that hit your shield.

Glowstone Shield
Creates light around the player while the shield ability is active.

Wither Shield
Applies the wither effect to enemies that attack you.

Ender Shield
Teleports attackers upward after they hit your shield.

Shulker Shield
Makes attackers levitate when they hit your shield.

Lapis Shield

The Lapis Shield is a special shield focused on experience. When a player blocks an attack with this shield, it summons XP, allowing the player to gain extra experience while fighting or defending.
