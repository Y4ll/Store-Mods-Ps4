Smart Villager Addon

Smart Villager Addon transforms Minecraft Bedrock villagers into a more active and autonomous civilization. Each profession has unique behaviors, tools, workstation interactions, daily routines, and combat abilities.

Features

Universal Villager Behavior

All villagers can:

- Collect dropped items within their surroundings

- Store collected items in their internal inventory

- Deposit items into nearby chests and barrels

- Build village chests when storage is unavailable

- Help expand villages by placing beds and workstations

- Automatically synchronize their behavior when changing professions

Profession Behaviors

Farmer

- Tills farmland and plants crops

- Uses bone meal to accelerate crop growth

- Harvests and replants mature crops

- Plants flowers and tree saplings

- Produces bone meal through composting

- Breeds cows, sheep, chickens, and pigs

- Feeds baby animals to accelerate their growth

- Defends the village with an iron hoe

Butcher

- Hunts adult cows and pigs

- Collects meat and leather

- Uses smokers to cook collected meat

- Automatically gathers fuel and raw meat for cooking

Shepherd

- Starts with companion sheep

- Shears sheep for wool

- Dyes sheep into different colors

- Feeds sheep to regrow their wool

- Protects sheep from hostile predators

- Works with looms to produce carpets

Armorer

- Repairs damaged Iron Golems

- Provides defensive buffs to nearby villagers and players

- Works with anvils

- Can construct Iron Golems during dangerous situations

Weaponsmith

- Sharpens weapons using grindstones

- Uses a Goat Horn to rally villagers during attacks

- Provides Strength to nearby allies

- Fights hostile mobs using swords and axes

Fletcher

- Patrols the village perimeter

- Uses bows and crossbows against hostile mobs

- Can fire flaming arrows near fire sources

- Applies combat effects against different enemies

- Crafts arrows at fletching tables

- Practices archery using target blocks

Fisherman

- Detects nearby rivers, oceans, and ponds

- Autonomously fishes using a custom fishing rod

- Collects fish from water

- Feeds stray cats

- Cooks fish using campfires

- Restocks ponds with fish

Librarian

- Plants and harvests sugarcane

- Works with lecterns to create books and paper

- Provides beneficial effects to nearby villagers and players

- Removes harmful status effects from allies

Cleric

- Performs Zombie Villager curing rituals

- Uses Weakness potions and Golden Apples

- Provides defensive effects during village attacks

- Works with brewing stands

- Creates and uses combat and healing potions

Dynamic Profession Synchronization

Villagers are continuously monitored for profession changes. When a villager claims, changes, or loses a workstation, their equipment, tags, and profession-specific behavior are automatically updated.

This allows villagers to transition between professions without leaving behind old behavior or equipment.

Village Expansion

Villagers can actively contribute to the growth of their village by:

- Placing beds with valid clearance

- Building additional workstations

- Claiming newly placed workstations

- Creating storage containers

- Collecting and organizing resources

- Supporting natural village breeding

Minecraft Version

Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.0 or higher.

The addon uses the Bedrock Scripting API and requires Beta APIs to be enabled.
