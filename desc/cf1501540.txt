TC Star Visuals is a shader pack in development for Minecraft Bedrock/PE, designed to be fully compatible with Vibrant Visuals and take the game's graphics to a new level. Its aim is to redefine how worlds are perceived in Bedrock, offering a more immersive and realistic atmosphere.

Among its main features already available are:

Realistic water with dynamic reflections, adding greater depth and naturalness to rivers, seas, and lakes.

A revamped sun, abandoning the classic square shape for a more organic and immersive look.

Improved lighting on blocks and in nighttime environments, bringing every structure and landscape to life even in the dark.

The most exciting thing is that TC Star Visuals is just getting started. The project promises to add many more features in future versions: from more detailed skies and atmospheric effects to dynamic shadows and a unique visual style that will set it apart from other shaders available in the community.

The vision behind this shader is clear: to become one of the most complete and aesthetically pleasing options for those looking to transform their Minecraft PE experience, combining performance with visual beauty.
