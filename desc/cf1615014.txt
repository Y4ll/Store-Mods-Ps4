BSL Vibrant Visuals is my ocean pack. Deep blue water with real wave movement, the sun shimmering across the surface, and golden sunsets sitting on top of it all.

What this changes

Ocean

Open water goes deep blue and moves properly, with the sky reflecting off the surface as it does. This is where the pack spends most of its effort.

Sun on water

The sun shimmers across the surface in a moving trail. Sailing toward a sunset is the single best thing this pack does.

Shallow water

Clear enough to see the floor through it, so beaches and rivers do not turn into flat blue sheets.

Sunsets and dusk

Golden light through sunset that shifts into purple as it goes down. The two tones do not fight each other, they hand over.

Sky

Strong blue daytime sky with crisp cloud edges, so clouds read as separate shapes rather than one flat band.

Waving plants

Grass and leaves move in the wind instead of standing frozen.

Requirements

THIS PACK IS FOR VIBRANT VISUALS ONLY
IT DOES NOT WORK WITH MB LOADER

Vibrant Visuals must be ON. This pack is built for Bedrock's Vibrant Visuals renderer. The pack will not do anything if it is off.

Where to turn it on: Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals

If that toggle is not there, your device or version does not support Vibrant Visuals yet.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate BSL Vibrant Visuals

- Turn on Settings > Video > Vibrant Visuals

- If the lighting still looks the same, leave the world and load it again once

FAQ

The pack is not working. Nothing changed after I installed it.
I get this comment a lot, and almost every single time the reason is the same. Vibrant Visuals is turned off.

This pack only works when Vibrant Visuals is ON. That is written clearly at the top of this page. With it off, the pack loads fine but nothing on your screen changes, because there is no renderer for it to work with. The pack is not broken.

Go to Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals and switch it on. If you cannot find that option at all, your device or Minecraft version does not support it yet.

Please check this before commenting that the pack does not work.

Is this the real BSL Shaders on Bedrock?
No. It is my own pack, made from scratch, going for a similar look. BSL itself is a Java shaderpack and cannot run on Bedrock at all. See the note above.
