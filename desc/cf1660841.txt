Minecraft, But Breaking Blocks Spawns Mobs...

Breaking Blocks Spawns Mobs completely changes the way you play Minecraft. Every time you break a block, a mob can spawn, turning even simple tasks like mining, gathering wood, or building into a dangerous adventure. You never know what will appear after breaking the next block, so every action comes with a risk.

Whether you're mining deep underground, exploring the Nether, or simply collecting resources, you'll need to stay alert and be ready to fight whatever spawns. The mod adds a simple but chaotic twist to survival that makes ordinary gameplay much more unpredictable and challenging.

Break a block. Spawn a mob. Survive the chaos.
