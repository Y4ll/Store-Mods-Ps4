Ferous 3D Gun provides gun variants with realistic mechanisms.
This gun add-on does not only provide single guns, but also dual guns,
where you can shoot twice at the same time at your targets.

Note: Accuracy when using dual guns will decrease, and the damage will be multiplied by 2 but reduced by 10% for each ammo.

To craft a dual gun, you only need 2 single guns and combine them.
This also applies to the ammo.

Please understand if there are any bugs, as this add-on is still in development.
