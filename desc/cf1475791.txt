SAITAMA ADDON BECOME THE ONE-PUNCH HERO!

Supported Versions: Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.50+

Ever wanted to experience what it feels like to be Saitama?

This addon turns you into the legendary One-Punch Man with a complete combat system featuring cinematic attacks, devastating shockwaves, super-speed movement, afterimages, massive explosions, and a 5-level Power system.

Punch harder. Move faster. Destroy everything in your path.

Normal & Serious versions of every main attack

Skill cooldowns displayed on screen

Your hotbar becomes a dedicated Saitama Skill Bar, giving you quick access to every ability.

HOW TO BECOME SAITAMA

Forget complicated armor setups.

1 Craft the Saitama Glove

You need:

Craft the glove on a Crafting Table.

2 Activate the Glove

Hold the Saitama Glove and right-click / use it.

Your transformation begins instantly!

The addon automatically equips the Saitama suit, activates your passive powers and replaces your hotbar with the Skill Bar.

Your normal items are temporarily stored during transformation and restored when you return to normal.

SKILL SYSTEM

Every attack has its own cooldown, and the cooldown message appears directly on your screen.

Choose a skill from the hotbar and right-click / use it to activate the ability.

1. PUNCH / SERIOUS PUNCH

Normal Mode Punch

A fast, powerful strike that sends your target flying.

Aim at a mob and activate the skill to dash toward the target and punch it.

If no target is selected, Saitama can still perform the attack at his current position.

Serious Mode Serious Punch

Now things get serious.

Saitama performs a cinematic punch sequence while a devastating shockwave tears through the world in front of him.

Massive damage
Destructive shockwave
Breaks blocks along the attack path
Cinematic camera sequence
Extremely powerful single-target hit

At higher Power Levels, the shockwave becomes dramatically larger and reaches much farther.

2. CONSECUTIVE PUNCHES

Turn your enemy into a punching bag.

Normal Mode

Release a rapid barrage of consecutive punches with custom attack animations, particles and impact effects.

Serious Mode Consecutive Serious Punches

A much more violent version of the attack.

Dozens of rapid punches
Multiple afterimages around Saitama
Continuous impact effects
The attack can carve a destructive path through blocks
Becomes stronger with your Power Level

The higher your Power, the longer and more devastating the barrage becomes.

3. JUMP / SERIOUS JUMP

Saitama doesn't simply jump.

He launches himself.

Normal Jump

Charge up, launch forward in the direction you're looking, then create an explosive impact when you land.

High-speed launch
Landing explosion
Custom jump and landing animations

Serious Jump

Charge your power before launching into the sky.

When you hit the ground:

Massive explosion
Large impact crater
Powerful shockwave
Screen-shaking effects
Cinematic landing sequence

Higher Power Levels increase the landing explosion and crater size, making every landing more destructive.

SERIOUS MODE

Ready to stop holding back?

Use the Mode Switch skill to enter SERIOUS MODE.

Once activated:

Your sprint becomes dramatically faster
Running leaves Saitama afterimages behind you
Punches become Serious Punches
Consecutive Punches become Serious Series attacks
Jump becomes Serious Jump
Attack effects become larger and more intense

This is where the addon truly starts to feel like Saitama.

5 POWER LEVELS

Serious Mode contains a dedicated Power Level system.

Power progression:

20% 40% 60% 80% 100%

Increase your Power Level through the Power Level skill.

As your power rises:

Punch damage increases
Shockwave range increases
Destruction radius increases
Consecutive Punches become stronger
