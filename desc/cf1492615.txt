CRAFTING RECIPES

- Battery

Can be used to craft various devices

- Dual mini Speaker

Used to craft a speaker

- Universal Motherboard

Required to craft various machines

- Portable Saw

Can be used to (Saw) the abnormal corpses/body parts that the entity dismantles.

Also drops useful loots.

- Powerbank

Can be used to recharge your phone's flash only if it's fully empty.

- Mobile Phone

Just in case you lost it, you can always craft another except that it's quite resourceful to craft another.

So don't loose it!

- Speaker

A very useful device that distracts the entity instantly due to its very sensitive sounds which only affects the entity.

- Walkie-talkie

This isn't for calling but rather a device that lets you detect the entity nearby.

It plays a static sound if none was detected,it also plays a very weird noise from the entity if it's nearby.
