Ultra Dynamic Light Addon With Head Light + Offhand

Ultra Dynamic Light is a Minecraft Pocket Edition add-on that brings Java Edition style Dynamic Lights into your world.

This add-on is fully Achievement Friendly, which means your world achievements will remain enabled even after applying the add-on.

How to Activate

To use this add-on, go to your world settings and activate both:

Resource Pack

Behaviour Pack

After enabling both packs, the Dynamic Light system will start working in your world automatically.

Features

Hold any light source block or item in your hand to emit Dynamic Light around the player.

All Glowing items and blocks support offhand

Select item in hotbar and 2 time sneak to sweep items in offhand

Dropped light source items also emit Dynamic Light on the ground.

Dynamic Lights are also fully visible underwater, including in deep water areas, allowing players to explore oceans, caves, and underwater structures with clear lighting effects.

Includes a brand new special helmet called Head Light.

Equip the Head Light helmet on your players head to get a stronger and better light source while exploring caves, night areas, and dark places.

Enjoy smooth and immersive Dynamic Lighting in Minecraft Pocket Edition with achievement support! :::
