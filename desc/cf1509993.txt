Avanced Player Cameras

Give Minecraft Bedrock a whole new way to feel.

More Player Cameras is a Bedrock add-on that adds multiple custom camera modes for the player, letting you switch between new perspectives directly in-game. Whether you want a tactical top-down view, a cinematic orbit, a wider third-person look, or a fully detached free camera, this add-on gives you more control over how gameplay is framed.

Features

Multiple Playable Camera Modes
Instantly switch between several custom views designed for different gameplay styles.

Free Camera
Move a detached camera through the world and look around with more freedom.

Far Third Person
A cleaner, more distant third-person view for better awareness and style.

Over-Shoulder Right
A tighter action-focused shoulder camera for a more dramatic feel.

Eagle Top-Down
A tactical top-down camera that works well for exploration and overview.

Adventure Isometric
A diagonal camera inspired by action-adventure and ARPG-style games.

Side Runner
A side view camera that changes how movement and traversal feel.

Orbit Follow
A smooth moving orbit camera that gives gameplay a more cinematic presentation.

In-Game Menu
Open the camera menu with a stick and switch modes without leaving your world.
