- THE SIFT BIOMES -

[ - Singer Meadow- ]

The main biome, where the player appears alongside the singer, who can sing and dance. This biome is full of trees, floating islands, and ichor lakes.

[ - Singer Canyon- ]

Singer's Canyons is a mega-biome featuring unique flora and fauna.

[ - Pink Meadow - ]

The Pink Meadow is a biome that attempts to emulate the "pink biome" shown during Minecraft Live.

[ - Carapace - ]

The Carapace is a desert biome. It is filled with bulbs, large skeletal torsos, and piles of rocks.

[ -Siftium - ]

Siftium is a mineral added to the dimension! It has a tier equal to Netherite and is difficult to obtain and craft. If you wear the full set of armor, you will gain Sift effects!

[ -Sift Portal - ]

To enter the "The Sift" survival mode, you have to go to the Ancient City portal, Place note blocks and play them in harmony!

To enter via commands, execute "/the_sift:travel" to enter automatically.
