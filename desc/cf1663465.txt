This Kei Car Pack brings six small, colourful, and practical cars into Minecraft: the

- Copen

- Pixis Mega

- Thats

- N Box

- Every

- Move RS.

Each vehicle has its own personality, from tiny sporty cars to practical little city vans, With up to 12 bright and colourful options, there is plenty of variety for city builds, roleplay worlds, garages, and everyday driving. This pack is also designed to fit alongside my previous Kei Car Pack, giving you even more small cars to build your collection.

Five of the six cars can carry up to four players, making these tiny vehicles surprisingly useful for travelling with friends. The Copen is the exception, with a sporty two-seat layout and a fully working convertible roof. When a player gets inside, the roof can open to turn it into a proper little open-top roadster. The roof can then automatically close when the car is switched off or when it starts raining, adding a fun bit of functionality that makes the Copen stand out from the rest of the pack.

These cars have been specifically tuned for Minecraft city driving rather than ridiculous top speeds. Their smaller size and improved speed tuning make them easy to control through narrow streets, parking areas, neighbourhoods, and dense city builds. Every vehicle also includes a full 27-slot inventory, giving you plenty of storage for survival items, building materials, or anything else you want to bring along. Despite their tiny appearance, they are genuinely useful vehicles for everyday Minecraft gameplay.

Each vehicle uses approximately 300 cubes or fewer, which is extremely lightweight compared with many highly detailed vehicle addons. This allows the cars to keep their recognisable shapes and interiors without using unnecessary geometry, helping reduce the performance impact when several vehicles are present in the same world. Combined with their compact dimensions, colourful designs, practical storage and city-focused handling, this pack is made to offer a fun kei-car experience without unnecessarily weighing down your world.
