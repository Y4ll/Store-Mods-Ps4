Features

- Reload mechanics (& cancel reload)

- Weapon swapping (& mid reload if in danger)

- Crouching for extra accuracy when target is far

- Draw/equip cooldown mechanics

- Headshot mechanics

- Knife Mode (slash & stab mechanics too)

- Emotive eyes

- Smooth animations

- Radio chatter

- Accuracy & damage increases based on difficulty

- Dynamic death & hurt animations based on how and where the terrorist was killed (e.g backstab)

- Dynamic blood spatter and gore features

- Hard difficulty only: Ignores armor

- Hard difficulty only: Strafes side-to-side

Factions

Phoenix Connexion

"You are stupid to think you will win."

Weapons: AK-47 & Seal Knife

Health: 30

Gun Damage: 7 (uncrouch), 5 (crouch)

Melee Damage: 3 (slash), 7 (stab)

Origin: Russia

Naturally spawns in 1-3 groups.

Drops its items upon death if you have the Actual Guns 2 Add-on enabled.
