A sleek, minimal UI overhaul for Minecraft that gives every menu a clean, dark aesthetic. Dark UI+ replaces the default interface with a polished dark theme across all screens from the loading splash and chat window to the inventory, crafting table, and creative menu.

Features:

Deep charcoal and dark grey panels replace the default brown/beige UI

Consistent dark styling across inventory, crafting, and creative search screens

Clean, readable white text that pops against the darker backgrounds

Subtle borders and refined slot styling for a modern look

Fully compatible with vanilla gameplay purely a visual enhancement

Whether you're sorting through hundreds of building blocks in creative mode or chatting with friends on a server, Dark UI+ keeps your interface sleek and distraction-free so the world stays the star of the show.
