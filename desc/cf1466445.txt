Transformers: Optimus Prime Action Figure Addon

Bring the legendary leader of the Autobots into your Minecraft world! This is a fully functional, transforming Optimus Prime Action Figure thats ready for display.

Depicted as the noble and brave commander of the Autobots, you can now own this iconic leader as a high-quality action figure in your blocky world!

Trailer

Cool Features

- Authentic Transformation : Seamlessly switch this action figure between its powerful Robot Form and iconic Truck Form.

- Detailed Handheld Figure : Hold the Optimus Prime Coin and see a detailed action figure right in your hand!

- Epic Sound Effects : Features those classic transformation sounds for ultimate immersion.

- Survival Ready : No need for Creative mode. Craft him, spawn him, and complete your collection.

- Drop System : If the figure dies, it will drop the Optimus Prime Coin so you can spawn it back anytime!

How to Use

1. Summon & Transform

- Deployment: Use the Optimus Prime Coin on the ground. The figure will spawn and immediately transform into its Truck Form.

Instant Truck Form display upon spawning.

- Robot Mode: Want to see it stand tall? Just interact with the truck to trigger the transformation into Robot Form.

Interact to switch forms.

2. Drop System (Never Lose Your Item!)

Don't worry about your figure. If it dies, it will drop the Optimus Prime Coin so you can pick it up and use it again!

On death, the coin drops back for you to collect.

3. Holding the Figure

Check out the detail! The Optimus Prime Coin displays the actual action figure in your hand.

Technical Deets

Built for stability and ease of use, right out of the box.

- Plug & Play: No "Experimental Toggles" required!

- Universal Compatibility: Works perfectly on Singleplayer, Multiplayer, Realms, and Dedicated Servers.

- Grab the .mcaddon file.

- Open it, and Minecraft does the rest.

- Enable Behavior + Resource Packs in your world settings.
