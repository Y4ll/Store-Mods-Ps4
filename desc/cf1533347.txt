What is LiftBlocks?

LiftBlocks is the most complete elevator addon for Minecraft Bedrock. Whether you want a simple one-click teleport elevator for your survival base, a hidden secret elevator disguised as a bookshelf, or a fully physical platform that moves block by block in real time LiftBlocks has it all.

No redstone. No commands. Just elevators that work.

Key Features

- 8 elevator types including 4 hidden camouflaged variants

- Physical Platform Elevator moves block by block in real time

- Group Elevator transports all players simultaneously

- Ownership Wrench with full in-game UI no chat commands needed

- Public / Owner Only / Team permission system

- Unique particles, sounds and effects per elevator type

- On-screen floor indicator when traveling

- Balanced crafting recipes for survival mode

- Full multiplayer support

Physical Platform Elevator

The most realistic elevator in the addon. A platform block that physically moves block by block up and down a shaft in real time. Players standing on it ride along automatically.

How to set it up:

- Dig a vertical shaft

- Place the Platform block inside the shaft at the starting floor

- Place Control blocks on the wall beside the shaft, one per floor always one block above where the platform should stop

- Right-click any Control block to operate the elevator

How it works:

- If the platform is not at your floor right-click calls it to you

- If the platform is at your floor right-click sends it up to the next floor Sneak + right-click sends it down

Features:

- Mechanical sound during movement

- Ding sound on arrival

- Smoke particles during travel

- Movement blocked if path is obstructed

- Cannot be activated while already moving

Platform Recipe: Iron Ingot + Piston + Redstone Block (center) 1 platform

Control Block Recipe: Iron Ingot + Redstone + Gold Ingot (center) 2 controls
