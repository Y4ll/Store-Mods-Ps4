DO YOU WANT TO BE A GOD?
Tired of being weak? With Ultimate God Level Up, every action you take makes you more powerful. There are no limits. No caps. Just pure, infinite power.

HOW IT WORKS:

SOUL REAPER: Every mob you kill increases your STRENGTH level.

MASTER MINER: Every 2 blocks you mine, your HASTE level goes up.

DIVINE METABOLISM: Every time you eat, gain a permanent level of SPEED or JUMP BOOST.

IRON WILL: Killing Iron Golems has a chance to grant you permanent RESISTANCE.

EAGLE EYES: Eat 10 Carrots to unlock permanent NIGHT VISION.

DIVINE HEART SYSTEM: Your soul has evolved! Every 100 mobs you defeat, you will permanently gain 2 extra hearts of ABSORPTION.

INFERNAL DOMINION: Master the heat of the Nether! Every Blaze or Magma Cube you defeat permanently grants you FIRE RESISTANCE.

THE POWER OF THE UNIVERSE:
This mod is so powerful that your levels are tied to your current soul. If you die, you lose all your progress and power. Your path to godhood requires ultimate survival skills!
