Looking for a simple, powerful way to manage waypoints on your Minecraft server or Realm? Look no further! Easy Waypoints by Iced Fox Studios provides an intuitive, in-game compass menu for setting homes, custom spawns, and shareable coordinatesall without the hassle of commands.

-

One-Click Compass Menu: Right-click your compass to access Warp Home, Set Home, Delete Home, List Homes, Share Coordinates, Teleport to Spawn, and Set Spawn.

-

Lightning-Fast Cooldown: Built-in cooldown ensures smooth gameplay without spam.

-

ActionBar Hints: Contextual prompts guide new players through each feature.

-

Server & Realm-Ready: Fully compatible with multiplayer servers, Realms, and cross-playno extra plugins required.

-

Persistent Data: Homes and spawn points are saved per player, even after relog or death.

-

Perfect for Any World Type: Ideal for adventure maps, survival servers, RPG realms, and creative builds.
