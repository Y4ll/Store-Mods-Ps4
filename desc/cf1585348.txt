Lucky Block (Pink) gives you 7 random items.

You will receive a Lucky Block the first time you log in after installing this addon.
After that, you will receive a Lucky Block every 1 hour only.

This add-on is achievements friendly.
This add-on does not include player.json.
This add-on does not require experimental features.
If the add-on does not work, please report it on Discord.
