DeltaForce 3D Custom Vanilla Weapons

This resource pack replaces vanilla Minecraft sword items with custom 3D models, textures, and animations inspired by Delta Force / anime-style weapon designs. The vanilla held-item rendering is hidden and replaced with fully custom attachable models for a more immersive weapon-holding experience.

Features:

Custom 3D sword models with unique textures

Original draw, inspect, and melee animations

Custom sound effects for each weapon

Renamed weapons with unique identities:

Wooden Sword Mercy

Stone Sword Desmoulins' Edge

Iron Sword Cobra Vanguard

Diamond Sword Dark Star

Netherite Sword Creed

Compatibility:

Minecraft Bedrock Edition, minimum engine version 1.17.0

Enjoy a fresh new look for your favorite melee weapons in Minecraft!
