G-Flow Xray

A lightweight visual pack for Minecraft Bedrock that makes ores easier to spot

G-Flow Xray hides common stone-type blocks while keeping ores fully visible. Designed for miners who want to save time without breaking achievements or changing gameplay mechanics.

Features

Stone, deepslate, tuff, netherrack, blackstone, basalt, soul sand, soul soil, end stone rendered invisible

Gravel left vanilla

Surface blocks left vanilla

Select which ores to highlight

8 normal ores with thin natural-color outlines

8 deepslate ore variants with matching outlines

PBR support for all 16 ore textures (emissive glow under Vibrant Visuals)

Fullbright ambient override for VV mode

Glowing ores
