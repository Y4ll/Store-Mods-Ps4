CHOP OP DROPS

Break Logs. Get OP Loot. Become Overpowered!

About This Addon

Chop OP Drops transforms the way you collect resources in Minecraft! Break a supported log or wood block and instead of receiving the normal wood drop, you will receive 1 random OP item.

OP Rewards

Netherite Ingots
Totem of Undying
Elytra
Diamond Blocks
Netherite Blocks
Netherite Sword
Netherite Pickaxe
Netherite Axe
Enchanted Golden Apple
Beacon
Shulker Box

How It Works

- Find any supported tree or wood block.

- Break the log using any method.

- Normal wood drops are removed.

- Receive 1 random OP item!

Features

- One guaranteed OP reward per broken log

- Normal wood drops removed

- Multiple Overpowered rewards

- Simple and lightweight system

- Perfect for survival challenges and OP gameplay

- Designed for Minecraft Bedrock Edition
