Wheel of Creation: Combat Update

Tired of Minecraft combat boiling down to "whoever has the best armor wins"? It's time to change the rules!

Combat Update completely reinvents combat in Bedrock Edition. Now, every material has a strategic purpose in your inventory. Gold annihilates the undead, iron destroys skeletons, and a patient archer can pierce diamond armor. Gear up and think before you strike!

Core Features

- Tactical Quiver & Special Arrows: Store and quickly swap between 4 types of arrows mid-combat.

- Damage Specialization: Every weapon material is highly lethal against a specific enemy type (and weak against others!).

- Dynamic Spawning: Monsters can now spawn as "Tank" or "Agile" variants.

Unique Armor & Piercing: Armors now resist specific damage types (Gold = Magic, Obsidion = Explosions). Beware, arrows now pierce through armor!

Training System: Fight with wooden weapons! Every hit trains you, raising your critical strike chance up to a massive 65%.

Hidden Boss & New Materials: Forge legendary Obsidion and Mythril Bronze gear, or summon the fearsome "Johnny" to claim the devastating Berserker Totem.

Discover all mechanics, crafting recipes, and full details in the Official Documentation!
