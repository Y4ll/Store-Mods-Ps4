Better Clear Water removes the dark, murky texture of vanilla water and replaces it with a crystal-clear, ultra-transparent look. This utility pack completely overhauls how you view lakes, rivers, and deep oceans, giving you perfect visibility both from above and while diving.

Whether you are hunting for ocean monuments, building an underwater base, or just tired of blind swimming, this pack is an essential upgrade for your Bedrock world.

Benefits

- Crystal Clear Oceans: Grants maximum transparency. See all the way to the ocean floor without the dense blue fog obstructing your view.

- X-Ray Exploration: Spot Ocean Monuments, Shipwrecks, Ruined Portals, and Buried Treasure structures instantly from your boat.

- Underwater Clarity: Drastically reduces visual noise when diving, allowing you to fight Drowned or mine prismarine with perfect vision.

- Luxury Pools & Lakes: Perfect for builders. Your fountains, pools, and custom rivers will look clean, modern, and high-end.
