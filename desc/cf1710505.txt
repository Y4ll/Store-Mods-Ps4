Kaleidoscope End (Unoffical)

Requires Minecraft Version 26.3+

Port Permission
This add-on is an unofficial Bedrock port of Kaleidoscope End, originally developed for Minecraft Java Edition by BmtUltra.

The original Kaleidoscope End project is distributed under the MIT License, which permits modification and redistribution under its terms. Special thanks to the Kaleidoscope End team and the Kaleidoscope Cookery team for creating the original mods!

Kaleidoscope End expands Kaleidoscope Cookery with End-themed foods, crops, ingredients, archaeology, dragon cuisine, Shulker dishes, Endermite foods, teas, placeable meals, and special equipment.

Explore the End to discover and grow Dream Berries and Ender Mint, gather new materials from End creatures, investigate suspicious End blocks, and prepare dishes using ingredients from Chorus Plants, Shulkers, Endermites, and the Ender Dragon.

The original Java mod centers heavily on new End crops, Dragon cuisine, Shulker cuisine, and Chorus-based dishes, and those systems have been adapted for this Bedrock port. CurseForge

For recipes, farming information, archaeology, mob drops, special food effects, preparation instructions, and explanations of the add-ons features, check the End section of the in-game Kaleidoscope Cookery Guidebook.

Some Special Features

- Dream Berries grow as custom End vines and can be farmed after obtaining them.

- Ender Mint is a growable End crop with multiple growth stages.

- Custom vine sections break when they lose support rather than remaining suspended in the air.

- New drops are available from Endermites, Shulkers, and the Ender Dragon.

- Endermite meat can be cooked using normal heat sources as well as Cookery equipment.

- Dragon meat has multiple cooking methods.
