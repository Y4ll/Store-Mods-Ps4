# Night Prowler Horror Mod

**Night Prowler** is a horror-inspired Minecraft mod that brings a new level of fear and tension into the game.

Originally created for **Minecraft Java Edition by PamitaMC**, this terrifying experience has now been **ported to Bedrock Edition** (port author currently unknown), allowing players from both editions to face the same horrifying challenge.

---

## The Monster Who is the Night Prowler?

The **Night Prowler** is not just another hostile mob. It is a supernatural predator designed to embody fear itself. Its tall, twisted form, glowing white features, and sinister grin make it an unforgettable sight in the dark.

* It lurks in the shadows, waiting for the perfect moment to strike.

* It doesnt behave like a normal Minecraft creatureits **goal is to stalk and hunt you down**.

* Its mere presence changes the atmosphere, turning the quiet night into a heart-pounding experience.

---

## Behavior & Abilities

The Night Prowler is built to surprise, scare, and overwhelm players:

* **Spawning**: Appears naturally during the night or in very dark areas. Can also be summoned in certain conditions (such as through special items like the *Orb of Chaos* in Java-related modpacks).

* **Hunting Instincts**: Unlike passive mobs, it actively chases the player once detected. It moves with speed, stealth, and aggression.

* **Powers**:

* **Deadly Attacks** Deals heavy damage, forcing players to prepare for battle.

* **ECLIPSE Ability** A unique move where it summons a dark portal above itself, creating a truly horrifying encounter.

* **Psychological Pressure** Uses sudden appearances and chilling sound effects to build tension before the fight even begins.

---

## Survival Impact

Installing this mod completely changes how you approach nighttime in Minecraft:

* Exploring caves or the overworld at night becomes a deadly risk.

* Players must build **safe shelters** and keep areas **well lit** to survive.

* Going outside without preparation means facing the **Night Prowlers wrath**.

* Multiplayer servers become even more thrilling, as friends can warn each other, team up to fight, or panic together when the Prowler appears.

---

## Why Play Night Prowler?

* Adds a **new horror-survival layer** to Minecraft.

* Perfect for players who love thrill, suspense, and unexpected challenges.

* Great for **custom maps** and **multiplayer events** looking to add a creepy twist.

* Works especially well with **shaders and sound packs**, enhancing the terrifying atmosphere.
