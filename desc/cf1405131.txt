Arsenal adds a small but focused set of firearms to Minecraft Bedrock, designed to feel immersive while staying close to the vanilla style.

It includes pistols, SMGs, assault rifles, and a sniper rifle, each with its own ammo type, reload system, muzzle flash effects, and basic third-person holding animations.

Every item can be crafted using the vanilla crafting table. This addon does not modify player.json, item.json or player.entity.json, ensuring high compatibility with other addons.

Weapons use a simple ammo system:

- AWM Sniper Rifle uses Rifle Ammo

- M4A1, MAC-10, Thompson SMG, and UMP-45 use Assault Rifle Ammo

- Revolver uses Pistol Ammo
