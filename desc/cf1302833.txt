More Vanilla Weapons+ Complete is for players who like Minecraft's familiar progression but want more than the usual sword and axe. It adds melee, ranged, and defensive gear built for survival. Recipes feel familiar, and every weapon has a clear role.

A Bigger Vanilla Arsenal

Pick a weapon that matches the way you like to fight:

Katanas and Shortswords are quick options for players who prefer movement and fast attacks.

Battle Axes and Hammers trade speed for weight, powerful knockback, and control over enemies.

Maces reward committed attacks and well-timed falling strikes with heavy impact damage.

Halberds provide extra reach and a right-click attack for keeping dangerous mobs at a distance.

Sickles apply harmful effects and work well in longer fights.

Elite Shields block melee hits and projectiles from the off-hand, giving defensive players a real alternative to simply dealing more damage.

Boomerangs travel in a wide arc, strike targets, turn smoothly, and return to their owner.

Slingshots use matching ammunition, while the expanded Crossbows fire regular arrows and provide different bonuses based on their material.

Seven Familiar Material Tiers

Most families are available in Wood, Stone, Iron, Copper, Gold, Diamond, and Netherite. These are not simple recolors: durability, damage, speed, range, knockback, charge time, and special effects vary between materials.

Gold favors speed, Diamond offers reliable high-end gear, Netherite carries the strongest effects, and Copper fills the middle of the progression.

Abilities That Belong in Combat

Weapons have active effects when you land a hit and passive bonuses while equipped. Depending on the family and material, you may gain Speed, Strength, Resistance, Haste, Regeneration, or Absorption. Enemies can be slowed, weakened, poisoned, ignited, knocked back, or affected by Wither.

The abilities support each weapon: Hammers feel heavy, Shortswords reward aggression, and Shields depend on timing.

Clear Visual Feedback

Custom hit effects, trails, flashes, ground impacts, and material particles make attacks easier to read without covering the screen. Heavy weapons feel forceful, quick blades feel sharp, shields clearly show a block, and projectiles leave short, readable trails.

It still feels like Minecraft.

Made for Survival

Every main weapon has a crafting recipe and an organized Creative inventory category. Material tiers follow the familiar order:

Wood Stone Iron Copper Gold Diamond Netherite

Craft the gear in a normal world, test it in Creative, or build a loadout around your favorite material.

More Vanilla Weapons+ Complete adds variety where Minecraft combat needs it while keeping its crafting, progression, and visual language recognizable.
