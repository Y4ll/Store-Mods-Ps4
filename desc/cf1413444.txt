Hey everyone! I am back with another highly requested survival utility. If you play on multiplayer servers or Realms, you already know the constant fear of someone sneaking into your base, or a Creeper blowing up your chests while you are busy mining underground.

I wanted a proper way to keep an eye on my base and stay safe, so I created the RR CCTV Security System. I spent a lot of time writing custom scripts for this addon to make sure it feels like a real, working security network right inside vanilla Minecraft Bedrock.

What does this addon do?

This addon allows you to set up fully functional security cameras anywhere in your world up to a massive 150-block range. You can link these cameras to a portable monitor and view live feeds. But it is not just for looking around anymore; the cameras actually act like real security guards and warn you if danger is near your base.

Main Features

- Smart Background Monitoring: Your cameras are always watching. Even if you are not viewing the camera, it scans the area every 5 seconds. If it detects a hostile mob (like a Creeper, Zombie, or Enderman) near your base, it sends a real-time alert in your chat with a warning sound.

- Smart Alert Cooldown: To make sure your chat does not get spammed, I added a 10-second cooldown per mob. You only get the warnings when you actually need them.

- Custom Camera Names & Distance: You can name your cameras whatever you want (like Kitchen, Gate, or Farm). When you view the camera, its custom name and exactly how many meters away it is will show on your screen. The system also automatically sorts them so your nearest cameras show up first.

- Night Vision Mode: I added a custom green-tint night vision effect. Even if your base is pitch dark, your cameras will see everything perfectly.

- Zero Lag Switching & Tablet UI: Switching between your cameras is super smooth. There are no annoying loading times. You also don't need to craft weird custom items; your standard vanilla Compass turns into your monitoring tablet.

How to Setup Your Cameras (Quick Guide)

I made the setup process very simple so anyone can do it in survival mode without using commands.

- Step 1: Place a regular Armor Stand exactly where you want your camera to look.

- Step 2: Take a Name Tag and rename it using an Anvil. You must use the format "CCTV_YourName". For example, name it "CCTV_MainGate" or "CCTV_Farm".

- Step 3: Name the Armor Stand with that tag. The system will immediately register it as an active camera.

- Step 4: Hold a Compass in your hand, Sneak (Crouch), and Use it (Right Click on PC or Long Press on Mobile). This will open your monitor and let you cycle through your active cameras.
