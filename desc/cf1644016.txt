# Ultimate Starter Kit

**Ultimate Starter Kit** is a Minecraft Bedrock Edition addon that gives you a powerful and fully equipped start whenever you begin a new survival world.

Tired of spending hours collecting resources and crafting basic gear before you can actually start your adventure? This addon gives you a complete **Starter Kit Shulker Box** as soon as you spawn in a world for the first time.

The Shulker Box contains powerful **max-enchanted Netherite gear, weapons, tools, Elytra, Spear, Mace, food, Golden Apples, Ender Pearls, Firework Rockets, and other useful survival items**.

Instead of starting from scratch, jump straight into your Minecraft survival adventure with everything you need to explore, fight, mine, travel and survive.

## How It Works

Simply install and activate **Ultimate Starter Kit** in your Minecraft Bedrock world.

When a player spawns in the world for the first time, the addon automatically provides the Starter Kit Shulker Box.

Open the Shulker Box and you'll find your complete collection of powerful gear and survival supplies ready to use.

The goal is simple: **skip the boring early-game preparation and start your adventure fully equipped.**
