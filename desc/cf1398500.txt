Simple Visuals is a lightweight Minecraft Bedrock texture pack designed to enhance your game without changing its original style. This pack focuses on cleaner colors, sharper details, and a more polished overall lookwhile keeping Minecrafts classic charm fully intact.

With improved textures, subtle visual upgrades, and a smoother aesthetic, Simple Visuals makes your world feel fresher and more immersive, all without impacting performance. Its perfect for players who want a cleaner, more modern look without turning the game into something completely different.

Features:

-

Cleaner and simplified block textures

-

Enhanced color balance for a softer, modern feel

-

Polished UI elements for a smoother experience

-

100% performance-friendly

-

Stays faithful to the vanilla Minecraft style
