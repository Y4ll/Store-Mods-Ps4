Fiery Combat

A timing-based combat overhaul for Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

Fiery Combat replaces Minecraft's default combat with a more responsive and skill-based combat system focused on timing, positioning, and weapon handling. Attacks, defense, movement, and hit reactions are designed to make every encounter feel more deliberate and engaging.

Trailer

Core Features

-

Timing-Based Combat
Replace traditional spam attacks with a combat system built around attack timing and deliberate inputs.

-

Weapon-Based Combat
Different weapon types have their own characteristics, allowing swords, greatswords, gauntlets, maces, and other weapons to play differently

-

Poise System
Attacks apply impact to a target's poise. Taking enough impact can break poise and create an opening for stronger attacks.

-

Guard & Parry
Defend against incoming attacks with well-timed guards and parries. Good timing can turn an enemy's attack into an opportunity.

-

Clash System
Certain attacks can collide with each other, creating weapon clashes and rewarding players who manage their attacks and timing carefully.

-

Roll & Evasion
Use directional rolls to avoid attacks and reposition during combat, with a short invulnerability window to reward precise timing.

-

Player Settings
Customize combat behavior, attack input, camera options, visual indicators, and other gameplay preferences.

Designed for Skill-Based Combat

Fiery Combat is built around a simple principle:

Don't just attack. Read the fight.

Position yourself, manage your actions, recognize enemy openings, and choose the right response. Whether you're fighting mobs, challenging another player, or building your own combat style, every action should have a purpose.

Fiery Combat aims to make Minecraft combat feel more responsive, readable, and rewarding while keeping the system accessible to Bedrock players.
