Hello! The addon is just a modification of the original Guard Villagers mod for Java, adapting it to work in Bedrock!

Yes, there is an original page to consult, but Bedrock has some limitations that can affect this addon, so the description should be read to see the real experience with this addon.

Did you ever felt alone, without allies? Don't worry, the villagers now have guards to join your cause!

LET'S BEGIN!

Guards are villagers with weapons and armor that are trained to fight any threat to their village, mostly being monsters and illagers.

(They have free arms, a bandana, a light leather armor and sometimes a quiver.)

guards mostly appear with axes, swords, and crossbows as their main weapons.

And even shields and armor!

They spawn in squads depending on the size of the village (a small village= 3 or 4 guards) and also spawn randomly in villages (but once an squad spawns, there won't spawn another), they are the village's main combat force, so when the irongolem dies, there still is a surviving chance.

Guards can also pickup better equipment leaved on the ground

Example: better weapons, shields and better armor

Juggernaut

Juggernauts are like the heavy version of an normal melee guard, they are mostly found with tridents or diamond swords, also they have a bigger chance to spawn with iron armor

"But how do recruit more guards?"

Well, don't be sad.

Recruiting villagers is simple.

To recruit a melee guard, interact on a weaponsmith using an iron sword.

To recruit a ranged guard, interact on a flecher using an crossbow

To recruit a juggernaut, give to an melee guard a trident or an diamond sword.

The medic!

A Medic is a villager with alchemist/cleric characteristics. They throw regeneration potions on allies, such as guards, villagers, players, and golems.

To recruit a medic, give to a cleric a redstone block

Can i hire guards?

Yes, you can tame/hire guards by using emeralds.

Also, i recommend you to not hit guards, they are very emotional after being enlisted in the village's army.
