Medieval Structures Add-On

Transform your Minecraft Bedrock world with new medieval structures that generate naturally while you explore!

The Medieval Structures Add-On adds 8 unique handcrafted structures throughout the Overworld. Explore forests, plains, and other suitable biomes to discover medieval towers, buildings, ruins, and other constructions hidden throughout your world.

Every structure is designed to blend naturally with Minecraft's environment, making exploration more interesting without completely changing the vanilla experience.
