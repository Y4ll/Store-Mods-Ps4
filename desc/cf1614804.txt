Outline X-Ray Resource Pack

Welcome to the next generation of mining utility. Built for modern Minecraft Bedrock engines, the Outline X-Ray Resource Pack redefines how you find resources underground by providing crystal-clear ore visibility without sacrificing your spatial awareness.

Why You Should Use This Pack ?

Instead of the traditional X-Ray experience where the entire world becomes an unnavigable void, this pack offers a smarter, safer approach to mining:

- The Flaw of Standard X-Ray: Traditional packs make non-valuable blocks completely invisible. While great for spotting diamonds, this makes navigating ravines incredibly dangerous and increases your risk of mining straight into lava pools or falling down sheer drops.

- The Outline Solution: Rather than erasing common blocks like stone, deepslate, and netherrack entirely, this pack renders a distinct, clean outline around their borders.

- Perfect Spatial Awareness: You get a clear visual grid of the cave structures around you. You will know exactly where every block is located, allowing you to build bridges and navigate complex cave systems safely.

- Uncompromised Ore Hunting: Even with the structural outlines visible, you still retain perfect visibility of all valuable ores glowing in the distance.

Essential In-Game Settings -

To ensure the outlines render correctly and to prevent visual glitches, you must navigate to your Video Settings and configure the following before entering your world:

- Smooth Lighting OFF: This prevents the game from attempting to blend shadows across transparent blocks, which can cause rendering errors.

- Fancy Graphics OFF: This optimizes the block rendering engine so it prioritizes your outlines without interference from vanilla aesthetic features.

- Beautiful Skies OFF: This heavily reduces the background rendering load, keeping your frame rate stable when looking through hundreds of transparent blocks.

- No Experimental Toggles Needed: This pack functions strictly as a visual enhancement. It works perfectly straight out of the box without the need to enable Beta APIs or experimental toggles in your world settings.

Brightness and Lighting Notice:

Because of the specific rendering techniques used to draw the block borders, lighting works seamlessly with this pack installed:

- Default Fullbright Included: This pack now includes default fullbright (Night Vision) built directly in, functioning natively in patched MCPE, BetterRenderDragon, and MBLoader.

- No External Fullbright Shaders: You do not need to install external fullbright shaders or night vision packs. Stacking external lighting mods can corrupt the custom materials and cause outlines to fail.

- Help Me -

System Compatibility and Requirements:

Please read this carefully before installing - Because this pack utilizes advanced rendering techniques for the block borders, it is not compatible with standard, outdated vanilla render engines. Your client must meet the following specifications:

- Supported Versions: This pack exclusively supports Minecraft Bedrock/Pocket Edition versions 1.26.30 and above.

- Required Render Engines: To process the custom materials, fullbright, and outlines correctly, your game client must be running a Better Renderdragon setup, MaterialbinLoader, or a properly patched version of MCPE.

- Vanilla Limitations: If you attempt to run this on an unpatched or older version of the vanilla game, the outlines will not render, and all blocks will simply appear as solid textures.

Note:- If you are following all the instructions and getting black blocks, try removing the pack and reapplying it and restarting the game.
