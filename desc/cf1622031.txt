Survival Essentials Overhaul is the ultimate all-in-one survival package that combines the best survival add-ons into a single collection. It includes everything you need to enhance your survival experience, along with exclusive and unique add-ons that introduce new mechanics, features, and quality-of-life improvements. Every addition is carefully designed to make your Minecraft world feel more immersive, dynamic, and alive.

Survival Essentials Overhaul Features

- Health Bar View the health of mobs and bosses in real time with an RPG-style health bar.

- Dynamic Light Hold torches or other light sources to illuminate dark areas without placing them.

- Vein Miner & Auto Smelt Instantly mine entire ore veins, with compatible ores automatically smelted when mined.

- Tree Capitator Chop down an entire tree by breaking a single log, making wood collection much faster.

- Stat Boost Gain permanent or progressive stat upgrades to become stronger as you survive.

- FPS Boost Improve game performance with built-in optimizations for smoother gameplay and higher frame rates.
