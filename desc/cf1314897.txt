This addon adds an entity named Siren head into minecraft. It can be found in any taiga biome at night, making its presence know with Siren sounds.

This addon was made by Ramses, Sinnies, and A15_Producions!

(Model & spawn egg texture by Ramses, textures and animations by Sinnies, code by A15_Productions)

This addon is based on Siren Head, a creature created by [Trevor Henderson].

Siren head's description:

Health = 7800

Does 35 damage with its stomp attack

It can Break wood & leaves

It can also start to roar, causing every mob within its vicinity to be effected with the wither effect for 5 seconds.

When it dies, it will become its second stage where its enraged.

It will only have 200 health, but it is more of a challenge as it crawls towards its prey with faster speed.

There are a few ways of countering Siren heads attacks, but saying that would be to easy :)

We hope yall have fun with this addon!
