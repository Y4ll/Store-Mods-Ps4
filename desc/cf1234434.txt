Insta Farm Addon allows players to easily create farms and redstone mechanisms. Simply place the block, add materials and build farms - upgrade them for more efficiency!

Featuring over 40 farms and redstone contraptions! Everything is fully survival-friendly and can be easily configured using the in-game guidebook.

To add the required materials, simply keep them in your inventory! If it feels like too much, you can adjust the material percentage in the settings to reduce the required amount.
