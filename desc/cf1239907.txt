AD FPS Booster (REMADE)

AD FPS Booster is a resource pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition built to improve performance on all devices.
Whether you play on a phone, tablet, console, or low-end PC, this pack helps deliver higher FPS, smoother gameplay, and a more stable experience.

Performance & Optimization Core Highlights:

- Texture Optimization: Introduced a new texture management system that automatically balances visual fidelity with performance, ensuring you get the best look for your hardware without the lag.

- Optimized Models & Leaves: Replaced standard complex models with new, lightweight alternativesincluding simplified leaf renderingto provide an immediate and noticeable boost to FPS.

- Terrain Optimization: Overhauled terrain rendering logic to improve chunk loading speeds and reduce CPU overhead when traversing the world.

- Smart AI Particles Reduction(Beta State): Implemented a smarter particle culling system. Unnecessary or non-visible particles are now dynamically reduced, slashing render times without making the game feel empty.

- Visuals Optimization(Beta stage): Streamlined core visual shaders and materials. We've cut down on unneeded rendering calculations, achieving higher base FPS numbers while preserving the pack's signature look.

- UI Optimization: Improved the performance of UI elements and forms. Navigating menus and interacting with the game interface is now snappier, more responsive, and consumes less memory.

Performance Gains

- Low-end devices: Up to +70% FPS

- Mid-range devices: Around +3040% FPS

- High-end devices: Stable frame rates and fewer stutters

Actual results depend on your device and world size

How to Install

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it with Minecraft Bedrock Edition

- Go to Settings Global Resources and apply the pack

- Restart Minecraft for best results

Supported Platforms

- Android / iOS

- Windows 10 & 11 (Bedrock)

- Xbox / PlayStation (via file import)
