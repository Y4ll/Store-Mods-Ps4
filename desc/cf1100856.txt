Ender Dragon & Warden Drop

Dragon Scal

When you kill a dragon, it drops Dragon Scal.

Cave Star

Dropped by the warden you kill. It's used to craft Dragon Star.

Dragon Star

Combination of Dragon Scal, Endron Ingot & Cave Star.

Smelt Dragon Star into the blast furnace & you get Dragon Ingot to craft armor.
