TC X-Ray

TC X-Ray is an advanced survival-friendly X-Ray vision radar addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. It reveals concealed ores, hidden dungeons, monster spawners, trial chambers, and buried treasure vaults through solid terrain using customizable 3D highlight wireframes, directional laser beams, and distance holograms.

How to Use:

- Right-click (or tap interact on mobile) while holding the Mob Tracker to open the search modal.

- Enter any mob name/ID in the text box or choose quickly from over 75 categorized Minecraft mobs.

- Consumes 1 Diamond only when a matching mob is successfully located in your render distance (1024 blocks). If no matching mob is found, your Diamond is preserved.

- Projects a real-time laser beam tracking the mob's exact location through solid blocks for 10 minutes.

- Auto-Retargeting: When the targeted mob is eliminated or despawns, the tracker automatically locks onto the next nearest mob of the same type.

- Hand-Free Navigation: Keeps tracking actively even when switching items to weapons or tools.

How to Access the Menu and Controls

You can open the configuration dashboard and choose your scanning profiles in two different ways:

Method 1: Using In-Game Items (No Chat Required)

- Basic Glasses: Equip on head, then press [Sneak + Jump].

- Ore Compass: Right-click / interact while holding the item.

- Endgame Glasses: Equip on head, then press [Sneak + Jump].

- Mob Tracker: Right-click / interact while holding the item.

Method 2: Using Commands

Type the custom command in chat:

- /tc:xray: Opens the main settings dashboard.

- /tc:xray toggle: Quickly toggles the radar ON or OFF without opening the menu.
