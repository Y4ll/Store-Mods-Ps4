Warden Shader transforms your Minecraft world into a darker and more mysterious environment inspired by the atmosphere of the Warden. This addon adds a deep blue color tone to the sky and lighting, creating a cold and eerie feeling across the world.

The shader enhances sunlight with a soft glow effect and improves fog rendering, making distant landscapes look more cinematic and immersive. Mountains, forests, and oceans appear deeper and more dramatic, while the horizon fades smoothly into thick atmospheric fog.

Water surfaces look darker and more reflective, giving oceans and rivers a calm but mysterious appearance. The lighting system also adds stronger contrast between bright areas and shadows, making the environment feel more alive and atmospheric.

Warden Shader is perfect for players who want a moody, cinematic, and mysterious Minecraft experience while exploring the overworld.
