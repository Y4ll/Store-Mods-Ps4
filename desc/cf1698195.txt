Better Biome Villages adds brand new villages to your world. Each biome gets its own village style.

It adds villages to these biomes:

- Plains

- Forest

- Taiga

- Snowy

- Desert

- Savanna

- Jungle

- Swamp

- Badlands

- Cherry Grove

To find the nearest one, use /locate structure voxen:village_plains. For other biomes, change plains to the biome name.

Commands

These commands can be used to summon or build a village where you are standing:

- /function village/plains

- /function village/forest

- /function village/taiga

- /function village/snowy

- /function village/desert

- /function village/savanna

- /function village/jungle

- /function village/swamp

- /function village/badlands

- /function village/cherry
