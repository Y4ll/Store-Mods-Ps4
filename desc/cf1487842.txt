Fiena Visual Shade is a visual shader designed to create a comfortable and relaxing atmosphere. It focuses on soft, pleasant visuals rather than realistic lighting, making the world feel cozy and easy on the eyes.

The shader blends calm blue tones with the gentle warmth of sunlight to produce a balanced and soothing environment. Instead of aiming for realism, it enhances colors and lighting in a simple, stylized way that keeps the game looking vibrant while still feeling natural. The result is a smooth, dynamic visual style that makes exploring the world feel more comfortable and enjoyable.

This shader will enhance your visuals. It is equipped with PBR, ACE tone mapping, white balance temperature, and complex midtones.
