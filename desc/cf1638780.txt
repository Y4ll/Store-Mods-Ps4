Dirt Drops OP Items

Turn ordinary Dirt and Grass Blocks into valuable treasure with Dirt Drops OP Items by FleetMc Studio! Instead of only collecting dirt, every time you break a Dirt or Grass Block you'll receive random Minecraft loot that can completely change your survival adventure.

From useful early-game supplies to powerful late-game equipment, every block has the chance to reward you with something valuable. Dig your way through the world and discover hundreds of possible rewards hidden beneath your feet.

What Can Drop?

- Max Enchanted Armor.

- Powerful Weapons.

- Enchanted Tools.

- Totems of Undying.

- Elytra.

- Shulker Boxes.

- Diamonds & Diamond Blocks.

- Netherite Items.

- Rare Building Blocks.

- Useful Survival Resources.

- Food and Valuable Items.

- ...and hundreds of other random Minecraft items!

Features

- Breaking Dirt drops random Minecraft loot.

- Breaking Grass Blocks also drops random loot.

- Huge variety of possible rewards.

- Receive both common and extremely overpowered items.

- Perfect for Survival challenges and lucky worlds.

- Lightweight and optimized.

- Achievement Friendly.

- No Experimental Gameplay required.

How to Use

- Download and import the addon.

- Enable the Behavior Pack.

- Join your world.

- Break any Dirt or Grass Block.

- Collect your random rewards!

Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

- Achievement Friendly.

- No Experimental Gameplay Required.

- Singleplayer & Multiplayer Supported.
