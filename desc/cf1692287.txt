Realistic Debo Shader by GURJAR

A cinematic realistic resource pack designed for Minecraft Bedrock Edition (1.21.x 1.26.x).

Inspired by high-end realistic Minecraft screenshots, this pack focuses on natural colors, soft lighting response, clear water, and lush vegetation while keeping the classic Minecraft blocky feel.

Features:
Natural cinematic color grading (warm sunlight + cool sky)
Improved grass, dirt, stone, sand, logs & leaves
Clear shallow water with better transparency look
Soft large clouds & atmospheric sky
PBR materials (normals + MER) for Vibrant Visuals
Better foliage color variation
Clean and performance-friendly textures

How to get the best look:
1. Import the pack
2. Enable Vibrant Visuals (or RTX if available)
3. Increase shadow quality, volumetric fog & reflections moderately
4. Place this pack high in your active resource packs list

Note: Bedrock Edition does not support full custom shaders like Java. This pack enhances textures + PBR materials so Vibrant Visuals can look closer to the cinematic reference style.
