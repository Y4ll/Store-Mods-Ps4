Commands

/infuse:cooldownsparkreset - [ADMIN COMMAND] Refreshes cooldowns for sparks

/infuse:adminconfig - [ADMIN COMMAND] Allows to change global stuff like give a effect on spawn

/infuse:spark1 & /infuse:spark2 - Ment for macros to use to activate abilities

/infuse:drain1 & /infuse:drain2 - For withdrawing your effects when not in use

/infuse:playerconfig - Allows to change some client only stuff

Method 2 - Crouch use Sword [Slot One] & Axe [Slot Two]

Extra Items

- Ability Activator. This is used to activated the Spark ability of effects. Crafted with a piece of dirt.

- Extractor. Will help to get equipped Effects/Sparks back to items. Crafted with 2 piece's of dirt.
