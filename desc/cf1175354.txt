Never lose items on death again! Let your Gravestone hold them for you until you return. Now with Void protection!

When you die a Gravestone will appear in your place, collecting all your items and half your XP for you.

You will be given a Grave Finder that will tell you the Cords of your Gravestone in any Dimention.

When you find your Gravestone, only you can access it, the Compass will give removed from your inventory.

This add-on DOES NOT require experimental features.

The player.json IS NOT used in this add-on
