How does it work?

This addon works by infinitely generating the backrooms below your world.

How do I get to the backrooms?

You can get to the backrooms by the following:

- Suffocating

- Flying into a wall

- Anvil

How do I survive?

Surviving in the backrooms will be difficult, you need to worry about hunger and mobs.

Floors:

Level 0:

Found by entering the backrooms in the overworld

Escape by finding exit doors

Level 1:

Found by entering the backrooms in the nether

Escape by finding exit doors

The End is Near:

Found by entering the backrooms in the end

Shelfs in "The end is near" may have vhs tapes, find the red one and put it in a computer to escape. But be careful to not be noticed...

Items:

Almond Water:

Can be found in filing cabinets or iron barrels. Drinking will fill you some hunger

New Music Disc: Burning

This music disc will play the backrooms song Its just a burning memory by the caretaker You can play it here,

can be found in filing cabinets

Shelfs in "The end is near" may have vhs tapes, find the red one and put it in a computer to escape

Entities:

Bactereia:

Common entity

Simply chases down and harms the player

Facling:

Common entity

They spawn in both levels 0 and 1, they all have different types of personalities they can have.

- Helpful, trades items, and tools, and advises the player

- Ignorant, they do not interact with the player, unless provoked.

- Fearful/Children avoid the player

- Rude/"Attack without reason", will always attack the player

Partygoer:

Rare entity, found in party rooms,

In a party room will have one "host" indicated by the party hat, they are a little stronger and will play music when in the area. They will drop the "Turtle Crusher" music disc

Smiler:

Very rare entity

Found in pitch black rooms

extremely fast, but a short notice range, so dont get too clone to one

Library Entity:

A very unique entity,

Only one spawns in "the end is near" they will appear to not be in the room, only noticeable by their footsteps.
Occasionally, or if disturbed, they will make a loud "click," this is when they are hunting.
Please DO NOT make any noise when this is occurring, or he will bolt toward you instantly killing you.
If you can find and look at him before he hears you he will hide again, repeating the cycle
