What is Levitas Visuals?

It is a lightweight bedrock shader pack made for low-end PCs, and mobile devices.

Version 1.1 now supports the Dappled Forest!

Awesome Features

- 1/16 Resolution, But Smooth: Downscales shadows and reflections for FPS boosts, using a filter to keep visuals smooth.

- Cinematic Fog: Adds beautiful fog and light shafts (GODRAYS).
