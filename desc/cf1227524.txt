Nico's Mobs is add-on introduces new mobs that spawn naturally in your world. Each mob has unique abilities and distinct loot. Some can be tamed, while others pose a serious threat. All mobs are designed to blend seamlessly with Minecraft's vanilla style. Aside from new mobs, this add-on also adds some new blocks and items.

(v1.15)
