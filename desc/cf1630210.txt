Jenny Dweller Addon

The nights are no longer safe.

A mysterious entity has begun wandering the Overworld, silently watching players from the darkness. Known only as Jenny Dweller, this creature is an intelligent predator that thrives on fear, turning every journey into a tense and unpredictable experience.

Jenny Dweller doesn't attack immediately. It stalks its victims, hides just out of sight, and waits for the perfect moment to strike. You may catch a brief glimpse of it in the distance... or hear unsettling sounds echoing through the night. By the time you realize you're being hunted, it may already be too late.

Every encounter is unique. Sometimes it will simply observe you, while other times it will relentlessly pursue you until one of you is gone. No two hunts are ever the same, making every adventure feel fresh and terrifying.

Whether you're exploring dense forests, abandoned structures, or the darkest caves, you'll never know when Jenny Dweller is nearby. Stay alert, trust your instincts, and don't let your guard down.

Features

- A terrifying new dweller that stalks players.

- Intelligent and unpredictable behavior.

- Creepy ambient sounds and intense chase music.

- Random encounters that make every world feel different.

- Designed to enhance survival with immersive horror.

You are never truly alone... Something is always watching from the darkness.
