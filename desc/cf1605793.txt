MorphX: Block Edition

Transform yourself into 200+ Vanilla Minecraft Blocks with MorphX: Block Edition!

Have you ever wanted to hide as a Grass Block , become a Furnace , disguise yourself as a Diamond Ore , or troll your friends as a TNT Block ? Now you can!

MorphX: Block Edition brings a brand-new Fullscreen Morph Menu that lets you morph into your favorite Minecraft blocks with just a few clicks. Whether you're playing Survival, Creative, Multiplayer, Hide & Seek, or making content, this addon makes block morphing smooth, immersive, and incredibly fun.

The addon also features a Hybrid Unlock System. In Creative Mode, every block morph is unlocked from the start. In Survival Mode, simply break a block once to permanently unlock its morph. Your progress is automatically saved, so you never lose your unlocked collection.

How to Open the Morph Menu

Step : 1 Crafting Recipe

Craft the **Morph Core ** to access the Morph Menu.

Step : 2 Get Morph Core

Step : 3 Follow Instructions

PC

Hold the Morph core and Right Click / Use.

Mobile

Hold the Morph Core and long tap on screen.

Console

Equip the Morph Core and press the Use / Interact button.

- Xbox RT

- PlayStation R2

- Nintendo Switch ZR

Installation Guide

1 Import the .mcaddon file.

2 Activate the Resource Pack.

3 Activate the Behavior Pack.

4 Create a new world or edit an existing one.

5 Enable all Experimental Features required by your Minecraft version (especially Beta APIs / Script API if prompted).

6 Join your world.

7 Craft or obtain the Morph Core.

8 Use the Morph Core to open the menu and start morphing!
