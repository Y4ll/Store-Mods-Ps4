v1.0.6

The Piggyback Add-on lets you carry other players on your back, making your Minecraft adventures more fun and interactive! Just point at another player and tap Ride (on mobile) or right-click (on PC) to piggyback. Jump to get off anytime.

Perfect for multiplayer, roleplay, or just having fun with friends. No setup needed! Just install and start playing. Build together, travel together, and enjoy the game like never before!

This add-on DOES NOT require experimental options.
Please DO NOT mix it with packs that use "player.json" to avoid errors.
Achievements Compatible
