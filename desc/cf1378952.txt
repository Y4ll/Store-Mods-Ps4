Clear Lava resource pack is a simple yet essential tool designed to entirely eliminate the lava fog effect in Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

Features

- Fog Removal: Say goodbye to the blindness caused by vanilla lava fog.

- Nether Navigation: Easily spot landmarks, ancient debris, and structures in lava oceans.

- PvP/PvE Advantage: Gain a massive tactical edge against mobs and other players.

Installation Guide

- Download the .mcpack file from the CurseForge download page.

- Open the .mcpack file to automatically import it into Minecraft Bedrock.

- Go to Settings -> Global Resources or access your World Settings.

- Activate the "Clear Lava" pack and ensure it is placed at the top of your resource pack list for maximum compatibility.
