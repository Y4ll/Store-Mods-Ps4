Alien: The Eaters are gigantic and horrifying creatures that roam the world searching for living beings to consume. Their enormous bodies, disturbing movements, and terrifying screams can turn even the safest night into a nightmare. Once they notice you, they will relentlessly chase you across the land without stopping. Hiding is almost useless, because these creatures are driven only by hunger and destruction.

It is highly recommended to install a gun addon or weapon addon before playing with this addon. Without powerful weapons, your only option may be to run for your life while being hunted by these monstrous aliens. Surviving an encounter with The Eaters is extremely difficult, especially when multiple creatures appear at the same time and surround their prey in the darkness.

The aliens only spawn at night and disappear when morning comes. Because of this, you should sleep as early as possible unless you want your world to become overrun by terrifying alien creatures. Once the night begins, the darkness quickly turns into a hunting ground filled with monstrous sounds, giant shadows, and relentless predators searching for anything alive to consume.
