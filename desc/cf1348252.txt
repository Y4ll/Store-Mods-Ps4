No particles + Mini tools

Author: BamCoca211

This mod combines two lightweight but useful features for Minecraft Bedrock Edition:

No particles Removes unnecessary particles such as fire, smoke, or potion effects to improve performance and provide a cleaner visual experience. Perfect for PvP or low-end devices.

Mini tools Adds compact and simplified tool models (pickaxe, sword, axe, etc.), giving a minimalistic look while keeping full functionality.

Features

Better FPS and smoother gameplay with no distracting particles.

Clean and modern mini-style tool models.

Simple, lightweight, and compatible with most other packs.
