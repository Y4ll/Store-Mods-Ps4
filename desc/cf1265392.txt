This resource pack transforms the second layer of any skin into a realistic three-dimensional effect, adding volume, detail, and a unique style to your character in Minecraft.

Ideal for:

Players who want to stand out with a more detailed and realistic appearance.

Improve the visual style without modifying the gameplay.

It is compatible with 64x64 and 128x128 skins, and is designed to fit both slim arms (Alex) and wide arms (Steve).
