Linxing's Paper Doll: Feel free to move the Paper Doll wherever you like no changes to gameplay! Easy to install and fully compatible with Bedrock 1.26+

How to Configure the Paper Doll

Step 1: Press the crouch button to make the Paper Doll appear.

Step 2: Go to the pause screen and press the button indicated in the image.

Step 3: Adjust the settings as desired. Once done, press the same button again.
