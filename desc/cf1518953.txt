DyingDayZ REBORN brings back the classic addon with revamped features, including smoother gun animations, 3D items, and new stats for Minecraft's latest version.

In this version of DyingDayZ, I revamped and changed the old one into new and fresh 3D stuff, basically, this addon still uses the old gameplay mechanic just added new stats and effect and change almost everything into 3D, trust me, it's better than old DDZ before.

So let's start from entering the world with the addon. At the start when you enter your world with survival mode active, you'll get starter loot and starter gun with some ammo to help you upgrading your tools, your inventory will be locked so your main task is to craft a backpack, and upgrade it, recipe will be shown once you get some material such as leather and chest.

Make sure to get tripwire hook to open your backpack! Each backpack has different storage size, upgrading your backpack will increase your inventory slot.

Sometimes, some vest armor will give you extra 4 slots to your inventory, but be careful, once you lose/remove it, you will lose your item on that pocket too!

Thirst-Bar

Thirst duration is now increased, making you stay hydrated longer than before, and the good news is, all beverages are now 3D but still with simple animation. Don't worry, it will be updated in future updates (maybe).

Some of the beverages give you some effect and have different thirst points. There are lots of 3D beverages here that you could explore by yourself. You can get them from loot boxes through structures.

Guns

Now for the guns, I have good news: the guns now don't have hit immunity delay, which means that you could use guns for PvP, but not for that shotgun lol. Bullets also don't ignore armor when hitting an entity that wears armor, and have a unique hit sound depending on where the bullet landed. Thanks to @Fritzertwwaddon and his team for letting me use their bullet and armor script.

Each gun is categorized into 3 types of range:

- Long Range: Infinity (depends on how much your chunk load)

- Mid-range: 30-32 blocks away

- Short Range: 14-15 blocks away

- Very Short Range: 7 blocks away

Each gun also has its own bullet type, recoil, and fire rate. You can find the bullet type information in the name of the gun.

Secret Gun

Secret gun cannot be obtained from cheat or creative inventory, you need to find it through loot box then craft it, or you can get it from a code, join my discord to get the gun codes or watch my video about how to get them. The Minigun is still work in progress, so you couldn't get it from survival, but you still can use and test it.

Attachment

There are various combinations of attachment, you can test it by yourself, it is also craftable in survival. To put attachment is so simple, just put your attachment on your offhand slot while holding the gun that supports the attachment, the attachment will automatically be added. To remove, put your gun that has an attachment on it on your offhand slot. More Updates, More Attachment.
