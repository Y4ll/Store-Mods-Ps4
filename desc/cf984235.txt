Hammer Time

Mine More. Dig Faster. Cut Trees. Harvest Crops. Fight Groups.

Hammer Time expands Minecraft Bedrock with powerful tools designed to make repetitive Survival tasks faster while still giving you control over how their abilities work.

Inspired by the Minecraft Java mod of the same name, Hammer Time adds Hammers, Great Swords, Great Axes, Sickles and Great Shovels, each with its own unique mechanics.

What Does Hammer Time Add?

Hammer Time includes 5 main tool types:

- Hammers Area Mining, Tunnel Mining and Vein Mining

- Great Swords Knockback and Cleave/Sweeping attacks

- Great Axes Fast tree cutting

- Sickles Leaf clearing, crop harvesting and Auto Replant

- Great Shovels Area digging, paths and farmland

Each tool is specialized for different Survival tasks, and many of their abilities can be customized directly in-game.

Full V3.1.0 Details

Hammer Mining Modes

Hammers now support three different mining modes, allowing you to change how your Hammer works depending on what you are doing.

Area Mining

Break multiple compatible blocks at once.

Available sizes:

- 1x1

- 3x3

- 5x5

- 7x7

Use smaller areas when you need precision or larger areas when clearing caves and large sections of terrain.

Tunnel Mining

Tunnel Mode is designed for quickly excavating underground passages.

You can configure:

- Tunnel Size

- Tunnel Depth

This allows you to create tunnels much faster without manually breaking every block one by one.

Vein Mining

Vein Mining detects connected ores and allows the Hammer to process them together.

You can configure the maximum number of connected blocks that can be processed in a single action.

Safe Mining

Large mining areas are powerful, but sometimes you don't want every nearby block to be destroyed.

Hammer Time now includes Safe Mining to help prevent certain important blocks from being accidentally broken.

Additional options include:

- Break only blocks of the same type

- Configurable mining limits

- Optional nearby ore protection

- Nearby ore protection is disabled by default

Quick Hammer Mode Switching

You don't need to constantly open the configuration menu to change your Hammer mode.

Use Sneak + Use while looking at a block to quickly switch between Hammer modes.

Real-Time Mining Preview

V3.1.0 introduces a new real-time mining preview.

Before you begin mining, Hammer Time can show exactly which blocks will be affected by your current mining mode.

The preview works when mining:

- Walls

- Floors

- Ceilings

- From different directions

The preview does not interrupt or restart your normal block-breaking progress.

A cleaner selection particle is also used to make affected areas easier to understand without filling the screen with unnecessary particles.
