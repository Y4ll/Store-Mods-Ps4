This addon allows you to use Creative Mode without losing your Achievements. You can get unlimited blocks and fly while keeping your world's achievements active at all times!

Your survival progress will stay 100% safe. You can use all creative features as much as you want without ever worrying about losing your trophies or progress.

How to Use

Step 1: Obtain a Stick from your inventory or a tree.
Step 2: Hold the stick and Right-Click (PC) or Long Press (Mobile) to open the menu.
Step 3: Click on the Creative button to switch modes instantly.
Step 4: Once you are done, use the stick again to switch back to Survival.
