Offhand Addon for Minecraft Bedrock/MCPE

The Offhand Addon brings a near-Java experience to Bedrock Edition by letting you use your second hand for useful items like torches, totems, shields, and more. Bedrock players usually have a limited offhand, but with this addon you can finally enjoy smoother gameplay and better utility just like Java Edition.

What This Addon Does

- Enables the offhand slot for more items

- Works like Java-style dual-wielding (depending on configuration)

- Great for mining, exploring & combat

- Mobile-friendly controls

- Lightweight & no lag

How to use?

Double crouch to swap items into off hand (only few things not work)
To use water Bucket (like mlg or whatever) crouch and right click three times

How to Install

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it to import into Minecraft

- Go to Settings Global Resources

- Activate the addon

- (If required) enable Experimental Features

- Restart the world and enjoy dual wielding
