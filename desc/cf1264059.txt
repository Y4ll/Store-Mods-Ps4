**Relic Player Animations V2.1 fix** is a player-focused animation overhaul addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. This version introduces smoother, more expressive and realistic character movements designed to enhance immersion during gameplay.

---

### Key Features:

- Fully redesigned animations for **walking, sprinting, jumping, crouching, swimming**, and more.

- Custom combat stances for **swords, axes, shields, and bows**.

- Subtle gesture and idle animations that bring your player to life.

- Multiplayer supported: animations sync properly for all players.

- Optimized for **performance**, works well on low-end devices.

- Improved transition speed between actions (e.g., run to stop, aim to attack).
