Herobrine Creepypasta [ADD-ON]

Hes watching...

Herobrine Creepypasta brings the classic legend back to Minecraft Bedrock through subtle, unpredictable, and unsettling encounters.

Herobrine will not simply appear anywhere. His manifestations depend on a small nearby structure that can provide cover. Stay close to trees, walls, cliffs, ruins, stacked blocks, or other formations, and you may notice someone watching from behind them.

Sometimes he will remain completely still. Other times, he may appear crouched, slowly hide behind nearby cover, or vanish instantly the moment he is noticed. Do not expect every encounter to happen in the same way.

The add-on also introduces several paranoia events designed to make the world feel increasingly strange. Mysterious signs may appear, unusual sounds can be heard, and Herobrine may return using different appearances.

An in-game menu allows players to customize their experience. Paranoia events, sounds, appearances, and other systems can be adjusted, allowing each player to control how intense and unpredictable their world becomes.

The add-on also includes an optional Herobrine Totem. It is not required for Herobrines appearances or the other events to begin. A tutorial showing how to build it can be found in the screenshots and demonstration images on the add-on page.

After building the totem correctly, stand on top of it for at least four seconds while keeping your view focused on the netherrack block. This allows the system enough time to recognize the completed structure and activate the ritual correctly.

Herobrine does not constantly chase the player. He watches, waits, and appears when the environment allows it. You may see him clearlyor only catch a brief glimpse before he disappears behind something.
