Swizzle7rel Glowing Ores

Light up the depths of Minecraft!

Swizzle7rel Glowing Ores is a resource pack that gives Minecraft ores a vibrant, glowing appearance, making them easier to spot while exploring dark caves and underground areas.

The ores retain their familiar Minecraft designs while receiving brighter colors and emissive-style highlights for a more striking underground experience.

- Bright emissive-style highlights

- Covers a variety of Minecraft ores
