Introducing Glimmering Armor Trims Minecraft Pack! Prepare to witness a whole new level of visual enchantment in your armors. Make your armor trims glow in dark! Works in mobile, pc and other devices.

This is a resource pack that can be applied in global resources to enjoy glowing armor trims in servers, multiplayer and singleplayer worlds. It is compatible with any texture or shader pack that does not modify the "color palette" textures of armor trims.
