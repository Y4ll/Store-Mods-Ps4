The Sift Concept Dimension Add-On

Explore an unofficial concept recreation of The Sift, a mysterious dimension filled with vibrant meadows, unusual terrain and strange new life.

The Sift expands Minecraft Bedrock with a fully scripted custom dimension. Its procedural terrain system combines multiple noise layers to create plains, forests, mountains, raised plateaus and contrasting meadow patches without relying on traditional feature rules.

Entering The Sift

Use the following command:

/eu_the_sift:enter

The surrounding terrain will be generated before the player is teleported into the dimension.

Development Status

The Sift is currently an early concept project. Terrain generation, biome distribution, creatures and structures may change as development continues.

Because the add-on uses experimental Minecraft Bedrock scripting and custom-dimension functionality, you may need beta api options enabled.

Creating a backup before updating the add-on is recommended.
