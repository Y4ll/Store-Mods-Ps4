Focus on simplicity, using only clear pixel outlines in a minimalist style that doesn't distract the eye or consume excessive system resources.

In this mod, a total of 11 ore types feature custom colored outlines as follows:

Overworld Ores:

1. Coal Ore

2. Iron Ore

3. Copper Ore

4. Gold Ore

5. Redstone Ore

6. Lapis Lazuli Ore

7. Diamond Ore

8. Emerald Ore

(Includes both standard Stone and Deepslate variants)

Nether Ores:

9. Nether Quartz Ore

10. Nether Gold Ore

11. Ancient Debris
