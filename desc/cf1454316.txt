Fruit From Leaves Addon Natural Fruit Drop System for Minecraft Bedrock 1.21+

Fruit From Leaves Addon is a survival friendly Minecraft Bedrock utility addon that makes tree leaves drop real fruits when broken, creating a more immersive and rewarding farming and exploration experience. Instead of only dropping saplings and sticks, leaves now have a chance to drop different fruit types depending on the tree species, turning forests into natural food sources while keeping vanilla balance intact.

If you are looking for a Minecraft Bedrock fruit addon, tree fruit mod, leaf drop system, or survival farming addon, this addon provides a realistic and performance safe solution for both singleplayer and multiplayer worlds.

What This Fruit From Leaves Addon Does
When a player breaks leaf blocks, the addon checks the leaf type and assigns a matching fruit.
Each tree type drops a unique fruit such as cherries, pears, bananas, plums, oranges, blueberries, pomegranates, persimmons, and more.
Fruit drops use a chance based system so they feel rare and balanced.
Works with both modern leaf blocks and legacy leaf variants for full compatibility.

Vanilla Safe Behavior
If the player uses shears, no fruit will drop, respecting vanilla mechanics.
If the tool has Silk Touch, fruit drops are disabled.
Prevents farming exploits and keeps survival balanced.
Boss blocks and non leaf blocks are never affected.

Smart Random Drop Logic
Uses a probability system for fruit spawning.
Fruit appears at the center of the broken leaf block naturally.
No forced drops and no guaranteed farming abuse.
Feels like apples from oak leaves but expanded to all trees.
