Register -

After activating this addon, you get a special item, Backpack Register. Use it to apply for a backpack ID, otherwise, your backpack will not work.

How to use?

- Craft backpack.

- Hold it & look down.

- An open button will appear.

You can wear a backpack just like a chestplate.
