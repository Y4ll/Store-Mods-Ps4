## ENGLISH DESCRIPTION

Welcome to the **Sanity System Addon**! This pack brings a rich survival-horror experience to Minecraft Bedrock Edition through a dynamic Sanity mechanic (ranging from 0 to 100). Below is the complete breakdown of how Sanity increases and decreases.

### Factors that INCREASE Sanity (+)

* **Sleeping:** **Sleeping in a bed** immediately restores Sanity back to the maximum **100**.

* **Environment & Time:** **Daytime & Clear weather** outdoors continuously increases Sanity (`+0.15` to `+0.25` per tick).

* **Nighttime Light Sources:** Standing near light sources at night within a 12-block radius helps dispel darkness:

* **Lava:** `0.9` (Very High)

* **Campfire / Soul Campfire:** `0.8` (High) *(+0.3 special bonus)*

* **Beacon (Light block):** `0.8` (High)

* **Glowstone / Shroomlight / Sea Lantern:** `0.7` (High)

* **Lantern / Jack o' Lantern:** `0.5` (Medium)

* **Torch / Soul Torch:** `0.4` (Medium)

* **Fire / Powered Redstone Lamp:** `0.3` (Low)

* **Redstone Torch:** `0.2` (Very Low)

* **Food & Consumables:** **High-tier / Cooked food** (Golden apples, cooked meats, cake, soup) restores **`+10`**. Basic food restores **`+5`**. Dried kelp restores **`+2`**.

* **Handheld Items & Music:** **Holding flowers** in the main or off-hand adds `+0.5`. **Standing near an active Jukebox** playing music adds `+1.5`.

* **Mobs & Allies:** **Pets & friendly animals** (cats, dogs, sheep, parrots, villagers) grant comfort (`+0.1` to `+0.3`). **Nearby players or team members** grant up to **`+1.0`**.

* **Special Structures:** **Beacons** (Iron, Gold, Diamond) restore `+3` to `+10`. **Emerald Beacons** double (`x2`) the recovery effect. **Honey Drips** from nectar-carrying bees have a chance to instantly restore **`+20`** Sanity.

### Factors that DECREASE Sanity (-)

* **Time & Weather:** **Nighttime & Midnight** outdoors drains Sanity (`-0.3` to `-0.8`). Midnight adds an extra `-0.4`. **Bad weather** (Dusk `-0.1`, Rain `-0.2`, Thunderstorms `-0.4`) further reduces Sanity.

* **Monsters & Bosses:** **Mobs & Undead** nearby drain Sanity based on threat level and crowd density (`-0.3` to `-0.7`). **Bosses** (Warden, Wither) apply massive psychological pressure, multiplying the threat factor by `1.5` (`-0.5` to `-1.5`).

* **Poor Quality Food:** **Raw meats** drain **`-5`**. **Rotten flesh, spider eyes, pufferfish, and poisonous potatoes** drain **`-15`**.

* **Dangerous Actions:** **Excessive swimming or swimming at night** drains **`-2 per second`** and causes exhaustion.

* **Low Sanity Penalties & Death:** **Self-harm:** Below 20 Sanity, players risk hurting themselves (`-1` health). **Total Depletion (Sanity = 0):** The player instantly suffers **`2000`** lethal damage, broadcasts a death message, and resets Sanity.
