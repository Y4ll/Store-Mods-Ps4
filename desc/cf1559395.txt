PG's More Armor and Tools is a Minecraft Bedrock Edition addon that introduces 19 new minerals and metals into the game, each with a full set of tools and armor. Materials range from common gems like Quartz and Lapis Lazuli to rare powerful minerals like Onyx, Sugilite and Platinum, offering a wide variety of progression options beyond vanilla. Every material comes with its own Sword, Spear, Axe, Pickaxe, Shovel, Hoe, Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, and Boots with unique stats, durability, and enchantibility.

CRAFTING AND SMELTING

Crafting Blocks

PG's More Armor and Tools also adds crafting recipes for several vanilla blocks using Platinum, Silver, and Titanium ingots as alternatives to Iron, allowing you to craft these blocks even if you are short on Iron. Simply replace any Iron Ingot in the original recipe with a Platinum, Silver, or Titanium Ingot to craft the following blocks:

Redstone Blocks

- Hopper

- Piston

- Sticky Piston

- Crafter

- Tripwire Hook

Utility Blocks

- Blast Furnace

- Stonecutter

- Cauldron

- Lodestone

- Lantern

- Soul Lantern

Transport

- Rail

- Activator Rail

- Detector Rail

Smithing & Crafting Stations

- Smithing Table

- Anvil

Some Useless Recipes

PG's More Armor and Tools also adds smelting recipes for Platinum, Silver, Titanium and Redstone armor and tools. You can smelt any piece of armor or any tool from these four material sets in a furnace or blast furnace to recover a single nugget. You can also smelt the ores added by this addon, however these recipes are not recommended as you only get a single nugget per ore, making it far more efficient to mine them with a Fortune III pickaxe instead.
