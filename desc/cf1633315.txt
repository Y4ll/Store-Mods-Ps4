Advanced Night Vision

Advanced Night Vision by Dr Addons lets you explore caves, mines, and dark areas with clear visibility at any time. Simply Sneak + Jump to instantly toggle Night Vision on or off without using commands, potions, or changing your game settings.

Overview

The addon is quick and easy to use. Whenever you need better visibility, perform the Sneak + Jump action to enable Night Vision. Repeat the same action to disable it whenever you want, making it perfect for both exploration and normal gameplay.

Key Features

- Toggle Night Vision using Sneak + Jump.

- No commands or potions required.

- Instant ON/OFF switching.

- Perfect for caves, mines, and nighttime exploration.

- Lightweight and simple to use.

- Works naturally during normal gameplay.

Perfect For

- Mining underground.

- Cave exploration.

- Nether adventures.

- Nighttime survival.

- Building in dark areas.

Why You'll Love It

Forget carrying Night Vision potions or increasing your screen brightness. With a simple gesture, you can switch Night Vision whenever you need it and continue playing without interruptions.

Compatibility

- Achievement Friendly.

- Survival Friendly.

- Compatible with Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

- Works in both new and existing worlds.

Installation

- Download the addon.

- Open the .mcaddon file.

- Import it into Minecraft.

- Enable the Behavior Pack in your world.

- Join your world and use Sneak + Jump to toggle Night Vision.
