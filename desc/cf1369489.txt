A part of AurorianCraft: Insane Survival complement that excludes the realistic survival features, only keeping the animals. This addition is perfect for those who liked the AurorianCraft fauna and didn't want the survival part - just the animals.

With this add-on, exploring is more unique. Expect finding the most interesting species. All mobs have realistic behaviors with unique features such as Natural Breeding, sleeping cycle and much more.

Animals

Animals have realistic behavior:

Passive animals flee away.

Defensive animals will attack or warn if they feel endangered.

Agressive animals attack if the prey is too close.

Sleeping cycle.

Natural breeding.

Genders.

When dead, the animal becomes a corpse:

The corpse can be skinned by interacting with a sharp object. Skinning is needed for the full loot, otherwise it'll just become a pile of bones, wich only drops bone.

The corpse can be stuffed if interacted with planks.

Players now can carry small animals (just the custom ones). Just shift and get close.
