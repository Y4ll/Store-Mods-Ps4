NightVision Elite is a lightweight Minecraft Bedrock addon that gives you complete control over night vision no potions, no commands, no hassle. Explore caves, dungeons, and the darkest corners of your world with crystal-clear fullbright vision at the press of a button.

Features

- Toggle On/Off Sneak + jump to activate or deactivate night vision instantly

- No Potions Needed Works without any items, brewing, or commands

- Persistent Effect The addon automatically refreshes your night vision

- No Particles Clean, distraction-free visual with no potion particle effects

- Lightweight Minimal performance impact; runs efficiently in the background

How to Use

- Download the addon and open the .mcaddon file it will automatically import into Minecraft Bedrock.

- Create or open a world and go to Behavior Packs in the world settings.

- Activate NightVision Elite from your available packs and apply it to your world.

- Once in-game, hold Sneak and Jump at the same time to toggle night vision on or off.

- A message will appear on your screen Night Vision ON or Night Vision OFF confirming the change.

- That's it! Your vision stays bright automatically. No need to reapply or carry any items.

Tip: Works in singleplayer and multiplayer. Every player can toggle their own night vision independently.

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.19.0+

- Behavior Pack enabled in your world

- Experimental Features: Scripting API must be enabled in world settings
