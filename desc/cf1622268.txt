Morph Into Verity Update V1.1.4: Added Verity Remake, Falsity, Lovity va Freakity Phase 1 va Phase 2. You can now morph into 44 Verity characters in total.

To morph into a Verity character, place any type of button near a Verity, then press the button. You will transform into the nearest Verity to your location.

In the Morph Into Verity, Falsity, Cruelity and Lovity addon, you can morph into the following characters:

- Verity

- Falsity

- Cruelity

- Lovity

- Verity Fusion (Verity fused with Minecraft mobs)

There are a total of 36 characters: 27 Verity characters, 8 Verity Fusion characters, and 1 Verity Nightmare character.

Characters such as Falsity, Cruelity, and Lovity are not listed by their specific names. Instead, they appear as Verity Phase 1 through Phase 27, Verity Fusion Phase 1 through Phase 8, and Verity Nightmare Phase 1.
