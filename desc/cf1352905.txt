This add-on adds two new vehicles, which you can purchase from a new structure found in biomes like Plains, Savannah, or Forest.
Upon reaching this structure, simply trade with the new merchant named "Brayan," who will sell you various types of three-wheeled vehicles at an affordable price.
It's worth noting that motorcycle taxis regenerate health through wind charges, and the new non-motorized vehicles require iron ingots.

You can also store your items in the vehicles, as they have 16 storage slots.
You can now unlock new audio by hitting some vehicles.
