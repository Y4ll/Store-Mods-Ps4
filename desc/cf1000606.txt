How StoneTombs Works

When you die, StoneTombs creates a grave containing your stored inventory and gives you a Grave Key connected to that exact grave.

Your grave stores information such as:

- Grave number

- Owner

- Dimension

- Coordinates

- Death time

- Death cause

- Stored XP

- Stored items

By default, only the owner can recover their grave.

Your key can also display the grave's location so you always know where your items were left.

Coordinates can also be shown in chat when the grave is created.

Both coordinate displays can be enabled or disabled by the server administrator.

Smart Safe Grave Placement

One of the most important V2.0 improvements is the new Smart Safe Grave Placement system.

This safety system is always enabled.

When you die, StoneTombs does not blindly place the grave exactly where you died.
