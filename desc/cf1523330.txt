This pack brings together two legendary Japanese sports cars from different generations, combining classic tuning culture with modern performance. One car represents the golden era of street builds and smooth, timeless design, while the other reflects a newer, sharper, and more aggressive style. Together, they create a perfect duo that feels balanced in both looks and driving experience, giving players the best of both worlds inside Minecraft.

Both cars are designed as 2-seaters, keeping the focus on the driver and a close passenger experience. They come in a variety of fun and vibrant colours, allowing you to customize your ride to match your builds or personality. The interiors are highly detailed, carefully crafted to reflect the vibe and layout of the real cars, making every drive feel immersive. From the seating position to the dashboard feel, everything is built to give a premium in-game experience.

In terms of features, both vehicles are fully functional and built for gameplay. They include smooth and well-made animations that make the cars feel alive, along with a working inventory system so you can carry items while on the move. A special boost mode adds an extra layer of fun, which can be activated using a fungus on a stick. This gives you bursts of speed whenever you need them, making the cars great for both casual driving and fast-paced runs across your world.
