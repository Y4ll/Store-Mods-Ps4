- VIKING ONSLAUGHT -

Survive the Viking invasion of Britain. Face relentless Viking raiders, discover their camps, recruit Saxon Huscarls to fight alongside you, and fight to survive as the invasion spreads across the land.

Explore a world shaped by the invasion, from fortified Anglo-Saxon settlements to crowded Viking camps and longboats along the coast. Gather equipment, build your strength, and decide when to fight, retreat, or call upon your Saxon allies as you struggle to survive the onslaught.

MINIMUM MINECRAFT VERSION REQUIRED : 26.40+

This add-on require Minecraft version 26.40 and above, otherwise all of entity in this addon wouldn't spawning. Please make sure to match your Minecraft version to required version.

LATEST UPDATE CHANGELOG :

(+) NEW :

- Kite Shield A shield that applies a knockback effect to attackers.

- Long Spear A long spear with increased attack reach.

- Giant Axe A berserker axe that deals significant knockback with normal attacks and can
