Japanese Historical Weapons & Armors

This addon adds 35 historical Japanese weapons and a full samurai armor set to Minecraft Bedrock. Every weapon has its own unique 3D model no flat sprites, no recolored vanilla items. Made for the Echoes of Past Modjam 2026 contest.

Weapons

Swords:

- Katana, Tachi, Chokuto, Wakizashi, Osuriagi, Tanto

Polearms:

- Naginata, Sasumata

Chain Weapons:

- Kusarigama, Chigiriki, Kusari-Fundo, Kyoketsu-Shoge

Clubs & Staves:

- Kanabo, Otsuchi, Bo Staff, Jo Staff, Tonfa, Nunchaku

Axes & Field Tools:

- Ono, Tobiguchi, Kuwa, Kama

Thrown:

- Shuriken, Kunai, Makibishi, Bo-Hiya

Close Quarters:

- Shuko, Ashiko, Tekko-Kagi, Tessen

Sidearms:

- Jutte, Sai, Kabutowari, Fukiya, Kiseru

Samurai Armor

A full 4-piece armor set inspired by real samurai armor. It gives the same protection as diamond armor and can be repaired with iron.

- Kabuto: Helmet

- Do-Maru: Chestplate

- Kusazuri: Leggings

- Suneate: Boots

Dyeable Armor

You can dye any armor piece using any of the 16 dyes on the crafting table.
