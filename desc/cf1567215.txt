Welcome to No Man's Land

No Man's Land is a medieval zombie apocalypse experience designed to make survivel feel tense, immersive, and unpredictable. This project focuses on atmosphere, stealth, and survival rather than overwhelming players with endless hordes. The undead are not just another hostile mob - they react to the world around them and force you to think before making every move.

This is the first public alpha release, and many features, creatures, and systems are planned for future updates.

Zombie Variants

Bad Vision

Their Hearing is often more dangerous than their vision. Careless movement, sprinting, or making noise dan attract unwanted attention even when you're safe.

Stealth System

Sneaking significantly reduces the chances of being detected by nearby zombies, allowing you to move through dangerous areas more carefully.

During rain, the sound of rainfall helps mask your presence, making stealth much more effective.

Day 5

The world starts ralatively peacefull, giving players time to gather resources and prepare.

The infected begin appearing from Day 6 onward or when indacator Days Played 5.

Moon Phase Spawning

Zombies still spawning on day or night, but they also react to the moon phase. You're safe on New Moon Phase, but probably die on Full Moon Phase.

Horde Climbing

When zombies gather in large numbers, they can help each other reach elevated positions, making fortified locations much less reliable than before.

Looting Corpse

Sneak near a corpse and interact whit it to search for random loots. Risk venturing into dangerous areas for a chance to find valuable resource.
