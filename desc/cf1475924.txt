What is included in the Simple Gun add-on?

This add-on allows you to craft guns with very simple recipes.
The maximum gun capacity is increased by 3. Normally, a gun can only hold 6 ammo, but in the Simple Gun add-on, you can get 9 ammo directly.

To craft a gun, you only need iron ingots and gunpowder.
To craft ammo, you only need iron nuggets.
