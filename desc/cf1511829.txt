The Re:Zero addon lets you spawn characters from the anime Re:Zero Starting Life in Another World as mobs in your survival world. You can bring your favorite characters into the game, interact with them, and add a fun anime-inspired twist to your gameplay experience.

There are Subaru Natsuki and Emilia as the main characters.
Reinhard van Astrea is a powerful character, similar to an Iron Golem.
Ram and Rem can support you or become your maid ;]
And Felix Argyle is a cute and adorable catboy character.
