About the Addon

Practical Tools upgrades your Minecraft Bedrock gameplay with smart, powerful tools built to save you time and effort. Mine faster with 33 hammers and chop entire trees in one go with mighty axes. Less grinding, more building, more fun.

Quick Overview & Features

Hammer

Heavy, efficient, and straight to the point.

- Breaks blocks in a 33 area

- Sneak (Shift) to activate

- Simple and intuitive to use

- Cannot break bedrock or unbreakable blocks

- Available in Gold, Diamond, and Netherite

Great Axe

Cut trees like a legend one swing is all it takes.

- Chops down the entire tree instantly

- Fast and satisfying

- No sneaking required

- Anti-house destroyer (wont ruin your builds )
