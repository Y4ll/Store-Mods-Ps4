**Simplistic Shader** is a clean, cinematic Vibrant Visuals pack that reworks Minecraft's lighting and atmosphere for a natural, sun-warmed look. Built around soft golden-hour tones and physically-based color science, it enhances the sky, fog, and light across every biome for a more immersive, believable world all running as a pure resource pack.

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1595771/description_73295041-9d93-496e-8164-601eacf9cd23.png)

Features

Warm, natural lighting with smooth sunrise and sunset transitions

Dynamic sky with realistic atmospheric scattering (rayleigh & mie)

ACES tone mapping and balanced highlight/midtone/shadow color grading

Soft sun rays and god rays for added depth

Volumetric height fog for air, water, and weather

Biome-aware fog connected across all Overworld, Nether, and End biomes

Gentle white-balanced temperature for a consistent cinematic tone

Subtle, natural sky and horizon color grading through every time of d

Added realistic sun and moon rendering

Minor polish and consistency improvements

added glowing ores

all ores have borderline
