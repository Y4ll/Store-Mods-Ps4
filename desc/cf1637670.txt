Flower Drops OP Items!

Turn every flower into a surprise loot source with Flower Drops OP Items! by FleetMc Studio! This addon transforms ordinary flowers into valuable treasure. Instead of dropping only flowers, every flower you break has a chance to reward you with random Minecraft items ranging from basic survival supplies to some of the most overpowered loot in the game.

Whether you're starting a brand-new survival world or simply exploring flower-filled biomes, every flower can reward you with useful resources, enchanted equipment, rare blocks, and valuable treasures. You never know what you'll receive next!

What Can Drop?

- Max Enchanted Armor.

- Powerful Weapons.

- Enchanted Tools.

- Totems of Undying.

- Elytra.

- Shulker Boxes.

- Diamonds & Diamond Blocks.

- Netherite Items.

- Rare Building Blocks.

- Useful Survival Resources.

- Food and Valuable Items.

- ...and hundreds of other random Minecraft items!

Features

- Every flower drops random Minecraft loot.

- Huge variety of possible rewards.

- Receive both common and extremely overpowered items.

- Perfect for Survival challenges and lucky worlds.

- Works with all vanilla flowers.

- Lightweight and optimized.

- Achievement Friendly.

- No Experimental Gameplay required.

How to Use

- Download and import the addon.

- Enable the Behavior Pack.

- Join your world.

- Find any flower.

- Break it and collect your random rewards!

Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

- Achievement Friendly.

- No Experimental Gameplay Required.

- Singleplayer & Multiplayer Supported.
