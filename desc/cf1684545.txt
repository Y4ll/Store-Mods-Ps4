LEVELING

Fight. Gain Experience. Level Up. Become Stronger.

Leveling adds a simple RPG progression system to Minecraft Bedrock. Fight mobs, gain experience, level up, and use your points to improve your character.

How Leveling Works

Every player has a level and gains experience by playing and fighting.

Gain enough experience Level Up
Every level gives you 3 points
Use those points to upgrade your stats

It's that simple! The more you play, the more you can improve your character.

Upgrade Your Stats

Spend your points in the Status menu to make your character stronger.

Vitality Increase your maximum health.
Fortitude Take less damage from attacks.
Might Deal more damage to enemies.
Precision Increase your chance to land a critical hit.
Lethality Make your critical hits deal more damage.

Level Up With Your Enemies

Mobs can have levels too, making combat more challenging as you progress. Prepare yourself, improve your stats, and take on stronger enemies!

Your Progression. Your Power.

Start as a beginner and work your way up. Earn experience, gain levels, collect upgrade points, and create a stronger character with every level.
