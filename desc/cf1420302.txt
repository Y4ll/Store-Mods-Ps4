Explorify Structures

Explorify Structures Add-on to Make Your Minecraft World More Exciting!

Discover the Minecraft world full of surprises with Explorify Structures! This add-on brings you a variety of new structures that will be naturally generated in your Minecraft world from hidden buildings and ancient ruins to interesting locations that you can explore or use as a base.

Features

New, diverse & detailed structures

Can spawn in various biomes naturally

Suitable for survival, adventure, or roleplay

Lightweight & compatible with many other add-ons

Loot table randomized in other chests at structures
