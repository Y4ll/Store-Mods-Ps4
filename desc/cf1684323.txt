Ascendant: Mastery brings skill-based progression to Minecraft Bedrock. Earn experience, spend skill points, unlock equipment and activities, and develop your character across twelve skills in a custom book interface.

This is the core addon in the Ascendant series. Ascendant: Vocation expands its progression with professions, while Ascendant: Sustenance adds an optional nutrition system.

- Twelve skills: Health, Strength, Agility, Defense, Stamina, Luck, Archery, Trade, Smithing, Mining, Farming, and Alchemy.
- Twenty levels per skill with persistent player progression.
- Skill bonuses for combat, movement, health, hunger, farming, mining, trading, smithing, brewing, and more.
- Level requirements for equipment, blocks, entities, mining, brewing, and smithing.
- Under-level players can still break restricted mining blocks, but receive no drops; the matching vanilla break sound still plays.
- Alchemy restricts drinking and throwing potions, not ingredient collection. Ingredients and potions can be collected, stored, and traded freely; existing workstation-use requirements remain unchanged.
- Extended potions require at least Alchemy 16, stronger potions 20, splash delivery 7, and lingering delivery 17. The highest applicable effect and delivery requirement wins.
- Restricted non-stackable equipment keeps requirement lore visible as red unmet lines that disappear as each requirement is met.
- Spears require both Agility and Strength; Netherite gear also requires Smithing in addition to its base skill requirement.
- Custom items, blocks, and entities can require multiple skills together. Every listed requirement must be met.
- Continuous unlock lists that show when content becomes available.
- A custom Mastery interface opened by the Ascendant book.
- Centered UI with fade-in/fade-out transitions only, without sliding or resizing.
- Every player receives the Ascendant book on first join and after every death.
- Craft another Ascendant book from one normal Minecraft book.
- Integrated navigation for Ascendant: Sustenance and the Ascendant: Vocation expansion when installed.
- Existing legacy save data is migrated automatically.

- Use the Ascendant book received when joining or respawning.
- Alternatively, run /asc:mastery.

1. Import the included behavior pack and resource pack.
2. Activate both packs on the same world.
3. Place the behavior pack high enough in the stack to avoid another addon replacing its player definition.
4. Back up an existing world before updating or changing addon stacks.

- Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.40
- Both the Ascendant: Mastery behavior pack and resource pack are required.

Mastery modifies the player entity to provide skill-based health values. Addons that also replace minecraft:player may conflict unless their player definitions are merged. Sustenance is an optional standalone-compatible companion. Vocation is an expansion for Mastery and should be installed alongside it.

Extensions now use simple, readable lists instead of versioned registration code. Add item, block, entity, mining, potion, and smithing requirements, and registered content appears in the matching unlock lists.

Single requirement:
{ item: "myaddon:ruby_sword", skill: "strength", level: 10 }

Multiple requirements:
{ item: "myaddon:swift_sword", requires: { strength: 10, agility: 5 } }

Both Strength 10 AND Agility 5 are required. Lore shows each unmet requirement.

An inactive, ready-to-copy extension_template is included inside the behavior pack. Edit scripts/lists.js; the supplied script handles registration, retries, and readable error messages automatically. Follow extension_template/START_HERE.txt to copy the template out, rename its manifest, and assign unique UUIDs. The template refers to existing content; it does not create items or blocks.
