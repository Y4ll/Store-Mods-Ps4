Ember Visuals is my fire pack. Deep reds and oranges on everything, cherry groves that burn instead of blush, and crimson skies at night.

What this changes

Color grading

Deep red and orange sit across every biome. Nothing is allowed to go cool, which is what keeps the world feeling lit from a fire rather than from the sun.

Cherry groves

Blossoms turn fiery instead of pink, with glowing petal particles drifting through the trees. This is the most striking part of the pack and the biggest departure from what that biome normally looks like.

Sunsets

Blazing skies with hard golden light coming in low. Dusk is the loudest moment in the day cycle here.

Villages

Ember tones hold on the buildings through the whole cycle, not just in the evening. Villages look lit from within even at midday.

Night sky

Rich crimson nights with the stars still visible through them, so it never goes flat black.

Nether portals

Portals throw real light onto the blocks around them with heavy shadow contrast behind. In this pack they finally match the rest of the world instead of standing out from it.

Reflections

Glass and blocks pick up reflections in the same fiery tones, so surfaces carry the pack's color instead of staying neutral.

Requirements

THIS PACK IS FOR VIBRANT VISUALS ONLY
IT DOES NOT WORK WITH MB LOADER

Vibrant Visuals must be ON. This pack is built for Bedrock's Vibrant Visuals renderer. The pack will not do anything if it is off.

Where to turn it on: Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals

If that toggle is not there, your device or version does not support Vibrant Visuals yet.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Ember Visuals

- Turn on Settings > Video > Vibrant Visuals

- If the lighting still looks the same, leave the world and load it again once
