Vors FPS Booster is a lightweight optimization pack built to strip away the visual overhead that quietly drags your framerate down without turning your world into a flat, lifeless mess. Every swap is tuned to look clean while costing your GPU less.

Compatible With: Most shader and texture packs stack it on top of your existing setup for an instant performance boost.

What's Optimized:

- 131 particle effects neutralized fire, smoke, explosions, spells, drips, bubbles, dust, the works. Combat and mob fights stop drowning your GPU in overdraw.

- Streamlined ore & deepslate textures same readability at a fraction of the texture complexity.

- Lightweight enchant glint the animated shimmer on your gear and shield no longer tanks FPS in your inventory or mid-fight.

- Simplified sky & weather sun, moon phases, and weather textures trimmed for a smoother day/night cycle.

- Faster glass & terrain blocks tinted glass, observers, dirt, grass, cobblestone, and friends, reworked to render quicker.

- Biome color left untouched grass and foliage colormaps are unmodified, so the world still feels alive, not bleached out.

Built For:
Low-end and mid-range devices struggling to hold steady FPS, and high-end setups pushing shaders or high render distance who want extra headroom.
