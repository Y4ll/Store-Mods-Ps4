Have you ever wanted more crops in Minecraft? This add-on brings you over 130 new real-world crops, ranging from fruits, vegetables, tubers, legumes, plants, seaweed, fungi, and grains. For now, the add-on doesn't include food recipes, but these will be added in future updates.

How can the crops be found?

You will be able to find these crops all over the world, as they spawn naturally; here is a table showing the biomes where each crop is found.

This add-on is currently under constant development, so the uploaded file is an initial BETA version, which already includes approximately 140 crop items.But they're not yet available in survival mode.
