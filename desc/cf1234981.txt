STRUCTURES:

Theres over 13 new structures that spawn in the Nether, heres some of the coolest ones:

Abandoned Lab: the Abandoned Lab is an extremely large structure that has Mechs of Mutation and Tantalum Drones guarding it, it can give an absurd amount of tantalum and iron if you manage to survive, it also has its own exclusive and usful item, Virolite

Ghast Nest: the Ghast Nest is where you can find the sleeping irradiated ghast:

Inferno Trial Chambers: the Inferno trial chambers has a series of rooms with trial spawners and vaults, it has its own exclusive key and the trial spawners when ominous have a completely unique set of things it spawns. theres a ton of exclusive loot here like for example, an item that turns your arrows into bats that attack nearby mobs and players

Skeleton Hotel: the Skeleton Hotel is a giant structure that has an absurd amount of skeleton and wither skeleton spawners, chances are you will die here unless you come in with extremely overpowered gear

Cinders Labyrinth: The Cinders Labyrinth is a giant cinderplate tower filled with secret rooms and is the only source of brimstone, it has its own crate that can be unlocked via a key dropped from the Hex Maniac

Piglin Mineshaft: This is the most dangerous structure in the add-on because of how many piglins there are in it, however the loot is nothing to scoff at, theres chests riddled throughout the whole structure filled to the brim with good loot

Soul Blacksmith: Inside is a new crafting block, the Soul Forge, its used for crafting a ton of items in the mod

MOBS:

Infernal Cyclord: Infernal Cyclords jump towards the player, if they land on or nearby them, they spawn a small blast of fire in the surrounding area, knocking back anything except itself back

Underworldly Eyes: these eyes fly around and spawn in babies whenever they attack you

Flesh Mess: periodically summons tiny eyes to defend itself, when attacked it also can spawn additional eyes, when attacked it makes all nearby undead mobs target the attacker
