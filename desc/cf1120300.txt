This addon will add even more food to your Minecraft world.
All food available in the addon is created from vanilla items. But some food drops only from animals. Now hunting animals will become even more interesting.

Embark on a journey through the vanilla world of Minecraft again. Get and create new food now!

Screens:

The screenshots show only a small part of the addon.

In the future, the addon is planned to be updated with updates, even more new and healthy food will be added, which will make your adventures even more intense. And most importantly you will always find something to eat.
