Better Flight System adds a smooth and immersive flying experience to Minecraft Bedrock using Elytra.

This addon introduces a barrel roll-style camera system that makes flying feel more dynamic and realistic. Instead of normal straight gliding, the camera slightly tilts based on your movement, creating a better sense of motion.

What's New? (v2.0)

- Completely reworked flight camera.
- Much smoother camera movement.
- Improved banking effect while turning.
- Better camera follow with reduced jitter.
- Camera orbit effect for a more cinematic flight experience.

Features:
- Smooth camera tilt while flying
- Speed-based FOV effect

How to Use:
1. Equip Elytra
2. Jump and start gliding
3. Move left and right to feel the tilt effect

Note:
- This addon does not add real 360 barrel roll due to engine limitations
- It creates a realistic illusion using camera movement

Works on:
- Minecraft Bedrock Edition (Windows / Android)

Installation:
1. Download the .mcaddon file
2. Open it with Minecraft
3. Activate Behavior Pack & Resource Pack
4. Enable Experimental Features
