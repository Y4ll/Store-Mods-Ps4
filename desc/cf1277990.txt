What saturation actually does

Saturation is a hidden value that sits on top of your hunger bar. It doesn't show up on screen, but it affects two things:

- It slows hunger drain. Saturation depletes before your visible hunger bar does, so eating high saturation foods like golden carrot, cooked beef, etc means your hunger bar stays full much longer.

- It enables fast hu'll regenerate 1 HP every half second same speed as Java Edition. Each heal costs 1.5 saturation, so richer foods keep you healing for longer.

Saturation caps at 20 and resets to 5 on death, exactly like Java.
