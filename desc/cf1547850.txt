This is a MCPE Optimizer+ pack, not only boosts FPS, it also has many QoL Features.

The Features:

Outlined ores(makes ores more visible in the darkness so caving is easier)

Subpacks(default and visible particles for escaping trolls)

No Pumpkin blur(makes your vision still good even when u wear a carved pumpkin)

Clear Spyglass(removes the black side of spyglass zoom so ur vision when zooming is better)

Small swords(swords don't block your vision because it's smaller)

Obvious player potion particles(makes seeing invisible players easier *requires visible player particles subpack activated*)

Low fire(makes fire not block your vision anymore)

Clear water(makes seeing things underwater easier)

Clear lava(You can see under the lava now!)

Bow Charging Indicator(makes seeing ur bow charging level easier)

Visible powdered snow(makes powdered snow not look like regular snow so you don't sink when traveling in snowy mountains)

Low shield(using a shield doesn't block your vision anymore)

Clear nether(vision in nether is upgraded)

And still Many more features
