Take your Minecraft Bedrock survival world beyond the normal limits with No Limits! This add-on pushes enchantments far past vanilla caps

How It Works:

To upgrade an enchantment, you need:

- Level 30+ to start. The cost goes up every 10 enchant levels.

- An Enchanting Table to interact with.

- A max vanilla enchant on the item. For example, Sharpness 5 or Mending 1.

Settings:

- Customize max enchant level to fit your world.

- Cheat mode toggle that ignores the XP requirement. With cheat mode on, players only need to set the enchant level they want when using the Enchanting Table.

- Level Currency customize level needed to upgrade enchant
