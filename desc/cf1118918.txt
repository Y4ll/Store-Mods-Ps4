This resource pack hides all touch buttons (such as D-pad, joystick, chat, emote, menu, jump, crouch, atack, interact......,etc.)

If you don't like, how touch button reduce your visibility in Minecraft PE, then this pack is for you

- You can use this pack is servers, SMP's

- Works with other packs

- Survival world friendly (it's an RP)

- Hides every single touch button

- Has setting options for customisation

Why you use this pack in 1.20+ ?

As you know, that we can Customise touch controls in 1.20, but in that way we cannot make buttons 100% transparent, and it only works for few buttons, that's why you should use this pack even in 1.20+, because this pack makes all buttons transparent (such as chat, emote & menu buttons)

Note:-This pack only makes buttons transparent, which means all buttons will perfectly work and you can use them normally.
