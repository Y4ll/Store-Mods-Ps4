Vanilla Optimizer combines the best features of Sodium and FPS Optimizer into one ultra-lightweight FPS boost texture pack. Built to deliver smooth, lag-free gameplay on all devices, from low-end to mid-range.

By merging two of the most popular FPS packs, Vanilla Optimizer cuts out heavy effects, reduces particle load, trims animations, and simplifies textures, all while keeping the original Minecraft feel intact.

Requires Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.40 or newer.

In-Game Settings

Tap the gear icon next to the pack in your pack list to tune it without reinstalling:

- Potion & Spell Particles turn off the swirls around potion effects, witches, evokers and cauldrons

- Geyser & Gas Particles turn off geyser poofs and noxious gas

- XP Orb Render stop experience orbs drawing entirely. They still work and still get collected, so this is a big gain at grinders

Vibrant Visuals Support

Vibrant Visuals works with the pack enabled. Shadows are set to blocky and snapped to a 16px grid, matching vanilla's pixel size instead of soft-filtering every edge cheaper to draw and truer to the vanilla look.

Two Modes

Tap the pack name in your pack list to switch:

- Optimized Fog + Vanilla UI short fog draw, highest FPS

- Clear Fog + Clean UI long, clear view in every biome, plus the lightweight Clean UI
