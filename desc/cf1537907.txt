Neq's Better Tree Cutter & Vein Miner

Upgrade your Minecraft Bedrock survival experience with Neq's Tree Cutter & Vein Miner a powerful utility add-on designed to make resource gathering faster, smoother, and more satisfying in MCPE 1.21+.

Created by Neq Gamerz, this add-on introduces smart mining mechanics that help players cut entire trees and mine connected ore veins instantly while keeping gameplay balanced and survival-friendly.

Features

Tree Cutter System

- Break a single log while sneaking to cut down the entire tree instantly.

- Supports multiple tree types including:

- Oak

- Birch

- Spruce

- Jungle

- Mangrove

- Cherry

- Crimson & Warped stems

- Natural block drops are preserved.

- Fast and efficient wood farming for survival worlds.

Vein Miner System

- Mine connected ores instantly by breaking one ore block while sneaking.

- Works with:

- Coal

- Iron

- Gold

- Diamond

- Emerald

- Copper

- Redstone

- Lapis

- Nether Quartz

- Ancient Debris

- Deepslate variants

- Automatically detects connected ore clusters.

Enchantment Support

- Fully compatible with:

- Fortune Enchantment

- Silk Touch Enchantment

- Correct drops are generated based on your tool enchantments.

Gravel Miner

- Instantly clear connected gravel clusters.

- Makes cave exploration and mining much easier.

Optimized Performance

- Designed for Minecraft Bedrock 1.21+

- Lightweight and optimized scripting system.

- Smooth gameplay with survival-friendly mechanics.

Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition / MCPE 1.21+

- Works in Survival Worlds

- Multiplayer Compatible

- Script API Supported
