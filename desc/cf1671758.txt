Reze Visuals is a full PBR resource pack for Minecraft Bedrock that transforms lighting, skies, and atmosphere with a deep violet-blue aesthetic a vibrant, cinematic upgrade for players with Vibrant Visuals enabled.

Reimagined Visual Style

- Rich violet-blue color identity that feels vibrant without losing clarity

- Clean, immersive atmosphere layered across every vanilla biome

- Enhanced depth and mood without overwhelming builds

- Cinematic color grading with ACES tone mapping and a warm 6400K white balance

Advanced Atmospherics System

- Custom sky zenith and horizon color tuning for a smooth day-night gradient

- Warm golden-orange sunrise and sunset horizon tones with strong blending

- Deep blue-violet daytime zenith for added sky depth

- Tuned Rayleigh scattering to prevent washed-out or overly dark skies

Recolored Local Lighting

- Custom light colors for torches, lanterns, candles, campfires, and other light sources

- True-to-color glow from soul fire, redstone, copper variants, amethyst, and more

- Adds warmth and richer ambiance to builds without changing gameplay

Full PBR Material Support

- Metalness, emissive, roughness, and subsurface maps applied across blocks, actors, particles, and items

- PBR-enhanced ores and terrain, including all deepslate ore variants and ancient debris

- Reworked water with custom flow/still textures, normal maps, and MER maps for realistic reflections

Violet Aurora Effect

- Custom animated aurora beam effect with organic, curve-driven movement

- Adds a striking extra layer of atmosphere without distracting from gameplay

Requirements

Reze Visuals requires Minecraft Bedrock/PE version 1.21.80 or higher, with Vibrant Visuals (PBR rendering) enabled in video settings.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open to import into Minecraft

- Enable Reze Visuals in Global Resources

- Turn on Vibrant Visuals in video settings
