This resource pack lets you change tridents into the aspects of Varatha The Eternal Spear from the game Hades.
Due to technical limitations, each trident is in a separate pack. SPEAR Version is also available!

- Guan Yu - Iron Spear

- Achilles - Gold Spear

- Hades - Diamond Spear

- Zagreus - Netherite Spear

Trident packs are not compatible with each other.

Modelled and textured by Mystickoko using Blockbench.
