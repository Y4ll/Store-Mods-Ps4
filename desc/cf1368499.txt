Digital Miner Addon v1.0

The Digital Miner Addon is a technology addon, but it's not difficult to use or create.
It's compatible with addons that add blocks to the world.

- Craft:

> Digital Miner:

To start on this addon you will need a hammer.

With the Hammer you can create "Iron Dust" and "Coal Dust".

By combining these two dusts you can create a "Steel Dust" and smelting it in a furnace you get a "Steel Nugget".

With 9 Steel Nuggets you can create 1 Steel Ingot;

With 9 Steel Ingot create 1 Steel Block.

Tips: Coal, Iron and Gold Dust can be placed in the furnace as fuel or ore (a small duplication of ores, so to speak).

Alright, after this, you can start a long jorney;

The Digital Miner need some components to be created, like:

- 4 Steel Plates;

- 1 Monitor;

- 1 Digital Processor;

- 1 Machine Engine;

- 1 Laser Drill.

See how to make all components below on the spoiler:

- Monitor:

Monitor is maked with:

- 4 Steel Ingots;

- 4 Integrated Circuits;

- 1 Diamond Glass.

The Diamond Glass is simple to create, 4 diamonds and 1 glass.

For the Integrated Circuit you will need, Capacitors and Steel Nuggets

- Digital Processor:

The Digital Processor is a little more complicated, because it'll use 4 Advanced Circuits, 1 Steel Plate, 2 Diamond Plates and 2 Gold Plates.

To craft the Advanced Circuit need 4 Diamond Plates, 2 Integrated Circuits, 2 Redstone Enriched Quartz and 1 Amthyst Shard.

Plates and quartz craft is more simple.

- Machine Engine:

It's the easiest craft of this addon, only 3 Steel Blocks, 2 Redstone Blocks, 1 Coal Block and 1 Blast Furnace.

- Laser Drill:

Laser Drill is easy too, 1 Diamond Glass, 1 Light Generator, 1 Steel Plate, 2 Redstone Enriched Quartz and 2 Steel Ingot.

> Ore Scanner:

Were you shocked by the cost of the Digital Miner? Don't worry, I guarantee you'll be able to more than triple those resources in just a few minutes after you get it.

But I know it would be difficult to collect all these ores, so make an Ore Scanner to help (Read how it work below).

The Upgrades helps you increase the Digital Miner efficiency.

The base for any Upgrade is a Template, it's make with:

- 2 Coal Dusts;

- 2 Gold Plates;

- 2 Steel Ingots;

- 1 Diamond Plate;

- 1 Integrated Circuit.

- How to Configure your Digital Miner:

Before you start, make sure you place a chest behide the Digital Miner for it works well.

> Main Painel:

When you interact with the Miner, a screen will appear saying:

- The amount of blocks found to mine;

- A button to Start or Stop the mine;

- A button to set filters;

- A button to install upgrades;

- A button to configure the Miner;
