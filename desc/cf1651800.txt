Bloom Visuals is a pack I made to give every biome its own mood instead of just copy-pasting the same lighting everywhere. Snowy villages get auroras, cherry blossom groves get a soft pink glow, and forests get a warm sunlit feel.

I also added PBR support so metal blocks and items get a bit of reflection on them. Kept it subtle, I didn't want it to look fake or overdone.

What this changes

Bloom

Light bleeds softly around bright things instead of stopping at a hard edge. Lanterns, the sun and the aurora all glow outward a little. This is where the pack gets its name and its whole feel.

Per-biome mood

Every biome is tuned on its own. Snowy villages, cherry groves and dark forests each get their own lighting rather than one setting stretched across the map.

Night sky and auroras

Starry nights with auroras running over them. They read best over snow and open plains, where there is something for the light to land on.

Sunrise and sunset

Warm golden light coming in low, with a pink lean at dawn that carries over into the rest of the pack.

Light shafts

Godrays break through canopies in dark forests and birch woods. With the bloom on top, this is the best looking part of the pack.

Fog

A bit more fog than vanilla for depth, mainly in forests so trees separate out instead of flattening into one wall.

PBR

Metal blocks and items pick up reflections, but kept deliberately subtle. I did not want gear looking like plastic.

PBR is also the heaviest part of any Vibrant Visuals pack, so it is not forced on you. It ships as a subpack, which means you can switch it on or off yourself. When you activate Bloom Visuals in Global Resources, pick the version you want. Turn PBR off if your device runs hot or drops frames.

Requirements

THIS PACK IS FOR VIBRANT VISUALS ONLY
IT DOES NOT WORK WITH MB LOADER

Vibrant Visuals must be ON. This pack is built for Bedrock's Vibrant Visuals renderer. The pack will not do anything if it is off.

Where to turn it on: Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals

If that toggle is not there, your device or version does not support Vibrant Visuals yet.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Bloom Visuals

- Pick the subpack you want. PBR on for the full look, PBR off for better performance.

- Turn on Settings > Video > Vibrant Visuals

- If the lighting still looks the same, leave the world and load it again once
