New Armored Elytras

Beharvior & Resources Packs Needed

This addon does not disable achievements.

Armored Elytras / Colytras combine the armor protection of a chestplate with the flight of an Elytra.

(The armor armount is the same as the chestplate it was made from)

Armor toughness and knockback resistance, in the case of Netherite, are preserved.

Broken Colytras stop providing armor and can be repaired using Phantom Membranes or ingots.

Supported Languages:
All
