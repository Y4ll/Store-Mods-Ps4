Simplest Hammers, Excavators & Scythes

A simple, vanilla-friendly tool expansion for Minecraft Bedrock.

This add-on adds Hammers, Excavators, and Scythes in multiple tiers, designed to make mining, digging, farming, and clearing vegetation faster without overcomplicating the game.

Hammers

Hammers work like 3x3 pickaxes, making them perfect for mining, tunneling, and clearing stone-heavy areas.

- Mine blocks in a 3x3 area

- Designed for pickaxe-type blocks

- Great for caves, tunnels, and large mining projects

- Available in multiple material tiers

Excavators

Excavators work like 3x3 shovels, ideal for clearing dirt, sand, gravel, snow, and other soft terrain.

- Dig terrain in a 3x3 area

- Useful for terraforming and large builds

- Designed for shovel-type blocks

- Speeds up repetitive digging tasks

Scythes

Scythes are farming and vegetation-clearing tools.

- Till farmland in a 3x3 area

- Harvest mature crops

- Automatically replant harvested crops

- Clear grass and weeds in a 3x3 area

- Great for farms, fields, and landscaping
