Simple Loot Booster - Tracker Tutorial

HOW TO GET THE TRACKER

1. Get a book

2. Get an anvil and some XP

3. Place the anvil and rename the book to: tracker

4. The book is now your tracker

HOW TO OPEN THE TRACKER

1. Hold the tracker book in your hand

2. Right-click / tap the use button

3. The stats UI will open

WHAT YOU CAN SEE

- Mob Kills - how many mobs you killed with boost active

- Items Duped - how many items were duplicated

- Bonus XP - how much bonus XP you received

- Subpack - which boost tier is active (x2, x3, x4, x5)

- Status - whether the boost is enabled or disabled

- Version - current addon version

HOW TO RESET YOUR STATS

1. Open the tracker

2. Press Reset My Stats

3. Your stats are cleared

ADMIN FEATURES (Operators or admin tag only)

- View any player's stats

- Reset any player's stats

- Enable or disable the boost for everyone

- To give admin tag: /tag <name> add admin

NOTES

- Crops and ores will NOT be duplicated

- Armor and weapons will NOT be duplicated

- Only player kills trigger the boost

- Boost has a 1 second cooldown per kill

Description:

Loot Booster enhances your Minecraft experience by increasing item drops from mobs and bosses, making every battle more rewarding. You can choose between Normal Mode for a balanced experience or Boosted Mode for higher drop rates.

It does not affect mob equipment, keeping gameplay fair and preventing free gear farming. Built to be achievement-friendly and fully compatible with multiplayer, this addon works perfectly in both solo and shared worlds.

The addon is also actively maintained, with regular bug fixes and improvements. Suggestions are always welcome to help make the addon even better.
