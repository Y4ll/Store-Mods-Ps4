How to Use

- Download and import the addon.

- Enable the Behavior Pack in your world.

- Join the world for the first time.

- Take the Starter Stick you receive.

- Use the stick to open the Kit Selection menu.

- Select OP Kit and receive your complete loadout.

Important Information

- No Cheats Needed The kit works through normal addon gameplay without requiring cheats or command access.

- No Experimental Toggles Required You can use the addon without enabling Experimental Gameplay settings.

- The kit is designed to give you a powerful head start in Survival.

- The Starter Stick is provided automatically on your first spawn.

How To Import

Android: Download the .mcaddon file, open it with Minecraft, and wait for the addon to import. Then activate the Behavior Pack from your world settings.

Windows: Download the .mcaddon file and double-click it to open it with Minecraft. Wait for the import to finish, then activate the Behavior Pack in your world settings.

Remember: The real fun of Minecraft is the grind. This addon is made for quick gameplay and a faster start for the full Minecraft experience, enjoy the progression and grind in the actual game.
