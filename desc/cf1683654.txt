Minecraft Bedrock hides a lot from you. Saturation decides how long a meal lasts and is never shown. Regeneration crawls compared to Java. Tools break with no warning, beds explode with no warning, and a cured villager says nothing at all. Vanilla Essentials fixes 16 of those, no commands, no crafting, no extra items.

- Features

Food and health

- Java saturation bar The hidden buffer that decides how long food lasts, drawn right on your hunger bar

- Java regeneration Heals at Java's fast rate while your hunger bar is full, and charges saturation for it exactly as Java does

On-screen warnings

- Durability warning Tells you before a tool, weapon or armour piece breaks

- Bed explosion warning Says so before you blow yourself up in the Nether or the End

- Inventory full alert Know the moment your mining haul starts hitting the floor

- Enchanting level alert Marks level 30, the number every enchanting table wants

Light

- Dynamic lighting Carry a torch, lantern, or any light source and it lights the world around you

- Off-hand torches Crouch to move a torch to your off hand and keep your main hand free

Quality of life

- Item magnet Crouch and dropped items drift to you, so a haul doesn't need sweeping off the floor

- XP magnet Orbs come to you instead of sitting over the lava you just knocked something into

- Crop experience Fully grown crops give a little XP when harvested

- Loyalty trident rescue A Loyalty trident thrown over the void comes back instead of being lost

Information

- Day counter The day number at dawn and when you join

- Respawn point Tells you where you'll actually wake up after sleeping or charging an anchor

- Sleep counter How many players still need to be in bed to skip the night

- Villager cure alert Curing a zombie villager already discounts its trades permanently; now you're told it worked

- Bell raid reveal Ring a bell during a raid and every nearby raider is marked, with a count and a bearing to the closest

- Coordinate converter /scriptevent ve:coords gives your position and the matching coordinates in the other dimension

World and recipes

- Ore Boost Extra veins of coal, copper, iron, gold, redstone, lapis and diamond, generated the way vanilla generates its own

- Better recipes Horse armour becomes craftable, rotten flesh smelts into leather, wool turns back into string, and other even trades vanilla never added

Everything else

- Achievement-safe Does NOT disable achievements or trophies. No experiments required

- Most features toggleable Switch any of them on or off in game, saved per world

- Multiplayer ready Works independently for each player

- Light on performance No per-tick scanning; everything runs on timed intervals and stops when there is nothing to do

- Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.90+

- Works on Android, iOS, Windows, Console

- Behavior Pack and Resource Pack both required

- Compatible with other addons. The Resource Pack edits the HUD, so place it above other HUD packs if they conflict

- Installation

- Download both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack

- Tap/double-click each to auto-import into Minecraft

- Apply both to your world, under Behavior Packs and Resource Packs

- Play. Everything is on by default
