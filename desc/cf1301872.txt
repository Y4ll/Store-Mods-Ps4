This resource pack removes the default borders from all glass blocks and panes in Minecraft Bedrock Edition, giving them a smoother, more modern, and visually appealing appearance.

Its perfect for clean builds, modern architecture, or simply if you want a less obstructed view through glass.

What does it change?

Removes all borders from glass blocks and panes, giving them a cleaner and more minimalist look.
