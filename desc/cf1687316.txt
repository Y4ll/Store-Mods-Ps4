Elytra Animation Bug Fix

Elytra Animation Bug Fix is an add-on that fixes a Minecraft Bedrock Edition multiplayer animation bug where other players appear to glide with an Elytra while standing upright instead of lying horizontally.

Related Bug Reports
MCPE-176172
MCPE-178376

With this add-on enabled, other players gliding poses are displayed correctly, making Elytra flight look more natural in multiplayer.

Starting with version 1.3, the fix will also apply to players who are already gliding when they are loaded!

The resource packonly version has been uploaded!
