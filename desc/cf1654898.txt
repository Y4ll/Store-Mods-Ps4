Ethereal Mystic Reworked is a cinematic Vibrant Visuals shader pack that transforms Minecraft's atmosphere and lighting into a mystical, vibrant world. Built around rich skies, enhanced lighting, atmospheric fog, and magical color grading, it gives every biome a more immersive and ethereal appearance while keeping the original Minecraft style intact.

- Features

- Custom-built orange-yellow sun glow with warm golden lighting during sunrise, sunset, and daytime

- Enhanced color grading with deeper shadows, vibrant midtones, and cinematic highlights for a richer visual atmosphere

- Custom-tuned local lighting for torches, lanterns, candles, and glowing blocks with improved source colors

- Atmospheric fog profiles for air, water, and weather tuned around warm orange-yellow tones for a richer and more immersive ambience

- Custom yellow-red aurora effect that adds a magical atmosphere to nighttime skies

- Enhanced sky colors with smoother transitions between day, sunset, twilight, and night

- Improved environmental lighting that gives caves, forests, oceans, and open landscapes more visual depth

- Soft and refined shadows for cleaner edges and a more natural-looking world

- Performance-focused visual enhancements designed to deliver a beautiful experience without unnecessary visual clutter

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.80+

- Vibrant Visuals enabled

- Recommended: mid-to-high end device for the best visual experience

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it with Minecraft installed it will import automatically

- In-game: Settings Global Resources Ethereal Mystic Reworked Activate

- Enable Vibrant Visuals in video settings for the full effect
