Automate your resource gathering with passive generation, even while you're offline!

Place a generator, walk away, and come back to full storages. Perfect for Skyblock, survival, or any world where you want steady resource income without constant mining or chopping. Generators run on real-world time, so they keep producing whether you're online, offline, or the world is closed.

Custom Recipes (new in v1.5.0)

Want different recipes for your world, server or Realm? Change any generator's recipe in the online Recipe Editor: pick the ingredients on a crafting grid and download a small pack. No files to edit by hand.

- Open the Recipe Editor, choose Bedrock and change the recipes you want.

- Download the pack and open the .mcpack file: Minecraft imports it.

- Edit your world Behavior Packs activate Idle Generators: Recipe Overrides and move it above Idle Generators.

Works on Realms, a new download replaces the old pack, and our updates never overwrite your recipes.

Advanced: editing the JSON by hand

The .mcpack is a normal zip: rename it to .zip, extract it and edit the files in recipes/crafting/ (one per generator, for example iron_generator.json). pattern is the 33 grid and key says which item each letter is; change only item ids, never identifier or result. Zip the contents again (manifest.json at the top level), rename to .mcpack and import it.

Features

69 Unique Generators in six sets: Minerals, Wood, Stone & Construction, Colors, Nature and Nether.

Offline Generation: resources keep generating when you're not playing. Uses real-world time to produce items, so play however you like: the generators never stop.

Smart Withdrawal System

- Left-click: withdraw 1 item

- Shift + left-click: withdraw up to a full stack (64)

Withdrawals drain the accumulated buffer, and sub-cycle progress is never lost.

Auto-output: place a chest, barrel, hopper or other container directly below a generator and it empties itself; a hopper below chains into your item network.

Settings Menu: operators run /idlegen:config to switch single generators or whole sets on/off, with each switch showing its production. No cheats needed, so achievements stay enabled. Prefer world-level control? Behavior Packs Idle Generators gear icon turns whole sets off before entering the world.

Animated Models: each generator is a unique animated visual entity.

HUD Readout: look at a generator to see its glyph, name, current amount / capacity, and progress % on the action bar.

Storage Caps: generators buffer 81024 items depending on the type (the Nether Star keeps just 8).

Vibrant Visuals Compatible: works alongside Vibrant Visuals and third-party PBR resource packs.

Balanced Progression: tuned for vanilla worlds. Feedback welcome!

Supported Languages: English, Espanol.

The Generators

Craft a generator, place it, and collect. Every set shares one recipe layout, so once you know a set you know all of it. Open a set to see its recipes and stats.

Minerals (11): Cobblestone, Coal, Copper, Iron, Redstone, Lapis, Gold, Quartz, Emerald, Diamond, Netherite

Each mineral generator uses glass on the corners, a themed material, and the resource's block in the center.

Wood Logs (12): Oak, Spruce, Birch, Jungle, Acacia, Dark Oak, Mangrove, Cherry, Pale Oak, Poplar, Crimson, Warped

All 12 share the same pattern: glass corners, bone meal, that wood's sapling (3) and its log in the center. Mangrove uses a Propagule, and Crimson / Warped Stems use their fungus.

Stone & Construction (12): Stone, Granite, Diorite, Andesite, Deepslate, Tuff, Calcite, Dripstone, Gravel, Sand, Red Sand, Clay

All 12 share the same layout: glass on the corners, cobblestone on the sides, a themed catalyst on top & bottom, and the output's block in the center. Red Sand uses sand on the sides; Dripstone and Clay craft with their block but produce the item (Pointed Dripstone / Clay Balls).
