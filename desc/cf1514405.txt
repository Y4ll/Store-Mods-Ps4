Overview

Something is "protecting" your world, and he's really jealous. "Omniville" introduces a powerful new threat (with plenty of animations) , a rare resource, and a full equipment set all seamlessly integrated into vanilla survival.

...discover what it means to live alongside him.

Meet the Omniville

Omniville is a villager... evolved. Enhanced. Unstable.
He watches over the world, protecting it and wants to be seen as the greatest hero Minecraft has ever known.

And as long as you don't interfere... you are safe.

Omniville watches and judges everything you do.
He does not fight evil: he fights anyone who dares to question whether he is the one true hero.

Omniville is a rare and extremely powerful Villager that spawns naturally in Villages.

It may look simple but it is anything but.

Complex Abilities:

Laser Attack fires a destructive beam with adjustable range Flight takes to the air with custom animations Kick/Clap/Airslam etc... launches players with massive knockback Shockwave releases a ground impact blast

Stats:

Cannot take damage from weapons that arent the Noxite Sword

Flexing:

Omniville is equipped with many animations by its own, but he also has 9 flex animations to AURA FARM on his fellow Villagers, or his victims.

Noxite Ore

A rare material hidden deep underground. (deepslate level)

Requires Diamond or Netherite pickaxe Diamond-tier hardness Drops raw Noxite

Used to craft powerful new equipment to have a chance at fighting Omniville.

Noxite Equipment, Forge a complete gear set:

Noxite Sword custom weapon (5-tick cooldown, 300 durability)

Full Armor Set Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots

Armor and sword are crafted using the Crafting Table.

Omniville is watching. You dont need to fight him. You just need to not disappoint him.
