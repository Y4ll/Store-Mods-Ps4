Player Hitbox Indicator

Greetings, this is a simple combat hitbox pack

This pack adds a clean wireframe outline around every player. Shifts from white red when player is in hitrange

Features

white by default red when in reach

Follows player state crouch, swim, sleep

Two modes: normal & see through walls

You can switch the state or on and off the hitbox in middle of the game by pack settings

Install

Download Open Apply in Global Resources > done.
