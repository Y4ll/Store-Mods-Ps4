This Add-On brings more life into your Minecraft world by adding in 6 new village types, as well as an adittional structure for the illagers!

WARNING: This is a BETA version of the Add-On, expect some minor bugs, but most importantly, expect future updates!

UPDATE 0.7

Sandy Village

Found on desert biomes. Loosely based on middle eastern bronze age architecture.

Sandy Village (Saffron Variant)

Found on mesa biomes.

Timber Village

Found on plains and taigas. A rustic looking village mainly comprised of wooden roofs and such.

Pillager Wild Camps

Found all over the overworld. These camps can spawn with several small structures such as tents, farms, watchtowers and more. The chests contain some loot and, like other structures, may contain a map to the "Big" Illager Camp from this Add-On.

UPDATE 0.5

Dark Oak Village

Found on the dark oak forest biome

Pale Garden Village

Why would anyone want to live here? I do not know but it can be found on the pale garden biome.

Arid Village

A colorful town, with randomly colored houses and buildings! Found in arid biomes such as deserts, savannas, or badlands.

Mushroom Village

Can be found on the plains biome as well as mushroom islands.

REVAMPED -> Village Layouts

All villages now have revamped layout and path generation, instead of the previous straight paths now all villages present more varied layouts as well as more detailed paths.

The classic villages plugin has also received updated paths system, which also results in more varied layouts for the classic villages

UPDATE 0.2

Jungle Village:

A cozy village, hanging gently in along the jungle trees. Found in the the Jungle Edge biome

Illager Watchtower

An illager made tower, you fill find a considerable amount of illager here,if you get to the top you might find a in the chests a map that can lead

you to an Illager Camp, as well as some other loot

REVAMPED -> Illager Watchhut

Now with a new look, a simple and small hut where you will find one or two illagers, however if you look inside you might find a map that can lead you to an Illager Camp!

REVAMPED -> Illager Camps

Now with a new look and improved generation, it's a well guarded encampment, you will find lots of Pillagers here, you might even find captive villagers!

Illager Camps ALSO feature a map room in the central building. In here, along with some loot, you might find maps that can lead you to some of the other villages of this very Add-On

If you follow these maps you will find a village according to what the map name says
