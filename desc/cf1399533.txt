Do you want to see your players while they're playing? Well, this resource pack is for you!

This resource pack adds a paper doll player to your HUD, chest, and crafting screens. You can also use this resource pack to view your armor.

There are 2 subpacks in this resource pack: 'Dynamic, but not always visible' and 'Always visible, but flat'.
