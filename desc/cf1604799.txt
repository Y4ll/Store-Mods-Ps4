One addon, nine smart breaking tools in a single clean menu. Dynamic Capacitor gives every tool a superpower mine entire ore veins, fell whole trees, harvest full crop patches, and clear stacked plants all in one break. Toggle each one on or off whenever you want.

Capacitors

- Ore Vein Miner pickaxe mines every connected ore block in the vein at once, Fortune enchant fully supported

- Tree Capacitor axe fells the entire tree in one break, all log types and stripped variants included

- Leaf Shears shears collect every connected leaf block at once, any leaf type

- Crop Harvester hoe harvests every ripe crop in the surrounding patch and auto-replants instantly

- Sugar Cane break the bottom stalk and the entire column harvests at once

- Bamboo break the base and the full bamboo stack harvests in one hit

- Cactus break the bottom and the full cactus column drops at once

- Kelp break the base and the entire kelp strand harvests instantly

- All Blocks break any block and every connected block of the same type breaks with it (use with caution)

Global Settings

- Master Toggle enable or disable all capacitors at once with a single button

- Auto Collect all drops go straight into your inventory instead of scattering on the ground

- Replant Crops harvested crops are automatically replanted in the same spots

- Max Blocks slider set how many blocks each capacitor can break per action, from 16 to 512

Commands

- /voxen:dc open the Dynamic Capacitor menu

Achievement Friendly?

Yes fully achievement friendly. Use it without any worries.
