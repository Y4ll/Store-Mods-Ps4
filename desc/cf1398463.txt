Decorate your Minecraft world using simple furniture.

- All furniture are designed to blend into your own Minecraft worlds, both in survival and creative.

- Includes tables and wall-mounted shelves, and 3 types of seating.

- Interact with a seat to sit on it. Interact with a table to crawl beneath it.

- Plus, for additional decoration, some select Minecraft items can be placed on tables and wall-mounted shelves.

95 Seating Blocks

- 23 Chairs, using wooden and metal variations

- 24 Log Benches, using all wood and stripped variations

- 48 Thrones, using wood, gold and netherite, with padding in all 16 colors each

23 Tables and 23 Wall-mounted Shelves

- Using wooden and metal variations.

- Oak, Birch, Spruce, Jungle, Acacia, Dark Oak, Mangrove, Cherry, Pale Oak, Bamboo, Crimson & Warped

- Iron, Copper (8 variations of oxidization and waxed), Gold & Netherite

Decorate your tables and wall-mounted shelves using standard Minecraft items.

- Place bowls, soups and stews.

- Place stacks of paper or empty maps.

- Place glass or water bottles.

- Place stacks of books on tables, or arrange on shelves.

Interact with furniture and decoration for additional gameplay.

- Interact with any seat to sit in it.

- Interact with the side of a table to crawl beneath it.

- Add items to stacks, or sneak while interacting to take items away.

Additional Information

Crafting

- Tables, chairs, thrones and wall-mounted shelves can be crafted using sticks and either slabs (for wood furniture) or ingots (for metal).

- Log Benches use logs or stripped logs to craft (including stems and bamboo blocks); a log bench cannot be stripped once crafted.

- To craft netherite furniture, combine the gold version with Netherite Scraps.

- Thrones require wool to be crafted and will the same color for the padding.

- Craft any thone with another colored dye to change its color.

Creative

- All furniture can be found in the Creative - Items menu.

- Block versions for items can also be found in this menu.

Placing

- Thrones are 2 blocks high and require an empty space above the block you are placing it onto.

- Consecutively placed tables will automatically remove the adjoining legs. Only legs on corners will remain.

- Shelves will adapt to bend around corners.

- The listed compatible items can only be added individually to the tops of table or shelf blocks.

- But, using Creative mode, their equivelent blocks can be placed anywhere.

- The number of items in a block can be adjusted by interacting in both Creative and Survival.

- Items that stack upwards have no limit to the stack size.

- Items cannot be mixed in the same block; e.g. you cannot add a water bottle to a glass bottle block.
