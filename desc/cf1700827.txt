With this add-on you can pat mobs and players. Crouch and hit a mob with an empty hand and it won't take damage, you'll pat it instead. A hand shows up over its head, the mob squishes down a bit and you hear a pat sound. Other players can see it too.

It works on pretty much every mob, so you can pat your dog, a villager, a creeper or your friend.

How To Use

Empty your hand, crouch and hit the mob. On mobile just crouch and tap the mob.

If you want to use right click instead, change it in the settings. After that you crouch and right click the mob, or hold on the mob on mobile.

Settings

Type /voxen:patpat to open the settings. You don't need cheats for it, and every player has their own settings.

- Enable Mod - turn it on or off

- Pat Button - Left Click (Hit or Click) or Right Click (Hold Screen)

- Who Can Be Patted - everyone, only wolf, wolf and players, only players, only tamed pets, only passive mobs or only hostile mobs

- Sounds and Volume

- Swing Hand - your arm moves when you pat with right click

- Camera Shaking - your screen shakes a little when someone pats you

- Enable Pat Me - turn this off if you don't want other players patting you

- Pat When Attacking - also pat mobs while fighting them (they still take damage)
