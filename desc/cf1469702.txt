Great Hunger

This mob is constantly hungry for tools. Offer it an unenchanted tool, and it will return it imbued with a random Level I enchantment.

WildFire

This powerful Blaze possesses immense resilience. You must break all of its shields before you can bring it down.

Barnacle

The Barnacle is a dangerous creature that inhabits the ocean, capable of dragging you into the depths. Be careful not to get too close, or you may find yourself pulled into the abyss.

Moobloom

The Moobloom has a deep fondness for dandelion. Be sure to offer one when you encounter it.

Iceologer

Beware of the Iceologer when it detects you, it will hurl a massive block of ice in your direction.

Glare

Lurking in the darkness, hostile entities gather. The Glare will warn you when darkness is nearby.

Tuff Golem

This little golem loves items. Interact with it to wake it up whenever it falls asleep!

Rascal

This small and mischievous rascal loves to play hide and seek. Good luck finding it... you might earn a reward.

Crab

Obtain the powerful Crab Claw to extend your block placement reach.

Penguin

Bring a penguin to the edge of your boat to increase its speed.
