Enchanted Outline

Ever lost your best sword or pickaxe in a chest full of junk? Yeah, we fixed that.

Enchanted Outline gives your enchanted gear a clean, glowing outline so it actually stands out no more squinting through a messy inventory or guessing which item is the good one mid-fight. It still feels 100% vanilla, just... better.

Pickaxe Full Customization

The pickaxe is where this pack really shows off. Every visual element is independently adjustable:

Separate Glint & Outline Colors** mix and match freely, they don't need to be the same color

Adjustable Outline Size** control outline thickness independently of the glint

Independent Sizing** fine-tune how the whole effect scales on the item

3 Rainbow Mode choose your vibe:

0 Off static colors, no animation

1 Outline Rainbow outline color cycles automatically every 2 seconds

2 Full Rainbow\ both glint *and* outline cycle together for a fully animated look

Sword Core Features

The sword keeps things simple for now, with more advanced options planned for a future update:

4 Sword Size Options** go small and subtle or big and bold

Adjustable Outline Size** control how thick or thin the glow looks

Glint & Outline Color** currently matched (independent colors coming soon )

Setup

No config files, no complicated steps:

1 Download the pack

2 Drag **Enchanted Outline** to the **top** of your active resource pack list

3 That's it you're done!

Works alongside your other packs just make sure it's loaded above them.
