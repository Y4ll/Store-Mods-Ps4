ACD Shaders

ACD Shaders is a high-quality shader pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition, designed to deliver realistic graphics and cinematic lighting while maintaining excellent performance on mid-range devices.

Built using Minecraft's official Vibrant Visuals rendering system, ACD Shaders enhances the game's atmosphere with realistic lighting, animated water, soft shadows, volumetric effects, and improved environmental details without compromising performance.

REQUIRES VIBRANT VISUALS TO WORK

Features

- Realistic dynamic lighting
- Animated Gerstner water
- Volumetric God Rays
- Atmospheric height fog
- Volumetric clouds
- Soft shadows
- Weather-based visual effects
- Improved reflections
- Enhanced End sky
- Custom Nether atmosphere
- Cinematic ACES color grading
- Animated vegetation
- Optimized for mid-range devices

Performance

ACD Shaders has been carefully optimized to provide an excellent balance between visual quality and performance. It is designed for players who want a modern graphical experience without requiring high-end hardware.

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition (1.20.x, 1.21.x or 26.x)
- Vibrant Visuals enabled
- Compatible with the official RenderDragon renderer

Installation

1. Download the .mcpack file.
2. Open it with Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
3. Activate the Resource Pack.
4. Enable Vibrant Visuals in your world's graphics settings.
5. Launch your world and enjoy ACD Shaders.

Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition
- Windows
- Android
- iOS
- Xbox (only with some file emulator)
- Other platforms compatible with RenderDragon and Vibrant Visuals (it can also be without vibrant visuals)
