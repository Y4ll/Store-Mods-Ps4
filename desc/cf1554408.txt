Alylica's Dungeons - Music Box is a subpack for Alylica's Dungeons

When the two add-ons are used together, Each boss battle will have its own theme music that plays in the background, additionally, Boss Loot will be updated to include new Music Discs to represent each track.

Included Tracks are:

Enderman (Peter Hont)
Redstone Monstrosity (Johan Johnson)
Necromancer (Johan Johnson)
Cauldron (Johan Johnson)
The Arch-Illager (Peter Hont)
Tempest Golem (Peter Hont)
Ancient Guardian (Peter Hont)
Broken Heart of Ender (Peter Hont)
To The Beginning (Peter Hont)
Wildfire (Peter Hont)
Ancient (Peter Hont)
Haunted Monstrosity (Remix) (Alylica)
Heart of Ender (Remix) (Alylica)

All music not remixed by me (Alylica) is permitted for use as stated on the official page Can I Use Minecraft Music? - all tracks featured are accessible via the Creator-Safe Playlist

This pack requires no experiments, it must be applied below Alylica's Dungeons for it to work!

Also, make sure you have music actually turned on, lol
