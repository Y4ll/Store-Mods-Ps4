See the Night Like Day with PermaNG!

Tired of fumbling in the dark? Do torches feel like a chore, constantly needing to be crafted and placed? Say goodbye to the endless struggle for light and embrace the crystal-clear visibility of Permanent Night Vision (PermaNG)!

Created by Jihrexz 666 & TerraDaIdiot, PermaNG revolutionizes your Minecraft Bedrock experience by banishing the darkness forever. No more missing hidden ores, stumbling into unexpected mobs, or struggling to navigate the deepest caves. With this add-on, the night will become as bright and clear as day, allowing you to explore, build, and survive with unparalleled ease.

PermaNG introduces two essential new items to your game:

* The Activation Eye: Use this mystical item to instantly grant yourself permanent night vision. Once activated, the effect will persist through death, ensuring you never lose your enhanced sight.

* The Deactivation Eye: Need a break from the constant brightness? Simply use the Deactivation Eye to toggle off your night vision whenever you wish.

And don't worry about misplacing these vital tools! Both the Activation and Deactivation Eyes can be easily crafted if you happen to lose them, ensuring you always have control over your vision.

Explore every cavern, conquer every dark corner, and build without limits. With PermaNG, you'll never worry again about not seeing what's in those giant caves or anywhere else in the Minecraft world. Experience the ultimate freedom of permanent light and see what you've been missing!
