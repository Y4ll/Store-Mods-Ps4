Fishing Is OP

Turn every fishing trip into an exciting treasure hunt with Fishing Is OP by FleetMc Studio. This addon completely upgrades Minecraft's fishing loot, replacing the usual fish and junk with valuable rewards that can help you progress much faster in Survival.

Whether you're fishing in rivers, oceans, or jungles, every successful catch has the chance to reward you with rare resources, powerful equipment, and other overpowered loot. Grab a fishing rod, cast your line, and see what incredible treasures you can pull from the water!

Possible Rewards

- Netherite Ingots

- Netherite Blocks

- Diamonds

- Diamond Blocks

- Emeralds

- Emerald Blocks

- Dragon Egg

- Nether Stars

- Totems of Undying

- Elytra

- Beacon

- ...and many more valuable rewards!

Features

- Completely replaces vanilla fishing loot.

- Fish anywhere to receive powerful rewards.

- Works in rivers, oceans, and jungle fishing locations.

- Perfect for Survival gameplay.

- Makes fishing useful and rewarding.

- Lightweight and optimized.

- Achievement Friendly.

- No Experimental Gameplay required.

How to Use

- Download and import the addon.

- Enable the Behavior Pack.

- Join your world.

- Craft or obtain a Fishing Rod.

- Start fishing and enjoy your upgraded loot!

Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

- Achievement Friendly.

- No Experimental Gameplay Required.

- Singleplayer & Multiplayer Supported.
