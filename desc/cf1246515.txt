Changes

- New complementary auroras have been added to the sky.

- New colors in the sky, more similar to Complementary Reimagined.,

- Implemented the Low Subpack

- Stars identical to those in the Complementary Shader have been implemented.

- The white pixels in the textures have been removed.

- Glow effect on minerals and glowing blocks.

- The lava has been improved for a more realistic appearance.

- The terrain color has been improved, making it similar to that of the Complementary Shader.

- New Complementary End.

- Itemhand, itemtextured, itemcolor, and actorglint have been added

Notes

- Need IPA File in iOS.
