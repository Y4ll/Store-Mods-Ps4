RPG Logic is a progression addon for Minecraft Bedrock. It adds player levels, free stat allocation, life skills, scaling enemies, a mana system, potions, and wand magic while keeping vanilla survival and exploration intact.

Progression

- Gain EXP from hostiles; level up for free stat points (default 3/level; world setting 1100)

- Level cap 99, or optional permanent 999 at first-join World Setup

- Stats: VIT (HP) / STR (damage, uncapped) / DEF (mitigation up to 85%, optional Resistance milestones) / AGI (Speed) / DEX (crit)

- Free respec from Lv.30+

- Optional XP soft cap after Lv.50; craftable XP Boost (2x / ~10 min)

Combat & world

- Hostile levels from area, world age, clear bonuses, variance; optional distance-from-spawn scaling

- End Dragon / Warden clears permanently strengthen those regions

- Mob HP / damage / speed scaling (damage & speed toggles in settings)

- Rare Boss variants (~2.5%): much higher HP/stats/EXP

- Nametags show level and HP

Magic (Magic Wand)

Craft: shaped diagonal 1 Amethyst Shard + 2 Sticks
Controls: Use = cast / Sneak+Use = cycle spell

- Fireball Unlock Lv.7 / MP 12 Custom projectile look; STR scales

- Torch Unlock Lv.9 / MP 10 Temporary light that dims and fades

- Heal Unlock Lv.11 / MP 22 About 14% of max HP

- Blink Unlock Lv.13 / MP 14 Short teleport along look direction

- Lightning Unlock Lv.15 / MP 18 Strike + AoE damage; STR scales

- Mana Shield Unlock Lv.17 / MP 16 Short Resistance / Absorption

- Frost Nova Unlock Lv.19 / MP 20 Large slow AoE + FX

- Drain Unlock Lv.21 / MP 22 Large damage AoE + self-heal; STR scales

Spell damage scales with STR. Magic kills grant EXP. Max MP is 40 + Lv2, regenerates +1/s. Eating food restores +10 MP over time (potions excluded).

Crafting recipes (all custom items)

- Magic Wand shaped diagonal: Amethyst Shard + 2 Sticks

- XP Boost shapeless: Lapis Lazuli + Sugar + Gold Nugget (2x EXP / ~10 min)

- Healing Potion I (+10 HP) Sugar Cane + Poppy + Water Bottle

- Healing Potion II (+25 HP) Sugar Cane + Healing Potion I + Glowstone Dust

- Healing Potion III (+50 HP) Sugar Cane + Healing Potion II + Glowstone Dust

- Mana Potion I (+15 MP) Sugar Cane + Blue Orchid + Water Bottle

- Mana Potion II (+35 MP) Sugar Cane + Mana Potion I + Glowstone Dust

- Mana Potion III (+60 MP) Sugar Cane + Mana Potion II + Glowstone Dust

Healing / mana potions: 5s cooldown per category. Vanilla Instant Health scales with max HP.

HUD & skills

- Top-right status HUD; hotbar-side HP/MP bars

- Life skills: Mining, Farming, Husbandry

- First-join World Setup. Hub via /rl:menu, or rename an item to menu and use it

Commands

- /rl:st allocate stats

- /rl:set world settings (operators)

- /rl:hu HUD toggle

- /rl:menu hub

- /rl:setup world setup
