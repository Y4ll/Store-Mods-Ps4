FEATURES

Instant Block Intelligence Aim at any block and the HUD tells you exactly what tool to use, whether the one in your hand is correct, and for crops precisely how far along they are. No more digging blind.

Container Preview Point at chests, barrels, hoppers, dispensers, or droppers to preview their complete contents in real time. Previously an experimental toggle, container preview is now a fully integrated, stable core feature.

Furnace Smelting Readout Look at any active furnace, blast furnace, or smoker to monitor estimated cooking progress, remaining fuel burn time, and output preview without opening menus. Dynamic timers provide a real-time approximation of your smelting cycle.

Smart Interaction Icons Instantly see if a mob can be bred, tamed, ridden, or traded and whether you're holding the right item for it.

Full Entity Readout Health, armor, and every active status effect, all rendered live the moment you look at any mob or player. A rendered 3D portrait puts a face to every stat.

Unified and comprehensive customization All settings are managed via two commands. Run /pmenu to open the personal panel and configure your settingsthis also works with any other GT add-ons you have installed.

Additionally, server administrators can run /menu to establish default settings for everyone, applying them globally to all players.

Floating Damage Viewer See exact combat damage float right above your crosshair in real time. Numbers cleanly drift apart during fast combos without overlapping, and colors can be fully customized via /pmenu.
