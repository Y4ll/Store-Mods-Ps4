BACKPACK

The basic backpack has 27 slots to store the items you need. You can dye it in different colors and upgrade it to add new features, such as increasing its inventory capacity.

RECIPE

How to open the backpack?

You need to have a backpack and interact with it to open your inventory.

How to carry a backpack on your back?

To carry the backpack, you must break it open with your hands and equip it in your inventory. The backpack won't be visible if it's in the quick access bar.

How to add updates?

The new upgrade feature works by combining Interact [Right Click] + Stealth. This will open the Upgrade menu.

BACKPACK COLOR VARIANT

How do I change the color of the backpack?

The backpack can be changed with dyes.
