How Does It Work?

The addon applies the Village Hero effect to players:

/effect @a village_hero infinite 255 true

This provides the powerful Hero of the Village trading discount, making villager trades much cheaper.

For example, a trade that normally requires multiple Emeralds can potentially become a 1 Emerald trade, while certain Emerald-giving trades can require only 1 item.
