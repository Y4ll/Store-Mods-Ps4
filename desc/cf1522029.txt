overview

chaotic world is a bedrock addon that completely overhauls combat with multi-phase scripted boss fights, a deep gear progression system, skill-based weapons and armor, and 200+ custom crafting recipes. every weapon has passive effects and active skills. every armor set modifies how knockback works. defeating key bosses unlocks access to the Nether and the End. the world gets harder but the gear gets much stronger.

in-game tutorial

right-click the Tutorial Scroll for a full in-game guide book (built with ActionForm/MessageForm UI) browse boss info, weapon skills, and crafting chains without leaving the game.

world progression dimension locks

killing certain bosses now gates progression:

- defeat the Giant Zombie unlocks the Nether portal

- defeat Robot Crab + King Frog + Giant Zombie (all three) unlocks The End

progress is tracked automatically and announced to all players in chat.

bosses & enemies

all bosses are fully scripted using @minecraft/server api. they have real-time skill systems, phase transitions with announcements, particle effects, and dynamic difficulty scaling.
