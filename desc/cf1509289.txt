Record your Minecraft builds and replay them cinematically! Whether you want a fast paced timelapse of your latest redstone mansion or a ghost NPC to reconstruct your base block-by-block, Construction Replay has you covered.

Note: The add-on is in beta phase. I require feedback from you about bugs and suggestions to make it stable and reliable.

Three Dynamic Replay Modes

1. Blocks Only Mode (legacy name: Normal Mode)

- Blocks visually place themselves.

- Great For timelapses

2. NPC Builder Mode

- Spawns a specialized "Builder" NPC.

- The NPC physically walks to each location, swings its arm, and places/breaks blocks exactly as you did.

- Give a custom name in settings.

3. Player Avatar Mode (Experimental)

-

- Follows your exact path and actions as how they were recorded.

- Most Real one, it's like a true recording.

Key Features

- Cinematic Camera Control: Set a per recording camera angle. Your player will be locked in place during the replay for the perfect shot. This is optional.

- Persistent World Storage: Recordings are saved directly into your world files. They stay with your world even if you close the game!

- Intelligent Replay System:

- Automatically handles complex blocks (doors, beds, tall flowers).

- Supports bucket usage (water/lava) and even TNT/Creeper explosions.

- Entity Displacement: Replays will safely move players or animals out of the way before placing a block.

- Area Reversion: Before every replay, the addon automatically reverts the area to its pre recording state, clearing the way for a fresh reconstruction.

- Intuitive GUI: Manage everything through UI menu.

How to Use

- Open the Menu: Use the Construction Remote item or type /replay:menu.

- Record: Select "Start Recording" and choose your preferred mode.

- Build: Walk around and place/break blocks as usual.

- Save & Replay: Stop the recording, go to "My Recordings," select your build, and hit Play!

Commands & Items

- Construction Remote (replay:remote): Right-click/hold to open the GUI.

- /replay:menu: Opens the main interface.

- /replay:record <start|stop|cancel>: Quick-access recording controls.

- /replay:speed <1-10>: Choose a Speed for the replays. 1 being the slowest and 10 being fastest. 5 is the default.
