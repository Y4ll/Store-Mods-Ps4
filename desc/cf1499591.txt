Reefys Better Leaves is a simple yet powerful texture pack designed to transform the way foliage looks in your world. It enhances Minecrafts default leaves by making them cleaner, denser, and more visually appealing while staying true to the vanilla style you already love.

This resource pack is inspired by Motshen's Better Leaves and recreates a similar improved leaf structure for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. It enhances the appearance of leaves to look fuller and more natural, while adapting the design to work within Bedrock limitations
