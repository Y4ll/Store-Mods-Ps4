What is Ghoulcraft BE?

The new Ghoulcraft BE is the continued ported version of Kao from MissGhouls Ghoulcraft CIT for Java. Before, Ghoulcraft BE is a Data-Driven Block Add-On Pack, or also known as a CIM pack. But now it is a combined with term I called 'ITM' stands for item dependent model. The new version fixes all of the blocks from the previous version and included new items to use into your world as decorations!

On this update, Most blocks are now can be interacted with stick but the sit option is not included (on blocks but on chairs and barstool you can now) but dont worry because on the next update it will be implemented. This is primarily intended for Creative players, but i am planning to make it available to be obtained in survival but at the moment, you can get the furnitures in creative menu or using commands at the moment.

Some of the models in the image are from the Mizuno's 16 CIT addon. It is used for aesthetic purposes only. Also, the models are been optimized for low end devices to avoid crashing and lags.

Other Add-ons I used: Connected Walls and Glass
