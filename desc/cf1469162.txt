Better Cats!
Over 130 New Variations and 69,000 Trait Combinations of Cats!

Remodeled cats to better match their breeds, with tons of new features like random eye colors and tail lengths, also includes the ocelot. This pack was originally made by CanineGray for Java Edition and was ported by me to Bedrock Edition

Breeds:

- Black: Solid Colour

- British Shorthair: Scottish Fold mixes

- Jellie: Exotic Shorthair

- Tuxedo: Bi-Colour

- White: Sphinx

Randomized Traits:

- Hair Length: Short, Fluffy, and none

- Tail Length: Normal and Bobtail

- Whiskers: Short, Medium, and Long

- Snout: Normal and Short nose

Eye Shapes and colors:

- Eye Shapes: Narrow, Round, and Almond

- Colors: Green, Yellow, Orange, Brown, Blue, Teal, Gray, and Heterochromia mixes

Subpack Settings:

- Vanilla Spawning: remodeled better cats and ocelts but with vanilla variants only

- Random Spawning: includes all 130+ cat and ocelot variants
