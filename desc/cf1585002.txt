How It Works In 4 Easy Steps

Simple, robust, and completely reliable mechanics.

STEP 1: PLACE A TNT BLOCK Place your standard TNT block in the designated firing position of your machine.

STEP 2: CONNECT SLIME OR HONEY Attach an adjacent Slime Block or Honey Block to latch onto the duper mechanism.

STEP 3: POWER WITH PISTON Direct a Piston or Sticky Piston to push or pull the connected slime/honey assembly.

STEP 4: FIRE & BOOM! The exact millisecond the piston moves, a live primed TNT entity drops down while your source block remains intact!
