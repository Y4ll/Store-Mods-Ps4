This add-on makes your world look more saturated, colorful, and beautiful.
You can set the resolution in the game settings.

How to use? Enable Vibrant Visuals before installing this add-on.
If you use a shader and it still isnt saturated, colorful, or beautiful enough, place this Saturated pack at the top.
My recommendation: just use Vibrant Visuals without additional shaders if you have lag issues.
