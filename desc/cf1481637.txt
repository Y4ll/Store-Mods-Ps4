Quick Wall Tool

Join Origami Studios Discord for updates!

Create and delete walls at blazingly fast speeds.

With the Resizer hold down the <build/interact> button and release when done.

Shift to Delete walls

Detailed Instructions

- Select a single block

- Pick a side of the block (this will control how the block will stretch)

- Hold down the build/interact button

- Move where you are looking to where you want the wall to end

- If you want to delete, hold shift

- Release the build/interact button
