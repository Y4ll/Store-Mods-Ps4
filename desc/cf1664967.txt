Simple Futirnure

Bring a futuristic touch to your Minecraft world!

Simple Futirnure is a Vanilla+ furniture addon for Minecraft Bedrock that adds modern and futuristic furniture while keeping the original Minecraft style.

Features

- Futuristic and modern furniture

- Decorative blocks for homes and buildings

- Clean Vanilla+ style

- Designed for survival and creative

- Perfect for modern, futuristic and sci-fi builds

- Lightweight and easy to use

Build better homes, create futuristic interiors, and give your world a fresh new look with Simple Futirnure!

Build simple. Build modern. Build the future.
