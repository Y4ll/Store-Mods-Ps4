Typing out endless coordinates just to build a wall or clear some terrain is tedious. This addon fixes that by giving you a quick, visual way to manage your builds without touching the chat console. Just grab a Wooden Axe and start creating.

How it works

First Right-Click: Set your first point.

Second Right-Click: Set your second point.

Third Right-Click: Open the menu.

Inside the Menu you can:

Adjust on the fly: You can see your selected coordinates inside the UI and tweak them manually if you need to perfect your selection.

Mass Fill: Type the block you want, hit confirm, and the entire area fills instantly.

Block Replacement: If you want to swap a specific block type rather than filling the whole area, put your new block in the first slot and the block you want to replace in the second slot.

Quick Reset: To clear your points and start over, click the reset button at the bottom of the menu, or just Shift + Right-Click with your axe in-game.

Limits and Dimensions

Universal Support: This works across the Overworld, the Nether, and the End, with no distance restrictions on where you can select your points.

Size Limit: Because the addon runs on the game's native /fill command, the maximum area size is bound by Minecraft's built-in limits to keep your game running smoothly without lag.

Grab a Wooden Axe, skip the command lines, and speed up your building process.
