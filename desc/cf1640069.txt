Leaves Drop OP Items

Turn every tree into a treasure chest with Leaves Drop OP Items by FleetMc Studio! Instead of only dropping saplings or sticks, every leaf block you break now rewards you with random Minecraft loot, making tree exploration and resource gathering far more exciting.

Whether you're clearing forests, gathering saplings, or simply decorating your world, every leaf block has the chance to reward you with valuable survival resources, powerful equipment, rare blocks, and other exciting items.
