Goblin Traders: Definitive Edition

Bring the complete Goblin Traders experience to Minecraft Bedrock with more merchants, valuable trades, RPG progression and useful companions. This edition expands the original Java concept with eight specialized traders, a Goblin Adventurer and the new Goblin Wayfinder.

Explore every dimension, discover rare goblins and build relationships through quests, gifts, dialogue and exploration.

Whats New in Version 2.3

Pale Garden Curator

A rare merchant exclusive to the Pale Garden, offering trades based on pale oak, resin, eyeblossoms and the Creaking Heart.

Its custom Pale Garden VFX combines falling moss, animated roots, resin fragments and a pulsing heart.

The Curator also possesses Creaking Ward. When nearby players are attacked, hostile creatures become trapped in resin before being violently expelled. Players within range receive temporary protection.

Goblin Wayfinder

The Wayfinder helps you mark important locations and creates a visible trail leading back to your selected destination.

Use its navigation interface to manage waypoints, follow particle paths and explore without losing valuable locations.

Goblin Adventurer

The Adventurer is a fully interactive companion with its own RPG systems.

Quest System

Complete hunting, gathering and exploration objectives for special rewards.

Dialogue and Reputation

Discover more than 50 conversations and progress through five relationship tiers, from Stranger to Trusted Ally.

Gifts and Companionship

Offer dozens of accepted items, improve your friendship and recruit the Adventurer to follow and fight alongside you.

Complete Goblin Lineup

Overworld

Normal Goblin: Classic trades, resources and enchanted equipment.

Desert Goblin: Desert materials, rarities and exclusive items.

Badlands Goblin: Powerful golden tools with high-level enchantments.

Pale Garden Curator: Pale oak, resin, eyeblossoms and Creaking Heart trades.

Nether

Nether Goblins: Rare potions, Nether resources, enchanted netherite equipment and valuable treasures.

The End

End Goblins: Chorus fruit, shulker supplies, enchanted equipment and End-exclusive rewards.
