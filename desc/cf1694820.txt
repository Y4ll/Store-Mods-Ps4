Visuals +

A visual pack that tweaks Minecraft's lighting, fog, and color grading. Nothing more. No new textures, no overhaul, just the vanilla look with the color and light dialed in properly.

Deserts and mesas get warmer, harsher lighting to match the heat. The deep dark gets darker, tighter fog for that "something's down here" feeling. Cherry groves, the End, oceans: each biome has its own lighting and fog setup instead of sharing one flat default. Water reads a bit clearer, colors pull a bit further from the washed out vanilla palette without tipping into oversaturation.

Runs on the resource pack's PBR capability where supported, but doesn't require it. No performance cost, no gameplay changes, compatible with basically anything else you're running.
