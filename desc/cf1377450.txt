Flicker - Dynamic Lighting for bedrock edition (mcpe)

This addon works perfectly on 26.0 and above upto latest versions (this line is updated regularly)

Lore: Fire emits light. Whether placed on a wall or held in a hand, the flame pushes back the darkness naturally.

(This addon will probably continue to work even after game updates, as it doesn't use experiments)

The best thing about this addon is that it is simple. No new blocks or duplicate items, just Dynamic Lights :)

If you want a cheap and extremely good server, check out gravelhost, use my link or the code for 25% off for your purchase.

Usage:

Hold any light emiting item in your hand or offhand and it will illuminate everything around you just like when its placed down.

Switching to off-hand:

just sneak/crouch twice quickly (for keyboards/controllers) or use the emote 'over there' on touch devices.

It is designed to automatically adapt to any torches or lanterns added in future by Mojang.

recommendation: if you want to put all items in offhand aswell as use them, check out my other addon:
https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft-bedrock/addons/doublehands

Some Special Things:

Lanterns work underwater, but torches do not as they have fire exposed.
