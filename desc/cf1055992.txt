Ever wanted your leaves to decay faster? Sick of waiting for a stick or an apple to drop from a tree? This quick leaf decay mod will speed up those straggling leaves that just don't seem to want to leave your tree!

Usage

Whenever you cut down a tree, the leaves should all fall within around 5-10 seconds.

<figure class="image"></figure>

Additionally, the tree and leaves drop loot as they normally do. The addon works with all current tree types and biomes.

Compatability

This addon is compatible with any other addon.

Experiments

The addon requires the "Enable Beta APIs" toggle to be flipped on!
