One Piece is one of the most iconic and long-running anime series, with thousands of episodes and millions of fans around the world. Because of its popularity and unique world, I decided to bring the anime into Minecraft as an addon. This addon aims to recreate the One Piece experience in Minecraft Bedrock with Minecraft-style characters, mobs, and content inspired by the anime.

This addon mainly focuses on models, textures, and mob designs rather than complex abilities or unique skill systems. It is a mob addon, not a player addon.

This addon is not designed to be used as a player addon, but as a mob addon. That means you can spawn these characters in your house, zombie areas, villages, or even inside your mansion to make your world feel more alive.
