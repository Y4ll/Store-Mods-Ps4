OP Farmer

Upgrade your farming experience with OP Farmer by FleetMc Studio. This addon completely transforms the Farmer Villager into one of the most useful villagers in Minecraft Bedrock. Exchange a single Seed for Emeralds, then spend those Emeralds on powerful food, farming supplies, max-enchanted Diamond equipment, and other valuable survival items.

Whether you're starting a new survival world or preparing for late-game adventures, OP Farmer lets you gear up quickly without spending hours grinding for resources.

Easy Emerald Farming

Getting Emeralds is incredibly simple! Trade just 1 Seed to receive 1 Emerald, giving you an easy way to unlock all of the Farmer's custom trades.
