"Aesthetic Sounds" is a Minecraft PE resource pack that introduces a hall effect to every sound, creating a rich acoustic experience. It also features unique sounds for block breaking and sword hits, enhancing the game's audio landscape and immersing players in a vibrant, dynamic environment as they explore and battle.

Showcase Video

Features -

- Hall Effect on All Sounds Adds spacious, echoing acoustics to every in-game sound for a richer atmosphere.

- Custom Block Breaking Sounds Unique audio feedback for breaking different blocks, adding realism and variety.

- Distinct Sword Hit Sounds Enhanced combat audio with sharper, more impactful sword strike sounds.

- Immersive Audio Overhaul Transforms the games soundscape into a more dynamic and engaging experience.

- No more scary sounds
