The best place for your getaway! But daytime only... so he doesn't notice

Unfortunately, due to bedrock limitations, Freylands couldn't be transferred, so Mood works in the regular world :(

Whats in the mod:

IT - a creature dwelling in the once-gentle Freylands, having taken them over...

The Fog - a desolation that warps the entire atmosphere... the world is no longer safe and tracks your noise.

HIS Arm - reaches out from the gloom; it can grab you, but any grasp can be broken.

Footsteps - through the fog, you hear something heavy circling around.

Noise - sprinting, hits, doors, and falling all fill an on-screen meter; make too much noise, and you summon HIM...

The Pale Figure - a former inhabitant of these lands, occasionally appearing on the horizon.

The Announcer - a strange entity that comments on the player's actions and offers advice.

The addon may have some bugs, but I hope they can be fixed! I've already spent a lot of time on the mod to bring it a little closer to the original, so I hope you enjoy the experience! Have a great game!
