Pure driving pleasure brought to Minecraft Bedrock.

Experience the power, precision, and iconic design of BMW vehicles in Minecraft like never before. This addon delivers a curated lineup of BMW performance cars and SUVs, combining sporty handling, immersive driving mechanics, and detailed animations built for players who value control, speed, and authentic driving feel on city streets and open highways.

MECHANICS:

GEAR SYSTEM:

The addon features a functional gear system that directly affects vehicle behavior:

Start Neutral

Neutral 1st gear

1st Neutral (only when stopped)

1st 2nd 3rd 4th

4th 1st (cycle reset)

IGNITION SYSTEM:

Vehicles can only be started from inside the car using a key.

The engine can only be turned off while in neutral, increasing realism.

EMISSIVITY:

Simulates the cars headlights.

COLORS:

Paint your car using any Minecraft dye.

SHOP:

Shop system using iron ingots + the required engine.
