Main Difficulty The Desert Hates You

You spawn safely inside a small house... but once you step outside, the temperature rises extremely fast, and your thirst drains rapidly. Every decision matters. Time your exploration, craft wisely, and avoid staying under the sun too long.

Early Progress Challenge

You need wood, but theres none nearby.
To gather it, you must leave the houseand outside waits a horde of custom desert husks, stronger and more dangerous than normal ones.

Custom Husk Loot

Defeating husks rewards you with valuable items:

- Sweet Berries used to craft cooling drinks to lower your temperature

- Starter Heart Shards combine them to increase your maximum health

These drops are essential for your early survival and long-term progress.

Basic Progression

Check the starter chest inside your shelter for early tools and guides.
Youll learn how to use:

- Sieve

- Wooden Barrel

- Stone Barrel

- Crucible

- Crook

These machines let you generate materials even in a resource-starved desert world.

Auto Machines & Infinite Resources

Progress is slow at first, but once you unlock the Generator and auto-machines, your world changes completely. Automate everything and gain infinite resources, turning the desert into your own factory!

Projectt Z Item Exchange System

Use Projectt Z tables to exchange one item for another. A perfect late-game upgrade path for players who want fast resource conversions.

Exploration in an Infinite Desert

The world is a single endless biome, but full of secrets.
Craft a jetpack, fly across dunes, and discover rare structures, new blocks, and hidden loot.

Custom Armour Collection

The modpack includes a large set of custom armours. Craft one, boost your stats, and prepare to take on stronger enemies waiting beneath the sands.
