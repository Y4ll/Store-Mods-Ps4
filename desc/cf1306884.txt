This mod adds a working TV into your Minecraft world. It also adds a non-functional TV. The working TV is 5x7 blocks and can be summoned by the spawn egg: Spawn TV. There are two remote items. Remote On and Remote Off. When close to a TV, you may use the remote to turn on or off the nearest TV. When turning on, a Rick Roll animation will play and the Never Gunna Give You Up song will play. When using the remote off, the screen will turn invisible and the sound will stop. It is recomended to place a 5x7 block wall of black blocks behind the TV so when it becomes invisible it looks "black" additionally, you may replace the middle bottom block behind the TV for an upside down stair block. This will allow the hitbox of the TV to be pushed into the gap. If you do this, the TV will be "wall mounted."

If you don't want to use the big TV, this addon adds a smaller one that is 2x3 blocks, however, this one is not functional.

Changes to the big TV's functionality coming soon.
