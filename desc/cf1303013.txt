1. Installation Guide:

Cordyceps addon has 2 versions of RESOURCE and BEHAVIOR packs: FULL and LITE These are the characteristics:

- Full Resource pack: Has all textures, sounds, particles, and full variations.

- Lite Resource Pack: Keeps the necessary sounds, removes block variants (meaning 1 texture per block), reduces custom particles to the minimum, or eliminates them in certain cases.

- Full Behavior Pack: Contains the complete experience regarding the gameplay of the addon, and the size of structures is at maximum. Here, cities, laboratories, and alcantarillas will feel colossal; sound events, infectados, and spawn probability will be original.

- Lite Behavior Pack: It is a reduced experience but not incomplete. All items, blocks, and entities exist just the same. What changes is the size of structures, and mechanics related to ambiance and entities might be restricted to a minimum depending on their typelike the flash when shooting, for example, or the spawn frequency of mobs. Giant structures reduce their size by half.

What should you install?

If you want to play the full experience, install the FULL packs. (maximun)

If you want to play the full experience at the level of behaviors and mechanics, you can combine the Full Behavior Pack with the Lite Resource Pack. (balanced)

If your device cannot handle so much, you can install both Lite packs. (low)

How does this affect multiplayer play?

When playing multiplayer, it is crucial for every player to have the same BEHAVIOR pack. The RESOURCE pack can vary for each player without issues. As a recommendation, have the packs already on your devices individually if you are going to play multiplayer, since due to its size there could be errors or difficulty loading the world.
