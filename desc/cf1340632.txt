Every box is a gateway to anime. Who will emerge?

Unleash the power of destiny with Anime Box, an addon that transforms your Minecraft world into a stage of legendary anime figures.

Break a box and you might release the unstoppable Gojo, the devastating Sukuna, the determined Tanjiro, the immortal Meliodas, or even the legendary Naruto.

Each character has its own rarity:

- Common: easy to find.

- Rare: hard to get, but within reach.

- Legendary: as rare as the sun at midnight.
