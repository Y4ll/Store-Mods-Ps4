This is a powerful time-lapse recording tool for Minecraft Bedrock.

In addition to player tracking, this tool allows for smooth movement and minimizes blur as much as possible.

While it's easy to create time-lapse recordings by simply tracking players, this tool allows for smooth tracking, jumping, and precise movements during building without blur.

Finally, the era of time-lapse recording has come to Minecraft Bedrock!

Tracking feature

You can smoothly switch between recording targets during recording.

Smooth movement

With finely adjusted easing function, you can smoothly change speed and achieve perfect smooth movement.

Blur reduction

It minimizes blur caused by close-distance movements or jumps, reducing the impact on video quality.
