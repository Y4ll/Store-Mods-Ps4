Have you even wanted to play with the upcoming features from Minecraft Dungeons 2? Well, it's your lucky day, since I present you, the Dungeons 2 Content Add-on. This Minecraft Dungeons 2 Add-on for Minecraft Bedrock Edition aims to recreate all the content from Minecraft Dungeons 2 in the most accurate way possible for you own personal enjoyment with textures that match the first game' style, adding enemies, bosses, weapons, blocks and more from the original game.

The addon currently only features a few mobs and items, and its not 100% compatible for survival experience, more dungeons will be added soon, and it will compatible for survival experience!

This pack works as a complement for my Dungeons Content Add-on, so they must be used together.
