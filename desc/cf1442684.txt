About This Addon

OP Loot & Starter Kit is a Minecraft Bedrock addon designed to provide players with a powerful starter kit when they join or spawn in the world for the first time.

The addon is ideal for survival worlds where players want a strong beginning while still maintaining balanced gameplay.

Features

- One-time starter kit for each player (multiplayer supported)

- Full Netherite armor and tools

- Fully enchanted weapons including sword, bow, and trident

- Essential survival items such as food, torches, map, compass, and buckets

- Elytra with fireworks for early mobility

- Kit is granted only once and will not repeat after respawn or rejoining

- Fully achievements friendly

Kit Contents

The starter kit includes:

- Netherite armor set

- Netherite tool set

- Netherite sword, bow, trident, and shield

- Cooked food and golden apples

- Map and compass

- Survival blocks and materials

- Torches, ender pearls, and obsidian

- Elytra with fireworks

Note - To Get All Enchantments, Hold All items in hand once. They will be enchanted automatically.

How It Works

When a player joins or spawns in the world for the first time, the addon automatically grants the starter kit.

After the kit has been received, the player is marked as already claimed, ensuring the kit is never given again even after respawning or rejoining the world.
