This Lamborghini model is an experimental model meant to explore the boundaries of cube style modeling while maintaining the ability to use standard texture styling. This Add-On is well optimized, using only one physical model with simple texture replacement variations, making the total cube count in game under 150. Thank you to JannisX11 for entity wizard base vehicle code. Each car does fit one passenger and one driver, and the speed is low enough to be controlled on mobile easily.
