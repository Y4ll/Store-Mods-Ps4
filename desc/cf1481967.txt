New small addon this time is sirenhead. I tried to repeat the appearance of the siren, its capabilities and sounds.

Changes in the fourth version of the addon:

1) Improved particles

2) Added new sounds

3) Improved intelligence

4) Improved basic mechanics

5) Fixed bugs

SIRENHEAD: Has 10000 health and ~20 attack units. Will spawn with a very small chance in the world of Minecraft. Has as many as 6 types of attack and can knock the player back during them, also climbs walls and breaks leaves. That's all this small addon adds.

I hope you like this small addition. Also on my YouTube channel you can find more interesting addons
