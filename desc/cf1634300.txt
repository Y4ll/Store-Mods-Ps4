Still carrying a torch just to place it every few blocks? This addon makes any light source light up the world around you as you walk. No crafting, no commands, no resource pack.

- How to Use

Simply hold a torch (or any light source) in your main hand or off-hand. The light follows you automatically. Sneak + use a torch to send it straight to your off-hand, so your sword stays in the main hand. That's it.

- Features

- Real light, not a trick Uses vanilla light blocks, so it works with any shader or texture pack

- 37 light sources Torches, lanterns, campfires, glowstone, sea lanterns, shroomlight, froglights, amethyst, glow berries, end rods, even a lava bucket

- One-tap off-hand Sneak and use a torch and it moves across on its own. No inventory screen, no dragging

- Off-hand support Light works from either hand, so you can fight and still see

- True brightness Every item lights at its real vanilla level, a soul torch is dimmer than a lantern

- Leaves nothing behind The light is cleaned up when you swap items, log out, or change dimensions

- Never touches your builds Only ever replaces air, never your blocks

- Achievement-safe Does NOT disable achievements or trophies

- Multiplayer ready Every player gets their own independent light

- Extremely light The lighting loop runs entirely on the script API: 1 block check per update while standing still, 3 while walking, and no commands at all

- Fully configurable Brightness threshold, update rate, off-hand light and off-hand transfer all editable

- Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.50+, 26.0+

- Works on Android, iOS, Windows, Console

- Behavior Pack only, no Resource Pack needed

- Works with shaders and texture packs

- Compatible with other addons

- Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Tap/double-click to auto-import into Minecraft

- Apply the pack to your world under Behavior Packs

- Play, hold a torch and watch the world light up!
