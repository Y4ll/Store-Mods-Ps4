Better Creeper Animations

Better Creeper Animations brings Minecraft Bedrock's most iconic mob to life with smooth, fluid animations and biome-specific variants.

Smooth Animations

Every action the creeper performs has been completely overhauled with carefully tuned animations:

- Idle gentle, lifelike sway

- Walking & chasing natural leg movement that responds to speed

- Swimming proper paddling motion in water

- Jumping, falling & landing three-phase aerial animation that reacts to ground contact

- Swelling dramatic body inflation and trembling as the creeper builds up to detonate

- Damage reactions visible hit feedback when struck

- Death animation replaces the abrupt vanilla death with a smoother fall

Biome Specific Variants

The creeper now adapts to its environment:

Default Creeper keeps the classic look in all other biomes

Cherry Creeper spawns in cherry grove biomes, decorated with soft pink cherry blossom petals

Snowy Creeper spawns in cold biomes (taiga, ice plains, frozen peaks, snowy slopes, and more) with a snow cap on its head

Pale Garden Creeper spawns in the pale garden with a drained, ashen-gray look and eerie glowing eyes to match the biome's haunting atmosphere

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Double-click to import into Minecraft Bedrock

- Enable the resource pack in your world settings

- Start a new world or reload an existing one
