Ores Booster

Make mining faster and more rewarding with Ores Booster by FleetMc Studio. This lightweight addon increases the generation of Overworld ores by approximately 70%, making it much easier to find valuable resources while exploring caves and mining underground.

Instead of spending hours searching for ores, you'll discover them more frequently, helping you progress through survival worlds much faster without changing the core Minecraft experience.

Boosted Ores

- Coal Ore

- Iron Ore

- Copper Ore

- Gold Ore

- Redstone Ore

- Lapis Lazuli Ore

- Diamond Ore

- Emerald Ore

Features

- Increases Overworld ore generation by approximately 70%.

- Makes mining faster and more rewarding.

- Supports all major Overworld ores.

- Lightweight and optimized for smooth gameplay.

- Achievement Friendly.

- No Experimental Gameplay required.

Installation

- Download the addon.

- Import it into Minecraft.

- Enable it under Behavior Packs.
