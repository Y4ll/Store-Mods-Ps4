Visual Clarity 1.2.5

Visual Clarity is a utility resource pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. It makes useful information easier to read, improves block visibility, and reduces screen obstruction while preserving Minecrafts original style.

Item and Inventory Information

- Displays durability as a percentage

- Shows durability inside the inventory and during gameplay

- Shows weapon attack damage

- Shows armor protection values

- Shows how many hunger bars food restores

- Explains potion effects

- Displays potion-effect duration

- Uses clean, readable text that matches Minecrafts interface

Mob Health Bars

- Adds compact health bars above mobs

- Displays the mobs current health

- Changes color as health decreases

- Keeps the display above the mobs head

- Works during regular survival gameplay and on servers

Outlined Ores

- Adds visible outlines to stone, deepslate, and Nether ores

- Includes ancient debris

- Makes ores easier to identify in dark areas

- Supports glowing ores with Vibrant Visuals

- Outlines still appear without Vibrant Visuals

Clear Borderless Glass

- Removes distracting borders and streaks

- Supports regular glass blocks and panes

- Supports every stained-glass color

- Supports tinted glass

- Gives windows and large glass builds a cleaner appearance

Crop Growth Indicators

- Displays numbered growth stages on crops

- Supports wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, and Nether wart

- Uses small indicators that keep the crops visible

- Crop Stages are enabled by default

- Turn Crop Stages ON or OFF through the packs gear icon

Powdered Snow Indicator

- Adds a blue border around powdered snow

- Covers every visible side

- Makes powdered snow easy to separate from regular snow

Visible Infested Blocks

- Adds a clear orange indicator to infested stone blocks

- Makes infested blocks easier to identify

- Keeps the original stone textures recognizable

Clear Pumpkin Vision

- Removes the obstructive carved-pumpkin overlay

- Keeps your view clear while wearing a carved pumpkin

Low Fire

- Lowers the first-person fire overlay

- Keeps more of the screen visible while burning

Lower Shield

- Lowers the first-person shield position

- Stops the shield from covering a large part of the screen

Visible Tripwire

- Makes placed tripwire easier to see

- Uses a visible orange texture

- Helps with traps and redstone builds

Directional Hoppers

- Makes hopper direction easier to identify

- Helps when building storage systems and redstone machines

Pack Settings

The gear icon controls one feature:

- Crop Stages: ON

- Crop Stages: OFF

Durability is always displayed as a percentage. There is no durability-format setting.

Compatibility

- Made for Minecraft Bedrock Edition

- Works through Global Resources

- Works in survival and creative worlds

- Server and Realm friendly

- No scripts required

- Achievement friendly

- Standard crosshair preserved

- Preserves Minecrafts vanilla appearance

These are only some of the improvements included in Visual Clarity. Try the pack in-game to see all its features.
