The functionality of this resource package is to guarantee comfort, efficiency, softness and a different perspective than the conventional interface, redesigning it to a more striking and vivid color, keeping vanilla in mind.

Continues 2 game modes. In Default mode (mode 1) find modifications in the social user interface, such as:

- The inventory

- The settings

- The dressing room

- Work tables

- Touch buttons

- Among other interfaces.

Note: To ensure that all details have been loaded, you need to restart the game after activating it.

Interface saturation may vary on the screen of some devices.

Position (in English language (United States))

Hotbar

Optimization mode and additional functions (mode 2) give you a hand when living a new adventure, following blocks, objects and modified interface to improve the player experience, changes such as:

- Improved video settings

- Vignettes and effects removed

- Vanilla animations removed

- Improved crystals

- Marked crops

- More vivid colors in forest biomes

- Single color leaves

- Short crosshair

- Wooden doors and hatches with views to the outside

- improved enchanted glint
