Welcome to New Shields Overhaul, the ultimate Minecraft Bedrock Edition addon that breathes new life into your defensive arsenal! With ten handcrafted shieldsfrom the rustic Wood Shield to the thunderous Dignified Shieldyou'll experience next-level protection, spectacular custom impact particles, custom sounds, and game-changing abilities that turn every block into an opportunity.

What's New in v1.5.0

Custom Impact Particles System - Each shield now features unique, spectacular particle effects that trigger on impact! Watch as colorful particles explode from your shield with every successful block, making each defensive moment visually stunning and satisfying.

Custom Shields & Their Powers

Dignified Shield: Summon lightning bolts on attackers and gain Resistance II & Regeneration I. Features electric blue impact particles that crackle with energy!
Elite Shield: Grant yourself Strength I and inflict Weakness on foes. Show your dominance with vibrant green elite particles!
Glorious Shield: Boost your Speed & Regeneration simultaneouslyperfect for hit-and-run tactics! Radiates brilliant golden-yellow particles on impact!
Knight Shield: Embrace Strength I for enhanced combat power. Classic metallic silver particles accompany your defensive strikes!
Majestic Shield: Activate Resistance I and hear the resonant bell-hit on every block! Majestic golden particles burst forth with regal splendor!
Piglin Shield: Knockback foes, set them ablaze , and play the piglin-angry roar! Fiery red-orange particles erupt like flames on impact!
Sculk Shield: Poison unsuspecting attackers with a neurotoxic Poison II effect. Dark purple sculk particles pulse with otherworldly energy!
Wood Shield: Slow down aggressors with Slowness Ithe classic starter defense. Natural brown wood particles scatter like splinters!
Amethyst Shield NEW: Gain Resistance I and Absorption effects while stunning enemies with beautiful purple amethyst particles that shimmer like crystals!
Cake Shield NEW: Restore health and gain Saturation & Regenerationthe sweetest defense! Delightful pink cake particles celebrate every successful block!
