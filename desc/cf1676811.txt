DryOut is a Minecraft Bedrock addon that upgrades, changes, and expands vanilla mechanics with custom content, turning overlooked parts of the game into dangerous, rewarding places worth exploring.

From harsh desert survival to powerful relics, ancient ruins, dangerous events, and custom gameplay systems, DryOut adds new reasons to explore, fight, survive, and discover what lies beneath the surface.

v0.3 Frost Bite

v0.3 focuses on improving and expanding the core DryOut experience with new systems, relic mechanics, achievements, balancing, and a large collection of content.

New Achievement System

v0.3 adds new achievements along with a custom Achievement Toast UI.

Earn achievements through exploration, combat, discoveries, relic usage, and special challenges.

New Relic System Chantable Relics

v0.3 introduces Chantable Relics, a new system that allows relic abilities to be prepared and upgraded before being unleashed.

To use a Chantable Relic:

- Use the relic with a Chant Sign.

- Right-click the Chant Sign to begin the chant.

- Chant Signs increase the tier of the relic's ability.

- When ready, crouch and right-click to unleash the stored power.

The Desert Reworks v0.1 to v0.2

The desert is no longer just an empty biome.

Explore ancient ruins, discover hidden locations, fight dangerous creatures, collect powerful relics, and survive the dangers buried beneath the sand.

Mobs

Scorpion, Scarab, Mummy, Tarantula, Tumbleweed, and more.

Structures

Tombs, Oases, statues, monuments, temples, and custom dungeons.

Relics

Powerful items that grant special abilities and spells, with mana management and unique effects.

Trinkets

Passive abilities activated while held in the mainhand or offhand.

Blocks and Items

Custom dungeon spawners, utility blocks, alchemy blocks, gameplay blocks, and a large selection of custom items.

Sandstorms

Dangerous desert events with powerful winds, reduced visibility, special particles, and ambient storm sounds.

Take shelter under a block to protect yourself from the storm.

Curse Events

Major encounters can trigger powerful curses that last for several days and can randomly change between different curse events.

Food Dousing

Hold an item in your offhand and food in your mainhand to apply custom effects through the Food Dousing system.

Achievements

Discover custom achievements through exploration, combat, relics, and special discoveries.

Craft the achievement guide using sand and a book to learn what you can unlock.

Oases

Find peaceful oases hidden throughout the desert.

Volcanic Desert

A dangerous variation of the desert featuring volcanic terrain and a different environment from the standard dunes.

Configuration

A beta configuration system that allows certain features of the addon to be adjusted.
