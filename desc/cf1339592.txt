- Very long spoilers ahead!

1 - You can disable those annoying blindness effects and darkness or what not in the settings.

2 - Howler can trap players and overtime howler can learn more and deadly ones

3 - It gets aggressive a lot if you get too close more often, especially the LR rate is set to (short) so it means immediate aggressiveness if a player gets close.

4 - The more the ambience sound effects play the more aggressive he is.

5 - Howler is a curious creature,you might encounter him in a cave before and was just staring waiting if you'll do something and then go away.

(Sometimes howler actually goes to you and roars aggressively,if you don't move it will walk away)

6 - Howler doesn't immediately break blocks,it adapts to break it.. An example is how much you keep placing blocks in front of him overtime it will learn to break blocks.

7 - Using shields or totems often when fighting howler will end up for the howler to grab you and force you to drop the item.

8 - If you build up and break the block beneath you Howler will then climb up either way and break the block on the top to get to you.

Same thing when digging straight down, Howler will then follow and learn to dig down and break the blocks underneath to get its way to the target.

9 - Some more skills it can learn:

- Cover (It will cover itself with its arms getting a huge defense) It will learn this if the player is hitting it too much or by using arrows.

- Howler can stomp (Pushing the enemies away)

- Howler speeds up if the player tries to run away

- Howler can detect if the wolves are targeting him or the golems,it will then call some friends of their own.
