Project Description:

Expand your inventory and explore without limits using the Actual Backpack Add-On. This vanilla-friendly upgrade seamlessly integrates into your world, ensuring you never have to leave valuable loot behind again.

Important Information:

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- On back or in hand storage options.

- Dynamic storage percent feature.

- Cheap crafting recipe.

- Behaviour & Resource packs are both required for the project to work.

- Have something to say? Join the Discord.

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

Once you've crafted your backpack, simply drop it onto the ground and you're good to gono complicated setup required!

Just open up your backpack to stash all your gear insideit's like having an extra chest right in your pocket!

Just sneak and tap while looking down to clip the backpack right onto your backhands-free storage on the go!

Need your gear back in your hands? Just sneak and look straight up to pop the backpack right off your back in a snap!

Want to pack it up? Just give your backpack a quick punch to drop it straight into your inventory! Plus, you can check your handy hotbar display anytime to see your current storage percentage at a glance.

Want a brand-new look? Just use any dye on your backpack to instantly swap its color and match your personal style!
