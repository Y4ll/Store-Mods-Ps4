Project Description:

What is the point of mining glowing ores when the tools you craft do not actually emit light? With this texture pack, your hard-earned gear will finally shine as brightly as the materials used to create them, adding a vibrant and immersive aesthetic to your world.

Important Information:

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Vibrant Visuals will need to be activated for some features.

- All tools have a glow.

- Lightweight project.

- Have something to say? Join the Discord.

All Iron, Gold, Diamond and Netherite tools will Glow, even when placed on the floor.
