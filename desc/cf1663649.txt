Standing around all day gets boring. Sit Anywhere lets you sit down wherever you are - no chairs to craft, no commands to type, no items to hold. Just empty your hand, look down at the ground and tap. Your player sits down properly, and a jump gets you back on your feet.

- Sit on almost any block - grass, dirt, stone, sand, wood and everything else

- Sit on stairs and slabs at the correct height, so you never float or sink into the block

- Sit on carpets, snow layers, hay bales, flowers, moss and other thin blocks

- Sit on beds without setting your spawn point or skipping the night

How to Sit?

- Empty your hand - it only triggers when your selected slot is empty

- Look down at the block you are standing on

- Tap or right-click the block to sit down

- Jump to stand back up
