God Is Watching

God Is Watching adds a unique supernatural experience to your Minecraft world. An unseen divine presence watches every action you take, silently judging your choices. Every decision matters, and your fate depends on the path you choose.

Will you live with kindness and be rewarded... or embrace evil and face divine punishment?

Features

- A divine presence constantly watches your actions.

- Your behavior determines how you are judged.

- Receive blessings and rewards for doing good.

- Face severe punishments for committing evil acts.

- Random supernatural events that remind you you're being watched.

- A morality system that reacts to your choices.

- Unpredictable encounters that make every world feel different.

---

How It Works

From the moment you enter your world, a higher power begins observing everything you do.

Your actions influence how this mysterious entity responds to you.

If you choose the path of good, you may receive:

- Valuable gifts.

- Helpful blessings.

- Protection during dangerous situations.

- Other unexpected rewards.

If you choose the path of evil, be prepared for consequences such as:

- Divine punishments.

- Strange supernatural events.

- Powerful curses.

- Unexpected dangers.

Every action has consequences, and nothing escapes the eyes of the one watching you.

Compatibility

Recommended Experience

For the most immersive experience:

- Play in Survival mode.

- Keep the game sounds enabled.

- Avoid using cheats.

- Let your choices guide your journey naturally.
