Introducing the Capybara Addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition! Add these lovable giant rodents to your Minecraft world. Watch them swim, lounge in water, and peacefully roam around. Capybaras are passive mobs that bring a touch of South American wildlife to your adventures. Perfect for nature lovers and players looking to expand their Minecraft's animal kingdom.

Capybaras are new passive mobs that naturally spawn in jungle biomes, bringing life to these environments. These friendly creatures can become your newest pet companion - simply feed them watermelon slices to tame them, and they'll loyally follow you on your adventures.

Once tamed, your capybara friends can be commanded to sit with a simple interaction, making them perfect guards for your jungle base or cute additions to your Minecraft home. Want more capybaras? Breed them using melon slices and create your own capybara family!
