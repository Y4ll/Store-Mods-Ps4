INSTANT Fullbright Night Vision (Achievement Friendly)

INSTANT Fullbright Night Vision is a Minecraft Bedrock mod by Neq Gamerz that provides a fullbright/night-vision-style visual experience, making dark areas significantly easier to see.

This pack is designed To work properly in vanilla minecraft.

Features

- Fullbright Night Vision See clearly in dark areas.

- Instant Visibility No need to place torches just to see.

- Minecraft Bedrock Compatible Designed for Vanilla MCPE/MCBE.

- Lightweight Pack Focused on improving visibility without unnecessary content.

- Created by Neq Gamerz

Requirements - Any Minecraft Version Above 1.21.80

Compatibility may vary depending on your Minecraft version and loader configuration.

Installation

- Download the ".mcpack" file.

- Open the file with Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

- Wait for the pack to be imported.

- Go to World Settings Enable It from Behaviour Packs section.

- Activate INSTANT Fullbright Night Vision.

- Launch your world and enjoy improved visibility in dark areas.
