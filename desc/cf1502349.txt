The Solo Leveling addon enhances your Minecraft gameplay with a progression system where everything you do makes you stronger.

Attacking mobs increases your power, and other activities like exploring and mining also help you grow. The more you play, the stronger you become.

Unlock new skills, fight tougher enemies, and build your own army as you progress from a beginner to a true shadow monarch.

This addon is still in development, so the summon ability is not available yet.
