Take control of your view with Free Cam! Triple-tap the Sneak button to activate a detached camera and freely explore your surroundings within a 100-block radius while your player stays in place. The camera automatically returns if you move beyond the limit or die. Double-tap Sneak to instantly exit Free Cam. Designed to remain achievement-friendly with no experimental features required.

Features:

Triple-tap Sneak to enable Free Cam.

Double-tap Sneak to disable Free Cam.

Maximum 100-block camera radius.

Camera automatically returns when the 100-block limit is reached.

Free Cam automatically disables when you die.

Achievement Friendly works without enabling cheats or experimental gameplay.

Camera cannot move below the world's lower movement limit (prevents underground clipping).

Supports touch controls and works in survival worlds.

Perfect for exploring builds, checking terrain, recording videos, and taking cinematic screenshots while keeping gameplay fair and balanced.
