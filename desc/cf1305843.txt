Fishing is OP By GamerFile

Turn peaceful fishing into power-farming! In this addon, every time you cast your fishing rod, youre pulling out more than just fish youre reeling in OP loot that can help you beat the Ender Dragon without breaking a sweat.

What is This Addon?

This addon transforms the simple fishing rod into a tool of ultimate progression.
Whether you're chilling by the water or grinding your early survival world, this lets you turn every cast into a chance at overpowered gear.

How to Use

Just craft a fishing rod and you're good to go.

Tip: Kill a few spiders to get string, craft your rod, and done!

Then head to any water source, cast your line, and be ready for surprises.
