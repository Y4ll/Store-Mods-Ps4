DecoDrop is a decoration-focused addon that brings your builds to life with hundreds of new blocks including chairs, tables, lamp posts, street lights, and more! Whether you're designing a cozy home, a bustling city, or a medieval village, DecoDrop has what you need to enhance your world with detail and style.

How to Create Terracotta Blocks?

Create beautiful terracotta items like teapots, mugs, toilets, and more through our ceramic system:

Crafting Process:

- Selection: Open the Ceramics Station interface while holding clay blocks and choose your desired item

- Firing: Place the crafted clay items on top of the Rustic Furnace to bake them into terracotta (this process takes time with random results)

- Coloring: Customize your terracotta blocks by combining them with any dye on a crafting table - you can also use brushes for detailed color patterns

New Kitchen Interfaces

In addition to decorative blocks, DecoDrop also introduces a variety of new food items and functional cooking blocks:

- Pot used to prepare hearty stews and soups

- Teapot perfect for brewing warm drinks

- Oven ideal for baking and roasting recipes

How to Use:

- The oven is always lit and ready for use

- Copper pots and teapots must be placed on top of the Rustic Furnace to function

- Open the interface to view available recipes

- Simply place your ingredients and cooking will begin automatically

Enjoy creating delicious meals with these new kitchen additions!
