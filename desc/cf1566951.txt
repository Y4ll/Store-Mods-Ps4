Infinite Villager Trades

Tired of villagers locking their trades after a few uses? This addon removes trade limits, allowing villagers to trade indefinitely without needing to restock.

Features

- Unlimited villager trading

- No locked trades

- Works with all villager professions

- Simple and lightweight

- Survival-friendly

- Compatible with existing worlds

- Emerald farms

- Trading halls

- Survival worlds

- Skyblock maps

- Players who want less waiting and more trading

Never wait for villager restocks again. Trade as much as you want whenever you want!
