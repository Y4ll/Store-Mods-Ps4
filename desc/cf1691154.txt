Key Features & Visual Upgrades

Radiant Self-Illuminating Glowing Ores

Never miss precious ores in dark caves again! All mineral ores and deepslate veins emit a sharp, beautifully balanced self-illumination that cuts through pitch black caverns without flooding your screen with hazy, blurry light scattering.

Volumetric Sun God Rays & Golden Sunlight

Experience dramatic cinematic sunbeams cutting through forest canopies, mountain ridges, and cloud formations. Enjoy warm golden hour sunrises and cinematic twilight sunsets that make every landscape feel majestic and alive.

Shimmering Northern Lights (Auroras)

Look up at night in frozen peaks, snowy tundras, and high-altitude biomes to witness breathtaking aurora borealis ribbons dancing across the dark sky, bathing the landscape in mystical turquoise and violet luminescence.

Organic Waving Leaves, Grass & Water

The world is in motion! Tree leaves gently sway in the breeze, while tall grass and golden wheat fields ripple like ocean waves. Engineered with lightweight vertex animation that stays pinned at 60+ FPS, paired with crystal clear water and custom biome fogs.

Click the gear icon on the Resource Pack in your Minecraft settings to choose your ideal graphics profile anytime:

- Performance Mode: Perfect for budget phones. Glowing Ores & Waving Foliage at guaranteed 60+ FPS.

- Balanced Mode (Recommended): The ideal survival preset. Adds God Rays & Shooting Stars.

- Ultra Quality (Cinematic): The complete visual package. Unlocks Northern Lights and Bioluminescent Fireflies!

Installation & Quick Setup

Installing ZenXveda Vanilla+ Shaders takes less than 30 seconds:

- Download the latest .mcpack file from the Files tab.

- Double-click or tap the downloaded file to automatically import it into Minecraft Bedrock.

- Go to your Settings > Global Resources and activate the pack.

- (Optional) Click the gear icon to choose your performance preset.

- Jump into your world and enjoy next-gen visuals with ZERO experimental toggles needed!
