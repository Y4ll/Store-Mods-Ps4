Ruby & Relic [Addon] (v1.6.1)

A new vanilla-friendly expansion for Minecraft Bedrock Edition, focused on magical Ruby tools, armor, and mysterious relics.
This addon introduces a rare new ore Ruby and a set of unique, situational tools and abilities.
Its designed to feel balanced, survival-friendly and immersive, not just OP ruby gear.

BLESSINGS UPDATE (1.5.2)

Blessings books are new items that can be found if you trade ruby with piglin only (for custom loot in structure including blessing go download the Custom Structure Chest Loot files). 5 Blessing books :

- Blessing of Respawning --> Keep Ruby Items upon death ! (Ruby tools, Ruby amor, Enderpearl launcher)

- Blessing of Tempering --> Can be used on the Ruby Pickaxe and the Ruby Shovel, It makes them efficient on the others block of there category (like deepslate for the pickaxe or sand for the shovel) but the counterpart is 6 times less durability !

- Blessing of heaviness --> Used on the Ruby Axe, it changed the range of the ability to freeze mobs from 5 blocks to 15 blocks but the cooldown is 3 minutes.

- Blessing Of Volatility --> Used on the Ruby Axe, it changed the range of the ability to freeze mobs from 5 blocks to 7 blocks but also gives you the effect Speed, Invisibility and Slow Falling. The cooldown is 3 minutes too.

- Blessing of the Furrow --> Used on the Ruby Hoe, When harvesting you get a chance (1/15) to obtain items such as bone meal or seeds but rarely you can find a iron nautilus armor or even an efficiency III book !

- Nerfed the ruby axe by giving player who are in the effect of weight of death (right-click) temporary resistance I, Fire resistance and Water Breathing. To avoid player from dying by a lava bucket by their ennemy while they cant move.
