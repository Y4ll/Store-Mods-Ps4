X-ray Pickaxe

This pickaxe transforms nearby stone and deepslate into glass while mining, creating an X-ray effect that reveals hidden ores and structures.

Recipe

How It Works

To turn nearby blocks into glass just mine a block holding the X-ray Pickaxe and it will turn nearby cave blocks into glass which will expose nearby ores.

Features

- Turns nearby blocks into glass while mining

- X-ray effect reveals ores clearly

- Works with stone,granite,andseti,diorite, tuff block and deepslate

- Suitable for survival/adventure maps

- Achievement friendly
