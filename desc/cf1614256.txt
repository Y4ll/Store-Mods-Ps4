Fake Player

Fake Player adds a psychological horror experience to your Minecraft world. An unknown player may appear at any moment, watching you from a distance, showing up in unexpected places, and disappearing before you can get close.

You'll never know when it will appear... or if it was just your imagination.

Features

- Random appearances of a mysterious player.

- Appears behind trees, near windows, doors, and other unexpected locations.

- Can appear inside dark caves and isolated places.

- Always tries to stay out of your direct line of sight.

- Strange and unsettling sounds during exploration.

- Sudden encounters designed to keep you constantly on edge.

- Multiple types of appearances to make every experience unique.

---

How It Works

As you play normally, the Fake Player may appear at random moments. It doesn't show up constantly, making every encounter feel unexpected.

You may find it:

- Watching you from a distance.

- Peeking through windows.

- Standing near doors.

- Hiding behind trees.

- Inside dark caves.

- Behind you when you least expect it.

Most of the time, when you try to approach... it simply disappears.

---

Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition

- Singleplayer

- Multiplayer

- Survival

- Creative

---

Recommended Experience

For the best horror experience:

- Play with the volume turned up.

- Explore during the night.

- Avoid using high brightness settings.

- Wear headphones.
