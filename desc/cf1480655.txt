This Freecam add-on for Minecraft Bedrock Edition lets you move the camera freely without moving your player, allowing you to fly through blocks and inspect your world easily.

Features

Freecam Controls

Open the FreecamBE Menu by using the FreecamBE item in your hand to toggle freecam on/off, lock/unlock the camera, and access all settings.

Category
Option
Description

Movement
Horizontal & Vertical Speed
Adjust camera speed (0100, default: 1.0)

Collision
Ignore Non-Solid Blocks
Pass through leaves, grass, etc.

Ignore Openable Blocks
Pass through doors, trapdoors, and fence gates

Ignore All Collision
Pass through everything (requires OP or Creative mode)

Visual
Freecam Spawn Position
Inside Player (default), In Front of Player, or Behind Player

Utility
Close on Damage
Automatically exit freecam when you take damage

Notification
Action Bar
Messages appear on the action bar

Chat (Private Message)
Messages appear in chat privately

Disabled
No notifications shown

Camera Lock

Freeze the camera in place with a locked rotation.
