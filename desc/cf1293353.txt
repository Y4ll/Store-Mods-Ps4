About The Pack

This is a texture pack for Dragon Mounts 2 BE, it is not a mod on its own and is entirely dependent on that addon.

This pack overhauls the textures of most of the dragons for more ease of telling the difference for male and female. There's also just more emissive parts than the original addon, I'm working on making the animations more lively as well.

Changes

-
Changed the textures of all the dragons, excluding moonlight, male enchant, male storm, female ender, skeleton, wither, and zombie dragons.

-
Added multiple new glowing textures! Almost every dragon has new glow textures, excluding the zombie, moonlights, and male enchant.

-
Changed the armor to look more like cloth, darker, less saturated colors.

-
Changed the textures of the scale blocks and items to match the new dragon colors.

-
Changed the dragon eggs to have the texture of the scale blocks instead of their own.

Known issues

- The glow textures look blurred slightly, more bulky and less detailed. I do not know why this happens.
