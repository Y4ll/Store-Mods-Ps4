Quick Move & Loot Utility [1.21+]

Created by Arrow Studios Official

Tired of slow inventory management in Minecraft Bedrock? Quick Move & Loot is a lightweight utility designed to make looting, moving, and dropping items instantaneous. Perfect for PVP, Skywars, and Survival players who need speed!

Key Features:

One-Tap Looting: Move items from chests to your inventory in a flash.

Instant Drop: Clear your inventory or drop items to teammates without the lag.

Better Inventory Layout: Enjoy a cleaner, more organized grid for faster navigation and better visibility.

Zero Particles: Optimized code to ensure your FPS stays at the maximum.

Clean UI: Simple, intuitive buttons that don't clutter your screen.

1.21+ Ready: Fully compatible with the latest Minecraft Bedrock/Pocket Edition versions.
