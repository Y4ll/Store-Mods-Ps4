Animated Koi Menu

Bring a calm and tranquil atmosphere to your Minecraft menus with this animated koi pond background.
Designed for the Start Screen and Settings Screen, this pack transforms the default UI into a peaceful waterscape where colorful koi glide beneath floating lily pads.

Soft water movement, drifting plants, and gentle animation create a relaxing scene every time you open the game.
