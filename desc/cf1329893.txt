HUD-X

Description

HUD-X is a standalone HUD pack from my NeBux UI+ pack. It enhances your Minecraft HUD screen by adding many useful features and tools. The design is simple, clean, and player-friendly, giving you quick access to buttons, real-time counters, and extra in-game information. With HUD-X, you can manage your gameplay more easily while keeping the screen neat and attractive.

Features

- F1 Hide or show the HUD instantly.

- F8 Toggle the Paper Doll.

- F3 Open the debug screen with advanced info.

- M Access the mod menu.

- Clock Always shows in-game time.

- Compass Always shows direction.

- Inventory Counter Displays filled and empty slots.

- XP Counter Shows your current XP level.

- Inventory Full Warning Alerts you when your inventory is completely full.

- Debug Screen Shows inventory status, Nether & Overworld coordinates, chunk coordinates, and more.
