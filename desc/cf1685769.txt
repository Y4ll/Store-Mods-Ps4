About

Durability HUD Viewer keeps the condition of your equipped gear visible without opening the inventory.

The compact panel shows the remaining durability percentage for your main-hand item, armor, and off-hand equipment. Color-coded values make worn gear easy to notice before it breaks, and an in-game settings screen can switch the HUD to exact current and maximum values.

Features

- Shows a compact durability percentage by default

- Lets Game Directors show or hide the HUD for the whole world

- Offers an alternative exact current/maximum durability format

- Includes an auto-saving in-game settings screen

- Tracks the main-hand item, helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots, and off-hand item

- Uses compact equipment icons instead of letter labels

- Displays healthy durability in green, warning durability in yellow, and critical durability in red

- Updates automatically when equipment changes or takes damage

- Hides slots that do not contain a damageable item

- Supports compatible custom items that use the standard Bedrock durability component

- Works independently for every player in multiplayer worlds

- Uses a compact HUD panel designed to stay out of the way

- Compatible with Vibrant Visuals

How to use

- Import the .mcaddon file into Minecraft Bedrock.

- Activate both the Durability HUD Viewer Behavior Pack and Resource Pack in your world.

- Equip armor or hold a damageable tool or weapon.

- The durability panel appears automatically in the lower-left corner.

Values remain green above 50% durability, turn yellow at 50% or below, and turn red at 20% or below.

Game Directors can run /durability-hud or /dh to open the settings screen and switch between percentage and exact-value formats. The selected format applies to the whole world and saves automatically.

Notes

- Items without a durability component are not shown.

- Both included packs are required: the Behavior Pack reads equipment durability, while the Resource Pack renders the custom HUD and icons.

- The add-on does not change item durability, repair costs, enchantments, or gameplay balance.

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.50 or newer

- No experimental toggles required

- No external add-ons required
