About This Pack

The Glowing Ores texture pack enhances all Minecraft ore textures by adding subtle emissive properties, making them glow faintly in darkness.
This applies to every vanilla ore:

Coal

Iron

Gold

Diamond

Emerald

Lapis Lazuli

Redstone

Copper

Quartz

Nether Gold

Ancient Debris

All Deepslate Variants

How It Works

This pack uses Minecrafts texture set system to add:

Metalness maps

Emissive roughness maps

These maps create a realistic glowing effect while preserving the original vanilla texture style.
The glow is intentionally subtle bright enough to improve visibility, but gentle enough to maintain Minecrafts natural atmosphere.
