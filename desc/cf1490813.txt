What's the point of earning an Elytra if you can't actually fly? Actual Elytra gives your wings true powered flightallowing you to soar freely across your world without needing thousands of firework rockets.

Say goodbye to awkward gliding and hello to true aviation. This add-on lets you use the Elytra with real, responsive flight mechanics. With simple up-and-down controls that seamlessly match the vanilla game style, navigating your world has never felt more natural.

Landing an Elytra shouldn't feel like a high-stakes emergency. With built-in speed control, all you have to do is sneak to brake, letting you glide right onto your landing pad or rooftop safely and smoothly.

Master the skies by turning your momentum into a weapon. Swooping directly into mobs or players delivers high-impact crash damage, letting you strike your targets from above before they even know what hit them.
