Bring a little companion along on every Minecraft adventure with Animated HUD Pets.

DISCORD:https://discord.gg/K5j5Y95tfn

This lightweight Bedrock resource pack adds a small animated pet to the corner of your HUD. Each pet has its own looping animation and reacts when the interaction button appears, making your interface feel more lively without changing gameplay.

Available pets:
Dog
Cat
Duck
Chicken
Pike
Adaptive Carousel, which switches pets every 30 seconds

Features:
Fully animated HUD companions
Five individual pet options
Automatic rotating-pet mode
Special interaction reactions
Support for desktop and mobile layouts
Purely visual and safe for normal gameplay
No behavior pack, commands, or experimental features required

Choose your preferred pet from the pack settings, activate the resource pack, and your new companion will appear directly on your HUD.
