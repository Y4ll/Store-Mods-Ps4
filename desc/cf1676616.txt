YGP! Immortal Dog

Your loyal companion never truly leaves.

YGP! Immortal Dog is a Minecraft Bedrock addon that turns your tamed wolf into an extremely powerful and protected companion.

Your Immortal Dog can have 240 HP, 50 attack damage, fall-damage protection, powerful defensive effects, automatic revival, emergency teleport, healing potions, owner binding, multiplayer-safe ownership, and special YGP control items.

The latest v3.1.0 update makes the dog stronger and adds life-saving rescue: if your health drops below 3, the dog comes to you from any distance and uses Healing and Regeneration until you are full.

Created by Yojitgamingpro / YGP Studio / YGP Minecraft.

UPDATE 3.1.0 EMERGENCY RESCUE & HEALING

Emergency Rescue

If the owners health drops below 3, the dog moves instantly.

It teleports to the owner from any distance.
It still comes from 100,000 blocks away.
It still comes across dimensions.
If the dog is too far to stay loaded, the same owned dog is summoned beside the owner.

Healing Potions

The dog uses Healing and Regeneration on its owner.

It keeps healing until the owner is back to full health.

The dog does not wait for a command. It sees the danger and comes.

YGP DOG CONTROL BONE

The YGP Dog Control Bone is the special owner-control item introduced in v3.0.0.

The Control Bone allows the owner to stop their Immortal Dog's defensive mode.

Owner Only

Only the correct owner can use the control system.

Other players cannot use their own Control Bone to control another player's dog.

Control

Use the supported:

Crouch + tap ground

interaction to stop defensive mode.

You can also use the supported interaction with your own dog.

First Tame

The first successful tame provides the owner with a YGP Dog Control Bone.

FALL-DAMAGE PROTECTION

The Immortal Dog is protected from fatal fall damage.

If your dog falls from a large height, including from high in the sky, fall damage is prevented from killing the dog.

The protection works independently of whether the owner is nearby.
