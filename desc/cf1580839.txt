Crown & Boots - Minimal Armor

A Minecraft Bedrock resource pack that turns your helmet into a crown, gives your boots a clean minimal look, and lets you choose exactly how much of your armor stays visible all without touching armor protection or stats.

Features

- Crown Helmet every helmet material (leather, chainmail, gold, iron, diamond, netherite, turtle shell) is replaced with a sleek crown-style design instead of the bulky default helmet.

- Minimal Boots boots get a small, clean accent design instead of the full chunky boot model.

- Hidden Chestplate & Leggings the chestplate and leggings are hidden by default, keeping your skin clean and visible.

- Zero Gameplay Impact this is a resource pack only. Armor points, enchantments, durability, and protection all work exactly as in vanilla Minecraft. Only the look changes.

- 5 Adjustable Visibility Levels pick exactly which pieces show, using Minecraft's built-in pack settings slider (no extra app or mod needed).

- Works for every armor material: Leather, Chainmail, Gold, Iron, Diamond, Netherite, Turtle Shell. (Copper is currently unsupported see Known Limitations below.)

How to switch levels in-game

- Go to Settings Global Resources (or your world's resource pack list).

- Find Crown & Boots - Minimal Armor in your Active packs.

- Tap the gear icon next to the pack name.

- Drag the slider to your preferred level (0 / 25 / 50 / 75 / 100).

The pack also shows an info panel (tap the pack for details) summarizing what each level does handy if you forget which is which.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file.

- Open it directly Minecraft will import it automatically into your Resource Packs.

- If it doesn't auto-open, go to Settings Storage Resource Packs Import, or drag the file into: games/com.mojang/resource_packs/ (or development_resource_packs/ for testing).

- Activate the pack from Global Resources, or attach it to your world from World Settings Resource Packs.

- (Optional) Set your preferred visibility level using the gear icon, as described above.

Compatibility

- Platform: Minecraft Bedrock Edition (Windows, Android, iOS, consoles)

- Minimum Engine Version: 1.26.0

- Type: Resource Pack (cosmetic only no behavior pack or scripting required)

- Safe to use on Realms and servers that allow custom resource packs (clients will be prompted to download it).

Known Limitations

- Copper Armor is not yet supported with the crown/boots design and will always remain fully hidden at every level, since no source design exists for it yet.

- The in-pack "Pack Settings" screen (accessible via pack info) includes a slider/label for reference only it explains the system but does not itself switch textures. The actual switch happens through the gear-icon slider described above, which is Minecraft's native mechanism for swapping bundled texture sets.
