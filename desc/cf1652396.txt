Skibidi Toilet Cold War: Elite Combat Edition

Total war between the Alliance (Cameramen, Speakermen and upgraded variants) and the Astros, with Toilets acting as a third hostile force.

Key features:
JavaScript combat system with unique elite abilities (ultimates, combos and elemental effects)
Dynamic battles that move across the map NPCs create distance, knock each other back and the fight spreads through the terrain (not just standing still punching)
Bosses with phases and mid-fight transformations
Progressive wave invasion mode (/function game/start)
Recruitment system: use gold to recruit allies and fight alongside your own army

Optimized and tested on low-end devices (including Motorola G04) with no lag even during large battles.

Unofficial fan project based on the Skibidi Toilet universe.
