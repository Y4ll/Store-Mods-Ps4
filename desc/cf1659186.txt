FPV Drone Add-On Add new controllable aircraft and ground vehicles to Minecraft Bedrock. This add-on enhances the gameplay experience with FPV drones, a unique control interface, sound effects, and additional decorative elements. Features - Controllable FPV drones - Various aircraft models - Unique FPV HUD - Sound and visual effects - Additional ground vehicles - Decorative elements for creating your own scenes and maps - Suitable for adventure maps, minigames, and experiments This add-on is designed to expand the capabilities of Minecraft Bedrock and add new vehicles and control mechanics.
