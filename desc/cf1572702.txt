Hold a light in one hand, use anything in the other. Offhand Torch lets you keep a torch, lantern, or any light source in your offhand slot so your main hand stays completely free for tools, weapons, or whatever you need.

How to Equip

Hold your torch or light source in your main hand, then:

- PC: Right click in the air while holding it

- Mobile: Hold the screen while holding it

It moves straight to your offhand. That's it.

Supported Light Sources

- Torch

- Lantern

- Soul Torch & Soul Lantern

- All other placeable light items

Features

- Dynamic lighting follows you as you move

- Main hand completely free use any tool or weapon

- Works with all light source items, not just torches

- Simple equip method no commands, no menus

- Bedrock compatible works around offhand slot limitations natively

Why You Need This

Bedrock normally doesn't let you place torches directly into your offhand from inventory. This addon solves that completely. Mine, fight, and build all while staying fully lit. No compromises.

Light in one hand. Freedom in the other.
