PUREVFX Immersive Visual Effects for Minecraft Bedrock

Settings

Every single feature is individually toggleable in-game through the PUREVFX Settings Book organized into Weather, Environment, Sky, Combat, and Ambient Sounds categories. A new Game Mode menu also lets you apply bulk quality presets (Low, Medium, High, Realistic) that set every toggle at once. No fake or dead toggles if it's in the menu, it works.
