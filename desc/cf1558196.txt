In the vast universe of Minecraft Pocket Edition (PE), where creativity knows no bounds, IndieCrew Studios is proud to announce the latest update. The "IC Clothes Addon: Outfits Update" is an expansion pack that brings a plethora of outfits and a variety of colors to the Minecraft PE experience, providing players with a collection of 896 new outfits across various categories, including hats, masks, suits, shirts, and pants.
