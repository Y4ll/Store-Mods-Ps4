Introducing New X Blissful rewrite v1.0

Newb X Blissful is one of the best minecraft bedrock edition shader available.

That offers realistic lighting,beautiful colors,realistic sky colors and makes your minecraft world more breathtaking.This visuals upgrade your Minecraft world to looking extra ordinary to make it more immersive and atmospheric.

This shader focus on making your Minecraft world more realistic while still remaining simple .

- Improved soft cloud

- Enhanced and realistic water

- Beautiful night skies

- Stars and galaxy

- Redesigned End sky

- Refined colour correction
