Story

When Hoeru hears a mysterious voice calling Number One, he is pulled into a strange cave and meets a giant mecha named TegaSword. There, he also finds a golden transformation device (basically a hand with sword attached) named after the mighty robot. TegaSword tells him that anyone who gathers all of the Sentai Rings will be granted one wish. Hoeru acceptsnot because he has a wish, but just to pass the time. With the GozyuWolf ring and golden TegaSword Changer in hand, he becomes the first Gozyuger with the power of GozyuWolf. He quickly proves his power by fighting evil and declaring himself the "Number One Savior."

He soon meets other ring-bearers: Rikuo, Ryugi, Kinjiro, Sumino and Mashiro who transform into powerful Gozyugers like GozyuLeon, GozyuTyranno, GozyuEagle, GozyuUnicorn and GozyuPolar.

Long before the story began, a great battle called the Universe War broke out. Many hero teams were lost or scattered leaving their respective power-source sentai rings, and only the Gozyugers remain as the last proper Super Sentai. Thats why its so important for them to rise up, collect the rings, and bring back balance to the universe.

What are the Sentai Rings?

These glowing rings are not just for show. They allow the heroes to transform, summon powerful weapons, and unite with the mighty TegaSword, a legendary robot known as the "Giant God." Each ring represents a color and power, and the more they collect, the stronger the Gozyugers become.

As the last hope of Earth in a world after the Universe War, they must battle enemies like the Aryee combatants and legendary ring-hunter Garyudo who constantly challenge them, and want to stop them from collecting all the rings and becoming Earths ultimate protectors.
