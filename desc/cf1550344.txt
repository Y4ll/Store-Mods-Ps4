Introducing Java Combat PvP+, an addon designed to make your PvP battles and sword combat feel more dynamic, responsive, and engaging.

Skills:
Active Skill 1: Sword Combat Increases your critical hit rate when fighting with a sword. You have a chance to knock back your enemy when attacking with a sword. You can also perform a sweep attack, similar to Java Edition, by attacking while sneaking.
Active Skill 2: Axe Combat Increases your critical hit rate when fighting with an axe. Jumping and attacking with an axe greatly increases your critical hit chance and critical damage. This ability has a cooldown.

This add-on is achievements friendly.
This add-on does not include player.json.
This add-on does not require experimental features.
