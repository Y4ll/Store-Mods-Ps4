I'm introducing my new project, which I've been investing a lot of time into, hence the name Definitive Vibrant Visuals. These realistic graphics will make you feel like you're inside Minecraft, thanks to the fact that each biome has a different setting depending on whether it's a warm or cold biome, making the game a more immersive experience.

Requirements

- Game Version: To try Vibrant Visuals, you need to enable the beta/preview version in Bedrock Edition and turn on the "Vibrant Visuals" option when creating or editing a new world.

- Vibrant Visuals in Minecraft Bedrock requires an Adreno 640 GPU (Snapdragon 855) or a Mali GPU (Mediatek 1000 series) for mobile devices. For PCs, a GPU with DirectX 12FL11 is recommended.

Mobile Devices

- Adreno 640 GPU: This GPU is found in the Snapdragon 855 processor.

- Mali GPU: The Mediatek 1000 series or a Mediatek GPU.

- A12 Bionic processor.

PC

- DirectX 12FL11: A GPU with DirectX 12FL11 is recommended for PCs.

Featured

- This works on Xbox, PS5, Android, iOS and Windows

- Anything metallic will have a metallic effect. For example: Iron Golem, Copper Golem... Some metallic textures may be missing, but I'll be adding new ones every day.

- This package has a Low version which removes wavey water for better optimization.

- Realistic shadows and lighting, polished.

- Realistic water with equally realistic caustics.

- Shiny minerals and metallic blocks.

- Tools and armor have metallic effects..
