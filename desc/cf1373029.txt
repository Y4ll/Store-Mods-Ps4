Mermaids are mythological figures present in legends that personify

the dangers and other aspects of the sea, and are therefore very common in the imagination of sailors or people who depended on the sea for their subsistence.

To gain mermaid powers, just come into contact with water in Minecraft Bedrock

and the addon does not contain craft, everything works automatically

<figure class="image align-center"></figure>

After entering the water you will have the powers of mermaids
