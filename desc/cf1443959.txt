The Last Dragon - AddOn

This AddOn adds a big tame and rideable dragon to your game!

How to tame your own dragon?

Wild dragons and dragon eggs spawn in the world!

- wild dragons can be tamed with cooked chicken, cooked beef and cooked porkchop
- A dragon egg must be lit with a flint and steel (right-click with flint and steel and wait 5 minutes);
after that you can tame it!

Equip your dragon with a saddle and fly through the air by looking up or down
