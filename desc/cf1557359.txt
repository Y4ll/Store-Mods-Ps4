How To Use

1. Crouch to open the Builder Menu 2. Select the build you want 3. Choose the required options 4. Look toward the location where you want the structure 5. Place the build and it will generate automatically.

100+ Build Collection

The addon contains a large collection of structures including houses, starter bases, farms, decorative builds, fantasy structures, utility builds and many more creations designed to make your Minecraft world feel bigger and more detailed.

Build Controls

- Undo: Remove previously placed builds when needed.

- Y-Level: Adjust the vertical placement of supported structures.

- Direction: Look toward the direction where you want your selected build to face.

Important Note

Some large structures require more chunks to be loaded before they can generate correctly. If a build cannot be placed or you receive a loading-related warning, increase your Render Distance and try placing the structure again.
