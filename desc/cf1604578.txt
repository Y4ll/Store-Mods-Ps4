What is the Chameleon at Home Addon?

Chameleon at Home is a Minecraft Bedrock addon that introduces the ultimate camouflage hide-and-seek experience, directly inspired by the hit game Meccha Cameleon. It allows you to dynamically scan ordinary Minecraft blocks and paint your armor pixel-by-pixel to blend seamlessly into your environment, hiding in plain sight.

Experience thrilling gameplay such as:

- Multiplayer Hide and Seek against your friends

- Singleplayer hunt modes against AI Camo Dummies

- Grid-based texture matching and painting

- Body Poses to Hide better

- Survival Flight and more!

If you can see it, you can become it!

Tools and How to Play

- Eyedropper: Your primary sampling tool. Point and use this item on any block within 10 blocks to extract its texture and save it to your temporary memory.

- Paintbrush: The core of the addon. Use this item to open the Canvas Mode UI to paint your sampled textures onto your head, chest, legs, or boots. You can also smack a fellow player with it to instantly paint the quadrant you hit!

- Color Gun: The Seeker's best friend. Fires a highly visible tracking laser up to 30 blocks away. Hitting a Hider with this weapon will instantly eliminate them with an explosive splash!

- Minigame Starter: Use this item to open the setup menu. Choose between Singleplayer (Zombies) or a Multiplayer invite system to assign roles and start the match.

Special Chameleon Blocks & Items

- Color Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, and Boots: A fully custom armor set given to Hiders. These pieces are divided into 4 quadrants each, allowing you to mix and match up to 16 different block textures on your body at once.

- Hider Dummy: A custom AI entity used in Singleplayer mode. They randomly spawn on top of highest blocks, automatically scan and paint themselves to match the floor, and pick a random pose to hide from you!

Fun Camouflage Mechanics

- Dynamic Player Scaling: To truly blend in, you can't be taller than a block! The moment a Hider equips their color armor, the physics engine dynamically shrinks them down to exact block proportions using custom animation scaling.

- Hover and Levitation: Jumping or sneaking while tiny applies custom slow-falling and impulse physics, allowing Hiders to glide smoothly into the perfect position. Basically Creative flight in Survival for the Hiders!

- Boundary Enforcement: Keeps the game fair. If any player tries to wander beyond the 40-block game radius, they are mathematically calculated and teleported directly back to the edge of the arena.
