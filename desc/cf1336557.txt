Tired of the same old survival routine? Ready to face unpredictable challenges that make every moment in your world a thrilling new experience?

Welcome to Random Events v3.6, the ultimate chaos addon that transforms your Minecraft gameplay. This pack introduces completely random eventsfrom helpful loot drops to terrifying Wither stormsthat trigger on a timer you control. With 35 unique events, you will never know what's coming next!

This is the perfect mod for any single-player world, multiplayer server, or realm looking to add a fresh layer of excitement and unpredictability.

What's New in v3.6?

No More Repeating Events! All 35 events now play in random order without repeating. Once all events have played, the queue reshuffles automatically.

Queue Display! Action bar now shows remaining events in queue.

Thunder Volley! Arrows rain from above with deadly lightning strikes.

Sky Drop! Get teleported 100 blocks high with a water bucket - MLG or die!

Storm Warp! Time changes with thunderstorms at night.

Frostbite! Freezing cold effects with snow particles.

Bug Fixes! Fixed Hot Hands and Storm Warp not working correctly.

Key Features

35 Unique Events: Rewards, chaos, danger, and extreme challenges.

No Repeats: Every event plays once before reshuffling.

Configurable Timer: Use a stick to set intervals (30 seconds to 1 hour).

Queue Display: See remaining events on your action bar.

Multiplayer Ready: Events affect all players online.

Simple Setup: Just apply the behavior pack and play!

The Events (Full List of 35!)

Reward Events (4)

- Sky Loot: Receive valuable items for free.

How to Install & Use

Installation:

- Apply the Random Events Behavior Pack to your world.

- IMPORTANT: In World Settings Experiments, enable Beta APIs

- Recommended: Enable cheats in world settings.

How to Play:

- Timer counts down on your action bar.

- 3-second warning before each event.

- Use a stick to open settings menu.
