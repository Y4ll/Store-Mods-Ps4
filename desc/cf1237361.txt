Caves & Cliffs Panorama with Shaders (Bedrock)

This is a really simple Resource Pack that adds shaders to the original panorama.

The seed hasn't been discovered by the Minecraft@Home team yet, but I used a recreation that was made by them, which is available from their discord.

The shaders used are the Complementary Reimagined Shaders.
