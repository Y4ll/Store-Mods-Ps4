About the Addon

Wooden Shapes expands your building possibilities in Minecraft by introducing a brand-new set of custom wooden geometries. Designed to seamlessly blend into the vanilla style, this addon adds vertical slabs, half slabs, mini cubes, and corner intersections for every wood type. Whether you are adding finer details to your furniture, crafting intricate house frames, or building complex structures, these new shapes give you the precision and creative freedom your builds have always needed.
