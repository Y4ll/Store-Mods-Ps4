Newb CraftEdge

A realism-focused visual overhaul for Minecraft Bedrock

Richer fog. Deeper shadows. Reflective water. Built to make your world feel alive.

Join our Discord

Overview

CraftEdge Visuals reimagines the way Minecraft Bedrock looks from thick atmospheric fog rolling across valleys, to sunlight breaking through the trees, to water that reflects the sky above it. Every effect is hand-tuned for a natural, cinematic feel while staying light enough for mobile devices.

Atmosphere & Fog

- Denser, height-based fog that hugs the ground and fades with altitude

- Natural distance haze that intensifies with weather thicker mist in rain, stronger haze on cloudy days

- Smooth exponential falloff for realistic depth perception

Lighting & Godrays

- Strong volumetric godrays for dramatic light shafts through trees and caves

- Deeper shadow contrast for a grounded, natural look

- Balanced ambient light so shadows stay readable, never flat

Water

- Subtle sky & cloud reflections softened and partial for a more natural, less mirror-like surface

- Moon reflections at night water now catches and reflects moonlight after dark

- Sharper wave ripples for crisp surface detail

- Realistic sun specular highlight that tracks the light source

- Clearer water with a natural blue-green tint

Rain & Wet Surfaces

- Reflective wet ground with scattered, natural puddles

- Stronger puddle reflections during rainfall

Clouds

- Soft forward-scattering glow when clouds face the sun

- Realistic shading based on light direction

- Optional Vibrant Clouds subpack punchier colors and higher contrast for a more stylized sky, matching the CraftEdge Visuals aesthetic

End Dimension

- Custom animated blackhole visible in the End sky, replacing the plain void backdrop for a more dramatic atmosphere

Compatibility

This shader pack requires a material/render binary loader to function it will not work on official, unmodified Minecraft. Follow the setup guide below for your platform.

How To Use

Android

- Download the .mcpack file from this page

- Open it with MB Loader it will automatically import the pack

- Open Minecraft and go to Settings Global Resources

- Find CraftEdge Visuals in the list and activate it

- Restart Minecraft for all effects to fully apply

Windows

- Download BetterRenderDragon

- Follow the installation guide included with BetterRenderDragon

- Import the Newb CraftEdge Shader resource pack

- Enable it in Settings Global Resources

- Restart Minecraft

Linux (x86_64)

For mcpelauncher-manifest users only.

- Download mcpelauncher-manifest

- Download mcpelauncher-materialbinloader

- Import the Newb CraftEdge Shader resource pack

- Enable it in Settings Global Resources

- Restart Minecraft if necessary

Tip: Applying it under Global Resources (not just a single world) ensures effects like fog, water, and lighting apply everywhere.
