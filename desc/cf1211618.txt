Gooblideco 2.0 is a personal project since 2025 featuring decorational blocks likes connected walls, connected roofs, connected glass and decorational items including the Filipino style store which is called "Tindahan or Sari-Sari Store.It is been updated for 2026 to support latest updates of minecraft bedrcock and I added bunch of more items reaching out 500+ blocks. This pack supports also low end devices for greater game experience and the project is still ongoing. With you guys, this addon will not be possible without your ideas, creativity and enthusiam and I am happy to see you in my Discord Server. Let's decorate our world with happiness and creativity.
