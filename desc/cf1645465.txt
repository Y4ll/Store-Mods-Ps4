Enhance your survival gameplay with sneak-activated vein mining, area digging, and tree felling for pickaxes, shovels, and axes full tool-tier, enchantment, and durability support!

This AddOn respects tool durability, and normal single-block mining still works exactly as before when you're not sneaking.

+ You can earn achievements with this AddOn!
ATTENTION: Achievements have been disabled since the Minecraft 26.50 update. I'm working on a solution.

Sneak while mine one ore and the whole connected vein comes with it!

Sneak while shoveling to clear a 3x3 patch of dirt, sand, gravel, clay and more!

Sneak while chopping to fell an entire tree, trunk and canopy, without touching the tree next to it.
