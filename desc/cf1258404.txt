It may seem like a huge car, but that's why they're called full-size! For the first country in terms of length of highways, why not?

The car has 4 seats and an inventory of 32 slots.

It is summoned using the /summon command and a spawn egg (there is a possibility to craft and extract as loot).

Iron golem sound effects are used.

Repaired with an copper ingot, iron ingot, gold ingot, netherite ingot, netherite scrap, iron nugget, gold nugget, redstone dust.

In the future, it is planned to add a color variation.
