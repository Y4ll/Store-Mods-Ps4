Legacy Panorama with Shaders (Bedrock)

This is a really simple Resource Pack that adds shaders to the legacy panorama used between versions b1.7.3-1.12.2.

The seed was discovered by the Minecraft@Home team.

- Seed: 2151901553968352745

- Coordinates: X=61.48~, Y=75, Z=-68.73~

- Version: Minecraft Java Beta 1.7.3

The shaders used are the Complementary Reimagined Shaders.

Note: GUI not included.
