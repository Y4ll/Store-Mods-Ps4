Chimedragons Elytra Collection brings the popular insect-inspired wings to Minecraft Bedrock. This pack features elytra designs based on butterflies, dragonflies, ladybugs, and more, giving your character colorful and natural-looking wings while flying. The original designs were made by Chimedragon for Java Edition, and this version ports them over so Bedrock players can enjoy the same look and feel in their own worlds.
