Velmora Visuals is a Vibrant Visuals shader pack built for Minecraft Bedrock's new rendering pipeline. It delivers a stunning Arctic Aurora atmosphere, dramatic crimson-to-violet sunsets, ice-blue moonlit nights, volumetric biome fog, animated living foliage, and ACES film-grade color grading all crafted to be both breathtaking and performance-conscious across a wide range of devices.

Arctic Aurora Sky & Atmosphere Custom sky/atmosphere with polar blues, coral sunsets, and a drifting glow-aurora particle effect at night.

Reimagined Visual Style
Signature Arctic Aurora identity with a cool, cinematic colour palette
Balanced between photorealistic depth and vanilla-friendly clarity
Immersive animated foliage grasses, ferns, vines, and crops breathe with smooth flipbook motion
Elegant fog transitions and raytraced-style water with deep blue underwater tinting

Advanced Atmospherics
Custom biome-based atmosphere settings for 60+ biomes including deserts, jungles, oceans, swamps, pale gardens, and more
Smooth sky colour transitions across 15+ keyframes throughout the full day-night cycle
Warm amber-to-crimson sunrises with dramatic horizon blending and layered Mie scattering
Deep midnight blue nights with a vivid zenith and soft moonlit ambience
Fiery orange-to-magenta sunsets with wide horizon bloom

Lighting & Sun Rays
Volumetric sun rays with high-intensity Mie glow at golden hour
Precise sun illuminance ramp bright warm midday dropping sharply at dusk to near-zero night
Cool-tinted moonlight casting soft ambient light across the landscape

Color Grading & Fog
ACES tone mapping with boosted contrast, elevated saturation, and warm highlights
White balance tuned at 6400K for a naturally vivid, sun-kissed look
Biome-aware volumetric fog with unique haze per environment purple roofed forests, blue-grey snow, amber deserts
Custom Henyey-Greenstein scattering for natural, physically accurate light diffusion

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open to import into Minecraft

- Enable Velmora Visuals in Global Resources

- Turn on Vibrant Visuals in Video Settings
