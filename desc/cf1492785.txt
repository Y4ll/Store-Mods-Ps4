Have you ever been frustrated with having to go back to a chat multiple times to remember what you have to do?

Or you are going to your friend's base, but you can't remember the coordinates?

This simple script pack can solve your problem!

It's easy to use - type on chat ".note " and add whatever you want!

You can always remove your note - type ".note remove" to make it gone.

To make this script works, don't forget to turn on Beta-API feature
