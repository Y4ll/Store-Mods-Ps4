## Ben 10 Volume 2 10 Aliens, Rituals, Gods & a Real Omnitrix

**It's Hero Time!** Strap on the Omnitrix and unlock the power to transform into 10 unique alien forms each with their own model, animations, and combat style. A fully working transformation system for Minecraft Bedrock, inspired by the Ben 10 universe.

### 10 Unique Aliens

- **Echo Echo** fast and agile, master of sonic attacks and unlimited summonable clones

- **Chromastone** a crystalline powerhouse that absorbs impact and fires energy beams

- **Swampfire** fire-immune and self-healing, unleashes explosive fire attacks

- **Big Chill** real flight (not a potion effect!), freezes enemies with ice attacks

- **Humungousaur** grows to massive size, crushing everything in his path

- **Brainstorm** telekinetic and electrical powers, genius-level alien

- **Spider-Monkey** web-slinging agility, traps enemies in sticky webs

- **Goop** shape-shifting alien that can shrink to squeeze through tight gaps

- **Arcticguana** ranged ice-beam specialist with a fully animated combat moveset

- **Alien X** a reality-warping cosmic being with 6 devastating powers: Black Hole, Black Hole Bullet, Time Manipulation, Telekinetic Grab, Follow Fire, and Mega Explosion

### Ritual Summoning System

No commands needed just drop the right items together on the ground:

- **Redstone + Emerald + Iron Ingot** receive the Omnitrix directly

- **Iron Block + Ice** summon the Ice God

- **Dirt + Iron Block** summon the Earth God

These gods cannot be found in the wild the ritual is the only way to summon them. Defeat them to earn a DNA Sample and permanently unlock Alien X or Arcticguana.

### A Real Working Omnitrix

Hold the Omnitrix, open the holographic selector, and pick your alien. Watch the transformation trigger instantly with custom particle effects and sound design.

### Battery System

Transformations aren't permanent the Omnitrix runs on a battery. Stay transformed as long as you can, then recharge before transforming again.

### Self-Destruct

A high-risk option in the Omnitrix menu a 30-second countdown ending in an explosion that destroys your Omnitrix. Perform the ritual again to get it back.

### Built for Immersion

Custom sound effects for every power, transformation, and event. Distinct particle effects throughout, including a cinematic falling capsule and holographic selector beam.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition (latest stable recommended)

- **Beta APIs must be enabled** in your world settings for the add-on to function
