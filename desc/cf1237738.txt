Vanilla+ Horses Addon for Minecraft BedrockTake your horseback adventures to the next level with the Vanilla+ Horses Addon! This addon improves Minecrafts horses with realistic models, a custom stamina system, and a range of new horse armors all while staying true to the vanilla style.But that's not all! It also includes a cinematic camera mode that can be toggled on or off while riding, giving your rides a more immersive and dramatic feel.Perfect for exploration, combat, or just showing off your favorite steeds this addon brings new life to horses in MCPE!

HOT FIX CONTENT:

- Improved horse gait selection by adding a new item: Horse Gaits Selector (crafted from string).

- Fixed an issue where horses could be damaged by their owner while riding.

- Fixed an issue where horses could be knocked back.

- Improved the cart attachment system to prevent accidentally opening the horse or cart inventory instead of attaching the cart.

- Added a recipe for Netherite War Horse Armor (crafted using Diamond War Horse Armor, a Netherite Ingot, and a Smithing Template).

- Increased horse jump strength.

Subpacks: Choose "Vanilla Horse Spawn" if you want to keep the vanilla horses, or "Replace Vanilla Horse" if you prefer to replace them with the custom ones.

This addon adds realistic horse gaits along with a stamina system when riding. In this beta version, it introduces two new horse breeds: the Thoroughbred and the Gypsy Vanner, each with unique strengths and weaknesses.

Thoroughbred: Moves quite fast but tires quickly due to a smaller stamina pool. Spawns in Plains and Savanna biomes. There are 6 color variants available: Dark Bay, Chestnut, Bay, White, Gray, and Brown.

Gypsy Vanner: Slower movement speed but has a much larger stamina pool, allowing for longer rides. Spawns in Taiga and Taiga Hills biomes. There are 3 color variants available: Tobiano, Blagdon Black, Blagdon Brown.

You can now craft horse armor, saddles, and name tags directly in the crafting table, making it easier than ever to gear up your horses!
The addon also introduces a brand new type of War Horse Armor, designed for battle-ready steeds!
It comes in 4 tiers: Golden, Iron, Diamond, and Netherite, each offering increasing protection and a unique look for your horse.

Golden War Armor: Reduces 20% of all damage taken.

Iron War Armor: Reduces 40% of all damage taken.

Diamond War Armor: Reduces 80% of all damage taken.

Netherite War Armor: Reduces 96% of all damage taken.

Horse Cinematic View? Yes, we can!
Experience riding like never before with the new Cinematic Camera System, activated by using the Golden Horseshoes item. It offers three camera modes to choose from:

Freecam A smooth, cinematic camera that follows the player dynamically.

Normal The classic riding view, just like in vanilla Minecraft.

Fixed Camera Places the camera at a fixed point, allowing the player to ride around freely while being filmed from one angle perfect for cinematic shots or roleplay scenes!

NEW FEATURES: VERSION 0.0.2 BETA.

In this update, we finally have carts that work in Minecraft!
Youll need to craft a Cart Wrench in order to attach a cart to a horse.

To attach a cart to a horse:
First, tame the horse. Then, use the Cart Wrench to select the horse (sneak + right-click on PC, sneak + long press on mobile).

After that, attach the cart to the horse using the same action (sneak + right-click on PC, sneak + long press on mobile).

To detach the cart:
Simply use the same action (sneak + right-click on PC, sneak + long press on mobile) on either the horse or the cart that is already attached.
