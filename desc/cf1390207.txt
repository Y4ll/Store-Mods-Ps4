MorphX

Bedrock Morph Add-on MorphX is a powerful Minecraft Bedrock morphing add-on that allows the player to transform into dozens of unique creatures, machines, animals, and fantasy entities. With one simple tap, you can take the shape, animations, hitbox, movement style, and abilities of any supported entity.

This add-on is perfect for Survival gameplay, roleplay, adventures, challenges, and content creators.

Installation Guide

Follow all 5 steps for the add-on to work properly: Download MorphX.mcaddon

Open the file Minecraft will import it

Go to World Settings Experiments and enable these 5 features:

Holiday Creator Features

Custom Biomes

Upcoming Creator Features

Molang Features

Beta APIs (if available on your device)

Go to Behavior Packs and Resource Packs

Activate MorphX in both sections

Start the world

How Morphing Works

You get the Soul Stone in Creative Mode The Soul Stone is the morph controller item. Use it to open the morph menu.

Kill an entity to unlock its morph When you kill any supported entity, its morph will automatically unlock inside the Soul Stone.

The more mobs you defeat, the more morphs you unlock.
