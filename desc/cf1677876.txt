Welcome everyone! Today, I am bringing you an unofficial port of the Chest Cavity mod so you can experience it on Minecraft PE.

The mod introduces a complete internal organ system for creatures in the game. You can open your own body or a mob's body to see the organs inside. But its not just for showit features fun gameplay mechanics that completely change the way you play.

How to Use and Play:

To open your own body, simply look down and long-press. This will open your internal body interface, allowing you to manage and replace your organs.

Mob Abilities:

You can take the hearts of animals or monsters and place them in your own chest. Once you do, you will gain the abilities of that creature! Imagine gaining the strength of a monster or the special ability of an animal just by swapping hearts.

Ability Activation:

Abilities don't activate on their own. To trigger your equipped organ ability, sneak three times in a row, and the ability will be ready to use

What's New in the V2 Update:

- Spider Heart: Climb walls and shoot webs just like a spider

- Volatile Stomach: Shoot fireballs at your enemies, just like a Ghast

- Llama Lung: Spit at your enemies to deal damage and knock them back

- Gills & Rotten Organs: Breathe underwater with gills, but beware of rotten muscles giving you slowness and hunger. Small animal organs will also make you weak and slow

- Fish & Saltwater Organs: Added for aquatic life support
