ChatoTerra 1.1 SlugTerra Addon For Bedrock

Inspired by SlugTerra. Bring the SlugTerra experience to Minecraft Bedrock with collectible slugs, blaster combat, storage.

Discord:
https://discord.gg/BuCPMpKdP7

Included Slugs

Seven playable species, each with its own element, habitat, and unique ability:

-

Infurnus Element:Fire Plateaus

-

Armashelt Element:Earth Underground caves

-

Bubbaleone Element:Water Beaches

-

Frostcrawler Element:Water Frozen areas

-

Electroshock Element:Energy Spruce forests

-

Polero Element:Earth Plains

-

Flaturlohinkus Air Plains

All slugs can spawn in the wild, be tamed, and trained up to level 20. As they level up, they unlock improvements to damage, speed, and area effects.

Slugpedia

An interactive in-game book. Navigate through elemental type slug detailed entry, featuring health, damage, speed, biome, taming method, and abilities by level.

It also includes a dedicated Tutorial with separate sections:

Storage and Transportation

-

Belt 3 slots, equippable in the chest slot.

-

Slug Carrier 6 slots, equippable in the chest slot.

-

Shayne Backpack 6 slots + automatic pickup of your slugs within 2 blocks.

-

Tubes One for each species, plus an empty tube. Stores, transports, and displays the slug in your hand in 3D.

-

Slugrack Take your slugs into battle with the Slugrack.

Utility Items

-

Slug Radar Craftable. Choose a species through its own menu using the same flow as the Slugpedia, with one species selected at a time. While held in the main hand or off-hand, it displays in the actionbar every 0.5 seconds whether a slug of that species is within 32 blocks, along with its distance [x,y,z].

-

Medicine Cures the blaster's slug status (powered-up slug).

-

Slug Kibble and Feeder Used to feed slugs for XP and taming.

-

Slug Rack A shelf used to transport and store up to 16 slugs.

Progression

Tame slugs using Slug Kibble, fight with them, and let them gain passive XP while stored in the Rack. Each slug saves its level, XP, owner, and variants.

The leveling system is more demanding and balanced up to level 20, rewarding consistent use without requiring excessive grinding.

Installation

Enable both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack in a world. Recommended for survival gameplay with friends.

Equip your belt, load your blaster, and become the best slinger in ChatoTerra!

Requirements

Requires both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack to be enabled together. Built for Minecraft Bedrock with Script API. The Beta APIs experimental toggle must be enabled.
