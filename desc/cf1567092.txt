Minecraft Bedrock 26.50 or newer is required for Health Bars 1.2.0+

About

See how much health a mob has left without opening a menu or guessing from combat.

Health Bars adds a lightweight pixel-art combat UI layer to Minecraft Bedrock. When a mob takes damage, a health bar appears above it, updates as health changes, and disappears after a short configurable delay.

The add-on includes four visual styles, optional player bars for PvP worlds, an optional HP number display, and an in-game settings menu opened with a command.

Features

- Shows pixel-art health bars above mobs after damage

- Updates the bar as the mob's health changes

- Four built-in visual styles: Golden, Simple, Classic, and RPG

- Optional player health bars for PvP and multiplayer worlds

- Optional HP number display with current and max health

- On-hit mode keeps bars temporary and clean by default

- Always-visible mode can keep tracked bars on screen

- One responsive in-game settings screen with live style previews

- Settings are saved per world

- Lightweight behavior/resource pack pair

How to use

Install the .mcaddon, activate both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack in your world, then fight normally.

When a mob takes damage, a health bar appears above it. The bar updates while the mob is alive and removes itself after the configured delay. Player bars are disabled by default so your own bar does not clutter normal gameplay.

Open the settings screen with:

/health-bars
/hb

The settings command is available to all players without operator permissions. Choose the style, display, and visibility options on the same screen. Changes are saved automatically, including when the screen is closed.

Notes

Damage Numbers is a separate add-on that can be used together with Health Bars.

Health bars are cosmetic only and do not change combat balance, mob health, damage, or loot.

Settings apply to the current world only.

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.50 or newer

- No additional behavior pack dependencies required
