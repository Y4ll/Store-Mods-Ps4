Wings Rings brings a new sense of freedom to your Minecraft travels! Designed for explorers who value speed and style, this addon introduces 4 unique wing types to make your journey much faster and more exciting. Turn long distances into effortless flights, gliding over landscapes with ease while giving your character a stunning new look. The perfect choice for players who want to explore their worlds with agility and a touch of elegance!

++ Warning ++

To be able to fly in Survival mode, you need to activate Minecraft Education.

++ Systems ++

Feather Wing Ring: Despite being easy to manufacture, this wing is unique in having a limited flight range. In real-life flight, it lasts approximately 2 minutes.

To make up for lost time, simply stop flying and time will start loading again.
