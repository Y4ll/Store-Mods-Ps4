An add-on that adds more hostile mobs and bosses into your game!

This add-on will receive updates so let me know if there are bugs I needed to fix.

Warning before you approach a boss, you'll need to prepare to be able to defeat them, they will be found in raids so be sure to be prepared if you want to start one!

Please max out your armor before trying to fight the bosses!

Spoiler Title Villagers

Sergeant Major

this mob is currently broken due to component depreciation and I couldn't get the time to fix him as of now, he doesn't spawn naturally too so he's low priority.

- HP: 520

- Phase 1 Melee Damage: 12

- Phase 1 Range Attack Damage: 25

- Phase 1 Attacks:

- Seed rain: the golem will shoot bullet seeds that can grow into trees to damage the target and heal the shooter if close.

- Seed snipe: slow charge time range attack but deals huge damage to the target.

- Golem smash: golem will smash the ground, which can lift the target if close enough.

- Phase 2 Melee Damage: 2 per tick

- Phase 2 Range Attack Damage: 25

- Phase 2 Attacks:

- Gapple heal: Sergeant Major will eat a golden apple to heal.

- Potion throw: Segreant Major can throw different potions at targets, like slowness, poison and instant damage.

- Emerald bomb rain: Sergeant Major will summon emerald bombs in the surrounding area.

- Shoot strafe: Sergeant Major will move in a zigzag backwards while shooting.

- Roll: does no damage but grants immunity for a bit.

This entity does not spawn naturally ATM since he will be reworked soon.

Cleave Carrot

Cleave Carrot is a melee plant that can slice enemies around 2 blocks range, due to their size they cannot be planted around near other cleave carrots, by doing so they'll pop and break if they are too and drop their stem item.

You can turn cleave carrots into Crusade Carrots if you use a golden ingot on them.
