About the Project

ModBlockTE Furniture is a large-scale furniture project designed to create a new type of gameplay experience in Minecraft.

The project connects furniture with the game system and introduces many features that are rarely seen in Minecraft addons.

Players can experience advanced systems such as:

Advanced farming

Machines and equipment

Interactive furniture

Shop and home management

Realistic gameplay simulation

The goal of this project is to make the player feel closer to a real-life experience while still playing inside the world of Minecraft.

About the functions contained in the full version :

- The size of the TV has been changed and the content of the channels has changed + the TV remote and the air conditioner remote have been added

-

- Grain Grinding Machine

You can grind grains such as barley or coffee beans.

- The grinding process takes about 5 seconds, with smoke effects and grinding sounds.

-

- Farming System

Place seeds on farm soil and water them.

After some time the crop will grow and you can harvest it by clicking on the plant.

- More seeds will be added in future updates

-

- Victorian Dough Mixer

A manual Victorian dough mixer allows you to create different types of dough.

Examples:

Flour + Water Pizza Dough

Flour + Milk + Water Victorian Bread Dough

-

- Victorian Oven

The Victorian oven allows you to cook different foods.

Features:

Requires wood and fire

Cooking time varies depending on the food

Needs new firewood every 2 minutes
