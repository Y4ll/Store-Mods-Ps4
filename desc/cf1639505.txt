F O R G O T T E N V I S I T O R S (OFFICIAL PORT)

YOU LEFT THEM.

Every night can become dangerous as different Visitors take turns approaching your base.

The world warns you with countdowns, sounds, visual glitches, camera shake, and distorted world effects. When the timer reaches zero, a Visitor appears nearby and begins hunting, stalking,

breaking in, or searching for the player.

JAVA MOD: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/forgotten-visitors

- Night visit system with rotating Visitors.

- Countdown before visits.

- World distortion during the visit event.

- Camera shake, warning text, glitch screens, and screens.

- Custom main menu, intro slides, menu music, hover sounds, and click sounds.

- Smart spawn system that places Visitors on safe surface locations near the player.

- Visitors despawn after the visit.

- Random events during gameplay.

- Config GUI for options.

- Player morph system.

- Custom Mickey body display.

- The Face machete weapon.

- Gore system with configurable gore level. (effects)

- Blood particles and limb damage effects.

- lore book.

- Multiplayer-compatible visit events.

Commands

-

/the_forgotten_visitors_config
Opens the normal config GUI.

-

/fv_events next
Shows tonights Visitor.

-

/fv_events visit <visitor>
Starts a selected Visitor event. Requires cheats/operator permission.

-

/fv_events select <event>
Same event selector command. Requires cheats/operator permission.

Available event IDs:
the_face, disembodied, acephalous, mickey_morph, photo_negative_mickey, willie, undying, impurity, all

Config toggle command:

- /fv_config_toggle <key>

Config keys:
jumpscares, sounds, animations, visuals, custom_body, block_breaking, climbing, gore_up, gore_down, gore_0 through gore max level.
