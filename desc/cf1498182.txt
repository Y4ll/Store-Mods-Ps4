Miracle Storis Addon

Number of talismans: 13 (Rabbit, Snake, Dragon, Butterfly, Bee, Ox, Ladybug, Cat, Peacock, Horse, Turtle, Fox) plus their kwamis.

Unique mechanics and details:

-

Animations and interface: Custom transformation animation, opening yoyo menu.

-

Interaction with kwamis: You can mute a kwami, force it to use its power, or prevent it from flying beyond a certain radius.

-

Miraculous boxes and obtaining: Boxes for talismans are present, along with a mechanic to obtain a talisman from a box.

-

Customization: Change costumes for Ladybug, Cat, Hawkmoth, Turtle, Fox.

-

Communication: Ability to message another player through a weapon (e.g., Cat's staff).

-

Weapon modes: Implemented so far for Cat's staff can switch between tossing and throwing.

-

Decorations: Marinette's and Adrien's bags, Guardian's staff, rings.
