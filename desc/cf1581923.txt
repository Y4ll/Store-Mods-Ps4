Want to become cute Baby Mobs or Custom Mobs in Minecraft PE?
With Morph Into Mobs, you can transform into many different mobs and use their special abilities.

Features:

- Morph into many Latest vanilla Minecraft mobs.

- Become cute Baby Mobs from the latest Tiny Takeover update.

- Transform into Custom Mobs like Windigo, Werewolf, and T-Rex.

- Use special mob abilities.

How To Use:
1. Activate both the Resource Pack and Behavior Pack before entering your world.

2. Craft the Morph Menu item using the recipe shown in the screenshots.

3. Use the item to open the Morph Menu.

4. Choose a Category: The addon includes multiple categories:

Mobs: Contains many latest vanilla Minecraft mobs that you can transform into.

Baby Mobs: Contains cute baby versions of various mobs from recent Minecraft Tiny Takeover update.

Custom Mobs: Contains custom Mobs.

5. Morph Into a Mob: Simply press the morph button of the mob you want to become.

6. Special Abilities, Some mobs have unique abilities.

- Spider: Climb walls just like a real spider.

- Enderman: Teleport to nearby locations.

- Creeper: Create an explosion ability.

- Warden: Use the Darkness Ability, Nearby players will receive the Blindness effect.

More abilities will be added in future updates.

7. Camera Mode: If you want to change your camera view after morphing, use the Camera Item.

This allows you to switch between:
First Person
Third Person

Some mobs currently have First Person disabled because of visual glitches. It will be re-enabled once a proper fix is available.

8. Back To Player: Open the Morph Menu and press the Back To Player button at the bottom of the menu.

9. This addon is currently in Beta. Some features may be added, changed, or removed in future updates. You may also encounter bugs or visual glitches while using the addon.

Let me know in the comments which mobs and abilities you would like to see in future updates.
