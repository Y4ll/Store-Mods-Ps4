RX FPS Booster

RX FPS Booster is a performance-focused resource pack designed to make Minecraft Bedrock run more smoothly by reducing particles, animations and unnecessary visual effects.
Choose between five different optimization profiles:

Balanced Improves performance while keeping most visual effects.

Performance Reduces demanding effects for a smoother experience.

Ultra FPS Prioritizes frame rate over visual quality.

Extreme FPS Removes most unnecessary particles and effects.

Ultra Extreme Maximum optimization for low-end devices, large worlds and demanding situations.Each profile lets you decide how much visual quality you want to sacrifice in exchange for better performance. You can switch profiles whenever you need without installing separate packs.
