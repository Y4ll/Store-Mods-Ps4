This addon adds over 500 pieces of furniture to the game. There's everything from kitchen utensils and bathroom equipment to things like a stove, shower products, and entertainment-related things like a pool table and gaming PC. It is a great addition if you want more options to decorate your home.

All furniture is solid 3D blocks, meaning you can easily rotate them in any direction you want. Some furniture, such as chairs and sofas, also have some functionality in that sense so that you can sit on them. Currently, all furniture can be crafted at the decorations table.

Creator: ROBERTGAMER69, Twitter, YouTube, PATREON

How does it work?

All the furniture are solid 3d blocks, this means you can easily rotate the furniture in any direction you want. Some furniture, like chairs and sofas, also has some functionality in that regard so that you can sit on them. Currently, all furniture can be crafted in the new Furnicraft Bench.

How can I change the color and material of the furniture?

Now you can change the color or material of the furniture more easily. You just need to have the brush and interact with the furniture.

List of changes of FURNICRAFT v28

Changes:

- Compatible with Minecraft v26.44+ (And Betas / Previews)

- It is no longer necessary to activate experimental games

- Now you can play with ACHIEVEMENTS (Achievements are active as long as you DO NOT activate commands in the world)

- Now all wooden furniture supports the new wood and poplar leaves from the next drop (use the brush to change it)

- Removed more blue lines on some furniture (fixing a bug where blue lines would appear on certain pieces).

- New system to change the color and texture of furniture. (Now, interacting with a paintbrush opens a menu where you can select a color or texture to apply to a piece of furniture).

- Complete redesign of the security camera system (now, placing a camera prompts you to name it, and using the tablet lets you view, rename, and share cameras with your friends) (cameras are private unless you share them with a friend).

- Complete redesign of the emote system (now you can perform partner or group emotes with up to 4 players) (several new emotes added).

- Complete redesign of the camera system (now you can place standard and tracking cameras) (several new cameras added).

- Classic Minigames have arrived (now, using the "Minigames Joystick" item, you can play various minigames like Minesweeper, Sudoku, and Tic-Tac-Toe) (you can change the color and height of the board, as well as the difficulty of each game independently).

- Interacting with globes will now make them spin continuously and gradually slow down to a stop.

- Added connectable glass blocks and connectable colored glass blocks.

- Added TheGic
