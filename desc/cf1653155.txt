How It Works

Waystones create fast-travel points through an intuitive interface. Teleport costs range from 1 to 10 XP levels, calculated by distance. Each dimension has a maximum Waystone limit to preserve performance:

- Main Edition: 12 slots in the Overworld, 7 in the Nether, 5 in the End.

- Unlimited: No XP required!

If a dimension reaches its limit, break an existing Waystone to register a new one.

Crafting Recipe:

Place the Waystone and click it to register. Rules:

- Name must be 316 characters.

- No duplicate coordinates (x, y).

- No duplicate names in the same dimension.

- Dimension slot limit must not be exceeded.
