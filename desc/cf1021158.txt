This resource pack/texture pack enables you to spot entities, storages, beds, etc through blocks. this is very useful resource pack for both survival as well as multiplayer survival. With this texture pack you can you can even find structures like Mineshaft, buried treasures, shipwreck, ocean ruin and many more as you can see the chests they contain.

- ESP Default: ESP applies to all entities like Players, Mobs, Chest, bed, etc. + Player health bar (new)

- ESP Minimal: ESP applies to only enties that have strategic value. eg. Hostile mobs, players, chests, bed etc. + Player health bar (new)

- ESP Minimal Singleplayer: Same as `ESP Minimal` But with player ESP disabled

Method to use ESP with the vibrant visuals:

1. Enable "Allow in-game Graphics Mode Switching" under Video Settings. (You cant enable this option when you are in-game, So enable it from Main menu).
2. Enter inside the world or Server. Then go into the settings, under video Settings.
3. Select Fancy or Simple Graphics mode if not already. And Then go back to the game.
4. Now again go to settings under video setiings, And now you can enable the Vibrant visuals mode now.
After this the ESP will work without any issue!

Note: You will have to do (from step 2) every time you exit and re-open Minecraft.
