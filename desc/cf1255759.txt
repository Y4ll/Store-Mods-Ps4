Can you imagine playing this game with dragons, cyclops, ogres, giants, centaurs, minotaurs and more mystical creatures? now is the time to find out how it will be, download this addon and capture dragon eggs, face bosses, tame beasts, and explore a world with more items and new dungeons waiting to be explored

An addon made in Brazil

Transform your Minecraft world into an epic fantasy adventure!
With the Mythical Creatures Addon, explore a land filled with dragons, cyclops, ogres, giants, centaurs, minotaurs, and many other legendary beings. Tame powerful beasts, challenge mighty bosses, and uncover ancient structures hidden across the Overworld, Nether, and even icy biomes!
