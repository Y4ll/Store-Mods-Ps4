How to Use

Basic Controls

- Aim at where you want to hook

- Use Item (right-click/tap) to shoot the hook

- Jump (in auto-pull) or Hold Jump (manual) to pull yourself

- Switch items to remove the hook

Key Features

Realistic Physics

- Hooks fly faster when you're already moving (momentum system)

- Follow the actual path your hook took for natural swinging

- Smooth acceleration and deceleration

- Physics-based rope tension and breaking

Two Control Modes

Manual Mode (Default)

- Toggle with Sneak + Use

- Hold Jump to pull yourself toward the hook

- Release Jump to stop pulling

- Ideal for precise positioning and technical gameplay

Auto-Pull Mode

- Hook automatically pulls you when it lands

- Perfect for fast exploration and parkour

- Sneak while swinging to set your preferred distance

- Jump for extra upward boost near the destination

Safety Features

- Grappling Insurance: Automatic slow falling when approaching ground

- No Fall Damage: Safe landings after swinging

- Rope Break System: Hook detaches if stretched too far

- Auto-Release on Stuck: Won't trap you in walls

Installation
