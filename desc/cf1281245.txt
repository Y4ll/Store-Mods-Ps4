Instant Build Addon!!

Spawn The Build Where Ever You Like!

How to Use:

Open the Creative Menu and grab the "Build Ball"

Right-click to spawn the build.

The build will generate starting at your height and expand upwards, extending to the southeast.
So plan the placement carefully!

(Maybe Use A Practice World First) There Is No Undo!

Troubleshooting!

Large Builds May Cause Performance Issues On Weaker Systems!

If A Build Doesn't Spawn Fully:

Respawn the build in the same location and stand near the gap to ensure it fills in.
Alternatively, Set your simulation distance to 12 or as high as possible to allow the build to load properly!
