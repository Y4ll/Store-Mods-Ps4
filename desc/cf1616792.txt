Someone's Glowing Ores

Never miss a diamond in the dark again!
This lightweight, achievement-safe resource pack focuses purely on making all ores emit a stunning PBR glow in the dark.

Features

- 100% Achievement Safe: Built purely as a Vanilla Resource Pack. No cheats or behaviors used!

- Global Resource: Apply in your main menu settings to use on every world, Realm, and Server.

- Stunning PBR Glow: All ores (Diamond, Iron, Gold, Redstone, Emerald, Lapis, Coal, and Nether Ores) emit a natural, beautiful light source.

- Performance Friendly: Contains only the exact textures needed for ores, keeping your game running smoothly.

Requirements

To see the glowing effect, you must have Deferred Technical Preview enabled in your Minecraft Video Settings. This pack utilizes the new PBR (Physically Based Rendering) pipeline in Bedrock.

Installation

- Download and double-click the .mcpack file.

- Go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Someone's Glowing Ores.

- Turn on Vibrant Visuals in your Video Settings!
