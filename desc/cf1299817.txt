Summary

Step into the wind with these 20 stunning skins, capturing every iconic moment from the fan-favorite 11th Heisei Rider series that aired from September 6, 2009 to August 29, 2010.

Kamen Rider W (pronounced Double) is the legendary detective Rider who protects Fuutothe Windy City. What makes W truly unique is that he is two people in one body: Shotaro Hidari, a passionate, hard-boiled detective, and Philip, his genius partner with a mysterious past.

Using the Double Driver and Gaia Memories which channel elemental and combat powers, they fuse to form a single Kamen Rider, fighting against Dopantshumans corrupted by Memory powers.

Ws powers are all about combination. Each Rider form is split vertically: Shotaro controls the right side (often using the Joker, Metal, or Trigger Memories), while Philip controls the left side (using Cyclone, Heat, or Luna).

This results in a wide variety of form combinations, each with its own fighting style, elemental power, and weaponry.

Why Accel, Skull, and Eternal are included?

Kamen Rider W is more than just one Riderits a world full of interconnected heroes and enemies, each with their own Memory systems and transformations.

Kamen Rider Accel

A trusted ally and member of Fuutos police force, Ryu Terui transforms using the Accel Driver and fights with high-speed, motorcycle-based power. His forms evolve from brute force to technical speed, reflecting his growth from a lone avenger to a true Kamen Rider.

Kamen Rider Skull

The original detective Rider and Shotaros mentor, Soukichi Narumi. Using the Skull Memory and a minimalist Lost Driver, he represents the origins of Ws justicea noir-inspired fighter in a fedora, embodying what it means to be hard-boiled.

Kamen Rider Eternal

The main villain, Katsumi Daido, leader of the mercenary group NEVER, uses the Eternal Memory and the Lost Driver to become a powerful yet emotionless undead Rider. Clad in white and blue with a black cape, Eternal is the dark reflection of Wone who fights not to protect, but to destroy. Cold, ruthless, and consumed by his past, Daido stands as Ws most dangerous and tragic foe.
