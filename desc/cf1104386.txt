This resource pack edits player animation for third person persective. Such That You can look around the player freely, which gives more dynamic look. usually when you move your point of direction in third person, the player's face also moves. But here the player remains still.

The Most useful usecase of this, is to make thumbnails or other cover pictures out of your player. You can take screenshot of player with pack enabled, and it will look more cinematic. And it will feel that actually a third person has taken the player's screenshot.

You don't have to do anything after activating it in resource packs. Once activated, Go to third person persection and try it out.
