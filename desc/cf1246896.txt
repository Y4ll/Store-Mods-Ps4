Welcome to the Ultimate 2D Pokemon Adventure! Paper Pokemon!

Paper Pokemon will bring the thrill and enjoyment of any Pokemon Add-on but in 2D, based around Paper, Paint and other Art Materials.

When you start a world you will be given a Letter from Professor Oak, Open this letter to choose a Starter Pokemon.

Anything Paper Pokemon Related will be crafted on an Easel.

Around your World you will find Apricorn Trees, interact with these Trees to recieve an Apricorn of the colour shown. Apricorn Trees will grow back an Apricorn after sometime.

You can place an Apricorn on a Crafting Table to get Apricorn Seeds to plant your own Apricorn Trees.

Create Mulch to help your Apricorn Trees grow. Growth Mulch will grow your Trees by one stage, Gooey Mulch will grow you your Trees by two stages.

Cook Apricorns to create Apricorn Dye, this is what will be used to create Paintbrushes.

You can mix Apricorn Dyes to create all the colours.

You will need to craft some Paintbrushes before you do anything else. Just change the colour of the Dye to change the colour of the Paintbrush.

Create Coloured Paper using Paper and Paintbrushes.

With the Coloured Paper craft your PokeBalls

Catching Pokemon is simple, throw your chosen PokeBall at a Wild Pokemon and hope!

Interact with the PokeBall to collect your Pokemon once it is caught.

When you have a Pokemon you can start earning Experience Points by using your Pokemon to Fight Wild Pokemon.When you send out a Pokemon they will automatically look for a Wild Pokemon to fight, you can change this by interacting with your Pokemon and choosing the "Follow" button.

When a Wild Pokemon is defeated it will drop an Experience Candy, you can then use this Candy to give yourself Experience Points.

When you have 20 Experience Levels you can Evolve your Pokemon.20 Levels will be taken from you when you choose to evolve your Pokemon

Some Pokemon require an Evolution Stone to Evolve, for this you will have to farm Pokestops for Painting Stones, Paint a Painting Stone with different Paintbrushes to create an Evolution Stone.

If your Pokemon faints during a Battle you can use a Max Revive to fully Heal it back to full health.

Healing a Pokemon does not require an Easel

When exploring your World looking for Pokemon you will come across PokeStops. Interact with them to earn some Materials for Crafting PokeBalls, they will reset after 5 minutes.

As well as PokeStops you will be able to find PokeGyms scattered around your world.

When you interact with a PokeGym you will get some useful items just like a PokeStop. If the PokeGym has a Pokemon image above it then you can interact to start a Pokemon Raid.

The Raid Pokemon will have more HP and more ATTACK so you may need more then one Pokemon to defeat it, if you do you will recieve that Pokemon as a reward with a high chance of a Shiny as well a good amount of reward loot.

This add-on DOES NOT require experimental features.

The player.json IS NOT used in this add-on
