Primeval Craft is a Minecraft Bedrock Add-on that adds creatures from the Primeval TV Series, including creatures from the past and fictional creatures from the future.

The creatures in this add-on spawn from anomalies, based on the series which are doorways through time. Anomalies can be found in almost any biome on both land and in water.

Neural Clamps are items that can be used to interact with the Future Predator to prevent it from attacking (unless provoked by the player). Neural clamps can be crafted with a redstone block in the middle and 8 iron ingots around it.

A few vanilla items have been renamed. Beef and Cooked Beef have been renamed to Meat and Cooked Meat. Spider Eye has been renamed to Bug Eye. Chicken and Cooked Chicken have been renamed to Bird Meat and Cooked Bird Meat.
