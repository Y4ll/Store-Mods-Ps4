A Minecraft Bedrock Chain Mining add-on lets players save time by breaking a single block to instantly mine an entire ore vein, tree, crop field, or cluster of leaves.

Overview

- Ore veins, chain-breaks connected ore, respecting pickaxe tier requirements, Fortune, Silk Touch, and XP drops just like vanilla

- Tree felling, chop one log with an axe and the whole trunk (and optionally the attached leaves) comes down together

- Crop harvesting, harvest a full field of mature crops at once, with optional auto-replanting (free or seed-consuming) and fortune-aware yields

- Leaf clearing, clear connected leaf blocks with shears or a hoe, keeping vanilla sapling/stick/apple drop chances

- Vanilla friendly (by design), tool tiers, Silk Touch, Fortune, Unbreaking, and hunger exhaustion all behave the way you'd expect, (Creative mode never touches your inventory or durability)

- Configurable, tune chain limits, search radius, durability cost, hunger cost, replanting mode, and more from the pack settings screen, no config file editing required
