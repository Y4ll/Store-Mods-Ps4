Eclipse Reflective Grasses is a PBR-based visual enhancement pack that brings realistic mirror-like reflections to Minecraft. It applies physically based rendering (PBR) materials to over 20 different blocks, including all grass variants, foliage, and even Nether blocks.

With full Ray Tracing compatibility, this pack delivers stunning reflections and lighting behavior, transforming your world into a more immersive and detailed environment without losing Minecrafts original aesthetic.

Features:

Realistic mirror reflections powered by PBR materials

Supports all grass and vegetation blocks

Includes custom reflections for Nether surfaces

Fully compatible with Minecrafts Ray Tracing mode

Optimized for visual quality and performance

Designed and created by AztralMC64

Version: 1.0.0

Type: Resource Pack (PBR / Ray Tracing Compatible)

Requirements: Minecraft Bedrock Edition with Ray Tracing enabled
