Java PvP Features for Bedrock Cooldown Bar, Crit Hearts & Sweep Edge

Bring the iconic feel of Java Edition PvP to your Minecraft Bedrock Edition world. This add-on recreates three signature combat features that make fights clearer, more skillful, and more satisfying: an Attack Cooldown Bar, Crit Hearts, and Sweep Edge. Whether youre dueling friends, running a PvP server, practicing sword combat, or recording content, these Java-inspired features help you time hits, read critical moments, and fight with more precision.

Features

Attack Cooldown Bar
A clean on-screen bar shows your weapons attack charge after every swing. Instead of spam-clicking, you can time your next hit perfectly just like Java 1.9+ combat. Great for swords, axes, and custom PvP gamemodes.

Crit Hearts
Critical hits are highlighted with Java-style heart and crit feedback. Instantly tell when you land a jump crit, fall crit, or fully charged critical strike, making every fight easier to read.

Sweep Edge
Swords display a sweeping arc and area-hit feedback that recreates Javas Sweeping Edge feel. Perfect for crowd control, multi-target fights, and making sword combat look and feel more dynamic.

Why Youll Like It

Makes Bedrock PvP feel much closer to Java Edition.
