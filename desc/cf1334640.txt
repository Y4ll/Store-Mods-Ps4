This is a bedrock port of "Tactical 3D Tools & 3D Weapons PvP"

This 3d tools resource pack changes the 2d vanilla tools and weapons into a more 3d models tools and 3d weapons, the design comes from those tactical weapons, but translated to minecraft's style, and it's also designed to be both medieval and military style. It is also designed to be a pvp pack, which is why this pack has 2 config options that adjusts the scale of the tools

Yes, this 3d tools pack will also expand to weapons from alot of addons, so feel free to suggest on what model i should add

How to make this pack work on Actions & Stuff:

1. Make sure you bought Actions & Stuff

2. Tap on Actions & Stuff

3. A setting icon should appear next to the "deactivate" button, tap on that

4. Slide down and enable "3D Tools" and "3D Arrow", then exit the A&S config

5. Apply the Tactical 3D Tools above Actions & Stuff

6. Go back to Menu
