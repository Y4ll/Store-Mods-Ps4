Mirai by Yuki

That Mirai is an unofficial compatibility fork of the original Mirai shader created by bambosan (sad).

This version updates the renderer and material pipeline for Minecraft Bedrock 26.45, with a focus on Android devices using MBLoader and Vibrant Visuals.

Version 2.19 will take a while to update, including bug fixes, Windows support, and resolution of some conflicts.

(Fixes to the Nether, The End, water, added rendering settings, and some other bugs that have been identified.
