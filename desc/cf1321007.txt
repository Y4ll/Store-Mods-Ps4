This simple texture pack removes the blurry overlay that appears when wearing a carved pumpkin.

It's perfect for players who want to wear pumpkins without the annoying visual obstruction.

Especially useful during Ender Dragon fights, where clear vision is crucial.

Fully compatible with Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21 and above.

Why Use This Pack?

Removes only the pumpkin blur nothing else is changed

Keeps your vision clear while wearing a carved pumpkin

Lightweight causes zero lag or performance drop

Works in survival, creative, and even Realms

Compatible with most other texture/resource packs
