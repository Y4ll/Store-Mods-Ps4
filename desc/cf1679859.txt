About

Vanilla Atmosphere gives Minecraft Bedrock a cinematic but recognizable look through the official Vibrant Visuals renderer.

It keeps the original texture style and focuses on the world around it: sunlight, moonlight, skies, water, shadows, and the color of local lights. Every environment is tuned to feel distinct without turning Minecraft into a different game.

Features

- Biome-aware atmosphere: temperate, warm, cold, desert, swamp, dark, Nether, and End environments receive purpose-made lighting, skies, and color grading

- Natural time of day: bright neutral daylight, warm golden sunrise and sunset, and cool moonlit nights

- Soft shadows: retains the smooth Vibrant Visuals shadow style with balanced indirect lighting

- Natural water: separate clear, warm, cold, swamp, and cave water treatments

- Waves and caustics: subtle animated water surfaces and moving underwater light in the Natural and Cinematic profiles

- Colored lights: warmer torches and lanterns, blue soul light, red redstone light, cool end rods, and distinct sea pickle and copper light colors

- Vanilla textures: does not replace the standard 1616 block and item artwork

- Three built-in profiles: Natural, Cinematic, and Performance

- Current biome coverage: includes Minecraft Bedrock 26.40 biomes, including Sulfur Caves

- Vibrant Visuals compatible: uses the official cross-platform PBR capability

Profiles

- Natural (Balanced, Default): balanced lighting, subtle atmosphere, readable nights, gentle waves, and soft caustics

- Cinematic (Darker Nights): stronger sunsets, deeper contrast, darker nights, ACES tone mapping, larger waves, and brighter caustics

- Performance (No Waves or Caustics): restrained color treatment with the pack's custom waves and caustics disabled

The Performance profile reduces the additional water effects supplied by this pack. Frame-rate results still depend on the device, resolution, world complexity, and Minecraft's own Vibrant Visuals quality settings.

How to use

- Import the .mcaddon file into Minecraft Bedrock.

- Activate the Vanilla Atmosphere resource pack for your world.

- Open the resource pack settings and choose Natural, Cinematic, or Performance.

- Enable Vibrant Visuals in Minecraft's Video settings.

- Put Vanilla Atmosphere above other packs that change biomes, lighting, skies, water, or color grading.

Notes

- The atmospheric effects require a device that supports Vibrant Visuals.

- The download is resource-pack-only and does not occupy a behavior-pack slot.

- On Simple or Fancy graphics, the world keeps its normal vanilla appearance.

- Resource packs that replace the same client biome or Vibrant Visuals files can conflict; the pack higher in the active resource list takes priority.

- This is not an RTX-only pack and does not require an NVIDIA RTX GPU.

- The Performance profile is a lighter visual preset, not a guaranteed FPS booster.

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.40 or newer

- A Vibrant Visuals-compatible device for the shader effects

- No external library or dependency required
