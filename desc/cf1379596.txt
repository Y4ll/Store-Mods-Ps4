Hoplite Weapons is a remake of SpeedSilver Weapons addon that adds the legendary weapons from the popular Java server hoplite.gg. This addon is perfect for multiplayer play, PvP and for SMP or just a simple way to spice up your survival world. Here are all the features from this addon

Compatible with Beyond's Java PvP Addon

Beyond's Java PvP introduces the weapon attack speed and combat system from Minecraft Java to Minecraft Bedrock in a clean and simple way. Adding this addon will give all the weapons in the addon Java style attack animation and attack speed.

Click Here To Download Beyond's Java PvP Addon

Make sure that Beyond's Java PvP addons bp and rp are bellow all the other addons. It will work otherwise but keeping it bellow is recommended.

Q: How do I know what a weapon does ?

A: When you hold the weapon in your main hand the weapon will show description then when you click on the weapon in your inventory you will be able to see what the weapon does.

Q: How do I obtain the weapons ?

A: Once you enter your world you will be give a item called Hoplite Codex. You can interact with that item to open a new ui where you can click on craft weapon then click on the weapon you want to craft. It will show you what items you need to craft the weapon. If you have all the required item and hit craft it will give you the weapon.

Additional Features:

- Custom Crosshair: Each weapon has it's own types of crosshair when you hold them, these are to resemble what that weapon does. Like if its a sword, throwable or interactable.

- Weapon Details: In your Hoplite Codex you can click on Weapon Details then the weapon you want to know about. Then you can see what the weapon does before you craft it.

- Admin Control: You can use the Admin Control tab in you Hoplite Codex to add or remove recipe also you can access some toggles like world announcement when weapon crafted or only once weapon craft. YOU NEED TO HAVE bey:hoplite_admin or bey:hoplite_owner TAG TO ACCESS THESE FEATURES
- If you or anyone else has the admin/owner tag it will show Admin/Owner next to their name.

- Chaos Mode: You can enable chaos mode through the Hoplite Codex while having the admin/owner tags. Once Chaos mode is activated all cooldown all weapons will be lowered by 80%.
