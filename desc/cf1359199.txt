Starting out:

Upon joining, you'll be given a guidebook that fully explains the addon and how it works, alongside a little note from yours truly. You can ignore the guidebook because this page explains the same concepts that it does.

Launch Pad:

Craft the launch bad with 3 iron ingots, 3 obsidian, and 3 copper blocks and place it on the ground.

!\[\](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/null/description\_1e5d35c0-009b-41da-bceb-b0d0b01267c9.png)

Space Suits:

Space suits are required on the moon or else you will die. Take any chestplate and helmet (minus leather + chainmail), and upgrade by surrounding them with their specific ore type in a crafting table. You only need a space chestplate and helmet, normal leggings and boots work just fine.

Rocket:

Crafted with a redstone block, 3 iron blocks, and 3 dispensers. Place it in the middle of your launch pad, then hop in and use the UI to go to the moon.

The Moon:

New dimension, iron and amethyst are abundant here, low gravity and low visibility. Collect moon rock and craft it into the new Moon Stone Bricks
