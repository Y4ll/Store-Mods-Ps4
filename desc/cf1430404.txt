Simply put, this addon provides fake players that can talk like real players.
They can join your world and leave.

Steve can give real items in 1.6 version. The items are random, but they are high quality. However, you must be within a radius of at least 6 blocks to receive them. (install bp and rp)

This add-on does not include player.json.
This add-on does not require experimental features.
