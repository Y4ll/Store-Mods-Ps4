Multiverse Verity

Multiverse Verity is an original addon that explores different versions and variants of Verity from alternate, non-canonical universes.

Inspired by the concept of That Mobs, this addon introduces alternate versions of Verity, each with its own characteristics, behavior, and identity within the multiverse.

The Multiverse of Verity

Prepare to encounter different variants of Verity from alternate realities. These versions are not part of Verity's official canon and are entirely original interpretations created for this addon.

Each variant represents a different possibility of what Verity could become in another universe.

Personal Virtual Helpers

When entering the world, the player receives a special item capable of spawning their own personal virtual helpers.

Use the item to summon your virtual companions and have them by your side while exploring and surviving the world.

Features

- A multiverse containing different Verity variants

- Alternate and non-canonical versions

- Personal virtual helper system

- Custom entities designed to accompany the player

- Different universes and interpretations of Verity

- A special item for summoning the helpers

- Original content created specifically for this addon

Disclaimer

Multiverse Verity is an original creation and does not represent the official canon or storyline of Verity.

All variants featured in this addon belong to alternate universes created specifically for this project.
