The Backrooms Dimensions 1.2 Update

A brand new level has been added, along with minor tweaks to Level 0. The levels are now inhabited by strange entities, and you can find Almond Water while exploring.

Backrooms Add-on:

How to Enter The Backrooms?

You can enter by suffocating inside any block, such as sand or gravel. For a more terrifying experience, it is recommended to enter without any equipment.

Entities:

Be careful, you might encounter strange creatures. The best solution is usually to run, but each entity requires a different approach:

Bacteria: If this entity spots you, run without stopping and try to lose it through the narrow hallways.

The Eye: If this creature manages to see you with its single eye, the best solution is to quickly hide in one of the rooms of Level 1 until it wanders off in a different direction.

Food:

If you run out of food, you can find Almond Water while roaming The Backrooms. This drink also grants you a temporary speed boost.

Transition:

To move from Level 0 to Level 1, keep moving forward until you find an opening in one of the walls. Entering it will instantly transport you to the next level.

How to Exit The Backrooms?

To escape, you must find an elevator in Level 1. Inside, you will find a Jukebox; activating it will send you straight back to the normal world. However, finding the Music Disc won't be an easy task!
