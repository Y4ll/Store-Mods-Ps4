OUTLINE SELECTION

Clear Bold Simple

About The Pack

Outline Selection improves Minecraft Bedrock's normal block selection outline by making it cleaner, stronger and easier to see.

This pack is useful while building, mining or working with detailed blocks where the default selection outline can sometimes be hard to notice.

Features

Bold White Selection Outline Makes targeted blocks easier to identify.
Improved Visibility Cleaner and stronger than the normal outline.
Vanilla Friendly Keeps the original Minecraft look simple and clean.
Better For Building Helps with accurate block placement and selection.
Better For Mining Clearly shows which block is currently targeted.
Lightweight No unnecessary scripts, entities or extra systems.
Easy To Use Import, activate and start playing.

Recommended For

Building Mining Precision Placement Survival Gameplay & Recording

Installation

1. Download and import the pack into Minecraft Bedrock.
2. Activate it from Global Resources or your world's Resource Packs.
3. Keep this pack above other packs that modify selection materials.
4. Enter your world and target any block to see the improved outline.

i Important Information

Minecraft Bedrock controls part of the selection outline rendering through the game engine. This pack improves the outline visibility as much as possible using resource-pack materials and shaders. The exact thickness may vary slightly depending on the device and rendering mode.
