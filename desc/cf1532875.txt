Bored of the same ol music discs? Want your favorite song in Minecraft? Well this texture pack is for you! This texture pack allows to import any song into Minecraft in a form of a music disc (replacing it). This video runs over the tutorial on how to set up the pack and how to make your own custom music disc. Don't worry. It's extremely simple. However, if you rather read out a tutorial, open the "READ ME IF CONFUSED.txt" in the .zip. It goes over everything.
