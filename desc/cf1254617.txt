This texture pack reduces the size of swords in Minecraft, making them shorter and more compact while retaining their original design and color schemes. The primary feature is a visual overhaul of all sword models, shrinking their blade length without altering hitboxes or attack mechanics. This change provides a cleaner, less obstructive appearance during gameplay, particularly in first-person view, where oversized swords often dominate the screen.

The smaller sword models improve visibility during combat, reducing visual clutter when swinging or blocking. Players who prefer a more minimalist aesthetic will appreciate the streamlined look, especially in PvP or fast-paced survival scenarios where screen space matters. Additionally, the pack maintains vanilla texture fidelity, ensuring compatibility with other visual mods or resource packs.

By minimizing sword size without affecting functionality, this pack enhances gameplay immersion, allowing players to focus on movement and precision rather than being distracted by bulky weapon models. If you want a less intrusive combat experience while keeping the vanilla art style, this texture pack offers a practical yet noticeable improvement.
