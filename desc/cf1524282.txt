Echoes of Multiplayer - Version V8 (Mega Update)

The most advanced AI Player simulation for Minecraft Bedrock just got even smarter!

Enchanted AI System (Autonomous Progression)

Smart Mining & Logging: AI players are no longer just wanderers. They will now autonomously scan for resources. If they find Ores (Diamond, Iron, Coal) or Wood, they will mine them using the correct tools.

Tool Intelligence: AI knows progression! They won't try to mine Diamonds with a Stone Pickaxe. They will gather Iron first to upgrade their gear.

Autonomous Crafting: When they have enough materials, AI will place a Crafting Table, craft better tools (Pickaxes, Axes, etc.), and then pack up their table to continue their journey.

Block Placing: AI can now place blocks for basic navigation or to manage their inventory space.

Advanced Food & Survival System

Real 24-Slot Inventory: Every AI now has a functioning 24-slot inventory. You can actually see the items they are carrying!

Auto-Pickup: AI will now automatically detect and pick up dropped food items within a 2.5-block radius.

Cooking Logic (Furnace): If an AI has raw food (Raw Beef, Chicken, etc.), they will look for a spot to place a furnace and cook it into a better meal.

Realistic Eating: AI now follows a 5-minute hunger interval with a 44% chance to eat from their inventory, making their survival feel organic.

Hunting: If they run out of food, they will actively hunt nearby animals to restock.

Improved Respawn & Identity

Skin Persistence: Fixed the issue where AI would change skins after dying. Now, they will always respawn with the exact same skin they had before.

Silent Respawn: Removed annoying "Joined/Back" chat notifications during respawns. Chat will now only show actual Joins and Leaves.

Respawn Limit: Each AI is limited to 2 respawns. After the 3rd death, they will leave the game permanently, adding stakes to their survival.

Global AI Management

Stable 20/20 Cap: Fixed the Global AI Cap logic. The system now proactively enforces a 20-player limit.

Oldest AI Eviction: When a new AI joins or a friend is resurrected, the oldest AI in the world will correctly receive a "Left the game" notification and be removed to make room.
