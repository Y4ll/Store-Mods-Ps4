Sand Drops OP Items

Turn ordinary Sand into valuable treasure with Sand Drops OP Items by FleetMc Studio! Instead of giving you only its normal drop, breaking Sand can now reward you with random Minecraft items ranging from useful survival supplies to powerful equipment and rare treasures.

Whether you're exploring deserts, beaches, or building your next survival base, every Sand block can surprise you with something valuable. Keep digging and discover what your next block has waiting for you!
