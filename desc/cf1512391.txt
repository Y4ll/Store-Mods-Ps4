With this add-on you can have plants to defend your garden from hostile mobs in both survival and creative modes.

HOW TO DOWNLOAD?

Go to files and download the BP and RP, import them to Minecraft and play.

GAMEPLAY:

HOW TO PLAY?

The first thing to do is craft the botanic table:

At this table you can craft the Add-on items and, most importantly, the Sunflower seed.

To obtain the plants you must craft the sprouts and feed them with suns, otherwise they will only be normal and will produce seeds

With these seedlings, you can grow all the plants by watering them with the watering can, depending on the amount of suns and the essence you add. Essences can be crafted and must be added to obtain plants of that specific family.
