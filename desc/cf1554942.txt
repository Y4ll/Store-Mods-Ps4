About

Clear Water FPS Booster makes Minecraft Bedrock water cleaner, clearer, and easier to play around on low-end devices.

It keeps the vanilla style, but tunes the effects that often make oceans, rivers, rain, and underwater views feel noisy.

Features

- Clearer water surface color

- Higher water surface transparency

- Longer underwater fog distance

- Neutral water tint across vanilla biomes, including Pale Garden

- Nearly invisible clean still and flowing water textures

- Calmer water splash, drip, wake, and bubble effects

- Cleaner flowing-water animation without the chunky vanilla noise

- Slower water, lava, fire, portal, kelp, sculk, lantern, and similar animated textures

- Reduced common particle clutter for busy vanilla effects

How to use

- Import the .mcaddon file into Minecraft Bedrock.

- Activate the Clear Water FPS Booster resource pack for your world. No behavior pack or experiments are required.

- Put the Clear Water resource pack above other visual packs if another pack also changes water, fog, particles, or animated textures.

Notes

- This is not an RTX pack.

- This is not a realistic water shader.

- Performance gains may vary depending on your device, world complexity, entity count, simulation load, and render distance.

- The pack is designed as a practical low-end visual optimization, not a cinematic graphics overhaul.

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.50 or newer

- No external dependency required
