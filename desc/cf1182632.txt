Return Potion does not use the player.json.

No experimental toggles are needed to use this add-on.

Available in Spanish and English!

Tambien disponible en Espanol!

Drinking this potion will instantly teleport you back to your spawn point, making it a lifesaver in dangerous situations or when you need a quick escape. Crafting it is straightforward, requiring only basic materials, making it an essential item for both adventurers exploring far from home and builders working on large-scale projects. Keep a few in your inventory to ensure you're always just one sip away from safety.

To make it you will need water bottles and an ender pearl.
