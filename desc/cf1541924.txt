KATANA OF THE VOID Update 2.0

3 new skills have been added to the Katana of the Void, bringing the total to 9 skills + 1 passive ability. All existing skills remain unchanged.

NEW: Void Prison Crouch 4s release crouch crouch again

Summons void chains that imprison all enemies within 6 blocks. Deals 10 damage and applies Slowness V, Weakness II, and Mining Fatigue V for 5 seconds. Each imprisoned enemy is surrounded by particle cage rings. Best used to lock down groups before following up with Void Slash or Domain of Void.

NEW: Void Collapse Hold 15s Right Click

Unleashes a massive void shockwave in a 12-block radius. Deals 25 damage to all nearby enemies, launches them outward with heavy knockback, and inflicts Levitation for 3 seconds. An expanding ring of particles radiates outward from the caster on activation. Previously, right-clicking while the void was charged was locked it now triggers this skill instead.

NEW: Soul Harvest Passive (triggers on kill)

When you kill any entity while holding the Katana of the Void, you instantly recover 3 hearts and gain Absorption I for 4 seconds. No input required the soul of every fallen enemy fuels your next fight.

SKILLS

Void Charge

Input: Hold the item for 15 seconds

Channel void energy for 15 uninterrupted seconds without crouching or jumping. Upon completion, the blade erupts in a spiral of violet particles granting Strength II, Speed II, and Haste II for 10 seconds.

Effects: Strength II Speed II Haste II 10s Duration

Shadow Stance

Input: Crouch for 4 seconds

Hold a crouching position for 4 seconds while gripping the katana. The blade dissolves into shadow granting Invisibility, Speed III, and Jump Boost II for 10 seconds. Void particles scatter from the body as you vanish.

Effects: Invisibility Speed III Jump Boost II 10s Duration

Void Ascension

Input: Hold 15s Jump (within 2 seconds)

After a full 15-second charge, press Jump to activate. The katana launches you skyward, then drives you down into a devastating void impact dealing 18 damage in an 8-block radius with knockback. A ring of void expands outward on landing.

Effects: 18 Damage 8-Block Radius Knockback Aerial Slam

Phantom Leap

Input: Crouch 4s Jump (within 2 seconds)

After crouching for 4 seconds, press Jump to blink through the void to the nearest entity within 25 blocks, appearing behind them with a sonic burst that deals 22 damage and inflicts Blindness for 3 seconds.

Effects: 22 Damage Instant Teleport Blindness 3s 25-Block Range

Domain of Void

Input: Hold 15s Crouch (within 2 seconds)

After a full 15-second charge, crouch to unleash a domain of pure void. Twelve expanding waves erupt outward each dealing 6 damage and inflicting Wither II and Slowness on every entity they touch. The caster gains Resistance III and Strength III throughout.

Effects:6 Damage 12 Waves Expanding AoE Wither II Slowness Resistance III Strength III
