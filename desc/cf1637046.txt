Offhand Freedom

Offhand Freedom adds functional Java-style offhand gameplay using normal vanilla items, quick controls, block placement, buckets, projectiles, and dynamic lighting.

Features

- Vanilla Items Only: No custom items or crafting recipes.

- Quick Hand Swapping: Double crouch to move an item between your main hand and offhand.

- Block Placement: Place many supported blocks directly from your offhand.

- Dynamic Lighting: Supported glowing items produce light while held.

- Bucket Support: Use water, lava, powder snow, fish buckets, and empty buckets.

- Farming Support: Plant crops while keeping a tool in your main hand.

- Projectile Support: Use eggs, snowballs, wind charges, ender pearls, XP bottles, and fireworks.

- Main-Hand Priority: Tools remain usable for mining, tilling, stripping logs, and other actions.

How to Use

- Hold a normal vanilla item in your main hand.

- Quickly crouch twice to move it into your offhand.

- Double crouch again to swap it back.

- Keep your main hand empty or hold a tool, then press the use button to use the offhand item.

- Sneak while interacting when you need to prioritize the offhand item.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file.

- Open it with Minecraft and wait for the import to finish.

- Create a new world or edit an existing world.

- Open Behavior Packs My Packs.

- Select Offhand Freedom and press Activate.

- Remove older test versions to prevent conflicts.

- Enter the world and double crouch while holding an item.

No custom items, textures, or crafting recipes are required.

Known Limitations

- Food cannot currently be eaten directly from the offhand.

- Some enchanted, named, durable, or data-containing items may have limited support.

- Some directional, technical, or two-block items may not place correctly.

- Functionality may vary between game versions, devices, and multiplayer servers.

This project is experimental and will continue receiving compatibility, stability, and item-support improvements.
