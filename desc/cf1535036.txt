LORE:

An unknown infection has broken out in your world. Scientists have named it Xenocordyceps Lunaris. It is a strange parasitic fungus that invades your body and takes control of it until you collapse. It's said that this parasite comes from the moon; the rest is up to you....

The addon is a progressive infection that is changing across the time. More infected will be added in the next updates.

There are some types of "INFECTED".

This Infected are slower but strong. They began to deteriorate over time; Wait 28 days untill he spawns.

This infected will try to make you explode. This infected mutated to become a living bomb. Its a variation to level 1 infected. This infected spawns 5/7 days later
