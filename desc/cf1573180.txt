True Shades Realism is a vanilla-faithful shader that transforms Minecraft's atmosphere through realistic lighting, immersive fog, dynamic reflections, and biome-based ambience while preserving the game's iconic visual style.

Designed for players who love Minecraft's original look but want a more cinematic and realistic experience, True Shades Realism enhances the world around you without changing the identity of the game itself.

Features

Realistic lighting and shadows

Biome-based fog for increased immersion

Biome-based color tinting

- Warm biomes feel warmer

- Cold biomes feel colder

- Each environment has its own atmosphere

Realistic water rendering

Realistic reflections

Colored block lighting

- Torches emit warm orange light

- Redstone torches emit bright red light

- End Rods emit soft cyan light

- Lava emits glowing orange light

- Crying Obsidian emits purple light

Enhanced cave ambience

Improved sky and environmental atmosphere

PBR support for compatible texture packs

Designed to remain faithful to vanilla Minecraft

True Shades Realism is intended for medium-end devices and above.

With optimized settings, many good low-end devices may still achieve playable performance, but this shader is more demanding than the original True Shades shader.

This shader requires a device capable of running Vibrant Visuals.

Please ensure your device supports Vibrant Visuals before downloading.
