Craters & Stuff brings somthing new into the terrain, adding craters, ponds, and other stuff. Craters & Stuff adds naturally-generated craters throughout your world, giving terrain a more rugged and adventurous feel. These craters can hold resources, hidden spots, or just unique scenery that makes exploring more interesting. Its a simple addon that makes your world look more alive and less flat.
