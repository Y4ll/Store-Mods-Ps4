STORY & PURPOSE

In the year 2047, the balance of power has shattered.The world is now ruled by three military super-blocs each convinced that their vision of peace is the only one worth fighting for.

The Western Tactical Alliance (WTA) dominates through cyber warfare, drone intelligence, and precision strikes. Their ideology: maintain order through control.

The Eastern Frontline Authority (EFA) believes in strength, unity, and discipline. Forged from the ashes of the eastern nations, they fight to rebuild the world through sheer force.

And then theres Global Security Directorate (GSD) the so-called peacekeepers. A neutral faction with an army of their own, enforcing stability by any means necessary.

Amid this fragile world, soldiers fight across dead cities, jungles reclaimed by nature, and deserts scorched by nuclear storms. There are no heroes left only factions chasing their version of peace.
