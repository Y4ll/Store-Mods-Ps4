SMP Utilities & Essentials is an all-in-one addon that combines the most important features every Minecraft Bedrock SMP needs into a single package. Instead of installing multiple separate addons, everything is brought together into one addon that's perfect for multiplayer survival worlds.

Featuring:

Lag Clear Automatically clears dropped items to help reduce lag and improve server performance.

Clans Create or join clans and play together with your friends.

Playtime Track Keep track of how long you've played on the server.

Teleports Set Homes, teleport to saved locations, and use Random Teleport (RTP) to explore the world.

Starter Kit Receive a full Iron starter kit, food, and supplies when you first join and every time you respawn.

Whether you're a server owner looking for essential utilities or a player wanting a smoother survival experience, SMP Utilities & Essentials has everything you need in one organized addon. Spend less time managing multiple packs and more time enjoying your SMP. Made by CyanXCodes.
