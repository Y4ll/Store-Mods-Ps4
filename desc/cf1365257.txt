A New Kind of Technical Adventure

Wans Redstone Caves is the first tech-focused addon for Minecraft: Bedrock Edition that seamlessly blends technology and exploration in a rich, atmospheric underground biome. Built for the CurseForge ModJam 2025, it reimagines Redstone as a core of both natural energy and machine-powered survival.

Explore the Redstone Biome

Delve deep into a brand-new Redstone-themed cave biome, a glowing underground world of crystalline ores, geometric structures, and synthetic ecosystems. Inspired by the visual design of Horizon Zero Dawn, the biome is populated by robotic creatures powered by the caves Redstone energy.

- New Magnetite and Hematite stone types

- Exclusive Redstone ores and crystals

- Massive cubic Magnetite formations and glowing stalactites

- Redstone vegetation and crystalline lava pools

- No vanilla lava, water, or stone its a whole new world

Meet the Machines

The Redstone biome is alive... literally. Mechanical wildlife roams the caves, sustained by Redstone energy nodes called Wild Network Cores.

- 6 new Hostile robotic mobs, bound to red cores

- Tame and convert them using Player Network Cores

- Robots can become pets, helpers, or farming companions

- Build your own custom robots with a clean, advanced design

- All machines require a connection to your Redstone energy network

Energy & Network System

The heart of Wans Redstone Caves is the custom Network system, a unique infrastructure for powering and automating machines. No cables, no hoppers required.

- Generators produce stable energy using a rare synthetic mineral: Bluestone

- Fixed-cost logic: machines use exact energy units (no ticks or timers)

- Network Cores automatically connect machines within range

- Create sub-networks for advanced storage routing and inventory control

Craft Advanced Machinery

Harness the power of the biome to craft machines in two tech tiers:

Tier 1 Made with Iron & Bluestone

Tier 2 Made with Magnetite & Pure Redstone

Each machine includes:

- Custom GUI for inventory filtering

- Network-linked storage access

- Full integration with your Redstone energy network
