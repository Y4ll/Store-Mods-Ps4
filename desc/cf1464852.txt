When you are in the middle of an intense PvP battle sharing potions, or strip mining and quickly trying to throw away blocks of dirt, this slow system is an absolute nightmare.

This highly optimized, context-aware script completely changes how you discard and manage items. By linking high-speed drop mechanics to a simple Feather, you can clear your entire inventory in seconds without ever opening a vanilla menu. Java-Style Quick Drop (Context-Aware)

I designed the script to be incredibly smart and context-aware. It actively changes its behavior based on where your character is looking.

If you want to instantly drop an item to a teammate or throw away a single piece of junk, simply hold a Feather, look straight ahead, Sneak (crouch), and Use the item (Tap the screen or Right-Click). The script will instantly eject one item from your inventory. There is absolutely zero delay. You can rapid-fire click to shoot out specific amounts of items. The dropped item will fly naturally in the direction you are looking with a smooth physics impulse, accompanied by a satisfying "Pop" sound effect and a clean Action Bar notification.

The Interactive Drop Panel & Bulk Management

What if you want to drop large quantities of items without spamming the screen? The addon features a beautiful custom Graphical User Interface (GUI) specifically designed for bulk inventory management.

To open the Drop Panel, simply hold a Feather, look directly down at the floor, and Sneak while Using the item. The script instantly scans all 36 slots of your inventory and displays a clean list of everything you are carrying, automatically formatting the technical item names to look neat and professional.

- Drop Half: Need to share food or torches with a friend? Select an item from the menu and hit "Drop Half" to instantly split your stack and toss exactly half of it on the ground.

- Drop All: Instantly eject a full stack of 64 items with a single click. A heavy "Break" sound effect will play to confirm the massive drop.

Quick Drop Mode (Turbo Clearing)

If you have just returned from a massive caving expedition, your inventory is probably filled with useless cobblestone, gravel, and rotten flesh. Clearing this out manually takes forever.

From the main Drop Panel, you can activate the brand new Quick Drop Mode. This is a fast-paced, turbocharged UI mode that completely bypasses all confirmation screens. Once activated, tapping any item on the list will instantly eject the entire stack from your inventory in real-time. You can clear out dozens of stacks of junk in a matter of seconds, saving you massive amounts of time.

Live Stats Tracking & Server Leaderboards

If you play on a multiplayer SMP Realm, managing resources is a massive part of the economy. The addon now features a silent background tracking system that monitors your inventory habits. It precisely counts exactly how many single items you have dropped, how many full stacks you have ejected, and calculates your total lifetime items dropped.
