A clean and simple PvP texture pack designed to improve visibility, reduce visual clutter, and provide a smoother combat experience for Bedwars, Skywars, and other PvP gameplay.

Features

- PvP-focused item and weapon textures

- Shorter, cleaner swords

- Simplified textures for easier visibility

- Cleaner visuals for combat

- Reduced visual clutter

- PvP-friendly projectile textures

- Retextured armor and items

- Great for Bedwars, Skywars, Survival PvP, and more

Perfect for players who want a cleaner PvP experience while keeping the classic Minecraft style.
