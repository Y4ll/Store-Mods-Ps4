What is AppleSkin - Bedrock Port?

It's a Bedrock Port of the Original AppleSkin on Java.

What it does?

Adds the Saturation Bar to the HUD, it shows the player Saturation Status.
And it also shows the how much Hunger/Sarutation you will restore from the holding food item:

It shows that for both Vanilla and Custom Food items!

Important Notes*

This addon does not convert the full java mod to bedrock due to Bedrock jsonUi Limitations, It's missing the Exhaustion Bar and the Restored Health.

And because this addon adds a new jsonUi element, we have to use titles in order to make it functional, So this addon is 100% incompatible with addons that use titles!

Curious how its coded?
