Heart Levels:

There are 5 upgradeable heart types, each stronger than the last:

Starter Heart Basic Heart Advanced Heart Prime Heart Superior Heart

Each new tier boosts your maximum health, making you tougher and more resistant to

damage.

How It Works:

- When you kill any entity, theres a chance to drop a Heart Shard.

- Collect 6 Heart Shards to craft a Starter Heart.

- Then, combine 9 Starter Hearts to craft a Basic Heart.

- Keep upgrading:

- 6 Basic Hearts 1 Advanced Heart

- 6 Advanced Hearts 1 Prime Heart

- 6 Prime Hearts 1 Superior Heart

- Each crafted heart gives you more total health, depending on its level

Heart Shard Ores Added found deep between Y -1 to -60
Very rare, but extremely useful for progression & crafting

Empty Heart :

Use to remove current heart, 6 coal required to craft this heart.

Become the Ultimate Survivor:

Keep collecting and upgrading to reach Superior Heart Level, granting the maximum possible health boost.
With this addon, every mob fight and boss battle becomes a chance to grow stronger
