Physics+

[FALLING TREES] [RAGDOLL CORPSES] [ITEM PHYSICS]

Physics+ adds physical behavior to Minecraft Bedrock without changing the basic Minecraft style. It includes falling trees, ragdoll corpses, and dropped items that react more naturally to the world.

Falling trees

Chop a supported tree with an axe and its connected logs, branches, roots, and natural leaves fall together. The addon supports oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, and pale oak trees.

The falling tree system makes woodcutting faster and gives each tree a physical collapse instead of removing every block separately.

Ragdoll corpses

When a supported mob dies, Physics+ leaves a matching corpse behind for a short time. This works with many vanilla mobs, including animals, villagers, hostile mobs, aquatic creatures, and larger mobs.

Corpses are removed automatically after some time, helping keep the world clean while still showing where mobs died.

Physical dropped items

Dropped items appear as physical objects with their normal Minecraft textures. They can settle on the ground, rotate into different positions, and react to the environment.

Items can move in water, react to lava and fire, and move away from hazards such as cacti. They can still be picked up normally.

Compatibility

Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.30 or newer.
