Project Description:

As your health fails and the world begins to fade, the small Pokemon effigy clutched in your palm erupts into a dazzling cascade of restorative energy, forcefully pulling you back from the brink of death and granting you an unexpected second chance at survival.

Important Information:

- This Project is 100% achievement compatible.

- No Experiments will need to be activated to play.

- No Player files are used in this project.

- Actual pokemon style totems.

- Choose your pokemon through subpacks.

- Have something to say? Join the Discord.

Use the slider in the resource settings to choose your favourite Pokemon.
