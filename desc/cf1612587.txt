Essential Utilities is an all-in-one utility addon that combines multiple quality-of-life features into a single package, eliminating the need to install them separately. It includes Dynamic Light, Tree Capitator, RPG Health Bar, Vein Mining, and many more useful features to enhance your Minecraft gameplay.

Dynamic Light allows light-emitting items, such as torches, to illuminate your surroundings while held in your hand. Explore caves and dark areas without placing torches everywhere.

Tree Capitator lets you chop down an entire tree by breaking a single log, making wood collection much faster and more convenient.

RPG Health Bar displays the health of nearby mobs and other entities above their heads, making it easier to monitor enemies during combat.

Vein Mining allows you to mine an entire vein of connected ores by breaking just one ore block, greatly speeding up resource gathering.
