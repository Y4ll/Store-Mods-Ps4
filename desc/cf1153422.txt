Felines Kingdom Natural & Fascinating Feline Behavior

This addon brings wildlife to Minecraft in a unique and immersive way, introducing felines with realistic behaviors, instincts, personalities, and social dynamics.
Each feline feels alive reacting to the environment, interacting with players, and forming bonds that make your world feel richer and more dynamic.

Main Features

1 Realistic Growth & Feeding

Felines begin life as tiny babies and grow naturally as they eat.
They consume chicken, beef, fish, lamb, and other meats to mature and gain strength.
This organic progression gives them a true sense of life and evolution.

2 Hunting Instincts & Natural Defenses

These felines are agile hunters capable of catching rabbits, chickens, pigs, and cows.
Their protective instinct activates when their owner is threatened becoming fierce defenders.
Brave companions ready to guard you on any adventure!

3 Deep Player Interaction

Feed them their favorite foods to tame them and build loyalty.
They follow you, stay close, and even grab food with their mouths to eat directly.
Their behaviors feel expressive, natural, and full of personality.

4 Eating Directly From the Ground

Some foods can be eaten straight from the ground.
Felines crouch down and consume them naturally, adding a wild and authentic touch.

5 Scratching & Finding Random Items

One of their most charming behaviors: scratching the ground to uncover random items.
They may find minerals, ingredients, or rare treasures adding exploration value and surprises.

6 Social Behavior & Reproduction

Felines can reproduce, forming families that grow and adapt to the environment.
Watching parents care for their babies creates tender and emotional moments.

7 Environmental Sensitivity

They react intelligently to weather and time:

Seek shelter during storms

Become more active at dawn and dusk

Look for warm places on cold nights

Know when to explore or relax
