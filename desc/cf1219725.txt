GUI Overhaul
Transform Minecrafts default interfaces into a vibrant and immersive experience.
GUI Overhaul replaces many of the standard block menus with beautifully redesigned interfaces inspired by their respective blocks.

From the Crafting Table to the Enchanting Table and Furnace, each interface has been carefully redesigned to feel more alive and visually engaging.
Some menus even include subtle animations, adding movement and personality to the games UI.

Choose between Dark Mode and Light Mode to match your preferred style and enjoy a cleaner, more colorful Minecraft interface every time you open a menu.
