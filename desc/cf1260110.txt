-- Introducing Solace V --

Solace is a Vibrant Visuals pack made to transform your Minecraft experience with multiple lighting options, and a stunning stylized aesthetic.

-- What to Expect in Solace --

Beautiful Visuals: Fine-tuned configs for atmospherics, lighting, fogs, color-grading, and water for an unmatched visual experience.

Three Distinct Presets: Whether youre feeling cozy, warm lighting, Gloomy, eerie lighting, or a natural, realistic experience, Solace has it.

Per-Dimension Lighting: Customized lighting, fog, and color grading tailored to each Dimension.
High-Resolution Details: Beautiful 128x normal maps, making every block satisfying to look at.

Hand-Crafted Texture AO: 128x Texture AO allows for unparalleled depth even in shaded areas.

Photorealistic Caustics: Lifelike water caustics rendered with path tracing through a simulated ocean in Blender.
