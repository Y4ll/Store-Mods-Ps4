Wild Hoof is a new add-on that I hope will become popular in the Minecraft community. Please subscribe to @slinguie_guy to support the add-on. With this add-on, you can have decorations in your stables and barns, such as cones, fences, feeders, and horses. So far, the add-on only has 10 horse coat colors; 2 more will be added with each update. Of course, we can't forget about horse breeds, of which we currently only have one, but more are planned for the future. We will try to fix all the bugs in the add-on. The innovations in this add-on are new realistic horses and new training items and feeders. The add-on will be constantly updated. Check out the add-on review.

It has pen gates with 4 colors. Corral trapdoor. Black and white corral fence. Polar wood. Red barn wood. Yellow grain wood. Polar fence. Rope fence. Cones. Feeder. Common horse feed. Horse. 10 coats. And more.
