SYC's Auto Totem (Achievements Friendly)

Auto Totem Offhand automatically equips a Totem of Undying in your offhand whenever one is available in your inventory. No commands, no settingsjust pure survival support.

This mod is achievement friendly and does not disable achievements. Perfect for survival, PvP, boss fights, and hardcore worlds.
