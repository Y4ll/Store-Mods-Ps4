DC LEGENDS

An independent project that imagines what "DC" characters could be like inside Minecraft Bedrock. Imagine being able to make Wars, Fights, Worlds with the characters you like the most in your mundo.

An addon focused on DC Comics characters for Minecraft Bedrock, focusing on survival gameplay with some new combat mechanics, new miners, structures, entities and even bosses.

Credits for the main skins of the project: DGteam - TGHeroes - Heropack from Fisk Superheros
