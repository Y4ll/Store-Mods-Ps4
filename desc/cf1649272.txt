Cockroach

Eye of Horus

Golden Clock

Your attacks add slowness

Golden Feather

Your fall damage is reduced by 50%.

Golden Lotus Chalice

Your potions last 50% longer.

Mirror of Ra

Returns 50% of the damage received

Moon Amulet

You get the night vision effect at night

What to do if the addon doesn't work?

-When creating the world, make sure this option is activated.

-Delete any previous versions of the addon and keep only the latest one.

-Stay on the latest official non-beta, non-preview versions of the game.

-If the addon still doesn't work for you, join my discord server so I can help you with your problem.

Note:

Don't forget to activate the beta-api from experimental options.
