English

Weapons can now be Upgraded! The new feature that allows you to upgrade your basic weapons to legendary status is now available.

You will be able to upgrade several of the armors and swords, you will have an improvement table, improvement items and they can be improved up to level +5, currently more armors and swords are still being implemented

Although this addon is not complete, you can try it out now and see how your sword changes completely as it levels up.

Discover this new add-on for Minecraft Pocket Edition (version 1.21+), which introduces a unique weapon evolution system. Its transformation is spectacular:

It starts at level 1 as a basic sword.

As it levels up, it changes visually, adding special effects and a more imposing appearance.

When it reaches legendary level, the sword displays a glowing design, particles, and silkscreen that highlight its power.

The video shows step-by-step how the sword evolves from its basic form to legendary, with smooth transitions and a demonstration of the effects in action. A link to the add-on is also included so you can download and try it out.

Descubre este nuevo complemento para Minecraft Pocket Edition (version 1.21+), que introduce un sistema unico de evolucion de armas. Su transformacion es espectacular:

Comienza en el nivel 1 como una espada basica.

A medida que sube de nivel, cambia visualmente, anadiendo efectos especiales y una apariencia mas imponente.

Al alcanzar el nivel legendario, la espada muestra un diseno brillante, particulas y serigrafia que resaltan su poder.

El video muestra paso a paso como la espada evoluciona desde su forma basica hasta su version legendaria, con transiciones fluidas y una demostracion de los efectos en accion. Tambien se incluye un enlace al complemento para que puedas descargarlo y probarlo.
