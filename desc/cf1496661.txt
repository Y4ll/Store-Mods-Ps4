Welcome to the Playtime Co. toy factory. This add-on includes the first two episodes of Poppy Playtime, and more are coming soon. Enjoy this add-on!

[This add-on does not require experimental modes]

The addon was made by GamerZkot and MidoriyaMC

-----MOBS-----

Kissy and Poppy are allies. Poppy can ride the player; just get close to her, and Poppy can ride Kissy. Don't provoke Kissy, or she'll kill you

Pj puga pillar will follow you like in the original game; if you can't move, if the lights go out, or else he will kill you

The mini huggy will approach the player and stalk you, but you can scare them away with grab packs by shooting them; just don't let them catch you

Bunzo Bunny is a mini-game where the bunny approaches the player by descending with a rope; you can tie it up with a chicken or with ropes using blocks

Mommy Long Legs is the main villain of Chapter 2. Don't let her catch you! She can also use her hand to stretch it out and reach the player. She can also jump and crush you

-----GRABPACK AND DOORS-----

The green hand is held with the object, and you can also use the charged green hand through energies; you can also open doors with the green hand charged with energies

The power sources and scanners work individually; however, you can connect them to make them function properly. You'll need the wrench to connect them, just like in the original game. It's quick and easy
