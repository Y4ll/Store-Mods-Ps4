Kaleidoscope Immersive Eating (Unoffical)

Requires Minecraft Version 26.3+

This add-on is an unofficial Bedrock port of Kaleidoscope: Immersive Eating by MemoryToame, originally developed for Minecraft Java Edition.

Ported to Bedrock under the terms of the GNU General Public License version 3 (GPLv3), which permits use, modification, and redistribution of the work, provided that derivative works are also licensed under GPLv3 and that source code (or a means of obtaining it) is made available to recipients. The original source is available at github.com/MemoryToame/ImmersiveEating.

Special thanks to MemoryToame and the Kaleidoscope: Immersive Eating team for creating the original mod!

Overview

Immersive Eating brings brand-new eating animations and an immersive experience to Kaleidoscope Cookery, giving the otherwise simple eating process richer visual presentation.
