Villagers Name

Villagers Name is a lightweight Minecraft Bedrock behavior pack that automatically assigns villagers random names inspired by cultures from all around the world.
A simple, cosmetic enhancement that makes your villages feel alive, diverse, and full of personality.

Features

Diverse Name Pool: Over 300 unique names from English, Spanish, Arabic, French, German, Italian, Chinese, Japanese, Russian, Indian, Portuguese, Korean, Turkish, Greek, Swedish, Dutch, Polish, Czech, Hungarian, Finnish, Danish, Norwegian, and Icelandic cultures.

Automatic Naming: Villagers receive a random name tag instantly upon spawning.

NonIntrusive: Zero performance impact purely cosmetic.

Tagged Entities: Named villagers receive a special tag for easy identification.

Installation

1 Download the behavior pack files.

2 Place the BP Villagers Name folder inside:

com.mojang/minecraftWorlds/[world_name]/behavior_packs/

3 Enable the pack in your world settings.

4 Enjoy your newly named villagers!

Compatibility

Compatible with Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80+.
Works alongside all resource packs, addons, and world types.
