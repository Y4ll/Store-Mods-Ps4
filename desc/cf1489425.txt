What is this?

So I made this for JJK fans. Gojo Satoru spawns naturally in the world as a proper custom mob custom model, custom textures, two skin states (normal and Infinity active), and his own sound effects for each ability. Blue, Red, Hollow Purple and Domain Expansion all have their own audio. It actually sounds like him.

He fights on his own and gets more dangerous as his health drops. Phase 1 he is already hitting for 15 damage and using Lapse Blue to pull mobs in. Drop him to 60% health and Phase 2 kicks in faster, stronger, starts using Reversal Red too. Get him below 25% health and Phase 3 activates. That is when Hollow Purple and Domain Expansion start happening. 500 HP boss, 90% knockback resistance. Not easy to kill.

You can also tame him. Sneak and use a Nether Star near him 15% chance he agrees to follow you. Once tamed he follows you around, teleports to you if you go too far, and fights mobs that are targeting you. Domain Expansion is a once-per-entity ability so it only fires once per Gojo wild or tamed. Drops diamonds, enchanted books and netherite scrap when killed.

Abilities

Lapse: Blue

Phase 1+

Gravitational pull sucks all mobs within 16 blocks toward Gojo and applies slowness. Plays the Blue ability sound. Cooldown 12 seconds.

Reversal: Red

Phase 2+

Repulsion blast launches all mobs within 18 blocks outward with heavy knockback and deals 25 damage each. Explosion particles. Cooldown 15 seconds.

Hollow Purple

Phase 3 only

Imaginary technique 150 damage to all mobs within 25 blocks plus Wither IV and Weakness III. Multi-wave explosion particles. Cooldown 25 seconds.

Domain Expansion

Once per Gojo

Unlimited Void 35 block radius, max slowness and blindness on all mobs, then two waves of massive damage (100 + 200). Gojo gets Strength V, Speed III and Regeneration IV after. Ring particle animation. Server-wide announcement.
