NURSERY FURNITURES :

Baby crib, changing table, bath tub, toy box, stroller, twin holder, wardrobe, high chair

TAMING :

The babies can be tame with a baby bottle! You can also feed them various types of food.

Toddler boy can be tame with regular milk!

Toddler girl can be tame with strawberry milk!

Grandpa and Grandma can be tame with a pumpkin pie!

Husband and Wife can be tame with a ring!

BABY CLOTHING:

use Minecraft dye to change the baby's clothes! Long press on the baby to let the magic happen!

Works well with Minecraft version 1.21.80 ++
