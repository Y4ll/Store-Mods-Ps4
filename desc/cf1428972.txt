| Smaller tools |

this is a texture pack that makes some tools and items smaller to cover less of the screen. It also contains slight re-textures and is compatible with vibrant visuals.

- Can be overlapped with other packs

- Smaller sheild (lowered sheild) also added

here are the tools that have been made small:

- All sword variants

- All axe variants

- All pickaxe variants

- All shovel variants

- All hoe variants

- All spear variants

- Bow

- Cross-Bow

- Totem of undying

- Mace

- Trident

- Fishing rod

- Flint and steel

- All potion bottles slightly smaller (Optional download)

- Also has slight charge animations for bow and crossbow

If you just want the smaller tools without the retexture then download "Smaller tools OG"
