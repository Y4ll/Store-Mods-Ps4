Cute Hello Kitty Hotbar

Give your Minecraft interface a soft and adorable makeover with Cute Hello Kitty Hotbar.

This resource pack changes your hotbar into a cute pink-themed design inspired by Hello Kitty, making your HUD feel sweeter, cleaner, and more playful while you explore, build, or play survival.

The charming style fits perfectly with cozy worlds, pastel builds, cute texture packs, and players who want something more colorful than the default Minecraft interface.

Add a little extra cuteness to every adventure with a hotbar that stands out without feeling too overwhelming.
