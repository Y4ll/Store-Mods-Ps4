GRTC or Gibonz Railway Train Controller is a train controller for Minecraft Bedrock.

Basic Information

GRTC Basic Specifications

- Forward / Backward / Emergency Brake

Basic controller to run your train.

- Door Open Close / Door Open Close Left / Door Open Close Right

Basic controller to open and close the train door.

- Stats / Reset / Horn

Basic controller to see your speed stats train, or reset the GRTC whole, and train Horn.

- Teleport / Direction / Kill

Basic controller to teleport your trainset incase derailed or just to move the train and use the direction to which way your train going to go X -X Y -Y by standing on front of your train, and if you want to delete the trainset use kill.

- Key / Chair / Boggie

Basic controller to control the trainset in this case A key, it will controller the A Trainset don't forget to put in your hot bar inventory to control it, to put Chair spread it in Rails and let the train pass though the chair now you train can seat anywhere you want, set your bogie at very back end trainset.
