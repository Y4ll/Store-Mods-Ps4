The WWII Gun Addon by TWW team brings a wide variety of historical weapons from different countries in World War II into Minecraft. Each weapon is based on real-life firearms, giving players a more realistic and immersive combat experience. The addon includes guns from major nations such as the United States, Germany, the Soviet Union, Japan, and others.

United States:

Bazooka a portable rocket launcher used against tanks.

M3A1 Grease Gun a simple submachine gun with a slow fire rate.

M1 Garand a powerful semi-automatic rifle used by infantry.

M1A1 Thompson a fast-firing submachine gun for close combat.

AN/M2 Stinger a modified aircraft machine gun with a very high fire rate.

Colt M1911 a standard semi-automatic pistol.

Springfield M1903 a bolt-action rifle, often used by snipers.

BAR an automatic rifle providing suppressive fire.

Model 1912 Shotgun effective at close range.

United Kingdom:

Lewis Gun a light machine gun with a top-mounted magazine.

France:

MAS-35S a semi-automatic pistol used by French forces.

Germany:

MP28 an early submachine gun.

MP40 a compact and widely used SMG.

Kar98k the standard German bolt-action rifle.

Gewehr 43 a semi-automatic rifle.

MG34 a versatile machine gun.

MG42 a high fire rate machine gun.

PzB 39 an anti-tank rifle.

M712 (C96 variant) a fully automatic pistol.

Walther P38 a service pistol.

Luger P08 a well-known German pistol.

FG42 an automatic rifle for paratroopers.

Italy:

MAB 38 a reliable and accurate submachine gun.

Japan:

Type 99 Rifle a bolt-action rifle.

Type 96 MG a light machine gun.

Type 11 MG an older hopper-fed machine gun.

Type 100 SMG a submachine gun.

Type 2A SMG a simplified late-war SMG.

Lunge Mine a close-range anti-tank explosive.

Soviet Union (USSR):

PPS-43 a compact SMG.

PPSh-41 a high fire rate SMG with drum magazine.

PPD-40 an earlier SMG design.

DP-28 a light machine gun with a pan magazine.

TT-33 a semi-automatic pistol.

Mosin-Nagant a durable bolt-action rifle.

SVT-38 a semi-automatic rifle.

Overall, this addon offers a diverse and realistic collection of WWII weapons, allowing players to experience different combat styles from various nations.
