Weather Immersion is a Minecraft Bedrock addon that completely transforms how weather looks and feels in your world. Rain becomes a layered, wind-driven downpour with surface splashes. Snow biomes erupt into dense blizzards with howling wind streaks. Deserts fill with thick, gritty sandstorms. Everything is automatic, just install and play.

Immersive Rain

- Falling rain streaks that react to wind direction

- Drizzle sheets filling the air with volume during storms

- Water surface ripples, crown splashes, and expanding impact waves

- Solid surface drip effects on grass, stone, and dirt

- Thunderstorm fog with reduced visibility

- Atmospheric mist layer during heavy storms

- Rain automatically skips deserts and frozen biomes

Blizzard

- Dense wind-driven snowfall with crystal snowflake particles

- Horizontal wind streaks for a howling gale effect

- Ground swirl snow kicked up from the surface

- Thick whiteout mist haze

- Blizzard fog that cuts visibility dramatically

- Activates only in frozen/snowy biomes during thunderstorms

Sandstorm

- Thick sand cloud with angular, gritty grain particles

- Fast-moving sand sweep sheets for dramatic wind gusts

- Ground dust kicked up from the desert floor

- Warm sandy brown fog reducing visibility

- Activates in desert and mesa/badlands biomes during any weather event

- Thunder intensifies the effect with denser particles

How It Works

Every effect is fully automatic. The addon detects your biome by scanning ground blocks around you:

Standing on snow/ice? You get blizzards. Standing on sand/terracotta? You get sandstorms. Everywhere else? You get immersive rain.

When you go underground, enter a building, or go underwater, all weather effects and fog automatically stop. When weather clears, everything cleans up instantly.

-Important Note

This pack will not appear in the main Settings Packs menu. To activate it, edit or create a world, go to the Behavior Packs section, and enable the addon there.

-How To Use

Install the .mcaddon file

Edit or create a world

Go to Behavior Packs and enable "Weather Immersion"

Weather effects activate automatically no commands, no settings, just play!

-Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.0+

- Works in Survival and Creative

- Multiplayer compatible

- Compatible with other addons (no vanilla file overrides except particle materials)
