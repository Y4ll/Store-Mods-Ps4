Dive into the infinite hallways of the Backrooms with this highly detailed Minecraft skin pack! This mod delivers over 30 custom Backrooms-themed skins with many more coming soon Every single skin features highly decorated textures and amazing craftsmanship to bring the eerie, nostalgic atmosphere of the Backrooms straight into your game.

Features

30+ Unique Skins: Choose from a variety of distinct Backrooms-inspired designs.

Expert Craftsmanship: Enjoy highly detailed, high-quality textures.

Future Updates: More custom skins are already on the way!
