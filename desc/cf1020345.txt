The Clear Water Texture Pack is a simple but amazing pack that makes the water in Minecraft look clear, clean and beautiful. You can see through the water as if it was glass, and admire the underwater scenery. You can easily spot structures underwater, such as shipwrecks, ruins and temples, and explore them without any hassle. You can also see the fish, coral and other marine life in all their colorful details, and interact with them.

Clear Water For Simple & Fancy Mode

The Clear water texture pack now has 3 amazing and beautiful water texture so you can decide the water texture of your on world. you can toggle between these textures through subpacks settings. The three water textures are

Clear and Clean water- Blue water (more like the default one) But complety clear from underwater as well as from outside.

Emerald Green Water- Green-blue water, gives shader vibes and looks aesthetic. This one is clear too.

Blue Water- A better shade of blue, Most clean water and looks like the RTX water (that trend which was popular back then). This one is most clear.

Clear Water For Vibrant Visuals Mode

The vibrant visuals water texture pack improves the clarity of the water. Water will look more clear from outside. Not just that, it has realistic waves. And the best part is that, You can choose use this pack either solely or pair it with any shader you want. It just works. But make sure, if you're using it with a shader then place this pack above it. Also the vibrant visual option should be enabled in settings for this to work.
