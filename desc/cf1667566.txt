Lynx More Weapons

Expand your arsenal with 7 new weapon types across 7 material tiers, totaling 50 new items built for balanced, vanilla-friendly combat.

Weapons

Katana

Scimitar

Greatsword

Greataxe

Glaive

Halberd

Battlestaff

Each is craftable in Wood, Golden, Stone, Copper, Iron, Diamond, and Netherite tiers, with damage and durability tuned so every tier feels like a genuine upgrade.

Crafting

Wood through Diamond tiers craft at a standard Crafting Table.

Netherite tiers upgrade at a Smithing Table, just like vanilla gear.

All recipes auto-unlock in the Recipe Book once you hold the required materials.

Requirements

Minimum Bedrock version: 1.21.60 or higher

No experimental toggles required

Installation

Download the .mcaddon file and open it with Minecraft.

Enable both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack in your world settings.

Load in and start crafting.

Compatibility

Uses the lynx: namespace safe to use alongside other add-ons. Works in Survival and Creative.

Bug reports and suggestions are welcome in the comments.
