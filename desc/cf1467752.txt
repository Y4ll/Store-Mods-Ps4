Plushie Addon (Unofficial) Complete Guide

About This Addon

The Plushie Addon brings a collection of cute, decorative plushie blocks to Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Perfect for decorating your bedroom, living room, or any space that needs a touch of warmth and cuteness!

Features

- 90+ Different Plushies - Including allay, armadillo, axolotl, and many more!

- Craftable Recipes - Each plushie has its own crafting recipe

- Beautiful Textures - High-quality, detailed textures for each plushie

- Decorative Blocks - Perfect for interior decoration and building projects

- Custom Models - Unique 3D models for each plushie type

How to Use

1. Craft Plushies - Use the crafting table to create your favorite plushies

2. Place & Decorate - Place plushies anywhere in your world as decorative blocks

3. Collect Them All - Try to collect every type of plushie available!

Compatibility

- Minecraft Version: 1.21.60+ (Bedrock Edition)

- Required: No additional mods or addons needed

- Multiplayer: Works in both single-player and multiplayer worlds

- Realms: Compatible with Minecraft Realms

Installation

1. Download the addon files

2. Import both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack

3. Enable both packs in your world settings

4. Start crafting and decorating!
