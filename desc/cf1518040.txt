Have you always wanted to create a beautiful atmosphere in the game?

Then this modification is for you! It is very easy to use: just write a simple command /sit and you will be able to sit down.

This modification is suitable not only for server admins but also for playing with friends.

The mod doesn't just let you sit; it also reacts to changes. If you break the block under you, the player will stand up.

You can also stand up by pressing the Sneak button (Shift).

The modification is very lightweight and will not load your device.

Attention: To use this mod, please enable Beta API in your settings.
