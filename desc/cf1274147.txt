- Settings:

Hold any wand in your hand and use it with the right button (or hold on the screen), make sure there are no blocks in front of you.

This will open a simple settings screen, where you can set wand's range and if it will get blocks on diagonal.

- Preview & Place:

Left button or hit the block to see a preview of where the blocks will be placed. It's based on the blocks in your inventory and wand durability.

Right button or click on the block to placed all blocks in your inventory.

Tip:

> Unbreaking enchantment helps with durability.
