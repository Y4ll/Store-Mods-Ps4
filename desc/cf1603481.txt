Newb Nightfall

A fantasy-inspired shader pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition featuring immersive skies, atmospheric lighting, enhanced clouds, and beautiful nighttime visuals.

Features

- Beautiful night sky

- Enhanced clouds

- Improved sunrise and sunset colors

- Fantasy-inspired lighting

- Atmospheric visuals

- Optimized for Minecraft Bedrock (RenderDragon)

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition

- Compatible patch / MB Loader required for RenderDragon shaders.
