Goku Ultra Instinct grants the player some of Goku's powerful abilities from the Dragon Ball anime.

When a mob attacks you, Ultra Instinct can automatically activate, allowing you to instantly teleport away from the attack.

You can also counterattack with an extremely powerful critical hit, dealing devastating damage to the mob that attacked you.

If you use this ability in PvP, Ultra Instinct can be very tricky to deal with. Why? Find out for yourself :)

I haven't tested it in multiplayer yet, so I'm not sure how it will behave or how the ability will apply to other players. But in single-player, everything works great so far.
