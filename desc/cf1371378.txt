About the Map

If youre the kind of player who loves a real Skyblock challenge, this map is for you. I hunted for the perfect seed (3838058575941151168) and set the world spawn exactly at -400 65 -400 to kick off the adventure.

Theres just one island in this map and no hidden starter packs. You start with the basics and thats it. The Nether and End both have regular terrain, so you can actually progress and explore when youre ready.

The best part? All the major biomes are pretty close by within 900 blocks from spawn. That means youll have plenty of room to expand, gather new resources, and build whatever wild designs you dream up.

So, if you want classic Skyblock but with easy access to biomes and a pure survival vibe, give this map a try.

Good luck and dont fall.
