Immersive Melodies

Immersive Melodies is a premium Bedrock Edition addon that brings interactive musical instruments and a vast library of legendary melodies to your Minecraft world.

Choose from 11 custom-modeled instruments, select one of the 34 pre-loaded songs, and start performing!
Dynamic musical note particles follow your player as you play, and full multiplayer compatibility lets you form your own medieval or modern band with friends.

Inspired by Immersive Melodies from Minecraft Java.

Watch the Showcase Trailer

Features

Authentic Audio Synthesis: Each instrument uses highfidelity, customsynthesized tones that capture its true character.

Curated Song Library: 34 iconic tracks sorted by popularity rock anthems, movie scores, video game themes, folk tunes, and community memes.

Loop & Trim Duration System: All tracks play for at least 30 seconds (looping short songs) and fade out smoothly at 65 seconds to avoid repetition.

Dynamic Visual Effects: Colorful, sizevaried musical note particles drift into the air and follow your character while performing.

Multiplayer Harmonization: Each instrument plays a distinct audio track, allowing multiple players to perform the same song as a rich ensemble.

How to Use

1 Equip your Instrument: Hold any instrument in your main hand.

2 Open the Song Selector: Sneak + Use (RightClick / tapandhold) to open the interactive menu.

3 Select a Song: Choose any of the 34 tracks.

4 Play the Music: Hold Use to perform. Note particles will follow you.

5 Stop the Song: Release Use or swap items.
