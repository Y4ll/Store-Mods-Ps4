GLOWING ITEM PHYSICS

Glowing Item Physics turns every item you drop into something you want to look at. Items tumble through the air, land, wobble for a moment and settle flat on the ground, while every pixel of every block, item and mob is traced by a thin, glowing white-yellow outline. It is a complete Bedrock add-on with realistic drop physics and a unique glowing pixel-grid look, made by Baku_Studio.

WHAT YOU GET

- Dropped items that tumble while falling, land, wobble and lie flat, each in its own random direction
- A thin glowing outline around every pixel of every block, item, mob and piece of armor
- A soft glowing contour around each dropped item, so loot is easy to spot
- Block colours that always stay clear and true, with outlines that fade away at a distance
- More than 3,800 textures at 256x and about 560 items and blocks with drop physics

REALISTIC ITEM PHYSICS

When an item is dropped, thrown or released by a broken block, it is replaced by a custom physics item. It keeps the speed it was thrown with, spins as it falls, and touches down with a small settle wobble before lying still. Tools, food, ingots, gems, armor, discs, boats and many more use their own texture, while common solid blocks such as stone, dirt, sand, ores, wool, concrete, planks and metal blocks fall as small tumbling cubes. Items are collected automatically when you walk close, with a gentle pull towards you and a pickup sound, and matching items that land next to each other merge into one stack. They disappear after five minutes, like normal drops.

SMART AND SAFE BEHAVIOUR

If an item lands in water or on top of a hopper, it turns back into a normal item, so your farms, item streams and sorting systems keep working. Lava and fire destroy physics items exactly like normal drops. Anything the add-on is not sure about, such as enchanted or renamed items, potions, maps, dyed leather, logs and grass, simply drops as a normal item and still receives the glowing outlines. Crops and gold items also stay normal, so villager farming and piglin bartering are not affected.

GLOWING PIXEL OUTLINES

Every pixel is its own small cell with a hairline border in warm white with only a hint of yellow. The line is brightest along the top and left edge of each cell and softer along the bottom and right, with a gentle glow that bleeds inward. The inside of each cell keeps its original colour, so stone is still stone, grass is still green and diamond ore still shows its cyan gems.

OUTLINES THAT FADE WITH DISTANCE

Up close you see the full pixel grid. As you move away, the outlines thin out and blend back into the block colour, so distant hills, forests and buildings look clean and natural instead of noisy.

WHAT IS INCLUDED

- Resources: outline textures for over 1,400 blocks, around 980 items, mobs and armor, plus a model and textures for the physics items
- Effect textures such as sky, clouds, particles, enchantment glint, armor trims and biome colours are left untouched, so nothing breaks
- Behavior: a custom item entity and a script for drops, pickup, merging and safety checks

HOW TO INSTALL

1. Remove any older Glowing Outline packs and any other item physics add-on. Two physics add-ons cannot run together.
2. Download the .mcaddon file and open it with Minecraft. Wait for the "Import successful" message.
3. Create or edit a world, open Resource Packs and activate "Glowing Item Physics [Resources]".
4. Open Behavior Packs and activate "Glowing Item Physics [Behavior]".
5. Enter your world and drop something.

REQUIREMENTS

Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.30 or newer on Android, iOS or Windows. The add-on uses the stable Script API, so no Beta APIs experiment should be needed on current versions. The download is about 25 MB. A mid-range device is recommended because the textures are 256x.
