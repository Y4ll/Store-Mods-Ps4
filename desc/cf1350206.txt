How to use:

Save

1.Get the Save Structure item under the equipment category, or do /give @s structurestick:save_structure

2. Get an anvil

3.Rename the item to the name you want to give the structure

4.click the 1st corner MUST BE A BLOCK

5.click the 2nd corner MUST BE A BLOCK

Load

1.Get the Load Structure item under the equipment category, or do /give @s structurestick:load_structure

2. Get an anvil

3.Rename the item to the name you want to give the structure

4.click on the block you want to load it on

Void Filler

1.Get the void filler item under the equipment category, or do /give @s structurestick:void_filler

2.click the 1st corner MUST BE A BLOCK

3.click the 2nd corner MUST BE A BLOCK

Volumer Ruler

1.Get the volume ruler item under the equipment category, or do /give @s structurestick:volume_ruler

2.click the 1st corner MUST BE A BLOCK

3.click the 2nd corner MUST BE A BLOCK (no distance limit)

Distance Ruler

1.Get the distance ruler item under the equipment category, or do /give @s structurestick:distance_ruler

2.click the 1st block

3.click the 2nd block (no distance limit)

What is what

A structure

A structure is a named & saved build in which you can load or can be loaded, the max size of a structure is 64x384x64 blocks.

Void

Structure voids can be placed as part of a structure and are ignored when the structure file is saved and loaded. This results in the blocks at the location where the structure is placed being maintained and not being overridden.

Distance Ruler

The distance between 2 blocks

Volume Ruler

The volume of a room between 2 blocks
