Hey everyone! Ever get completely lost deep in the Nether, or venture 10,000 blocks from home only to dread the walk back? I really missed the classic Java Edition stasis chambers, so I built an add-on to bring them to Bedrockand honestly, made them even better.

This isn't just a simple teleport command. It uses real Ender Pearl physics and lets you bind a normal fishing rod to your base. The best part? It works across dimensions. You can be exploring an End City, right-click your rod, and boomyou're instantly beamed back to your Overworld base.

Why you'll love it:

Works Anywhere, Literally: Nether, End, or the middle of nowhere. The add-on forces the game to keep your stasis chunk loaded, meaning you can travel as far as you want without the chamber breaking or freezing.

Make As Many As You Want: Want a whole teleport hub? The add-on uses a smart registry system, meaning you and your friends can set up dozens of stasis chambers at the same time without them interfering with each other.

Oops-Proof Security: Accidentally dropped your linked rod? Or died and dropped your items? The second the rod hits the ground, the add-on completely wipes the magical link so nobody else can steal your teleport key or accidentally trigger it.

We all know in java edition if you through a pearl through portal it teleports you there but in bedrock it didn't nothing but not anymore now if you through a pearl through a portal it will teleport you to the other side.

How to use it:

Build a normal stasis chamber (a soul sand water elevator with a trapdoor and a pressure plate next to it).

Throw your Ender Pearl into the bubbling water.

Cast your Fishing Rod bobber directly onto the pressure plate.

Go through nether and walk 30 blocks or more build another nether portal.

Go through second portal.

Go explore! Whenever you want to go home, just right-click your rod to reel it in. The trapdoor will snap shut, the pearl will hit, and you'll be teleported.

And for totem stasis build a normal stasis chamber and right click the pressure plate with totem.

In version 3.1.4 you can type command /toggle if you don't like to change the dimension when setting up the stasis
