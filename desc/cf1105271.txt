Connected Glasses is an addon that improves the visual aspect of Minecraft, giving you the opportunity to have connected glass without removing the vanilla glass.

Before:

After:

This add-on works with all vanilla glasses, to obtain the new glasses, just place a vanilla glass on the crafting table and you will get its connected version.

At the moment it only works with glass blocks, glass panels will come in the future!

if you have a connected glass, when you put it on the crafting table again, it will give you a normal glass, don't worry about that!

NEW!: VARIANTS

if you miss the old minecraft glass texture, now you can use it with this addon, just configure it in the external settings of the add-on!

you can also clear all distractions!!!

IMPORTANT

The add-on does NOT need experimental options, feel free not to activate them!
Means that it also works in preview versions.
