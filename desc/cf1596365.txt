Minimap Add-on

Never get lost again! This lightweight Minimap Add-on adds a clean, circular minimap to the top-right corner of your screen, making exploration easier without cluttering the HUD.

Features

Circular minimap in the top-right corner

Real-time terrain rendering

Player position indicator

North, East, South, and West compass

Live coordinates (X, Y, Z)

Current biome display

Mobile-friendly UI

Lightweight and optimized for performance

Compatible with the latest Minecraft Bedrock Edition

Perfect for survival, exploration, and adventure worlds. The minimap is designed to blend naturally with Minecraft's interface while staying out of the way of gameplay.
