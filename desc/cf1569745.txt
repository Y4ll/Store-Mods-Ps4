Chunks Are Random

Chunks Are Random completely changes the way Minecraft worlds generate. Instead of generating normally, each chunk can be replaced with a random chunk, creating a unique and chaotic world to explore.

This addon is perfect for players who enjoy:

- Randomized gameplay

- Exploration challenges

- Unique survival worlds

- Playing Minecraft in a completely new way

Whether you're starting a fresh survival world or simply looking for a fun challenge, Chunks Are Random ensures that every adventure is filled with surprises.

Important

- For the best experience, create a new world or explore unexplored areas to generate random chunks.

- Every 2 minutes, all items on the ground will be automatically deleted to help prevent lag and improve performance.

- A warning message will be displayed 10 seconds before the item cleanup occurs, giving players time to pick up anything important.
