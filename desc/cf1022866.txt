Introducing the ultimate solution for Minecraft players who miss the Full Bright resource pack after the 1.16 update on Bedrock. With Render Dragon no longer supporting external shaders, Full Bright stopped working. But Here I made a new resource pack, which increases the gamma(brightness) of the game even beyond 100%. With Brightness increased to that level, You get to see everything dark much clearer. See It's not exactly like the full bright we had, But as of now this is the best soltuion. Thought it is not good as full bright But it actually does a decent job, you will be able to see clearly in caves and all! And believe me, The game becomes brighter so that you could even explore dark areas without torches.

Note: Set your Brightness to 100% (both in-game and of your display)

Version 2: green mode added

The green mode creates an green light overlay rather than white overlay which all resource packs do now. The green mode will be helpful if you don't the white strain with of the default gamma. Also it does provide better clarity and brightening than that of the default or the other gamma present there.

Compatiblity:

This texture pack supports all the versions of minecraft from 1.16 to the latest (v26.1 right now). It works on both Minecraft bedrock and Minecraft pocket edition. It is also compatible with other texture packs. Also, as it is just a resource pack, You don't need cheats enabled or experimental features.
