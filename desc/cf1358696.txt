This resource pack reduces the size of shields in first-person view. By shrinking the model, shields block far less of your screen, giving you better visibility during combat, exploration, and everyday gameplay.

Only the first-person view is affected

-

In third-person, shields look completely normal.

-

In the inventory and hotbar, shields remain unchanged.

That means no impact on gameplay or design consistency just a smoother, less obstructed first-person experience.
