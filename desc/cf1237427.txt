pack.png Panorama with Shaders (Bedrock)

This is a really simple Resource Pack that adds shaders to the most iconic of all Minecraft seeds!

The seed was discovered by the Minecraft@Home team.

- Seed: 3257840388504953787

- Coordinates: 116, -31

- Version: Minecraft Java Alpha 1.2.2

The shaders used are the Complementary Reimagined Shaders.
