About the Mod

Brazilian Delight is an addon for the Farmer's Delight mod. It adds recipes, crops and features related to the brazilian culture!

Brazil is a tropical country with a rich and diverse culture, and this mod aims to bring some of that to the game. There are many things to explore and discover!

Features

Currently, the mod adds:

- Guarana

- Acai

- Coconut

- Lemon

- Cassava

- Coffee

- Yerba Mate

- Black Beans

- Carioca Beans

- Collard Greens

- Corn

- Garlic

Due to the amount of native brazilian fruits and vegetables, there are many more to come!

The mod adds a LOTS of new foods, with many more coming, such as:

- Coxinha

- Cheese Bread

- Carrot Cake with Chocolate

- Brigadeiro

- Feijoada

- Green Soup

- Stroganoff

- Tropeiro Beans

- Fish Moqueca

- Acai Cream

- Cassava Fritters

And many more! Oof!
