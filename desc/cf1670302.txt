About This Pack

Ever wondered exactly how much health your opponents, teammates, or dangerous mobs have left? ZenXveda Health Indicator Bar brings crystal-clear visual health bars directly above players and entities. Built with ultra-lightweight logic and native rendering controllers, this addon delivers a smooth, competitive UI with zero frame drops and full achievement support!

Key Features & Highlights

Real-Time Health Sync

Instantly synchronizes damage, natural healing, regeneration, absorption, and potion effects with 100% precision.

Dynamic Color Tiers

Health bars shift from Green (Safe) Yellow (Warning) Red (Critical) based on exact HP percentages.

Single & Multiplayer Modes

Includes a standalone Singleplayer Resource Pack and a full Multiplayer Add-on for Dedicated Servers, LAN, and Realms.

PvP & Boss Optimized

Essential tactical advantage during PvP duels, End Dragon & Wither battles, Nether raids, and mob farming.

Quick Installation Guide

- Download the desired .mcpack or .mcaddon file from the Files tab.

- Double-click or tap the file to automatically import it into Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

- Go to World Settings Resource Packs Activate ZenXveda Health Indicator Bar.

- (Multiplayer only): Also activate the Behavior Pack and turn ON Beta APIs / Experiments in World Settings.

- Launch your world and enjoy real-time health indicators!
