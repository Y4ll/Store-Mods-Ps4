Tensura Reincarnated MCPE The Ultimate That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime Addon for Minecraft Bedrock

Tensura Reincarnated MCPE is a complete Minecraft Bedrock Edition addon inspired by the anime and light novel That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime (Tensura). This addon transforms the vanilla survival experience into a progression RPG where players evolve through races, skills, magic, bosses, and powerful abilities from the Tensura universe.
