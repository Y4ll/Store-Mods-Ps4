Full description:

In survival mode you will be able to manufacture all the weapons that are available in this add-on, for this you will have to manufacture the weapon workbench from the workbench:

Place the weapon workbench on the ground and interact with it to open an interface that will show you all available weapons:

When selecting a weapon you need the necessary items to manufacture it. You should have the items in your inventory and click on Manufacture.

To make ammunition you need the ammunition box:

Place the ammunition box and interact with it:

To manufacture ammunition you must have in your inventory an ingot of iron and gunpowder by quantity, from the interface of the box select the ammunition and the quantity you want to manufacture.

Controls:

As long as you have the gun in your hand you can perform these different actions:

Reload:

To reload you need to have in your inventory the ammunition of the respective weapon, then while you have the weapon in your hand press the interact button, you must wait for the weapon to reload, if you change the slot the reload is canceled.

Shoot:

While the gun is loaded, it interacts with the gun to fire.

Aim:

While holding the gun, press the crouch button to aim.
