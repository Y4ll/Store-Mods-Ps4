This pack offers a variety of color options for ore outlines. Some variants feature subtle corner-only outlines for a less intrusive look, or options that highlight only Ancient Debris, keeping other textures untouched while still making Netherite easier to spot.

How to Change the texture for Bedrock Edition?

For Bedrock Edition, everything is included in a single all-in-one pack. To customize the texture style, go to your world settings and open the Resource Packs section. Select the Outlined Ores pack, then click the settings icon. This will bring up a slider that lets you choose the outline color for the ores.
