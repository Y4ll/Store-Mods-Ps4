Paxels combine the power of a pickaxe, axe, and shovel into one highly efficient tool. Whether youre mining deep underground, chopping trees, or clearing dirt, Paxels handle it all seamlessly, saving both time and inventory space.

AIOTs (All-In-One Tools) take versatility to the next level, merging every core toolpickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, and swordinto a single powerful item. Perfect for adventuring, farming, and combat, AIOTs are the ultimate upgrade for players who value convenience and performance.

Every tool supports enchantments and achievements, works flawlessly with any other addon, and requires no experimental optionsmaking it lightweight, fully compatible, and survival-friendly.
