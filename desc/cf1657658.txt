Please Leave Me Alone Minecraft Bedrock Horror Addon

Did you think your home was a safe place? Did you think the caves were empty? You were wrong. Please Leave Me Alone turns your Minecraft world into a psychological nightmare where you are never truly alone. Creatures are watching your every move through windows, doors, and from the deepest darkness.

There is nowhere to run. Nowhere to hide. They are always watching.

Key Features:

- Home Invasion: Creatures lurk and attack through the doors and windows of your own base.

- Underground Terror: Caves and mines become completely claustrophobic and dangerous.

- Constant Pursuit: Stalker mechanics that make you feel watched at all times.

- Immersive Atmosphere: Sounds and sightings designed to create extreme tension.

Requirements and Settings:

To ensure it works correctly in your world, enable the following options:

- Minecraft Bedrock: Compatible with version 1.26.40 or higher.

- Holiday Creator Features / Beta APIs: Must be ENABLED in the world settings.

- Recommended Game Mode: Survival, Hard Difficultyand play with headphones!

Supported Languages:

- Portuguese (Brazil) - Initial Support

- English (US) - Initial Support (More languages and full translations will be added in future updates).
