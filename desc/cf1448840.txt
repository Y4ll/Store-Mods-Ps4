The Advanced Tactical Gear Minecraft add-on brings a modern military aesthetic to player gear while maintaining a Minecraft style. This add-on focuses on individual operator gear, not vehicles or heavy weapons.

DETAILED ARMOR

Advance tactical has armor with good 3d model details with simple textures to keep vanilla Minecraft

VARIOUS TYPE OF ARMOR

Advance Tactical Gear has various types of armor to meet various types of missions ranging from light plate carriers to heavy full body armor, from light helmets to helmets with ballistic face shields.

SURVIVAL FRIENDLY

All armor and item can be crafted in survival Minecraft with just vanilla item. Make sure to have a lot of iron because it's the main material for a lot of armor
