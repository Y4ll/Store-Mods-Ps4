Newb Aetheris v1.1.2

Newb Aetheris is a colorful and cinematic shader made for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. It focuses on beautiful lighting, vibrant skies, smooth color transitions, and a more atmospheric world.

Enjoy colorful daylight, warm sunsets, cool and immersive nights, glowing blocks, improved clouds, and atmospheric effects that make the world feel more alive.

Newb Aetheris is designed to keep Minecrafts original style while adding more depth, color, and atmosphere to your gameplay.

SETTINGS

Recommended Settings

Graphics : Fancy

Clouds : On

Smooth Lighting : On

Beautiful Sky : On

Brightness : 50 - 90

Subpacks

Default : Main visuals, enables all effects, may be heavy on some devices.

Low : Slightly reduces the quality of effects or disables them. Uses vanilla clouds. A lighter subpack for low-end devices.

NOTES

Compatible only with minecraft version 26.45+

Compatible with Linux, Windows, Android, and iOS. need tools!! join newb community, get tools and tutorials (It's in the MCBE Patches section).

Performance may vary depending on device specifications and Minecraft settings.

Some visual effects may look different depending on graphics settings, render distance, and device performance.

For the best experience, use the recommended Minecraft graphics settings.

Clouds will only work if using beautiful graphics, turn them off if you're using simple graphics.
