Before you get the wrong idea, let me explain what the Not Just a Waifu Addon is.
Have you ever downloaded a zombie apocalypse addon where zombies spawn and attack you? Well, in Not Just a Waifu Addon, all those zombies are turned into cute waifus ready to attack you.
The difference is, unlike regular zombies, you can still become friends with them.
Some run fast, some move slowly. Which waifu do you fear the most?

(The addon is still in development, so please understand if you encounter any bugs.)
