QoL Enhanced

The Bedrock utility toolkit that fixes the boring parts and keeps the fun.

Tired of spending half your play session repairing Creeper craters, building temporary dirt bridges, or chasing dropped items across hills and caves?
QoL Enhanced is designed to eliminate repetitive maintenance and give you clean, powerful tools that feel great in real gameplay.

This add-on focuses on one goal:
Less grind. More action. Better world flow.

What Is QoL Enhanced?

QoL Enhanced is a quality-of-life add-on for Minecraft Bedrock Edition that adds a curated set of utility tools for terrain control, mobility, cleanup, and resource convenience.

It does not try to replace vanilla progression.
It upgrades the moment-to-moment experience with tools that solve common world problems quickly and reliably.

Feature Highlights

Hole Filler

Explosion damage? No problem.
Throw the orb into a crater and it intelligently repairs the hole with nearby block context, helping terrain look natural again instead of patchy.

Bridge Wand

Need to cross a ravine, valley, or mountain gap?
Use the wand to generate a bridge path that searches for a valid route and builds forward with practical placement logic.

Leveling Wand

Preparing a base area is now fast and clean.
Flatten terrain over a large radius to make building platforms, farms, roads, or structured builds with minimal manual digging/filling.

Magnet

Activate it and pull nearby dropped items toward you.
Perfect for mining sessions, mob farms, and high-chaos combat zones where loot scatters everywhere.

Water Balloon

Throwable utility projectile that creates flowing water at impact.
Great for emergency falls, quick fluid placement, vertical movement setups, and terrain interactions.

Lava Balloon

The hot counterpart to the Water Balloon.
Creates flowing lava on impact for traps, terrain control, smelting setups, or lava-based utility builds.

Drain Sphere

When water gets out of control, throw this and clear connected water areas efficiently.
Useful for underwater prep, cave cleanup, and fixing accidental flooding.
