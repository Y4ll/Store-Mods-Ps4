Fast Piglin Barter Addon v1.0.5

Fast Piglin Barter Addon supercharges your bartering experience by drastically reducing the time Piglins take to barterand best of all, it doesnt disable achievements!

Say goodbye to slow, tedious bartering cycles. With this addon, you can trade gold for Piglin loot almost instantly. Its perfect for resource farming, testing, speedruns, or loot-focused gameplay. This pack keeps the drop rates and loot tables completely vanillano scripts, no commands, just faster bartering!

Fully compatible with vanilla Minecraft mechanics
Works great for ender pearl farming, blackstone collection, and more
Ideal for technical players, casual survivalists, and addon creators

Customizable Barter Speeds

You can choose from 19 speed options based on your preference:

- Normal

- 1.25x, 1.5x, 1.75x

- 2x 10x (in 0.5 increments)

Each version is a separate filejust choose the one that fits your playstyle!

Server Compatibility Info:

This addon may not work on servers (like Aternos or Realms) because servers often force the default version of the pack or load other conflicting ghast behavior packs.

If you want to use this on a server, please join our Discord and download the separated "Custom Options" version of the addon. These files are manually selectable to ensure they work as intended in server environments.
