Vors Vein Miner

Sneak, break one ore, and watch the whole vein disappear. Vors Vein Miner instantly clears connected ore clusters in a single swing no more tunneling block-by-block through a diamond vein.

- Works with any pickaxe normal, Fortune, or Silk Touch, all handled correctly

- Fortune-aware drops multiplies yields on Fortune-eligible ores just like vanilla mining

- Silk Touch support drops the raw ore blocks instead of resources when equipped

- Smart detection only triggers on veins of 2+ connected ore blocks, so single ores mine
normally

- Covers every ore coal, copper, iron, gold, redstone, lapis, diamond, emerald, quartz, nether gold, and ancient debris

- Balanced by design mines up to 512 blocks per vein within a 12-block radius.

Just sneak and mine the rest of the vein comes with it.
