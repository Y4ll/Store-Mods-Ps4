Ever wanted to know how much health a Zombie has left? Now you can. Health bars gives every mob in the game a Health Bar so you can see exactly how much health they have left!

-This add-on is for Vanilla Mobs only!-

Join the Four Worlds Studios Discord

You will see the Health Bar fall as you attack Mobs.

All Vanilla Mobs will have Health Bars.

Health Bars will change colour as their life decreases.

You can change the style of Health Bar you want to use in the games settings.
