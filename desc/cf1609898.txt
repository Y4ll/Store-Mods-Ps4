Minecraft But You Get Every Block You Look At...

Ever wondered what would happen if every block you looked at instantly became yours?

You Get Every Block You Look At is a simple but entertaining mod that automatically gives you the exact block your crosshair is pointing at. Every glance rewards you with another block, creating a unique challenge where your inventory quickly fills with whatever catches your eye.

The concept is incredibly simple, but it completely changes the way you play. Exploring caves, forests, villages, the Nether, or the End becomes a game of collecting everything you see. Rare blocks become exciting to spot, and even ordinary blocks can quickly pile up as you travel through the world.

Whether you're challenging yourself to collect every block, racing against friends, or just looking for a fun twist on normal gameplay, this mod offers a fresh experience with almost no learning curve.

Features

- Instantly receive the block you're looking at.

- Works with the blocks you encounter during normal gameplay.

- Creates a fun and unique gameplay challenge.

- Great for challenge runs and multiplayer fun.

- Lightweight and easy to use.

If you're looking for a simple mod that adds a surprising new way to experience Minecraft, You Get Every Block You Look At is the perfect challenge to try!
