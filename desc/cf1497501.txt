A&S Tools

This texture pack refreshes the tool designs in Minecraft Bedrock while maintaining the standard 16x resolution. The goal is to provide a more detailed and polished look while preserving the game's original Vanilla aesthetic.

Compatibility

This pack is fully compatible with the Actions & Stuff addon in its Vanilla version, ensuring that the textures align perfectly with the animations without any visual glitches.

Installation

Download the .mcpack file.

Open the file to automatically import it into Minecraft.

Activate the pack under Settings > Global Resources.
