# OneTouch Essentials

A quality-of-life add-on that streamlines villager trading, crafting, and survival essentials with simple one-touch interactions.

## Features

- **One-Touch Trading** Quickly complete villager trades with a single tap, no more repeated clicking.

- **Control Book** A custom item that gives you quick access to toggle the add-on's features in-game.

- **Xray Tome** Craftable item that grants temporary X-ray vision to help locate ores and structures.

- **Auto Armor** Automatically equips the best armor available in your inventory.

- **Auto Totem** Automatically equips a Totem of Undying to your offhand when available.

- **Auto Water Drop** Automatically places water to break your fall from height.

- **Vein Miner** Mine entire ore veins in one go.

- **Tree Capitator** Chop down entire trees with a single hit.

- **Saturation HUD** Java-style saturation bar for better hunger management.

- **RGB Experience Bar** A colorful animated XP bar for a fresh HUD look.

- **Improved Villager Trade UI** Fixed trade screen so all tiers and offers are visible, not just the villager's current level.

## Compatibility

Works on **Mobile, PC, and Console** designed to work well across all control types (touch, mouse/keyboard, and gamepad).
