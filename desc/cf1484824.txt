LOW FIRE, SHIELD & SMALL TOTEM (v1.21+)

Improve your combat efficiency and screen visibility with this essential PVP utility pack! This pack is designed to remove screen obstructions, giving you a clear view of your opponents during intense battles in Minecraft Bedrock and Pocket Edition.

Main Features:

Low Fire & Soul Fire: Reduced fire height so your vision stays clear even when you're on fire.

Lowered Shield: The shield occupies much less screen space, allowing you to see what's ahead while blocking.

Small Totem: A compact Totem of Undying texture that doesn't block the middle of your screen when popped.

PVP Optimized: Clean textures designed to maintain high FPS on all mobile devices.

How to Install:

Download the .mcpack file from the link above.

Tap the file to import it directly into Minecraft.

Go to Settings > Global Resources > My Packs and Activate "Low Fire, Shield & Small Totem."
