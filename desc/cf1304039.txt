HOW TO ACCESS THE ITEMS:

In the new 26.1 update, players can now use a stick to change an item's rotation, and a feather to change its position; which is good for making clutter decorations.

All items can be obtained in the stonecutter using a stick.

Please note that some objects dont have this feature yet, such as chairs.
