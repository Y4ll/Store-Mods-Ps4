A lightweight resource pack engineered to maximize frame rates on Minecraft Bedrock Edition without sacrificing the core vanilla feel. Every component - fog, particle systems, block animations, sounds, and UI, has been tuned for smoother, more stable gameplay across all device tiers.

- Maintains a clean, vanilla-style look without heavy visual changes

- Designed for stable, lag-free gameplay on any hardware

Two Selectable Subpacks

Choose the subpack that fits how you play. Switch anytime from the pack settings in-game.

1. Pure FPS Maximum performance. 186 particle emitters overridden with 175 fully zeroed, including dragon, guardian and enchanting table effects, plus tightened fog distances and no PvP overlays Best for: weakest devices, maximum FPS

2. PvP + FPS Performance lift with combat-focused additions. Much lighter particle stripping 48 emitters zeroed instead of 175 so bubbles, dragon, guardian and enchanting effects still read correctly during boss fights, plus the PvP UI suite and a saturated colormap Best for: PvP players who still want the performance gain

What's Optimized

- Fog Fog definitions for all 89 vanilla biomes, a catch-all default for anything unlisted, and 10 direct overrides of vanilla's own fog identifiers as a second layer of coverage: default, dry, humid, semi-humid, lush caves, pale garden, dark forest hills, sulfur caves, dappled forest and powder snow. None of the pack's fog definitions carry a volumetric block, so the raymarched volumetric fog Bedrock renders under Vibrant Visuals the most expensive fog cost on the GPU never runs on a covered biome. Pure FPS additionally pulls distance fog in tight; PvP + FPS pushes it out for maximum sightlines.

- Particles Pure FPS zeroes 175 of 186 emitters: explosions, smoke, portals, bubbles, falling dust, falling leaves, campfire smoke, geysers, dragon, guardian and enchanting table effects. PvP + FPS zeroes 48, keeping bubbles and boss-fight effects intact. Potion and spell particles are never zeroed in either mode they carry real gameplay information, and are instead handed to you as toggles.

- Block animations Water, lava, portals, sea lanterns, campfires and cauldrons ship as single-frame textures instead of multi-frame flipbooks, so they stop cycling entirely. Fire is deliberately left animated so it stays readable, at half the vanilla frame count.

- Sounds Rain audio silenced to cut mixing overhead during storms.

- Animations A lightweight custom book animation, plus a low-shield animation on PvP + FPS that stops the shield from covering your screen while held.

- UI Dark UI throughout and the vignette removed on both subpacks. PvP + FPS additionally adds a numbered hotbar, a quick-craft button on the crafting, stonecutter, and trade screens, and a durability counter on the bottom-left.

- Device tiering Pure FPS sits at the lower tier, so low-memory devices auto-select it rather than the heavier option.
