True Hammers - by Mikayil022

Adds 7 tiered hammers (Wooden to Netherite) that mine a 3x3 area in a single swing - faster mining without leaving vanilla balance or your achievements behind.

Features

- 3x3 mining area with every swing

(Sneak (crouch) while mining to break a single block instead of the full area.)

- 7 tiers matching vanilla pickaxe progression: Wooden, Stone, Copper, Iron, Golden, Diamond, Netherite

- Mining speed, durability and enchantability scale like their pickaxe counterparts

- Respects Efficiency, Unbreaking, Fortune and Silk Touch

- Craftable and repairable using standard recipes

- Ores are excluded from the 3x3 effect to keep resource balance fair
