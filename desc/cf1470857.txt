Ender Dragon Shader provides a magical atmosphere during sunrise, a beautiful magical feeling at sunset, and an epic vibe at night. It makes the ocean look dynamic and vary from crystal blue to deep blue depending on the water depth. It also makes your gameplay more moody, making you want to stay and keep playing Minecraft.
