How to find this ore?

To find heart ore, you have to venture into the depths where deepslate exists. This mineral is extremely rare, so searching for it can be quite time-consuming. Heart ore can be affected by the Fortune enchantment, allowing you to obtain 1 to 3 heart fragments.

Heart Fragment

This item can only be obtained from Heart Ore, which is scattered throughout the world. It is only used to craft "Heart Tanks."

Heart Tank

This special item increases your maximum health by 2 hearts. Perfect for going on adventures!

How do I use the heart tank?

To use the heart tank, you need to interact with the item. It will be consumed automatically, granting you an upgrade of (4) extra health or 2 more hearts to the player. Since the player can have unlimited life, it can be annoying if you have too many hearts as it may clutter the screen.

How to reduce hearts if you have used too many heart upgrades?

Remove Heart

This item removes hearts if you have active upgrades from the Heart Tank. This item works with "Right-Click" or "Interact."
