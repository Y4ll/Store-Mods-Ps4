Author: Baku_Studio

BK Shader is an original Minecraft Bedrock Edition resource pack built for the official Vibrant Visuals graphics mode. It delivers a clean, premium cinematic look with natural lighting, rich greens, soft atmospheric depth and beautiful day-to-night transitions while fully preserving Minecrafts classic block style.

Pack Information

- Pack Name: BK Shader

- Author: Baku_Studio

- Type: Vibrant Visuals / PBR Resource Pack

- Supported Versions: Minecraft Bedrock & Pocket Edition 1.21.120 and newer (1.21 1.26 generation)

- Required Mode: Vibrant Visuals

- Platforms: Android, iOS, Windows, Xbox, PlayStation (devices that support Vibrant Visuals)

Visual Style

The pack focuses on a clean, polished and immersive atmosphere. Expect bright natural daylight, warm golden sunsets, readable midnight with cool blue ambient light, rich emerald green vegetation, soft shadows and clear cyan-blue water. The overall mood feels premium and cinematic while still looking unmistakably like Minecraft.

Installation

- Import the .mcpack file

- Activate BK Shader in Resource Packs (place near the top)

- Go to Settings Video Graphics Mode Vibrant Visuals

- Enter any world

Performance

Lightweight configuration-only pack. Runs well on mobile and higher-end devices that support Vibrant Visuals.
