IKEA: 3008 CLOSING TIME CHRISTMAS UPDATE

The SCP IKEA 3008 ADDON is an addon that will allow you to make your own scp ikea 3008. The mod features over 500+ new furniture blocks, over 20+ types of food, new mobs, and many new items.

Radio

The radio displays and announces key map locations in the chat, such as basement entrances or floating houses. Now you won't have to search for them for so long just craft a radio and head to the designated column. However, keep in mind that the radio has a delay between messages, and part of the message will be encrypted. And yes, it will be craftable!
