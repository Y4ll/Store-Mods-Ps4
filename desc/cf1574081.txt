Azure Visuals is my loudest pack. Cyan skies, lime green grass and saturation pushed as far as I could take it without the world falling apart.

What this changes

Saturation

Every color in the game is pushed hard. Nothing is left dull or washed out, in any biome, at any time of day. This is the whole pack in one line.

Sky

Deep azure blue running into cyan, with hard white clouds sitting on top of it. The clouds actually separate from the sky instead of blending into it.

Grass and foliage

Grass and leaves go bright lime green. Plains and forests read as loud and alive rather than the muted green vanilla gives you.

Contrast

Shadows are kept strong so the bright colors have something to sit against. Without that the whole thing would just look flat and blown out.

Terrain and trees

Bark and terrain pick up more depth up close, so they hold together even with the saturation turned this far up.

PBR

Blocks react to light with real depth, which is where that close up detail comes from.

PBR is also the heaviest part of any Vibrant Visuals pack, so it is not forced on you. It ships as a subpack, which means you can switch it on or off yourself. When you activate Azure Visuals in Global Resources, pick the version you want. Turn PBR off if your device runs hot or drops frames.

Requirements

THIS PACK IS FOR VIBRANT VISUALS ONLY
IT DOES NOT WORK WITH MB LOADER

Vibrant Visuals must be ON. This pack is built for Bedrock's Vibrant Visuals renderer. The pack will not do anything if it is off.

Where to turn it on: Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals

If that toggle is not there, your device or version does not support Vibrant Visuals yet.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Azure Visuals

- Pick the subpack you want. PBR on for the full look, PBR off for better performance.

- Turn on Settings > Video > Vibrant Visuals

- If the lighting still looks the same, leave the world and load it again once

FAQ

The pack is not working. Nothing changed after I installed it.
I get this comment a lot, and almost every single time the reason is the same. Vibrant Visuals is turned off.

This pack only works when Vibrant Visuals is ON. That is written clearly at the top of this page. With it off, the pack loads fine but nothing on your screen changes, because there is no renderer for it to work with. The pack is not broken.
