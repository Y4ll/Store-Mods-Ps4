Transform farming into a faster and more enjoyable experience with Sneak Grow, a lightweight Minecraft Bedrock addon that lets players grow plants naturally by sneaking nearby.

Instead of using bonemeal or special tools, simply sneak near supported plants to trigger a single growth stage. Each growth requires you to release sneak and sneak again, preventing accidental continuous growth and keeping the gameplay balanced.
