Whats the deal?

Vanilla elytra is cool until you want to actually fight in the air. Elytra Expansion bolts on a full combat kit for Bedrock: craft unlock licenses, load ammo, take off, and go loud.

- Cockpit HUD speed, altitude, heading, ammo at a glance

- Full arsenal cannon, MG, missiles, bombs, and the big MEGA

- Dive bombing bomb-cam + Jericho-trumpet-style siren vibes

- Flares & callsigns break locks, name your bird

The main course: dive bombing

Yeah, this packs personality is steep dives and loud drops. Tip the nose down, the bomb camera kicks in, and you get that Jericho trumpet energy (Stuka-flavored not a ripped movie clip). Flip between HE / AP / carpet / incendiary / cluster / MEGA, line it up, and let go.

No copyrighted movie siren audio. Just the vibe.

- /ee:help in-game /ee:settings for HUD prefs

Getting started

- Enable both BP and RP. One pack alone = nothing useful happens.

- Turn on Beta APIs in world experiments.

- Craft an unlock license right-click to unlock that weapon.

- Craft ammo (or grab it) toss it in inventory; it auto-loads into reserve.

- Take off. Shoot things. Drop things. Try not to become a crater.

- Optional: craft a Callsign Plate (name tag + paper + iron + redstone) and pick your callsign.

Creative folks: grab Creative Ammo Supply for infinite reserve.
Admin toys (cheats ON): /ee:kit /ee:unlock_all /ee:minigame, etc.
