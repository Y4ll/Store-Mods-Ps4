Alan Becker Staffs (Minecraft Bedrock)Bring the legendary power of Alan Becker's "Animation vs. Minecraft" universe into your Bedrock worlds! This add-on introduces unique craftable staffs made from different blocks, giving you custom abilities to completely transform your gameplay.

Features OfferedBlock Staffs: Craft distinct staffs using vanilla Minecraft blocks.Custom Abilities: Every block variant unlocks a unique superpower or tactical skill.Bedrock Optimized: Fully functional add-on designed for a smooth experience across mobile, console, and PC.

How It Affects Your ExperienceNew Playstyles: Switch between different staffs to adapt instantly to combat, exploration, or building.Vanilla Integration: Enhances your survival or creative worlds without breaking the classic Minecraft feel.
