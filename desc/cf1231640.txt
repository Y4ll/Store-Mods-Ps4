Abyssal Ascent has so many new content starting off with the 3 base layers of the End

Void Layer: Void Layer is at the bottom of the dimension, it has several dangerous threats inside it, but also has some amazing rewards
(Note: due to a minecraft bug with fog, the area in the image isnt fully accurate)

Clouded Layer: this is the highest layer and has the least amount of content, its the main source of Sapphire Ore and has a bunch of cloud related plants and blocks

BIOMES:

Endra Forest: Endra forest is a giant wooden mushroom biome

Distorted Forest: Distorted forest is a giant biome filled with flower trees

Chorus Grove: has a bunch of shulker themed mobs and edible trees

Arkose Peaks: Arkose Peaks are mountain ranges made up of mostly Arkose Blocks, which is the Ends version of stone, only 2 mobs spawn here, Evupuls and Final Skeletons:

Lavender Fields: This Biome is similar to the normal end, except instead of normal end stone, its cobbled end stone, which acts similar to farmland and is used to grow End Crops (note you need the crops to be in the End for them to grow)

End Wilds: The End Wilds are a biome consisting of several Golden Plants and End Sand (used to get glass in the End)

Abyssal Swamp: Abyssal Swamp is filled with water and fish, a great place for you to go to fish

Cosmic Frontier: the Cosmic Frontier is a Cosmic/star-themed biome thats filled with a bunch of Cosmic mobs, Cosmonium, and more

Dragonspire Wield: this biome has 3 different dangerous dragon mobs, as well as tall dragon shale spires

Blushie Swamp: Blushie Swamp is a dense biome, filled with plants and has pockets of water in it

Portal Plague: Portal Plague has a ton of eyeballs and fossils, the landscape is covered in glowing Portal Mold Blocks

STRUCTURES:

Ender Chamber: The Ender Chambers are a varient of the trial chambers with its own unique gimmicks

Ruined End Portal: this place contains a quick exit to the overworld

Dragon Bastion: this structure has different variations depending on the biome

Voided Soul Blacksmith: this structure contains the Soul Forge, which is used for a lot of the crafting in this addon

Void Pope Church: this structure contains the spawn block for the Void Pope (place an ascendant block on top of the spawn block to summon Void pope)

Shrine of the Forgotten Eye: this structure serves to be the spawn location and arena for Sightfull Obelisk of Ender

End Ruins: Ruins are randomly scattered across the End, theyre similar to ocean ruins, you dig around them and find the treasure, they also have a Crystal which is used for potion brewing and throwing knives:

Dragonbird Meeting Zone: these structures are similar to Dragonfolk Bastions, but Elyrae mobs spawn in them, they have several varients depending on the biome

Misc Structures:

MOBS:

Elyrae: allies with Dragonfolk, they can spawn with either an enchanted sword or jade caltrops, theyre passive unless provoked, stolen from, or if you anger a dragonfolk

Evupul: This mob comes in 3 different colors, theyre a passive mob that you can ride, you cannot control this mob, they just fly around randomly, they drop Evupul Wings

(the golden one spawns in the Clouded Layer and the other one spawns in the Normal End Layer)

(Dark one is hostile and spawns in the Void Layer)

(Credit to DeadVoxels End Update Mod, on Modrinth for permission to add this mob)

Bijaws: Bijaws are flying teeth creatures that attack you for 8 damage

Blastling: Blastlings launch purple pulses at you

Snareling: fires a Web at you that locks you into place, then the Snareling runs up to you and starts doing melee damage to you

Watchling: The Watchling is a melee mob who has extreme speed and damage

Endersent: deals splash damage to nearby mobs and players, whenever the mob cannot hit you, it will start smashing the ground, releasing a powerful shock
