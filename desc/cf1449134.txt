The Blood Steve Addon is not meant for normal survival.
Blood Steve is extremely thirsty for blood and will do anything to hunt you down while youre in a survival world.
He has unique AI that makes him difficult to defeat.

Blood Steve usually appears only at night, so you should be safe during the day hopefully.

Blood Steves stats are still unknown. The cause of his existence and the reason why he became Blood Steve remain a mystery.
Maybe now is the time for you to find out why Steve turned into Blood Steve.

Blood Steve will spawn at 3:00 AM and will despawn when exposed to direct sunlight.
