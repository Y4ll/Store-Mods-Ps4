Tricky Trials Panorama with Shaders (Bedrock)

This is a really simple Resource Pack that adds shaders to the original panorama.

There's no world seed this time, because I had to painfully recreate the panorama learning how to use structure blocks in the process, this was fun to do.

The shaders used are the Complementary Reimagined Shaders.

Note: GUI not included.
