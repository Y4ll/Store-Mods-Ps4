Survival Flashlight

Survival Flashlight adds a simple, craftable flashlight to Minecraft, giving players a portable light source for caves, mines, and dark areas.

Instead of placing torches everywhere, you can carry a flashlight and explore more naturally while keeping the survival feel.

Features

- Craftable in survival

- Portable light source

- Great for caves and mining

- Useful for horror maps and adventure worlds

- Simple and lightweight

Crafting Recipe

The Survival Flashlight can be crafted using:

- 1 Redstone

- 1 Iron Ingot

- 1 Glass

Simple, affordable, and available early in a survival playthrough.

Perfect For

- Survival worlds

- Cave exploration

- Mining trips

- Adventure maps

- Horror maps

- Players who want a more immersive way to explore the dark
