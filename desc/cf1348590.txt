All about Dogs Plus

Dogs Plus is an old yBrothers add-on that promises to bring new, more interactive, and fun dog breeds for players to play in their worlds! It was released and updated only once in 2022, and was discontinued that same year... Until now! Well, we're bringing you Dogs Plus: Reimagined! A completely new and updated version with new mechanics, breeds, visuals, blocks, items, and much more! And this time, it's here to stay, with frequent updates on the way, we want to bring you an increasingly better experience while playing with these cute dogs!

Each of the new dogs has unique mechanics, although these don't directly reflect a breed-specific personality, they're actually geared toward the dogs' size, which is divided into three categories: Small Breeds, Medium Breeds, and Large Breeds! It's therefore common for dogs of different sizes to have different behaviors, health points, and so on!

Small Breeds

Small breed dogs are cute, aren't they? They don't attack, not even to defend their owners. Try protecting them instead!

Medium Breeds

Medium-sized dogs are fearless, but not that much... They can attack creatures that attack their owners, or that are attacked by their owner! A good companion, right?

Large Breeds

Large breed dogs are even better... They attack any monsters that invade their territory while holding a bone! A great way to keep the area around your base protected!

More from Dogs Plus:

Dogs also can get dirty and can be bathed! There are also different ways to keep them close, depending on what works best for you! Want to let them free? No problem, just tame your dog. Want to have them by your side at all times? No problem, equip them with a collar, and they'll follow you everywhere! Want to keep them safe in one place so they can't run away? No problem, give them a bone, and they'll always stay close to that spot! They also sit, btw!
By sitting for a long time, they may have a chance of getting dirty!!
