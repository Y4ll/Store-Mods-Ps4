Kraftoid's Modern Furniture Addon returns now with 60+ furniture choices (including 100+ color varieties), survival mode compatible and interactable! An addon for Bedrock community!

- Modern and minimalist furniture style with variety of choices and colors!

- Includes living room, bathroom, kitchen, bedroom, gaming, toys and misc. furniture!

- Survival mode compatible (craftable!)

- Interactable, playable and rideable!

- Connectable tables, with variety of table designs!

- Compatible with latest Bedrock versions and Vibrant Visuals!

Works with Survival Mode (Craftable!): Kraftoid's Modern Furniture works with both Creative and Survival Mode, and in multiplayer too!

More Colors with Furniture: Comes with 16 colors for sofa, mini sofa, bed and will be expanding to more furniture!

You can switch easily the color of your furniture using the newest Change Color Tool! Click the tool to the desired furniture, and can be done as many as times as you want!

Connectable Tables: Building your own house in Minecraft: Bedrock gets even better! Use the Mini Table, Mini Table Drawer, Layered Table and Counter, with three different colors, fully connectable and compatible!

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

- Addon Installation

On your mobile phone or PC, tap or click the .mcaddon file to install the resource and behavior pack for Modern Furniture. Activate Modern Furniture Addon in Settings > Global Resources, as well as in your world settings for behavior pack.

2. Performance / Lag Issues
This addon is expected to work with most devices, including mobile and PC. However, should issues arise, feel free to reach out for feedback.
