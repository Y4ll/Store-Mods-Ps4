This addon adds a drone that you can control to explore the world, you can also view its camera from anywhere, there are many settings that you can change like enabling password protection or changing speed, and its multiplayer friendly.

First craft the drone and the controller.

place the drone and interact with the controller to control it.

Use W / touch controls/ joy stick to control the drone.

Move the camera to move in different directions, and press jump to boost vertical speed, press crouch to stop controlling the drone.

When you stop controlling the drone it stays in that location and falls into the ground.

type this command to open settings menu (you must be OP to use this)

players can use /drone:settings to change their local settings, they can change drone speed upto the maximum speed set by the admin.

To view the drone camera right click/hold touch/interact using the controller item to open menu of your drones, press on a drone to view its camera

You are vulnerable to attacks while viewing the camera but you can change that in the settings.

Press crouch to stop viewing the camera.

To pickup the drone interact with it with empty hand.

If you are stuck or something wrong happens use this command: /drone:fix

To change the color interact with the drone with any dye. or use a cauldron

You can also dye the controller using the cauldron.

Installation

- you need version (1.21.101+) for this addon.

- install mcaddon file and open it to import.

- make a copy of your world and add the behavior and resource packs to your world

- No need for any experimental features.
