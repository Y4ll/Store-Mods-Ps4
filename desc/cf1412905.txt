Days Counter By Ancxliy

Days Counter By Ancxliy is a lightweight HUD pack that enhances your Minecraft experience by displaying essential world information in a clean, vanilla-friendly interface.

Features

Day Counter Displays the current Minecraft world day.

Coordinates Shows your live X, Y, and Z position.

World Playtime Tracks how long you've played in the current world.

Moon Phase Displays the current Minecraft moon phase in real time.

Why Use This Pack?

Clean and minimal design.

Fits naturally with the vanilla HUD.

Lightweight and performance-friendly.

Perfect for survival, exploration, and everyday gameplay.

Stay informed while keeping your screen clean and immersive!
