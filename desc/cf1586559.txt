This addon adds Verity to your world. A box will appear next to you; click on it and Verity will come out. Use the right mouse button to interact with him. Ask him whatever you want. On day 2, he will start asking you questions. On day 3, his face will change, but if you get close, he will give a big, huge smile, and the questions will change. On day 4, the questions will change again. On day 5, at night, he will say that something is coming. On day 7, a message will appear in the chat that Verity wants to talk to you, and he will say scary things. Run from him! An anomaly will appear at night; protect yourself!
