DESCRIPTION OF VERSION 1.0.0 (29 AUGUST, 2026) :

This add-on brings the iconic stick figures from Alan Becker's Animator vs. Animation universe into Minecraft Bedrock Edition, allowing players to transform themselves or Minecraft mobs into animated stick figures. It features five characters: Red, Yellow, Green, Blue, and The Second Coming (TSC), with each character having its own appearance, texture, item, and animation-compatible model. TSC uses a separate model and texture from the other four stick figures.

The addon uses Minecraft's armor/attachable rendering system to display the stick figures. The stickman items occupy the head armor slot, while the original player or mob is made invisible through continuously refreshed invisibility effects. This makes the stick figure effectively replace the appearance of the player or mob while preserving the underlying entity's movement, AI, health, attacks, abilities, and other normal behavior. The stick figures therefore animate according to the movement state of whatever entity is wearing them rather than being completely independent mobs.

Stickman characters and items

There are five wearable stickman items:

Red, Green, Blue, Yellow, The Second Coming

Each has a maximum stack size of 1 and can be equipped in the head slot. They have their own colored textures and attachable definitions.

There are also five corresponding Stickman on a Stick items:

Red Stickman on a Stick

Yellow Stickman on a Stick

Green Stickman on a Stick

Blue Stickman on a Stick

The Second Coming on a Stick

These items are deliberately not wearable. Instead, using one on a Minecraft entity transfers the corresponding stickman into that entity's head slot. The original item is consumed when successfully applied. This allows practically any supported mob to be converted into a moving stick figure while retaining the mob's normal behavior.
