Dan's Waystones brings a flexible and immersive Waystone teleportation system to Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Discover, craft, and place Waystones throughout your world to create a convenient network of fast-travel locations.

---

Whether you're exploring distant lands, building large settlements, or simply looking for a better way to travel between your favorite locations, Dan's Waystones provides a simple and reliable solution.

---

What's Included:

- Multiple Waystone variants

- Supporting teleportation items

- Waystone structures always appear in villages.

- Custom crafting recipes

- A simple and easy-to-use teleportation system

- Survival-friendly gameplay

- Achievements friendly

Features:

Multiple Waystone Variants:

- Build your Waystones using a variety of materials and styles. Different variants allow you to match Waystones with your builds and create unique fast-travel points throughout your world.

Fast Travel:

- Activate Waystones and use them as destinations for quick transportation between different locations.

Supporting Items:

Recall Necklace:

- It will return you to your most recently saved Waystone location or your set bed, provided your health is below 20%.

Warp Scroll ( has an 'active' status):

- When you click on an activated waystone, it enters an active state, allowing you to teleport to a previously selected location.

Warp Stone:

- It allows you to open the waystone menu and teleport to your desired location without needing a waystone.

Survival Friendly:

- Waystones and their supporting items are integrated into the survival experience with crafting recipes designed to fit naturally into Minecraft gameplay.
