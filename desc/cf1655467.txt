Natures Smallfolk Development Document

Natures Smallfolk introduces a detailed ecosystem of insects and tiny creatures for Minecraft Bedrock.
Every species features natural behaviors, biomespecific spawning, color variants, and environmental interactions that make the world feel richer, more dynamic, and biologically alive.

Mob Behaviors

Below is the full catalog of creatures included in the development document, each with unique movement, interactions, variants, and environmental behavior.

<li>
<strong> Butterfly</strong><br>
<em>Spawns:</em> Flower Forest, Meadow, Plains, Forest<br>
<em>Variants:</em> 8 colors<br>
<em>Behavior:</em> Erratic, prolonged flight; lands on flowers and leaves; attracted to flowers; flees if approached too fast.
</li>

<li>
<strong> Ant</strong><br>
<em>Spawns:</em> Plains, Forest, Jungle, Savanna<br>
<em>Behavior:</em> Fast walkers searching for food; collect items and store them in Ant Piles (up to 10).<br>
<em>Ant Pile:</em> Breaking it drops stored loot and spawns 36 furious ants that swarm the player.
</li>

<li>
<strong> Dung Beetle</strong><br>
<em>Spawns:</em> Savanna, Desert, Mesa<br>
<em>Behavior:</em> Slow walkers; roll dung; avoid water.
</li>

<li>
<strong> Firefly</strong><br>
<em>Spawns:</em> Swamp, Roofed Forest, Forest, Lush Caves<br>
<em>Behavior:</em> Slow, low-altitude flight; emits light via emissive textures or dynamic lighting.
</li>

<li>
<strong> Ladybug</strong><br>
<em>Spawns:</em> Plains, Sunflower Plains, Meadow, Flower Forest, Forest<br>
<em>Behavior:</em> Alternates between slow walking and short flights; peaceful; attracted to crops and flowers.
</li>

<li>
<strong> Atlas Moth</strong><br>
<em>Spawns:</em> Jungle, Forest, Taiga, Caves<br>
<em>Behavior:</em> Nocturnal; disappears during the day; attracted to light sources.
</li>

<li>
<strong> Praying Mantis</strong><br>
<em>Spawns:</em> Forest, Plains, Jungle<br>
<em>Behavior:</em> Ambush predator; leaps onto prey; neutral to players but attacks nearby entities.
</li>

<li>
<strong> Snail</strong><br>
<em>Spawns:</em> Swamp, Forest, Jungle, Lush Caves, Dripstone Caves<br>
<em>Variants:</em> Brown, Cyan, Green, Pink<br>
<em>Behavior:</em> Extremely slow; hides inside its shell when threatened or during heavy rain.
</li>

<li>
<strong> Tarantula</strong><br>
<em>Spawns:</em> Jungle, Forest, Savanna<br>
<em>Variants:</em> 5 colors<br>
<em>Behavior:</em> Fast; climbs walls; neutral by day, aggressive in darkness; hunts insects.
</li>

<li>
<strong> Centipede</strong><br>
<em>Spawns:</em> Underground Caves<br>
<em>Behavior:</em> Fast zigzag movement; prefers dark areas; neutral but defends itself with mild poison.
</li>

<li>
<strong> Cricket</strong><br>
<em>Spawns:</em> Plains, Forest<br>
<em>Variants:</em> Green, Brown<br>
<em>Behavior:</em> Long, fast leaps; chirps in the dark; flees quickly from players and predators.
</li>

<li>
<strong> Dragonfly</strong><br>
<em>Spawns:</em> Swamp, River<br>
<em>Variants:</em> Blue, Green, Purple, Red<br>
<em>Behavior:</em> Fast, precise flight; hovers in place.
</li>

<li>
<strong> Mosquito</strong><br>
<em>Spawns:</em> Swamp, Jungle<br>
<em>Behavior:</em> Erratic flight; aggressively chases the player; deals low damage but is very annoying.
</li>

<li>
<strong> Scorpion</strong><br>
<em>Spawns:</em> Desert, Mesa, Underground Caves<br>
<em>Variants:</em> Black, Desert<br>
<em>Behavior:</em> Moderate walking speed; neutral; poisons only when provoked.
</li>

<li>
<strong> Sea Slug</strong><br>
<em>Spawns:</em> Ocean, Beach (Underwater)<br>
<em>Variants:</em> Aqua, Green, Orange<br>
<em>Behavior:</em> Very slow; walks on ocean floors and coral reefs; peaceful but toxic when interacted with.
</li>

Glass Jars System

Players can collect and display insects using 35 unique glass jars plus the empty jar.

- Capture: Use an empty jar to pick up a creature without harming it.

- Filled Jars: The jar item stores the captured mob and its color variant.
