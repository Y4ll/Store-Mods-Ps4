Demon Tools [JA+] is an animated resource pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition, specially designed to work with JA+ to deliver smoother, more immersive, and visually impressive tool animations. Inspired by demonic power and dark fantasy themes, this pack brings a unique visual style while preserving the recognizable identity of Minecraft's original materials.

One of the standout features of Demon Tools [JA+] is its vanilla-friendly color palette. Every material, including Wood, Stone, Iron, Gold, Diamond, and Netherite, retains colors that closely match the original Minecraft design. This allows players to instantly recognize their tools without confusion while enjoying a significantly enhanced visual experience.

The pack also features full All Tools support, giving every tool in the game its own animated appearance. From Swords, Pickaxes, Axes, Shovels, and Hoes to many other tool variations, each item has been carefully designed with smooth and detailed animations. These effects make every tool feel more alive whether you're exploring, mining, building, or battling in Survival and Creative mode.

In addition to its stunning animations, Demon Tools [JA+] is optimized to provide a smooth gameplay experience across a wide range of Minecraft Bedrock Edition devices. The visual enhancements are designed to improve immersion without negatively affecting normal gameplay.
