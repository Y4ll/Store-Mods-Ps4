Sonic's Gang Addon

Ever wished you could bring characters from Sonic the Hedgehog into Minecraft? This addon makes that happen. It adds three iconic characters straight into your world, giving your gameplay a fast and exciting twist.

THE MOBS:

Sonic:

Health: 500

Attack Damage: 10

Sonic is very fast that attacks any monster. If you give Sonic the Chaos Emeralds, Sonic will turn into Super Sonic.

Super Sonic:

Health: 1000

Attack Damage: 25

A more faster version of Sonic, this form can appear if you give Sonic the Chaos Emeralds.

Tails:

Health: 300

Attack Damage: 10

Tails is somewhat fast, he can fly and will have a blaster that will shoot fireballs.

Knuckles:

Health: 500

Attack Damage: 15

Knuckles' speed is comparable to Tails. This guy cannot take fall damage because he will just glide.

ITEMS:

Chaos Emeralds:

If given to Sonic, he will turn into a new form
