In the aftermath of the Black Hour, five silent survivors roam a world torn apart by the infected. Clad in torn hoodies, marked by blood and battle, and identified only by their vivid hair, these mysterious figures are built for immersive storytelling. Use them as NPCs or player skins to your apocalyptic maps, cities, and bunkers.

Story

When the world falls to betrayal, to war, to the infected some still choose to fight.

The collapse began not with zombies, but with bombs. Cities burned, governments fell, and then the dead started to rise.

The infection spread in three days.

Civilization collapsed in five.

And by Day 12... only five survivors emerged. Not heroes. Not soldiers. Just human beings pushed to the edge of extinction. But something inside them refuses to give up.

Before the Black Hour, they lived normal lives students, travelers, people with dreams. But everything changed when the outbreak began.

Now, five lone survivors roam the broken world. Their hair red, blue, purple, black, and white once told stories of personality. Now it only helps you remember whos still breathing.

Each bears the evidence of combat: bloodied bandages, deep wounds on shoulders, chests, feet, and arms. Some were injured in ambushes. Others in riots. One pulled shrapnel from their side after an airstrike.

Their clothes torn hoodies and heavy coats are all that shield them from the cold... the infected... or the next bullet.

Will your world survive or fall like the rest?
