This Health Bar add-on has color variations so you can detect enemy health without needing to see numbers.

Health color meanings:
Green = 80%+ (still plenty)
Yellow = 50%+ (about half)
Orange = 25%+ (1/4 health left)
Red = below 25% (almost dead)

This add-on does not include player.json.
This add-on does require experimental features. (please use API)
If you are missing dependencies, it means you havent downloaded the BP or RP.
