English

A Realistic Farming Revolution for Minecraft Bedrock

Agriculture Update is the first installment of a 3-part trilogy under the Wheel of Creation brand. Each major update in this series consists of two separate deliveries.

Included in this Repository:

- Agriculture Update I: The foundation. Introduces the core realistic farming system: biome-based climates, soil hydration, and plant growth cycles.

- Agriculture Update II: The expansion. This version contains all the mechanics of Update I, expanded with a diet system and a wider variety of crops to master.

What's Next?

The second chapter of the trilogy will be the Wild Update. I am currently working on Wild Update I, which focuses on a complete mob rework. Following that, Wild Update II will further expand the experience.

As for the third and final update of the trilogy... its details will remain a secret until Wild Update II is ready.

Inspiration & Credits

Inspired by TerraFirmaCraft This addon takes inspiration from some mechanics of the popular Java mod TerraFirmaCraft. However, this is NOT an official port. All systems have been completely reimagined and reinterpreted for Minecraft Bedrock Edition by HormigaDev.
