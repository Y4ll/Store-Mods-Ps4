Minecraft Bedrock Edition By DtA_MC

About the Baby Mod

This mod makes Minecraft survival much easier and more enjoyable. Hostile mobs behave differently, some lose their abilities, and some cannot even harm you properly.

Loot is improved, gameplay is faster, and the overall experience becomes less frustrating and more fun.

Features

- Baby Player Smaller character with a unique feel

- Nerfed Dangerous Mobs Creeper, Wither, Warden and more are controlled

- Unarmed Enemies Some mobs cannot properly attack

- Mob Knock Effect Mobs fly when they hit you

- No Negative Effects Poison, blindness etc. do nothing

- Auto Enchant Gear automatically gets strong enchantments

- Better Mining Blocks give useful loot

- OP Mob Drops Diamonds, emeralds, rare items

- Better Trades Villagers give useful items

- Free Boat System Enter water get a boat
