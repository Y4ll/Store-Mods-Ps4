I want to start by saying Suvile's Slick Pack is a passion project of mine, and I will be taking it at my own pace. However, I will ensure this pack is kept up to date with any new Minecraft versions, as well as with any of my other packs.

As seen in the trailer above, Suvile's Slick Pack introduces new textures for 53 blocks, 48 items, Low fire textures, UNO reverse card totem, Invisible helmets for servers, Rift ender pearl textures. updated hunger and health bars. Additionally, optional short swords are available in the pack settings.

Currently, I'm unsure of the direction I want to take this pack, so Ill be relying heavily on your feedback.

I originally posted this pack on MCPEDL and will continue to keep it updated there. However, moving forward, my primary focus for new projects will be on CurseForge.

If you enjoy the pack and want to contribute to future development, feel free to join my Discord! There, you can leave suggestions and vote on new textures.

If you run into any issues with the download feel free to join my discord to get access to direct mediafire links
