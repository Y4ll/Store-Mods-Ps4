Soul Land Dimension

Enter a forgotten dimension where powerful creatures, mysterious structures, ancient powers, and dangerous secrets await.

Soul Land Dimension is an adventure-focused Minecraft Bedrock addon built around exploration, powerful creatures, unique equipment, magical abilities, and a mysterious world filled with danger.

The Soul Land is not simply another place to explore. It is a hostile world where every discovery can lead to something far more dangerous than expected

Enter the Soul Land Dimension

The journey begins with the Wandering Trader.

Trade an Iron Ingot with the Wandering Trader to gain access to the Soul Land Dimension.

Once inside, you will discover a dark and mysterious environment filled with strange structures, dangerous creatures, hidden loot, and secrets waiting to be uncovered.

But entering is only the beginning.

How to Leave

Returning to the Overworld is not as simple as walking through a portal.

Use a Nether Star and hold/click the screen to return from the Soul Land Dimension.

Dangerous Creatures

Soul Land is inhabited by creatures with completely different abilities, behaviors, and mechanics.

Some can become powerful companions.

Others exist only to hunt you down.

And some should never be underestimated.

Pyroclast

A massive and powerful creature that towers over the player.

Pyroclast possesses deadly poisonous fangs and can become a useful companion when properly tamed.

Abilities & Features

Poison Fang
Its bite carries dangerous poisonous power, making it a threatening opponent.

Taming
Pyroclast can be tamed using Soul Sand.

Pyroclast Meat
Defeating Pyroclast can provide its meat.

Cooked Pyroclast Meat grants Fire Resistance.

Its meat can also be used to craft Warden Food.

Behavior Control
Use a Stick to control your Pyroclast.

You can command it to Sit or Stand.

Red Death

One of the rarest and most terrifying creatures within the Soul Land.

Red Death is a gigantic dragon associated with devastating flames and blood, making it one of the most dangerous creatures you can encounter.

But its true danger comes from its transformations.

Four States

Red Death possesses 4 different states.

With every transformation, its power increases.

Defeating one state does not necessarily mean the battle is over.

The Final State

When the final state of Red Death is defeated, it will merge into the player who dealt the killing blow.

Returning to the Overworld will cause Red Death to be automatically summoned as a friendly and tamed companion.

Taming

Red Death can be tamed using Beef.

Dragonhorn

Use a Dragonhorn to command Red Death and activate its powerful special abilities.

Beware the Red Death.

Only the strongest Soul Masters will be prepared for all four states.

Verdant Ravager

A massive bipedal dinosaur and one of the largest creatures in the Soul Land.

Its enormous size is surpassed only by the legendary Red Death.

Devastating Bite

The Verdant Ravager's bite is extremely powerful.

Its attacks can inflict Poison while heavily weakening its enemies.

Verdant Meat

Verdant Ravager can drop its meat when defeated.

Cooked Verdant Meat grants a Speed Boost.

Its meat can also be used to craft the powerful Warden Food.

Verdant Fang

Verdant Ravager has a rare chance to drop a Verdant Fang.

This fang can be used to craft the:

Verdant Ravager Sword

A powerful weapon dealing 25 damage per hit.

Its true strength comes from its poison mechanic.

Every successful strike gradually accumulates poison on the enemy.

The longer you continue attacking, the more dangerous the poison buildup becomes.

Glucose Aldehyde

A mysterious creature specializing in ranged combat.

When enemies approach, Glucose Aldehyde can continuously launch small fireballs at close range.

Rapid Fire

Instead of relying entirely on melee attacks, Glucose Aldehyde repeatedly fires small fireballs at nearby enemies.

Glucose Meat

Defeating this creature can provide Glucose Meat.
