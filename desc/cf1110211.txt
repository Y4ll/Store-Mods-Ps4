FAQ

HOW TO Setup?

Add resource & behavior pack, activate all experimental features, then play.

HOW TO Drink?

You have 2 different bottles: unpurified & purified.

1. Unpurified -

You get it like a vanilla water bottle, just hold the glass bottle & click on water.

- Gives you poison when you drink it.

- Craft it with any potion.

- Use it to craft an awkward potion like a water bottle.

2. Purified -

- Cook an unpurified bottle on a furnace or campfire, it'll be turned into purified.

- No harm when you drink it.

What happens if don't drink?

You will get the weakness effect and then be killed.
