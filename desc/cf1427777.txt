[ - Achievement-Friendly - ]

This addon is fully achievement-friendly and does not require Experimental APIs, ensuring full compatibility with standard Minecraft Bedrock worlds.

[ - Functionality - ]

Mythical Items enhances gameplay by adding visual particle effects to items based on their rarity. Common items remain simple, while rarer and more valuable items emit unique particles that reflect their tier, making loot more immersive and rewarding to find.
