3rd Person Name-Tag Visibility

Ever wanted to see your own name-tag while running around in third-person view? Whether you are recording a video, snapping the perfect screenshot, or just want to admire your character, this lightweight UI tweak has you covered!

Normally in Minecraft Bedrock Edition, your own name-tag disappears when you switch out of first-person. This pack changes the internal UI files to keep your name perfectly rendered and visible above your head in both third-person back and third-person front perspectives.

Features

Self Name-Tag Visibility: Fully enables your personal name-tag to render flawlessly whenever you switch to a third-person camera angle.

Clean & Minimalist: Built with clean aesthetics in mind. It blends seamlessly with the vanilla game without adding any unnecessary clutter to your HUD.

Content Creator Essential: Ideal for recording intros, outros, or cinematic gameplay shots where you want your username clearly displayed.

Multiplayer Ready: Works flawlessly on servers like The Hive, making it a great addition for BedWars mains or casual PvP matches.

Compatibility

This pack is carefully built using JSON UI modifications and is incredibly lightweight. It is fully compatible with the RenderDragon engine and works smoothly across all devices, including mobile (Android/iOS) and Windows.
