Stay close or get pulled. Chained Together links every player on the server with a visible rope or chain wander too far and you get yanked back. A chaotic, fun multiplayer experience where teamwork is not optional.

Features

- Rope or Chain style each player picks their own visual style on join; switch anytime from the menu

- 6-block leash length move too far apart and the rope starts pulling you back toward each other

- Hard snap at 24 blocks get too far and you are instantly teleported back together

- Manual connect/disconnect choose exactly which players to chain together, or cut specific connections

- Chain Everyone mode instantly links all players in a line with one tap, no manual setup needed

- Cross-dimension pull if a chained player enters another dimension, they get pulled back automatically

- Remove All Chains instantly free everyone with a single button

- No cheats required works fully in survival mode

Commands

- /voxen:ct open the Chained Together menu

How to Use

PC: Type /voxen:ct in chat or right-click to open the menu. Pick your style and connect to players.
Mobile: Type /voxen:ct in chat to open the menu. Pick your style and connect to players.

On first join, a style picker will appear automatically choose Rope or Chain and you are instantly linked.

Achievement Friendly?

Yes fully achievement friendly. Use it without any worries.
