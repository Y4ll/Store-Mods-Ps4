- How can I get the Furnitures?

There are what we call POLY-CLASS BLOCKS it's purpose is meant give better category for all the blocks in the game, (WHICH IS THOUSANDS) you can also use these blocks for decorating.

"Blocky Furniture Crate Recipe"

-Contains All Interior Decorations

"Garden Crate"

-Contains All Outdoor/Garden Related Furnitures

"Halloween Furniture Box"

-Contains All Halloween Related Furnitures

"The Specials Box"

-If you've known my other projects, specifically my custom adventure maps, all blocks from those maps will be found here.

"Sign Board Pakage"

-Contains All Letters, Numericals and Signages Furnitures.

For your convience every POLY-CLASS BLOCKS is craftable in any wood types, if it uses wood.

(except bamboo)

After Crafting the POLY-CLASS BLOCK use a Stonecutter to get the furnitures. (it's a classic for furniture mods)
