About

Cut down whole trees without slowly breaking every log.

Tree Capitator Lite automatically fells the connected trunk after you break one log with an axe. It keeps the experience lightweight and close to vanilla: axes lose durability, Unbreaking is respected, leaves decay naturally, and sneaking lets you chop individual blocks whenever you need precise control.

Features

- Whole-tree chopping break one log with an axe to fell up to 64 connected logs of the same wood family.

- Build protection matching leaves or Nether wart blocks must be nearby, helping prevent accidental destruction of log-only structures.

- Vanilla tool wear every additional log can consume axe durability, with full Unbreaking support.

- Precision override sneak while chopping to break only the selected block.

- Broad tree support works with oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, pale oak, crimson, and warped trees, including stripped variants.

- Natural leaf decay only logs are removed; leaves and their drops continue to use normal Minecraft behavior.

- Multiplayer friendly works independently for every player without menus or commands.

- Lightweight one Behavior Pack, no recurring world scans, custom items, or configuration screens.

How to use

- Import the .mcaddon file into Minecraft Bedrock.

- Activate the Tree Capitator Lite Behavior Pack for your world.

- Break a tree log with an axe while standing normally.

- Sneak while chopping whenever you want normal single-block breaking.

Notes

- The add-on searches upward from the chopped block and removes no more than 64 logs per tree.

- Extremely large trees whose canopy is beyond the search limit may be left unchanged as a safety measure.

- Wooden structures without nearby matching leaves are not treated as trees.

- Creative mode removes the connected logs without spawning drops or damaging the axe.

- No experimental toggles are required.

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.30 or newer

- Tree Capitator Lite Behavior Pack must be active

- No external add-on dependencies required
