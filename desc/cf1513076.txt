Manhunt AI Bot v1.0.2

A Real AI Hunter for Minecraft Bedrock

Turn any Minecraft Bedrock world into a complete single-player Manhunt challenge.

Manhunt AI Bot adds one or more intelligent hunters that can track you, gather resources, craft tools, upgrade equipment, build routes, survive dangerous terrain and fight using player-like tactics.

This is not a normal mob with increased speed.

The hunter has its own decision system and can change its goal depending on what is happening in the world. It may chase you directly, stop to gather blocks, craft a missing tool, mine iron, escape a trap, build upward or prepare better equipment before attacking again.

Version v1.0.2 is a major rebuild of the original v0.7.0 addon.

How to Start a Hunt

- Install and activate both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack.

- Enter the world in Creative Mode.

- Get the Hunter Compass:

/give @s manhunt:hunter_compass

- Use the compass to open the Manhunt configuration menu.

- Select the hunter count, difficulty, mode, preparation style and other options.

- Start the hunt and survive.

The addon handles the countdown, hunter spawning, tracking, win conditions, deaths, respawns and end-of-hunt statistics.

Intelligent Goal-Based AI

The hunter now uses a central decision system instead of following only one chase script.

Possible goals include:

- Chase the runner

- Attack the runner

- Gather wood

- Gather food

- Gather building blocks

- Mine stone

- Mine ores

- Craft tools

- Smelt resources

- Upgrade equipment

- Build a route

- Climb toward the runner

- Escape water

- Escape lava

- Heal and eat

- Follow a portal

- Share resources with another hunter

- Recover from being stuck

The hunter commits to its selected task and can temporarily disable normal chase behaviour while gathering, crafting, mining or building.

If an action fails repeatedly, the AI can abandon it and generate a different plan.

Resource Gathering and Progression

Hunters can begin with an empty inventory and progress through the world naturally.

The normal progression includes:

Logs Planks Crafting Table Sticks Wooden Tools Stone Tools Furnace Iron Equipment

Hunters can gather:

- Logs

- Planks

- Dirt

- Stone

- Cobblestone

- Food

- Coal

- Raw iron

- Gold

- Flint

- Obsidian

- Building blocks

- Other progression resources

The AI understands tool requirements.

Examples:

- Uses an axe for logs

- Uses a pickaxe for stone

- Requires a stone pickaxe or better for iron

- Requires suitable tools for valuable ores

- Avoids mining blocks that cannot produce the expected resource

- Avoids mining directly beneath itself without a safe plan

- Rejects unreachable or completely buried targets

- Avoids exposed lava, unstable sand and dangerous gravel

Advanced mining can be enabled or disabled in the settings.

Complete Crafting System

Hunters can craft and manage their own progression.

Supported equipment includes:

- Wooden sword

- Wooden axe

- Wooden pickaxe

- Stone sword

- Stone axe

- Stone pickaxe

- Iron sword

- Iron axe

- Iron pickaxe

- Crafting table

- Furnace

- Shield

- Iron helmet

- Iron chestplate

- Iron leggings

- Iron boots

- Bucket

- Water bucket

- Flint and steel

- Bow

- Arrows

- Boat

- Torches

- Cooked food

- Golden apple when enough resources are available

The crafting planner reserves important materials instead of spending everything immediately.

For example, the hunter can preserve iron for a shield, bucket or pickaxe before using the remaining iron on armour.

Building and Vertical Pursuit

The hunter can build when normal pathfinding cannot reach the runner.

Building options include:

- Horizontal bridging

- Diagonal staircase building

- Spiral staircase planning

- Controlled vertical pillaring

- Reusing existing bridges

- Reusing existing staircases

- Breaking runner pillars

- Emergency block clutching

- Safe route recovery

Vertical pursuit follows a priority system.
