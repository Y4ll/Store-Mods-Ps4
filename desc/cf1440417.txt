Ever thought about the most common block on the map being the key to everything? With this mod, that dirt you always throw away turns into literally anything. There are over 1800+ new recipes where you only need one block of dirt to create whatever you want, from a bed to the rarest netherite items. The goal here is to stop wasting time mining and jump straight into the fun.

Since this is the first version (v2.0), there is an important detail: because there are so many recipes using the same dirt block, the system sometimes gets confused. If you try to craft too fast, the item might change at the last second, like wanting a sword and getting a boat instead. If you want to craft an item twice, for the second craft you must click on the item again to make sure you get the right one.
