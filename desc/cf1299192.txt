Whats Kitchenette?

Kitchenette comes with all the little details that makes your kitchen feel cozy and complete. Youll find cute counters, shelves, cabinets, and other decorative pieces that add vibrance and personality to your builds. Its a simple way to make your kitchen look much better ~

Note that this is for decoration and aesthetic purposes only.

How to Use Kitchenette?

You have to activate both resource and behavior pack of Kitchenette from your world settings. Then, you can get the items from your Equipment Inventory. All the inclusions have an AE tag.
