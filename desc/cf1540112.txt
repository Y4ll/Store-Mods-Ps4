Fake Hardcore Hearts

Hardcore looks. Your usual survival experience.

Give your Minecraft Bedrock health bar a bold new look with Fake Hardcore Hearts by Baku. This resource pack replaces normal heart textures with hardcore-style hearts while keeping your gameplay unchanged.

Features

- Hardcore-Style Hearts: Enjoy the distinctive hardcore heart design in your regular survival worlds.

- Matching Health States: Includes full hearts, half hearts, and damage-flash textures.

- Status Effect Support: Matching textures for poison, wither, freezing, and absorption hearts.

- Lightweight Design: A texture-only pack with no scripts or behavior changes.

- Vanilla-Inspired Appearance: Crisp pixel textures that complement Minecrafts existing HUD.

Installation

- Download and open the .mcpack file with Minecraft.

- Wait for the resource pack import to finish.

- Open your worlds Resource Packs settings and activate the pack.

- Place it above other packs that change heart textures, then enter your world.

Important Information

This is a visual resource pack only. It does not enable Hardcore mode, increase difficulty, change your maximum health, or prevent respawning. Other HUD or heart texture packs may override its appearance.
