No Nether Fog, the best texture pack for removing fog in nether and increasing render distance without creating much lag.

Note that this texture pack varies on your render distance. By default render distance is fixed in areas like water, lava etcetera, it doesn't matter that your render distance is 5 or 96 in lava or water.
