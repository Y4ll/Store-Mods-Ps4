What you get

A proper advancement screen. Five tabs Minecraft, Nether, The End, Adventure, Husbandry each with its own background and its own branching tree. Tap any advancement to read what it wants and what it pays; the panel opens right beside the window, so you can browse the whole tree without backing out once.

Three kinds of goal, three kinds of frame. Square for a normal task, round for a goal, spiked star for a challenge. Locked ones sit dark, earned ones light up white, gold and purple.

Toast notifications. Finish something and a panel slides in from the top right with the right sound and the right wording: Advancement Made!, Goal Reached!, Challenge Complete! Earn five things at once and you get five toasts in a row, not one on top of another.

Announcements. Every unlock is called out in chat the way Java does it green for tasks and goals, purple for challenges so your friends know exactly what you just pulled off.

Secret advancements. A handful stay hidden behind ??? until the moment you earn them.
