KI: THE PARALLEL

About The Addon

The Parallel (v2.2.5) is an atmospheric psychological horror addon created for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Unlike typical monster mods that spam heavy entities and lag your world, The Parallel focuses on dread, paranoia, and unpredictable stalker mechanics. You are never safe, and every shadow could be watching you.

Designed and optimized directly on mobile devices to ensure maximum performance (under 4MB) without lagging your game or breaking vanilla mechanics.

Key Features

Dynamic Stalker Mechanics: Entities that observe you from a distance, hunting your peace of mind and disappearing when you least expect it.

Mind Games & Survival: Not every creature behaves the way you expect. Learning how to deal with each encounter is crucial to stay alive.

Ultra-Optimized Performance: Built cleanly to keep file sizes extremely light, delivering maximum terror even on low-end mobile devices.

Pure Vanilla Horror: Blends smoothly into standard survival gameplay to catch you completely off guard.

Warning & Recommendations

Brightness Settings: For the best experience, lower your in-game brightness to Moody.

Multiplayer Compatible: Test your survival skills alongside your friendsor watch them panic.
