Enjoy Bare Bones even more customizing it the way you like it!

This texture pack modifies the leaves in Bare Bones to make them more like the ones in Vanilla Minecraft! Now you can see the sun go through them with Vibrant Visuals or spot enemies hiding being the trees!

Now Compatible with Wilderness Bound!!

Watch the official release trailer here!

Use them with Vibrant Visuals!

The new leaves take advantage of Vibrant Visuals and the way the sun shines through them, go and take pictures of beautiful landscapes! Here is an example of how they can look in-game!

Watch a preview of the leaves and how they work with Bare Bones & Stuff!

The new leaves can be used with Bare Bones & Stuff! You just have to put Bare Bones Leafier Leaves above it! This way the leaves in your hand can look like the block you're placing to avoid breaking the immersion!
