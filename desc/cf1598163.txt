NEW CROSSBOWS OVERHAUL

New Crossbows Overhaul completely rebuilds the crossbow experience with 8 custom crossbows, themed arrows, unique projectile visuals, powerful scripted abilities and spectacular particle effects made for players who want every shot to feel massive.

Bambu Crossbow

Breeze Crossbow

Demon Fire Crossbow

Rascal Crossbow
