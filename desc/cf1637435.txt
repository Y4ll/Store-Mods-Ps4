Ores Dimension adds 10 unique dimensions to Minecraft Bedrock one for every major ore. Each dimension has its own portal, custom trees with ore-themed logs, planks and leaves, and a completely unique environment to explore.

Dimensions

- Coal Dimension

- Copper Dimension

- Iron Dimension

- Gold Dimension

- Redstone Dimension

- Lapis Dimension

- Quartz Dimension

- Emerald Dimension

- Diamond Dimension

- Netherite Dimension

How to Build Portal

Use any block and build a portal frame then which ever dimension you're willing to open portal for, use that ore to activate that dimension portal

Each Dimension Includes

- Unique portal to enter the dimension

- Custom ore-themed trees, logs, stripped logs, wood, planks and leaves

Important

This addon requires Beta APIs to be enabled in your world settings. Go to World Settings > Experiments and turn on Beta APIs. The addon will not work without it.
