SERP Additions (Mining and Cooking Update) is a complementary add-on for Serp Pokedrock, designed to expand the game's content with new mining, cooking, advanced crafting, and quality-of-life mechanics, while maintaining a consistent and polished aesthetic.

For a complete visual experience, this add-on is designed to work seamlessly with the SERP Reworked Texture Pack.

Daycare:

A new breeding system has been created. With the new Day Care machine, you can store up to 6 Pokemon to breed. You can also add up to 2 other people to bring their Pokemon and create the perfect Pokemon.(To open the menu, you must have the Pokedex in your hand and press the action button.)

(A chest must be placed to the left of the machine so that the eggs can be generated inside)

This won't work if the player moves too far away and isn't charged. To prevent this, use this command standing next to the machine to keep it charged. It may or may not cause lag and can only be used 10 times. (Change where it says "(Name)" and put something like daycare1)

Comand: /tickingarea add circle ~~~ 2 (Name)

New Pokemon eggs:

These eggs will hatch from the Day Care when two Pokemon of the same species or from the same Egg Group are placed there (there's a book to determine which Pokemon belong to each Egg Group, which can be obtained at the crafting table). Each egg has a predefined hatching time of 2 minutes. These eggs must be in your inventory at all times for the timer to count down. If they are placed in a bag or any other storage container, the time will not decrease. Each egg displays all the information about the Pokemon that will hatch:

(They can also be created manually using an object called Egg Editor in the creative menu)

More Equipables:

Eight new equippable items have been added to Pokemon, along with the Shiny Charm (which increases the chance of encountering Shiny Pokemon both in the Day Care and in the wild when held in your inventory). These items will help train Pokemon's EVs more efficiently (in the Day Care, they will pass on a specific IV from the parent holding the item, and the Everstone will pass on the Pokemon's Nature). The Everstone will prevent the Pokemon from evolving, which is for those who like their Pokemon just as it is.

All the items are found at the crafting table; the Everstone is dropped by Geodudes when they are defeated.
