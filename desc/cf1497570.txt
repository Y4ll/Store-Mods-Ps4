Haebi's Enhanced Pets (This add-on is achievement-friendly!)

100% Compatibility & Requirements: This add-on is purely Behavior Pack (BP) driven and does NOT require a Resource Pack (RP).

It is fully compatible with any texture packs or shaders you currently enjoy using!

Please note that this add-on requires Minecraft Bedrock version 1.26 or higher to run properly.

Our first pet AI enhancement is the Wolf!

Have your battles with wolves ever not gone as planned?

Wolves are incredibly brave, but they often lack situational awareness and can die in an instant.

If you're tired of adopting new wolves after every tragic accident and want to enjoy a long, safe Minecraft life with your beloved pet, give this add-on a try!

We have massively boosted the wolf's survivability and completely overhauled its combat AI.

1.0.1 NEW PET!!

The Deadly Assassin 'Ocelot'

You can finally tame Ocelots! (Just use some Raw Cod or Raw Salmon ).

Combat AI : They will actively attack enemies that hit you or targets you attack. They also have the intelligence to immediately counterattack if they are hit first.

Pounce! They'll leap at their targets and deal melee strikes with a 1.3x speed boost.

Tactical Retreat: If things get too dicey and their HP drops below 30%, they'll smartly disengage and fall back. Once you heal them back up to 80% or more, they'll jump right back into the action!

Buffed Up: Tamed Ocelots are tough! They boast 30 Max HP, 12 Attack Damage, 0.5 Knockback Resistance, and even a Lifesteal ability so they don't go down easily. Oh, and they run even faster than Wolves!

Growing Up: Once tamed, they bulk up and become 1.15x larger!

The Healer 'Cat'

AoE Healing System: Cats now have a
magical "Purr" ability! They heal you and your allied pets (Wolves, Ocelots) within a 12-block radius. They'll automatically detect anyone who isn't at full health and patch them up.

Healing Rhythms: They restore 2 HP every 3 seconds while standing around, and a faster 2 HP every 2 seconds if you make them sit and focus.

More Health: Max HP bumped up to 20 so your little healers can survive better.

Golden Dandelion Support: 100% implemented the newest vanilla feature! You can pause or resume the growth of any baby pet, totally without needing any experimental toggles.

1.0.2 NEW PET!!

The Feathered Hitchhiker 'Parrot'

Enhanced Stats: Health increased to 10, 50% damage reduction, and 50% knockback resistance. Immune to fall damage.

Intelligent Multi-Mount: Parrots can now ride not only on the player's shoulders but also on the backs of tamed wolves and ocelots.

Emergency Evacuation: Automatically dismounts and takes flight if the mount (player/pet) enters combat or touches water/lava. (Includes a 5-second re-mount cooldown).

Intuitive Controls:

From Shoulder: Sneak with an empty hand to dismount.

From Pet: Simply touch the parrot (or the pet) with an empty hand to make it sit on the ground.

Improved Follow AI: Flight speed increased by 1.5x, tighter follow distance, and optimized teleportation range.

Realistic Diet: Can be healed using Apples, Melon Slices, Sweet Berries, and Glow Berries.

The Aqua Vanguard 'Axolotl'

Intuitive 3-State FSM Modes: Easily control your axolotl's behavior to suit your current situation.

Wait Mode: Stays obediently in place and rests.

Free Mode: Roams and swims freely around the area, acting independently without strictly following the owner.

Follow Mode: Stays close to the owner and actively assists in combat by sharing the owner's targets.

Enhanced Stats & Survival: Increased Health and Attack damage to make them more reliable guardians of the sea that won't die easily.

Persistent Follow AI: Improved pathfinding in both water and on land to ensure they stay close to their owner while in Follow mode.

Status Indicator: Real-time health conditions can be monitored via the Health Bar UI.
