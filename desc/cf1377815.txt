Bring your mining adventures to life with Animated Glowing Ores! Every ore is outlined, illuminated, and smoothly animated turning the dull stone walls of your cave into a vibrant treasure map.This pack makes all ores easy to spot, even in pitch-black caves. Each ore type has its own unique glowing color and a subtle pulse animation, so youll instantly recognize diamonds, redstone, and emeralds from afar.

Features:

- Full animation + glowing outlines for every ore (Overworld + Deepslate).

- Works perfectly with OptiFine or emissive texture support.

- Optimized for visibility find ores quickly without breaking immersion.

- Compatible with most shaders and other texture packs.
