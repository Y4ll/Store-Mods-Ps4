A modScript/Addon that allows you to have the same inventory as the rest of the players in your world, if a player dies and loses inventory you will also lose inventory, if a player gets diamonds you will also have those diamonds.

How to play

You just have to venture into a normal survival with your friends and learn to share your inventory.

If a player dies and loses their inventory, you will also lose your inventory.
The durability of the items will also be the same.
Compatible with any Add-On/Script
