Break one log. Drop the whole tree. Voxen Tree Capitator lets you chop any tree instantly by breaking a single log with your axe trunk, branches, and all. No more breaking logs one by one.

How to Use

Hold any axe, break one log of a tree the entire tree falls. Type /voxen:tc in chat to open the settings menu anytime.

Achievement Friendly?

Yes fully achievement friendly. Voxen Tree Capitator does not disable achievements or lock your world. Play survival the way you want, earn everything you would normally.

Supported Wood Types

Works with every wood type Oak, Spruce, Birch, Jungle, Acacia, Dark Oak, Mangrove, Cherry, Pale Oak, and Nether stems (Crimson & Warped). Also works on stripped logs and bark blocks.

Smart Build Protection

- Your builds are safe only chops logs that have leaves attached

- Confirmation prompt if build blocks are detected nearby

- Natural terrain never triggers false warnings

Extra Features

- Auto-Mine Just look at a tree while holding an axe, it chops itself

- Auto-Collect All drops go straight into your inventory

- Auto-Replant A matching sapling is planted back automatically after chopping

- Break Leaves Optionally remove leaves along with logs

- Axe Durability Axe loses durability per log broken, balanced for survival

- Max Block Limit Adjustable cap (161024) to keep giant trees lag-free

Works with Modded Trees Too

Auto-detects custom tree blocks from other addons using standard naming and block tags. Most modded trees work with zero setup.

In-Game Menu /voxen:tc

- Toggle Tree Capitator on or off

- Enable or disable each axe type individually

- Adjust all settings and the max block slider

- Reset to default with one tap asks before resetting

All settings save to the world they stay after restarts and apply for every player on the server.
