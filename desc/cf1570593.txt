Vibrant RTX Visuals is my sharpest pack. Hard volumetric clouds, strong contrast and clean shadows across the whole landscape. Saturated color on top of all of it.

What this changes

Volumetric clouds

Clouds have real shape and thickness instead of sitting flat against the sky. This is the thing people notice first and the reason screenshots from this pack hold up as wallpapers.

Contrast and depth

Every block is separated harder from the one next to it. Edges read clearly, surfaces do not blend together, and the world stops looking like one flat sheet at midday.

Shadows

Smooth shadow casting across the entire landscape, not just under trees. Hills throw shadows onto the ground behind them properly.

Sky

Rich clear blue that stays clean rather than washing out, so the clouds have something solid to sit against.

Grass and foliage

Deeply saturated greens. Not neon, but well past vanilla, and held in check by the contrast so it never turns into a smear.

Terrain detail

Tall grass and ground surfaces stay crisp up close instead of softening off when you walk up to them.

Requirements

THIS PACK IS FOR VIBRANT VISUALS ONLY
IT DOES NOT WORK WITH MB LOADER

Vibrant Visuals must be ON. This pack is built for Bedrock's Vibrant Visuals renderer. The pack will not do anything if it is off.

Where to turn it on: Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals

If that toggle is not there, your device or version does not support Vibrant Visuals yet.

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it. Minecraft imports it on its own.

- In your world go to Settings > Global Resources and activate Vibrant RTX Visuals

- Turn on Settings > Video > Vibrant Visuals

- If the lighting still looks the same, leave the world and load it again once

FAQ

The pack is not working. Nothing changed after I installed it.
I get this comment a lot, and almost every single time the reason is the same. Vibrant Visuals is turned off.

This pack only works when Vibrant Visuals is ON. That is written clearly at the top of this page. With it off, the pack loads fine but nothing on your screen changes, because there is no renderer for it to work with. The pack is not broken.

Go to Settings > Video > Graphics > Vibrant Visuals and switch it on. If you cannot find that option at all, your device or Minecraft version does not support it yet.

Please check this before commenting that the pack does not work.
