About

Store your death items in a compact gravestone instead of scattering them across the ground.

When you die in Survival or Adventure mode, Gravestone stores your items in a small grave marker. It looks for nearby safe ground if you die in lava, the void, or an enclosed space. Interact with the grave to recover your items through a simple menu.

Features

- Creates a compact gravestone at or near the death location

- Moves the grave to safe ground after deaths in lava, the void, or enclosed terrain

- Stores inventory, armor, and offhand items, including any item held in the offhand

- Restores items from new graves to their original inventory, hotbar, armor, and offhand slots when those slots are available

- Leaves items that do not fit in a full grave with the player

- Makes graves private to their owner and world operators by default; operators can allow public access

- Lets you locate your latest grave or list recorded graves with chat commands

- Creates a small rescue platform after a void death in the End when no safe ground is available

- Uses a custom grave model instead of a bulky storage block

- Compact recovery UI with Take All, Inspect Items, and Drop Items actions

- Inspect Items view shows item stacks with icons

- Keeps remaining items in the gravestone if the player inventory is full

- Removes the gravestone when all stored items are recovered

- Includes English and Russian menus and messages

How to use

Install the add-on and enable it on your world.

When you die, look for the grave near your death location. If that spot is unsafe, check the coordinates and dimension shown in chat. Interact with the grave to recover your items.

Use Take All to recover everything that fits, or Inspect Items to recover individual stacks. If your inventory is full, the rest stays in the grave.

Use /grave to locate your latest recorded grave and /graves to list your recorded graves. A world operator can use /gravepublic true to allow anyone to open graves, or /gravepublic false to restore private access.

Notes

Gravestone uses an entity with its own inventory, so it does not place a chest or replace nearby terrain. The End void rescue feature may add a small platform in empty space.

Graves made before 1.1.0 remain recoverable. Original slot restoration applies to new graves; an older grave appears in the recorded list after you interact with it.

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.0 or newer

- No additional behavior pack dependency required
