Lower Shield reduces the height of the shield in first-person perspective, lowering its position to clear your vision so you can see your enemies and the environment perfectly.

It modifies both hands, making the shield practically disappear from the left side when placed in the off-hand to grant the maximum field of view possible. Essential for intense PvP combat, cross-play battles, or standard survival gameplay.
