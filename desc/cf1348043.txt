Conquest Structure is part of the TerraDraconis Addon Series a collection of addons that come together to create a vast, living world filled with dragons, settlements, and immersive gameplay. While other addons in the series focus on hunting, taming, or combat, this addon is all about building and bringing your world to life.

This addon expands your creative possibilities with a wide variety of building blocks in different shapes and sizes, inspired by the Conquest Reforged mod on Java (but not a direct port). Whether youre constructing mighty fortresses, cozy villages, or sprawling medieval towns, these blocks allow you to add incredible depth and detail to your creations.

Highlights include:

Hundreds of new block shapes for advanced building

Medieval-inspired design perfect for castles, fortresses, and rustic builds

Full compatibility with most texture packs and addons

ALL THE BLOCKS CAN BE OBTAIN VIA STONECUTTER

Optimized to shine alongside medieval-style packs, especially the Conquest Texture Pack (Bedrock port of Conquest Reforged, available on the Marketplace)

One of the standout features of Conquest Structure is its seamless integration into the TerraDraconis world. Build hunter settlements, craft defensive fortifications, or design immersive medieval towns all to stand against the dragons that roam the land.
