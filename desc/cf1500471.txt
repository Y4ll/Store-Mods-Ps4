In-Game Settings

Tap the gear icon next to the pack in your pack list:

- Particle Lifetime (0100%) how long each effect particle survives. 100 is vanilla timing; lower values clear them off screen sooner so fewer draw at once; 0 removes them entirely

- Potion & Spell Particles turn off the swirls around potion effects, witches, evokers and cauldrons

- Bubble Columns turn off the bubbles in soul sand and magma columns. Max FPS already removes them

- XP Orb Render stop experience orbs drawing. They still work and still get collected, so this is a big gain at grinders

How to Install

- Download the .mcpack file below

- Tap/click to open Minecraft will import it automatically

- Go to Settings Global Resources Activate XiaoBoost Ultimate

- Tap the pack name to choose your tier (Default or Max FPS)

- Tap the gear icon to open the settings panel and tune particles

- Enjoy smoother gameplay!

Tip: Place XiaoBoost Ultimate at the top of your resource pack list for best compatibility with other packs.
