Craftable Structures Addon

Tired of spending hours (or even days) building a starter base, while using Creative mode completely ruins the survival experience?

Craftable Structures bridges the gap perfectly! Earn your buildings legitimately by gathering resources, but skip the tedious block-by-block placement. This addon introduces a smart blueprint system that allows you to craft, preview, and instantly deploy massive structures directly in your vanilla survival world.

Key Features

100% Survival Friendly No cheating. You must gather every single block required for the final structure.

Visual Particle Outlines Placing a Structure Anchor creates a clear grid of blue or red particles. You can see the exact dimensions of the building before deploying it.

Smart Rotation Sneak and interact with the Anchor to instantly rotate the particle outline by 90.

Smart Auto-Deposit Don't want to click a hundred times? The Blueprint UI automatically scans and pulls required materials from any chests within a 2-block radius.

Flexible Materials Missing Oak? Use Spruce or Birch instead. Out of Cobblestone? The system easily accepts Deepslate or regular Stone. It completely adapts to your inventory!

Safety & Anti-Grief Systems Made a mistake? Disassemble the blueprint to get your materials back (costs a small XP penalty). Obstacle detection also prevents spawning structures inside mountains.

How it Works (Step-by-Step)

1. Craft a Blueprint Surround a structure item (like a book, glass, etc.) with 8 Papers in a Crafting Table.

2. Check the Required Resources Use the blueprint item in the air to open the menu and see the exact block checklist.

3. Deposit Materials Gather the resources in your inventory or stand next to your chests and click the Auto-Deposit All button.

4. Get the Anchor Once the blueprint reaches 100% completion, it transforms into a *Structure Anchor*.

5. Build the Structure:

* Place the Anchor on the ground to activate the particle grid outline.

* Sneak + Interact to rotate the building.

* Interact without sneaking to confirm and deploy! (You have 60 seconds before the preview expires).

Important: Make sure to enable Experimental Toggles in your world settings for all features of this addon to function correctly!
