Jujutsu Reincarnation is an add-on that lets you experience the power of each character from Jujutsu Kaisen. Each character comes with their own unique abilities, techniques, and fighting styles inspired by the anime.

You can use the limitless power of Gojo, controlling space with techniques like Infinity and unleashing devastating attacks such as Hollow Purple. His abilities allow you to dominate battles with overwhelming strength and precision.

You can also experience the lightning-based power of Kashimo, a powerful sorcerer known for his electric cursed energy. His attacks are fast, destructive, and focused on close combat, making every fight intense and explosive.

The item you use will be visible in first person and will not be visible in third person.

How to use the addon

-

Convert a Nether Star into Jujutsu Skills.

-

Hold the Item and sneak to activate the skill or just hold the Item.

The gameplay is intentionally designed to be very simple, because this addon is mainly created for mobile users. This control system makes it easier to use psychic abilities on mobile devices. For PC players, using it should be even easier.

Available Characters

Gojo Satoru

Hold the item and sneak to pull enemies toward you and engage them in combat. Stop sneaking to push them away.

Kashimo

Lightning Stats Boost: enhances your combat abilities with the power of lightning.
Lightning Hand: casts lightning onto targets through your melee attacks.
Paralyze Effect: has a 30% chance to be applied to your target and lasts for 3 seconds.

Yuta Okkotsu

Stats Boost: increases your overall combat performance and physical abilities.
