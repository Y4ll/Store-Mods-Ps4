The Shovel is a horror add-on about the fear we all have: work. Try to escape from a shovel that wants to give you a job application, The add-on has several functions, such as missions or events, like one which is inspired by the "Evil Hunter - by PnTMC" add-on, a really good add-on with a great story, it also has Other events, like the shovel hunt, where the shovel starts chasing you and you have to run for your life
