Tree Capitator Fast Tree Chopping Addon

Welcome to Tree Capitator by BAKU, a lightweight Minecraft Bedrock addon created to make wood collection faster, easier, and more efficient.

With this addon, you can break a single log and automatically chop the entire connected tree. It is perfect for survival players who want to save time while gathering wood without changing the natural Minecraft experience.

Addon Features

- Full Tree Chopping Break one log to chop the entire connected tree.

- Faster Wood Collection Collect large amounts of wood in seconds.

- Connected Leaf Breaking Leaves connected to the tree are cleared automatically.

- Survival Friendly Designed to improve survival gameplay without making it complicated.

- Bedrock Optimized Smooth performance on mobile, Windows, and other Bedrock platforms.

How to Use

- Install and activate the addon in your world.

- Hold an axe or your preferred tool.

- Sneak and break one log from the tree.

- The connected logs and leaves will automatically break.

Gameplay Benefits

- Saves Time No need to break every log separately.

- Better Building Progress Gather wood quickly for houses, farms, and large projects.

- Useful for Large Forests Clear trees efficiently while exploring.

- Lightweight Performance Minimal impact on gameplay performance.
