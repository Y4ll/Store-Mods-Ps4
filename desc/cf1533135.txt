Fire Theme Swords adds 10 craftable fire swords to Minecraft Bedrock. Each hit sets the target on fire, and the stronger swords burn longer. Made by The_Zahin_GAMER.

Version 2.1.1 Ignition

All 10 swords appear in the Creative Equipment tab.

A hit sets the mob on fire.

Solar Judgement and the Infernal Sovereign Blade also ignite nearby mobs.

Health and speed buffs fade a few seconds after you put the sword away.

Prismatic Inferno, Solar Judgement, and the Infernal Sovereign Blade have an enchanted glint. Higher swords also give extra health while held. The last two give a speed boost.

How to get them

Craft the Ashen Doomblade, then upgrade one sword into the next. The crafting grid is sword-shaped.

You can also upgrade at a smithing table. Put a fire charge in the template slot, the previous sword in the middle, and the next material in the last slot. Smithing keeps the enchants.

In Creative, open the Equipment tab. They are listed after the vanilla tools.

Install

Download the .mcaddon file.

Open it with Minecraft.

In world settings, turn on both Fire Theme Swords packs. The behavior pack and the resource pack must both be active.

Leave the world and enter it again.

Needs Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.50 or newer. If a hit does not ignite, turn on Beta APIs in that worlds settings and reload.
