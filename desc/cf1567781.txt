THE STALKERS

Something is out there... watching.

The Stalkers is a strange and terrifying entity that prefers to remain unseen, silently observing players from the darkness. It has one unusual weakness: it cannot move while someone is looking directly at it..

As long as you keep your eyes on it, nothing happens..

But once your attention slips away...

It starts moving..

Relentlessly..

The Stalkers can appear almost anywheredeep forests, dark caves, forgotten ruins, or just beyondyour safety home after sunset. You may notice it standing perfectly still between the trees or staring through a distant window..

And every time you look away, it seems to get closer.......
