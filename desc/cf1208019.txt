Balls Addon

Created by Daniye

Bring sports to Minecraft with the Balls add-on! This pack introduces realistic basketballs and soccer balls that you can dribble, kick, and shoot using simple mechanics. Play a quick soccer match with friends or set up a basketball court and sink some shots. Whether you're scoring goals or making three-pointers, this add-on adds a whole new way to play in Minecraft!
