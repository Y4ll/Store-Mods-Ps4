This pack brings nature to life with enhanced weather and ambient particle effects. Experience better rain with realistic splashes and puddles forming during storms, butterflies fluttering naturally, and improved falling leaves that react with physics drifting, landing flat on the ground, or gently floating on water. All these details combine to create a more immersive and atmospheric Minecraft world!

Better Falling Leaves

This texture pack adds more variety to falling leaf particles and enhances their physicsthey now land on the ground, drift in water, and make your worlds feel more alive.

Better Rain, Splashes & Puddles!

This texture pack enhances rain by improving textures and adding ripples, splashes, and puddles for a more realistic, lively world. It also upgrades water effects and particles.

Features:

- New, improved rain texture

- Realistic ripples and splashes when rain hits the ground or water

- Randomly spawning puddles during rainfall

- Water ripples and splashes when items are thrown in

- Water ripples and splashes when players swim

Butterflies!

Butterflies are added as new ambient particles that naturally spawn around leaves. They come in four random variants, each with animated flapping textures, bringing more life and movement to forests and green spaces.
