Highlights of the pack!

La Nature by Carrot

The biggest nature overhaul resource pack ever made for Bedrock.

Your journey begins now!

Keep in mind that this is a resource pack and not an add-on. It will NOT change how Minecraft works.

Any change made by this pack is purely visual and auditory.

Have fun building!

Who am I?

I am a French student and recently discovered how to make resource packs. I love it!

So, yes! This is my first ever Resource Pack!

I go by LaCarotteRebel and most of the time, I am called Carotte. Thus why this pack is "by Carrot".

Why resource packs and not Add-ons?

- I don't know how to make add-ons

- I don't like the idea of disabling achievements

- Well, maybe I am making an add-on after all
