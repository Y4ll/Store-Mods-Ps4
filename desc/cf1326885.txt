Welcome to the Faramir's Furniture Addon! Get ready to transform your builds with stylish, vanilla-inspired furniture. This addon brings your spaces to lifenow with support for Vibrant Visuals and RTX.

CRAFTING FURNITURE

To create any furniture item, you'll first need the Furniture Workbench and a Copper Pan. The Copper Pan is a special block that can only be crafted inside the Furniture Workbench, so make sure to build the workbench before starting your decorating!

WOODEN PLANKS VARIANTS

Discover new plank styles! These new variants can be crafted exclusively using the Stonecutter, offering more design variation while staying true to the vanilla aesthetic.

COPPER HAMMER

The Copper Hammer is a tool that lets you modify certain furniture designs with ease. Before you start decorating, make sure to craft oneits essential for personalizing your builds!

BRAZIERS

Braziers are decorative fire blocks that enhance builds with ambient lighting.

Interact with Soul Sand to switch the flame to Soul Fire Use Charcoal to revert to the default fire

CHANDELIERS & CANDELABRAS

Both chandeliers and candelabras share a unique feature: you can change the color of their candles simply by interacting with them while holding any candle item. Customize your lighting to match your builds ambiance!

ITEM DISPLAY

Put your favorite Music Discs or Armor Trims on full display using the Item Display block! Each item is rendered in full 3D, making it perfect for decorating builds, creating collections, or simply flexing your rare finds to your friends.

PLACEABLE ITEMS

Decorate your house with placeable items to make it feel more alive. You can place almost any item with a 3d model to add personality to your space. You can always decraft them to their original item

SINKS

Sinks offer usefull interactions when interacting:

Water Loading:
If there's a water source a few blocks below, interacting with the sink will fill it.

Water Collection: Once filled, you can:

1. Use an empty bucket to collect water
2. Use glass bottles to fill them

Material Conversion: Interacting with the sink while holding:

1. Dirt gives you mud
2. Concrete powder gives you solid concrete

CUPBOARDS & DRAWERS

Cupboards & Drawers Simple, Stylish Storage Decor are decorative blocks that can be opened or closed by interacting with them. While they dont store items, you can customize their top surface by using certain blocks.

Due to Bedrock limitations, using entities for storage can cause item loss and restrict entity spawning across the world. To avoid these issues, Ive chosen not to implement entity-based storage in this addon.

HANGING SIGN

Hanging signs are versatile decorative blocks designed to enhance your builds with style and character.

Customize Designs: Right-click with a Copper Hammer to cycle through available sign designs.
Add Foliage: Interact with vines to drape natural greenery over the sign.
Remove Foliage: Use shears to clean up the vines and restore the base look.

WALL TORCH

The Wall Torch is a functional decorative block that lets you hold any type of torch directly onto your builds.
