How does Blockball Ultimate work?

Blockball Ultimate transforms Minecraft into a completely dynamic and realistic soccer experience. The ball isn't just a simple object: it's a physical entity that reacts to the player's movement, creating an authentic sense of control and gameplay.

TRAILER

BALL CONTROL (Automatic Dribbling)

When the player approaches the ball:

The ball automatically sticks to their feet.
It dynamically adjusts its position based on:
Camera direction
Player speed
Actual movement (walking, running, jumping)

The ball isn't static; instead:

It moves forward when you run.
It adjusts if you move slowly.
It rises if you're in the air.

This creates a realistic dribbling feel.

MOVEMENT AND PHYSICS

The system detects:

Player speed
Direction of movement
Whether they are moving forward or backward
If you move backward:
You gradually lose control.
After a certain distance you lose the ball.
If the ball is floating:

(e.g., you're in the air or on an edge)
After A few seconds you automatically lose the ball
DRIBBLING (plays with crouching)

When the player crouches (sneak):

Dribbing animations are activated
Progressive effects are generated:

Speed
Jump

The more dribbles you do in a row:

The stronger the effects become
The faster and more agile you are

This allows you to:

Perform feints
Quick changes of direction
Technical plays

CONTROLLING ACTIONS WITH CROUCHING

The system uses crouching as the primary control:

1. Normal crouch (hold)

Activates dribbling

2. Quick crouch (double tap)

Activates shooting mode

SHOOTING MODE

When you activate shooting mode:

The player enters a shooting position
They no longer control the ball while it's close to them

When approaching the Ball:

Executes shooting animation
The ball receives realistic physical force
