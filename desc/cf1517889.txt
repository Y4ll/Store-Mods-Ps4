ClearVision X-Ray (1.26.x)

A clean and optimized X-Ray texture pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
This pack improves underground visibility by making ores clearly visible while keeping gameplay smooth and stable.

Features:
Full ore visibility
Clean and simple textures
No lag optimized
Works on low-end devices
Supports latest versions (1.26.x)
Outline selection support

Compatibility:
Survival Mode supported
Achievements are NOT disabled
Works in Realms & Servers

Recommended Settings:
- Turn OFF Smooth Lighting
- Turn ON Outline Selection

Installation:
1. Download the pack
2. Import into Minecraft
3. Activate in Resource Packs
4. Enjoy better mining visibility

Note:
This pack enhances visibility and may not work correctly with other texture packs that modify block rendering.

Updated for Minecraft 1.26.x with improved stability and performance!
