Minecraft But Shearing Is OP

Give your Shears a whole new purpose with Minecraft But Shearing Is OP by FleetMc Studio! Instead of only collecting wool, every time you shear a sheep you'll instantly receive random Minecraft loot, turning every sheep into a valuable source of powerful rewards.

Whether you're building a wool farm or exploring plains full of sheep, every shearing action has the chance to reward you with useful survival resources, overpowered equipment, rare blocks, and valuable treasures. Farming sheep has never been this rewarding!

What Can Drop?

- Max Enchanted Armor.

- Powerful Weapons.

- Enchanted Tools.

- Totems of Undying.

- Elytra.

- Shulker Boxes.

- Diamonds & Diamond Blocks.

- Netherite Items.

- Rare Building Blocks.

- Useful Survival Resources.

- Food and Valuable Items.

- ...and hundreds of other random Minecraft items!

Features

- Every sheep shearing gives random Minecraft loot.

- Wool is still dropped normally.

- Huge variety of possible rewards.

- Receive both common and extremely overpowered items.

- Perfect for Survival challenges and lucky worlds.

- Lightweight and optimized.

- Achievement Friendly.

- No Experimental Gameplay required.

How to Use

- Download and import the addon.

- Enable the Behavior Pack.

- Join your world.

- Craft or obtain a pair of Shears.

- Shear any sheep and collect your random rewards!

Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

- Achievement Friendly.

- No Experimental Gameplay Required.

- Singleplayer & Multiplayer Supported.

Every sheep becomes an opportunity to earn incredible loot. Shear sheep, collect powerful rewards, and enjoy a fun new survival experience with Minecraft But Shearing Is OP.
