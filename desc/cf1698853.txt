oSkullo's Armor HUD

Ever wanted to check your armor durability without opening your inventory mid-fight? oSkullo's Armor HUD puts your equipped armor right on your screen in first-person helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots each shown as a clean hotbar-style icon with a live durability bar and percentage number underneath.

No commands, no behavior pack, no inventory-checking. Just glance at your screen and know exactly how close your gear is to breaking perfect for PvP, survival, or any server where gear management matters.

Features

Live armor display automatically shows whichever pieces you have equipped, updates in real time

Durability bar color-shifts red yellow green based on remaining durability

Durability numbers exact percentage shown under each piece

Two layouts vertical stack or horizontal row, your choice

Fully repositionable move the whole group, the background cells, the bars, or the numbers independently

Resizable separate size controls for icons, bar thickness, and numbers

Lightweight resource-pack only, no behavior pack required, minimal texture footprint

Customization

Every option is controlled through the pack's own settings menu (Settings Global Resource Packs oSkullo's Armor HUD gear icon) no file editing needed:

Toggle the hotbar background, durability bar, and numbers on/off individually

Switch between vertical/horizontal arrangement

Fine-tune X/Y position for the group, background, bar, and numbers

Adjust icon size, bar thickness, and number size

Installation

Download the .mcpack file

Open it with Minecraft (or import via Settings Global Resource Packs)

Apply it to your world

Open pack settings to customize to your liking
