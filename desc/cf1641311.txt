Small, Big & Giant Never encounter the exact same mob twice.

Bring life and visual diversity to your world without breaking Minecraft Bedrocks balance! With this addon, every creature spawns with its own unique scale from tiny critters to towering colossi.

6 Size Variations (Equal Spawn Rate):

Mini (0.1x) Microscopic encounters!

Small (0.5x)

Medium (1x) The classic vanilla size

Large (2.5x)

Giant (3.5x)

Mountain (5x) True colossi on your horizon!

Addon Features:

Instant Spawn: Mobs spawn directly in their calculated size no popping, glitches, or visual jumps.

Universal Compatibility: Works seamlessly with natural spawning, mob spawners, and spawn eggs.

Broad Mob Support: Compatible with 30+ mobs (Zombies, Creepers, Skeletons, Witches, Wardens, and many more).

Multiplayer Ready: Fully compatible with local worlds, servers, and Realms.

Purely Visual (Vanilla-Friendly)

Zero Stat Changes: Health, damage, speed, and AI remain 100% untouched and identical to the base game.

Plug & Play: No extra resource packs or complex commands required. Just activate and play!

How to Install:

1.Download the .mcpack file.

2.Activate the Behavior Pack in your world settings.

3.Go explore and enjoy the variety!
