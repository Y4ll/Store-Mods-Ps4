BSLB Dreamy Visuals brings golden god rays cutting through the canopy, tuned skies from dawn to midnight, and air that changes with every biome you walk into.

Features

- Per-Biome Fog, Lighting & Colour Grading all 89 biomes

- Volumetric God Rays with Time-Based Density

- Dreamy Dawn-to-Midnight Skies

- Dappled Forest amber haze and warm shafts through the autumn canopy

- Cherry Grove soft rose-gold sun rays through the blossoms

- Reworked Nether & End Biome by Biome

- Deep Dark lit in Sculk Cyan

- 101 Coloured Light Sources

- Full PBR Textures (Default & 32x)

- Vanilla or BSLB Clouds Your Choice

- Compatible with other Packs

Installation

- Download BSLB Dreamy Visuals.mcpack

- Open it to import

- Enable BSLB Dreamy Visuals in your pack list

- Turn on Vibrant Visuals

- Optional: open the pack settings (gear icon) to pick your resolution and cloud style
