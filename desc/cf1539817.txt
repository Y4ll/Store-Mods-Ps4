Knives

NEW Mechanics

Food Points

- Eating food items grants food points, with each item adding or deducting a specific amount. Better foods give more points, while worse foods deduct more points.

- Reaching a certain amount grants you with 10 minutes of health boost. Duration can stack.

- Eating when not hungry will not grant food points this excludes food items that deduct food points.

Seed Replanting

- Breaking a crop while holding a seed will automatically replant it. While holding any hoe, it replants seeds in your inventory.

XP from Harvesting Crops

- Gain XP from harvesting certain fully grown crops! (Can be toggled)

Penalty Effect System

- Eating certain food items will provide a positive buff or effect but comes with a trade off after it wears out. (NOTE: Will be expanded in future versions)
