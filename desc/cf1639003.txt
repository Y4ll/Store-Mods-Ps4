Grass Drops OP Items

Turn ordinary grass and plants into valuable treasure with Grass Drops OP Items by FleetMc Studio! Instead of dropping their normal items, many grass and plant blocks now reward you with random Minecraft loot every time you break them.

Whether you're exploring the Overworld or the Nether, every plant you destroy has a chance to drop useful survival resources, powerful equipment, rare blocks, and exciting rewards. Even the smallest patch of grass could contain your next game-changing item.
