Introduction

"Actual Guns" or "AGCSO" is a weapon addon featuring over 120 items, based on its soon-to-be iconic successor: Actual Guns 2 (a project released just under a year ago that's already on its way to receiving a remake... and even a movie!)

Originally released on July 29, 2019 by TheEnderface, Keyyard, and myself, Actual Guns brought custom guns to Minecraft Bedrock and pushed beyond the limitations of simple retextured bows and snowballs. Its mechanics helped pave the way for the many gun addons that exist today.

However, 7 years later, the weapons from AGCSO (known as 'Legacy Weapons') are being threatened to be wiped away from existence by G-Man in the upcoming movie. Will this be the end?

Movie Synopsis

Actual Guns: Edge of Time follows Sir Stab I, a former elite SAS operative and a major weapons inventor, who finds himself thrown into a super-violent conflict after a sudden encounter with the mysterious G-Man. Confronted aboard a halted train, Stab makes a choice he does not fully understand (Click Me!), and as a result, will be forced into a battle he has no chance of winning. Stripped of control, hunted by enemies he once knew, and bound by a relentless timer that refreshes upon every kill.

Now framed internationally for acts of biological warfare tied to his experimental enhancements of his body, Stab becomes one of the most wanted men alive. Pursued by elite forces across multiple countries and blamed for incidents he did not cause, he is pushed deeper into isolation as governments and former allies begin to view him as less human, but as a living weapon that must be put down.

G-Man activates the countdown and places Stab in a controlled combat zones in different countries (Russia, Morocco, Spain, and Syria), where terrorists and counter-terrorist units (including operatives he once fought beside) are waiting to rip him apart.

Each encounter is brutal and personal, pushing him to his limits physically and mentally as exhaustion, injuries, and nearly a week without sleep begin tearing at his sanity. Every kill brings him closer to saving his legacy weapons, while testing the boundaries of his skill, ruthlessness, and mind.

Desperate to protect the only legacy left from his deceased friend and late grandfather, Stab does everything in his willpower, but will it be enough?
