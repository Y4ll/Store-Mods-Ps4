Borderful Glowing Ores

Caves are dark, and finding ore usually means burning through a stack of torches just to see what you're swinging at. This pack fixes that. With Vibrant Visuals on, every ore glows with its own emissive light. Diamond shines pale blue from across a ravine, redstone burns red in the dark, and gold catches your eye the moment you round a corner. No behaviour pack, no commands, no lag.

Two Styles, One Pack

Default Glowing Ores
The subtle option. Only the ore itself glows, exactly where the mineral sits in the stone. Nothing is outlined, so it just looks like the ore is lit. Best if you want realism and don't want your world to look modded.

Borderful Glowing Ores
The visibility option. Every ore block gets a coloured, glowing border on top of the glowing ore, so it reads instantly at a distance and in dim light. Great for resource gathering, speedrunning, or anyone who mines with brightness turned down. Swap between the two styles any time in the pack settings, no reinstall needed.

Compatibility

It's 100% resource pack, with no behaviour pack, so achievements stay enabled and it's safe to add to an existing world. It works alongside shaders, PVP packs and other resource packs. Just keep Borderful Glowing Ores above them so its ore textures win.

Compatible with Actions & Stuff
Tested with Actions & Stuff by Oreville Studios, and both packs work together. Put Borderful Glowing Ores above Actions & Stuff in your active pack list and the glow works as normal.

What's Covered

Every ore in the game
Coal, copper, iron, gold, diamond, emerald, lapis and redstone, plus all their deepslate versions, nether gold ore, nether quartz ore and ancient debris.

Beyond ore
Amethyst clusters and all three bud stages, the amethyst on calibrated sculk sensors, and cauldron water all get emissive maps too, so geodes light up the same way your mineshaft does.

Coloured block light
The pack also sets custom light colours for over 100 light-emitting blocks in Vibrant Visuals: torches, soul torches, lanterns, copper torches and lanterns, campfires, candles in every colour, froglights, glowstone, sea lanterns, lava, lit redstone ore and more.

Real PBR texture sets
These are proper PBR texture sets with tuned emissive and height maps, not brightened colour textures. The glow works with Minecraft's lighting instead of looking like a flat sticker pasted on the wall.

Turn The Glow On

This is the one step people miss. The glow comes from Minecraft's PBR pipeline, so it only shows up when Vibrant Visuals is active:

Settings Video Graphics Mode Vibrant Visuals (or Ray Tracing, on supported devices)
Move Borderful Glowing Ores to the top of your active resource pack list
Requires Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.80 or newer

If your ores look completely normal, it's almost always because Vibrant Visuals is still off. Flip that switch and they'll light up immediately.

Performance

The pack adds no entities and no scripts. It only swaps texture and lighting data, so on its own it costs essentially zero FPS. Vibrant Visuals is the demanding part, and that's Minecraft's renderer, not this pack. If your device runs Vibrant Visuals, it runs Borderful Glowing Ores.
