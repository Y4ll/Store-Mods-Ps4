Mechanical Keyboard Sounds by oSkullo

Ever wanted your world to sound as satisfying as your keyboard? This pack replaces a huge chunk of Minecraft Bedrock's default sound effects with real mechanical keyboard clicks all personally recorded, not ripped from anywhere.

What's included:

Footstep, jump, land, and fall sounds for 12 block categories (stone, grass, wood, gravel, sand, snow, cloth, glass, metal, ladder, netherrack, soul sand)

Block breaking and placing sounds to match

Mining/hit sounds while breaking blocks

Punch sounds including the air-swing whiff and critical hits

Armor equip sounds

Potion throw, splash, and drink sounds

Explosion sounds

UI click sounds

Every sound is a genuine mechanical keyboard recording no AI generation, no ripped audio. 800+ sound variations pulled from over 190 individually recorded keypresses across multiple press styles (normal, hard, and super-hard presses) for natural variety so nothing feels too repetitive.

Installation: Download the .mcpack file and open it Minecraft will install it automatically. Then enable it in your world's resource pack settings.
