Durability Viewer

This resource pack shows durability of a tool in the hotbar and container screens. When the behavior pack is applied, the shield durability and armor durability is shown as well on the HUD screen.

So, it's mainly a resource pack. The behavior pack is required only if you want armor and shield durability to be shown (Bottom left corner of HUD screen). Achievements friendly.

Features

- Mainhand Item Durability in HUD

- Item Durability in hover text of container screens (Inventory, Chest etc.)

- Blink Animation when durability goes below 20%

- Armor Durability in HUD [Requires Behavior Pack]

- Shield Durability in HUD [Requires Behavior Pack]

- Config Options [Through _global_variables.json File]

Usage:

- Download the RP file and activate in your world

- If you want to display Armor and Shield Durability, simply download and activate the BP file

Note: You can't use the behavior pack in servers. For PvP, use Utility HUD

Download Behavior Pack:

- CurseForge

- MCPEDL [File with BP in its name]
