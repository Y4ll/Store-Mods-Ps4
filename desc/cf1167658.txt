If you have played any of Call of Duty Black Ops games then you are very familiar with Zombies. The very first, and most iconic wonder weapon in zombies is the Raygun!

This texture pack allows you to use the Raygun in minecraft! It is simply a retexture of the Bow in minecraft, so no experiments needed!
