Explore and survive in a world of wilderness and fantasy. Encounter new mobs on your journey, build your base with new blocks, and discover items to enhance your survival skills!

Forsaken Odyssey is an add-on that enhances your world exploration and survival experience while keeping the Vanilla style intact.

This pack combines 15 of my add-ons and includes exclusive strcutures, mobs, blocks, items, and other more features.
