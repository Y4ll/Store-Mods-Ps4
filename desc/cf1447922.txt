Quick Container is one of the MOST POWERFUL resource packs for managing items in Minecraft Bedrock. It's feature-rich yet lightweight for low-end devices. Quick Container also allows players to customize which features to enable or disable, according to their needs.

NOTICES

- Don't use Bulk Items Buttons feature on server to play Skywars etc. because you can be kicked or banned for looting all the items in the chest instantly.

FEATURES

1. Quick Items

Added 4 new buttons: Auto Place, to move items from inventory to chest or vice versa with one click, either all items (64x) or just one item (1x). Drop, to drop items, either all items (64x) or just one item (1x). Destroy, to remove items from existence (only available for inventory screen), and Default.

2. Quick Craft

Added new button to crafting, stonecutter, and trade screen to craft all items with just one click. Quick Craft button on crafting player will only appear if there is item in crafting input slot!

3. Spam Craft

Now you can spam click to CRAFT ITEMS! which you couldn't do before for some reasons, like there is delay after craft an item.

4. Multiple Craft

Added a new button to craft a specific number of items to inventory and crafting screen. You can edit the crafting amount in Global Variables.

5. Bulk Items

Added new buttons to move/drop all items with one click to Inventory, Chest, and Redstone screens. Click Switch button to change the button function to Move or Drop mode. But for Bulk Items Buttons in inventory screen, the function depends on which Quick Items Button is selected.

6. Move Matching Items

Now you can move multiple items at once. Simply tap the item you want to move, then press one of Bulk Items Buttons depending on where you want to move it.

7. Search Items

Added a Search Bar to chest screen to highlight items to make it easier to find them. I also added a Quick Search Button to make it easier to highlight items without having to type. You can also move/drop highlighted items with the Bulk Items Buttons.

8. Move Items per Column

Added a new button to the chest and inventory screens to move items by column/row. Useful for arranging items.

9. Clear Recipe Crafting

Added a button to clear crafting slots to crafting and inventory screen.

10. Fast Unequip Armor

Added a button to remove all player armor in one click to inventory screen.

11. Crafter Util Buttons

Added three new buttons to the crafter screen: Disabled All Slot, Enabled All Slot, and Flip All Slot.

12. Bulk Disenchant Item

Added a button to disenchant all items in inventory with just one click to grindstone screen.

13. Mass Craft

Added a button to crafting screen to craft large quantities of items quickly and efficiently (like in Java mods). For a tutorial on how to use it, watch this video and read the description.

You can turn off features you don't need with variables in the /ui/_global_variables.json file.
