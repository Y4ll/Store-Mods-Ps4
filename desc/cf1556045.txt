Walid's 3D Items for MCPE

This resource pack is a tribute to the creator Walid. I made this pack myself as a Minecraft Bedrock adaptation inspired by his original Java work. My goal is to bring realistic and immersive 3D items to Bedrock Edition and hopefully reach the same level of quality and realism as Walid's creations.

This project recreates the 3D item experience for MCPE/Bedrock while adapting models and features to work properly on Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
