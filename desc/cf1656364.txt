Uncraft

Overview

Uncraft adds a useful resource recovery system for tools, armor, horse armor, and weapons.

Recycle unwanted equipment and reclaim its original materials based on its remaining durability. This helps you make the most of your resources and avoid wasting valuable materials during survival.

The addon works especially well with mob farms and automated farms, making it easier to recycle unwanted gear and turn it back into useful resources.

Features

Achievement Friendly

The addon does not disable Minecraft achievements, allowing you to enjoy its features while keeping your survival experience intact.

Resource Recovery

Recycle most tools, armor, and weapons to recover their original crafting materials based on the item's remaining durability.

Nether Equipment Support

Netherite equipment can also be recycled, allowing you to recover its corresponding resources.

Enchanted Item Recycling

Recycle enchanted equipment and recover the corresponding Enchanted Books along with the recovered materials.

Farm Friendly

Works especially well with mob farms and automated farms, allowing you to efficiently recycle unwanted equipment and manage large amounts of loot.
