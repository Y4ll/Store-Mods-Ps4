This small resource pack modifies the models of some blocks to give them a 3D look. Due to the limitations of bedrock texture packs, not all blocks can be edited. Here is the list of current modified blocks:

- Sugar Canes

- Dripstone

- Vines

(works properly with RTX or shaders; may not work on all devices)

- Saplings

- Mushrooms
