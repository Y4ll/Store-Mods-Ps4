**Terrain Landscapes** is a world generation addon that enhances Minecraft by introducing richer, more dynamic terrains across all dimensions.

This addon completely improves **Overworld**, **Nether**, and **End** terrain generation, featuring:

* More natural and varied landscapes

* Higher mountains, deeper valleys, and smoother terrain transitions

* Improved biome distribution

* Enhanced environmental details that make exploration more immersive

Terrain Landscapes focuses on performance-friendly world generation while still delivering visually impressive results, making every new world feel unique and alive.
