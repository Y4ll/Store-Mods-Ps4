Installation Guide

Android:

- Use MBLoader (Draco) or Patched APK

- Activate Fullbright in Global Packs

Windows:

- Use Wyvern or Matject or manually replace ".material.bin"

- Activate Fullbright in Global Packs

- Have DirectX 10+

IOS:

- Use Hynis RD Loader or custom Patched IPA that supports shaders

- Activate Fullbright in Global Packs
