Unlimited Loch Villager Trades

Trade with villagers without:\/\/ worrying about their offers getting locked! Unlimited Villager Trades increases the usage limit of every supported trade, allowing you to trade billions of times.

Features

- Removes the normal villager trade limit

- Supports every villager profession

- Supports the Wandering Trader

- Covers 279 vanilla trade offers

- Keeps original items and prices unchanged

- Preserves normal villager levels and XP progression

- No experimental features required

- Multiplayer-friendly

How to Use

- Download and open the .mcaddon file using Minecraft.

- Activate Unlimited Villager Trades from the world's Behavior Packs section.

- Place it above other packs that modify villager trades.

- Enter the world and start trading!

Important Information

For the best results, use newly generated or previously untraded villagers. Villagers whose offers have already been saved in the world may retain their previous trade limits.
