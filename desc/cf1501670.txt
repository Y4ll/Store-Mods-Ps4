Real Player CCTV A Simple Live Monitoring Tablet

Tired of complicated CCTV setups just to keep an eye on players? We made it simple.

Real Player CCTV is built on one rule: Keep It Simple. Just hold your CCTV tablet, select a player from the menu, and watch them live in real-time. When you're done, just sneak to close the screen. Simple, functional, and zero hassle.

Why It's So Simple

- No Commands: Save your fingers! No /camera typing.

- Simple UI: Dropdown menu. Pick a player. Hit submit. Done.

- Smart Tracking: The camera automatically tracks the player.

- Dual Camera: Press Forward (W / Analog Up) to toggle views instantly!

- Night Vision: Just Jump to toggle Night Vision on/off instantly!

- Cross-Dimension: Tracks targets anywhereOverworld, Nether, or The End!

- Instant Exit: Just Sneak to stop watching instantly.

- Custom 3D Item: Sleek custom model for first-person and third-person views.

- Admin Only: Locked strictly for Operators.

- Plug & Play: No Experimental Toggles required. Just activate and play!

- Survival Ready: Fully craftable in Survival Mode.

How to Use

No complicated setups. Just pick a target and watch.

- Open It: Hold the Real Player CCTV Tablet and use it (Right-click/Long press).

- Select Target: Pick a player from the simple dropdown list.

- Submit: Hit submit to start watching them in real-time!

- Stop: Press Sneak to stop monitoring.

How to Get It

Craft it easily in Survival Mode or grab it in the Equipment Tab in your Creative Inventory.

Fully craftable using vanilla materials.
