Written instructions:

- Download the .mcpack and open it with Minecraft!

- Use the following order: Actions & Stuff 1.11, Bare Bones and Bare Bones & Stuff 1.11 on top!

- Use the Gear icon on the texture pack to change the subpack! (Match the A&S subpack you selected)

- Apply this order onto your Global Resources, World or Realms to make them work!

- Also make sure to put it above the Realistic Biomes RP in case of using it for it to make effect!

What's the purpose of Bare Bones & Stuff as a whole?

Have you ever seen a Minecraft trailer and wanted your game to look like that? Actions & Stuff is an amazing way to get a similar result, and using Bare Bones on top of it to make it even more accurate can make your game be almost like the Trailers! But you might notice when using A&S Custom Experience that mobs lack expressions, that your villagers aren't as lively anymore, that your 3D items are 2D once again.

That's what Bare Bones & Stuff is for! A way to make your world shine with life again giving every feature the Full Experience and Vanilla Experience from Actions & Stuff can offer a look that matches Bare Bones!
