HATCH. GROW. FIGHT!
In this addon, you get to hatch and raise A DRAGON!

Traverse the updated mushroom island, and explore the ruins of an ancient civilisation, to uncover powerfull dragon eggs!
Incubate the eggs and hatch them, tame them and grow them, until you can take mount on your fire-breathing friend!

Get yourself a Dracarys Item, and set the world a lite with fire! Burn your friends and unleash chaos on the world! Or use your dragon to create a world of peace!

Along with the dragons, you can also explore the world to find many more magical creatures, from Direwolves, to Mammoths, this world is rich with fantastical animals!

There are up to 28 dragons to uncover, with each one having its own unique and rare variants!
