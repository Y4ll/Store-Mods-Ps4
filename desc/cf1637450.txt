Introduction

Survival Support is a quality-of-life addon designed to make your Minecraft Bedrock survival experience smoother, faster, and more enjoyable. Instead of changing the vanilla gameplay too much, it adds a collection of useful tools that reduce repetitive tasks while keeping the survival feeling intact.

Whether you're mining deep underground, building large structures, or managing your farm, Survival Support helps you spend less time on tedious work and more time enjoying the game.

How to Use

Opening the menu is simple:

- Sneak (Crouch).

- Hold a pickaxe in your hand.

- Look into the air (don't aim at any block or entity).

- Left-click (or use the interact button).

The Survival Helper Menu will appear, allowing you to enable or disable each feature individually whenever you want.

Features

Auto-Replenish Blocks

Automatically refills your selected hotbar slot with the same block from your inventory when it runs out.

Auto-Replace Tools

When your tool breaks, it is instantly replaced with another tool of the same type from your inventory.

Auto-Replant Crops

Harvest crops with a single action while automatically planting new seeds.

Auto-Replant Saplings

When chopping trees, saplings will automatically be replanted to help forests regenerate.

Treecapitator (Fast Chop)

Break the entire tree by chopping a single log, making wood collection much faster.

Vein Miner

Mine connected ore veins or similar blocks at once instead of breaking them individually.

Auto-Smelt

Certain ores and blocks are automatically smelted when mined, saving both fuel and time.

Notes

- Every feature can be turned ON or OFF individually through the Survival Helper Menu.

- This addon is designed to improve survival gameplay without replacing the vanilla experience.
