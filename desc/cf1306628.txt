Do you imagine having a Hot Wheels Toys in Minecraft? now you have it! I'm introduce to you Hot Wheels Minecraft Character Cars! in this series have 8 total of cars! they mainly designed by Minecraft Mobs!

Basic Information

Hot Wheels Minecraft Character Cars Basic Specifications

Performance:

- Speed: 5 Blocks/Second. [0.50 Speed]

- Toys Speed: 2 Blocks/Second. [0.25 Speed]

Miscellaneous:

- Health: 20 Points.

- Seat count: Depends on Vehicle.

Damage:

- The Vehicle will get damage animation if under max health condition and if the Vehicle in Critical Health [2 health] will release particles if the car moving. so fix your car using Wrench.

Wrenches:

- Wrench: Fixes 1 point to the vehicle.

- Tweak: Changes the vehicle size.

Nitrox2:

- The blue one is Full Nitrox2. Use it carefully! You can use Nitrox2 only 3 times! So, save it if you really need it. How to use it? Just the same as with a pig!

Commands, Eggs and Colors available to change:

- /summon hp:hwmcc_creeper [NONE]

- /summon hp:hwmcc_spider [NONE]

- /summon hp:hwmcc_skeleton [NONE]

- /summon hp:hwmcc_enderman [NONE]

- /summon hp:hwmcc_ocelot [NONE]

- /summon hp:hwmcc_zombie [NONE]

- /summon hp:hwmcc_iron_golem [NONE]

- /summon hp:hwmcc_redstone_monstrosity [NONE]
