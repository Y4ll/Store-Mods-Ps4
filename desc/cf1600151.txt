lIs your village frequently attacked by aggressive monsters that a Golem cannot handle on its own?

Do you want villagers to be able to defend their homes without your intervention?

"Dan's Guard Villagers" introduces brave village guards with intelligent combat behavior, helping villagers fight back and survive the dangers of the Minecraft world.

Features:

- Guard Villagers spawn in villages in groups.

- Guard Villagers can be tamed using Emeralds.

- Hold a weapon, then hold the sneak key and right-click on a Nitwit or an unemployed villager to turn them into a Guard Villager.

- Use Badges to recruit Guardians.

Guard Villagers:

They appear in villages in small groupscomprising archers and guardsscaled to the village's population; they are ready to fight at the first sign of danger, or even attack you (so don't go kidnapping the villagers !).
