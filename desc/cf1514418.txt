Twilight Visuals is a cinematic shader pack inspired by the cold, moody atmosphere of the Twilight movie. Designed around a blue-teal color palette with muted rose sunsets, it transforms Minecraft Bedrock into a Pacific Northwest forest, misty, melancholic, and hauntingly beautiful.

Features

Cold blue-teal daytime atmosphere with desaturated overcast skies

Muted lavender-rose sunrise and sunset. never warm or orange

Deep blue moonlit nights with teal water reflections

Hable tone mapping for cinematic highlight roll-off and film-grade feel

Height-based volumetric fog that pools in valleys and lakeshores

Colored local lighting, 85+ block types with custom light colors

Realistic animated water with 20-octave wave detail and underwater caustics

Per-biome atmospherics, fog, lighting, and color grading

Feedback and suggestions are welcome to help shape future versions.
