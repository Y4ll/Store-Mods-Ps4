Narra's Lore

Narra had been drifting in space for so long that might as call it forever, She Cried until no more tears fell, As time goes by, She loses her sense of time and herself

She become numb, Losing her feelings and emotions, only a deep ache in her heart remains.

She was summoned by Prince Zedrak out of the void to fight for him, She is deemed "A perfect tool" as she doesn't feel any pain, She had no emotion and she follow order without question.

Narra uses her Sorrow as her weapon to strike down her Foe like a machine, like a tool, her heart is cold yet you can still see in a glimpse of her eyes that she is still Yearning...

Yearning one day to be free and to feel.
