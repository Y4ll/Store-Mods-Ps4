YGP! X-RAY

See What Others Can't!

YGP! X-RAY is a Minecraft Bedrock resource pack designed to make underground exploration easier by improving the visibility of valuable ores and resources.

The updated version focuses on clearer ore identification, stronger underground visibility, improved Nether resource visibility, and a lightweight resource-pack-only design.

Created by YGP Studio / YGP Minecraft.

WHAT DOES YGP! X-RAY DO?

YGP! X-RAY reduces visual obstruction around underground resources so important ores are easier to locate and identify.

The goal is simple:

Find ores faster. Identify them clearly. Explore underground more easily.

The pack focuses on resource-pack rendering rather than gameplay scripts.

VIBRANT VISUALS

If your Minecraft version and device support Vibrant Visuals, you can try using it together with YGP! X-RAY.

Vibrant Visuals can require additional GPU performance.

If you experience:

FPS drops

Stuttering

Heating

Lag

consider turning Vibrant Visuals off.

Performance varies depending on your device, Minecraft version, render distance, world complexity, and other active resource packs.
