This is the bedrock version of a texturepack made for a youtuber named Bunji for his 4k subs on youtube.

This 1.21 PvP texturepack features a light blue theme on most crystal PvP related items and gui alongside a lot of quality of life features like invisible explosion particles and a lot of nice animations.

Everything changed

- Short Retextured Swords

- Iron, Diamond and Netherite Armor

- Diamond Set, Blocks and Ores

- Various PvP-Related Items like Ender Pearls, Exp Bottles, Wind Charges, Golden Apples

- Mace, Trident, End Crystals and Anchors

- Enchant Glint and Enchanting Table

- GUI and HUD

- Animated Totem of Undying, Fireworks, Potions and Tipped Arrows

- Invisible Explosion Particles, Block Breaking Particles, Eating Particles and Smaller Totems

- No Explosion Sounds
