Auto Replant

Tired of replanting your farm after every harvest? Auto Replant does it for you the instant you break a fully grown crop, a fresh one is planted right back in its place. No more walking rows of dirt clicking seeds one by one.

Features:

Automatically replants crops the moment you harvest them but only if they were fully mature (breaking an unripe crop behaves exactly like vanilla)

Works with wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, nether wart, sweet berry bushes, cocoa pods, torchflower crops, and pitcher crops

Drops and Fortune enchantment behave exactly like vanilla this addon only handles the replanting, not the loot

Pure add-on, no resource pack, no texture changes fully compatible with other add-ons

Lightweight scripting-based logic with virtually no performance impact

Works on singleplayer, Realms, and dedicated (BDS) servers

Requirements:

Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.40 or later

"Beta APIs" experimental toggle must be enabled in world settings

How it works:

Auto Replant listens for block-break events. When you harvest a crop that had reached its final growth stage, it instantly resets that spot back to the seedling stage so all you have to do is keep harvesting.
