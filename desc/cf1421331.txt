Are you looking for a texture pack that balances competitive PvP advantages with stunning high-definition visuals? Doyl x128 is a Bedrock Edition resource pack designed to elevate your gameplay.

At 128x resolution, every block and item is crisp and detailed, but we haven't sacrificed performance for looks. This pack is tailored for players who want a clean interface, maximum visibility in combat, and a beautiful world to explore.

Key Features: PvP Optimized Tools: Featuring "short" swords and stubby tools. This design increases your screen visibility during intense battles, ensuring your own gear doesn't block your view.

Highlighted Ores: Never miss diamonds again. All valuable ores have distinct colored outlines, making them instantly recognizable in dark caves. Perfect for UHC games and efficient mining.

Low Fire: Obstructive fire overlays are reduced significantly. When you are on fire, it won't blind you, allowing you to keep fighting back.

Custom Sky: Replace the boring default sky with a stunning, immersive teal nebula and starry night atmosphere.

Smooth Armor & Clean Items: Armor textures are smoothed out for a clean look, and items (like food and potions) are detailed yet easy to identify at a glance.
