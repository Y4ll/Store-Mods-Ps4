HOW DOES SATURATION WORK?

Behind your hunger bar lies an invisible stat called Saturation. It acts as a shield for your hunger, and it directly controls how fast you regenerate health. The higher your saturation, the faster and more efficiently you heal.

Different foods give vastly different saturation values:

Food
Saturation Points
Effect

Golden Carrot
14 pts
Extremely fast healing

Cooked Beef
12.8 pts
Fast healing

Bread
6 pts
Slow healing

Apple
2.4 pts
Very slow healing

This means that what you eat matters just as much as how much you eat. Eating a Golden Carrot will sustain your health regeneration far longer than eating bread, without needing to eat again.

FEATURES

Java Regeneration Wave

When you gain a heart of health, the entire row of hearts performs a left-to-right wave ripple, bouncing slightly up and down in sequence exactly as seen in Java Edition.

White Heart Flash

As each individual heart (or half heart) is actively restored, it briefly flashes with a bright white highlight before settling into its solid red state. The flash lasts precisely 0.25 seconds an accurate recreation of Java Edition's 5-tick heart pop effect.

Saturation HUD Display

Your hidden saturation value is now visible in real time as glowing golden outlines overlaid directly on your hunger bar drumsticks. Watch your saturation drain as you regenerate, and see exactly how much healing power your food has given you.

- Saturation display automatically hides when your hunger is depleted

- Disappears cleanly on death or on switching to Creative/Spectator mode

- Zero flickering, zero ghost highlights

Clean, Minimal HUD

No cluttered action bar text. No black background boxes. No redundant status effect icons. Just a clean, seamless vanilla-feel UI.
