SCP FOUNDATION HORROR ADDON FOR MINECRAFT 1.26

Enter a world where fear is real and survival is not guaranteed...

The SCP Foundation Addon transforms your Minecraft into a dark, terrifying experience inspired by the secretive world of SCP. Built and optimized for Minecraft 1.26, this addon introduces dangerous entities, advanced AI systems, and a deeply immersive horror atmosphere.

WHAT IS SCP FOUNDATION?

The SCP Foundation is a secret organization responsible for containing anomalous and dangerous entities.
In this addon, those entities have escaped... and now you must survive.
