Easy Creative Items [ECI]

Easy Creative Items [ECI] adds all those hidden, hard-to-reach blocks directly into your creative inventory organized into clean groups right where they belong. No commands, no hassle.

The original idea: concept inspired by VitooorGames' addon Cheats and Secret Items in the Creative Menu. Credit to VitooorGames.

1. Features Overview

All the secret blocks, where they belong in your creative inventory.

2. Images

No commands. Just the blocks, where they belong. Behavior pack only, fully vanilla-compatible.
