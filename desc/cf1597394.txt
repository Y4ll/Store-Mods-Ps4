FPS DISPLAY

Achievement-Friendly Real-Time FPS Counter for Minecraft Bedrock Edition

A lightweight, smooth, and accurate FPS display designed for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Monitor your current frame rate in real time without cluttering your screen, helping you optimize your gameplay and device performance.

Overview

FPS DISPLAY is a clean and performance-friendly Resource Pack that adds a live FPS counter directly to your in-game HUD.

Unlike bulky debug screens, this pack provides only the information you needyour current Frames Per Secondwhile keeping the game immersive and distraction-free.

Whether you're testing shaders, texture packs, worlds, or simply monitoring performance, FPS DISPLAY gives you a convenient way to keep track of your game's rendering performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this disable achievements?

No.

This is a Resource Pack only and does not require a Behavior Pack, so it is achievement-friendly.

Does it work on Android & iOS?

Yes.

It works on Android, iOS, Windows, and other Bedrock Edition platforms that support Resource Packs.

Does it reduce FPS?

No.

The pack is designed to be lightweight and has a negligible impact on performance.

Does it work in Multiplayer & Realms?

Yes.

Since it is a client-side Resource Pack, it works in single-player and most multiplayer sessions where Resource Packs are allowed.

Can I change the HUD position?

Yes.

Choose your preferred position by selecting one of the included subpacks before enabling the Resource Pack.

How often is the FPS updated?

The FPS value updates continuously while you play, providing a smooth real-time reading.

Is this a Behavior Pack?

No.

This project is a Resource Pack only.

The FPS counter isn't showing. What should I do?

- Make sure the Resource Pack is enabled.

- Place it above other HUD/UI packs in the Resource Pack order.

- Restart Minecraft after enabling the pack if needed.

Can I use this in my own videos?

Yes! You're welcome to showcase the pack. A link back to the CurseForge page is appreciated but not required.

How Does It Work?

FPS DISPLAY uses Minecraft Bedrock's built-in client rendering system to monitor frame timing and continuously update the on-screen FPS value.

Everything runs locally on your device without requiring commands, scoreboards, or external applications, allowing the HUD to stay lightweight while providing smooth real-time updates.
