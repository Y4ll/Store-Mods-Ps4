Odyssey NPCs The Ultimate NPC & Server Framework

Tired of broken NPC add-ons that require unstable Beta APIs, endless command lines, or crash your world upon every game update?

Welcome to Odyssey NPCs a standalone NPC framework built from scratch for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. It requires ZERO Beta APIs and ZERO commands. Everything is managed through intuitive, performance-optimized UI menus.

Whether you are creating an immersive RPG adventure map, managing a multiplayer server, building cinematic scenes, or looking for loyal companions in your survival world, Odyssey NPCs gives you complete control over your NPCs.

What's New in v1.1.0

NPC Size & Scale System

Customize the physical size of your NPCs directly from the Appearance menu.

-
Baby: 0.5 scale

-
Medium: 0.75 scale

-
Normal: 1.0 scale

-
No extra entities or textures are required.

-
NPC scale is fully integrated with the cloning system, so cloned NPCs retain their selected size.

Give Held Item

Equip your NPCs with items directly from your hand without manually entering Item IDs.

Simply hold an item, select the desired equipment slot, and use Give Held Item.

-
Supports weapons and armor from standard Minecraft.

-
Supports compatible items from other add-ons.

-
Useful for RPG, RP, cinematic, and modded-server creators.

-
Equipment functionality such as damage, armor values, and durability can work normally when supported by the item's own implementation.

This system has been tested with equipment from third-party add-ons such as Epic Knights.

Compatibility Note: Add-ons that rely on highly customized player-only animations, rendering systems, or complex two-handed weapon behavior may not visually function correctly on NPCs.

Core Features

Advanced AI & Behaviors

-
Follow, Wait, & Roam: Command your NPCs to follow you, remain at a location, or freely explore the environment.

-
Smart Teleportation: If an NPC in follow mode falls too far behind, it can automatically teleport to your position.

Quick-Guard Combat System

-
Reflexive Defense: Toggle combat mode instantly by Sneaking + Right-Clicking your NPC (restricted to Owners and Whitelisted players).

-
Smart Target Filtering: NPCs can defend you against hostile threats while strategically ignoring world-destructive entities such as Creepers, Wardens, and the Wither.

-
Aggressive Mode: Turn NPCs into combatants or base guardians that can target survival players.

Advanced Ranged Combat

-
Equip an NPC with a bow and arrows through its inventory.

-
NPCs can use ranged combat while in supported combat modes.

-
When enemies get too close, the AI can dynamically switch between ranged and melee combat.

Deep Equipment & Durability

-
Full Gear Customization: Equip helmets, chestplates, leggings, boots, main-hand items, and off-hand items.

-
Give Held Item: Transfer the item you are currently holding directly into a selected equipment slot.

-
Functional Mechanics: Supported enchantments, damage values, armor values, and durability can function normally.

-
Quick Equip: Vanilla armor can also be equipped directly by interacting with the NPC while holding compatible gear.

Walking Chests & Inventory

-
NPCs feature a custom inventory system for carrying items.

-
Players can add or retrieve items through the NPC interface.

-
Includes a toggleable safety feature allowing NPC inventory to be dropped upon death.

Navigation, Patrols, & NPC Groups

-
Go-To System: Order NPCs to travel to selected coordinates.

-
Cyclic Patrols: Create multi-point patrol routes that continuously loop between locations.

-
NPC Linking: Link NPCs together so multiple NPCs can follow a designated leader.

Visual Customization

-
10 Built-in Skins: Choose from the included NPC skins.

-
Custom Skin Support: Add your own 6464 PNG skins through a compatible resource pack.

-
Dynamic Names: Customize NPC nametags directly through the UI.

-
NPC Size System: Change NPC scale between Baby, Medium, and Normal.
