Furniture Variety

Chairs & Sofas: Classic, modern, dining chairs, and sofas in 16 colors

Tables: Coffee tables, dining tables, shelf tables, and stools

Storage: Kitchen cabinets, drawers, and nightstands

Appliances: Refrigerators, ovens, microwaves, and toasters

Decoration: Mirrors, clocks, globes, teapots, and utensils

Bathroom: Showers, bathtubs, sinks, toilets, and towels

Outdoor: Blinds, dog kennels, and ladders

Customization Options

16 colors available for upholstered and decorative furniture

13 wood types for wooden furniture

Metal variants in white, gray, and iron

Rustic and modern styles

Lamp blocks that illuminate and can be toggled on/off

New dyeing system: Dye any colored block by clicking it with dye in hand

Inventory

Many furniture pieces include their own inventory with custom names. Drawers visibly open while the inventory is active.

Due to Minecraft limitations, breaking these blocks requires an axe, pickaxe, or other tool or simply crouch.

Installation

1. Download the Rustic Furniture resource pack

2. Place the file in your resource_packs folder

3. Enable the pack in world or global settings

4. Enjoy your new rustic furniture!

Compatibility

Compatible with most resource packs

Works with vanilla and modified textures

Supports PBR (Physically Based Rendering)
