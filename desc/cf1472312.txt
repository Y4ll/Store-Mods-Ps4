Howard Eclipse Shader provides a beautiful atmosphere that makes your world stand out. But thats not all this shader also features a special event that sets it apart from other shaders. Yes, that event is a daily eclipse event.

When the event occurs, all light becomes dimmer and your view gets darker. This shader has unique sun rays and sun glare effects. It also uses render fog, which enhances the visuals and makes everything feel more dynamic.

Howard Eclipse Shader also delivers unique and complex illuminance and color tones for a more immersive experience.
