Kaleidoscope Cookery is a feature-rich cooking and farming add-on packed with ingredients, crops, cookware, kitchen stations, recipes, placeable meals, drinks, furniture, tools, and food-focused gameplay.

Grow and harvest crops like tomatoes, lettuce, chili peppers, rice, and wild rice, gather fresh ingredients, press seeds and grains into cooking oil, and turn raw foods into a huge variety of meals. Slice and prepare ingredients on the Chopping Board, stir-fry dishes in the Wok, simmer soups and stews in the Stockpot, steam foods in the Steamer, brew tea in the Teapot, roast food on the Shawarma Spit, and grind ingredients with the Millstone.

The add-on includes everything from noodles, rice bowls, soups, dumplings, meat dishes, sashimi, pastries, platters, fried foods, teas, and other specialty recipes to more unusual culinary creations. Many foods can be placed in the world as serving dishes, while baskets, racks, oil containers, tables, chairs, stools, and other kitchen furnishings let you build a much more complete cooking space.

You can also discover recipes, collect special ingredients, use kitchen knives and shovels, harvest crops with the Sickle, wear farmer clothing and straw hats, store and display food, manage cooking oil, prepare multi-ingredient recipes, and experiment with different cooking stations and techniques.

There is also an in-game Guidebook that goes into much more depth on recipes, ingredients, cooking methods, farming, equipment, progression, and the add-ons many other features.
