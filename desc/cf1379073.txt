White 3D Outline (Selection Box)

Upgrade Minecraft Bedrock's default black selection box with a clean and immersive White 3D Outline.

This texture pack replaces the default black selection box with a smooth white 3D outline, improving block visibility while preserving the vanilla experience. Lightweight, simple, and fully compatible with the latest Minecraft Bedrock versions.
