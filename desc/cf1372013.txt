Animated XP Bars

Bring calm, minimal beauty to your Minecraft HUD with the Animated XP Bar.

This pack replaces the default XP bar with a smooth, modern gradient design that adds a relaxing and stylish touch to your screen. Perfect for players who love clean visuals and a cozy, aesthetic vibe while exploring, building, or surviving!

Features:

- Soft gradient animation for a soothing look

- Smooth 18-frame animated colour flow

- Minimalist, modern design

- 9 styles to choose from in pack settings

- Works with shaders

- Lightweight and performance-friendly

Subpack Descriptions for Animated XP Bar

1) Anemo XP Bar

A cool, wind-themed XP bar with teal tones inspired by elemental aesthetics. Calm, refreshing, and perfect for a clean fantasy-styled HUD.

2) Aesthetic XP Bar

A soft, clean, and minimal glow-style XP bar designed for players who want a smooth and modern HUD without distractions.

3) RGB XP Bar

A vibrant, colour-cycling XP bar with smooth rainbow animation. Perfect for players who love stylish UI effects and a flashy gameplay vibe.

4) Sakura XP Bar

A pink, pastel-inspired XP bar with gentle Sakura-style highlights. Great for aesthetic builds, soft themes, and cozy gameplay styles.

5) Purple XP Bar

A purple XP bar with a gentle purple glow. Great for players who love a purple colour theme.

6) Dark XP Bar

A dark XP bar with a spooky vibe. Perfect for night players and dark UI lovers.

7) Ocean XP Bar

A deep ocean-inspired XP bar. Perfect for players who love a calm, immersive aesthetic inspired by the sea.

8) Snowy XP Bar

A snow-themed XP bar for those who love snow and cold biomes.

9) Emerald XP Bar

A fresh emerald-green XP bar inspired by Minecraft's classic XP colour, with the same smooth animated glow. Perfect for players who want a vanilla-friendly look with a modern touch.

How to choose a style

- Download and open the .mcpack Minecraft imports it automatically

- Go to Settings Global Resources (or your world's Resource Packs) and activate Animated XP Bars

- Click the gear icon on the pack and use the slider to pick one of the 9 styles
