Magic Brooms

Magic Brooms adds 14 fully rideable broomsticks to Minecraft, built around real flight, magical abilities and survival progression.

Flying feels like riding an actual broom rather than using Creative Mode. Brooms have momentum, controlled ascent and descent, smooth steering, banking animations and visual feedback that follows every movement.

There are seven broom families, each with a standard and upgraded version:

Magic, Fire, Ice, Lightning, Ender, Grove and Tide.

The six magical families have their own scroll ability, particles, sounds and gameplay style. Cast rings of fire, summon lightning, create frost storms, blink through the air, protect allies with nature magic or launch enemies with a tidal wave.

Build Them Yourself

Brooms are assembled physically on the Broom Assembly Bench.

Add wood, attach the hay, infuse it with a magical catalyst and optionally install an upgrade. Every step appears directly on the bench, and the finished broom floats toward you when completed.
