The Glider is an adventure-focused mobility tool designed for RPG-style exploration. It lets you safely glide down from cliffs, towers, mountains, and other high places, turning vertical travel into a controlled, cinematic descent instead of a risky fall.

1.0.3 - Achievement friendly!

Core gameplay
- Use the glider to plan routes from high ground, cross gaps, and descend into valleys while keeping the feeling of an RPG journey.
- It is ideal for exploration, scouting, and reaching hard-to-access terrain without relying on teleport style movement.

Activation and controls
- The glider can be OPENED or CLOSED at any moment using:
- Jump
- Use (Right Click)
This makes it responsive in mid-air: you can quickly deploy it when falling, or close it to drop faster when needed.

The player can be knocked down by projectiles.

Durability and risk
- The glider has 128 durability.
- Because durability is limited, you must pay attention: if the glider breaks at the wrong moment, you can suddenly lose control and fall from a dangerous height.
- Treat it like real expedition gear powerful, but not something you can ignore.

Crafting
Crafted on a crafting table using:
- 2 sticks
- 5 leather
- 2 iron ingots
