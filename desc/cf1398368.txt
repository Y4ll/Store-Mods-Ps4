Adds smooth, animated water splash effects that appear naturally around the player, enhancing the visual quality of every water interaction.

Features

Animated Splash Bursts

- Every water entry, dive and swim triggers a hand-animated 10-frame splash, stretched to the length of each particle so no two bursts look identical

- Splashes fade out smoothly instead of popping a colour gradient carries each particle from full white to transparent

Subpacks

Three size options pick one in the pack settings slider:

Big Splashes Large, detailed splashes for maximum visual impact.

Medium Splashes Balanced splash size, great for most devices.

Small Splashes Lightweight and subtle, ideal for lower-end devices.

Each option tunes particle count, splash size, speed and lifetime together, so the effect stays balanced at every setting.

Compatibility

- Resource Pack Only no Behavior Pack, no commands needed

- Works in Singleplayer, Multiplayer & Realms

- Compatible with Vibrant Visuals

- Performance friendly pick the Small subpack on lower-end devices

Installation

- Download the .mcpack file

- Open it Minecraft will import the pack automatically

- In your world settings, go to Resource Packs My Packs and activate Water Splash Animation

- Use the subpack slider to choose Small, Medium or Big Splashes
