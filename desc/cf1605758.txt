About

Glowing Ores makes resource gathering in Minecraft Bedrock easier and more satisfying. Ores stand out on every device: brightened textures on classic graphics, and genuine glow in the dark with Vibrant Visuals.

Features

- Works on every graphics mode:

- Classic (Simple/Fancy): ore pixels are brightened and saturated, so veins stand out clearly even in dark caves.

- Vibrant Visuals: ore veins literally glow in the dark. The glow is graded by each ore's natural shading crystal facets and metal flecks shine brightest for a natural, non-flat look. Materials are layered on top of Minecraft's official Vibrant Visuals maps, so metals keep their shine, gems stay glossy, and amethyst keeps its translucency.

- Complete Ore Coverage (stone and deepslate variants):

- Coal subtle ember glow

- Iron, Copper (including oxidized green flecks), Gold metallic glow

- Redstone the strongest glow of all ores

- Lapis Lazuli, Emerald, Diamond bright gem glow

- Nether Quartz and Nether Gold

- Valuable Blocks Support:

- Ancient Debris (side & top) glowing metallic bands

- Gilded Blackstone glowing gold flecks

- Amethyst Block, Budding Amethyst, and all Amethyst Clusters and Buds soft crystal glow

- Mob Spawner faint ember glow on the cage, making it easier to spot in dark caves

How to use

- Import the .mcaddon file into Minecraft Bedrock.

- Activate the Glowing Ores resource pack for your world.

- Put it at the top of your active resources list to override default textures correctly.

- (Optional) Turn on Vibrant Visuals in your Video settings to see the ores glow in the dark.

Notes

- This pack does not contain "X-ray" features; it only enhances blocks you are already looking at.

- The actual glow-in-the-dark effect requires Vibrant Visuals. On classic graphics the brightened textures make ores highly visible but do not emit light.

- The glow is a surface effect: ores shine in darkness but do not light up the surrounding area.

- Fully Vibrant Visuals compatible enabling this pack never turns Vibrant Visuals off for your world.

Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.50 or newer

- No external dependency required
