Rapid-Xp V1 [Achievement Friendly]

Description

Rapid-Xp is a lightweight Minecraft Bedrock Edition add-on designed to dramatically speed up your XP progression. Once enabled, the add-on automatically grants experience levels continuously while you play, allowing you to reach high enchantment levels, repair gear, and unlock XP-dependent features much faster.

Whether you're preparing powerful enchanted equipment, testing game mechanics, or simply want a faster survival experience, Rapid-Xp provides a constant stream of experience without requiring commands or cheats.

Features

- Automatically grants XP levels while playing

- Works continuously for all players in the world

- Extremely fast level progression

- Useful for enchanting, repairing, and testing

- Multiplayer compatible

- Achievement Friendly

- Lightweight and easy to use

- No manual activation required

How It Works

After the add-on is enabled in your world, players automatically receive experience levels every game tick. This results in rapid XP growth, making it easy to reach high levels in a very short time.
