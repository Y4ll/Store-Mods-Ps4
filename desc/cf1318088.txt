ACT LEGACY

A Minecraft: Bedrock Edition gun mod with four fully custom weapons, deep attachment systems, real-world-calibrated ballistics, and full Survival crafting.

Overview

ACT LEGACY adds four hand-built weapons to Minecraft Bedrock, each with its own attachment roster, magazine lineup, reload behavior, and personality. This isn't a reskin pack every gun has bullet velocity tuned to its real-world caliber, a tactical reload that's genuinely faster than a full reload, a proper weapon-inspection animation, and stats that shift depending on what you've got attached and loaded. Everything every gun, every magazine, every round of ammo is craftable in Survival.

The Guns

M17 Glock 17 (9x19mm)

The sidearm. Reflex sight, suppressor, and laser, plus three magazine types. Reliable, cheap to craft, and always in your back pocket.

SS2 Pindad SS2 (5.56x45mm)

A full-size assault rifle with 5 optics, 3 suppressors, 3 lasers, an extra slot, and 4 magazines (Straight Magazine equipped by default no more pulling a fresh SS2 with nothing loaded). Each suppressor tier has its own distinct suppressed-fire sound instead of one generic hush.

CR56 AK-pattern rifle (7.62x39mm)

The most customizable gun in the mod: 4 optics, 4 suppressors, 2 lasers, a stock, an extra slot, and 7 magazines ranging from a compact 25-rounder up to a 150-round Extended drum. True tactical-vs-full reload swap a partial mag quickly, or rack a fresh one from empty.

M82 Barrett-style .50 BMG heavy sniper

The showpiece. This gun alone carries:

7 optics with a real tradeoff higher magnification means more accuracy but more movement slowness while aiming, not just a stat sticker

4 suppressors, each doing something different on hit: one sets targets on fire, one adds flat bonus damage, one causes nausea, one slows the target down

5 magazines, including an Explosive Magazine round detonates on impact with a player, mob, or block

4 cosmetic camo skins Gold, Parrot, Snow, and Magma that reskin the gun body while attachments keep their own look

Muzzle velocity that actually feels like a .50 cal, not a machine gun

Systems

Real ballistics every gun's bullet speed is scaled to its actual caliber, not a flat generic number

Tactical vs. full reload a genuinely different, faster animation when you're topping off instead of running dry

Weapon inspection sneak + punch to check out your gun up close, with its own animation and sound

Suppressed fire visually and audibly distinct from unsuppressed shots

Attachment-driven stats optics and lasers shift your hipfire/ADS accuracy, suppressors trade accuracy for stealth (and on the M82, for a status effect), stocks and extras round things out

Mag-driven ammo & damage swap magazines and your capacity and damage scale with what's loaded, not a fixed number regardless of the mag in the gun

Full Survival Crafting

Nothing in ACT LEGACY is creative-only.

Gun parts Body, Barrel, Stock, and Magazine Housing are craftable from plain vanilla materials, and every gun uses the same four parts

Assembling a gun the four parts plus one tier material distinguishes which weapon you build: iron for the M17, gold for the SS2, diamond for the CR56, netherite for the M82

Magazines craftable straight from vanilla metals, scaled to match each gun's power

Bullets built the way a real cartridge is: copper ingot for the casing, flint for the primer, gunpowder for the propellant (the M82's .50 BMG swaps in iron for a heavier projectile)

Bulk ammo packs craft loose rounds into a pack for less inventory clutter
