Vein Miner Pro Addon Smart Ore Mining System for Minecraft Bedrock 1.21+

Vein Miner Pro Addon is a survival focused Minecraft Bedrock mining addon that allows players to mine entire ore veins at once instead of breaking blocks one by one. By sneaking and mining a single ore block with the correct tool, all connected ores of the same type are automatically detected and mined in a controlled and balanced way. It is designed to save time, reduce repetitive mining, and keep gameplay fair through durability limits and block caps.

If you are looking for a Minecraft Bedrock vein miner addon, ore chain mining mod, fast mining system, or survival ore breaker addon, this addon is built to deliver powerful mining while remaining fully compatible with survival gameplay and SMP servers.
