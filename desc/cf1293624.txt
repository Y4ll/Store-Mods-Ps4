The Box: Source of Terror

It all starts with a wooden box.

- Awakening: Hit the box multiple times to "start" Jackie. She'll jump out, remaining attached, swaying slightly and making characteristic sounds.

- Release: If you stay near her, Jackie will jump off the box and begin the chase.

Jackie's States: 4 Levels of Nightmare

Jackie changes, becoming increasingly dangerous and terrifying.

- 1. Simple: Undamaged, just jumped out of the box. Chases you and other mobs. Uses powerful attacks and unexpected jumpscares.

- 2. With Yellow Eyes: After the first "defeat," Jackie returns with glowing yellow eyes. She's more aggressive, and her jumpscare is more intense.

- 3. Broken: After the second "defeat," Jackie appears in her final, broken form. Her movements are chaotic, and her jumpscare is pure madness.

- 4. Lying Down: After defeating Jackie, she falls and twitches.
