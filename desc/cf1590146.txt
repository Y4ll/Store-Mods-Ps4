In this addon you are able to transform into 4 different titans each with different abilitys like stomp roar and more You also get items like odm gear revolver sword and a sniper that help you.
Odm gear : You are also able to use an item called odm gear to move around the map way faster and there are pure titans that spawn that you can kill with the odm gear, you are also able to craft the odm gear in survival mode so you are always safe. There are gas refills stations where when you run out of gas for the odm gear you can build a gas refill station and refill your gas to start flying around again
Titans : When you transform into a titan you get extra heart's you are faster and stronger, each titan has different skills, one has more skills and another one less, for example beast titan has 4 skills, there is stomp, slash, roar and throw rock, when you use the throw rock skill it throws a rock that explodes, or when you roar if you infect a villager with an item called pure titan potion and then you roar with beast titan it transforms into a pure titan.

With the odm gear you get about 6 minutes of gas once you run out you have to refill it or else you are going to get eaten by a titan

For servers only 1 person can hold a titan power and you can also loose the titan power if you are not careful enogh.
