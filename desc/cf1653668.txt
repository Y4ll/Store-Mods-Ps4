Omnichunk

Every block you place, break, or change echoes across the entire world, forever!

Build a house in one chunk, walk somewhere you've never been, and it's already there. Dig a moat and river systems ripple across the map behind you. Light a nether portal or complete an end portal frame and it opens everywhere. No plugins, no commands to remember, no waiting it just happens as you play...

How it works

Omnichunk treats your world as one infinite repeating pattern. Every change you make gets remembered at its position within a chunk, and a background patrol continuously stamps that pattern onto every chunk near you including ones you've never set foot in. Travel 10,000 blocks in any direction and the pattern is waiting for you the moment the chunk loads. No range limit, no cooldown, no "must interact first."

Features

Infinite radius not a fixed copy radius, a genuinely unbounded repeating pattern

Full block-entity sync chests, barrels, and shulkers stay in sync in real time; beds, banners, skulls, and item frames keep their exact color/pattern/contents

Portals light a nether portal or complete an end portal anywhere and every copy lights up too

Signs, jukeboxes, redstone, and mob spawns also get copied

Natural growth stays in sync bonemealed trees, growing crops, and spreading fire replicate just like anything you build by hand

Fully persistent closing and reopening your world doesn't lose a thing

Built for performance enforcement is spread across ticks and prioritizes chunks near you first, so it never hitches even on long play sessions

Commands (requires cheats enabled)

/scriptevent omnichunk:pause freeze recording/stamping so you can build freely

/scriptevent omnichunk:resume turn the madness back on

/scriptevent omnichunk:status see pattern size, tracked chunks, and more

/scriptevent omnichunk:reset wipe the pattern and start fresh

/scriptevent omnichunk:verify clean up any stale records near you

Requirements

Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90+

Beta APIs experimental toggle enabled when creating/editing your world
