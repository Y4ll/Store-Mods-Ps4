Hello! Welcome to another pack submission of mine. This pack, named "Assorted Allays", was originally made by GillsBills for Java Edition and was ported by me to Bedrock Edition. Assorted Allays is the perfect pack for your allays, bringing many custom variants that will bring joy to anyone!

These mob variants all can be renamed to their appropriate skin and can spawn naturally. The pack is formatted in a way that allows for the easy creation of add-ons! Nametag Version is also available

Naming Guide!

Oi!

This text sheet is for anyone looking to rename a mob into a specific skin variant!

The first name will be the orginal texture of the mob followed by the names of the variants!

If any of the names has a "|" in it, the variant can be named in more then one way.
