Glowing Ores is a texture pack that makes all ores emit a gentle glow, making them easier to find while mining. It enhances visibility in caves while keeping the original Minecraft style.

Benefits: You can easily find ores without using torches or night vision. Perfect for mining and exploring dark areas with improved visibility.

Subpacks Included:

- Outlined Ores: Adds a clean outline around each ore for better visibility.

- Outlined Ores Glow+: Same as outlined version but with a stronger glow effect.

- No Outline Ores: Keeps ores in their normal shape but adds a smooth glow effect.

- Animated Glowing Ores: Adds a dynamic, moving glow effect to the ores for a more immersive feel.

Compatibility: Works smoothly with most vanilla-style texture packs and doesnt affect other blocks or performance.

Important: To use this pack, make sure Vibrant Visuals are turned ON in your game settings. Without this, the pack will not work properly.
