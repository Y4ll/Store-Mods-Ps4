Advanced Mining Tools

Each hammer is available in all vanilla material tiers, from basic stone to powerful netherite. However, the most advanced mining modes are unlocked only when you reach diamond tier and above - rewarding players who invest in better materials!

Smart Mode System:

- Shift + Right Click to cycle through different mining patterns

- HUD Display shows current mode and tool durability above your hotbar

- Full Compatibility with all vanilla enchantments (Efficiency, Fortune, Silk Touch, etc.)

Mining Modes Available:

Area Mining:

- 3x3 Mode - Perfect for small-scale excavation

- 5x5 Mode - Medium area clearing

- 7x7 Mode - Large-scale mining operations

Tunnel Mining:

- Deep Tunnel Mode - Creates long straight tunnels

- Branch Mining - Efficient ore hunting patterns
